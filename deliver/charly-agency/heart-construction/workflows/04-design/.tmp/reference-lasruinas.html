— Frequently Asked

Everything to know before your visit.

Everything to know before your visit.

What exactly is a bathhouse?

A wellness space designed around heat, water, and rest. You move freely between saunas, steam rooms, cold plunges, pools and relaxation areas at your own pace. Unlike a spa, there are no treatments or appointments. The experience is the space itself.

What should I bring?

Swimsuit, workout clothes for the gym, and sandals or slippers. We provide towels, water, shampoo, conditioner, shower gel, and blowdryers. Robes, slippers, and sauna hats are available to purchase on-site.

Are all spaces mixed gender?

Yes. All thermal and common spaces are mixed gender. Locker rooms, showers and bathrooms are gendered. Swimsuits required in all thermal areas. No nudity.

Is there parking?

We don't have on-site parking, but we're a short walk from Old San Juan's main public garages: La Cochera, Ballajá, Doña Fela, La Puntilla, and Covadonga.

Is the thermal circuit safe during pregnancy?

If you're pregnant or have any medical conditions, please consult your healthcare provider before using heat and hydrotherapy installations.

Do I need to book in advance?

Booking ahead is recommended, especially on weekends. Walk-ins welcome subject to availability. Entry slots are every 30 minutes.

Is there an age requirement?

Las Ruinas is an 18+ facility.

How is the water kept clean?

Our pools use ozone and UV filtration systems. Pools are professionally maintained on a weekly basis and tested regularly.

What does the Day Pass include?

2.5 hours of full facility access: the gym, all eight thermal experiences (saunas, steam, cold plunge, thermal bath, saltwater pool, dousing bucket), the tea room, and our lounge spaces. Towels and water included.

Are massages available?

Massage services are not currently available. Updates will be announced on Instagram @lasruinaspr.

Can I bring food or drinks?

Outside food and drinks are not permitted. Our Smoothie Spa offers smoothies, fresh juices, and wellness shots. Complimentary tea, nuts, and popsicles in the Tea Room.
