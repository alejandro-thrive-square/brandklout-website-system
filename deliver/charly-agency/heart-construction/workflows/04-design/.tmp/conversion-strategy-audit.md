## Current Heart Site: Conversion Assets Worth Preserving

The current Squarespace site is more conversion-ready than the first prototype in several areas:

1. **Immediate category clarity**
   - Hero labels Heart as `Los Angeles Builders`.
   - Current site already includes `Custom Homes Built With Intention`.

2. **Stronger outcome promise**
   - `Build the home of your dream, without the headache`.
   - `We take the stress out of building`.
   - `Rest easy knowing we will handle everything at every step`.

3. **Trust and emotional proof**
   - Four testimonial cards with specific homeowner concerns: permits, design meetings, landscaping, budget, responsiveness, trust, schedule, and team assembly.
   - Testimonials should be moved higher on the landing page, not buried.

4. **Process clarity**
   - Existing process is simple and conversion-friendly:
     - The Plan: vision, goals, budget, timeline.
     - The Team: assemble the right team.
     - The Call: agreement/retainer to begin permits, estimating, and design meetings.

5. **Portfolio proof**
   - Current site includes project names and imagery: Malibu, Hollywood Hills, Malibu Pier, La Cuesta, Hancock Park, Marina Del Rey.
   - The custom homes page needs project proof before the final CTA.

6. **Service and location SEO routes**
   - Current navigation exposes service pages and location pages that matter for Framer migration/redirect planning.
   - Custom Homes page should preserve pathways to custom homes, full remodels, ADUs, outdoor living, and key cities where relevant.

7. **Contact proof**
   - Current site includes phone numbers, email, physical locations, and license number. These are useful conversion trust elements but should be confirmed before final launch.

## Las Ruinas: Useful Reference Patterns

Las Ruinas is useful less for construction copy and more for conversion design patterns:

1. **Hero with concise promise + proof badge + two CTAs.**
2. **Immediate practical information strip** below the hero.
3. **A `Why` section with three numbered differentiators.**
4. **Proof section before deep explanation** using review score/social proof.
5. **Experience/product cards that make the offering tangible.**
6. **A step-by-step visit/process section that removes uncertainty.**
7. **Offer/price block and FAQ before final CTA.**
8. **Final CTA repeats the same action, not a new/unclear action.**

For Heart Construction, translate these patterns into custom-home buying psychology:

- Replace day pass with consultation/project fit.
- Replace thermal circuit with custom-home journey/process.
- Replace membership/pricing with project-fit qualifiers and budget/timeline expectations.
- Replace guest FAQ with homeowner concerns: budget, timeline, permits, team, change orders, living through construction, and next steps.

## What the Current Prototype Was Missing

- Too much generic strategy language before proof.
- Not enough use of current Heart assets and existing conversion copy.
- Testimonials/project proof were too low and too placeholder-driven.
- The process section was informative but not conversion-sequenced around the buyer's next step.
- The form appeared before enough qualification and reassurance.
- No FAQ/objection handling before final conversion.
- No clear `what happens after I submit?` microcopy.
- Framer build direction was noted but not translated into a Framer component/section conversion map.

## Recommended New Conversion Flow

1. Hero: `Custom Homes Built With Intention` + `without the headache` subpromise + CTA.
2. Trust/Info Strip: service area, project type, process promise, phone/license if verified.
3. Problem/Empathy: major investment, too many decisions, budget/timeline uncertainty.
4. Outcome Promise: Heart takes stress out of building.
5. Why Heart / 3 Differentiators: Plan, Team, Communication.
6. Proof Early: testimonial carousel/cards and project image strip.
7. Custom Home Project Types: ground-up, hillside, rebuilds, major remodel/addition adjacency.
8. Signature Process: Plan -> Team -> Call -> Pre-construction -> Build.
9. Portfolio / Recent Projects: Malibu, Hollywood Hills, Malibu Pier, La Cuesta, Hancock Park, Marina Del Rey.
10. Qualification Section: who this is for / who this is not for.
11. FAQ / Objection Handling.
12. Final CTA + form: consultation request with `what happens next` details.
