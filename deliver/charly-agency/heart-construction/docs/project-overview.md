# Heart Construction Project Overview

## Project Summary

Heart Construction is a Los Angeles residential construction company focused on helping homeowners navigate complex construction projects with clarity, experience, and care.

Core brand line:

> Build With Intention.

The website and landing page work should position Heart Construction as a trusted, experienced, transparent, and highly organized partner for major residential construction projects — not as a general handyman or low-cost contractor.

## Brand Positioning

Heart Construction helps homeowners navigate complex residential construction projects with:

- Clarity
- Experience
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Communication
- Care
- Project management
- Pre-construction planning
- Quality craftsmanship

The site should communicate that Heart Construction's value begins before construction starts. Their pre-construction process helps align scope, budget, timeline, permitting, design, and expectations so clients avoid costly surprises.

## Core Message

Recommended custom homes landing page message:

> Custom homes built with intention.

Supporting message:

> Heart Construction helps Los Angeles homeowners navigate custom home projects with clarity, experience, transparent communication, and genuine care from the first conversation through completion.

Broader supporting message for future pages:

> From custom homes and full remodels to fire rebuilds and hillside construction, we guide every step with thoughtful planning, transparent communication, and intentional execution.

## Client Pain Points to Address

The website should directly address common homeowner concerns:

- Budget uncertainty
- Poor contractor communication
- Delays and missed deadlines
- Lack of transparency
- Difficulty coordinating consultants
- Feeling overwhelmed by the process
- Cost overruns
- Quality control
- Managing many decisions
- Living through construction
- Fear of choosing the wrong contractor

## Trust and Social Proof Needs

Recommended trust elements:

- Testimonials
- Project case studies
- Project galleries
- Before-and-after visuals where available
- Process explanation
- Service area proof
- Credentials, licenses, awards, or associations where available
- Client education content

## Tone and Creative Direction

Tone should be:

- Professional
- Educational
- Practical
- Authentic
- Experienced
- Premium but approachable
- Calm and reassuring

Avoid language that feels generic, salesy, overly corporate, or focused on discount pricing.
