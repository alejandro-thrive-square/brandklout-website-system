01

Logo

The wordmark carries the brand. One weight, two inks, no exceptions. When the full wordmark cannot fit, fall back to the Z-mark — never a cropped wordmark.

Clearspace
1× 1× 1× 1×

Keep a margin of 1× the mark height on all sides. Nothing enters the clearspace — no type, no imagery, no edges.

Minimum size
120px — wordmark floor
24px — mark floor

Below 120px the wordmark loses its cut. Switch to the Z-mark, never scale past the floor.

05

Motion

Movement is confident and short: one signature ease, small travel, staggered groups. Hover a curve to run it. Everything degrades to opacity for reduced motion.

Expo out Signature

Reveals, page transitions, hero entrances. Fast arrival, long settle.

Standard Interface

Layout shifts, accordions, and anything that moves while on screen.

Micro Feedback

Hovers, toggles, focus rings. Felt, not watched.

Ambient dust

The particulate field on this page is a brand layer for dark, high-impact sections. Density ≤ 120 particles, alpha ≤ 0.5, pointer pull ≤ 0.001 — it must never compete with copy.

07

Imagery

Two languages only: 3D brand renders on ink, and real product screens inside case covers. No stock photography, no illustration mixing.

  • Placement — renders float on ink with generous clearspace; never boxed on paper.
  • Treatment — saturate 1.1–1.2, contrast 1.05; no other filters.
  • Grain — the noise texture overlays hero and footer surfaces at 4–6% opacity.
  • Case covers — always 3:2, cropped from the live site, device-free.
