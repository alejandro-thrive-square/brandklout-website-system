## Immediate Redesign Inputs

### Strong reusable assets/content

Keep or rework:
- The "Strategy + Experience + Functionality = Profit" concept can become a stronger strategic thesis.
- 35+ projects, 4+ years, 25+ clients are useful credibility metrics.
- Project portfolio is substantial and should become a central proof system.
- Performance score proof is concrete and valuable, especially for Webflow development.
- Migration details like 80 blog posts migrated and 160 URLs redirected are strong operational proof.
- Pricing packages are concrete and scannable, though they likely need strategic reframing.
- Testimonials give client-voice credibility.

### Critical content/design debt

Issues to fix in redesign:
- Placeholder text is visible in navigation.
- Header/dropdown categories duplicate.
- There are at least two inconsistent site generations across pages.
- Footer years differ: 2024 on Mused page, 2025 on homepage/other project pages.
- Legal links are inconsistent; homepage Terms points to Upwork.
- Case study details contain repeated/generic result bullets that conflict with project scope.
- Several pages show CMS empty states or incomplete links, including "No items found." and "Not Available."
- Typography/capitalization/copy style is inconsistent: webflow/Webflow, Wordpress/WordPress, google tag manager/Google Tag Manager.
- Multiple typos: portofolio, maintainance, Convertions, URL's.
- Some CTA labels duplicate in the DOM: "Book a Call Book a Call."
- The site currently positions as "freelancer" while the brand name is "Studio"; redesign must decide whether the market wants personal expert, boutique studio, or agency positioning.
