FAQs Clifton Homeowners Ask Our Siding Contractor Team

How long does it take a siding contractor to replace siding?

Depending on the size of your home (and other factors), it can take a siding contractor anywhere from 3 days to 2 weeks to complete a siding replacement or installation project.

Will you bring the siding materials on the start date?

Siding materials will be shipped to your home prior to our start date.

Do you paint siding as well?

Our siding contractors do not paint siding. All of our siding is ordered in the color that reflects your vision for your home.
