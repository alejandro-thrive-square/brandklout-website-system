Three distinct services. Each built around your specific goal — not a generic process. The deliverable is the same: a model you can use, not a pitch.

01Invest
First-time buyers

Your first multifamily.

A duplex or triplex you live in, while tenants pay your mortgage. The strategy explained, the property modeled, the offer sized.

Most first-time buyers don't realize they can start building wealth from day one. Instead of locking yourself into a single-family home with one mortgage and no income, a duplex or triplex lets you live in one unit and rent the rest — your tenants help pay your mortgage while your equity grows.

03Develop
Homeowners with land

Build more than you think.

SB 9, ADU law, density bonus. Free lot review, full feasibility, end-to-end project management — concept through certificate of occupancy.

Recent California law opened up small-scale development on lots that used to be locked. SB 9 lets you split a single-family lot and build up to four units. State ADU law lets you add a detached second unit on nearly any residential property.

  • Feasibility analysis — what can you build and will it pencil
  • Full proforma modeling before you commit a dollar
  • Team assembly — architect, engineers, general contractor
