01 Brand Essence

Who Giovanni is.

An advisor, not a salesman. Multifamily investment and small-scale development for buyers and developers who treat real estate like the financial instrument it is.

“The best brands stand for something — a big idea, a strategic position, a defined set of values, a voice that stands apart.” — Alina Wheeler, Designing Brand Identity

Giovanni works with two kinds of clients: people buying their first or next multifamily property (duplex through 12-unit), and homeowners or investors looking to build additional units on land they already own. The brand exists to make clients feel informed, confident, and trusting — grounded in real analysis, not in a pitch.

Do

  • ✓Use “Serrano” in Terracotta #E07A5F on every version.
  • ✓Maintain clear space equal to the height of the “G” on all sides.
  • ✓Keep the gradient rule between the name and descriptor.
  • ✓Use Deep Navy as the primary dark background.
  • ✓Use Warm Canvas — never pure white — as the light background.
  • ✓Minimum size: 1.5" wide in print · 150px wide in digital.

Don't

  • ✗Change the color of “Serrano” to anything other than Terracotta.
  • ✗Place the logo on a busy photographic background.
  • ✗Stretch, skew, rotate, or distort the wordmark.
  • ✗Change the font of the wordmark to anything other than Lora.
  • ✗Add drop shadows, outlines, or visual effects to the logo.
  • ✗Use all-caps for the wordmark name itself.

Color rules

Terracotta is an accent — never use as a large background fill.
Pure black #000 is never used. Deep Navy replaces it.
Pure white #FFF is avoided. Warm Canvas replaces it.
Golden Sun is never used as body text — decorative and accent only.
Never introduce colors outside this palette without approval.
When in doubt: Deep Navy bg, Warm Canvas text, Terracotta accent.

05 Brand Voice

What Giovanni says.

The tagline invites — the thesis grounds it. Direct, declarative, no hype. Specific over general. San Diego, not Southern California.

Move with intention.
Official tagline · use everywhere the brand is introduced
01.
Confident, not arrogant
You know your market. You say it directly. You never brag — you just deliver results and let them speak.
02.
Warm, not casual
You're approachable and real. But you're also a professional. Friendly — never sloppy or informal.
03.
Energetic, not frantic
You move fast and bring energy to every deal. But you're never rushed or pushy. You're driven.
04.
Authentic, not performative
You say what you mean. No corporate speak, no buzzwords. Real talk — always honest, always human.

Giovanni sounds like

“Most agents sell properties. I analyze deals.”
“Let me model this before you make an offer.”
“Here's what the numbers actually say.”
“The deal works or it doesn't — and the proforma will tell us which.”

Giovanni never sounds like

“I'm so passionate about finding your dream home!”
“Over 100 five-star reviews on Zillow!”
“Your dream home awaits — call today!”
“FREE consultation — limited spots available!”
