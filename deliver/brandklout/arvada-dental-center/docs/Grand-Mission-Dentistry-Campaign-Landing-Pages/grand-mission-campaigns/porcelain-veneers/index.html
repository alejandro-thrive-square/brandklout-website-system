Veneers can address deep discoloration, chips, wear, small gaps, uneven shapes, and some minor alignment concerns. Significant misalignment or active disease should be addressed first.

Many porcelain veneers last 10–20 years with good care, though lifespan varies with bite, grinding, hygiene, habits, material, and maintenance.

The treatment is planned around your face, tooth proportions, preferred shade, and natural translucency. A preview and temporaries help guide shape and length before final placement.

Traditional porcelain veneers usually require some enamel preparation and are not considered reversible. The consultation should compare bonding, whitening, alignment, and no-prep options when appropriate.
