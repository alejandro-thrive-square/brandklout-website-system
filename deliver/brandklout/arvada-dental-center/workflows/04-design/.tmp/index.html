Three fabrications were purged and stay purged.

Individually attributed reviews remain: each is a named person's published words, quoted verbatim, with no score attached.

How to review

  • Scroll slowly through the pinned chapters. They are scrubbed — the page holds still and the scene plays at whatever pace you scroll, forwards or backwards. Scrolling past quickly is the one way to miss the whole point.
  • The nav shows which chapter you are in and how far through the page you are.
  • Review in a focused tab. A background tab freezes transitions and timers and will make working motion look broken.
  • The open/closed pill is live against the practice's real Mon–Fri 8–5 hours, so it changes depending on when you look.
  • Forms validate and confirm, but deliberately do not post anywhere yet. Payloads are logged to window.__arvadaLeads.
  • Every image is client-hosted, pulled from arvadadentalcenter.com — but several are evidently licensed stock the practice already uses. Nothing is captioned as a patient or a result. Rights need confirming before launch.
  • Every review is real and named, with no score attached. Sources in content-intake-summary.md; verification in qa-report.md.
