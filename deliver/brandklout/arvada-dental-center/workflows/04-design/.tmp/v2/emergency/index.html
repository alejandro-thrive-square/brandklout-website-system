What counts as a dental emergency?+
Severe or persistent tooth pain, facial or jaw swelling, a knocked-out or displaced tooth, a painful broken tooth, an abscess, uncontrolled bleeding, or fever with a dental infection all warrant urgent guidance.
What should I do with a knocked-out tooth?+
Pick it up by the crown, not the root. Rinse it gently without scrubbing, keep it moist in milk if possible, and call immediately. Time can affect whether the tooth can be saved.
Can you see me today?+
Urgent visits are scheduled according to clinical need and availability. Call as early as possible so the team can assess your situation and offer the earliest appropriate appointment.
Can I get sedation for emergency treatment?+
Sedation options are available for appropriate patients and procedures. Dr. Bennett will review your health history and the planned treatment before recommending a safe option.
