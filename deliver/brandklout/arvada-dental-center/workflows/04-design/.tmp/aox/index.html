Is full arch right for you?

You don't have to keep living this way

Full-arch implants are usually the answer when the problem has stopped being one tooth and started being every meal.

  • You are missing most or all of the teeth in an arch
  • Your dentures slip, click, or need adhesive to stay put
  • You avoid whole categories of food and have stopped mentioning it
  • Your remaining teeth are decayed or failing and you're facing extractions
  • Your face has begun to change shape as the jawbone recedes
  • You want something fixed in place, not something you take out at night
