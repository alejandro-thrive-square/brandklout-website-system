The Straight Answer

When veneers are the wrong choice

A cosmetic dentist who only ever recommends the cosmetic treatment is not worth trusting with your face.

Veneers hide problem areas. They do not address functional concerns. If your issue is a missing tooth, a failing tooth, or a bite that is doing damage, a veneer sits on top of an unsolved problem — and Arvada Dental Center will tell you so.

Patients with serious oral health issues such as periodontitis are generally not candidates until that is treated. Where the concern is alignment rather than appearance, Invisalign® or Six Month Smiles is usually the better answer. Where it is function, that is what implants are for.

Candidacy · three things Dr. Bennett checks first

Gum health

Veneers meet the gum line, so inflammation or periodontal disease has to be resolved first. The practice offers LANAP laser therapy where it is needed.

Decay and structural soundness

A veneer bonds to enamel. Where there is significant decay or a tooth is structurally compromised, a crown is the sounder option.

Bite, grinding and clenching

Porcelain is strong but not indestructible. If you grind, that gets addressed as part of the plan rather than discovered afterwards.

The Method

Two visits, one design.

Visit one 01

Preparation and design

Dr. Bennett removes a small portion of enamel from the front of the teeth being treated, and the design is worked out against your facial features, skin tone and gum line rather than a catalogue shade.

Between 02

Made to fit you

The practice's own technology handles the design, and the veneers are fabricated for your case rather than picked from a tray.

Visit two 03

Fitted and bonded

The finished veneers go on. Veneers can be combined with laser gum surgery where the gum line itself is part of the problem — a full smile makeover rather than a set of shells.

In the room 05

The Materials

Not all veneers are the same thing

"Veneer" covers two quite different treatments. The difference shows up years later, which is exactly when it is hardest to undo.
