What they fix

Straight answer

When veneers are the wrong choice

A cosmetic dentist who only ever recommends the cosmetic treatment is not worth trusting with your face.

Veneers hide problem areas. They do not address functional concerns. If your issue is a missing tooth, a failing tooth, or a bite that is doing damage, a veneer sits on top of an unsolved problem — and Arvada Dental Center will tell you so.

Patients with serious oral health issues such as periodontitis are generally not candidates until that is treated. Where the concern is alignment rather than appearance, Invisalign® or Six Month Smiles is usually the better answer. Where it is function, that is what implants are for.

Candidacy

How Dr. Bennett decides

Most people with common cosmetic problems are good candidates. Three things get checked before anything is prepared.

Gum health

Veneers meet the gum line, so inflammation or periodontal disease has to be resolved first. The practice offers LANAP laser therapy where it is needed.

Decay and structural soundness

A veneer bonds to enamel. Where there is significant decay or a tooth is structurally compromised, a crown is the sounder option.

Bite, grinding and clenching

Porcelain is strong but not indestructible. If you grind, that gets addressed as part of the plan rather than discovered afterwards.

The smile design process

Two visits, one design

At your first consultation Dr. Bennett removes a small portion of enamel from the front of the teeth being treated, and the design is worked out against your facial features, skin tone and gum line rather than a catalogue shade.

The practice's own technology handles the design; the veneers are fabricated and fitted at the second visit. Veneers can be combined with laser gum surgery where the gum line itself is part of the problem — a full smile makeover rather than a set of shells.

Materials

Not all veneers are the same thing

"Veneer" covers two quite different treatments. The difference shows up years later, which is exactly when it is hardest to undo.

Composite veneers compared with the porcelain veneers used at Arvada Dental Center
  Composite / traditional Porcelain, minimal-prep
Appearance in daylight More opaque — can read flat Translucency matched to real enamel
Tooth preparation Often more enamel removed Minimal-prep, tooth-conserving
Staining Picks up stain over time Highly stain-resistant
Visits to complete Varies, often repeat touch-ups As few as two
Typical service life† Around 4–8 years 10–20+ years with proper care

† Service-life ranges are general characteristics of the materials, not a guarantee for any individual case. How long yours last depends on your bite, your habits and your hygiene — Dr. Bennett will be straight with you about that at the consultation.
