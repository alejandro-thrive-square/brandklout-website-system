What's happening

Find your symptom

Dental problems get worse quickly and rarely settle on their own. These are the ones we treat most often.

Sudden or severe toothache

Pain that keeps you awake, or that has stopped responding to painkillers.

Knocked-out tooth

Time-critical. Keep the tooth moist, handle it by the crown not the root, and call immediately.

Fractured or broken tooth

From an accident, a sports injury, or biting down on something hard.

Infected or abscessed tooth

Swelling, a bad taste, or fever alongside the pain. Infection needs treating, not waiting out.

Loose tooth or lost filling

A filling that has come out, or a tooth that has started to move.

Chipped or cracked crown

Damage to existing work, including a crown that has come off entirely.

Painful or inflamed gums

Gums that bleed, throb or have swollen up over a short period.

Broken or damaged denture

A denture that has cracked or no longer fits, leaving you unable to eat properly.
