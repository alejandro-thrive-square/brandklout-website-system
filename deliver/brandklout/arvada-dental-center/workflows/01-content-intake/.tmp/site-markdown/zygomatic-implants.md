### What Are Zygomatic Implants?

Zygomatic implants are extra-long implants that bypass the upper jawbone entirely and anchor into the zygomatic bone -- the cheekbone. This makes them viable for patients who have lost too much jaw bone for conventional implants.

The cheekbone is naturally dense and does not require grafting to support an implant. Because of this, zygomatic implants can often be placed and loaded with a temporary restoration on the same day as surgery.

### Am I a Candidate for Zygomatic Implants?

Zygomatic implants are best suited for patients with significant upper jaw bone loss who do not qualify for traditional implants. This includes patients who have worn dentures for many years and experienced ongoing bone resorption, or those who have been told bone grafting alone would not be sufficient.

Ideal candidates are in good overall health and free from active oral infections. Smokers are advised to quit before treatment, as smoking significantly increases the risk of implant failure. A consultation with 3D imaging is the only way to confirm whether zygomatic implants are the right fit for your anatomy.

### What to Expect: The Zygomatic Implant Process

1

### Consultation and 3D Imaging

We start with a full evaluation and 3D CBCT scan to map your bone structure, sinus anatomy, and cheekbone density. This scan drives every placement decision before surgery begins.

2

### Surgical Placement

Surgery is performed under sedation or general anesthesia. Implants are precisely angled through the upper jaw and anchored into the cheekbone. In some cases, additional conventional implants are placed alongside for extra support.

3

### Immediate Temporary Restoration

A temporary full-arch restoration is attached the same day. You leave our office with a complete smile while your implants heal.

4

### Healing and Integration

Over the following months, your implants fuse with the bone. We schedule follow-up visits to monitor progress and adjust your temporary restoration as needed.

5

### Final Restoration

Once fully healed, your permanent full-arch restoration is custom-fabricated and secured to your implants for a beautiful, lasting result.

### Zygomatic Implants vs. Traditional Implants

Both solutions replace missing teeth with implant-supported restorations -- but they serve very different patient situations.

### Traditional Dental Implants

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c4281e6c553da8f2a0363_Traditional%20Dental%20Implants.jpg)

- Anchors into the upper or lower jawbone
- May require bone grafting if volume is insufficient
- May require a sinus lift for upper jaw placement
- Several months of healing before final restoration
- Best suited for patients with adequate bone density
- Performed by most implant dentists

### Zygomatic Implants

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c41027a85dfe414b12aae_zygomatic%20implants.%20Medically%20accurate%203D%20illustration%20of%20human%20teeth%20and%20dentures.webp)

- Anchors into the cheekbone (zygoma)
- No bone grafting required
- No sinus lift needed
- Same-day temporary teeth in most cases
- Designed for severe upper jaw bone loss
- Requires a specialist with advanced implant training

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c43d50fb322270ce6078c_zygomatic%20implant%20result.webp)

### Recovery and Aftercare

Expect noticeable cheek swelling and soreness for the first one to two weeks following surgery. Prescribed antibiotics and pain medication are standard, and a soft diet is required for several months during osseointegration. Most patients return to light activity within a week. Our team provides detailed aftercare instructions and stays closely available through every stage of your healing.

What are zygomatic implants and how are they different from regular implants in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Zygomatic implants are longer implants that anchor into the cheekbone rather than the upper jawbone. Zygomatic implants skip the jaw entirely.

Is bone grafting required for zygomatic implants?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) No. Because zygomatic implants anchor into the cheekbone, grafting is not needed. Patients who would otherwise require months of bone grafting and healing can often move directly to implant placement.

Can I get teeth the same day as zygomatic implant surgery?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) In most cases, yes. Zygomatic implants typically achieve enough initial stability to support a temporary full-arch restoration the same day. Your permanent restoration follows once osseointegration is complete, usually several months later.

How long does the surgery take?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Most zygomatic implant surgeries take two to four hours depending on how many implants are placed. The procedure is performed under sedation or general anesthesia so patients remain comfortable throughout.

Is zygomatic implant surgery painful?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) The procedure itself is performed under anesthesia, so there is no pain during surgery. Post-operative discomfort is managed with prescribed medication. Cheek swelling peaks in the first few days and gradually resolves over one to two weeks.

How long do zygomatic implants last?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) With proper care, zygomatic implants can last 20 years or more. The cheekbone is one of the densest bones in the skull and provides a highly stable long-term anchor. Biannual checkups at Arvada Dental Center & Implants keep your restoration and surrounding tissue in good health.
