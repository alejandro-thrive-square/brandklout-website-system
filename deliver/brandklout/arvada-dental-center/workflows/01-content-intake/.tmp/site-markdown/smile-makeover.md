Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are custom porcelain caps that fit over a natural tooth to improve its color, size, and shape while restoring full strength and function. They are used after root canal therapy, to anchor a dental bridge, and to complete a dental implant restoration. A crown protects a weakened or damaged tooth while giving it the appearance of a healthy, natural one.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69972950737f6fb4ba3ce517_dental%20fixed%20bridge%20at%20aravada.webp)

Dental Bridges

A dental bridge uses a series of connected porcelain crowns to replace one or more missing teeth in a row. The bridge anchors to the healthy teeth on either side of the gap, restoring the appearance and function of a complete smile without surgery. Bridges are a reliable and time-tested option for patients who want to close a gap and protect the alignment of surrounding teeth.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699b6d7d2594e3a9dc7cc6ba_veneers%20materials.webp)

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers address gaps, chips, crookedness, and discoloration all at once. Each veneer is a thin custom shell bonded to the front surface of a single tooth, creating a dramatically improved appearance with minimal alteration to the underlying tooth structure. Veneers are custom-crafted to blend naturally with your surrounding teeth and are one of the most versatile cosmetic treatments available.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699b740af69e377bb4679f46_teeth%20whitening%20at%20arvada%20dental.webp)

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening brightens your smile multiple shades in a single session using a professional-grade formula that penetrates into the inner layers of the tooth to remove both surface stains and deep discoloration. Unlike over-the-counter products, our in-office treatment is personalized and monitored for safe, consistent results that last.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c39a6ee8ab788b9b802ef_Orthodontics.webp)

Orthodontics

Orthodontic treatment uses brackets and wires to reposition crooked or misaligned teeth by applying controlled pressure over time. We treat a range of cases from minor spacing and crowding to more significant bite misalignment. Treatment length varies by case and can range from a few months to a couple of years depending on the complexity of your correction.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c2edb804cb4b4840a7b6c_invisalign%20at%20arvada%20dental.webp)

Invisalign

Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gradually straighten teeth without metal brackets or wires. The aligners are virtually invisible when worn, making them a popular choice for teens and adults who want effective orthodontic results without the visibility of traditional braces. Because the aligners are removable, eating, brushing, and flossing remain completely unaffected throughout treatment.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c398f09393533a4a6f7f4_Occlusal%20Adjustment.webp)

Occlusal Adjustment

An occlusal adjustment corrects a misaligned bite by carefully reshaping the surfaces of teeth that do not come together properly. By redistributing bite pressure evenly, the procedure helps reduce teeth grinding, jaw pain, and headaches. It also protects any cosmetic or restorative work you have had done by preventing uneven forces from wearing treatments down prematurely.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c39460e98a80727672558_Composite%20Fillings.webp)

Composite Fillings

Composite fillings are a metal-free alternative to traditional amalgam fillings that match the natural color of your tooth for a completely seamless result. They are durable, long-lasting, and eliminate the gray or dark spots that metal fillings leave behind. Composite fillings can be used to treat new cavities or to replace existing metal fillings for a cleaner, more natural appearance.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c395c81a16a7c898b0990_Inlays%20and%20Onlays.webp)

Inlays and Onlays

Inlays and onlays are custom-crafted restorations used when a tooth has moderate damage that is too extensive for a filling but does not require a full crown. An inlay fits within the cusps of a back tooth, typically after a large cavity is treated. An onlay covers a larger area of the tooth, including one or more cusps, providing more coverage while preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c39768dc4be9f740b7cc0_Crown%20Lengthening.webp)

Crown Lengthening

Crown lengthening is a minor surgical procedure that reshapes excess gum tissue to create a more balanced and proportionate tooth-to-gum ratio. It is commonly used to correct a gummy smile where the teeth appear shorter than they are because too much gum covers the crown. Crown lengthening may also be performed to expose a portion of the tooth affected by decay or to prepare a tooth for a crown when not enough structure is visible above the gumline.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/693c51e685b19f0d0c149e1c_dental-implants.jpg)

Dental Implants

Dental implants permanently replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring a titanium post directly into the jawbone, which then supports a custom crown on top. They are considered the gold standard in tooth replacement due to their superior strength, longevity, and ability to preserve jawbone that would otherwise deteriorate after tooth loss. Implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth and require no special maintenance beyond standard brushing and flossing.
