Arvada Dental Center is proud to offer denver dental implant services to patients in the Denver metropolitan area. Dental implants are a permanent and natural-looking solution for replacing missing teeth. They are made of biocompatible titanium that is surgically inserted into the jawbone. Once in place, dental implants fuse with the jawbone, providing a strong foundation for a dental crown, bridge, or denture.

### Benefits of Dental Implants

There are many benefits to choosing dental implants over traditional dentures or bridges. Some of the most notable benefits include:

- **Improved appearance:** Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth. They can restore your smile and boost your confidence.
- **Enhanced oral health:** Dental implants help to preserve the health of your jawbone. They also prevent the shifting of teeth, which can lead to further tooth loss.
- **Durability:** Dental implants are very strong and can last for many years with proper care.
- **Comfort:** Dental implants are comfortable to wear and do not cause irritation to the gums.
- **Convenience:** Dental implants do not require any special care or maintenance.

### The Dental Implant Process at Arvada Dental Center

The dental implant process at Arvada Dental Center is a straightforward one. During your initial consultation, we will discuss your individual needs and goals to determine if dental implants are the right choice for you. If you are a good candidate for dental implants, we will develop a personalised treatment plan.

The dental implant process typically involves the following steps:

- **Consultation:** We will discuss your dental history and goals to determine if dental implants are right for you.
- **Treatment planning:** We will develop a personalised treatment plan that outlines the steps involved in your dental implant procedure.
- **Placement of the dental implant:**The dental implant will be surgically placed into your jawbone.
- **Osseointegration:** This is the process where the dental implant fuses with your jawbone. It typically takes several months for osseointegration to occur.
- **Abutment placement:** Once the dental implant has osseointegrated, we will place an abutment on top of the implant. The abutment connects the dental implant to the restoration.
- **Restoration placement:** We will place a dental crown, bridge, or denture on top of the abutment.

### Common Dental Implant FAQs

- **Am I a good candidate for dental implants?** The best way to determine if you are a good candidate for dental implants is to schedule a consultation with us. However, some general factors that may make you a good candidate for dental implants include good oral health, healthy jawbone, and non-smoking status.
- **How much do dental implants cost?** The cost of dental implants varies depending on the number of implants you need and the complexity of your treatment plan. However, dental implants are a valuable investment in your oral health and can last for many years.
- **Will dental implants hurt?** Dental implant surgery is performed under anaesthesia, so you will not feel any pain during the procedure. You may experience some soreness or discomfort after the surgery, but this can be managed with medication.

If you are considering dental implants, let’s schedule a consultation with Arvada Dental Center. We will be happy to answer any questions you have and help you determine if dental implants are the right choice for you.

We hope this article has been helpful. If you have any questions, please don’t hesitate to contact Arvada Dental Center.
