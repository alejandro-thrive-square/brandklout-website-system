Meet our founder

### Dr. Gregory Bennett, DDS

![Dr. Gregory Bennett, DDS, implant dentist at Arvada Dental Center in Arvada, Colorado](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69daece9b0e2d0e6388c5713_BB8A2838.jpg)

Dr. Gregory Bennett received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from The University of Iowa in 2001 and has been practicing dentistry in Colorado for nearly 20 years. Recognized as a **Top Dentist by 5280 Magazine**, Dr. Bennett is passionate about advancing his clinical expertise through immersive surgical training each year to deliver the highest standard of care.
‍
Dr. Bennett specializes in full-arch and full-mouth restorative care, implant dentistry, oral surgery, cosmetic dentistry, and the lab-driven precision of implantology. He has been instrumental in bringing state-of-the-art implant techniques to Arvada Dental Center & Implants, serving patients throughout Arvada and the greater Denver area with advanced, comprehensive treatment solutions.
‍
To better serve anxious patients, Dr. Bennett is [Moderate IV Sedation](/iv-sedation-dentistry) Certified, a credential that requires ACLS certification and ongoing advanced training. This allows him to provide a comfortable, anxiety-free experience for patients who need complex surgical or implant procedures.

### Expert Dental Implants & Full Smile Restorations

From single tooth implants to life-changing full-arch restorations like All-on-4, our experienced team led by Dr. Gregory Bennett is dedicated to delivering natural-looking, long-lasting results. Reclaim your confident smile, enjoy your favorite foods again, and improve your quality of life with advanced dental implant solutions in Arvada.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/694763dce59d9bc4ff602eab_Arvada%20dental_Procedures_All-on-4_dark-icon.svg)

#### All-on-4 Dental Implants

Regain a complete, functional smile with the revolutionary All-on-4 dental implant system. Dr. Bennett strategically places just four dental implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth, providing a permanent solution that restores your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. This innovative technique often eliminates the need for bone grafting and allows you to leave with a temporary set of teeth the same day.

[Learn More](/all-on-4-dental-implants)

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/694763dc10573e660af2218b_Arvada%20dental_Procedures_Dent-Implants_Dark.svg)

#### Dental Implants

Stop letting missing teeth hold you back. Dental implants provide a secure, comfortable alternative to traditional tooth replacement that won't slip or click. Dr. Bennett's precise placement ensures optimal function and aesthetics, giving you the freedom to eat your favorite foods and smile confidently.

[Learn More](/dental-implants)

#### Same-Day Crowns (CEREC)

CEREC technology allows us to design, craft, and place custom dental crowns in just one visit. Using advanced digital scanning and in-office milling, we eliminate messy impressions, temporary crowns, and multiple appointments so you can restore your tooth with precision, comfort, and convenience the very same day.

#### Bone Grafting

Don't let bone loss prevent you from getting dental implants. Bone grafting restores your jaw's strength and volume, making implant placement possible even after years of missing teeth. Dr. Bennett's expertise ensures proper healing and integration, setting the stage for a successful, lasting implant restoration.

[Learn More](/bone-grafting)

#### IV Sedation

Don't let dental anxiety prevent you from receiving the care you deserve. IV sedation provides deep relaxation and comfort during complex procedures, allowing you to undergo treatments like implant placement or oral surgery without fear or discomfort. Our experienced team monitors you throughout the entire process, ensuring your safety while you rest peacefully in a calm, stress-free state that makes even lengthy procedures feel like minutes.
