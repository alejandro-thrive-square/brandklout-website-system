### What Is All-On-4?

All-On-4 is a full-arch implant technique that uses four strategically placed implants to support a complete set of replacement teeth. Two implants are placed vertically at the front of the jaw and two are angled at the back to take full advantage of the bone that is available.

The angled rear implants are what make All-On-4 uniquely efficient. They maximize bone contact without requiring additional grafting in most cases and create enough immediate stability to support a temporary restoration the same day as surgery. The result is a permanently fixed, full-arch smile that functions exactly like natural teeth.

### Am I a Candidate for All-On-4?

Good candidates are patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both arches, or those with multiple failing teeth who are ready for a fixed permanent solution. Most patients with moderate bone loss qualify without grafting thanks to the angled implant design. Patients currently wearing dentures who want a more stable, permanent option are often ideal candidates.

Patients with severe upper jaw bone loss may be better suited for zygomatic implants, which anchor into the cheekbone and eliminate the need for grafting entirely. Active gum disease or infections must be resolved before treatment begins. Heavy smokers face a significantly higher risk of implant failure and are strongly encouraged to quit prior to surgery. A 3D imaging consultation is the only reliable way to confirm your specific candidacy.

### Healing and New Smile Installation

During the healing period your temporary restoration keeps you functional and comfortable. Once your implants have fully fused with the bone, your permanent custom restoration is fabricated and secured in place. Dr. Bennett personally oversees the final fitting for fit, bite, and aesthetics.

### Recovery and Aftercare

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c4ab8f20c5a5feb6f9cc1_all%20on%204%20recovery.webp)

The first few days after surgery typically involve mild swelling, soreness, and fatigue. Prescribed antibiotics and pain medication manage discomfort effectively for most patients. A soft diet protects the implant sites during early healing and is required for several months during osseointegration.

Most patients return to light daily activity within a week. Long-term care is straightforward: brush twice daily, use a water flosser around the implants, and visit Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months for routine checkups. Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol as both impair healing and long-term implant stability.
