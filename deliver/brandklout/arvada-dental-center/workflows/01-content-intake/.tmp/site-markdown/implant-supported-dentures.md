### The Benefits of Implant Supported Dentures

Implant supported dentures sit at the intersection of accessibility and function. They offer meaningful improvements over traditional dentures at a price point that is more achievable than a fully fixed restoration.

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **Snaps Securely Onto Implants:** Locator attachments connect the denture to your implants for a stable, secure fit throughout the day. No adhesive needed.
- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **Significantly More Stable:** Implant support dramatically reduces the movement and slipping that makes traditional dentures frustrating during meals and conversation.

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **Preserves More Bone:** Because implants stimulate the jawbone, implant supported dentures slow the bone loss that continues with a conventional removable denture.
- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **Removable for Easy Cleaning:** Unlike a fixed bridge, implant supported dentures can be removed at night for thorough cleaning of both the appliance and your gums.

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **An Upgrade Path:** Many patients start here and later transition to a fully fixed restoration as their needs or budget change. The implants placed now can support that future upgrade.
- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **More Affordable Than Fixed Options:** Implant supported dentures deliver substantially better results than traditional dentures at a fraction of the cost of a full fixed restoration.

For patients who want more than a removable denture but are not yet ready for a fully fixed restoration, implant supported dentures are often the right next step.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c4391a952e72cbca85dbd_zygomatic%20implant%20patient.webp)

### Am I a Candidate for Implant Supported Dentures?

Good candidates are patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both arches and want more stability than a traditional denture provides. You should be in reasonably good health and have adequate bone density to support implant placement. Patients who have worn dentures for years and are frustrated with slipping or discomfort are often ideal candidates.

All-On-4 is designed for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both arches, or patients with multiple failing teeth who are ready for a permanent, fixed solution. Most patients with moderate bone loss qualify without bone grafting due to the angled implant placement.

### Your Smile Steps to Full Mouth Dental Implants

1

### Consultation and Planning

At your consultation, Dr. Bennett reviews your oral health, takes 3D CBCT imaging, and maps out a precise surgical plan. We assess your bone density, bite, and overall health to confirm All-On-4 is the right fit and determine whether any preparatory work is needed. You will leave with a full understanding of the timeline, process, and cost before committing to anything.

2

### Advanced Dental Implant Placement

We use cone beam CT imaging to achieve surgical accuracy that protects your anatomy and maximizes implant stability. On the day of surgery, your existing failing teeth are extracted if needed, and your four implants are placed in a single procedure under sedation. Temporary teeth may be attached the same day.

3

### Healing and New Smile Installation

Once your implants have fully integrated with the bone, your final restoration is custom-fabricated and secured in place. Dr. Bennett personally oversees the fitting to ensure your bite, aesthetics, and comfort are exactly right. This is the moment most patients say they cannot believe how natural it feels.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c43d50fb322270ce6078c_zygomatic%20implant%20result.webp)

### Recovery and Aftercare

Following implant placement, expect mild swelling and soreness for the first few days. Prescribed medication manages discomfort and a soft diet is required during the initial healing period. Most patients return to normal activity within a week. Once your implants have integrated and your denture is fitted, maintenance is straightforward: remove and clean the denture nightly, brush your gums and any remaining natural teeth, and visit Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months for checkups.

### FAQs

Have questions about implant supported dentures in Arvada, Colorado? Here are answers to our most common patient questions.

[contact us](/contact)

How much do implant supported dentures cost in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Implant supported dentures at Arvada Dental Center & Implants typically range from $6,000 to $15,000 per arch depending on the number of implants needed and the type of denture. We provide a full cost breakdown at your consultation. Financing is available through CareCredit, Cherry, Sunbit, PatientFi, and others.

How are implant supported dentures different from traditional?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Traditional dentures rest on the gums and rely on suction or adhesive for retention. Implant supported dentures snap onto dental implants, providing a much more stable and secure fit. They reduce slipping, improve chewing power, and slow down jawbone loss.

Can implant supported dentures be removed?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Yes. Unlike fixed restorations, implant supported dentures are removable for cleaning. They snap onto the implants securely during the day and are removed at night for thorough cleaning.

How many implants do I need for implant supported dentures?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Most implant supported dentures use two to four implants per arch depending on the design and your bone structure. Our team determines the right number at your consultation based on your 3D imaging and treatment goals.

Is bone grafting required for implant supported dentures?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Not always. Patients with adequate bone density can often proceed directly to implant placement. Those with moderate bone loss may need a bone graft first. A 3D CBCT scan at your consultation gives us a clear picture of your bone volume.

Does insurance cover implant supported dentures in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Coverage varies by plan. Some insurance plans offer partial benefits for implant procedures. Our team reviews your policy before treatment begins and helps you maximize your benefits. Multiple financing options are available for out-of-pocket costs.
