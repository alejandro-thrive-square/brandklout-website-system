### Complete Dental Services Near You

As your Arvada family dentist, we offer a full range of dental services for all ages. Our approach includes preventive care, cosmetic dentistry, and restorative treatments designed to provide the best dental care for you and your loved ones in a comfortable and welcoming setting. Contact us today at (303) 421-7611.

#### General Dentistry to Maintain Your Oral Health

Maintaining your oral health starts with consistent, high-quality general care. Our services include routine checkups and cleanings to prevent dental issues, fillings and restorations to repair cavities and damaged teeth, root canal therapy to save infected teeth, and sealants and fluoride treatments to protect against decay. Prevention is always better than treatment.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a47415da0e2d0f07f3a493_dental-implants-in-arvada-co-dental-implants-in-denver-dentist.png)

#### Cosmetic Dentistry to Enhance Your Smile

Enhance the appearance of your smile with our personalized cosmetic options. We offer professional teeth whitening to brighten your smile, custom veneers to correct chips, gaps, and discoloration, bonding to repair chipped or cracked teeth quickly, and Invisalign clear aligners for discreet and comfortable teeth straightening.

#### Dental Implants to Restore Your Smile

Restore your smile and full functionality with our dental implant solutions. We offer single tooth implants to replace individual missing teeth, implant-supported bridges and dentures for stability and comfort, All-On-4 full-arch restorations for a permanent secure fit, and zygomatic implants for patients with advanced bone loss.

#### Emergency Dentistry When You Need It Most

Dental emergencies can happen anytime and quick action is essential. Our team provides same-day appointments for urgent cases, immediate pain management solutions, expert care for dental injuries including knocked-out teeth and fractures, and fast intervention to prevent infections and further complications. Call (303) 421-7611 right away if you need emergency care.

### FAQs

How much do dental implants cost in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) The cost depends on the type of implant and the complexity of your case. Single tooth implants, All-On-4, zirconia fixed bridges, and zygomatic implants each have different starting prices. We offer a free consultation with transparent upfront pricing and no hidden fees.

Does Arvada Dental Center & Implants accept dental insurance?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Yes. We work with most major dental insurance providers and submit claims electronically on your behalf. Our team reviews your benefits before treatment begins so you know exactly what is covered and what your out-of-pocket costs will be.

What financing options are available at Arvada Dental Center & Implants?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) We offer financing through CareCredit, Cherry Payment Plans, Sunbit Finance, PatientFi, Proceed Finance, and LendingClub. Most patients receive a same-day approval decision and applications can be completed online from home, by phone, or in the office.

What should I do in a dental emergency in Arvada?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Call us immediately at (303) 421-7611. We prioritize emergency cases and offer same-day appointments for urgent issues including severe tooth pain, broken teeth, knocked-out teeth, and infections. Do not wait as prompt care prevents further complications.

What is the difference between All-On-4 implants and traditional dentures?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) All-On-4 implants are permanently fixed to the jawbone using four titanium posts and do not slip or require adhesives. Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can shift over time. All-On-4 also helps preserve jawbone density, which dentures do not.
