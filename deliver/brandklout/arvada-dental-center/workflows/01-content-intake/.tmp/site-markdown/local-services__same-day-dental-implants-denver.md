### What Exactly Is Teeth in a Day and How Does It Work?

Teeth in a Day, also called immediate loading or same day implants, is a technique in which dental implants are placed and a temporary full-arch restoration is attached in a single visit. Rather than waiting months for the implants to integrate before receiving teeth, patients leave the same appointment with a complete, functional smile.

The process is made possible by advanced 3D planning that allows Dr. Bennett to determine the ideal implant positions before surgery begins. When implants achieve sufficient stability at placement, a temporary prosthetic can be securely loaded the same day. Once osseointegration is complete, the permanent restoration replaces the temporary.

### Our Same Day Dental Implant Process

1

### Consultation and 3D Planning

Your journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation and cone beam 3D CT imaging. Dr. Bennett uses this data to map your bone density, assess implant positioning, and determine whether your anatomy supports same day loading. Denver patients leave this visit with a clear plan before any commitment is made.

2

### Implant Placement

On surgery day, implants are placed under local anesthesia with sedation available. Any failing teeth are extracted at the same appointment if needed. Each implant is positioned for maximum stability using the surgical plan developed from your 3D scan.

3

### Immediate Restoration

Once implants are placed and stability is confirmed, your temporary full-arch restoration is attached the same day. You leave our Arvada office with a complete, functional smile and make the drive back to Denver with teeth.

4

### Healing and Osseointegration

Over the following months, your implants fuse with the jawbone. Follow-up visits at our Arvada office monitor your healing progress and make any needed adjustments to the temporary restoration.

5

### Final Restoration Placement

Once osseointegration is complete, your permanent custom restoration is fabricated and permanently secured to your implants. Dr. Bennett oversees the final fitting for bite, aesthetics, and comfort.

### Am I a Candidate for Same Day Dental Implants?

Good candidates are patients with adequate bone density to support implants that achieve high initial stability, which is the key requirement for same day loading. Most patients in good overall health who have failing or missing teeth in one or both arches are worth evaluating. Patients currently wearing dentures who want to move to a fixed, permanent solution are among the most common candidates we see from the

Patients with active gum disease or oral infections must have those conditions treated before implant placement. Heavy smokers face a higher risk of implant failure and are strongly encouraged to quit before pursuing any implant treatment. Severe bone loss may prevent immediate loading and require a staged approach or consideration of zygomatic implants. A 3D imaging consultation at our Arvada office is the most accurate way to confirm candidacy.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a303aabc19ea6f57dcc0a9_AdobeStock_1688264765.webp)

### Recovery and Aftercare

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/693c51e685b19f0d0c149d6d_markis%20cosmetic%20service.webp)

Expect mild swelling, soreness, and fatigue for the first several days following same day implant surgery. Prescribed antibiotics and pain medication manage discomfort effectively for most patients. A soft diet is required during the healing period to protect the implant sites and the temporary restoration.

Most patients return to light daily activity within a week. Avoid smoking and alcohol during healing as both impair osseointegration. Once your permanent restoration is placed, long-term maintenance is simple: brush twice daily, use a water flosser, and visit Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months for routine checkups.

Are same day dental implants as strong as traditional implants?

Once the bone has fully integrated, the permanent restoration is placed and the long-term outcome is equivalent.

Is everyone a candidate for same day dental implants?
