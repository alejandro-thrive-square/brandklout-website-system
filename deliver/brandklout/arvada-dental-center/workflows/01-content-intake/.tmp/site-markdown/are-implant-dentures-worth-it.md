## Considering Implant Dentures? Here’s What You Need to Know

October 3, 2024

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/693fabbfc06349439373746c_f8c8cf4cac6b30154b4e08e571738b71_Dr.%20Gregory%20Bennett.png)

[Medically Reviewed by Dr. Gregory Bennett](/dr-gregory-bennett)

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a4b9b94463d68671bc519d_are-implant-dentures-worth-it.png.webp)

If you’ve been exploring options to replace missing teeth, implant dentures might have caught your attention. But what exactly are they, and why are they becoming such a popular choice? Let’s dive into what implant dentures are, why they’re significant, and whether they’re truly worth the investment.

### What Are Implant Dentures?

[Implant dentures](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/implant-supported-dentures/) are a type of denture that is securely anchored to your jaw using dental implants. Unlike traditional dentures that rest on the gums and can shift around, implant dentures are fixed in place by implants that are surgically inserted into your jawbone. This creates a more stable and permanent solution for tooth replacement.

The process involves placing metal posts (implants) into the jawbone, which act like tooth roots. These posts fuse with the bone over time, offering a sturdy foundation for the dentures to attach to.

### Why Are They Significant?

The main reason implant dentures stand out is their ability to combine the best of both worlds: the convenience of dentures with the stability of [dental implants](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/dental-implants-vs-dentures/). Traditional dentures have been the go-to solution for years, but they come with limitations like discomfort, gum irritation, and difficulty eating certain foods. Implant dentures address these issues by providing a secure, natural-feeling alternative.

By stimulating the jawbone, implant dentures also [help prevent bone loss](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/the-connection-between-dentures-and-bone-loss/), which can occur over time with traditional dentures. This helps maintain the structure of your face and keeps your oral health in check.

### So, Are They Worth It?

Now that you know what implant dentures are and why they matter, let’s talk about whether they’re worth it for you.

#### Stability and Comfort

One of the biggest advantages of implant dentures is the stability they offer. Unlike traditional dentures that can slip or shift, implant dentures stay firmly in place because they’re attached to your jawbone. This makes daily activities like eating and speaking much more comfortable. You won’t have to worry about dentures moving around or causing discomfort throughout the day.

#### Better for Your Oral Health

Implant dentures do more than just replace missing teeth—they also contribute to better long-term oral health. The implants stimulate your jawbone, which helps prevent bone loss. This is crucial because bone loss can alter the shape of your face and lead to other oral health issues. Traditional dentures don’t offer this benefit, which makes implant dentures a smarter choice for many people concerned about their future oral health.

#### Long-Term Durability

Implant dentures are built to last. With proper care, they can last for decades, while traditional dentures may require adjustments, repairs, or even replacements over time. The initial cost of implant dentures might be higher, but their long-lasting nature can save you money on future maintenance. Many people find that this long-term value outweighs the upfront investment.

#### A Natural Look

Implant dentures are custom-made to fit your mouth, which gives them a more natural appearance compared to traditional dentures. This means you can smile, eat, and speak confidently, knowing your dentures look and feel like real teeth. For many, this boost in confidence is one of the most significant benefits of choosing implant dentures.

#### Enjoy Eating Again

One of the major frustrations with traditional dentures is the limitation on what you can eat. Hard or sticky foods can be difficult to chew. With implant dentures, you can enjoy a wider variety of foods without worry. This makes mealtimes more enjoyable and helps you maintain a balanced diet.

### Is the Investment Worth It?

While implant dentures involve a higher upfront cost than traditional dentures, their durability, comfort, and health benefits make them a worthwhile investment for many. You’ll likely spend less on repairs or replacements over time, and the improved quality of life is something many find invaluable. After reading this comprehensive guide, you might get to decide on if implant dentures are worth it & [right for you](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/are-dentures-right-for-you-heres-what-you-need-to-know/).

If you’re ready to explore whether implant dentures are right for you, [give us a call today](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/survey.php). We’re happy to walk you through your options and help you make the best decision for your smile and your lifestyle.
