### What Is LAPIP Treatment?

LAPIP is a specialized laser treatment that saves failing dental implants by eliminating infection and promoting bone regeneration around the implant site.

Unlike traditional surgery requiring cutting and stitches, LAPIP uses the PerioLase laser to target bacteria while preserving healthy tissue.

### LAPIP Treatment Benefits

LAPIP offers a non-invasive option to save your implant and avoid the time and expense of implant removal, bone grafting, and replacement.

### The Best Candidates For LAPIP®

You may be a candidate if you have a failing dental implant with signs of infection but still have adequate bone support. Early intervention dramatically improves success rates.

Symptoms that indicate you need LAPIP include bleeding or pus around the implant, swelling, pain, loosening, or bone loss detected on X-rays. Dr. Bennett will evaluate your implant to determine if LAPIP can save it.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c8e8203d9fd528d0608a4_failing%20dental%20implant.webp)

### Reasons For Dental Implant Failure

Dental implants fail due to bacterial infections such as peri-implantitis, poor oral hygiene, bone loss, tobacco use, or improper placement. Common symptoms include swelling, pain, pus, or bleeding around the implant site.

- Bacterial infection
- Medication side effects
- Poor oral hygiene
- Underlying health conditions
- Tobacco or alcohol use
- Trauma during surgery
- Grinding or clenching
- Gum recession
- Poor bone quality
- Implant movement

Early detection and immediate intervention with LAPIP offers the best opportunity to reverse damage and save your implant.

### What to Expect: The LAPIP Process

1

### Evaluation

Dr. Bennett examines your implant and takes cone beam CT imaging to assess bone loss and infection severity. He determines if LAPIP can save your implant.

2

### Laser Treatment

Under local anesthesia, the PerioLase laser is directed into the peri-implant pocket to eliminate bacteria and diseased tissue. The laser stimulates bone regeneration and natural healing.

3

### Follow-Up Care

We monitor healing at regular follow-up appointments. Most patients see improvement within weeks as inflammation decreases and the implant stabilizes.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c8aefa13d3fae2447792c_recovery%20laser%20dentistry.webp)

### Recovery After LAPIP

Most patients return to normal activities within 24 hours. Maintain excellent oral hygiene with gentle brushing and flossing. Avoid hard or crunchy foods for the first week. Recovery is much more comfortable than traditional implant surgery.
