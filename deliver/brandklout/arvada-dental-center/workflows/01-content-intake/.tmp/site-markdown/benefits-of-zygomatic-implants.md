## Benefits of Zygomatic Implants: A Reliable Solution for Complex Cases

October 3, 2024

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/693fabbfc06349439373746c_f8c8cf4cac6b30154b4e08e571738b71_Dr.%20Gregory%20Bennett.png)

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a47afaeb7c11052b089048_benefits-of-zygomatic-implants.webp)

If you’ve been told you’re not a candidate for traditional dental implants due to significant bone loss in the upper jaw, zygomatic implants might be the solution you’re looking for. Unlike conventional implants, zygomatic implants offer a unique way to restore your smile and confidence without requiring extensive bone grafting. Let’s dive into the key benefits of zygomatic implants, this innovative option, and how it can change your dental health.

#### 1. A Lifesaver for Those with Severe Bone Loss

One of the biggest challenges people face when considering [dental implants](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/the-benefits-of-dental-implants/) is having enough healthy bones to support them. Over time, factors like aging, tooth loss, and gum disease can lead to bone deterioration, especially in the upper jaw. Zygomatic implants provide a solution for patients with severe bone loss by anchoring into the zygomatic bone, or cheekbone, which is much denser than the jawbone.

This makes the procedure ideal for[patients who don’t have sufficient bone in their upper jaw](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/are-zygomatic-implants-the-solution-for-patients-with-insufficient-jawbone/) for traditional implants. Rather than undergoing additional surgeries like bone grafting, you can rely on zygomatic implants to offer the support you need for a stable, long-lasting dental prosthesis.

#### 2. Skip the Bone Grafts – Faster, More Convenient Treatment

Traditional dental implants often take several months to complete, especially when [bone grafts](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/21727-dental-bone-graft) are needed. Bone grafting extends the healing time before implant placement, making the treatment longer than most patients prefer.

With zygomatic implants, you can skip this step entirely. Since the implants anchor directly into the cheekbone, there’s no need for bone grafts, saving you both time and extra surgical procedures. Patients can often receive a fixed prosthesis immediately after the implants are placed, dramatically reducing the total treatment time. This quick turnaround is a significant advantage for those eager to restore their smile as soon as possible.

#### 3. Long-Lasting Stability and Durability

One of the most common concerns with dental restorations is how long they will last. Patients want a solution that feels secure and durable. Zygomatic implants are known for their exceptional stability because they’re anchored in the dense cheekbone, offering a firmer foundation than implants placed in the jawbone, which may be weakened by bone loss.

This stronger anchor provides long-lasting durability, meaning you can rely on your new set of teeth for years to come. You won’t have to worry about discomfort or instability, whether eating, talking, or smiling. For many patients, this durability translates into a significant improvement in their overall quality of life.

#### 4. Ideal for Patients Who Have Been Turned Down for Traditional Implants

Have you been told that you’re not a good candidate for traditional dental implants because of insufficient bone in your upper jaw? This can be discouraging, but [zygomatic implants](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/zygomatic-implants/) can offer hope. Because they use the cheekbone as an anchor point, even those with significant bone loss can still be eligible for this procedure.

Whether your bone loss is due to aging, health conditions, or extended periods of tooth loss, zygomatic implants provide a reliable option. This makes them a game-changer for patients who may have thought dental implants weren’t impossible.

#### 5. Restore Your Natural Smile – Aesthetic Benefits

Beyond functionality, zygomatic implants offer remarkable aesthetic benefits. Missing teeth or ill-fitting dentures can make you self-conscious about your smile, but with zygomatic implants, you can regain a beautiful, natural-looking smile.

These implants support a full arch of teeth, significantly improving your facial structure and giving you a more youthful appearance. With your new teeth securely in place, you’ll no longer have to worry about the embarrassment of slipping dentures or gaps in your smile. Instead, you can enjoy the confidence of knowing your smile looks and feels just right.

#### 6. Enjoy a Swift Recovery Process

Surgery often comes with concerns about recovery time. However, most patients who undergo zygomatic implant surgery report a smooth, relatively quick recovery. Since this procedure eliminates the need for additional surgeries like bone grafting, the healing time is generally shorter.

While every patient differs, many can resume normal activities soon after the procedure with minimal discomfort. This makes zygomatic implants a convenient option for those who want effective results without the lengthy downtime often associated with traditional implants.

#### 7. Regain Functionality and Comfort

Eating your favorite foods, speaking confidently, and living without discomfort are all major goals when undergoing dental restoration. Zygomatic implants offer improved functionality, allowing you to enjoy a wide range of foods without the limitations imposed by dentures or missing teeth.

Thanks to the strong foundation provided by zygomatic implants, you won’t experience the typical issues of slippage or discomfort that come with traditional dentures. The implants provide a secure, stable solution that mimics the feel of natural teeth, allowing you to eat and speak confidently.

After learning the benefits of zygomatic implants, if you’re ready to explore how they can transform your dental health and bring back your smile, [call us today](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/survey.php) to learn more about this life-changing option.
