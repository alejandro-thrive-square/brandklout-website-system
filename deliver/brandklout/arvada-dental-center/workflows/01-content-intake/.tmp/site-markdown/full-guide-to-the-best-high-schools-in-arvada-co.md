### Top-Rated High Schools in Arvada, Colorado

For families looking to move to the [Arvada](https://www.arvadaco.gov/) area or for current residents preparing for their children to start high school, finding the right fit is crucial. Arvada is part of the Jeffco Public Schools district, which is one of the largest school districts in Colorado with several excellent high school options to choose from. Here’s an in-depth guide to some of the highest rated high schools in Arvada.

### Ralston Valley High School

Consistently ranked among Colorado’s top high schools, Ralston Valley is a standout choice for families seeking excellence in education. Boasting an impressive graduation rate of 95%, it consistently surpasses state averages in academic achievement. Ralston Valley distinguishes itself with an extensive array of AP and honors courses, enabling students to earn college credits early. The school is also renowned for its award-winning music and arts programs, reflecting a commitment to holistic development. Sports are another highlight, with numerous state championship titles earned by both boys’ and girls’ athletic teams.

### Arvada West High School

[Arvada West High School](http://arvadawest.jeffcopublicschools.org/) boasts a remarkable graduation rate of 93% and excels in academic performance, scoring well above average in math, reading, and science tests. Known for its comprehensive offerings, Arvada West stands out as a school with top-tier academics, robust arts programs, competitive sports teams, and a wide array of engaging electives and clubs. What sets Arvada West apart is its diverse curriculum, including unique options like aviation, business and marketing, and construction programs. Additionally, as an International Baccalaureate (IB) World School, Arvada West provides ambitious students with a challenging global education curriculum.

### Pomona High School

Pomona High School stands as an award-winning institution celebrated not only for its strong academic performance but also for its extensive extracurricular offerings and athletics. With a college continuation rate exceeding 90%, Pomona provides students with opportunities to excel through its International Baccalaureate (IB) program and specialized tracks in business, STEM courses, and the arts/music. The school prides itself on a diverse array of activities, including nationally-ranked speech & debate, theater, music, and robotics, complemented by over 80 clubs catering to various interests. Known for its welcoming and spirited environment, Pomona also boasts competitive sports teams across a range of disciplines.

### Arvada High School

The original high school in this area, [Arvada High](https://arvadahs.jeffcopublicschools.org/our_school/history) has been educating students since 1888. While it doesn’t always rank as highly as some other area high schools, it offers smaller class sizes and a close-knit community feel. Students can get individual attention while still participating in honors and AP courses, college prep programs, clubs, arts, and athletics. Arvada High provides a quality education along with ample opportunities for involvement.

### Warren Tech High School

For students seeking technical training or career-focused education, Warren Tech offers a wide array of programs. Areas of focus include construction, manufacturing, automotive, commercial arts, health sciences, and more. Students can earn certifications and college credits while gaining hands-on vocational experience. While not a traditional high school atmosphere, Warren Tech provides exceptional career prep along with core academics. Many graduates move directly into well-paying technical careers.

There are certainly many other quality high school options in the broader Denver metro area and Jeffco district, but these exemplary schools in [Arvada](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/) itself allow students to receive an exceptional education while being part of an engaged and thriving local community. The variety of academic focuses, extracurricular opportunities, and overall cultures across these leading high schools ensures families can find the ideal fit for their children’s interests and goals. Learn more about [sports and rec around](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/sports-and-rec-around-arvada-co/).
