### Dental Implants

Missing teeth affect more than your smile. Dental implants replace lost teeth from the root up, restoring full function, preventing bone loss, and delivering a result that looks and feels completely natural. Built to last a lifetime.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c47e14f7faa37047afdcd_all%20on%204.webp)

[Learn More](/all-on-4-dental-implants)

### All-On-4 Dental Implants

Replace an entire arch of failing or missing teeth with just four implants. Most patients leave with a full set of functioning teeth the same day as surgery. No bone grafting required in most cases. No more dentures.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/693c51e685b19f0d0c149e37_Implant%20Supported%20Dentures%20-%20Orlando%2C%20FL.webp)

[Learn More](/implant-supported-dentures)

### Implant Supported Dentures

Traditional dentures shift and slip. Implant supported dentures snap securely onto implants, giving you the stability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. All the convenience of a removable appliance, without the frustration.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69972950737f6fb4ba3ce517_dental%20fixed%20bridge%20at%20aravada.webp)

[Learn More](/zirconia-fixed-bridge)

### Zirconia Fixed Bridge

A permanently fixed, full-arch restoration made from high-strength zirconia ceramic. Secured to implants, it never comes out, never slips, and never needs adhesive. The closest thing to having your natural teeth back.

### Your Arvada Implant Dentistry Experts

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a4740554a815a4d5068969_dental-implants-in-arvada-co.png)

Dr. Bennett and our team have placed hundreds of implants for patients throughout the Arvada and Denver area. We use advanced cone beam CT imaging and digital surgical planning to ensure every implant is positioned with precision for optimal function, aesthetics, and long-term success. From single tooth replacements to full arch restorations, we handle every stage of your implant journey in-house, saving you time and unnecessary referrals. Our goal is to give you a result that looks, feels, and functions exactly like your natural teeth.

### THE BEST IMPLANT DENTISTRY IN ARVADA

Permanent, Comfortable, and Built to Last

Missing teeth affect far more than your appearance. They impact how you eat, speak, and feel about yourself every single day. Unlike dentures or bridges, dental implants replace the tooth root itself, preserving your jawbone and providing a stable, permanent foundation. We offer a full range of implant solutions so every patient can find the right fit for their needs and budget.
