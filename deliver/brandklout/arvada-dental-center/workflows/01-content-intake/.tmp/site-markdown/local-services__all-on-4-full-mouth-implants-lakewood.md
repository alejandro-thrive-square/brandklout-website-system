### What Are All-On-4 Full Mouth Implants?

All-On-4 is an advanced full-arch restoration that uses four dental implants to permanently support a complete set of fixed teeth. Two implants are placed vertically at the front of the jaw and two are angled toward the back. This strategic placement maximizes bone contact and stability without requiring bone grafting in most cases.

The result is a fixed, non-removable set of teeth that looks and functions like a natural smile. Unlike traditional dentures, All-On-4 restorations do not shift, require adhesive, or need to be removed at night. For Lakewood patients who have lost most or all of their teeth, this approach offers a permanent path forward with fewer implants and a faster recovery than full individual implant placement.

### Am I a Candidate for All-On-4?

All-On-4 is designed for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both arches and want a permanent, fixed replacement. Ideal candidates have adequate bone density to anchor the four implants, though the angled placement technique is specifically designed to work with reduced bone volume. Many patients who would not qualify for traditional full implant placement are still strong candidates for All-On-4.

Good overall health is important as the procedure involves oral surgery. Patients with uncontrolled diabetes, active oral infections, or heavy smoking habits face higher risks and will need to address those factors before treatment can begin. A consultation with 3D cone beam imaging at our Arvada office is the most accurate way to confirm candidacy and determine the right approach for your specific anatomy. We welcome Lakewood patients for that first step with no commitment required.

### What to Expect: The All-On-4 Process

1

### Consultation and 3D Imaging

Your journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation and 3D CBCT imaging at our Arvada office. We assess your bone structure, bite, and overall oral health and build a fully customized surgical plan. Lakewood patients leave this visit with a clear picture of the timeline, process, and total cost.

2

### Implant Placement

Surgery is performed under IV sedation or local anesthesia for your comfort. Any remaining failing teeth are extracted at the same appointment. Four implants are placed at precise angles to maximize stability within your available bone.

3

### Same-Day Temporary Restoration

In most cases, a temporary full-arch restoration is attached the same day as surgery. Lakewood patients drive home from our Arvada office with a complete, functional smile and do not go through healing without teeth.

4

### Healing and Osseointegration

Over the following months, your implants fuse with the jawbone through osseointegration. We schedule follow-up visits to monitor healing, confirm integration progress, and make any adjustments needed to your temporary restoration.

5

### Final Restoration

Once your implants have fully integrated, your permanent full-arch restoration is custom-fabricated and secured. The final result is a durable, natural-looking smile built to last for decades.

### Recovery and Aftercare

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c4ab8f20c5a5feb6f9cc1_all%20on%204%20recovery.webp)

Expect swelling, soreness, and fatigue for the first several days following All-On-4 surgery. Prescribed antibiotics and pain medication are standard and manage discomfort effectively for most patients. A soft diet is required for the osseointegration period, typically several months, to protect the healing implants.

Most patients return to light daily activity within a week. Long-term maintenance is straightforward: brush twice daily, clean under the bridge with a water flosser or interdental brush, and schedule routine checkups at Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months. With proper care, All-On-4 restorations are built to last a lifetime.
