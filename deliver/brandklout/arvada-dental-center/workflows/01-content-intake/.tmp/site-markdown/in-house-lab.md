## In-House Lab

in Arvada, CO

Better technology means more accurate diagnoses, safer procedures, and better results. Our in-house lab takes it one step further by keeping your restorations under our direct control from start to finish.

[Request Appointment](https://www.patientsreach.com/schedule/Arvada-Dental-Center/patient_types/)

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a635926ffeb565c9cb382b_in%20house%20lab.webp)

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/6945fdb44db8d7e81c5b1f00_arvada%20logo2.png)

### You Deserve a Confident Smile.

Full Arch. Four Implants. One Life-Changing Result.

At Arvada Dental Center & Implants, every tool we use has been chosen because it makes a measurable difference in your care. From the 3D imaging that guides your surgical plan to the same-day crown technology that eliminates a second appointment, our technology investments are focused on one thing: better outcomes for you.

What sets us apart from most practices is that our lab work is handled through our partner lab under Dr. Bennett's direct oversight. This means your restorations are not sent off to an unknown facility and returned weeks later. The quality, fit, and aesthetics of everything we place are controlled and accountable from the moment treatment begins.

### CEREC Same-Day Crowns

CEREC technology allows us to design, mill, and place a permanent porcelain crown in a single appointment. There is no temporary crown, no second visit, and no waiting one to two weeks for a lab to fabricate your restoration. For patients with busy schedules or dental emergencies, same-day crowns are a significant convenience.

- Full crown designed, fabricated, and placed in one visit
- No temporary crown required while you wait for a lab
- Custom-matched to the color and shape of your surrounding teeth
- Made from durable, high-quality ceramic material

### 3D Cone Beam CT Scanning

Three-dimensional cone beam CT imaging gives us a complete view of your jaw, bone density, nerve pathways, sinus boundaries, and tooth positions before any treatment begins. For implant cases, this scan is the foundation of every surgical plan we build. It replaces guesswork with precision and allows us to protect critical anatomy throughout your procedure.

- Captures high-resolution 3D images of the entire jaw in a single scan
- Measures bone volume and density for accurate implant planning
- Identifies nerve locations and sinus anatomy to protect surrounding structures
- Completed in minutes at our Arvada office with minimal radiation
- Reduces risk of complications by eliminating surgical estimation

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a635f3342282fe81a9cb67_in%20house%20lab%20tech.jpg)

### Panoramic Dental X-Rays

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699cb0bd9e67116062860863_panoramic%20x-ray.webp)

Digital panoramic X-rays replace traditional film with instant, high-resolution images that appear on screen within seconds. This advanced technology exposes patients to significantly less radiation than conventional film X-rays while allowing our doctors to zoom, enhance, and review images with you in real time during your appointment.

**Benefits of Digital Panoramic X-Rays:**

- Up to 90 percent less radiation compared to traditional film X-rays
- Instant on-screen images for immediate review with your doctor
- Enhanced image clarity for more accurate diagnosis
- Easy electronic storage and secure sharing between providers when needed
- More comfortable than traditional bite-wing X-rays for many patientsMore comfortable than traditional bite-wing X-rays for most patients
