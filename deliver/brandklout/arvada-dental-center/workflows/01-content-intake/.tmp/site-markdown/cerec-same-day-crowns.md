### What Are CEREC Same-Day Crowns?

CEREC (Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics) is an advanced in-office technology that designs and mills your permanent ceramic crown in a single visit. Traditional crowns require two appointments and weeks of waiting.
A digital scan replaces messy impression materials. Our design software creates a precise 3D model and the milling machine crafts your crown in under 30 minutes.

CEREC crowns are made from durable, tooth-colored ceramic that matches the shade and translucency of your natural teeth. They're strong enough to withstand normal chewing forces.
Because the process happens in one visit, you avoid the inconvenience of temporary crowns. You leave the same day with a permanent, fully functional restoration.

### What to Expect: The CEREC Crown Process

1

### Examination & Planning

We examine your tooth, take digital X-rays, and confirm a CEREC crown is the right solution. We discuss your options and answer any questions before proceeding.

2

### Tooth Preparation

We gently remove decay or damage and shape your tooth to receive the crown. Local anesthesia ensures you're completely comfortable throughout the process.

3

### Digital Scan

Instead of messy impression materials, we use a digital scanner to capture a precise 3D image of your prepared tooth and surrounding teeth in seconds.

4

### Crown Design

Our CEREC software uses your digital scan to design a perfectly fitted crown in minutes. We can adjust the shape, size, and color to match your natural teeth exactly.

5

### In-Office Milling

Your crown is milled from a solid block of high-quality dental ceramic right in our office while you wait. The entire milling process takes approximately 15-30 minutes.

6

### Placement & Polish

We bond your new crown to your tooth, make final adjustments for perfect bite alignment, and polish it to a natural finish. You leave with a permanent crown — all in one visit.

### Is a CEREC Same-Day Crown Right for Me?

You may be an ideal candidate for a CEREC crown if you have a cracked, broken, or severely decayed tooth that needs protection and restoration. CEREC works well for patients who want to avoid multiple appointments, are uncomfortable with traditional impression materials, or simply value the convenience of same-day treatment. It's also an excellent option for completing a root canal in a single extended visit.

In some cases, traditional crowns may be recommended — for example, if your tooth requires complex preparation or if you're having a crown placed on a dental implant. During your consultation, we'll evaluate your tooth and recommend the most appropriate solution. Our goal is always the most conservative, effective treatment that fits your lifestyle and budget.

### Recovery & Crown Care

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699b71695c2064cf1dc53c53_same%20day%20crowns%20results.webp)

Most patients experience little to no downtime after a CEREC crown. You may notice mild sensitivity to temperature for a few days, which resolves on its own. Avoid very hard or sticky foods for 24 hours while the bonding fully sets. Care for your crown just like a natural tooth — regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups. With proper care, your CEREC crown can last 15 years or more. We recommend a nightguard if you grind your teeth to protect your investment.
