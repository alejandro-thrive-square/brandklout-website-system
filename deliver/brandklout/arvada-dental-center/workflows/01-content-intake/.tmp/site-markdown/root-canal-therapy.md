### Lingering Tooth Pain?

The Problem With Your Toothache

A toothache is almost always a sign that infection has damaged the pulp within your tooth root. This can result from a severe crack, injury, or extreme decay that has reached the inside of the tooth. Without treatment, infection will continue causing pain, can lead to an abscess, and in worst cases requires tooth extraction. If you're experiencing severe toothache, we have a solution.

### What Is Root Canal Therapy?

Root canal therapy removes infected pulp from inside your tooth root to eliminate pain and save your natural tooth.

The procedure involves cleaning out infection, disinfecting the canal, filling it, and sealing the tooth to prevent future infection.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/6999f2d9b16aee63a2ad8a65_arvada%20dental%20root%20canal.webp)

### Am I a Candidate for Root Canal Therapy?

You may need a root canal if you have severe toothache, prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold, pain when biting, swollen gums, or a darkened tooth. These symptoms indicate infection has reached the tooth's pulp.

Dr. Bennett will examine your tooth and take X-rays to confirm infection and determine if root canal therapy can save the tooth. In some cases, extraction may be the only option.
