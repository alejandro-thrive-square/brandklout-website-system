WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

### Know what comes next before you begin.

Four short steps, so nothing about the process is a surprise.

01

#### We call you back

A member of the team calls Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm to confirm a time that works and answer anything you want to ask first.

02

#### Your urgent visit

You see Dr. Bennett or one of the doctors at 6695 Wadsworth Blvd. Bring anything relevant about your medical history and any insurance details.

03

#### A plan, in writing

You are told what is going on, what the options are, and what each would cost, before anything is scheduled.

04

#### You decide

Nothing is booked unless you want it booked. Monthly payment plans are available if you would rather spread the cost.
