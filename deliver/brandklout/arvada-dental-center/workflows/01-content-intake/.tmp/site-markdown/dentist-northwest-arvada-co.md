#### General Dentistry to Protect Your Oral Health

Our general dentistry services form the foundation of your oral health. We offer comprehensive checkups and professional cleanings, fillings and root canal therapy to repair damaged teeth, gum disease treatment to protect your gums and overall health, and preventive care including sealants and fluoride treatments. Regular visits to our Northwest Arvada office help maintain your smile and prevent more serious dental problems from developing.

#### Cosmetic Dentistry to Give You a Radiant, Confident Smile

Our cosmetic services are designed to enhance the appearance of your teeth with natural-looking, long-lasting results. We offer professional teeth whitening to remove stains and brighten your smile, porcelain veneers to correct discoloration, chips, and gaps, and dental bonding to quickly repair chipped or cracked teeth. Each treatment plan is tailored to your unique needs and goals.

#### Dental Implants for a Permanent, Natural-Looking Solution

Restore your smile with permanent, natural-looking dental implants. Whether you need a single tooth replacement or a full-mouth restoration, our advanced implant techniques deliver optimal results. We use high-quality materials and precise placement to create durable implants that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, improving your oral health, appearance, and confidence.

How much do dental implants cost in Northwest Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) The cost depends on the type of implant and the complexity of your case. Does Arvada Dental Center accept dental insurance for Northwest Arvada patients?

We work with most major dental insurance providers and submit claims electronically on your behalf. What financing options are available for dental treatment in Northwest Arvada?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) We offer financing through CareCredit, Cherry Payment Plans, Sunbit Finance, PatientFi, Proceed Finance, and LendingClub. Most patients receive a same-day approval decision and applications can be completed online from home, by phone, or in the office.

What should I do in a dental emergency in Northwest Arvada?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Call us immediately at (303) 421-7611. We offer same-day appointments for urgent issues including severe tooth pain, broken teeth, knocked-out teeth, lost fillings, and infections. Do not wait as prompt care prevents further complications.

What is the difference between All-On-4 implants and traditional dentures?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) All-On-4 implants are permanently fixed to the jawbone using four titanium posts and do not slip or require adhesives. Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can shift over time. All-On-4 also helps preserve jawbone density, which dentures do not.
