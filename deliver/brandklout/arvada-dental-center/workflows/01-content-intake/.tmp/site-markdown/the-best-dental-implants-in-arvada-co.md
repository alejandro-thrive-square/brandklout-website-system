### Individual dental implants

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a5b204723cf5a80a5d7848_procedure-single-dental-implant-black-background.webp)

When replacing one or two missing teeth, single dental implants represent an optimal and enduring alternative. Single dental implants, which are fabricated to complement the remainder of the mouth, are virtually identical to natural teeth. Additionally, the dental implant’s post stimulates the jawbone, thereby promoting bone growth.

### Implant Supported Dentures

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a5b2173712fd0c48d727eb_procedure-overdenture-black-background.webp)

Consider opting for implant-supported dentures if you’re seeking the convenience of removable dentures minus the need for adhesive. These full-mouth tooth replacements offer a more natural look and feel compared to traditional dentures. They attach securely to two or more dental implants that are strategically positioned in the mouth through a surgical procedure.

### The revolutionary All-On-4® dental implants in Denver

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a5b22b7a8c46c39ad623f2_procedure-full-arch-both-black-background.webp)

#### 1. What are dental implants?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a5b24e8e460527f61d896e_Denver-Dental-Implants-768x411.webp)

A dental implant comprises three distinct components: an abutment, a restoration, and a titanium or ceramic post. Dental implants offer stable foundational support and bone support, resembling that of natural tooth roots. They can be utilized to support a single dental crown, dental bridge, denture, or full arch prosthesis.

#### 2. Who Is Eligible To Receive Dental Implants?

Numerous patients are already qualified for dental implants, or can become so following preparatory procedures such as tooth extractions and bone grafting. The determination of candidacy is enhanced in precision subsequent to a thorough consultation and assessment conducted by an implant dentist.

#### 3. What is the cost of treatment?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a5b262b1b1abbe5b415c8a_cost-dental-implants-in-arvada-dentist-in-denver-dental-implants.webp)

[Numerous factors](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/your-full-guide-to-dental-implants-in-denver-dental-implants-in-arvada-co/) influence the price of dental implants, such as the quantity and duration of missing teeth, the materials utilized, and any additional procedures that may be required. To assist you in affording treatment, our staff will file insurance claims and assist you in applying for dental financing.

#### 4. What is the timeframe for treatment?

Treatment with dental implants may last anywhere from several months to over a year. Implants can frequently be surgically implanted immediately following the conclusion of the consultation and treatment plan. In the case of patients who require bone grafting, a separate healing period of several months may elapse prior to dental implant placement. Three months or longer may elapse following the placement of dental implants prior to optimal integration and the permanent attachment of the final restoration. Patients are frequently permitted to wear a temporary restoration while they heal.

#### 5. What is the anticipated lifespan of dental implants?

Dental implants have the potential to last for the remainder of your life, in addition to decades. In addition to personal health practices, surgical technology and clinical experience have a significant impact on the durability of dental implants. Choosing an [experienced implant team](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/meet-the-doctors/), such as the one at Arvada Dental Center & Implants, ensures the long-term success of your treatment through more precise and accurate surgery.
