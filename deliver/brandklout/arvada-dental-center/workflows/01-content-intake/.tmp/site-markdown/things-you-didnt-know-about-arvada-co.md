Nestled in the heart of the Denver metropolitan area, [Arvada, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvada,_Colorado), is a city brimming with surprises that often go unnoticed. Beyond its charming suburban landscape, this vibrant community harbors a wealth of unique features and intriguing facts that are sure to captivate both residents and visitors alike. Let’s delve into the lesser-known aspects of Arvada that will leave you with a newfound appreciation for this remarkable city.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a478cc21be492ebec6a8d8_Things-you-didnt-know-about-Arvada-CO.jpg.webp)

Image Source: [https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvada,_Colorado](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvada,_Colorado)

### A Pioneering Spirit in Early Aviation

Did you know that [Arvada](https://www.arvadaco.gov/) played a pivotal role in the history of aviation? In the early 20th century, the city was home to one of the first airfields in Colorado, known as the Arvada Aviation Field. This historic site witnessed the daring exploits of aviation pioneers who took to the skies, paving the way for the advancements in modern air travel we enjoy today.

### A Haven for Outdoor Enthusiasts

While Arvada is nestled within urban sprawl, it provides a remarkable retreat into nature. The city features an expansive network of over 125 miles of trails, ideal for hiking, biking, and immersing oneself in the great outdoors. Among these, the Ralston Creek Trail stands out, winding through verdant landscapes and offering breathtaking vistas of the Rocky Mountains.

### A Culinary Mecca

Arvada’s culinary scene offers a delightful array of dining options to suit every taste. From trendy farm-to-table eateries to cherished local institutions, the city’s food choices mirror its lively and dynamic community. Indulge in the famous “Smellyburger” at the iconic Arvada Tavern—a must-try experience that captures the essence of Arvada’s distinctive culinary charm.

### Historic Treasures

Arvada’s rich history is reflected in its well-preserved architectural gems. The [Olde Town Arvada](https://www.oldetownarvada.org/) district, with its charming boutiques, art galleries, and historic buildings, transports visitors back in time. Don’t miss the Arvada History Museum, housed in a former church, which offers a fascinating glimpse into the city’s past through its engaging exhibits and artifacts.

### A Thriving Arts Scene

Contrary to popular belief, Arvada is a thriving hub for the arts, with a vibrant cultural scene that celebrates creativity in all its forms. The Arvada Center for the Arts and Humanities stands as a cornerstone, offering a rich tapestry of performances, exhibitions, and educational programs catering to diverse artistic interests. Beyond this, the city’s neighborhoods are adorned with captivating public art displays, from sculptures to murals, adding a distinct and colorful flair to the community.

### A Community of Believers

Arvada is home to a unique and captivating attraction known as the “Mother Cabrini Shrine.” This iconic religious site draws thousands of visitors annually to honor the first American citizen canonized as a saint by the Catholic Church. Set against the backdrop of the Rocky Mountains, the shrine offers a tranquil atmosphere and breathtaking panoramic views, providing a serene and spiritual experience that resonates with visitors of diverse faiths.

From its pioneering role in aviation history to its thriving arts scene and diverse culinary offerings, [Arvada, CO](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/), is a city that defies expectations. Whether you’re a resident or a visitor, take the time to explore its hidden gems and uncover the unique stories that make this community truly special. ### FAQS

FAQ 1: What is the significance of the Arvada Aviation Field?
Answer: The Arvada Aviation Field was one of the first airfields established in Colorado during the early 20th century. It played a crucial role in the development of aviation in the state, serving as a launching pad for daring pilots and aviators who pushed the boundaries of flight.

FAQ 2: What makes the Ralston Creek Trail so special?
Answer: The Ralston Creek Trail is a true gem for outdoor enthusiasts in Arvada. Spanning over 21 miles, this scenic trail offers stunning views of the Rocky Mountains and passes through lush greenery, providing a peaceful escape from the urban surroundings. It’s a popular spot for hiking, biking, and enjoying nature right within the city limits.

FAQ 3: What is the “Smellyburger” at the Arvada Tavern?
Answer: The “Smellyburger” is a beloved and iconic dish served at the Arvada Tavern, a historic restaurant in the city. It’s a unique burger that features a blend of exotic spices and a distinctive aroma that has earned it a cult following among locals and visitors alike. Trying the “Smellyburger” is an experience that should not be missed when visiting Arvada.

FAQ 4: What is the Mother Cabrini Shrine, and why is it significant?
Answer: The Mother Cabrini Shrine is a religious site in Arvada that honors Saint Frances Xavier Cabrini, the first American citizen to be canonized as a saint by the Catholic Church. The shrine attracts thousands of visitors each year and is known for its peaceful atmosphere and stunning panoramic views of the Rocky Mountains. It’s a significant spiritual destination for believers and a unique cultural attraction for all visitors.

FAQ 5: What makes the Arvada Center for the Arts and Humanities so special?
Answer: The Arvada Center for the Arts and Humanities is a renowned cultural hub that showcases a diverse range of artistic expressions. From live performances and theater productions to art exhibitions and educational programs, the center offers a rich and vibrant experience for art enthusiasts of all ages. It’s a testament to Arvada’s thriving arts scene and commitment to fostering creativity within the community.
