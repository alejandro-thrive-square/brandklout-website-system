### What Are Zygomatic Implants?

Zygomatic implants are longer implants that bypass the upper jawbone and anchor directly into the zygomatic bone -- the cheekbone. Because the cheekbone is one of the densest structures in the skull, it provides a strong, stable foundation even when the upper jaw has experienced significant bone loss.

This anchoring approach allows patients who are not candidates for conventional implants to receive a full-arch restoration without bone grafting or sinus lifts. In most cases, a temporary set of teeth is placed the same day as surgery, giving Denver patients immediate results before heading home.

### Zygomatic Implants vs. Traditional Implants

Both options replace missing teeth with implant-supported restorations, but they serve very different patient situations.

### Traditional Dental Implants

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c4281e6c553da8f2a0363_Traditional%20Dental%20Implants.jpg)

- Anchors into the upper or lower jawbone
- May require bone grafting if volume is insufficient
- May require a sinus lift for upper jaw placement
- Several months of healing before final restoration
- Best suited for patients with adequate bone density
- Performed by most implant dentists

### Zygomatic Implants

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c41027a85dfe414b12aae_zygomatic%20implants.%20Medically%20accurate%203D%20illustration%20of%20human%20teeth%20and%20dentures.webp)

- Anchors into the cheekbone (zygoma)
- No bone grafting required
- No sinus lift needed
- Same-day temporary teeth in most cases
- Designed for severe upper jaw bone loss
- Requires a specialist with advanced implant training

### Who Is a Candidate for Zygomatic Implants Near Denver?

Denver patients who are the best fit for zygomatic implants typically have significant upper jaw bone loss from long-term tooth loss, extended denture wear, failed implants, or advanced gum disease. If you have been told you need extensive bone grafting before implants are possible, or that implants are simply not an option for you, zygomatic implants may change that answer.

Patients should be in reasonably good overall health and free from active oral infections before treatment begins. Heavy smokers face a meaningfully higher risk of implant failure and are encouraged to quit prior to surgery. A consultation with full 3D imaging at our Arvada office is the only reliable way to confirm candidacy and determine the right treatment approach for your specific anatomy.

### What to Expect: The Zygomatic Implant Process

1

### Consultation and 3D Imaging

Every zygomatic case begins with a comprehensive evaluation and cone beam 3D CT scan at our Arvada office. This imaging maps your cheekbone density, sinus anatomy, and available bone with precision, giving us everything needed to build a customized surgical plan before any procedure begins.

2

### Surgical Placement

Surgery is performed under sedation or general anesthesia for complete comfort. Implants are carefully angled through the upper jaw and anchored into the cheekbone. In some cases, conventional implants are placed alongside zygomatic implants to provide additional support for the restoration.

3

### Immediate Temporary Restoration

Most patients receive a temporary full-arch restoration the same day as surgery. Denver patients leave our Arvada office with a functional, complete smile and do not have to go through the healing period without teeth.

4

### Healing and Integration

Over the following months, your implants fuse with the cheekbone through osseointegration. Follow-up visits monitor progress, confirm healing, and make any adjustments needed to the temporary restoration.

5

### Final Restoration

Once fully healed, your permanent full-arch restoration is custom-fabricated and secured to your implants for a beautiful, lasting result.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c43d50fb322270ce6078c_zygomatic%20implant%20result.webp)

### Recovery and Aftercare

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c43d50fb322270ce6078c_zygomatic%20implant%20result.webp)

Most patients experience cheek swelling, mild bruising, and soreness for the first week to ten days following zygomatic implant surgery. Prescribed antibiotics and pain medication are standard aftercare, and a soft diet is required during the osseointegration period. The majority of Denver patients return to light daily activity within a week. Our team provides complete aftercare instructions and remains accessible throughout your recovery, regardless of how far you have traveled to see us.
