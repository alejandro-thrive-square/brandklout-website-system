### FAQs

[contact us](/contact)

How much do dental implants cost near Applewood, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) The cost depends on the type of implant and your case complexity. Single tooth implants, All-On-4, and zygomatic implants each have different starting prices. We offer a free consultation with transparent upfront pricing and no hidden fees.

How do I know if I need dental implants?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Common signs include difficulty chewing or speaking due to missing teeth, shifting teeth causing bite misalignment, and jawbone loss visible on X-ray. Our team can assess your case during a free consultation and recommend the right solution.

What financing options are available for dental treatment near Applewood?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) We offer CareCredit, Cherry Payment Plans, Sunbit Finance, PatientFi, Proceed Finance, and LendingClub. Most patients receive a same-day decision and applications can be completed online, by phone, or in office.

Is sedation dentistry available near Applewood for anxious patients?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Yes. We offer nitrous oxide, oral sedation, and IV sedation. Dr. Bennett holds Moderate IV Sedation Certification with annual ACLS renewal, making us one of the few practices in the area qualified to provide in-house IV sedation.

What is the difference between All-On-4 implants and traditional dentures?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) All-On-4 implants are permanently fixed to the jawbone using four titanium posts and do not slip or require adhesives. Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can shift over time. All-On-4 also preserves jawbone density, which dentures do not.
