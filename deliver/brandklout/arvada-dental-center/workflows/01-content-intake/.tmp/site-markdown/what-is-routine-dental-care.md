## Your Guide to Routine Dental Care: Keeping Your Smile Healthy and Strong

November 14, 2024

At Arvada Dental Center, we’re all about helping you keep your smile healthy and bright with just a bit of regular care. Routine dental care isn’t complicated, but it makes a huge difference in keeping your teeth and gums in top shape. Let’s talk about what’s involved in routine dental care and why it’s worth fitting into your schedule.

### What’s Included in Routine Dental Care?

Routine dental care includes a few key steps that work together to keep your mouth healthy and free from problems. Here’s what you can expect during your visits:

#### **Regular Dental Exams**

When you come in for a [dental checkup](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/the-importance-of-regular-dental-check-ups-in-arvada-co/), we’re not just giving your teeth a quick glance. Our exams allow us to get a clear look at your gums, teeth, and mouth, helping us spot any signs of cavities, gum issues, or other concerns early. We usually recommend scheduling these exams every six months, but it can vary depending on your dental needs.

#### **Professional Cleanings**

Brushing and flossing are fantastic for day-to-day care, but even the best at-home routines can leave some plaque behind. During a professional cleaning, our dental hygienists use special tools to remove any built-up plaque and tartar from those hard-to-reach spots. This keeps your gums healthy and prevents decay, giving you that fresh, clean feeling only professional cleaning can provide.

#### **X-Rays and Imaging**

Sometimes, we need to see what’s happening below the surface. [X-rays](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/panoramic-dental-x-rays-what-they-are-how-they-work-and-their-benefits/) help us catch things like hidden cavities, impacted teeth, or even early bone loss. We’ll take X-rays based on your specific needs, making it easier to spot potential issues before they develop.

#### **Preventive Treatments**

If needed, we may recommend fluoride treatments or dental sealants to give your teeth a little extra protection. Fluoride treatments strengthen enamel, while sealants create a barrier on the back teeth to keep out bacteria. These treatments are especially [helpful for children](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/childrens-dental-health-month/), teens, or anyone more likely to get cavities.

### Why is Routine Dental Care Important?

Taking the time for regular dental care isn’t just about avoiding cavities—it comes with a lot of long-term benefits. Here’s how routine care makes a difference:

- **Catch Problems Early** Routine exams help us catch dental issues like cavities or gum disease before they get worse. By addressing them early, we can keep treatments simpler and more manageable.
- **Support Overall Health** Your oral health is connected to your overall health, so taking care of your teeth and gums helps keep you healthier overall. Research has shown that poor oral health can link to conditions like heart disease and diabetes, so a healthy mouth is a great step toward a healthy body.
- **Save Money in the Long Run** Staying up-to-date with routine care helps you avoid bigger issues that could require more complex and costly treatments down the line. Preventive care often translates to fewer dental bills in the future.

#### Building an At-Home Routine

Keeping up with routine dental care also includes a solid at-home routine. Here’s a quick reminder of the basics:

- **Brush Twice a Day** Use fluoride toothpaste and brush in gentle, circular motions to keep plaque under control.
- **Floss Daily** Flossing reaches the spaces between your teeth that brushing can’t reach, helping to keep your gums healthy.
- **Rinse with Mouthwash** Using an antimicrobial mouthwash can add an extra layer of protection, reducing bacteria and keeping your breath fresh.

When it comes to routine dental care, a few simple steps can keep your teeth and gums healthy for years to come.
