### What Are Full and Partial Dentures?

Dentures are removable prosthetics that replace missing teeth and restore oral function. They're custom-crafted to match your natural tooth color, shape, and bite alignment.
Full dentures replace all teeth in the upper or lower jaw. They rest on your gums and are held in place by natural suction or denture adhesive.

Partial dentures fill gaps when you still have some healthy natural teeth. They attach to existing teeth with clasps, preventing teeth from shifting while restoring your smile.
Modern dentures are comfortable, natural-looking, and durable. We use high-quality materials and digital technology to create dentures that feel like a natural extension of your smile.

### What to Expect: The Denture Process

1

### Comprehensive Consultation

We examine your oral health, discuss your goals, take X-rays, and determine whether full or partial dentures are your best option. This is your opportunity to ask questions and explore financing.

2

### Custom Impressions

Using advanced digital scanning or traditional impression materials, we create precise molds of your mouth to ensure your dentures fit perfectly and comfortably.

3

### Bite Registration

We record your bite relationship to ensure your dentures align properly, providing natural function and preventing jaw strain or discomfort.

4

### Try-In Appointment

Before final fabrication, you'll try on a wax model of your dentures. This lets us verify fit, appearance, and tooth color, making adjustments as needed.

5

### Final Fitting

Your completed dentures are ready! We ensure they fit comfortably, make any necessary adjustments, and teach you proper insertion, removal, and care techniques.

6

### Follow-Up & Adjustments

We schedule follow-up visits to monitor your adjustment period and make refinements. Minor adjustments are normal as your mouth adapts to your new dentures.

### Are Dentures Right for Me?

Dentures may be ideal if you're missing multiple teeth, experiencing difficulty chewing or speaking, or want a non-surgical tooth replacement option. They're more affordable than dental implants and work well for patients who prefer a removable solution.

If you prefer a permanent, non-removable solution with maximum stability, dental implants or implant-supported dentures may be better options. Schedule a consultation and we'll help you determine which solution fits your unique situation, lifestyle, and budget.

### FAQs

Have questions about dentures? Here are answers to common concerns. Don't see your question? Schedule a consultation and we'll address all your concerns.

[contact us](/contact)

How much do dentures cost in Arvada, CO?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Full dentures typically range from $1,300 to $3,500 per arch, while partial dentures cost $700-$2,500, depending on materials and complexity. We offer financing options and membership club discounts to make treatment affordable for every budget.

Will my dentures look natural?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Absolutely! Modern dentures are custom-designed to match your facial features, natural gum color, and tooth shade. We use high-quality materials that mimic the translucency of natural teeth. Most people won't be able to tell you're wearing dentures.

How long do dentures last?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) With proper care, dentures typically last 5-10 years before needing replacement. Your jawbone and gums change shape over time, so periodic relines or adjustments ensure optimal fit and function throughout the life of your dentures.

Can I eat normally with dentures?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Yes! There's an adjustment period of 2-4 weeks, but most denture wearers can eat a wide variety of foods. We recommend starting with soft foods and gradually introducing harder textures. Implant-supported dentures offer even more stability for challenging foods like apples or corn on the cob.

Do dentures hurt?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Properly fitted dentures should not cause pain. Initial soreness for the first few days is normal as your mouth adjusts, but persistent pain indicates the need for adjustment. We fine-tune your dentures until they're completely comfortable.

How do I clean my dentures?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Remove and rinse dentures after eating. Brush them daily with a soft denture brush and non-abrasive denture cleaner (never use regular toothpaste, as it's too abrasive). Soak them overnight in denture solution, and rinse thoroughly before wearing.

Can I sleep with my dentures in?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) While it's possible, we recommend removing dentures at night to give your gums and jaw time to rest and recover. This also reduces bacteria buildup, prevents fungal infections, and extends the life of your dentures. ‍

What's the difference between dentures and dental implants?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Dentures are removable prosthetics that rest on your gums. Dental implants are permanent titanium posts surgically placed in your jawbone that function like natural tooth roots. We also offer implant-supported dentures, which combine the stability of implants with the affordability and ease of dentures.
