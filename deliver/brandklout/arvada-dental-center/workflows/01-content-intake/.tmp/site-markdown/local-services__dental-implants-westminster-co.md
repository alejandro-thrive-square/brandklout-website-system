### What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed directly into the jawbone to serve as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant fuses with the bone through osseointegration, a custom crown is attached on top to complete the restoration. The result looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth.

Unlike bridges or dentures, an implant replaces both the visible tooth and the root beneath it. This is what makes implants the only tooth replacement option that also preserves jawbone. They are designed to last for decades and require no special maintenance beyond normal brushing and flossing.

### Consider Your Tooth Replacement Options

Not every tooth replacement is equal. Here is how implants compare to the alternatives so you can make the most informed decision for your smile and your long-term health.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487600bb422bace3e85ef_Dental-Implant-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Single Tooth Implant

One implant and one crown replace a single missing tooth independently. No healthy neighboring teeth are altered. The most conservative and complete solution for individual tooth loss.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f10bb422bace3eb105_dental-implant-crown-crcl.png.webp)

#### Implant-Supported Bridge

Two implants support a bridge spanning a gap of two or more missing teeth. More stable than a traditional bridge and does not require altering adjacent healthy teeth.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1e772925a9bfd4bff_Full-Arch2-icon-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Implant-Supported Dentures

A removable denture that snaps securely onto implants. Far more stable than a conventional denture and can be upgraded to a fixed restoration in the future.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1e772925a9bfd4bff_Full-Arch2-icon-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Full Arch Fixed Restoration

Four or more implants support a permanently fixed full-arch prosthetic. The closest option to a natural set of teeth in both function and appearance for patients with full-arch tooth loss.

### What to Expect: The Dental Implant Process

1

### Consultation and Planning

Your treatment begins with a full evaluation and 3D CBCT imaging at our Arvada office. We assess your bone density, bite, and oral health and build a customized treatment plan. Westminster patients leave this visit knowing exactly what their treatment involves, the timeline, and the total cost.

2

### Implant Placement

The titanium implant post is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Sedation is available for patients who prefer it. The procedure is straightforward and most patients return to light activity within a day or two.

3

### Osseointegration

Over the following three to six months, the implant fuses with the jawbone. A temporary restoration may be placed during this period to maintain your appearance and function while healing progresses.

4

### Abutment Placement

Once the implant has fully integrated, a small connector called an abutment is attached to the top of the post. This is what the final crown will be secured to.

5

### Final Crown Placement

Your custom crown is fabricated to match the size, shape, and color of your surrounding teeth and permanently secured to the abutment. Dr. Bennett checks the fit and bite before you leave to ensure everything feels completely natural.

### Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults in good general health who have one or more missing teeth are candidates for dental implants. Adequate jawbone density is needed to anchor the implant, and gums should be free from active disease before treatment begins. Patients with well-controlled systemic conditions such as diabetes can often qualify with appropriate medical coordination.

Patients with bone loss may need a bone graft or sinus lift before implant placement. Smokers are strongly encouraged to quit as smoking significantly raises the risk of implant failure. Westminster patients with severe upper jaw bone loss may be candidates for zygomatic implants, which bypass the need for grafting entirely. A consultation with 3D imaging is the most accurate way to confirm what applies to your situation.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a269c8c372519655ab3785_Dental-implant-Arvada-Dental-Center-and-Implants.png.webp)

### Recovery and Aftercare

Most patients experience mild soreness and some swelling at the implant site for the first few days. Prescribed or over-the-counter pain medication manages discomfort effectively. A soft diet is recommended for the first one to two weeks while the site heals.

Long-term care is simple. Brush twice daily, floss normally, and schedule routine checkups at Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months. With consistent oral hygiene and regular professional care, your implant is built to last for decades.
