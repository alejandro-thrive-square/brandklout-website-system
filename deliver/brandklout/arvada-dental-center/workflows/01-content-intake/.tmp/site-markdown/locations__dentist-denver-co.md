#### Family Dentistry for Every Stage of Life

We proudly serve families in the Denver area with quality dental care for children, adults, and seniors alike. Every member of your household receives personalized attention at every visit.

#### Restorative Dentistry to Reclaim Your Smile

Missing or damaged teeth affect more than your appearance. Our restorative services bring back full function and beauty to your smile. From dental fillings and crowns to bridges and implants, we use advanced techniques to provide results that last. We also offer modern full and partial dentures, including implant-supported options for enhanced comfort and stability.

#### Cosmetic Dentistry to Transform Your Smile

A beautiful smile can boost your confidence and transform the way you interact with the world. We offer porcelain veneers to cover chips, gaps, and discoloration, professional teeth whitening to remove stains safely and effectively, and Invisalign clear aligners for a discreet and comfortable way to correct misaligned teeth without traditional braces.

#### Dental Implants and Emergency Dentistry

Dental implants provide a permanent, natural-looking solution for missing teeth that can last a lifetime with proper care. We also offer prompt emergency dentistry for urgent issues including toothaches, broken teeth, and sudden dental pain. For patients with dental anxiety, sedation options are available to ensure a calm and stress-free experience for any procedure.

How much do dental implants cost near Denver, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) The cost depends on the type of implant and the complexity of your case. Does Arvada Dental Center & Implants accept dental insurance for Denver patients?

We work with most major dental insurance providers and submit claims electronically on your behalf. Our team reviews your benefits before treatment begins so you know exactly what is covered and what your out-of-pocket costs will be.

What financing options are available for dental treatment near Denver?

Most patients receive a same-day approval decision and applications can be completed online from home, by phone, or in the office.

What is the difference between All-On-4 implants and traditional dentures?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) All-On-4 implants are permanently fixed to the jawbone using four titanium posts and do not slip or require adhesives. Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can shift over time. All-On-4 also helps preserve jawbone density, which dentures do not.

What is the difference between All-On-4 implants and traditional dentures?
