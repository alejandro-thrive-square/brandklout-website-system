#### Why Choose Dental Implants?

Unlike dentures or bridges, **dental implants** are a long-term solution that feel and function like natural teeth. Implants fuse to your jawbone, providing a stable foundation that doesn’t shift or come loose. They’re also the only tooth replacement option that helps prevent bone loss.

Key benefits of implants:

- Eat any food you want without discomfort
- Maintain your facial structure and jawbone
- Avoid the hassle of adhesives or nightly removal
- Smile confidently—no more hiding your teeth
- Restore up to 90% of your natural bite force

#### Dental Implant Options We Offer in Arvada & Denver

We understand every patient is different. That’s why we offer multiple solutions based on your needs, goals, and budget:

##### ✅ **Single Tooth Dental Implants**

Perfect if you’re missing just one tooth. A single implant replaces the root, topped with a custom crown that blends perfectly with your natural teeth.
👉 [Learn more about single implants here](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/single-dental-implant/)

##### ✅ **Multiple Tooth Implants**

If you’re missing several teeth in a row, we may recommend implant-supported bridges—fewer implants than one per tooth, but with the same strength and esthetics.

##### ✅ **All-on-4 Implants** (Same-Day Full Arch Replacement)

For patients missing most or all of their teeth, **All-on-4** is a revolutionary treatment. Just **four implants** can support a full arch of fixed teeth—top or bottom.

- No bone grafting required for most patients
- Teeth fixed in place the same day
- Look, feel, and function like real teeth
- Proven long-term results

We use advanced imaging, guided surgery, and sedation options to make the process smooth, predictable, and comfortable.

##### ✅ **Snap-In Dentures / Implant-Supported Dentures**

If you want more stability than traditional dentures but don’t want a full fixed bridge, snap-in dentures offer a middle-ground solution. They “snap” into place over implants—no glue required.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### What are dental implants and how do they help me smile confidently in Denver?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that are placed in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or bridge. They help improve your smile by providing a strong and stable foundation for your new teeth, allowing you to eat and speak without worry.

#### Am I a good candidate for dental implants in Denver?

If you have missing teeth or severely damaged teeth, you may be a good candidate for dental implants in Denver. However, a thorough examination by a qualified dentist is necessary to determine if dental implants are the right solution for you.

#### What is the process for getting dental implants in Denver?

The process for getting dental implants in Denver typically involves several steps, including a consultation, X-rays and impressions, the placement of the implants, and finally, the placement of the replacement teeth. The entire process can take several months but results in a strong and natural-looking smile.

#### Will dental implants in Denver look and feel like my natural teeth?

Yes, dental implants are designed to look and feel just like your natural teeth. They are custom-made to match the size, shape, and color of your remaining teeth, providing a seamless and natural appearance.

#### How long do dental implants typically last?

Dental implants are a long-term solution for missing teeth and can last a lifetime with proper care. They are made of durable materials such as titanium and porcelain, which are resistant to decay and staining.

#### Are there any special care instructions for dental implants in Denver?

Caring for dental implants in Denver is similar to caring for your natural teeth. You should brush and floss daily, visit your dentist regularly, and avoid habits such as smoking that can affect the longevity of your implants. Your dentist may also provide specific care instructions based on your individual needs.
