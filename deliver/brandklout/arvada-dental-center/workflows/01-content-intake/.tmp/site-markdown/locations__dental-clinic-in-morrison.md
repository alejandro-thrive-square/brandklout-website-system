#### General Dentistry to Keep Your Smile Healthy for Life

Preventing dental issues before they arise is the foundation of good oral health. Our preventive services include routine checkups and cleanings to maintain optimal hygiene, sealants and fluoride treatments for cavity prevention, tooth-colored fillings to restore minor decay while preserving your natural teeth, and gum disease treatments to protect your smile and overall health. Staying proactive helps you avoid costly and complex treatments in the future.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a47415da0e2d0f07f3a493_dental-implants-in-arvada-co-dental-implants-in-denver-dentist.png)

#### Cosmetic Dentistry to Transform Your Smile

Our cosmetic services help Morrison patients achieve the look they have always wanted. Whether you have discolored, chipped, or misaligned teeth, we offer veneers for a flawless even smile, professional teeth whitening for a brighter whiter appearance, and Invisalign to straighten misaligned teeth discreetly. We deliver natural-looking, long-lasting results tailored to your needs.

#### Restorative Dentistry to Rebuild Your Smile

Missing or damaged teeth can impact your daily life, making eating and speaking difficult. Our restorative services focus on rebuilding your smile with durable, natural-looking solutions including dental implants as a permanent replacement for missing teeth, full and partial dentures for a comfortable fit, and bridges and crowns to restore both function and appearance.

#### Emergency Dental Care When You Need It Most

Dental emergencies can happen when you least expect them. Our team is ready to handle urgent issues and relieve pain as quickly as possible. We treat severe tooth pain and infections, broken, cracked, or knocked-out teeth, lost fillings, crowns, or bridges, and swollen gums or facial injuries. Call (303) 421-7611 immediately and we will do everything we can to provide same-day care.

### FAQs

How much do dental implants cost near Morrison, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) The cost depends on the type of implant and the complexity of your case. Single tooth implants, All-On-4, zirconia fixed bridges, and zygomatic implants each have different starting prices. We offer a free consultation with transparent upfront pricing and no hidden fees.

Does Arvada Dental Center & Implants accept dental insurance for patients near Morrison?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Yes. We work with most major dental insurance providers and submit claims electronically on your behalf. Our team reviews your benefits before treatment begins so you know exactly what is covered and what your out-of-pocket costs will be.

What financing options are available for dental treatment near Morrison?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) We offer financing through CareCredit, Cherry Payment Plans, Sunbit Finance, PatientFi, Proceed Finance, and LendingClub. Most patients receive a same-day approval decision and applications can be completed online from home, by phone, or in the office.

What is the difference between All-On-4 implants and traditional dentures?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) All-On-4 implants are permanently fixed to the jawbone using four titanium posts and do not slip or require adhesives. Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can shift over time. All-On-4 also helps preserve jawbone density, which dentures do not.

Is IV sedation available for dental procedures near Morrison?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Yes. Dr. Bennett holds Moderate IV Sedation Certification with annual ACLS renewal. IV sedation is available for implant surgery, oral surgery, and patients with dental anxiety who want a comfortable, relaxed experience throughout their procedure.
