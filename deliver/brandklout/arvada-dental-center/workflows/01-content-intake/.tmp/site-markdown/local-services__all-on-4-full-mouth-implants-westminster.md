### What Is All-On-4?

All-On-4 is a full-arch implant technique that uses four strategically placed implants to support a complete set of teeth. Two implants are placed vertically at the front of the jaw and two are angled at the back to maximize contact with available bone.

Because of the angled placement, All-On-4 often eliminates the need for bone grafting even in patients with moderate bone loss. The result is a permanently fixed, full-arch restoration that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth.

### Am I a Candidate for All-On-4?

All-On-4 is designed for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both arches, or patients with multiple failing teeth ready for a permanent fixed solution. Most patients with moderate bone loss qualify without bone grafting due to the angled implant placement.

Patients with severe upper jaw bone loss may be candidates for zygomatic implants rather than standard All-On-4 placement. Heavy smokers are strongly encouraged to quit before treatment. A consultation with 3D imaging at our Arvada office is the only accurate way to confirm your candidacy.

### Recovery and Aftercare

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c4ab8f20c5a5feb6f9cc1_all%20on%204%20recovery.webp)

Expect swelling, soreness, and fatigue for the first several days following All-On-4 surgery. Prescribed pain medication and antibiotics are standard. A soft diet is required for several months while your implants integrate with the bone.

Most patients return to light activity within a few days. Once your permanent restoration is placed, caring for it is simple: brush twice daily, floss with a water flosser, and schedule routine checkups at Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months.

Do I need bone grafting for All-On-4?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) In most cases, no. Can I get teeth the same day as All-On-4 surgery?

The implants themselves are typically a lifetime solution. The prosthetic restoration may need replacement after 15 to 20 years depending on wear and material.

Is All-On-4 surgery painful?
