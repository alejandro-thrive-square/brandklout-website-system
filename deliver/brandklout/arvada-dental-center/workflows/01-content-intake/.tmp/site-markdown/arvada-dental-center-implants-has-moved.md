### Arvada Dental Center & Implants New Address

We’re thrilled to announce that Arvada Dental Center & Implants has recently relocated! Our new address is 6695 Wadsworth Blvd, just 1 Mile North of our previous location. Now, you can find us conveniently close to Dollar Tree and Walgreens, nestled in the vibrant heart of Arvada.

#### Dental Implants in Arvada

At our new location, we continue to offer top-notch dental services, with a special focus on dental implants. [Dental implants](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/dental-implants-vs-dentures/) are a revolutionary solution for those seeking a permanent and natural-looking replacement for missing teeth. Our experienced team at Arvada Dental Center & Implants is dedicated to providing personalized care and ensuring your comfort throughout the implant process.

If you’re considering dental implants or have questions about the procedure, our expert staff is ready to guide you. We believe in restoring not only your smile but also your confidence and oral health.

##### Dental Office in Arvada

Our new office resides in the space where the Colorado Coins Cards & Comics store used to be. Yes, you read that right – we’ve transformed a space once filled with comic books into a state-of-the-art dental office! While you may miss browsing through the comics, we promise a different kind of excitement as we create beautiful smiles and foster excellent oral health.

With our commitment to patient satisfaction, we strive to make your dental visits enjoyable and stress-free. Our modern facility is equipped with the [latest technology](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/lanap-treatment/), and our skilled team is here to address all your dental needs.

##### Visit Us

If you’re ready to experience exceptional dental care at our new location, here are our details:

**Arvada Dental Center & Implants***Address:* 6695 Wadsworth Blvd, Arvada, CO 80003, United States*Phone:* +1 303-421-7611*Appointments:* [arvadadentalcenter.com](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/)

Come and discover the new home of Arvada Dental Center & Implants. We look forward to welcoming you to our updated space and continuing to serve the Arvada community with the highest standard of dental care. Don’t hesitate to reach out for appointments or any inquiries – your journey to a healthier, more beautiful smile starts with us!
