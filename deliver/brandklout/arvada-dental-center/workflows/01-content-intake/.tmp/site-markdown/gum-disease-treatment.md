### What Is Gum Disease?

Gum disease is a bacterial infection of the gums caused by plaque buildup. It begins with gingivitis and can progress to periodontitis if left untreated.

Without treatment, gum disease causes gum recession, bone loss, and eventual tooth loss. Early detection and treatment can reverse damage and save your teeth.

What causes gum disease?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Gum disease is caused by bacterial plaque buildup on teeth and along the gum line. Without proper brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings, plaque hardens into tartar and causes gum inflammation, infection, and eventual bone loss.

What are the signs of gum disease?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Common signs include swollen or bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, receding gums, loose teeth, and changes in your bite. If you notice any of these symptoms, schedule an evaluation at Arvada Dental Center & Implants immediately.

Can gum disease be reversed?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Early-stage gum disease (gingivitis) can be reversed with professional cleaning and improved home care. Advanced gum disease (periodontitis) cannot be fully reversed but can be controlled and managed with periodontal treatment.

What is scaling and root planing?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Scaling and root planing is a deep cleaning procedure that removes plaque and bacteria from below the gum line and smooths tooth roots to promote healing and prevent future infection.

What is LANAP laser gum therapy in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) LANAP (Laser Assisted New Attachment Procedure) uses laser energy to remove diseased tissue and bacteria while preserving healthy gums. It is less invasive than traditional gum surgery with faster healing and less discomfort.

How often do I need periodontal maintenance cleanings?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Patients with gum disease typically need periodontal maintenance cleanings every 3 to 4 months. These deep cleanings help control infection and prevent disease progression.
