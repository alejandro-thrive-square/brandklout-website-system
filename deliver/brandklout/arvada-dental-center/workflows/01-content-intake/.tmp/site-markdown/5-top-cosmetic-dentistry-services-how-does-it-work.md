Our [**cosmetic dentists (Arvada)**](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/cosmetic-dentistry/) have transformed countless smiles over the years. And if there’s anything you need to know about cosmetic dentistry, it’s that everyone’s needs are different. We strive to provide the best options for your needs, or combinations of treatments to help you achieve the aesthetic goals you have in mind.

If you’re considering your options, here are our top five requested cosmetic treatments:

### **Dental Veneers**

There is no cosmetic dental treatment that offers as dramatic of an outcome as porcelain dental veneers. [**Cosmetic veneers**](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/veneers/) instantly transform everything about the way your teeth look. From the alignment and shape of your smile to the size and color of each tooth, you can recreate your smile from start to finish.

Veneers are thin restorations that we bond over the front of your natural teeth. Most patients get anywhere from 6-10 veneers across their upper, visible teeth. Our Arvada Dentists may even decide to craft a mock-up version of your veneers design so that you can see the proposed smile transformation before your treatment actually starts.

### **Teeth Whitening**

Whitening your teeth instantly takes years off of your appearance. Whiter teeth are more attractive and tend to be seen as healthier. You might find that stand-alone teeth whitening is the only thing you need to help your teeth look their best. But if you’re planning on updating any dental work in the more visible areas of your smile, our [**Arvada dentists**](https://www.arvadadentalcenter.com/) recommend whitening beforehand, so that we can match the materials to a brighter shade.

Professional teeth whitening can take several shades of stain and natural discoloration off of your teeth. You’ll see stronger and faster results, as opposed to using commercial kits sold in stores.

We offer [**same-day teeth whitening**](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/teeth-whitening/) for instant results in just about an hour. You can also request take-home trays to touch up with between dental checkups.

### **Composite Dental Bonding**

Do you have a chipped tooth, a small gap between teeth, or an irregular area that detracts from your overall smile? Cosmetic bonding provides same-day results without any downtime for recovery. In most cases, numbing isn’t even required as bonding is non-invasive to your enamel.

Cosmetic bonding uses tooth-colored material (similar to white fillings) to cover over and reshape specific areas of concern. Once our Arvada dentist achieves the desired look, we use a special light to cure the material so that it immediately hardens into place.

Bonding is a great choice for people who do not need dental veneers but still have visible issues on one or two specific teeth. The bonding material blends in with the adjacent enamel so that the entire restoration blends seamlessly with the rest of your tooth structure.

### **Gum Recontouring**

Do you have a “gummy” smile? Uneven gum tissues can be genetic, caused by medications that you’re taking, or even due to hormones. But asymmetrical gumlines can also detract from the appearance of your teeth. By adjusting the margins of your gums, we can help crooked or lopsided-looking teeth look symmetrical and properly shaped. Gum recontouring also lengthens teeth, minimizing the appearance of gummy smiles, so that you can feel confident knowing your teeth get the attention they deserve.

We may recommend combining gum recontouring with other aesthetic treatments, such as [**whitening**](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/what-to-know-about-the-teeth-whitening-process/) or bonding, depending on the shape of your smile.

### **Dental Implants**

Although dental implants are a restorative treatment, they also serve a cosmetic purpose. By replacing your missing teeth, they help transform your entire outlook on life. Your confidence and self-esteem will instantly be boosted the same day. [**Dental implants (near me)**](https://www.arvadadentalcenter.com/dental-implants/) are the only tooth replacement that is designed to function and look like anatomical teeth. Whether you need to replace one tooth or all of them, Arvada dental implants can provide you with lifelong results that you’ll be able to depend on.

Looking for a great cosmetic dentist? Arvada Dental Center has you covered. With options like smile makeovers and [**sedation dentistry**](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/iv-sedation-dentistry/) near me, you can enjoy amazing results and an exceptional experience from start to finish.
