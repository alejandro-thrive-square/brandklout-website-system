### What Are Zygomatic Implants?

Zygomatic implants are extra-long implants that anchor into the zygomatic bone -- the cheekbone -- rather than the upper jawbone. This makes them the ideal solution for patients with severe upper jaw bone loss who cannot support conventional implants, even with grafting.

Because the cheekbone is naturally dense, zygomatic implants do not require bone grafting before placement. In most cases, a temporary full-arch restoration is attached the same day as surgery, so patients leave our Arvada office with a complete smile before returning home to Westminster.

### Zygomatic Implants vs. Traditional Implants

Both options replace missing teeth with implant-supported restorations, but they serve very different patient situations.

### Traditional Dental Implants

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c4281e6c553da8f2a0363_Traditional%20Dental%20Implants.jpg)

- Anchors into the upper or lower jawbone
- May require bone grafting if volume is insufficient
- May require a sinus lift for upper jaw placement
- Several months of healing before final restoration
- Best suited for patients with adequate bone density
- Performed by most implant dentists

### Zygomatic Implants

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c41027a85dfe414b12aae_zygomatic%20implants.%20Medically%20accurate%203D%20illustration%20of%20human%20teeth%20and%20dentures.webp)

- Anchors into the cheekbone (zygoma)
- No bone grafting required
- No sinus lift needed
- Same-day temporary teeth in most cases
- Designed for severe upper jaw bone loss
- Requires a specialist with advanced implant training

### Who Is a Candidate for Zygomatic Implants Near Broomfield?

Zygomatic implants are best suited for patients with significant upper jaw bone loss who do not qualify for traditional implants. This includes patients who have worn dentures for many years, experienced ongoing bone resorption, or been told that bone grafting alone would not be sufficient to support implants.

Ideal candidates are in good overall health and free from active oral infections. Smokers are advised to quit before treatment, as smoking significantly increases the risk of implant failure. A consultation with 3D imaging at our Arvada office is the only accurate way to confirm your candidacy, and we welcome patients from Broomfield and surrounding communities to schedule that first step.

### What to Expect: The Zygomatic Implant Process

1

### Consultation and 3D Imaging

Your journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation at our Arvada office, including 3D CBCT cone beam imaging to assess your bone structure, sinus anatomy, and cheekbone density. We build a fully customized surgical plan before anything begins.

2

### Surgical Placement

Surgery is performed under sedation or general anesthesia for your comfort. Implants are precisely angled through the upper jaw and anchored into the cheekbone. Depending on your case, traditional implants may be placed alongside zygomatic implants for additional support.

3

### Immediate Temporary Restoration

In most cases, a temporary full-arch restoration is attached the same day as surgery. Broomfield patients leave our Arvada office with a complete smile and return home without a gap in their appearance during healing.

4

### Healing and Integration

Over the following months, your implants integrate with the bone. We schedule follow-up visits to monitor healing and make any needed adjustments to your temporary restoration.

5

### Final Restoration

Once fully healed, your permanent full-arch restoration is custom-fabricated and secured to your implants for a beautiful, lasting result.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c43d50fb322270ce6078c_zygomatic%20implant%20result.webp)

### Recovery and Aftercare

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c43d50fb322270ce6078c_zygomatic%20implant%20result.webp)

Expect noticeable cheek swelling and soreness for the first one to two weeks following surgery. Prescribed antibiotics and pain medication are standard, and a soft diet is required for several months during osseointegration. Most patients return to light activity within a week. Our Arvada team provides detailed aftercare instructions and stays closely available through every stage of your healing, whether you are local or coming from Broomfield.
