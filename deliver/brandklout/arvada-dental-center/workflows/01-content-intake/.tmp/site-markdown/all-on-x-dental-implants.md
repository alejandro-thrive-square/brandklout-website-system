### What Is All-On-X?

All-On-X is a full arch restoration technique that uses 4 to 8 strategically placed titanium implants to support a complete set of permanent, non removable teeth. Dr. Bennett evaluates bone quality, density, and bite forces to determine the ideal number of implants for each patient. Based on these factors, patients may receive anywhere from 4 to 8 implants to create long term stability, durability, and the strength needed to withstand daily bite forces for decades.

Unlike removable dentures, All-On-X is permanently fixed to your jawbone. You can eat, speak, and smile with confidence. Most patients receive a full arch temporary restoration on the same day as surgery and leave our Arvada office with a complete, functional smile.

### What to Expect: The All-On-X Process

1

### Consultation and CT Scan

We take a full cone beam CT scan to assess your bone structure, density, and quality. Dr. Bennett reviews your health history, evaluates your bone, and determines both your candidacy and the ideal number of implants for your case.

2

### Treatment Planning

Using your CT scan, Dr. Bennett maps the exact position, angle, and depth of each implant. The number of implants is finalized based on bone quality and your bite forces. Fully guided surgical templates are fabricated for precision placement.

3

### Extractions (If Needed)

If you have remaining failing teeth, they are removed the same day as your implant surgery. You never go without teeth at any stage of treatment.

4

### Implant Placement

Between four and eight titanium posts are surgically placed into strategic positions in your jawbone under IV sedation. The angled placement maximizes bone contact, stability, and long-term durability.

5

### Temporary Teeth Placed

Abutments are attached and a full arch temporary restoration, your healing teeth, is secured the same day. You leave with a complete, functional smile.

6

### Final Zirconia Restoration

After three to five months of healing and osseointegration, your final permanent zirconia restoration is custom-crafted and placed. This is your forever smile.

### Are You a Candidate for All-On-X?

You may be an ideal candidate if you are missing most or all of your teeth in one or both arches, struggling with ill-fitting dentures, or dealing with failing teeth that need extraction. All-On-X is designed to work with reduced bone volume in many cases, and Dr. Bennett's approach of customizing the implant count to your bone quality means more patients qualify than with a rigid four-implant technique.

Good general health, non-smoking or willingness to quit, and commitment to aftercare are the key factors in long-term success. Some patients benefit from preparatory treatments like bone grafting or gum disease therapy. Dr. Bennett evaluates your complete health history and CT scan at your consultation and gives you an honest, personalized assessment with no pressure.

### Recovery and Aftercare

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c4ab8f20c5a5feb6f9cc1_all%20on%204%20recovery.webp)

Most patients experience mild soreness and swelling for three to five days following implant placement, with mild to moderate pain managed in-house. We recommend a soft diet for the first week. The full osseointegration process takes three to five months, during which we monitor you at regular checkup appointments. Once your final zirconia restoration is placed, care for your implants just like natural teeth with daily brushing, flossing, and regular professional cleanings.

### How We Plan Your All-On-X With Precision

Precise implant placement starts long before surgery. Our cone beam CT scanner creates a detailed 3D image of your jaw, bone structure, nerve pathways, and anatomy. Dr. Bennett uses this to evaluate your bone quality, finalize implant count, and map every position digitally before a single incision is made. Fully guided surgical templates are built from this plan, ensuring every implant lands exactly where intended for optimal strength, aesthetics, and long-term success.

How much does All-On-4 cost in Arvada, Colorado?

Why does Dr. Bennett use All-On-X instead of always placing exactly four implants?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Every patient's bone is different. Dr. Bennett evaluates bone density, volume, and bite forces during your consultation and places between four and eight implants based on what will provide the greatest stability and longevity for your specific case. The goal is a restoration built to last many decades, not just a few years.

How is All-On-4 different from traditional dental implants in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Traditional implants replace individual teeth one at a time. All-On-4 uses four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of teeth at once. It is a more efficient and often more affordable solution for patients who need to replace a full arch rather than a single tooth.

Can I get teeth the same day as All-On-4 surgery?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) 99.9% of the time, we always send patients home with teeth! A temporary full-arch restoration, or "healing teeth," is attached to the implants on the day of surgery so you leave with a complete smile. Your permanent final restoration, made of zirconia, is placed once the implants have fully integrated, typically three to six months later.

How long do All-On-4 implants last?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) With proper care, All-On-4 implants are designed to last decades. The implants themselves are typically a lifetime solution. The prosthetic restoration may need replacement after 15 to 20 years depending on wear and material.

Can I get teeth the same day as All-On-X surgery?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) 99.9% of the time, yes. A temporary full-arch restoration, also called your healing teeth, is attached to the implants on the day of surgery so you leave with a complete, functional smile. Your permanent final restoration, milled from zirconia, is placed once the implants have fully integrated, typically three to five months later.

How long does All-On-X last?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) With proper care and regular checkups, All-On-X implants are designed to last many decades to a lifetime. The titanium posts typically never need replacing. The zirconia prosthetic is highly durable and resistant to chipping and staining, and may need evaluation after 15 to 20 years of normal wear.

Does insurance cover All-On-X in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Some dental insurance plans contribute toward All-On-X, particularly the surgical and crown portions. Coverage varies by plan. We verify your benefits before treatment begins and help you maximize what your plan provides. Financing through our dozen-plus lending partners covers any remaining out-of-pocket costs.

Am I too old for All-On-X?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) There is no upper age limit. The procedure is regularly performed for patients in their 70s and 80s. Good general health and adequate bone structure are the primary requirements, not age.

What is the difference between All-On-X and traditional dentures?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Traditional dentures are removable, rest on your gums, can slip, require adhesive, and accelerate bone loss. All-On-X is permanently fixed to implants in your jawbone, functions like natural teeth, and actively preserves the bone. Most patients describe the difference as life-changing.

How do I care for All-On-X implants?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Care for your All-On-X just like natural teeth. Brush twice daily, use a water flosser or interdental brush to clean under the bridge, and attend regular professional cleanings at Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months.
