### What Is Full Mouth Reconstruction?

Full mouth reconstruction is a series of treatments designed to restore the function and health of your mouth completely. We use crowns, bridges, implants, and other procedures to rebuild your entire smile.

The treatment plan is customized to your specific needs and may include restorative, cosmetic, and periodontal procedures performed over several months.

### Full Mouth Reconstruction Treatments

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487600bb422bace3e85ef_Dental-Implant-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are placed over restored teeth that are cracked or have had tooth decay. These restorations are custom-made to match the size, shape, and color of your natural teeth. Crowns protect teeth from further damage and restore full function and aesthetics.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f10bb422bace3eb105_dental-implant-crown-crcl.png.webp)

#### Dental Bridges

Dental bridges are a series of custom-made crowns joined together that cover the gap where missing teeth would be. This restoration option bridges the space and ends with natural teeth taking hold on one or both end points.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1bbc5ec965ac794f9_Implant-denture-white-circle-copy.png.webp)

#### Porcelain Veneers

At our office, you can have veneers applied on your teeth to cover up any flaw or discoloration. Veneers correct minor cracks, chips, gaps, and discoloration. Porcelain veneers look natural, match the rest of your teeth and remain stain resistant.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1e772925a9bfd4bff_Full-Arch2-icon-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Teeth Whitening

Everyone deserves to see their smile the way they want it to appear, so we also offer treatments to whiten your teeth. Professional whitening makes your restoration results stand out by brightening surrounding natural teeth to match.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1e772925a9bfd4bff_Full-Arch2-icon-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Dental Implants

Dental implants are some of the most popular fixtures to restore missing teeth. Implants use titanium posts to anchor them in your jaw bone that last a lifetime. Once the post and the permanent restorations are loaded, such as a crown or bridge, they function and feel just like natural teeth.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1e772925a9bfd4bff_Full-Arch2-icon-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Crowning

Creating a beautiful smile is possible using a variety of different treatments. If you only need one or two crowns to restore damaged teeth as part of your full reconstruction, we offer high-quality restorations.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1e772925a9bfd4bff_Full-Arch2-icon-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Sedation

Many full mouth reconstructions require multiple appointments and lengthy procedures. We offer sedation options to keep you comfortable throughout treatment. Sedation dentistry allows anxious patients to receive the care they need in a relaxed state.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1e772925a9bfd4bff_Full-Arch2-icon-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Orthodontics

In some cases, your smile may need proper alignment before completing reconstruction. Invisalign or traditional braces can straighten teeth into ideal positions for optimal reconstruction results.

### Our Smile Reconstruction Process

Full mouth reconstruction is a collaborative process between you and Dr. Bennett. First, we perform a comprehensive examination including X-rays, CT scans, bite analysis, and discussion of your goals. Dr. Bennett then creates a detailed treatment plan outlining which procedures are needed and in what order.

### FAQs

Have questions about full mouth reconstruction in Arvada, Colorado? Here are answers to our most common patient questions. Schedule a consultation for a personalized evaluation.

How much does full mouth reconstruction cost in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) The cost of full mouth reconstruction varies greatly depending on which procedures are needed and how many teeth require treatment. A comprehensive reconstruction can range from $20,000 to $60,000 or more. Dr. Bennett will provide a detailed treatment plan and cost estimate during your consultation. We offer flexible financing through CareCredit, Cherry, and other providers.

How long does full mouth reconstruction take?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Most full mouth reconstructions take 6 to 18 months to complete, depending on the complexity of your case. Some procedures like implants require healing time between appointments. Dr. Bennett will outline a timeline during your consultation.

Does insurance cover full mouth reconstruction in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Most dental insurance plans cover a portion of restorative procedures like crowns, bridges, and implants. Coverage varies by plan and may have annual maximums. We verify your benefits before treatment and help maximize your coverage.

What is the difference between full mouth reconstruction and smile makeover?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Full mouth reconstruction focuses on restoring function and health to severely damaged or missing teeth using restorative procedures. A smile makeover focuses primarily on cosmetic improvements to healthy teeth. Many patients need both approaches combined.

How long do full mouth reconstructions last in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) With proper care and regular dental visits, crowns and bridges typically last 10 to 15 years. Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime. Good oral hygiene and regular checkups are essential for maintaining your reconstruction long-term.
