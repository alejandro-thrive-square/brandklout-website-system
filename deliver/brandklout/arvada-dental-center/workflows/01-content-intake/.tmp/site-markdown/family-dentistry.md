### Healthy Smiles for the Whole Family.

Your Family's Dental Home in Arvada, CO.

Finding a family dentist in Arvada, CO that every member of your household actually looks forward to visiting makes a real difference. At Arvada Dental Center & Implants, we welcome patients of all ages and build lasting relationships rooted in trust, transparency, and genuine care.

### Personalized Family Dentistry Services

We offer a full range of dental services so your entire family can be seen in one place. Here is a look at what we provide.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487600bb422bace3e85ef_Dental-Implant-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Preventive Dentistry

Preventive care is the foundation of everything we do. Regular exams and cleanings help us catch problems early, protect your enamel, and reduce the risk of decay and gum disease. We tailor our approach to each patient's age and risk level.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f10bb422bace3eb105_dental-implant-crown-crcl.png.webp)

#### Pediatric Dentistry

We help children build healthy habits from their very first visit. Our team is gentle, patient, and experienced at making kids feel comfortable at the dentist. We also monitor jaw and tooth development at every appointment to catch concerns early.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1bbc5ec965ac794f9_Implant-denture-white-circle-copy.png.webp)

#### Emergency Dentistry

Dental emergencies do not keep business hours. We see emergency patients as quickly as possible and work to resolve pain and damage the same day. Call us right away and we will be ready for you.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f10bb422bace3eb105_dental-implant-crown-crcl.png.webp)

#### Crowns and Bridges

Whether you have a cracked tooth, a large cavity, or a missing tooth that needs replacing, we offer custom crowns and bridges in Arvada, CO to restore your smile. We also offer same-day CEREC crowns fabricated in-house so you can skip the temporary and get back to your life.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1e772925a9bfd4bff_Full-Arch2-icon-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Composite Fillings

Composite fillings are the mercury-free, tooth-colored alternative to traditional metal amalgam. They blend naturally with your smile and are placed in a single visit. The result looks natural, feels comfortable, and eliminates the risk of decay caused by cracked or worn metal fillings.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/6999f4e06c51d7bd4df93d32_icons8-dental-veneer-64%20(1).png)

#### Cosmetic Dentistry

Improve the appearance of your smile with cosmetic care from our team. From teeth whitening and veneers to Invisalign and smile makeovers, we help you put your best smile forward. Consultations are always welcome.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1e772925a9bfd4bff_Full-Arch2-icon-white-circle.png.webp)

#### Sleep Apnea Treatment

If you snore at night or feel unrested in the morning, sleep apnea may be the cause. We offer custom oral appliances that help keep your airway open while you sleep. A simple, non-invasive alternative to a CPAP machine for many patients.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699487f1e772925a9bfd4bff_Full-Arch2-icon-white-circle.png.webp)

#### TMJ Treatment

Jaw pain, clicking, headaches, and difficulty chewing can all be signs of a TMJ disorder. We assess the joint and surrounding structures and create a personalized treatment plan to relieve your discomfort and protect your long-term jaw health.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69a4740554a815a4d5068969_dental-implants-in-arvada-co.png)
