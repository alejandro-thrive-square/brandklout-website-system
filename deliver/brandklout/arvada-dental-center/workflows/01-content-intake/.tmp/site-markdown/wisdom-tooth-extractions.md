### What Are Wisdom Teeth?

Wisdom teeth are the third molars that typically emerge in your late teens or early twenties. Most people have four wisdom teeth, one in each corner of the mouth.

When there isn't enough space in your jaw, wisdom teeth can become impacted, grow at wrong angles, or only partially emerge, causing pain and complications.

### Why Remove Wisdom Teeth?

Wisdom tooth extraction prevents pain and protects your oral health from serious complications that develop when these teeth don't have room to grow properly.

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **Prevents Crowding:** Removes teeth that push against neighboring molars, causing misalignment and crowding of your other teeth.
- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **Eliminates Pain:** Relieves the chronic pain, pressure, and discomfort caused by impacted or partially erupted wisdom teeth.

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **Prevents Infection:** Removes partially erupted teeth where bacteria easily accumulate, causing painful infections and gum disease.
- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **Avoids Cysts and Tumors:** Prevents the development of fluid-filled cysts around impacted teeth that can damage jawbone and nerves.

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **Protects Adjacent Teeth:** Stops wisdom teeth from damaging the roots of neighboring molars, which can lead to tooth loss.
- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) **Easier When Younger:** Younger patients heal faster with fewer complications because roots aren't fully developed and bone is less dense.

Early removal prevents future problems and is generally easier and safer than waiting until complications develop.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/6999b044af2db8094d58f827_widom%20tooth%20extraction.webp)

### Am I a Candidate for Wisdom Tooth Extraction?

You may need wisdom tooth extraction if your wisdom teeth are impacted, partially erupted, causing pain, growing at wrong angles, or crowding other teeth. Many dentists recommend removal in late teens or early twenties before problems develop.

Dr. Bennett will examine your wisdom teeth with X-rays to determine if extraction is necessary. Even if your wisdom teeth aren't causing problems now, early removal may prevent future complications.

### What to Expect: The Extraction Process

1

### Consultation and X-Rays

Dr. Bennett examines your wisdom teeth using digital X-rays or cone beam CT imaging to determine their position and the best approach for safe removal.

2

### Extraction Procedure

Under local anesthesia or sedation, Dr. Bennett carefully removes the wisdom teeth. For impacted teeth, a small incision may be made in the gum to access the tooth.

3

### Recovery at Home

You'll rest for a few days while the extraction sites heal. Most patients return to normal activities within a week with proper aftercare.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/6999b04446e1613237af8d87_arvada%20widom%20tooth%20extraction.webp)

### Recovery After Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Recovery typically takes 3 to 7 days. You may experience some swelling, discomfort, and minor bleeding for the first few days. These symptoms are normal and manageable with prescribed or over-the-counter pain medication.

Stick to soft foods, avoid using straws, don't smoke, and gently rinse with salt water after 24 hours. Most patients return to work or school within 2 to 3 days. Dr. Bennett provides detailed aftercare instructions to ensure smooth healing.

### FAQs

Have questions about wisdom tooth extraction in Arvada, Colorado? Here are answers to our most common patient questions. Schedule a consultation today.

How much does wisdom tooth extraction cost in Arvada, Colorado?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) The cost varies depending on how many teeth are removed and whether they are impacted. Simple extractions typically cost $200 to $400 per tooth. Impacted wisdom teeth range from $300 to $600 per tooth. Most dental insurance plans cover 50 to 80 percent of wisdom tooth extraction. We provide a detailed cost estimate after your consultation.

Does wisdom tooth extraction hurt?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) No. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation so you feel no pain during extraction. Some soreness and swelling are normal after the anesthesia wears off, but this is manageable with pain medication.

Should I have all four wisdom teeth removed at once?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Many patients choose to remove all four at once to avoid multiple procedures and recovery periods. Dr. Bennett will recommend the best approach based on your specific case and comfort level.

When should I have my wisdom teeth removed?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) Most dentists recommend removal in your late teens or early twenties before roots are fully developed and complications arise. However, wisdom teeth can be removed at any age if they cause problems.
