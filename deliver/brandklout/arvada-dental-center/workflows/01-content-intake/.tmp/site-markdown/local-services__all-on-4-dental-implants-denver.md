### What Is All-On-4?

All-On-4 is a full-arch implant technique that replaces an entire upper or lower arch of teeth using just four strategically placed implants. Two are positioned vertically at the front of the jaw and two are angled at the back to take maximum advantage of available bone.

The angled placement is what sets All-On-4 apart from conventional implant approaches. It allows most patients with moderate bone loss to qualify without bone grafting, and it creates a stable enough foundation that a temporary full set of teeth can often be placed the very same day as surgery.

### Am I a Candidate for All-On-4?

All-On-4 is well suited for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both arches, patients with several failing teeth, and patients who have been wearing dentures and want a fixed permanent alternative. Most people with moderate bone loss qualify without grafting thanks to the angled implant placement.

Patients with severe upper jaw bone loss may be better served by zygomatic implants, which anchor into the cheekbone rather than the jaw and bypass the need for grafting entirely. Heavy smokers face a significantly higher risk of implant failure and are encouraged to quit before pursuing any implant treatment. A consultation with 3D imaging confirms whether All-On-4 is the right fit for your anatomy.

### Consultation and Planning

Your treatment begins with a comprehensive evaluation and 3D CBCT scan at our Arvada office. ### Advanced Dental Implant Placement

Your four implants are placed in a single surgical procedure performed under sedation. Any remaining failing teeth are extracted at the same appointment if needed. Cone beam imaging guides every placement decision, protecting your anatomy and maximizing stability from day one.

3

### Healing and New Smile Installation

Over the following months, your implants fuse with the jawbone. Once osseointegration is complete, your permanent custom restoration is fabricated and secured in place. Dr. Bennett oversees the final fitting to confirm fit, bite, and aesthetics before you leave.

### Recovery and Aftercare

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c4ab8f20c5a5feb6f9cc1_all%20on%204%20recovery.webp)

The first few days after All-On-4 surgery typically involve swelling, soreness, and fatigue. Prescribed antibiotics and pain medication manage discomfort effectively for most patients. A soft diet is required during the healing period while your implants integrate with the bone.

Most patients are back to light daily activity within a week. Long-term maintenance is simple: brush twice daily, use a water flosser or interdental brush around the implants, and visit Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months for routine checkups. With consistent care, your restoration is built to last for decades.

Do I need bone grafting for All-On-4?
