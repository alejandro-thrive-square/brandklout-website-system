What’s the first thing anyone notices about you? Your smile, of course! And a clean smile makes a great first impression. That’s where veneers come in, a cosmetic dental procedure that helps hide imperfections and natural changes to your teeth that occur over time.

Veneers conceal the gaps, chips, discoloration, and misshapen appearances of your teeth by covering them with a permanent or semi-permanent material, leaving your smile radiant. Like any dental procedure, it take time to install, care for upkeep, and are costly. Hence, you need to consider a few factors before deciding if they’re the right treatment for you — the most important one being your choice of dentist and clinic.

Lucky for you, the dental care in Arvada, Colorado is excellent, and the first choice for many people seeking cosmetic dental procedures which are affordable, safe, and trusted by many in the area. Which means you’re guaranteed a terrific consultation and treatment from our dental practice at the [Arvada Dental Center](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/).

So read below to learn more about veneers, their advantages, the different types, and where you can get the best ones for your teeth.

### **What Are Veneers?**

Veneers are shell-like coverings that envelop the front of your teeth, giving them a clean and aligned appearance. Dental technicians use a fingernail-thin material to make it, usually from tooth-colored porcelain or resin-composite, customized to your teeth and mimicking their exact shape and color.

Of course, it’s arguable that you can get braces, aligners, implants, or teeth whitening done to cover imperfections in your teeth, but such procedures need to be taken in tandem, often costing thousands of dollars at a time and requiring regular maintenance, specific care, and in some cases, discomfort.

That’s why veneers are your best bet to transform the appearance of your teeth quickly, with minimal pain and [an average low cost](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-veneers#cost) compared to other dental procedures.

### **Choosing The Right Veneers For You**

Veneers come in different materials such as porcelain, composite, and no-prep (also known commonly under brand names like Lumineers). So, you can choose between the different types according to your dentist’s recommendations, budget, duration of need, and any other considerations.

Below, we’ll review the different materials so you can pick the best one for your teeth.

#### **Porcelain Veneers**

[Porcelain veneers](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/veneers/) offer the most permanent solution for your teeth, as they can last 10-15 years, offer a strong covering, and are resistant to stains and breakage. Of course, that means porcelain veneers also cost more, averaging around [$925-2500 per tooth](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-veneers#cost).

The process for getting porcelain veneers is also time-consuming, as it involves grinding down some of your tooth’s surface, getting dental impressions, and sending them to a technician to prepare your custom-made veneers. During this time, your dentist will place temporary veneers until the permanent ones are delivered.

#### **Composite Veneers**

Composite veneers are good for a timely and cost-effective covering for your teeth, as they’re usually applied in a single visit to the dentist. However, they have a shorter life span than porcelain veneers (4-8 years) and are less resistant to breakage or discoloration over time.

Dentists will apply a thin layer of the composite resin material over your teeth and finish with a curing light to harden it. Over time, you may have to revisit the dentist to apply additional layers of the resin composite.

#### **No-prep Veneers**

With advancements in medical technology, you can now get a quicker solution to aesthetic imperfections with brands like Lumineers and Vivaneers. These brands offer a porcelain veneer that is mildly invasive in its application and only removes parts of your tooth’s enamel.

In addition, these are also custom-fit to your teeth and are less expensive than traditional porcelain veneers.

No matter which solution you decide on, it all comes down to what your dentist recommends, so make sure to [get a professional opinion and consultation](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/) before getting this cosmetic procedure done.

### **Advantages Of Getting Veneers**

It’s no secret that certain social settings and professions require us to have perfect appearances, especially for celebrities, models, business people, influencers, and public speakers. Although looking ‘perfect’ is subjective and unattainable, it’s also your personal choice to alter physical imperfections. That’s why veneers are a widely used choice for hiding dental flaws and are recommended by dentists worldwide as a safe and sturdy treatment for all ages.

So aside from boosting your confidence and giving you that million-dollar smile, it have the following benefits:

- Veneers are less costly than other dental solutions.
- The process of getting is minimal to mildly invasive.
- They significantly alter your smile’s appearance and look radiant under light.
- They hide several defects like gaps, chips, stains, and breakage.
- They protect your teeth from staining.
- They are virtually unnoticeable (unlike braces) and take the exact appearance of your natural teeth.
- They’re durable and do not require special upkeep.

### **What Should I Know Before Getting Veneers?**

Getting veneers is a medically safe and well-practiced procedure, so there’s no harm or mishap possible in their application. However, while getting a consultation with your dentist, you should have them give a detailed run-down of the procedure and have the following considerations in mind:

- Veneers are a [cosmetic procedure](https://arvadadentalcenter.com/cosmetic-dentistry/), so they’re not covered by insurance.
- Porcelain veneers involve grinding down parts of your enamel and tooth which do not recover over time.
- Composite veneers can get chips and breaks, which require reapplication.
- Many veneers aren’t reversible as their application leaves your teeth unprotected underneath.
- It can make your teeth sensitive to extreme temperatures.
- You won’t be able to bite hard foods like apples, candy, and tough meat from your front teeth with veneers, and using your back teeth is recommended.
- It can fall off or get dislodged with use.

### **The Step-By-Step Process**

You can usually get veneers within 1-2 visits to your dentist and maintain them over time with minimal upkeep. So read the steps below to understand how your first veneers are put in and what it entails.

Dentists and technicians will offer multiple options (such as removable vs. permanent). Still, the procedure for getting them is mostly similar and pretty simple. It starts by getting a regular checkup where your dentist examines your teeth and determines whether they’re fit for getting installed. Next, your dentist recommends which material you will use and follows the procedure accordingly.

You’ll have a piece of enamel removed from the surface of your tooth, and the dentist will take your dental impressions to send to a technician. The technicians make custom veneers using these impressions, which can take up to a few weeks. During this time, you can have temporary veneers put in.

On your next visit, the dentist will align the newly-arrived veneers to your teeth, matching color and size before making adjustments. Finally, they’ll install it using permanent dental cement. Afterward, you check your bite, make any further adjustments, and get instructions for upkeep.

Fortunately, it don’t require any special care; as long as you brush twice a day (don’t overbrush), floss, and have regular checkups, it will last a good while. However, the process can be somewhat painful depending on which procedure you get, so you’ll also receive anesthesia, which can take a while to wear off.

### **Where Should I Get Veneers?**

Veneers are a long-term and costly investment, so you’ll only want the best professionals to do yours. Most dentists offer this cosmetic procedure alongside their other services, so you can easily get a consultation for veneers on your regular appointments.

However, it’s best to consider all the options in your area before deciding on one, and that’s where we come in to help you decide. You’ll want to pick a clinic that has done:

- Multiple veneer applications successfully.
- Offers a range of veneer materials to choose from.
- Has positive testimonies of the application process and staff treatment.
- Is within your budget.
- And offers a good maintenance plan.
