### What Is a Zirconia Fixed Bridge?

A zirconia fixed bridge is a full-arch prosthetic made from zirconia, a high-strength ceramic material known for its durability and natural appearance. It is permanently anchored to dental implants and cannot be removed, functioning exactly like a natural set of teeth.

Unlike removable dentures, a zirconia fixed bridge does not shift, slip, or require adhesive. It stimulates the jawbone through the implants it attaches to, preventing the bone loss that follows tooth removal. For Westminster patients looking for a lifelong solution to full-arch tooth loss, it is one of the most advanced options available today.

### Am I a Candidate for a Zirconia Fixed Bridge?

Westminster patients who are the best fit for a zirconia fixed bridge have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both arches and want a permanent, fixed solution. Adequate bone density is needed to support the implants that anchor the bridge. Patients with insufficient bone may require a bone graft or sinus lift beforehand, or may be candidates for zygomatic implants depending on the severity of their bone loss.

Good overall health is important as the process involves surgery. Smokers are strongly encouraged to quit before treatment as smoking significantly increases the risk of implant failure. A consultation with 3D imaging at our Arvada office is the most accurate way to confirm candidacy and map out the right treatment plan for Westminster patients.

### What to Expect: The Zirconia Fixed Bridge Process

1

### Consultation and Planning

Your treatment begins with a comprehensive evaluation and 3D CBCT imaging at our Arvada office. We assess your bone volume, bite, and overall oral health and build a fully customized treatment plan. Westminster patients leave this visit with a clear picture of the timeline, process, and total cost.

2

### Implant Placement

Implants are placed surgically under local anesthesia with sedation available. Any remaining failing teeth are extracted at the same appointment if needed. The number and position of implants are determined by your 3D imaging and the design of your bridge.

3

### Healing and Osseointegration

Over the following months, your implants fuse with the jawbone through osseointegration. Follow-up visits at our Arvada office monitor your progress and ensure healing is on track. A temporary restoration keeps you functional and presentable throughout this phase.

4

### Final Bridge Fabrication

Once your implants have fully integrated, precise impressions and digital scans are taken and sent to the dental lab. Your zirconia fixed bridge is custom-fabricated to fit your bite and match the natural appearance of your surrounding tissue.

5

### Placement and Final Adjustments

Your permanent bridge is secured to your implants and checked for fit, bite, and comfort. Minor adjustments are made until everything feels exactly right. Westminster patients leave with a fully functional, permanent smile.

### Recovery and Aftercare

Following implant surgery, Westminster patients can expect mild swelling, soreness, and fatigue for the first several days. Prescribed antibiotics and pain medication are standard and manage discomfort effectively for most patients. A soft diet is required during the osseointegration period.

Most patients return to normal light activity within a few days. Long-term care is straightforward: brush twice daily, floss with a water flosser or interdental brush, and schedule routine checkups at Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months. With proper care, your zirconia fixed bridge is built to last for decades.
