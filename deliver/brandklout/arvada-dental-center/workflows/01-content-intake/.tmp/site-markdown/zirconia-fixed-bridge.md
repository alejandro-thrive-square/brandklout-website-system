### What Is a Zirconia Fixed Bridge?

A zirconia fixed bridge is a permanent, full-arch dental restoration supported by strategically placed implants. Unlike removable dentures, it is anchored into your jawbone and never comes out, giving you a completely stable, natural-feeling result.

Zirconia is a high-strength, metal-free ceramic material that mimics the translucency and durability of natural tooth enamel. It is bio-compatible, resistant to chipping, and custom-shaded to match your ideal smile — making it the most lifelike full-arch option available.

### Am I a Candidate for a Zirconia Fixed Bridge?

Ideal candidates are patients who have lost most or all of their teeth on one or both arches and are looking for a permanent, fixed solution. You should be in reasonably good health, a non-smoker or willing to quit, and have adequate jawbone density to support implants. Patients who are unhappy with existing dentures or hybrid restorations are also excellent candidates.

Some patients may need preparatory procedures such as bone grafting or tooth extractions before implant placement can begin. At your consultation, our doctors will evaluate your bone structure and overall oral health to determine the best path forward for you. Age alone is not a disqualifying factor — we regularly treat patients in their 60s, 70s, and beyond.

### What to Expect: The Zirconia Fixed Bridge Process

1

### Consultation and Planning

Your journey begins with a thorough exam, including 3D CBCT imaging, to assess your bone density, gum health, and bite. Our doctors review your full medical and dental history and develop a customized treatment plan tailored to your goals.

2

### Implant Placement

Under local anesthesia or sedation, your implants are precisely placed into the jawbone. The number of implants varies based on your bone structure and which arch is being restored.

3

### Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next several months, your implants fuse with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. You will wear a temporary restoration during this period so you are never without teeth.

4

### Final Bridge Fabrication

Once your implants are fully integrated, detailed impressions or digital scans are taken. Your final zirconia bridge is custom-milled and hand-finished to achieve the ideal fit, color, and shape.

5

### Placement and Final Adjustments

Your permanent bridge is secured to the implants and checked for fit, bite, and aesthetics. Final adjustments are made to ensure everything feels natural and comfortable before you leave our office.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c45e5075f9153d00970c4_fixed%20bridge%20Recovery%20and%20Aftercare.webp)

### Recovery and Aftercare

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699c45e5075f9153d00970c4_fixed%20bridge%20Recovery%20and%20Aftercare.webp)

Following implant placement, most patients experience mild swelling, soreness, and fatigue for the first few days. These symptoms are manageable with prescribed or over-the-counter pain medication and typically resolve within a week. You will follow a soft diet for several weeks while your implants heal. Full recovery and final bridge placement typically occur within three to six months, depending on your healing timeline. Once your permanent bridge is in place, caring for it is simple: brush twice daily, floss with an interdental brush or water flosser, and attend routine checkups at Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months.

How is a zirconia fixed bridge different from All-On-4?

- ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/69fc01ff84943f23a7e399c0_check-svgrepo-com%20(1).svg) All-On-4 is a technique that uses four strategically angled implants to support a full-arch restoration, and it can be paired with a zirconia bridge as the final prosthetic. A zirconia fixed bridge refers to the material and type of the prosthetic itself — it is permanently attached and cannot be removed.
