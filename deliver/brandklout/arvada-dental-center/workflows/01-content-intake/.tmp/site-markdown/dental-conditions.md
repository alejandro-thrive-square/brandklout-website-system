Loose Dentures

**Condition**:
Loose or ill-fitting dentures can make eating and speaking uncomfortable and affect your confidence every day. Over time, the jawbone beneath a denture shrinks from disuse, causing even well-fitted dentures to loosen and shift. This can lead to sore spots, irritation, and difficulty eating.
‍
**How We Fix It**:
We offer implant-supported dentures and [All-On-4](/all-on-4-dental-implants) full-arch restorations that replace your loose denture with a permanently stable solution anchored in the jawbone. If you are not ready for implants, we can also evaluate refitting or relining options to improve your current denture's fit.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699e501a9febdd02352febdd_gum%20disease%20at%20arvada%20dental.webp)

Gum Disease

**Condition**:
Gum disease ranges from early-stage gingivitis, which causes redness, swelling, and bleeding, to advanced periodontitis that destroys the bone and tissue supporting your teeth. It is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults and has been linked to serious systemic health conditions including cardiovascular disease and diabetes.
‍
**How We Fix It**:
We provide professional deep cleaning, scaling and root planing, and ongoing gum maintenance to stop disease progression and restore gum health. In more advanced cases, surgical intervention may be needed. We also emphasize consistent professional monitoring to keep gum disease from returning.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699e4fe9a13a6b84b0b3df1f_bad%20breath%20soulotion.webp)

Bad Breath

**Condition**:
Persistent bad breath, known as halitosis, is usually a sign of an underlying oral health issue rather than just morning breath or something a breath mint can solve. Common causes include gum disease, dry mouth, bacteria on the tongue, tooth decay, and poorly cleaned dental restorations.
‍
**How We Fix It**:
We identify the root cause of your bad breath through a thorough exam and treat it directly. This may include a professional cleaning, [gum treatment](/gum-disease-treatment), cavity treatment, or advice on improving your at-home hygiene routine. Addressing the source is the only lasting solution.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699e559cbdd3163a371ea038_Gummy%20Smile.webp)

Gummy Smile

**Condition**:
A gummy smile occurs when excess gum tissue covers too much of the tooth crown, making teeth appear shorter than they are and creating a disproportionate gum-to-tooth ratio. It can affect the overall harmony and aesthetics of your smile significantly, even when your teeth are healthy and straight.
‍
**How We Fix It**:
Crown lengthening is a precise procedure that reshapes the gumline to reveal more of the natural tooth surface. We contour the gum tissue symmetrically to create a more balanced, proportionate smile. In some cases this can be completed in a single appointment with minimal downtime.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699b428560e66956c6c0ea47_gum%20disease%20signs.webp)

Bleeding Gums

**Condition**:
Gums that bleed when you brush or floss are one of the earliest warning signs of gum disease. Many patients assume bleeding is normal or caused by brushing too hard, but healthy gums should not bleed. Left untreated, the underlying inflammation can progress to more serious periodontal disease and eventual tooth loss.
‍
**How We Fix It**:
A professional cleaning removes the bacteria and tartar buildup that causes gum inflammation. We then assess the depth of any gum pockets to determine whether additional periodontal treatment is needed. Catching the problem early leads to faster, easier recovery and protects your long-term oral health.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699e50652ca327082f02f95b_missing%20or%20failing%20teeth.webp)

Missing or Failing Teeth

**Condition**:
Missing or failing teeth affect far more than your appearance. They make eating difficult, shift surrounding teeth out of alignment, and trigger jawbone loss that changes the shape of your face over time. The longer missing teeth go unreplaced, the more complex and costly the eventual solution becomes.
‍
**How We Fix It**:
We offer the full range of tooth replacement solutions including single dental implants, implant-supported bridges, All-On-4 full-arch restorations, [zygomatic implants](/zygomatic-implants) for patients with severe bone loss, and[implant-supported dentures](/implant-supported-dentures). Dr. Bennett will present every option available to your specific situation at your consultation.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699e52958df95fe9351a6dff_cracked%20tooth.webp)

Cracked Tooth

**Condition**:
A cracked tooth can range from a minor surface fracture to a deep crack that extends into the root. Symptoms include sharp pain when biting, sensitivity to temperature, and discomfort that comes and goes unpredictably. If left untreated, a crack can worsen and lead to infection, nerve damage, or tooth loss.
‍
**How We Fix It**:
Treatment depends on the location and severity of the crack. Options include a [dental crown](/cerec-same-day-crowns) to protect and stabilize the tooth, a dental bonding repair for minor surface cracks, [root canal therapy](/root-canal-therapy) if the nerve is involved, or extraction followed by implant placement in severe cases. Early treatment almost always leads to simpler, less costly solutions.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699e5214483a29b012c3e9eb_toothache.webp)

Toothache

**Condition**:
Toothaches range from mild sensitivity to severe, throbbing pain that disrupts sleep and daily life. Common causes include cavities, cracked teeth, gum infections, exposed roots, and abscesses. A toothache is rarely something that resolves on its own and usually signals a problem that needs professional attention promptly.
‍
**How We Fix It**:
We perform a thorough exam and [X-rays](/panoramic-dental-x-rays)to identify the source of your pain and provide the most conservative effective treatment. Depending on the cause, this may include a filling, crown, root canal therapy, gum treatment, or extraction. We also offer emergency appointments for patients in acute pain who need to be seen quickly.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699e53d05fe3ec9b1c082428_Crooked%20Teeth.webp)

Crooked Teeth

**Condition**:
Crooked or crowded teeth are not just a cosmetic concern. Misaligned teeth are harder to clean properly, increasing the risk of decay and gum disease. They can also cause jaw pain, uneven bite pressure, and accelerated wear on certain teeth over time. Many patients live with crooked teeth simply because they do not realize how treatable the condition is.
‍
**How We Fix It**:
We offer [Invisalign clear aligner](/clear-aligner-specialist-for-invisalign-braces-colorado) therapy for most cases of crowding, spacing, and mild to moderate bite misalignment. Invisalign is removable, virtually invisible, and allows you to maintain normal oral hygiene throughout treatment. For more complex cases, we will discuss all orthodontic options available and refer to a specialist if needed.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/693c51e685b19f0d0c149cab/699e53662efb44b9ef7cff84_yellow%20teeth.webp)

Yellow or Discolored Teeth

**Condition**:
Tooth discoloration can be caused by foods and drinks like coffee, tea, and red wine, by smoking, by certain medications, or simply by natural aging as enamel thins and the yellower dentin beneath becomes more visible. Surface stains respond well to whitening while deeper discoloration may require a different approach.
‍
**How We Fix It**:
We offer professional in-office [teeth whitening](/teeth-whitening)that brightens teeth multiple shades in a single session, penetrating deeper than any over-the-counter product. For discoloration that does not respond to whitening, porcelain veneers provide a permanent, stain-resistant solution that covers the front surface of the tooth for a completely transformed smile.
