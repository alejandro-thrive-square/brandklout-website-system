### What Is OralID Oral Cancer Screening?

OralID is an FDA-cleared oral cancer screening device that uses blue fluorescent light to identify abnormal tissue in your mouth. It detects pre-cancerous cells and early-stage oral cancer that may not be visible during a standard visual examination.

Unlike other screening methods that require rinses or dyes, OralID is quick and non-invasive. The screening takes approximately 2 minutes and requires no needles, no uncomfortable liquids, and no waiting for results.

### Who Should Get OralID Screening?

Everyone age 18 and older should receive annual oral cancer screening. You may need more frequent screening if you have any traditional risk factors such as smoking, heavy alcohol use, previous history of oral cancer, or use of smokeless tobacco. However, 40 percent of oral cancer cases occur in patients with no risk factors.

OralID screening is quick, painless, and often included in your regular comprehensive dental exam. There is no reason not to be screened. Early detection dramatically improves treatment outcomes and saves lives.
