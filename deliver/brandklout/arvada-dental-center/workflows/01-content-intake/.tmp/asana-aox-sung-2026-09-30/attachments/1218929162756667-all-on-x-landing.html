From first call to final smile

Two main visits with healing in between. You’re never without teeth at any stage.

  1. Free consultation and 3D scan

    Your first visit

    Dr. Bennett examines you, takes a cone beam CT scan and tells you plainly what’s possible. You leave with a written plan and price, and no pressure to book that day.

  2. Surgery day

    One appointment, under sedation

    Any failing teeth are removed and four or more implants are placed from a guided plan. Most patients go home the same day with fixed temporary teeth.

  3. Healing

    About 3 to 5 months

    Expect mild soreness for three to five days and a soft diet at first. We check in as the implants fuse with your jawbone.

  4. Your final zirconia arch

    After healing

    Your permanent arch is finished in the practice’s in-house lab, matched to your face and smile, and fitted by Dr. Bennett.

Questions people ask before they call

Will I ever be without teeth?
No. Extractions and implant placement happen in the same visit, and most patients go home with fixed temporary teeth that day. Your permanent zirconia arch goes in after three to five months of healing.
Some offices advertise permanent teeth in 24 hours. Why is my final arch made later?
Your gums and bone change shape as they heal after extractions and implant placement. Making your final zirconia arch after that healing means it’s fitted to your mouth as it will be long-term. Until then you wear fixed temporary teeth, so you’re never without a smile.
Does it hurt?
Dr. Bennett gives IV sedation himself, so you’re comfortable and relaxed during surgery. Most patients have mild soreness and swelling for three to five days, managed with medication.
I’ve been told I don’t have enough bone. Can I still get implants?
Often, yes. Implants can be angled to use the bone you have, and Dr. Bennett decides how many to place from your 3D scan. For severe upper-jaw bone loss, zygomatic implants are another option. The scan at your free consultation gives you a real answer.
What does $18,795 include?
Your exam, 3D CT scan, any extractions, four to six implants, same-day fixed temporary teeth and your final zirconia arch. Bone grafting and IV sedation are priced separately, and only if you need them. You’ll have the full number in writing before you commit to anything.
Can I pay monthly?
Yes. With approved financing, one arch starts at about $235 a month. The practice works with CareCredit, Cherry, Sunbit, PatientFi, Proceed Finance and LendingClub, and your coordinator will talk through options at your consultation.
Will my insurance help?
Some dental plans contribute toward implant treatment. The team checks your benefits before treatment and applies whatever your plan covers.
Am I too old for this?
There’s no upper age limit. Patients in their 70s and 80s have this treatment regularly. Your general health and bone matter far more than your age.
How long does it last?
With good care and regular checkups, the implants are designed to last decades. The zirconia arch may need an evaluation after 15 to 20 years of normal wear.
