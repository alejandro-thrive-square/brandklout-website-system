Common Questions

Porcelain Veneers: Your Questions, Answered

Everything Arvada, Wheat Ridge, Lakewood, and Denver Metro patients ask before booking their smile consultation.

How much do porcelain veneers cost in Arvada, CO? +
Porcelain veneer costs depend on how many teeth you treat and your case complexity. At Arvada Dental Center, low monthly payment plans are available through 10+ lending partners, starting around $105/month for 4 veneers, $185/month for 8 veneers, and $230/month for a full 10-veneer smile makeover. Nationally, porcelain veneers typically run about $900–$2,500 per tooth. The most accurate number comes from a complimentary cosmetic consultation with Dr. Bennett.
How long do porcelain veneers last? +
With proper care and regular dental checkups, porcelain veneers can last 10 to 20 years or more. Porcelain resists staining far better than composite bonding, so your smile stays bright for the long haul.
Do veneers ruin your natural teeth? +
No. Modern porcelain veneers are a minimal-prep treatment — Dr. Bennett removes only a thin layer (often less than a millimeter) of enamel from the front of the tooth to make room for your custom shell. The healthy tooth structure underneath stays intact and protected.
What's the difference between porcelain veneers and composite bonding? +
Porcelain veneers are custom lab-fabricated shells that mimic natural enamel translucency and typically last 10–20+ years while resisting stains. Composite bonding is sculpted directly onto the tooth in one visit, costs less upfront, but stains more easily and usually needs redoing every 4–8 years. Veneers are ideal for a dramatic, long-lasting makeover; bonding suits small, budget-friendly touch-ups.
Am I a good candidate for veneers? +
Most patients hoping to improve chipped, stained, gapped, uneven, or slightly misaligned teeth are excellent candidates. You'll need reasonably healthy teeth and gums — patients with active gum disease, significant decay, or teeth grinding may need those addressed first. Dr. Bennett will confirm candidacy at your consultation.
How many appointments do porcelain veneers take? +
Most patients complete their transformation in as few as two visits: the first for digital smile design, minimal prep, and shade matching (with temporary veneers placed), and the second for bonding your final, permanent porcelain veneers.
