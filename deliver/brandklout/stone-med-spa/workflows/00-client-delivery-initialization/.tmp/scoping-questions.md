## Draft message

Hi — the Surgical Arts site is built and on a staging link. Before we start the med spa we need
four things answered, and the first one is genuinely blocking.

**1. Surgical Arts is already selling the entire med spa menu. Which brand owns it?**

The design Dr. Mona approved has four categories and two of them are *Skin* and *Non-Surgical*. The
treatments page we built from it now carries a published fee schedule for Botox and Xeomin, dermal
filler, Sculptra, Morpheus8, FaceTite, CO2 laser resurfacing, HydraFacial MD, microneedling,
SkinPen, chemical peels, Cosmelan and PRP, plus hair restoration.

That is the whole product a med spa sells. So one of three things has to be true, and we cannot
choose for you:

- **The med spa is a second site competing with the first** for the same searches and patients. We
  would advise against it.
- **We move the non-surgical menu out of Surgical Arts** into the med spa. That is the cleanest
  split, and it means editing a site the client has already reviewed.
- **The med spa is a different proposition** — memberships, skin maintenance, wellness, weight loss
  — that shares a treatment room but not a menu. If so, tell us what it sells.

We flagged this overlap back in August. It was a planning question then. It is a blocking one now
because the surgical site has published.

**2. Does the brand exist, and is identity ours?**

Asana says *Stone Med Spa*. Instagram says *Westlake Medical Aesthetics*. We cannot find a logo, a
palette or a type system for either. A med spa and a surgical practice should not share one visual
identity, so if identity work is ours it has to finish before design starts and it moves every date
after it. Which name is the brand, and does anything visual already exist?

**3. What domain?**

The med spa has none. `stonesurgicalarts.com` currently resolves to the implant centre and the
Surgical Arts build is already waiting on it, so that one is spoken for. Who controls DNS?

**4. The archived content, and the patient photos.**

The old `stonesurgicalarts.com` had real pages for most of the non-surgical treatments and we can
recover the copy. It also had fifteen before-and-after galleries of real patients — botox, lip
fillers, CO2 laser, hair restoration. **We will not republish patient photography pulled from a web
archive.** Can the client supply the originals with current written consent per case, or should we
build without before-and-afters?

The task is due 3 September. We can hit that date with a scope decision and a page list. We cannot
hit it with a website, because nothing has been specified yet.
