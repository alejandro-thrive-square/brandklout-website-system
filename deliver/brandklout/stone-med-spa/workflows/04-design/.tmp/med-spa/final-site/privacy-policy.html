Westlake Medical Aesthetics (“we,” “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle the personal information you share with us through our website, westlaketxmedspa.com (the “Site”), and when you contact us by phone or email.

This policy covers information collected through the Site. Information you give us as a patient while receiving care is governed by our separate Notice of Privacy Practices under HIPAA. You can get a copy at our office or by asking us.

01Information we collect

Information you give us

We collect the information you choose to give us, for example when you:

  • Request a consultation, in person or by video
  • Ask about a treatment, our membership or financing
  • Send us photos ahead of a virtual consultation
  • Fill in patient forms before your first visit
  • Call us, email us or message us on social media

This can include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, date of birth, the concerns and goals you describe, and anything else you decide to tell us.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect some information about your device and how you use the Site, including:

  • Your IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type, operating system and device identifiers
  • Pages visited, time spent on them and the page that sent you here
  • Information from cookies and similar technologies (see section 07)

Health-related information

If you send photos of your face or skin, or describe a concern, we handle that information with the same care as any patient health information. Please don’t send detailed medical histories or other sensitive health information through website forms, social media messages or unencrypted email. For anything sensitive, call us on (817) 674-2061.

02How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Answer your questions and schedule consultations and appointments
  • Assess whether a treatment suits you, prepare your treatment plan and coordinate your care
  • Provide the treatments you ask for
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders, aftercare instructions and follow-ups
  • Set up and manage your membership, if you join
  • Take payments and help you with financing options
  • Send news about treatments and offers, but only if you have agreed to receive it
  • Improve the Site, our services and your experience with us
  • Meet our legal, regulatory and professional obligations

03Text messages

Our contact form has a checkbox for text messages. If you tick it and give us your mobile number, you agree to receive text messages from us at that number. Ticking it is not a condition of being treated here, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

The messages we send are:

  • About your appointment: confirmations, reminders, rescheduling, preparation notes and aftercare follow-ups.
  • Replies: answers from our team to a question you have asked.
  • News and offers: only if you have agreed to receive them.

How often we text depends on your appointments and on what you ask us. Your mobile carrier’s message and data rates may apply; we don’t charge for the messages themselves.

To stop: reply STOP to any message from us. We will send one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and then stop. For help: reply HELP, or call us on (817) 674-2061.

We do not sell or share your mobile number or your consent to be texted with anyone for their own marketing. We share it only with the providers that send the messages for us, and only so the message reaches you.

Text messages are not a secure channel. Please don’t send health, financial or other sensitive information by text.

04How we share your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when we need to run the practice and look after you, including with:

  • Service providers that support the Site, our contact form, scheduling, communications, payments and analytics, under agreements that limit how they can use your information
  • Financing companies, when you ask to apply for financing through them
  • Other healthcare providers involved in your care, including a practice we refer you to, if you ask us to or agree to it
  • Legal and regulatory authorities, when the law or a subpoena requires it, or to protect rights, property or safety

If the practice is ever sold, merged or restructured, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, as the law allows.

05Third-party services

Some features of the Site are provided by other companies. These may include:

  • Our booking and contact form, and the customer relationship system behind it, provided by Go High Level (LeadConnector). What you type into that form is sent to them and stored there for us.
  • Financing providers such as Cherry and CareCredit, whose applications you complete on their own websites
  • Google Maps, for directions to our office
  • Google Business Profile, where our patient reviews are published
  • Instagram and TikTok, where we post updates
  • A video platform for virtual consultations
  • Web analytics services, such as Google Analytics, that show us how visitors use the Site

These companies may collect information about you under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.

06Membership and payments

If you join our membership, we collect the details needed to enrol you, charge the recurring payment and keep your account up to date. Card payments are handled by our payment processor, and we don’t store full card numbers on the Site. The terms of your membership are set out in the membership agreement you sign.

07Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small data file saved on your device when you visit a website. We and our service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep the Site working and secure
  • Remember your preferences
  • Understand how visitors find and use the Site

You can control cookies in your browser settings. If you turn them off, parts of the Site may not work properly.

08Your choices and rights

  • Stop marketing emails by clicking “unsubscribe” in any email, or by contacting us.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us (see section 13).
  • Change your cookie settings in your browser.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under laws such as the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act or the California Consumer Privacy Act. To use those rights, contact us. We will reply within the time the law requires, and we will not treat you differently for using them.

Your medical record is governed by HIPAA and Texas law, which may limit our ability to delete or change clinical records.

09How we protect your information

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your personal information. However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. To protect yourself, please don’t send confidential health information through website forms, social media or unencrypted email.

10How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, to meet our legal and professional obligations, to resolve disputes and to enforce our agreements. Medical records are kept for as long as Texas law and the Texas Medical Board require.

11Children’s privacy

The Site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent us information through the Site, contact us and we will delete it.

12Links to other websites

The Site links to websites we don’t control, including our social media profiles, review sites, our financing partners and Stone Surgical Arts, a separate practice. We are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices, so please read their policies before you share information with them.
