What it is

Morpheus8 uses a handpiece with very fine needles. As the needles enter the skin, they release radiofrequency energy into the deeper layers. The heat and the tiny channels together prompt your skin to repair itself and make new collagen.

Most of the energy lands below the surface, so the top layer stays largely intact. You'll see some change within a few weeks. It keeps improving for about three months.

What it can do for you

Morpheus8 works on the deeper layers, where laxity and scarring live.

  • Loose skin along the jawline and neck looks firmer.
  • Acne scars become shallower and less noticeable.
  • Fine lines and crepey texture smooth out.
  • Enlarged pores look smaller.

Is it right for me?

Morpheus8 suits most skin types and tones, which is one reason it's popular. It's a good fit for mild to moderate laxity. More significant loose skin may need a surgical option, and we'll tell you honestly if that's the case.

Not recommended if

You're pregnant or breastfeeding, have an active infection, cold sore or acne flare in the area, have taken isotretinoin in the last six months, have a pacemaker or metal implants near the area, or tend to form keloid scars.

Who does your treatment

Performed by our medical providers.

Morpheus8 is done by one of our medical providers, who sets the depth and energy for each area of your face. You'll have numbing cream first, and we'll check in with you throughout.

Before and after your appointment

A few simple guidelines.

The two weeks before

  1. Avoid tanning and strong sun exposure.
  2. Stop retinoids and exfoliating acids about five days before.
  3. If you get cold sores, tell us. You may need an antiviral first.
  4. Come with clean skin, and allow time for numbing cream before we start.

The first few days after

  1. Expect redness and swelling, like a sunburn, for two to four days.
  2. Use only the gentle cleanser and moisturizer we recommend.
  3. Wait a day before wearing makeup.
  4. Skip workouts, saunas and swimming for 48 hours.
  5. Wear sunscreen every day while your skin heals.

Questions

The ones we hear most.

Does Morpheus8 hurt?

We apply numbing cream first. Most people feel warmth and pressure, and some areas are more sensitive than others.

How many Morpheus8 treatments does it take?

Usually three, about four to six weeks apart. After that, one session a year helps keep the result.

When will I see a difference?

Some change shows at about three weeks. Collagen keeps building, so it continues to improve for around three months.

Is it safe for darker skin?

Morpheus8 is generally suitable for all skin tones because most of the energy goes below the surface. Your provider will adjust the settings for your skin.

Can I go back to work the next day?

Many people do. Your skin will look pink and a little rough for a few days, which some prefer to wait out at home.
