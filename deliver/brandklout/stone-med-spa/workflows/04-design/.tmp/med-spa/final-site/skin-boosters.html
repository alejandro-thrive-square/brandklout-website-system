What it is

Boosters are concentrated serums added to a HydraFacial. Each one targets a specific concern, such as dullness, uneven tone or dryness. They're infused during the hydration step of your facial.

They're not injections. Nothing goes under the skin with a needle. Your aesthetician picks the booster based on how your skin looks on the day.

What it can do for you

A booster lets the same facial do a little more for one concern.

  • Brightening boosters help dull or uneven skin.
  • Firming boosters help skin feel more supple.
  • Hydrating boosters help dry or tight skin.
  • Your booster can change from visit to visit as your skin changes.

Is it right for me?

If you can have a HydraFacial, you can almost always have a booster. Your aesthetician will suggest one only if it suits your skin that day.

Not recommended if

You have an active rash, sunburn or infection on your face, or you're allergic to an ingredient in the serum. Tell us if you're pregnant or breastfeeding so we can choose a suitable booster.

Who does your treatment

Chosen by our medical aesthetician.

Abby, our medical aesthetician and senior laser technician, looks at your skin at the start of your facial. She'll explain which booster she'd add and why.

Before and after your appointment

A few simple guidelines.

The days before

  1. Stop retinoids and exfoliating acids about two days before.
  2. Avoid waxing or other facial hair removal for a few days.
  3. Tell us about any new skincare or allergies.

The 24 hours after

  1. Leave the serum on. Skip washing your face until the evening.
  2. Skip makeup for a few hours if you can.
  3. Avoid heavy exfoliants and retinoids for a day or two.
  4. Wear sunscreen, as you would any day.

Questions

The ones we hear most.

Are skin boosters injections?

Not here. Our boosters are serums added to your HydraFacial. Nothing is injected.

Do I need a separate appointment for a skin booster?

No. The booster is added during your HydraFacial, so it fits in the same visit.

Which skin booster should I choose?

Tell us what's bothering your skin. Your aesthetician will suggest one after looking at it.

Can I use a different skin booster each time?

Yes. Many people change boosters with the seasons or as their skin changes.
