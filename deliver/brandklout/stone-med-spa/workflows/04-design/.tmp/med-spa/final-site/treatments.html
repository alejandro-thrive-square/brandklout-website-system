II

Skin & Resurfacing

Controlled injury, then repair. These treatments work by asking the skin to rebuild itself, which is why they run as a series and improve for months after the last one.

04 Morpheus8 Appointment45–60 min Shows3 weeks, building to 3 months Lasts12+ months Downtime2–4 days

Radiofrequency delivered below the surface through microneedles, so the heat lands in the layer that holds the jawline and the surface is left largely intact.

Placed
Lower face, jawline, neck
Addresses
Laxity & jawline, Texture & pores, Acne scarring
Cadence
3, then yearly

III

Restoration

Your own platelets, concentrated and returned to the tissue that needs them. The slowest results on the menu and the longest-lasting.

06 PRP Treatment Appointment45–60 min Shows4–6 weeks LastsA series of 3, then maintenance Downtime1–2 days

Your blood is drawn, spun to concentrate the platelets, and the plasma is returned to the tissue. Nothing foreign goes in, which is why it is the treatment for people who will not have filler.

Placed
Under-eye, face, scalp
Addresses
Texture & pores, Hair thinning
Cadence
3, then 2 a year
