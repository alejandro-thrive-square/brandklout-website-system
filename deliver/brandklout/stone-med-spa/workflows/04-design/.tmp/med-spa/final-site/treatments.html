Treatments

Our treatments.

Non-invasive treatments that soften lines, restore volume and help your skin make more of its own collagen, so you look your best without surgery. Open any treatment to see how long it takes, when you'll see results and how long they last.

Start with your concern

What would you like to improve?

  • Fine lines & wrinklesCreases from expression and time, around the eyes, forehead and mouth. Treated with Botox, Xeomin, Dysport, Jeuveau, SkinPen Microneedling, Skin Boosters.
  • Volume lossHollowing and flattening in the cheeks, temples and lips. Treated with Dermal Fillers, Sculptra.
  • Texture & poresRough, uneven skin and enlarged pores. Treated with Sculptra, Morpheus8, Chemical Peels, SkinPen Microneedling, HydraFacial, Dermaplaning, PRP Treatment.
  • Pigmentation & toneSun spots, uneven tone and discoloration. Treated with Chemical Peels, HydraFacial, Skin Boosters.
  • Laxity & jawlineLoosening skin and a softer jawline as collagen declines. Treated with Dermal Fillers, Sculptra, Morpheus8.
  • Acne scarringPitted or uneven marks left behind by acne. Treated with Morpheus8, SkinPen Microneedling.
  • Dryness & dullnessDry, tired-looking skin that has lost its glow. Treated with Chemical Peels, HydraFacial, Dermaplaning, Skin Boosters.
  • Hair thinningThinning hair on the scalp, in men and women. Treated with PRP Treatment, Hair Restoration.

I

Injectables

Neuromodulators soften the lines that come from movement, fillers restore volume, and biostimulators like Sculptra help your skin build its own collagen over time. Every injection is done by our nurse practitioner.

01 Botox Appointment15–20 min Shows3–7 days Lasts3–4 months DowntimeNone

Botox relaxes the muscles that crease the skin, softening forehead lines, frown lines and crow's feet. It has the longest track record of the four and is very predictable in small areas.

Placed
Forehead, frown lines, crow's feet
Addresses
Fine lines & wrinkles
Cadence
every 3–4 months

Biostimulators

06 Sculptra Appointment30–45 min Shows6–8 weeks, building LastsUp to 2 years Downtime1–2 days

Sculptra is a biostimulator. Instead of filling a space, it prompts your skin to rebuild collagen, so results appear gradually over a few months and can last two years or more.

Placed
Cheeks, temples, jawline
Addresses
Volume loss, Laxity & jawline, Texture & pores
Cadence
2–3 sessions, then every 2 years

II

Skin Rejuvenation

These treatments prompt your skin to repair and renew itself. Most are done as a short series, and results keep improving for a few months after the last session.

07 Morpheus8 Appointment45–60 min Shows3 weeks, building to 3 months Lasts12+ months Downtime2–4 days

Radiofrequency energy delivered through fine microneedles tightens and smooths the deeper layers of the skin while leaving the surface largely intact. It works well on the jawline, the neck and acne scarring.

Placed
Face, jawline, neck
Addresses
Laxity & jawline, Texture & pores, Acne scarring
Cadence
3 sessions, then yearly
Read the full page → Ask about this
08 Chemical Peels Appointment30–45 min Shows5–7 days LastsMonths, as a series Downtime0–7 days

A peel lifts away damaged surface skin to even out tone, fade sun spots and refine texture. Light peels have almost no downtime, deeper ones need about a week, and we'll help you pick one that fits your schedule.

Placed
Face, neck, chest, hands
Addresses
Pigmentation & tone, Texture & pores, Dryness & dullness
Cadence
2–4 a year
Read the full page → Ask about this
09 SkinPen Microneedling Appointment45–60 min Shows2–4 weeks LastsMonths, as a series Downtime1–3 days of redness

SkinPen creates thousands of tiny channels in the skin, which starts its natural repair process and boosts collagen. It's a good fit for acne scars, enlarged pores and early fine lines.

Placed
Face, neck
Addresses
Texture & pores, Acne scarring, Fine lines & wrinkles
Cadence
3–4 sessions, a month apart

IV

Restoration

PRP uses a concentrate of your own platelets to support skin and hair. Results come on gradually, and nothing synthetic is involved.

13 PRP Treatment Appointment45–60 min Shows4–6 weeks LastsA series of 3, then maintenance Downtime1–2 days

A small amount of your blood is drawn and spun to concentrate the platelets, which are then returned to the skin. It's a good choice if you'd rather avoid fillers or synthetic products.

Placed
Under-eyes, face, scalp
Addresses
Texture & pores, Hair thinning
Cadence
3 sessions, then 2 a year
Read the full page → Ask about this
14 Hair Restoration Appointment45–60 min Shows3–4 months LastsMaintenance every 6–12 months DowntimeMinimal

PRP injected into the scalp to support thinning hair. It works best on follicles that are still active, so starting early tends to give better results.

Placed
Crown, hairline, part line
Addresses
Hair thinning
