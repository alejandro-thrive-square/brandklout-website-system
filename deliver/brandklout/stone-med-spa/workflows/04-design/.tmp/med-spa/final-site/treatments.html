I

Injectables

Neuromodulators soften the lines that come from movement, fillers restore volume, and biostimulators like Sculptra help your skin build its own collagen over time. Every injection is done by our nurse practitioner.

01 Botox | Xeomin Appointment15–20 min Shows3–7 days Lasts3–4 months DowntimeNone

Botox and Xeomin relax the muscles that crease the skin, softening forehead lines, frown lines and crow's feet. Xeomin has no accessory proteins, which can help if Botox seems to wear off faster than it used to.

Placed
Forehead, frown lines, crow's feet
Addresses
Fine lines & wrinkles
Cadence
every 3–4 months

Two more neuromodulators that work the same way as Botox. Dysport spreads a little further, which can suit a larger area like the forehead, and Jeuveau is often chosen for frown lines.

Placed

Hyaluronic acid filler restores shape and volume to the lips, cheeks, chin and jawline, and can soften hollows under the eyes. Placement is planned around your facial structure so the result looks like you.

Biostimulators

04 Sculptra Appointment30–45 min Shows6–8 weeks, building LastsUp to 2 years Downtime1–2 days

Sculptra is a biostimulator. Instead of filling a space, it prompts your skin to rebuild collagen, so results appear gradually over a few months and can last two years or more.

Placed
Cheeks, temples, jawline
Addresses
Volume loss, Laxity & jawline, Texture & pores
Cadence
2–3 sessions, then every 2 years

II

Skin Rejuvenation

These treatments prompt your skin to repair and renew itself. Most are done as a short series, and results keep improving for a few months after the last session.

05 Morpheus8 Appointment45–60 min Shows3 weeks, building to 3 months Lasts12+ months Downtime2–4 days

Radiofrequency energy delivered through fine microneedles tightens and smooths the deeper layers of the skin while leaving the surface largely intact. It works well on the jawline, the neck and acne scarring.

Placed
Face, jawline, neck
Addresses
Laxity & jawline, Texture & pores, Acne scarring
Cadence
3, then yearly
Ask about this treatment at your consultation and we'll go through it with you. Ask about this
06 Chemical Peels Appointment30–45 min Shows5–7 days LastsMonths, as a series Downtime0–7 days

A peel lifts away damaged surface skin to even out tone, fade sun spots and refine texture. Light peels have almost no downtime, deeper ones need about a week, and we'll help you pick one that fits your schedule.

Placed
Face, neck, chest, hands
Addresses
Pigmentation & tone, Texture & pores, Dryness & dullness
Cadence
2–4 a year
Ask about this treatment at your consultation and we'll go through it with you. Ask about this
07 SkinPen Microneedling Appointment45–60 min Shows2–4 weeks LastsMonths, as a series Downtime1–3 days of redness

SkinPen creates thousands of tiny channels in the skin, which starts its natural repair process and boosts collagen. It's a good fit for acne scars, enlarged pores and early fine lines.

Placed
Face, neck
Addresses
Texture & pores, Acne scarring, Fine lines & wrinkles

IV

Restoration

PRP uses a concentrate of your own platelets to support skin and hair. Results come on gradually, and nothing synthetic is involved.

11 PRP Treatment Appointment45–60 min Shows4–6 weeks LastsA series of 3, then maintenance Downtime1–2 days

A small amount of your blood is drawn and spun to concentrate the platelets, which are then returned to the skin. It's a good choice if you'd rather avoid fillers or synthetic products.

Placed
Under-eyes, face, scalp
Addresses
Texture & pores, Hair thinning
Cadence
3, then 2 a year
Ask about this treatment at your consultation and we'll go through it with you. Ask about this
12 Hair Restoration Appointment45–60 min Shows3–4 months LastsMaintenance every 6–12 months DowntimeMinimal

PRP injected into the scalp to support thinning hair. It works best on follicles that are still active, so starting early tends to give better results.

Placed
Crown, hairline, part line

Hair thinning
