Questions

About paying.

Will insurance cover any of this?

No. Cosmetic treatment is not a covered expense. If something is genuinely medical, Dr. Stone will say so and refer you.

Can I use an HSA or FSA?

Generally not for cosmetic work. Your plan administrator is the authority on your account.

Why are there no prices here?

Because a number without an assessment misleads. Units and syringes depend on you. You are quoted at the consultation, before anything is done.

Does the membership save money?

For someone already coming three or four times a year, it is designed to. If you are not sure you will repeat, pay per visit. The two tiers.
