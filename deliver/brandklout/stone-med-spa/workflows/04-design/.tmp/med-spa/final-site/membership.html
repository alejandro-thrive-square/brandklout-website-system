Membership

You are going to come back. Plan for it.

Non-surgical results are a cadence, not an event. If you are having Botox three or four times a year anyway, a membership makes it cheaper, keeps your appointments held in advance, and means you stop deciding every four months whether to bother.

Two ways in

Which one you are depends on one question.

Are you maintaining something, or changing something? Maintenance is a standing appointment. Change is a course of treatment with an end date, and then it becomes maintenance.

These tiers are a proposal, not an offer. The structure below is our recommendation based on how this practice actually runs; the inclusions, the names and the pricing are for the client to set. Nothing here has been approved and no fee is published on this site.
