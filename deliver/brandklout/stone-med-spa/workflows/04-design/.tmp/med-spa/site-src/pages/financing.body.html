Questions

About paying.

Will insurance cover medical spa treatments?

No. Cosmetic treatment isn't a covered expense. If something looks medical rather than cosmetic, your provider will tell you and point you to the right place.

Can I use an HSA or FSA for med spa treatments?

Usually not for cosmetic treatment. Your plan administrator can tell you for sure.

Why aren't med spa treatment prices listed online?

Most treatments are priced by units or syringes, and how much you need depends on you. You'll get an exact quote at your consultation, before anything is done.
