The training

She learned the face from the inside.

Oral and maxillofacial surgery is the specialty that treats the jaws, the facial bones and the soft tissue over them. It is reconstruction, trauma, implants and orthognathic work — years of operating on the structure that everything cosmetic sits on top of.

Aesthetic injection is a small, precise application of that anatomy. Most people who perform it learned it as a procedure. Dr. Stone learned the territory first and the procedure second, which is why her consultations are longer and her first doses are smaller.

It is also why she is comfortable telling patients that an injectable is the wrong answer. She has the surgical option available and no commercial reason to avoid recommending it, or to recommend it when it is not needed.

The approach

Under-treat first.

A first treatment here is deliberately conservative, and you are booked for a review two weeks later. Adding a little more at that review takes five minutes. Removing too much takes three months of waiting.

That approach costs the practice a second appointment and saves patients the experience most of them are actually afraid of, which is not the needle. It is walking out looking like they have had something done.
