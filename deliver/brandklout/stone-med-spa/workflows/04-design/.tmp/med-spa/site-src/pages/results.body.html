Straight answers

About the proof on this page.

Are the reviews real patients of this practice?

The reviews are drawn from the practice's public Google listing, where anyone can read them in full and unedited. We do not solicit reviews in exchange for anything, and we do not remove the ones we would rather not have.

Why are there no before-and-after photographs?

Because we do not have current written consent for them yet, and we are asking each patient individually rather than relying on a form they signed years ago for a different website. It is slower. It is also the only version of this that is actually consented.

Will the photographs be retouched?

No. When they are published they will be shot in the same lighting, at the same angle and with the same lens on both visits, and they will not be edited beyond cropping. A before-and-after where the lighting changed is not a result, it is a photograph of better lighting.

Are the models on this website patients?

No, and they are never presented as such. The people photographed across this site are models. Any image of a patient will be labelled as one, on the results page, with consent. This is a rule we hold to on all three of Dr. Stone's websites.

Can I speak to someone who has had it done?

Sometimes. A few patients have offered, and we will ask them rather than give you a number. What we will not do is hand out a patient's contact details, for the same reason we will not publish their face without asking.
