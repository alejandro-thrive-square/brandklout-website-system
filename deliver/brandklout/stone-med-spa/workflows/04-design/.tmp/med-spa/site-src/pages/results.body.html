Straight answers

About the proof on this page.

Are the reviews real patients?

They come from the practice’s public Google listing, readable in full there. Nothing is solicited in exchange for anything, and nothing is removed.

Why no before-and-after photographs?

Because current written consent for this website does not exist yet, and we are asking each patient rather than reusing a form signed for another one.

Will the photographs be retouched?

No. Same lighting, angle and lens on both visits, cropped and nothing else.

Are the people on this website patients?

No. They are models and are never presented as results. A patient will only ever appear on this page, labelled as one, with consent.
