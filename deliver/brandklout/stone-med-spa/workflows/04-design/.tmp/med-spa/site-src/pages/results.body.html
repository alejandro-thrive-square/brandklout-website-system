Questions

About our office.

What to expect at Westlake Medical Aesthetics?

A private office in Westlake with parking at the door. Your first visit is a consultation with one of our medical providers: we ask what you'd like to change, go through your medical history and look at your skin. You leave with a written plan and a quote, and whether you start that day is up to you.

Who performs the treatments?

Our own medical providers. Brooke, our nurse practitioner injector, does every injection. Abby, our medical aesthetician and senior laser technician, looks after facials and skin treatments.

How is Dr. Stone involved with the office?

Dr. Mona Stone, DDS, a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon, is the leader of Stone Surgical Arts, Stone Dental Implant Center and Westlake Medical Aesthetics. She doesn't treat patients here; our nurse practitioner and aesthetician do. If surgery would serve you better, we'll refer you to her team at Stone Surgical Arts.

What areas do you service?

Our office is at 72 Andorra Dr #110B in Westlake, TX, and we see patients from across the Dallas-Fort Worth area. Your first consultation can also be by video.
