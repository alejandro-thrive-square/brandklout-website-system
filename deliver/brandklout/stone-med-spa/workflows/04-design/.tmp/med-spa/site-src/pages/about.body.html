Who does what

Two teams. Your plan.

Westlake Medical Aesthetics is its own practice, with its own medical aesthetics team. Surgery is done by a separate surgical team at our sister practice, Stone Surgical Arts. If your provider thinks surgery would give you a better result, they'll tell you and refer you.
