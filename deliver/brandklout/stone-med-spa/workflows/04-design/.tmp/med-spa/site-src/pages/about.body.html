Who does what

Surgery next door. Aesthetics here.

If a procedure needs an incision, Dr. Stone performs it at Stone Surgical Arts. Everything on our menu is done here by our own medical providers. If your provider thinks surgery would give you a better result, they'll tell you and can refer you next door.
