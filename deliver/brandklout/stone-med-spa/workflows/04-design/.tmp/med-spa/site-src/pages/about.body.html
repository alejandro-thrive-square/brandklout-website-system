About

One surgeon, one suite, three names.

Stone Med Spa is the non-surgical half of Dr. Mona Stone's practice in Westlake. It exists so that the treatments that need an operating room and the treatments that need forty-five minutes are not sold from the same menu.

Why two websites

Because they are two different decisions.

Choosing to have a facelift takes months. Choosing to have Botox takes an afternoon. They need different information, different photographs and a different amount of your attention, and a single site that tries to serve both ends up serving neither.

So the menus are split at the incision. Anything that requires one is at Stone Surgical Arts. Anything that does not is here. Same surgeon, same address, same phone. You will not be handed between two teams.

Looking for surgery?

Stone Surgical Arts carries facelifts, neck lifts, eyelid surgery, FaceTite, facial implants and fat transfer. There is also Stone Dental Implant Center for implants and jaw surgery.

The place

Small on purpose.

One suite off 114 in Westlake, with parking at the door. There is no waiting room designed to impress you and no retail wall you have to walk past on the way out. There is a desk, a consultation room and treatment rooms.

Because every injection is placed by Dr. Stone, the practice takes fewer patients per week than a med spa of this size normally would. That is the trade: harder to get an appointment, and the appointment is with the surgeon.

How we work

Three rules we do not bend.

01

The surgeon injects

Not a delegate, not on her days off, not for a quick top-up. If Dr. Stone is not in, you are not injected.

02

Under-treat, then review

First doses are conservative and a two-week review is built into the plan. Adding is easy; waiting out too much is three months.

03

No before-and-after without consent

Every published photograph of a patient carries that patient's current written permission for that specific use. This is why our gallery is smaller than it could be.
