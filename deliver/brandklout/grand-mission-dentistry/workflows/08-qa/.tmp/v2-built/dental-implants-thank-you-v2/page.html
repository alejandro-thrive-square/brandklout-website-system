before you call

Any questions ? before you call

Is the dental implant procedure painful?
+

The procedure is done under local anaesthetic and sedation options are available, so you should not feel it. Some soreness and swelling during early healing is normal and manageable. Most patients tell us it was far easier than they expected.

Can I really get a new tooth in one day?
+

Sometimes, yes. Depending on your bone quality and overall health, a temporary tooth can be placed the same day as your implant. Your 3D scan at the consultation is what tells us whether you qualify.

What if I have been told I do not have enough bone?
+

Do not rule yourself out. Bone grafting, sinus lifts and alternative implant approaches make treatment possible for many patients who were told no elsewhere. A CBCT scan shows exactly what is possible in your case.

How long do implants last?
+

The implant post is designed as a lifetime replacement root. With healthy gums, daily care and regular checkups, implants can last decades. The crown on top may need maintenance over time, like any tooth.

When will I find out the cost of my treatment?
+

At your consultation, once the 3D scan shows what your case actually needs. A complete single implant, meaning post, abutment and crown, typically runs $1,900 to $2,800 at Grand Mission Dentistry. Bone grafting or extractions, if needed, are quoted upfront.

Do you take my insurance?
+

Insurance often covers part of the treatment, such as the crown or an extraction. Our team verifies your benefits before treatment and walks you through the financing options, so you know your exact out of pocket cost before you commit to anything.

What is the process for getting financing?
+

We handle it at the consultation. Once your treatment plan is set, our team runs the application with you and brings back the monthly options it returns. Nothing is agreed until you have seen the numbers in writing.
