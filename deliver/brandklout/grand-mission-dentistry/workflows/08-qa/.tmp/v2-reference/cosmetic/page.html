Everything Patients Ask Before Booking

What can cosmetic dentistry improve?

+

Cosmetic dentistry can address stains, chips, worn or uneven edges, small gaps, tooth shape, asymmetry, old dental work, and the overall balance of your smile. Your plan may include whitening, bonding, veneers, crowns, gum recontouring, Invisalign, or a thoughtful combination.

How do I know whether I need veneers or bonding?

+

Bonding is often a conservative choice for smaller chips, gaps, or shape changes, and can usually be completed in a single visit. Veneers are typically considered when several teeth need a more comprehensive change in color, shape, size, or symmetry, and they last considerably longer. Dr. Sung will explain the tradeoffs after evaluating your teeth, bite, and goals.

Will my smile look natural?

+

That is the goal. Your design is based on your facial proportions, lips, natural tooth characteristics, and preferred level of brightness. Porcelain is chosen for the way it mimics the translucency of natural enamel, and shade is matched under natural lighting — so the result looks like it belongs to you, not like a generic set of teeth.

Can I preview the result before treatment?

+

Yes. Virtual smile design lets you see your potential result before treatment begins — you can even start by uploading photos from home. You approve the design before any tooth preparation, and temporaries let you experience the shape and proportions while your final restorations are crafted.

How long does cosmetic treatment take?

+

It depends on your starting point. Bonding and whitening can often be completed quickly, porcelain veneers typically take two visits, and a comprehensive makeover involving alignment or restorative work may take several. You will receive a clear sequence and timeline before deciding to begin.

How much does cosmetic dentistry cost?

+

It depends on the number of teeth involved, the procedures selected, and the materials used. Porcelain veneers generally range from $900 to $2,000 per tooth, so a six-veneer makeover typically falls between $5,400 and $12,000. After your evaluation you will get a clear written estimate before you decide, and financing through Cherry, including 0% APR options, is available.

Is cosmetic dentistry covered by insurance?

+

Procedures done purely for appearance are generally not covered. When treatment also restores a damaged or unhealthy tooth, insurance may cover part of the cost. Our team verifies your benefits and walks you through your options so you know your out-of-pocket cost before you commit to anything.
