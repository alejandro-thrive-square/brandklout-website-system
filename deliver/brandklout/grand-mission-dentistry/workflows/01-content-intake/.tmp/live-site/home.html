Welcome to Grand Mission Dentistry

Grand Mission Dentistry is Richmond’s trusted home for comprehensive family and advanced surgical dental care. Led by Dr. William Sung and our experienced team, we combine modern technology with clinical precision to deliver everything from preventive care to complex implant and periodontal procedures — all in one convenient location.
‍
Whether you need a routine cleaning, dental implants to replace missing teeth, bone regeneration to restore lost support, or gum grafting to protect your smile, we provide thoughtful, personalized treatment designed for long-term health and stability. Our approach focuses on function, aesthetics, and lasting results.

No pressure. No shortcuts. Just honest guidance, advanced surgical expertise, and care tailored to your individual needs.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most PPO dental insurance plans. Our team will verify your benefits and help you maximize your coverage. We also offer financing for treatments not covered by insurance.

What should I bring to my first dental appointment?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card (if applicable), and a list of any medications you're currently taking. You can complete new patient forms online before your visit to save time.

Do you offer sedation for anxious patients?

Yes. Grand Mission Dentistry offers multiple sedation options including nitrous oxide (laughing gas), oral sedation, IV sedation, and general anesthesia for more complex procedures. During your consultation we will recommend the safest option for your comfort level and treatment needs.

How much do dental implants cost at Grand Mission Dentistry?

Our all-inclusive single dental implant starts at $2,999 and includes the implant, abutment, and crown. Final cost depends on your specific needs, such as whether bone grafting or extractions are required. During your consultation we review your case and discuss flexible financing options through Cherry.

Learn more
Is Invisalign suitable for adults?

Yes. Invisalign works for both teens and adults. Many of our adult patients choose Invisalign for its discreet appearance, convenience, and shorter typical treatment time of 6 to 18 months. Our Invisalign special is $3,799 and includes aligners, refinements, a retainer, and whitening.
