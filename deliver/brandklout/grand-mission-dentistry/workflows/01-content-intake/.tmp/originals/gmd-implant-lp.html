In some cases, patients may need preliminary procedures to prepare for a dental implant. After a consultation, Dr. Will Sung might recommend treatments like Bone Graft, Gum Graft, or Socket Preservation to ensure your jaw and gums are ready for the implant.

Is a Single-Tooth Dental Implant Right for Me?
Dr. Sung will evaluate your dental health and recommend the best solution for replacing your missing tooth. Single-tooth dental implants are ideal for patients seeking a permanent, natural-looking, and functional replacement for a lost tooth.
How Does a Single-Tooth Dental Implant Work?
A single-tooth dental implant involves a titanium post surgically placed in your jaw to act as an artificial tooth root. Once healed, the implant is topped with a custom-made dental crown, restoring the appearance and function of your natural tooth.
