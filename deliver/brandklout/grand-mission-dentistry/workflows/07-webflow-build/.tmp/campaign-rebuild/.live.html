How much is a single dental implant?
+

Grand Mission lists an all-inclusive single implant starting at $2,999 for the implant, abutment, and crown. The final fee depends on whether extraction, grafting, sedation, or other treatment is needed.

What if I do not have enough bone?
+

Bone loss does not automatically rule out implants. A 3D scan shows whether grafting, ridge augmentation, an alternative implant plan, or another restoration is appropriate.

How long does the process take?
+

Many implants need several months to integrate before the final restoration. The number of teeth, grafting needs, healing, and whether a temporary tooth is possible all affect the timeline.

Do implants hurt?
+

Local anesthetic is used, and multiple sedation levels are available. Most patients should expect temporary soreness afterward; your health and procedure determine the safest comfort plan.
