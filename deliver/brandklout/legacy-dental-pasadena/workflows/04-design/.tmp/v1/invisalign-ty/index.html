WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Four things, and the first one is ours

The consultation takes about an hour. Nothing is decided at it, and there is nothing to pay.

01

We call to confirm

A member of the team rings to agree a time that works, and answers anything you want to ask before you come in.

02

The exam and the scan

Dr. Sunyoto examines your teeth and takes an iTero digital scan. There are no putty impressions, and the scan takes a few minutes.

03

You see the result first

The scan builds a preview of where your teeth would finish. You see it, and the plan behind it, before deciding anything.

04

You decide, or you do not

If you want to go ahead, a $100 refundable deposit reserves the $3,379 price while your aligners are made. If you would rather not, that is the end of it.
