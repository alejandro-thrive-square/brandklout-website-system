What is Invisalign?+
Invisalign is a series of clear, removable aligners that straighten your teeth without metal braces. They are comfortable, nearly invisible, and allow you to eat, brush and floss normally during treatment.
How long does Invisalign treatment take?+
Most Invisalign treatments take 6 to 18 months, depending on your case. Many patients start seeing noticeable improvements within the first few weeks.
What does the $3,379 special include?+
Everything you need from start to finish: a comprehensive exam and 3D digital scan, your custom Invisalign aligners, a personalised treatment plan, all office visits and adjustments, and one set of retainers. No hidden fees.
Are payment plans available?+
Yes. Alongside paying in full, there is an in-office plan starting with $1,000 down, and a 0% interest option of about $166 a month over 24 months. You can start treatment with a low down payment.
Are retainers included?+
Yes, retainers come standard with every treatment. They are essential for keeping your teeth in their new position, so we make sure you are set up for lasting results.
Do I need to come in often?+
No. Because the movement is planned digitally, you will have fewer visits than with traditional orthodontics.
