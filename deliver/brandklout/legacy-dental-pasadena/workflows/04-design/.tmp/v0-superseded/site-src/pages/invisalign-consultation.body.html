Chapter02The Visit

What the appointment actually involves.

The commonest reason a booked consultation is not kept is not knowing what happens in it. So here it is, in order.

We look at your teeth

Dr. Sunyoto examines your bite and your alignment, and tells you whether clear aligners are the right treatment for your case. Sometimes the answer is no.

You see the 3D preview

Advanced 3D imaging shows you the result before you commit to anything, so the decision is made looking at an outcome rather than at a description of one.

You get the number in writing

The price, what it covers and the four ways to pay it. Nothing is signed on the day and nothing is owed for the visit.
