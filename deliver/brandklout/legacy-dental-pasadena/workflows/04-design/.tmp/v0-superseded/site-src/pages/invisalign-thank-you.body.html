Chapter01The Fast Track

What happens between now and your first aligner.

Step one

We call you back

The front desk calls to fix a time that works. Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM, and Saturdays are flexible. Clinical days are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday.

Step two

Your free consultation

Dr. Sunyoto looks at your teeth and builds the plan, and you see the 3D preview of your result in the same visit. Nothing is owed, and nothing is decided under pressure.

Step three

Data collection

If you go ahead, the front desk schedules the scan, photos and X-rays, all included in the price. A $100 fully refundable deposit locks in the discount and is credited toward the payment due at that appointment.

Chapter02The Preparation

Three things worth bringing.

Your insurance details

Coverage varies by plan and not all orthodontic benefits apply to Invisalign. Where a plan does cover it, reimbursement typically runs $1,000 to $3,000. The practice files on your behalf and the payment is sent to you.

Any dental history you have

Previous X-rays, retainers you already own, or notes from another practice. None of it is required, and none of it changes the price.

The question you actually care about

Whether that is how long it will take, whether anyone will notice, or what happens if your teeth shift afterwards. It is a consultation, so use it.

Chapter03The Team
