OUR SERVICES

At Legacy Dental Pasadena, we provide a full spectrum of high-quality dental services in Pasadena, CA, tailored to every stage of your oral health journey. From routine general dentistry and professional cleanings to advanced dental implants, cosmetic smile makeovers, and restorative treatments, our experienced team led by board-certified Dr. Sean Sunyoto combines state-of-the-art technology, precision care, and personalized attention to deliver lasting, confident smiles.

We proudly serve families and individuals throughout Pasadena with gentle, patient-centered dentistry, including emergency dental care, sedation options for anxiety-free visits, and advanced procedures like same-day CEREC crowns and guided implant surgery.
