Meet Dr. Sean Sunyoto, DDS, MICOI

As a second-generation dentist, Dr. Sean Sunyoto has earned a reputation as a top dentist in Pasadena, continuing a family legacy of compassionate, patient-centered care. In 2018, he took over the practice from Dr. Rodney Jones and has since blended time-honored values with cutting-edge innovation to serve the community he loves.

Patients appreciate Dr. Sunyoto not only for his clinical excellence but also for the genuine personal attention he provides. He prioritizes comfort above all and ensures every visit leaves patients feeling informed, at ease, and truly cared for. This applies to routine check-ups as well as complex implant surgery.

Dr. Sunyoto believes dentistry is about more than restoring teeth. It is about building confidence, function, and long-term oral health. By combining artistic precision with the latest technology, including dental implants, digital smile design, Invisalign, and advanced cosmetic solutions, he creates beautiful, natural-looking smiles that last.

Beyond the chair, Dr. Sunyoto is deeply committed to education and community. His advanced training, teaching experience, and professional leadership reflect a passion for helping both patients and fellow dentists grow.

For Dr. Sunyoto, dentistry is a calling rooted in service and compassion. He looks forward to partnering with you on your journey to a healthier, more confident smile.

Education & Advanced Training

Dr. Sean Sunyoto built his career on a solid foundation of academic excellence and ongoing advanced training. A second-generation dentist, he graduated at the top of his high school class before earning his undergraduate degree from the University of California, Berkeley. He then completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) at the prestigious Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry at the University of the Pacific.

His commitment to mastery in implant dentistry earned him Fellowship status (2022) and Mastership status (2024) with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI), prestigious recognitions reserved for those who demonstrate exceptional skill in implant surgery and restoration.

Dr. Sunyoto actively shares his expertise with the next generation of dentists. As a faculty member at Oasis Implant Academy, he teaches live implant surgery courses and mentors colleagues nationwide. He also serves as a speaker for Align Technologies (Invisalign), presenting on advanced digital workflows for crowns, night guards, and computer-aided smile design.

Through continuous education, hands-on teaching, and national speaking engagements, Dr. Sunyoto remains at the forefront of modern dentistry, bringing the latest techniques and technology to his patients in Pasadena.
