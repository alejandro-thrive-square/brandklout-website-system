How long does Invisalign take?

Most patients achieve their dream smile in 6 to 18 months, depending on individual needs.

Is Invisalign painful?

Most patients feel mild pressure when transitioning to new aligners, which is normal. Overall, many find Invisalign to be more comfortable than traditional braces. It’s designed to minimize discomfort.

Can I eat and drink with aligners?

You should remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. This ensures proper hygiene and prevents damage to the aligners. After meals, remember to brush your teeth before reinserting them.

How many hours a day do I wear them?

For optimal results, wear your aligners for 20 to 22 hours a day. Only remove them for meals and cleaning. Consistent wear is crucial for achieving your desired smile.

Is Invisalign as effective as braces?

Yes, Invisalign is effective for most mild to moderate orthodontic cases. However, complex cases may still require traditional braces. A consultation will help determine the best option for you.

Will Invisalign affect my speech?

Only slightly at first. Most patients adjust in just a few days.
