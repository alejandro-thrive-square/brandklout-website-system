BUILDING A Legacy OF TRUST AND Smiles IN OUR COMMUNITY

At Legacy Dental Pasadena, we firmly believe that dentistry is not just a routine appointment but a personal journey tailored to each individual. Led by Dr. Sean Sunyoto, our dedicated team of professionals combines the latest advanced technology with a deep commitment to compassionate care, creating an environment where our patients feel valued and understood.

We strive to ensure that every visit is not only a step towards a healthier mouth but also a pathway to a more confident and radiant smile. Dr. Sunyoto takes the time to listen to each patient’s concerns, educate them about their options, and provide personalized treatment plans that cater to their unique needs because your comfort, confidence, and oral health are always our top priorities.

Our Team of Experienced Professionals

At Legacy Dental Pasadena, every doctor and clinical team member brings a minimum of five years of hands-on experience. We are committed to providing exceptional care with the highest level of clinical expertise, ensuring every patient receives personalized, high-quality treatment in a comfortable and supportive environment.

Dr. Sean Sunyoto, DDS

Dr. Sean Sunyoto, DDS, MICOI blends cutting-edge dentistry with genuine compassion. As a Fellow and Master of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists and a respected educator in implant surgery, he’s committed to providing personalized, high-quality care that helps patients feel confident and cared for at every visit.

Meet Dr. Sunyoto

Dr. Justin Cho, DDS

Dr. Justin Cho, DDS is a leading general and cosmetic dentist in Los Angeles. He earned his B.S. and DDS from the University of the Pacific after serving as a Sergeant Combat Medic in the ROK Army. Dr. Cho offers comprehensive care including cosmetic dentistry, implants, oral surgery, root canals, and Invisalign.
