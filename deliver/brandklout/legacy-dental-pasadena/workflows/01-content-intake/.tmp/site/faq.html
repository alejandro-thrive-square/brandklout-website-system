What is the average cost of dental implants at Legacy Dental Pasadena?

The price of a dental implant at Legacy Dental Pasadena depends on factors like the number of teeth being restored, your bone health, and whether grafting is needed. We start every case with a complimentary consultation and 3D scan so you know the exact cost upfront, with no surprises. Flexible payment options including PPO insurance, CareCredit, HSA, and FSA make treatment manageable for any budget.

Legacy Dental Pasadena works with most PPO dental insurance providers and handles all the paperwork on your behalf. For out-of-pocket expenses, we accept CareCredit, HSA, and FSA payments. Our front desk team will run a full benefits verification before your treatment begins so you know exactly what's covered.

Will my dental implants need to be replaced over time?

When properly cared for, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime, making them one of the most cost-effective tooth replacement solutions available. The titanium post bonds with your jawbone for permanent stability. With over 1,000 implants placed using guided surgery technology, Dr. Sean Sunyoto delivers long-term success rates that rival or exceed industry standards.

How does same-day CEREC crown technology work?

CEREC technology lets us complete your crown from start to finish in one appointment. We take a digital scan, design your crown on the spot, mill it from a solid ceramic block in our office, and bond it to your tooth, all in a single visit. You leave with a permanent, custom-fit crown and skip the messy impressions, temporary crowns, and follow-up appointments.

What options are available if I have dental anxiety?

We understand dental anxiety is real, which is why we offer three sedation levels: nitrous oxide for mild relaxation, oral sedation for moderate calm, and IV sedation for deep relaxation during longer procedures. Beyond sedation, every patient has access to our comfort menu featuring warm blankets, neck pillows, and noise-canceling headphones to make every visit a positive experience.

Dental emergencies cannot wait, and neither do we. Legacy Dental Pasadena reserves time daily for urgent cases including severe toothaches, knocked-out or broken teeth, lost fillings or crowns, abscesses, and trauma from accidents. Call (626) 584-1800 as early as possible and we'll get you in the same day whenever it's clinically safe to do so.
