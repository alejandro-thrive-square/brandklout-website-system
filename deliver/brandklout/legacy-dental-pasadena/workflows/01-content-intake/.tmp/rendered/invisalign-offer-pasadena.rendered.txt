What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a series of clear, removable aligners that straighten your teeth without metal braces. They are comfortable, nearly invisible, and allow you to eat, brush, and floss normally during treatment.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most Invisalign treatments take 6 to 18 months, depending on your case. Many patients start seeing noticeable improvements within the first few weeks.

Are retainers included?

Yes, retainers come standard with every treatment. They're essential for keeping your teeth in their new position, so we make sure you're set up for lasting results. Learn how to use them in our Retainer Instructions guide.
