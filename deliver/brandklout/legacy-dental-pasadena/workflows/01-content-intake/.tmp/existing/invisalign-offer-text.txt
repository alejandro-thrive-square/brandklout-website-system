USING INSURANCE FOR INVISALIGN
Many dental insurance plans include some level of Invisalign benefits, but coverage varies. It’s important to check your specific plan, as not all orthodontic benefits apply to Invisalign. Some policies also limit orthodontic coverage based on age, often between 18 and 21.
If your plan does include Invisalign, reimbursement typically ranges from $1,000 to $3,000. Depending on your provider, payments may be sent to you or directly to the dental office.
Cost of Invisalign Without Insurance
Not having dental insurance doesn’t mean you have to put off improving your smile. We offer flexible payment options designed to fit a wide range of budgets, making Invisalign more accessible. If cost is a concern, our team can help you find a solution that works for you.

What is Invisalign?
Invisalign is a series of clear, removable aligners that straighten your teeth without metal braces. They are comfortable, nearly invisible, and allow you to eat, brush, and floss normally during treatment.
How long does Invisalign treatment take?
Most Invisalign treatments take
6 to 18 months
, depending on your case. Many patients start seeing noticeable improvements within the first few weeks.
