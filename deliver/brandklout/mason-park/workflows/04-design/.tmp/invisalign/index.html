What clear aligners correct

Six bite problems Invisalign® is built for

Left alone, a misaligned bite is not only a cosmetic matter — it wears enamel, complicates cleaning and puts strain on the joint. These are the cases we treat most often.

01

Crossbite

The upper and lower jaws are misaligned. This can be the reason for degradation of teeth, loss of bone and periodontal disease.

02

Gapped teeth

Gaps between the teeth may occur due to abnormal growth of jawbone, or a lack of surrounding teeth allowing them to shift into the created space.

03

Underbite

This may take place due to inadequate upper teeth or growth anomalies in the jaw, causing an inappropriate bite.

04

Overbite

The upper jaw sits well over the lower jaw. An overbite may cause wearing of teeth on the lower jaw, joint problems and gum problems.

05

Crowded teeth

Untreated crowding can become extremely crooked. Misaligned teeth are harder to clean, which invites gum disease, plaque and tooth decay.

06

Open bite

An open bite happens when the teeth in the upper and lower jaws do not meet properly.

For more complicated cases, fixed, lingual or clear braces may suit you better. Dr. Sung will tell you which at your consultation.

Questions

Before you book

How long does Invisalign® treatment take?
Treatment length varies based on individual needs. Some patients see results in a few months, while others require longer treatment.
Is Invisalign® painful?
Some mild pressure may occur when switching aligners, but discomfort is temporary and minimal.
Who is a good candidate?
Teens and adults with mild to moderate spacing, crowding, or bite concerns are typically good candidates.
Can I eat and drink with Invisalign®?
Aligners should be removed before eating or drinking anything other than water, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods.
Does dental insurance cover Invisalign®?
Many dental insurance plans include benefits that apply to Invisalign® treatment. Our team can help verify your coverage.
Are payment plans available?
Yes. We offer flexible financing options to help make Invisalign® treatment accessible.
Will I know my treatment cost before starting?
Yes. A detailed treatment plan and cost breakdown are provided before treatment begins.
Will I need a retainer afterwards?
Yes. Retainers are essential to maintain your results and prevent teeth from shifting. A set of Vivera® retainers is included in your treatment.
