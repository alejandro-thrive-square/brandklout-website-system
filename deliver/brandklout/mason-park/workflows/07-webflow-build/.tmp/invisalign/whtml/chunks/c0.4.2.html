Chapter 02 · What It Corrects

Six bite problems clear aligners are built for.

These are the cases we treat most often. Keep scrolling to walk through them.

01

Crossbite

The upper and lower jaws are misaligned. This can be the reason for degradation of teeth, loss of bone and periodontal disease.

02

Gapped teeth

Gaps between the teeth may occur due to abnormal growth of jawbone, or a lack of surrounding teeth allowing them to shift into the created space.

03

Underbite

This may take place due to inadequate upper teeth or growth anomalies in the jaw, causing an inappropriate bite.

04

Overbite

The upper jaw sits well over the lower jaw. An overbite may cause wearing of teeth on the lower jaw, joint problems and gum problems.

05

Crowded teeth

Untreated crowding can become extremely crooked. Misaligned teeth are harder to clean, which invites gum disease, plaque and tooth decay.

06

Open bite

An open bite happens when the teeth in the upper and lower jaws do not meet properly.

For more complicated cases, fixed, lingual or clear braces may suit you better. Dr. Sung will tell you which at your consultation.
