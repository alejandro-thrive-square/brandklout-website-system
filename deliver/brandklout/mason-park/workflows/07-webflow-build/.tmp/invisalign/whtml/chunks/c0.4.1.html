Chapter 01 · The Hesitation

A crooked bite is not only a cosmetic matter. It wears enamel, complicates cleaning, and puts strain on the joint.

Which is why putting it off rarely stays a cosmetic decision. Crowded teeth are harder to clean, and that invites gum disease, plaque and decay.

The question worth answering is not whether to straighten them. It is what it actually involves, what it actually costs, and whether you are a candidate at all — which takes one free appointment to find out.
