Before you book

The questions we are asked most.

How long does Invisalign® treatment take?

Treatment length varies based on individual needs. Some patients see results in a few months, while others require longer treatment.

Is Invisalign® painful?

Some mild pressure may occur when switching aligners, but discomfort is temporary and minimal.

Who is a good candidate?

Teens and adults with mild to moderate spacing, crowding, or bite concerns are typically good candidates.

Can I eat and drink with Invisalign®?

Aligners should be removed before eating or drinking anything other than water, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods.

Does dental insurance cover Invisalign®?

Many dental insurance plans include benefits that apply to Invisalign® treatment. Our team can help verify your coverage.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. We offer flexible financing options to help make Invisalign® treatment accessible.

Will I know my treatment cost before starting?

Yes. A detailed treatment plan and cost breakdown are provided before treatment begins.

Will I need a retainer afterwards?

Yes. Retainers are essential to maintain your results and prevent teeth from shifting. A set of Vivera® retainers is included in your treatment.
