Chapter 02 · What Happens Next

The next three steps.

1

We call to confirm

A member of the team will call during office hours to agree a time that suits you, and to answer anything you want to ask before you come in.

2

We prepare for your visit

We will check your insurance benefits in advance and send you anything you need to fill in, so your appointment is spent on your teeth rather than on paperwork.

3

You come and see us

Your exam, imaging and cleaning happen in one visit, and you leave with a written, itemised treatment plan. Nothing beyond that is started without your say-so.

If you do not hear from us within one business day, please call — we would rather answer twice than miss you. Note that we are closed Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

Chapter 03 · The Preparation

Before you come in.

Nothing has to happen before we call — this is not a to-do list. But if these three are to hand, your first visit is spent on your teeth rather than on admin.

01

Your insurance card

We check your benefits before you arrive, so what is covered — and what is not — is settled between us before anything is started.

 02

Any treatment plan you were given elsewhere

Bring it with you. A second opinion here is free, and it is the quickest way to know whether the plan you were handed is the one you actually need.

 03

The questions you have not asked yet

Write them down while they are fresh. Nothing is started at your visit without your say-so, so the more you ask now, the less there is to decide in the chair.

That is the whole list. The rest of it is ours to do.
