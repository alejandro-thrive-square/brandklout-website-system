Chapter 03 · The Visit

What actually happens on the day.

1

We look properly

A comprehensive exam and digital x-rays, using cone beam imaging and an intra-oral camera so you can see what we are describing.

2

We explain what we found

Your oral health needs, goals and priorities are the focus of your customised treatment plan — written down, itemised and priced.

3

You decide what happens next

Nothing beyond the cleaning is started on the day. You leave with the plan and the costs, and choose in your own time.
