Everything Patients Ask Before Booking

What can cosmetic dentistry improve?

+

Cosmetic dentistry can address stains, chips, worn or uneven edges, small gaps, tooth shape, asymmetry, old dental work, and the overall balance of your smile. Your plan may include whitening, bonding, veneers, crowns, gum recontouring, orthodontics, or a thoughtful combination.

How do I know whether I need veneers or bonding?

+

Your visit covers a comprehensive examination, x-rays, a cleaning and a full written treatment plan. If anything beyond that is needed, it is quoted separately and never started without your approval.

Will my smile look natural?

+

That is the goal. Your design is based on your facial proportions, lips, natural tooth characteristics, and preferred level of brightness. Shape, texture, and shade are selected so the result looks like it belongs to you - not like a generic set of teeth.

Can I preview the result before treatment?

+

Yes. Digital smile design can help you see the proposed direction before permanent treatment begins. Depending on your case, a physical trial smile may also let you experience the shape and proportions before final restorations are made.

How long does cosmetic treatment take?

+

The timeline depends on your starting point and treatment plan. Whitening or bonding may be completed quickly, while veneers, alignment, or a more comprehensive smile makeover may take several visits. You will receive a clear sequence and timeline before deciding to begin.

How much does cosmetic dentistry cost?

+

The investment depends on the number of teeth involved, the procedures selected, the materials used, and the complexity of your case. After your evaluation, the team will explain your options and provide a clear estimate before you decide how to proceed. Financing options may be available.

Is cosmetic dentistry covered by insurance?

+

The new patient special is designed for patients without dental insurance, so no claim is needed. If you do have coverage, our team will check your benefits and apply them instead. Ask about the Mason Park Dental Savings Plan no annual maximum, no deductible, and no waiting period.
