Any Questions Before You Call?

What is included in the $189 new patient special?
+

Your comprehensive exam, a full assessment of teeth, gums and bite, including an oral cancer screening, plus digital x-rays, a professional cleaning, and a written, itemised treatment plan. It is the whole appointment in one price.

Is there anything else to pay on the day?
+

No. This is the whole appointment, in one price, not a loss-leader that turns into a quote at the desk. Additional services, where clinically required, are quoted for your approval before treatment begins.

What actually happens at the visit?
+

We look properly, with a comprehensive exam and digital x-rays using cone beam imaging and an intra-oral camera so you can see what we are describing. We explain what we found and write your plan down, itemised and priced. Then you decide, and nothing beyond the cleaning is started on the day.

Do you take my insurance?
+

We are an insurance-friendly office and our team will help verify what your plan covers before you commit to treatment. A dental savings plan is also available for patients without insurance; ask the front desk when you book.

Are payment plans available?
+

Yes. Cherry financing gives you 0% APR with no hard credit check, so you can pay monthly and take better control of your finances rather than paying everything at once.

Can I bring a treatment plan from another dentist?
+

Please do. A second opinion here is free, and it is the quickest way to know whether the plan you were handed is the one you actually need.

Do you see children too?
+

Yes. A new patient exam for children under 13 is $129, seen by Dr. Tam Nguyen, our board certified pediatric dentist. We welcome new patients of all ages.
