Front Desk

Jill

Front Desk / Office Manager
Jill has more than two decades of experience in dentistry and is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable, informed, and genuinely cared for. She enjoys helping patients understand their insurance benefits, reviewing treatment options, and making every visit feel more personal and less overwhelming. As the Office Manager at Grand Mission Dentistry, Jill is deeply committed to the success of the practice. A large part of that commitment is her passion for training and developing her team, helping each team member build confidence, strengthen their skills, and become the best they can be. She considers it a privilege to work alongside one of the most talented and compassionate doctors in the Houston area. Although she now calls the Austin area home, her love for the practice, its patients, and her team keeps her present and connected to Grand Mission Dentistry.
A proud Texan, Jill was born in Midland and raised in the Houston and Katy area. Outside of the office, she is a proud mother of two wonderful daughters and values spending time with her family, growing in her faith, and nurturing her relationship with the Lord.
