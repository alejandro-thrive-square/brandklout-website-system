What Is a Single Tooth Implant?

A single tooth implant is a long-lasting, natural-looking solution for replacing a missing tooth—without affecting the surrounding healthy teeth. This dental restoration closely mimics the function and appearance of a natural tooth and is made up of three essential components:

Implant Post: A small titanium screw that is surgically placed into the jawbone. This acts as the artificial root, bonding with the bone through a process called osseointegration, providing a stable and durable foundation.

Abutment: A connector piece that is attached to the implant post. It serves as the link between the implanted root and the visible crown.

Crown: The top, tooth-shaped cap made from porcelain or other tooth-colored materials. This is custom-crafted to match the shape, size, and color of your surrounding teeth, ensuring a seamless smile.

Replacing a missing tooth with a dental implant is one of the most reliable and long-lasting solutions in modern dentistry. 🔍 Cost Breakdown for a Single Tooth Implant

To better understand where the cost comes from, let’s break it down into its main components:

Implant Surgery:

Estimated cost: $1,500–$2,500

This involves the surgical placement of a titanium post into the jawbone, which acts as a root replacement. The cost may vary depending on bone density, sedation needs, and the type of implant system used.

Abutment Placement:

Estimated cost: $300–$500

The abutment is the connector piece placed on top of the implant that holds the crown. Custom abutments tend to be more expensive than standard ones.

Crown Placement:

Estimated cost: $1,000–$2,500

This is the final visible part of the implant—a lifelike ceramic or porcelain crown customized to match your natural teeth.

💡 In some cases, bone grafting or sinus lifts may be necessary before implant placement, which can increase the total cost.
