Explore Our Range of Services

Preventative Dentistry

Preventing dental issues before they arise is crucial for long-term oral health. Our Preventative Dentistry services include regular cleanings, exams, and fluoride treatments. By addressing potential problems early, we help you maintain a healthy smile and avoid more complex treatments in the future.

Children’s Dentistry

Creating a positive dental experience for children is a priority at our practice. Our Children’s Dentistry services are designed to make visits to the dentist enjoyable and stress-free. Our board-certified pediatric dentist, Dr. Tam Nguyen, specializes in providing gentle and effective care for young patients.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance the appearance of your smile with our Cosmetic Dentistry services. We offer teeth whitening, veneers, and bonding treatments to help you achieve the smile you’ve always wanted. Our cosmetic solutions are designed to provide natural-looking results that boost your confidence.

Restorative Dentistry

Our Restorative Dentistry services can restore function and esthetics if you have damaged or missing teeth. We offer fillings, crowns, and bridges to repair and replace damaged teeth, ensuring you can enjoy a complete and healthy smile.

Hygiene and Periodontics

Maintaining healthy gums is essential for overall oral health. Our Hygiene and Periodontics services include thorough cleanings and treatments for gum disease. We focus on preventing and managing gum issues to support your overall dental health.

Invisalign

For a discreet and effective way to straighten your teeth, consider Invisalign. This dental treatment uses clear aligners to gradually move your teeth into the desired position, offering a comfortable and virtually invisible alternative to traditional braces.

Root Canal Therapy

If you experience severe tooth pain or infection, Root Canal Therapy may be necessary to save your tooth. Our team performs root canal procedures with care and precision, using advanced techniques to ensure a comfortable experience and effective treatment.

Oral Surgery

For more complex dental issues, our Oral Surgery services may be required. We offer extractions and other surgical procedures with expertise, ensuring you receive the care you need with minimal discomfort.

Advanced Technology for Superior Care

At Mason Park Dental, we utilize state-of-the-art technology to enhance your dental care experience. Our advanced equipment includes:

Cone Beam Imaging: Provides detailed 3D images for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

Digital X-rays: Offer clear images with reduced radiation exposure.

Intraoral Camera: Captures high-resolution photos of your mouth, helping you understand your dental health.

Soft Tissue Laser: Used for precise and comfortable treatment of soft tissue issues.

Learn more about our technology and how it benefits your care by visiting our Technology page.
