Our Extensive Range of Services

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative Dentistry is crucial for maintaining a healthy smile and preventing future dental issues. Our Preventative Dentistry services include regular cleanings, exams, and fluoride treatments designed to detect problems early and avoid more complex procedures later.

Children’s Dentistry

Our Children’s Dentistry services are tailored to create a positive dental experience for children. Dr. Tam Nguyen, our board-certified pediatric dentist, ensures that kids receive gentle and effective care while learning the importance of good oral hygiene.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Transform your smile with our Cosmetic Dentistry options. We offer a variety of treatments, including teeth whitening, veneers, and bonding, to enhance the appearance of your teeth and boost your confidence. Our team uses advanced techniques to achieve natural-looking and beautiful results.

Restorative Dentistry

If you have damaged or missing teeth, our Restorative Dentistry services can help restore both function and esthetics. We provide treatments such as fillings, crowns, and bridges to repair and replace damaged teeth, allowing you to regain a complete and healthy smile.

Hygiene and Periodontics

Maintaining healthy gums is essential for overall oral health. Our Hygiene and Periodontics services include thorough cleanings and treatments for gum disease. By focusing on gum health, we help prevent issues affecting your teeth and overall well-being.

Invisalign

For a discreet way to straighten your teeth, consider Invisalign. This orthodontic treatment uses clear aligners to gradually shift your teeth into the correct position without traditional braces. Enjoy a straighter smile with the comfort and convenience of Invisalign.

Root Canal Therapy

If you experience severe tooth pain or infection, Root Canal Therapy may be necessary to save your tooth. Our team performs root canal procedures with precision and care, using the latest techniques to ensure a comfortable experience and effective results.

Oral Surgery

Our Oral Surgery services may be required for complex dental issues. We offer extractions and other surgical procedures performed with skill and attention to your comfort, ensuring you receive the care you need.
