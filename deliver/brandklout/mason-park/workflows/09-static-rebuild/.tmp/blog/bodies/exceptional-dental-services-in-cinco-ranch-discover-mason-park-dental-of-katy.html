Our Services

Preventative Dentistry

Prevention is key to maintaining a healthy smile. Our Preventative Dentistry services include regular cleanings, exams, and fluoride treatments designed to catch issues early and prevent future problems. By focusing on prevention, we help you avoid more complex procedures.

Children’s Dentistry

Our Children’s Dentistry services are specifically designed to make dental visits enjoyable for kids. Dr. Tam Nguyen, our board-certified pediatric dentist, creates a friendly atmosphere where children can learn about good oral hygiene practices and receive the care they need with minimal stress.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Achieve the smile you’ve always wanted with our Cosmetic Dentistry options. From teeth whitening to veneers and bonding, we offer a variety of treatments to enhance the appearance of your teeth and boost your confidence. Our team uses advanced techniques to deliver results that look natural and beautiful.

Restorative Dentistry

If you have damaged or missing teeth, our Restorative Dentistry services can help restore your oral health and functionality. We offer treatments such as fillings, crowns, and bridges to repair and replace damaged teeth, allowing you to regain a complete and healthy smile.

Hygiene and Periodontics

Maintaining healthy gums is essential for overall oral health. Our Hygiene and Periodontics services include regular cleanings and treatments for gum disease. By focusing on gum health, we help prevent issues affecting your teeth and overall well-being.

Invisalign

Invisalign offers a modern solution for those seeking a discreet way to straighten their teeth. This dental treatment uses clear aligners to gradually shift your teeth into the desired position without traditional braces. Enjoy the benefits of a straighter smile with the convenience and comfort of Invisalign.

Root Canal Therapy

If you experience severe tooth pain or infection, Root Canal Therapy may be necessary to save your tooth. Our team performs root canal procedures with precision and care, using the latest techniques to ensure a comfortable experience and effective results.

Oral Surgery

In certain situations, oral surgery may be required to address complex dental issues. Our Oral Surgery services include extractions and other surgical procedures performed with skill and attention to your comfort.
