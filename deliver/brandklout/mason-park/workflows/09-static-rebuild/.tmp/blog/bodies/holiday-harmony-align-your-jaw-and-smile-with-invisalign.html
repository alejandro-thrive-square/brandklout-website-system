FAQs

Q1: Can Invisalign really improve my jaw alignment?

Yes. Invisalign trays, especially when guided by a provider like Dr. Cho, can reposition teeth and guide the jaw into better alignment.

Q2: How long does it take to notice jaw improvement?

Most patients begin to feel relief and better bite balance within a few months, with full results in 12–18 months depending on complexity.

Q3: Is Invisalign better than braces for jaw correction?

Invisalign offers a more comfortable and discreet approach, and under top care, it can be just as effective for many jaw-related issues.

Q4: Will I need a retainer after my treatment?

Yes. A custom retainer ensures that your jaw and teeth stay in their new, ideal positions long-term.

Q5: Do I need to wear Invisalign all the time?

Yes. For best results, wear your aligners all the time except during oral hygiene. Consistency is key to achieving jaw and bite correction.
