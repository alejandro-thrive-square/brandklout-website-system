Not All Clear Aligners Are the Same

When you’re considering aligners to straighten your teeth, it’s important to understand that Invisalign and its competitors offer different levels of care, technology, and results. Dr. Dave Cho helps patients navigate these options based on their needs—not just the price tag.

Invisalign: Trusted Technology, Trusted Care

Invisalign uses SmartTrack material, designed specifically for controlled, gradual tooth movement. It’s one of the few systems that can reliably handle moderate to complex cases. What sets it apart even more is the in-office supervision—you’re never left on your own. Every step of the process is monitored and adjusted by Dr. Cho, ensuring safe, accurate results.

Alternatives Like Clear Correct and Sure Smile

Both Clear Correct and Sure Smile are FDA-approved, dentist-guided aligner systems. They use medical-grade plastic and are well-suited for mild to moderate misalignment. They’re often more affordable than Invisalign, making them good options for patients with simpler cases—without sacrificing the safety that comes from professional supervision.

The Risks of Mail-Order Aligners

Mail-order aligners may seem like a deal, often costing between $1,200 and $2,000, but they come with major compromises. Most don’t include any in-person supervision, and some don’t even require dental X-rays. This means your treatment could go off track without anyone catching it—leading to poor outcomes, delayed progress, or long-term damage.
