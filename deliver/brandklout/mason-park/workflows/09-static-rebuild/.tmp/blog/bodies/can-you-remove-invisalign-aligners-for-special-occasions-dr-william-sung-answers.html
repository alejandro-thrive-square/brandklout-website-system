Introduction to Invisalign

Since its debut in 1997 by Align Technology, Inc., Invisalign has consistently redefined dental care by offering a clear, discreet alternative to traditional metal braces. With more than 18 million smiles transformed around the globe, Invisalign’s appeal lies in its ability to progressively guide teeth into optimal alignment using custom-made, transparent aligners crafted from the advanced SmartTrack thermoplastic material, which is free from BPA, latex, and gluten. This modern approach not only enhances esthetics but also integrates seamlessly into the lifestyle of individuals from all walks of life.

The Invisalign Treatment Process

A deep understanding of the Invisalign treatment process is essential for appreciating the flexibility and effectiveness of this modern dental system. Below, we outline the key stages of treatment and how each step is designed with patient convenience in mind.

Step 1: Initial Consultation

Your journey to a transformed smile starts with an in-depth consultation at Mason Park Dental. During this session, Dr. William Sung conducts a comprehensive dental examination that includes:

Dental Evaluation: Utilizing X-rays, photographs, and in some cases, digital scans to assess your current dental alignment.

Treatment Planning: Leveraging advanced software such as ClinCheck, Dr. William Sung develops a personalized 3D treatment plan that clearly maps out the progression of teeth movement over time.

Step 2: Crafting Your Custom Aligners

Once your customized treatment plan receives the green light, the next phase involves creating your unique set of aligners. This process includes:

Digital Scanning: A state-of-the-art scanner, like the iTero Element, captures a precise 3D image of your teeth, replacing older methods that were less comfortable and less accurate.

Precision Manufacturing: Using the detailed digital impressions, Invisalign’s manufacturing facilities produce each set of aligners with precision to ensure gradual, consistent tooth movement that is tailored specifically to your dental structure.

Step 3: Fitting and Initiating Your Treatment

At your subsequent appointment, your new aligners are fitted with careful adjustments to ensure maximum comfort and effectiveness. Dr. William Sung will demonstrate the proper use and care for these aligners, emphasizing the importance of adhering to a recommended daily wear schedule to achieve the desired results.

Step 4: Monitoring and Adjustments

Regular follow-up visits—typically scheduled every 6 to 8 weeks—allow Dr. William Sung and the Mason Park Dental team to track your progress meticulously. During these appointments:

Progress Reviews: Adjustments are made, if necessary, to ensure that the movement of your teeth follows the personalized treatment plan.

Treatment Optimization: The monitoring process assures that your Invisalign journey remains consistent and effective, adapting to any minor changes in your dental alignment as needed.

Step 5: Completion and Retention

After achieving the desired alignment, the final phase focuses on preserving your new smile:

Transition to Retainers: Once your Invisalign treatment is completed, you will be fitted with a set of custom retainers—often Vivera retainers—to help maintain the position of your teeth.

Long-Term Success: Continued care during the retention phase is vital to locking in the benefits of the treatment.
