FAQs

1. How long does the full dental implant journey usually take?

Most implant journeys take several months from consultation to final restoration. Healing time varies, but the process is designed to prioritize long-term stability rather than speed.

2. Is recovery painful after implant placement?

Most patients report mild discomfort that fades quickly. Following Dental implant recovery tips from Dr. Dave Cho helps minimize soreness and supports smooth healing.

3. Will my implant look different from my natural teeth?

No. Natural-looking dental implant solutions are crafted to blend seamlessly with surrounding teeth in color, shape, and proportion.

4. Can implants replace more than one missing tooth?

Yes. Tooth replacement with modern implants can support single teeth, multiple teeth, or full restorations depending on individual needs.

5. How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular checkups, implants are designed for long-term durability and everyday use.
