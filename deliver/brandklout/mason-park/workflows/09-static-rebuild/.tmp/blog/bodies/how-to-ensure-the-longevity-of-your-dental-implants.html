Introduction – Dental Implants

Understanding dental implants starts with recognizing that they are artificial tooth roots made from titanium. Surgically placed in your jawbone, these implants serve as a stable foundation for replacement teeth, providing a strong and permanent solution for those suffering from tooth loss.

An advantage of dental implants is their ability to significantly improve quality of life. Unlike traditional methods, implants offer a permanent solution that looks and feels like natural teeth, allowing you to confidently eat, speak, and smile

Implants can also help preserve your jawbone, preventing deterioration that often occurs when teeth are lost. Immediate Post-Surgery Care

Aftercare begins immediately after your dental implant surgery. It’s vital to follow the instructions given by your dental team closely. A: Good oral hygiene is necessary to ensure the longevity of your dental implants. Brush your teeth at least twice daily with fluoride toothpaste and floss daily to remove plaque around the implant. Q: What foods should I avoid after getting dental implants?

A: After dental implant surgery, it’s advisable to avoid hard, crunchy, or sticky foods that could put stress on the implants during the healing process.
