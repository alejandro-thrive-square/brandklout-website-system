The Journey to a New Smile

Every transformation begins with a clear vision. At Mason Park Dental, the process is carefully planned to ensure long-term success and patient comfort.

Step 1: Personalized Consultation

Your journey starts with a detailed consultation. This is where your dentist evaluates your oral health, discusses your goals, and determines if Teeth replacement with full mouth implants is the right solution for you.

Digital imaging and scans help create a precise understanding of your jaw structure and bone health. This step ensures your treatment is fully customized.

Step 2: Digital Planning

Using advanced technology, your dentist maps out your entire treatment. This includes the exact placement of implants and the final look of your smile.

This stage allows you to see a preview of your transformation before the procedure even begins.

Step 3: Implant Placement

The next stage involves the Full jaw dental implant procedure. Titanium implants are carefully placed into the jawbone, where they act as strong anchors for your new teeth.

Over time, these implants fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a secure and long-lasting foundation.

Step 4: Temporary Teeth

In many cases, temporary teeth are placed on the same day or shortly after the procedure. This means you do not have to go without teeth during the healing phase.

Step 5: Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, your final set of teeth is placed. These are custom-designed to match your facial features, bite, and natural appearance.

This is where the full transformation becomes visible and life-changing.

FAQs

1. How long does a full mouth implant treatment take

The complete process can take several months, depending on healing time and individual needs. Some patients receive temporary teeth much sooner.

2. Are full mouth dental implants painful?

3. How long do full mouth implants last?

With proper care, implants can last many years. Regular dental visits and good hygiene play a key role in their longevity.

4. Can I eat normally after getting implants?

Yes, once healing is complete, you can comfortably enjoy a wide range of foods.
