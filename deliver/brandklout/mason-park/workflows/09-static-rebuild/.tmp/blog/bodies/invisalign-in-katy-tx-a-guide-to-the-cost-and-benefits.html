What is Invisalign?

Definition and explanation of Invisalign

Before we delve into the cost and benefits of Invisalign, it’s crucial to grasp the essence of this innovative treatment. Invisalign is a contemporary teeth-straightening method that employs clear aligners, offering a discreet and comfortable alternative to conventional metal braces.

These aligners are designed to gradually shift the teeth into the desired position. What sets them apart is their customization for each individual, ensuring a comfortable and effective treatment process.

The Invisalign Process

Initial consultation and treatment planning

For those considering Invisalign treatment, the journey begins with an initial consultation. Dr. Sung & Dr. Cho will assess your teeth and discuss your treatment goals during this appointment. They will create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs and desired outcomes using advanced technology, such as digital scans and imaging.

Customized aligner creation

One key benefit of Invisalign treatment is the creation of customized aligners. These clear, removable aligners are designed to shift teeth into the desired position gradually. Using the latest technology, including 3D printing, each set of aligners is precisely crafted to fit your unique smile, ensuring optimal comfort and effectiveness.

Invisalign treatment involves wearing a series of aligners, typically changing to a new set every 1-2 weeks, as directed by Dr. Sung & Dr. Cho. These aligners should be worn for 20-22 hours a day, only removing them for brushing, and flossing. The aligners should be cleaned regularly with lukewarm water and a soft toothbrush to maintain proper hygiene and clarity.

Follow-up appointments and progress tracking

Throughout your Invisalign treatment, Dr. Sung & Dr. Cho will schedule regular follow-up appointments to monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments. These appointments are crucial for tracking your teeth’s movement and ensuring your treatment is on track. Dr. Sung & Dr. Cho will provide guidance and support to help you achieve the best results from your Invisalign treatment.
