What Makes Dental Implants Different

Dental implants are small posts placed into the jaw where a tooth is missing. Over time, the bone naturally bonds with the implant, creating a stable anchor for a replacement tooth. This connection allows implants to function more like natural teeth than other options.

What truly sets implants apart is not just that they replace a tooth above the gum line. They replace the missing root below it. That detail matters more than most people realize.

When a tooth is lost, the jaw no longer receives stimulation in that area. Without stimulation, bone can slowly weaken and shrink. Implants restore that stimulation, helping the jaw stay strong and supported.

This is one of the reasons Dr. Dave Cho explains implant advantages as being rooted in both structure and comfort, not just appearance.
