The Finder

Start from what is wrong.

What brought you here on the left, where the practice would begin under it. Nothing is started on the day you arrive.

  • 01 A toothache, or something broke today Where we startSame day slot. Emergency dentist; root canal if the nerve is involved.
  • 02 A chipped or discoloured front tooth Where we startBonding in one visit; veneers when shade and shape both change.
  • 03 A missing tooth, or several Where we startImplants placed and restored in house, or bridges and dentures.
  • 04 Crooked or crowded teeth Where we startInvisalign®, planned on a 3D scan and priced first. Teens and adults.
  • 05 A cracked tooth, an old filling, a large cavity Where we startA tooth coloured filling, or a crown when there is not enough tooth left.
  • 06 Bleeding gums, or a long time since a cleaning Where we startA deep cleaning, then ongoing gum care with the same hygienist.
  • 07 Jaw pain, clicking, or grinding at night Where we startTMJ and TMD treatment, and a custom night guard.
  • 08 Anxious about the chair Where we startNitrous, oral or IV sedation, so a long appointment is one appointment.
  • 09 A wisdom tooth that has to come out Where we startExtractions, straightforward and surgical, in the same building.
  • 10 A plan from elsewhere you are not sure about Where we startA free second opinion. Bring the plan in.
