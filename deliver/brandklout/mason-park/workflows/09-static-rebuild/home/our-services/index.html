The Finder

Start from what is wrong.

Left, the thing that brought you here. Right, where the practice would begin. A first visit settles which; nothing is started on the day you arrive.

A toothache, or something broke today
Same day slots for pain, breaks and knocked out teeth. Emergency dentist, and if the nerve is involved, root canal therapy.
A chipped or discoloured front tooth
Bonding in a single visit for chips and gaps; porcelain veneers when shade and shape both need to change.
A missing tooth, or several
Implant placement and implant restorations in house, or bridges and dentures, full and partial.
Crooked or crowded teeth
Invisalign®, planned on a 3D scan and priced before it starts. Teens and adults.
A cracked tooth, an old filling, a large cavity
Tooth coloured fillings, or a crown when there is not enough tooth left to fill.
Bleeding gums, or a long time since a cleaning
A deep cleaning, scaling and root planing, and ongoing gum care.
Jaw pain, clicking, or grinding at night
TMJ and TMD treatment, and a custom night guard.
A child’s first visit, or a tongue tie
Dr. Nguyen, board certified pediatric dentist, from six months old. Children’s dentistry and frenectomy.
Anxious about the chair
Sedation dentistry: nitrous oxide, oral conscious sedation and IV sedation, so a long appointment is one appointment.
A wisdom tooth that has to come out
Extractions, straightforward and surgical, and oral surgery in the same building.
