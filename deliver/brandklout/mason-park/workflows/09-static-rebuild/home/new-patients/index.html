The Questions

What first visits ask.

What should I expect during my first visit?

During your first visit, we focus on providing a comprehensive new patient experience. You will begin by filling out some paperwork that includes your health history. Afterwards, either Dr. Dave Cho or Dr. William Sung will conduct a thorough dental exam. This will include checking your teeth and gums, evaluating your oral health, and discussing any concerns you may have.

Are there any new patient specials available for my first visit?

Yes, we offer new patient specials. These promotions can make your first visit more affordable while ensuring you receive the top-quality care you deserve. Please contact our office directly to learn more about the current new patient specials we have available.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

To make the most of your first appointment, please bring your insurance card (if applicable), a valid ID, and a list of medications you currently take. This information will help us provide the best new patient care and assess your dental needs accurately.

Can I request a tour of the practice before becoming a new patient?

At Mason Park Dental, we understand that choosing a dental practice is an important decision. We encourage prospective patients to schedule a tour of our facility. This allows you to meet our staff, view our treatment areas, and understand our welcoming environment.

There is no fee to tour our dental practice. We believe in providing transparency and comfort to our prospective patients, so we welcome you to visit us at no cost.

How do Dr. Dave Cho and Dr. William Sung approach new patient care?

Dr. Dave Cho and Dr. William Sung prioritize personalized care for each new patient. They take the time to listen to your concerns, answer any questions, and discuss the best treatment options for you. They aim to create a welcoming environment that fosters trust and open communication about your dental health.
