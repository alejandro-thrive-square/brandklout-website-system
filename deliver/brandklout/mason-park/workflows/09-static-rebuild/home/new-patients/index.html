The First Visit

What happens, in order.

01

Book it

Pick a time online or ring the front desk. Tell us who is coming; adults and children can share one afternoon.

02

Forms, ahead of time

The new patient forms can be completed before you arrive. Ask the front desk to send them when you book, and the visit starts in the chair rather than on a clipboard.

03

Arrive

Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of the medications you take. Free parking at the door, suite 104A on the ground floor.

04

The exam

A thorough exam of your teeth and gums, digital x-rays, and the intra-oral camera, so everything is on a screen you can see too.

05

The plan and the price

You sit with Dr. Sung or Dr. Cho, say what concerns you, and the plan is written out with what insurance covers and what it does not. Nothing is started today.

06

The cleaning, and the next one

A cleaning with the hygienist, then a recall you do not have to remember. The same hygienist sees you next time.

The Questions

What first visits ask.

What should I expect during my first visit?

During your first visit, we focus on providing a comprehensive new patient experience. You will begin by filling out some paperwork that includes your health history. Afterwards, either Dr. William Sung or Dr. Dave Cho will conduct a thorough dental exam. This will include checking your teeth and gums, evaluating your oral health, and discussing any concerns you may have.

Are there any new patient specials available for my first visit?

Yes, we offer new patient specials. These promotions can make your first visit more affordable while ensuring you receive the top-quality care you deserve. Please contact our office directly to learn more about the current new patient specials we have available.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

To make the most of your first appointment, please bring your insurance card (if applicable), a valid ID, and a list of medications you currently take. This information will help us provide the best new patient care and assess your dental needs accurately.

Can I request a tour of the practice before becoming a new patient?

At Mason Park Dental, we understand that choosing a dental practice is an important decision. We encourage prospective patients to schedule a tour of our facility. This allows you to meet our staff, view our treatment areas, and understand our welcoming environment.

There is no fee to tour our dental practice. We believe in providing transparency and comfort to our prospective patients, so we welcome you to visit us at no cost.

How do Dr. William Sung and Dr. Dave Cho approach new patient care?

Dr. William Sung and Dr. Dave Cho prioritize personalized care for each new patient. They take the time to listen to your concerns, answer any questions, and discuss the best treatment options for you. They aim to create a welcoming environment that fosters trust and open communication about your dental health.
