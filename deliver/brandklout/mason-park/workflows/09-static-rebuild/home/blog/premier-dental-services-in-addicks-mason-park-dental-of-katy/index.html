    If you’re in Addick’s and looking for exceptional dental care, Mason Park Dental of Katy is the premier choice. Located conveniently at 21830 Kingsland Blvd #104A, Katy, TX 77450, our practice is dedicated to providing top-notch dental services in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Under the expertise of Dr. William Sung, Dr. Tam Nguyen, and our skilled team, we deliver a wide range of dental treatments using the latest advancements in technology.

    Why Choose Mason Park Dental of Katy in Addicks?

    Comprehensive Dental Care for All Ages

    At Mason Park Dental, we offer a broad spectrum of dental services to cater to every need, from preventative care to advanced restorative procedures. Our commitment is to ensure that every patient receives personalized care tailored to their unique dental health requirements.

    Our Extensive Range of Services

    Preventative Dentistry

    Our Preventative Dentistry services are essential for maintaining oral health and preventing future dental issues. Regular check-ups, cleanings, and fluoride treatments help you avoid potential problems and keep your smile healthy.

    Children’s Dentistry

    We understand the importance of making dental visits a positive experience for children. Our Children’s Dentistry services are designed to provide gentle and effective care for your little ones, ensuring their dental health is well taken care of from a young age.

    Cosmetic Dentistry

    Enhance the beauty of your smile with our Cosmetic Dentistry treatments. Whether you’re interested in teeth whitening, veneers, or dental bonding, we offer various solutions to help you achieve a radiant and confident smile.

    Restorative Dentistry

    Our restorative dentistry services provide effective solutions for those needing repairs or replacements for damaged or missing teeth. From fillings and crowns to bridges and implants, we restore teeth’s function and appearance.

    Hygiene and Periodontics

    Maintaining healthy gums is crucial for overall oral health. Our Hygiene and Periodontics services focus on preventing and treating gum disease through comprehensive cleanings and advanced therapies.

    Invisalign

    Achieve a straighter smile with Invisalign, a clear aligner system that offers a discreet and comfortable alternative to traditional braces. Learn more about how Invisalign can help you straighten your teeth without the hassle of metal braces.

    Root Canal Therapy

    If you’re experiencing severe tooth pain or infection, Root Canal Therapy may be necessary to save your tooth. Our team performs root canals with precision to relieve pain and restore dental health.

    Oral Surgery

    For complex dental issues that require surgical intervention, our Oral Surgery services include extractions and other procedures to address your needs with care and expertise.

    Cutting-Edge Technology for Superior Results

    Mason Park Dental uses the latest dental technology to enhance your treatment experience and outcomes. Our state-of-the-art equipment includes:

    Cone Beam Imaging: Provides detailed 3D images for accurate diagnostics and treatment planning.

    Digital X-rays: Offers clear imaging with minimal radiation exposure.

    Intraoral Camera: Captures high-resolution images of your mouth for better diagnostics and communication.

    Soft Tissue Laser: Used for precise and comfortable treatment of soft tissue issues.

    For more details about our technology and how it benefits your care, visit our Technology page.

    Convenient Scheduling and Emergency Care

    We prioritize your convenience with flexible scheduling options to fit your busy life. In a dental emergency, we are equipped to provide prompt and efficient care to address urgent issues and alleviate discomfort.

    Meet Our Experienced Dental Team

    Our team at Mason Park Dental is dedicated to providing exceptional care in a friendly and professional environment. Meet our skilled professionals:

    Dr. William Sung: A general dentist who offers comprehensive care focusing on patient comfort.

    Dr. Tam Nguyen: A board-certified pediatric dentist specializing in gentle care for children.

    Dr. Dave Cho: A general dentist committed to personalized and attentive care.

    Visit our Meet the Team page to learn more about our team and their dedication to your dental health.

    Contact Us Today

    Experience the difference in dental care at Mason Park Dental of Katy. Schedule your appointment today, and let us help you achieve a healthier, more beautiful smile!
