The Treatment

Two options, and they are not the same thing.

The practice offers both, and which suits you is mostly a question of how fast you want it and how sensitive your teeth are.

Professional, rather than off the shelf

Professional-grade agents, applied to teeth that have just been examined and cleaned. That combination is what a supermarket strip cannot do.

In the practice’s words

In-office whitening. Achieve noticeably whiter teeth in just one visit. Our in-office whitening treatment uses powerful, safe whitening agents that can lift years of deep stains from coffee, wine, smoking, and aging.

Take-home whitening kits. Prefer a more gradual approach? Our customized take-home trays allow you to brighten your smile conveniently and comfortably at your own pace, with professional-grade results.

Where whitening appears elsewhere at this practice

  • With Invisalign®A professional whitening gel (Kör) is included in every Invisalign treatment plan here.
  • With the laserThe dental laser is used to activate whitening agents, as one of its five uses.
  • With AIRFLOW®Not whitening as such — but removing surface stain brightens teeth naturally, and sometimes that is all that was needed.
  • Inside a smile makeoverWhere it is one of five treatments, and almost always the first one done.

Whichever you choose, it is the same category of stain being lifted: deep discolouration from coffee, wine, smoking and simply getting older.

Three things worth knowing first

Whitening is simple, and it has three limits that catch people out.

  • It only lightens enamelExisting fillings, crowns, bonding and veneers do not change colour. If you have work on the front teeth, whiten first and match the new shade afterwards.
  • Health comes firstDecay and gum disease are treated before cosmetic work. Whitening over an untreated cavity is uncomfortable and pointless.
  • Some stains will not liftDiscolouration that does not respond is covered rather than bleached — with bonding or veneers. Dr. Sung will say which yours is.

Questions

Questions patients ask before they decide.

In-office or take-home?

In-office gives noticeably whiter teeth in a single visit, using powerful agents applied here — good when there is a date in the diary. Take-home trays are customised to your teeth and let you brighten gradually at your own pace, which suits sensitive teeth better.

What kind of stains does it remove?

Years of deep stains from coffee, wine, smoking and aging. Surface stain can sometimes be removed by an AIRFLOW® cleaning alone, which brightens teeth naturally without bleaching.

Will my fillings and crowns whiten too?

No. Whitening lightens enamel, not restorations. Fillings, crowns, bonding and veneers keep the shade they were made in, which is why whitening is done before anything is colour-matched.

What if whitening does not work on my teeth?

Some discolouration does not respond to bleaching. In that case it is covered rather than lightened — with bonding for a single tooth, or porcelain veneers across several. Dr. Sung will tell you which category yours is at the consultation.

Is it included with anything?

A professional whitening gel is included in every Invisalign® treatment plan at this practice.

Should I have a cleaning first?

Yes. Whitening works on clean enamel, and a cleaning and exam also confirms there is no decay or gum disease to treat first.
