The Treatment

What it is, and what it involves.

What is a dental bridge?

A false tooth anchored between two crowns, which sit on the natural teeth either side of the gap — or on implants. Fixed in place, not removable.

In the practice’s words

Losing a tooth doesn’t just affect the way your smile looks — it can impact how you chew, speak, and even the alignment of your remaining teeth. Dental bridges are designed to replace missing teeth by anchoring a false tooth (or teeth) between two crowns placed on adjacent natural teeth or dental implants. A fixed dental bridge can restore both function and esthetics, preventing future oral health complications.

At Mason Park Dental, we are proud to offer dental bridges in Katy, TX that are crafted using the latest materials for strength, comfort, and a natural appearance. We design every fixed dental bridge to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth.

Four kinds, and when each one is used

  • TraditionalCrowns on the teeth either side of the gap, a pontic between them. The common one, when both neighbours are healthy.
  • CantileverOnly one neighbouring tooth to work with. For parts of the mouth that take less bite pressure.
  • MarylandA framework bonded behind the neighbouring teeth. Minimal alteration to them, often for a front tooth.
  • Implant‑supportedAnchored to implants in the jawbone rather than to natural teeth. For several missing teeth in a row.

The full detail of each visit

Step 1: Consultation and examination. We begin with a comprehensive oral examination to assess the extent of tooth loss and overall dental health. We will discuss your options for dental bridges in Katy, TX, including materials and types that suit your needs.

Step 2: Tooth preparation. For traditional bridges, the adjacent teeth (abutment teeth) are prepared by removing a small portion of enamel to make room for the crowns. Digital impressions are then taken to ensure a precise fit.

Step 3: Temporary bridge placement. While your permanent bridge is being crafted in the lab, a temporary bridge will protect the prepared teeth and maintain your smile’s appearance.

Step 4: Permanent bridge placement. Once your customized bridge is ready, Dr. Sung will carefully fit and adjust it for maximum comfort and esthetic appeal. The bridge will be cemented into place, providing a fixed dental bridge solution you can rely on.

Questions

Questions patients ask before they decide.

What is a dental bridge for?

A dental bridge is used to fill the space left by one or more missing teeth. It restores the appearance, bite function, and prevents other teeth from shifting out of place.

How long does a dental bridge last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, a fixed dental bridge can last anywhere between 5 to 15 years or even longer.

Is getting a dental bridge painful?

The process is minimally invasive and typically involves local anesthesia. Patients generally experience little to no discomfort during and after placement.

Can I eat normally with a dental bridge?

Yes. After a short adjustment period, you should be able to enjoy most foods without difficulty. Your bridge for missing teeth will feel secure and natural.

How do I care for my dental bridge?

Maintaining a bridge is similar to caring for your natural teeth.

  • Brush twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush.
  • Floss daily using special floss threaders or water flossers designed for bridge maintenance.
  • Schedule regular cleanings and checkups so the bridge and the teeth around it are monitored.
  • Avoid very hard foods that could stress or damage the bridgework.
Should I get a bridge or an implant?

A bridge relies on the neighbouring teeth for support, which makes it the faster solution — often completed in a few weeks. An implant requires surgical placement into the jawbone and a longer healing time, but does not affect the teeth beside it. Dr. William Sung performs a thorough evaluation and recommends the option that serves your long-term oral health, rather than defaulting to either.
