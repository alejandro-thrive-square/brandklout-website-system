More reasons patients choose it

Consider Invisalign® if:

  • You want a discreet way to straighten your teeth. Unlike metal braces, Invisalign® aligners are clear and virtually unnoticeable, allowing you to smile confidently throughout your treatment.
  • You prefer a more comfortable dental experience. With smooth plastic aligners, you avoid the irritation caused by metal wires and brackets.
  • You have mild to moderate alignment issues. Invisalign® effectively treats cases such as overcrowding, gaps, mild bite issues, and slight tooth misalignment.
  • You’re a teenager or an adult seeking a flexible solution. Whether you’re in high school, college, or the professional world, Invisalign® provides an adaptable treatment that fits seamlessly into your lifestyle.
  • You want the freedom to eat and drink without restrictions. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign® aligners can be removed when eating, allowing you to enjoy all your favorite foods without worry.
  • You value convenience in your dental care routine. Since the aligners are removable, you can brush and floss your teeth normally, making it easier to maintain good oral hygiene throughout your treatment.
  • You have a busy lifestyle and need a low-maintenance option. Invisalign® requires fewer dental visits compared to traditional braces, making it a great choice for individuals with demanding schedules.

Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign® clear aligners offer a removable and virtually invisible way to straighten teeth, allowing patients to enjoy their favorite foods and maintain their oral hygiene routines with ease. If you’re looking for a comfortable, discreet, and effective way to achieve a straighter smile, Invisalign® could be the perfect solution for you.

The full detail of each visit

Step 1: Initial consultation. Your Invisalign® journey begins with a consultation with Dr. Dave Cho. During this visit, he will carefully assess your teeth, discuss your dental history, and understand your goals for a straighter, healthier smile. This is the perfect opportunity to ask questions about the process, expected results, and any concerns you may have.

Step 2: Digital scanning and customized treatment plan. Next, we use advanced 3D imaging technology to take precise digital scans of your teeth. These scans allow us to create a fully customized treatment plan that maps out each step of your Invisalign® journey. You’ll even get a preview of how your smile will transform over time. Unlike traditional impressions, our digital approach ensures accuracy and comfort.

Step 3: Aligner fitting and wear instructions. Once your aligners are ready, you’ll receive your first set, custom-made to fit snugly over your teeth. Dr. Cho will provide detailed instructions on how to wear them, emphasizing the importance of keeping them in place for 20–22 hours per day. You’ll only remove them for eating, drinking (except for water), and oral hygiene. Wearing your aligners as directed is key to achieving the best results within the estimated timeframe.

Step 4: Periodic progress checkups. Every 4–6 weeks, you’ll return for a progress checkup, where Dr. Cho will evaluate your teeth’s movement and provide your next set of aligners. Each new set gradually shifts your teeth closer to their ideal positions. These visits are essential to ensure your treatment stays on track and adjustments can be made if necessary.

Step 5: Completion and retainers. Once your Invisalign® treatment is complete, you’ll see the beautifully straight smile you’ve been working toward. To maintain your results, Dr. Cho will recommend a retainer to keep your teeth in their new positions. Wearing your retainer as instructed helps prevent shifting and preserves your confident new smile for years to come.

Questions

Questions patients ask before they decide.

How long does Invisalign® treatment take?

Most patients complete treatment in 12–18 months, but some may see results in as little as 6 months.

Can I eat and drink while wearing Invisalign®?

You should remove aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water to prevent damage or staining.

Is Invisalign® painful?

Some patients experience mild discomfort as their teeth adjust, but it is far less painful than traditional braces.

How often do I need to visit the dentist during treatment?

You will need check-ups every 4–6 weeks to monitor progress and receive new aligners.

Does Invisalign® work for severe misalignment?

Invisalign® can treat most cases, but severe alignment issues may require alternative treatments.

Why is Invisalign® a good option for teens?

If you’re a parent, or a teen yourself, exploring orthodontic options, Invisalign® offers some benefits tailored to teenagers:

  • Nearly invisible. Invisalign® aligners are clear and discreet, making it easier for teens to smile confidently during treatment, without the need to hide in photos or worry about school events.
  • Comfort first. With smooth, custom-fit aligners, none of the poking or irritation comes with traditional braces.
  • Removable for real life. Aligners can be taken out for brushing, flossing, sports, and oral hygiene, so there are no food restrictions.
  • Fewer emergencies. Since there are no brackets or wires, there is much less risk of sudden orthodontic emergencies or surprise trips to fix broken hardware.
  • Faster treatment timeline. Many teens complete their Invisalign® treatment in less time compared to traditional braces, depending on their specific alignment needs.
Does dental insurance cover Invisalign® treatment?

Many dental insurance plans do offer coverage for Invisalign® as part of their orthodontic benefits. Each plan is a little different so coverage amounts can vary. In general, your insurance may pay a portion of your treatment cost, similar to how it would for traditional braces.

We’re happy to help you navigate your individual coverage details and maximize your benefits. If you’re unsure about your insurance, our team can check your plan and explain any out-of-pocket expenses before you get started. Flexible payment plans are available to make your new smile affordable, with no unexpected costs along the way.
