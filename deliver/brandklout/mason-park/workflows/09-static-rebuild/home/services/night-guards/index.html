Is this you

You cannot feel it happening. You can see what it leaves.

Bruxism is unconscious, so nobody arrives sure they have it. The evidence is in the morning, and in the mirror.

01

Morning headaches

Present on waking rather than building through the day.

02

A sore or stiff jaw

Muscles that have been working all night, and a joint that clicks.

03

Teeth that have flattened

Edges worn level, and teeth visibly shorter than they were.

04

Sensitivity you cannot explain

Hot and cold reaching the nerve because the enamel over it has gone.

And the damage that has already started, if you recognise those

  • EnamelWorn thin. It is the hardest tissue in the body and it does not regenerate.
  • FracturesTiny cracks through the teeth, which become a crown later.
  • GumsReceding under the same force.
  • FillingsLoosened, and restorations chipped.
  • The jointStrain on the temporomandibular joint, which becomes TMD.

The Treatment

What it is, and how it works.

What is a night guard?

A durable barrier between the upper and lower teeth, made to the shape of your own mouth. It does not stop you grinding — it makes the grinding harmless, and takes the load off the joint while it does.

In the practice’s words

Night guards are specialized dental appliances designed to protect your teeth while you sleep. Custom-made to fit the unique shape of your mouth, they create a comfortable, durable barrier between your upper and lower teeth — minimizing the harmful effects of nighttime habits like grinding and clenching.

At Mason Park Dental, your night guard will be designed specifically to address your grinding pattern, bite alignment, and jaw tension points for optimum protection and relief. Our custom night guards are crafted from premium materials, offering a thinner, more comfortable experience compared to store-bought versions.

Four things it does while you are asleep

  • Cushions the grindingThe teeth do not make contact, so the pressure is absorbed instead of transmitted. No chips, no fractures, no flattening.
  • Relaxes the jawIt repositions the jaw slightly, taking strain off the muscles and the temporomandibular joint.
  • Shields the enamelThe outer surface of a tooth does not regenerate once worn away. This is the only part of the damage that is genuinely irreversible.
  • Improves your sleepClenched muscles disrupt sleep cycles. Less tension is deeper, more restorative sleep and less morning fatigue.

What a night guard does for a TMJ disorder

Temporomandibular joint disorders often arise from stress, misalignment, or unconscious habits like clenching or grinding. A custom night guard can:

  • Reduce pressure on the jaw joints.
  • Prevent further inflammation.
  • Improve jaw mobility and comfort.
  • Decrease associated headaches and ear pain.

By realigning your bite and cushioning your jaw, night guards for TMJ promote healing and protect against further damage. If the joint itself is the problem rather than the grinding, see TMJ and TMD.

The Alternatives

What else is on the table.

Wearing one, against waiting to see.

A guard now vs Leaving it
The enamel you have is the enamel you keep. It wears away, and does not come back.
One appliance. A crown per cracked tooth, eventually.
Jaw strain eases within weeks. Strain becomes a diagnosed TMJ disorder.
Deeper sleep, less morning fatigue. Disrupted sleep cycles, indefinitely.

A night guard is not a cure for bruxism. It is the thing that stops bruxism costing you teeth while you deal with what is causing it.

And the one from the pharmacy?

Over-the-counter guards are convenient and they do work, up to a point. The practice is direct about where that point is.

  • BulkyWhich is why so many end up in a drawer after a week.
  • Poorly fittedA guard that moves in the night protects unevenly, and can shift your bite.
  • Limited protectionThinner material that wears down under real grinding pressure.
  • Short-livedMonths rather than years, so the cost difference is smaller than it first looks.

If a store-bought one is what you have and you are wearing it every night, that is better than a custom one you have not booked. Come in when you are ready.

Questions

Questions patients ask before they decide.

How do I know I grind my teeth if I am asleep?

You usually do not, which is why it goes untreated for years. The signs are morning headaches, a sore jaw, flattened or shortened teeth, receding gums, loose fillings and unexplained sensitivity. Dr. Sung can read the wear pattern on your teeth at an exam and tell you.

How long does it take to get used to?

Most patients find they adapt within a few nights. A custom guard is thinner and better fitted than a store-bought one, which is most of the reason.

Will it stop me grinding?

No, and it is not meant to. It stops the grinding from doing damage: the teeth do not contact each other, the pressure is absorbed, and the jaw sits in a more relaxed position. The grinding itself usually has a cause — often stress or bite misalignment — worth addressing separately.

Will it help my TMJ pain?

It can. A night guard reduces pressure on the jaw joints, prevents further inflammation, improves jaw mobility and comfort, and decreases the headaches and ear pain that come with a TMJ disorder. See TMJ and TMD for the joint itself.

How long will one last?

With proper care, a custom night guard from Mason Park Dental can last for years, which is why it works out cost-effective against replacing a store-bought guard every few months.

I already have crowns and veneers. Does that change anything?

It makes the guard more urgent, not less. Grinding chips and loosens restorations, and replacing them costs considerably more than protecting them. Tell Dr. Sung what work you have had done so the guard is designed around it.
