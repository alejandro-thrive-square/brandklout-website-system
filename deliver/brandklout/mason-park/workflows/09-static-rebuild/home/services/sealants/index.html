The Treatment

What it is, and what it involves.

What is a dental sealant?

An ultra-thin coating of plastic or resin painted onto the chewing surfaces of the back teeth. It fills in the deep grooves and pits so food and bacteria cannot sit in them.

In the practice’s words

Dental sealants are ultra-thin, protective coatings made of plastic or resin that are applied to the chewing surfaces of your back teeth — the molars and premolars. These areas are especially prone to cavities due to their deep grooves, pits, and uneven surfaces, which easily trap food particles and bacteria. Even with good brushing habits, these areas can be difficult to keep clean.

Sealants act like a shield, sealing off the grooves of your molars and blocking out harmful bacteria, plaque, and food debris. At Mason Park Dental of Katy, we use only high-quality, BPA-free sealant materials that are safe for children and adults alike. Sealants are colorless or slightly white in appearance and blend naturally with your teeth.

Six things the practice says a sealant does

  • Stops cavities startingA physical barrier keeping bacteria and acids out of the deep grooves in the molars.
  • Avoids later workNo cavity means no filling, no crown and no root canal on that tooth.
  • Painless and quickApplied in minutes, no drilling, no anaesthetic, no discomfort.
  • Works at any ageOften applied to children’s teeth, and effective for adults too.
  • Cost-effectiveThe cheapest possible version of every restorative treatment it prevents.
  • It lastsFive to ten years, and longer with proper care.

Keeping them, and why patients choose this practice for them
  • Brush and floss regularly to prevent plaque building up on the teeth around the sealed ones.
  • Limit hard or sticky foods — caramel, ice, jawbreakers — which can dislodge a sealant.
  • Come in every six months so we can monitor the sealants alongside everything else.

The Path

What happens, step by step.

01

Cleaning and drying

The molars and premolars are cleaned thoroughly and dried. A sealant will not bond over plaque.

02

Etching

A gentle solution roughens the enamel very slightly, so the resin has something to grip. Painless.

03

Application

The sealant is painted onto the chewing surface and flows into the grooves.

04

Curing

A curing light hardens it into a strong bond, then it is checked for smoothness and fit. You can eat straight away.

Questions

Questions patients ask before they decide.

What are dental sealants?

Thin, protective coatings applied to the back teeth to help prevent cavities and tooth decay.

How long do dental sealants last?

Anywhere from 5 to 10 years, and in some cases longer with proper dental care.

Are dental sealants safe?

Yes. Sealants are made from materials approved by the American Dental Association, and Mason Park Dental uses only BPA-free sealants.

Does it hurt?

Not at all. The process is quick and non-invasive, and requires no drilling and no anaesthetic.

Can adults benefit from sealants?

Yes. Adults with deep grooves and cavity-prone teeth who have not had previous fillings in those teeth can benefit greatly.

Can a sealant be reapplied?

Yes. If a sealant wears out over time it can be reapplied quickly and easily during a checkup, which is one reason we look at them every six months.
