The Treatment

What it is, and what it involves.

What is a custom mouth guard?

An appliance moulded to the exact shape of your teeth and gums, rather than boiled into an approximation of them. Thinner, more secure, and — the part that matters — comfortable enough that you keep wearing it.

In the practice’s words

A custom mouth guard from Dr. William Sung is a small investment in long-term oral health. Unlike generic options from sporting goods stores or pharmacies, our custom-fit guards offer comfort, durability, and maximum protection.

Precision fit: molds perfectly to your teeth for security and ease of use. Comfortable wear: no bulkiness or shifting during use. Improved performance: athletes breathe easier and grind less with a better fit. Durability: constructed from top-quality materials that outlast store-bought alternatives.

Three kinds, and what each one is for

  • SportsImpact injuries to teeth and jaw in contact sport. It also absorbs shock from a blow to the jaw, and is thin enough to breathe and talk through.
  • NightBruxism — grinding in your sleep. It cushions the teeth and takes the pressure off the clench.
  • TMJRepositions the jaw slightly to relieve pressure on the temporomandibular joints, reducing pain, stiffness and the clicking.
  • Children and teensLightweight versions for developing smiles, for sport or for grinding. Early use prevents the serious problems later.

Questions

Questions patients ask before they decide.

Is a custom guard really better than a store-bought one?

Yes, and the biggest reason is not protection but compliance. Store-bought guards are often bulky, uncomfortable, or poorly fitted; they slip, cause discomfort, and end up unused. A custom guard fits perfectly, lasts longer, protects more effectively, and is easier to wear consistently.

How do I clean it?

Rinse with cool water after each use, clean it with a soft toothbrush and mild soap, air dry it completely, and store it in a ventilated container. Avoid hot water — it warps the material. With good care a guard lasts several years.

Can my child have one?

Yes. Children and teens benefit from custom guards, especially if they play sports or grind at night. The pediatric versions are lightweight and comfortable, and early use prevents serious dental problems later.

Will it help my jaw pain?

Often. A guard gently repositions the jaw into a more relaxed state, reducing muscle strain and promoting healthier joint alignment. Many patients report a marked improvement in jaw comfort, less clicking and popping, and fewer headaches. If you have been diagnosed with a TMJ disorder, see TMJ and TMD.

Does it make sport harder?

The opposite, generally. Because custom guards are thinner and properly contoured compared with one-size-fits-all versions, they allow easier talking, breathing and hydration during play.

I only play non-contact sport. Do I need one?

Skateboarding, cycling and gymnastics all produce the same falls, and accidents happen in any of them. A guard protects without compromising performance or breathability.
