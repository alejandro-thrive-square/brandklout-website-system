The Treatment

What it is, and what it removes.

What is AIRFLOW® cleaning?

A controlled mist of air, water and a fine powder — usually erythritol or bicarbonate — sprayed at the tooth. It lifts biofilm, plaque and surface stain without a metal instrument ever touching the enamel.

In the practice’s words

AIRFLOW® teeth cleaning is a dental hygiene technique that uses a controlled mixture of air, water, and specially formulated powders to eliminate dental biofilm, plaque, and surface stains. Unlike traditional scaling, this method is gentle on teeth and gums, highly effective for stain and biofilm removal, and efficient for a deep, thorough cleaning.

Using the EMS AIRFLOW® system, patients at Mason Park Dental of Katy experience a comfortable cleaning without the discomfort often associated with traditional dental scalers.

Biofilm, and why it is the actual target

  • What it isA sticky, colourless layer of bacteria that forms on teeth and under the gums. You cannot see it, which is most of the problem.
  • Gum diseaseUntreated biofilm is where periodontal disease begins.
  • Tooth decayThe same bacteria producing the same acid, in a layer a brush skims over.
  • Bad breathHalitosis that survives brushing is usually biofilm.
  • Implant failureBiofilm around an implant is what loses it.

Three things people get wrong about it
  • “It’s only cosmetic.” It does whiten teeth naturally by removing stains, but its primary goal is plaque and biofilm removal, which is a health treatment rather than a cosmetic one.
  • “It’s painful, like a normal cleaning.” It is designed to be pain-free, even for patients with highly sensitive teeth or gum disease.
  • “It can’t replace deep cleaning.” For many patients, especially those in maintenance therapy, it can reduce the need for invasive scaling or root planing. It does not replace a deep cleaning where the pockets call for one.

Why patients choose this practice for it: the latest EMS AIRFLOW® system, personalised attention, a focus on gentle and stress-free treatment, and a welcoming environment designed for comfort.

Questions

Questions patients ask before they decide.

Is it good for sensitive teeth?

It is one of the main reasons the practice offers it. Unlike traditional tools that can irritate exposed dentine or gums, AIRFLOW® minimises contact with the tooth surface, avoids scraping and vibration, and uses a fine powder for a softer touch.

Is this just a cosmetic polish?

No. It does whiten teeth naturally by removing surface stains, but its primary purpose is plaque and biofilm removal, which is what prevents cavities, gum disease and infection.

Can it replace a deep cleaning?

For many patients, especially those in maintenance therapy, it can reduce the need for invasive scaling or root planing. Where pocket depths already indicate periodontal disease, a deep cleaning is still the right treatment.

Is it safe with braces or implants?

Yes, and it is better than the alternative for both. It is safe around braces, Invisalign attachments and implants, where traditional scaling is limited.

What is the powder?

A specially formulated cleaning powder, usually erythritol or bicarbonate based, delivered in a controlled mixture with air and water.

What is biofilm, exactly?

A sticky, colourless layer of bacteria that forms on teeth and under the gums. Left alone it causes gum disease, tooth decay, bad breath and implant failure. AIRFLOW® disrupts and removes it more effectively than traditional methods.
