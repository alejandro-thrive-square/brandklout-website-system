What Invisalign® is

A series of clear, removable aligners, each custom-made, applying gentle controlled pressure to move teeth over time. You change to a new set periodically as the smile improves.

Questions

Twenty-one questions, in seven groups.

Clear aligner basics

What is Invisalign® treatment?

Invisalign® is a teeth-straightening system that uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gradually move teeth into proper alignment. Each aligner is custom-made for precision and comfort.

How does Invisalign® work?

Aligners apply gentle, controlled pressure to shift teeth over time. You’ll switch to a new set periodically as your smile improves.

Does Invisalign® look natural?

Yes. Invisalign® aligners are virtually invisible, allowing you to straighten your teeth discreetly.

Safety and effectiveness

Is Invisalign® safe?

Yes. Invisalign® aligners are made from BPA-free, medical-grade material and are safe for daily use.

Does Invisalign® really work?

Yes. Invisalign® effectively treats crowding, spacing, and bite-related concerns when worn as instructed.

Is Invisalign® effective for adults and teens?

Yes. Invisalign® is suitable for both teens and adults with mild to moderate alignment concerns.

Comfort and daily life

Is Invisalign® painful?

Some mild pressure may occur when switching aligners, but discomfort is temporary and minimal.

Can I eat and drink with Invisalign®?

Aligners should be removed before eating or drinking anything other than water, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods.

Can I brush and floss normally?

Yes. Invisalign® aligners are removable, making daily oral hygiene simple and effective.

Treatment timeline

How long does Invisalign® treatment take?

Treatment length varies based on individual needs. Some patients see results in a few months, while others require longer treatment.

How often are dental visits needed?

Visits are usually scheduled every few weeks to monitor progress and provide new aligners.

Can Invisalign® offer faster results than braces?

In many cases, Invisalign® can provide results in less time compared to traditional metal braces.

Candidacy and age

Who is a good candidate for Invisalign®?

Teens and adults with mild to moderate spacing, crowding, or bite concerns are typically good candidates.

Is Invisalign® suitable for teenagers?

Yes. Invisalign® is a popular option for teens due to its comfort, appearance, and flexibility.

Can adults straighten their teeth with Invisalign®?

Absolutely. Invisalign® is an excellent choice for adults of all ages.

Insurance and financing

Does dental insurance cover Invisalign®?

Many dental insurance plans include benefits that apply to Invisalign® treatment. Our team can help verify your coverage. See insurance and financing.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. We offer flexible financing options to help make Invisalign® treatment accessible.

Will I know my treatment cost before starting?

Yes. A detailed treatment plan and cost breakdown are provided before treatment begins.

Results and maintenance

Will I need a retainer after Invisalign®?

Yes. Retainers are essential to maintain your results and prevent teeth from shifting.

Can teeth move after treatment?

Without proper retention, teeth can shift over time. Wearing your retainer helps preserve your smile.

How long will Invisalign® results last?

With consistent retainer use and good oral hygiene, results can last long term.
