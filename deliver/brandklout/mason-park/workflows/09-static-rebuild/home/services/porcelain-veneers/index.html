The Treatment

What it is, and what it involves.

What is a porcelain veneer?

A thin, durable, tooth-coloured shell, custom-fabricated and then bonded onto the front of the tooth. The improvement is immediate, because the shell arrives finished.

In the practice’s words

Porcelain dental veneers are a type of cosmetic dental solution. Veneers are thin, durable, tooth-colored “shells.” After being custom-fabricated, they are bonded onto the front of your tooth. Veneers look natural and beautiful for an immediate improvement in the appearance of your smile.

These ultra-thin, custom-crafted ceramic shells correct issues like chips, cracks, stubborn discoloration, small gaps, and even minor misalignments. They are stain-resistant and designed to mimic the light-reflecting properties of natural enamel, giving you a flawless yet natural-looking smile. With veneers, it’s possible to completely reinvent your smile with minimal alteration to your natural teeth.

What makes a veneer look like a tooth rather than a nail

  • TranslucencyPorcelain is designed to mimic the light-reflecting properties of natural enamel. Opaque material is what makes bad veneers obvious.
  • Custom fabricationEach shell made for one tooth, in a laboratory, from impressions of your mouth.
  • Minimal alterationThe practice’s own phrase. The less removed from the tooth underneath, the better the long-term outcome.
  • Stain resistanceThey do not pick up coffee and wine the way enamel and resin do.

Keeping them

Porcelain veneers can last for years with proper care. Continue brushing, flossing, and regular professional cleanings and exams — the veneer will not decay, but the tooth under it and the gum around it still can.

The habits that chip bonding chip porcelain too: biting nails, chewing pens, opening things with your teeth. If you grind, a night guard protects the investment.

Do these first, if they apply

Veneers are usually the last step of a plan rather than the first. Three things change what you will need.

  • HealthDecay and gum disease treated first. The condition is healthy teeth, and it is not negotiable.
  • PositionInvisalign® first if the teeth need moving. Straightening changes which teeth show and how much shell each needs.
  • ColourWhiten first. Porcelain does not lighten later, so the veneers are matched to the shade you have settled on.

Questions

Questions patients ask before they decide.

Am I a candidate?

Veneers may be ideal if your teeth are healthy but stained, gapped, crooked or chipped. Healthy is the condition — decay or gum disease is treated before any cosmetic work goes over it.

How long do porcelain veneers last?

Ten to fifteen years or more with proper care, against three to seven for bonding. Keep brushing, flossing and coming for regular professional cleanings and exams.

Will enamel be removed?

It may be. Veneers may require enamel removal, where bonding is minimally invasive. Enamel does not grow back, so this is worth understanding fully before you decide — the practice’s stated aim is minimal alteration to your natural teeth.

Will they look fake?

They are designed to mimic the light-reflecting properties of natural enamel, which is what separates a natural-looking veneer from an obvious one. The stated goal is flawless yet natural-looking.

Do veneers stain?

Porcelain is stain-resistant, so it holds its colour better than enamel or resin. That also means it will not lighten with whitening later, which is why whitening comes first.

Should I get veneers or bonding?

Bonding suits minor flaws: one visit, minimally invasive, more affordable, three to seven years. Veneers suit moderate to major cosmetic change across several teeth: multiple visits, higher cost, ten to fifteen years or more. Dr. Sung will tell you which case yours is.
