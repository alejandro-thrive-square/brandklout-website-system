Is this you

No pain does not mean no problem.

The practice’s own words: you might not notice any pain or discomfort around your wisdom teeth, but that does not necessarily mean they are healthy.

01

They are impacted

Unable to grow through the gum because there is not enough space. As many as 90% of patients have one, according to the AAOMS.

02

Partial eruption

Half through the gum, which leaves a flap that traps bacteria and leads to infection.

03

Crowding

Pushing the teeth in front of them out of line, and changing the bite.

04

They are hard to clean

At the very back of the mouth. An uncleaned tooth becomes a breeding ground for bacteria, infection and gum disease.

05

They are damaging the tooth in front

An impacted tooth pressing against its neighbour, or forming a cyst in the bone.

The Treatment

Three states, three different procedures.

What the practice does

A specialised procedure with thorough evaluation through digital imaging and customised planning, with sedation offered. The aim is removing problematic wisdom teeth before they cause damage, discomfort or infection.

In the practice’s words

Wisdom teeth (third molars) are the last teeth to erupt and often cause complications such as crowding of other teeth, partial eruptions leading to infection, and impaction against neighboring teeth or bone.

At Mason Park Dental, wisdom tooth extractions are a specialized procedure involving thorough evaluation through digital imaging and customized planning. We offer sedation options to help you stay relaxed and comfortable throughout the procedure. Our goal is to remove problematic wisdom teeth before they cause damage, discomfort, or infection.

How far through the gum decides the procedure

  • Fully eruptedVisible and accessible above the gumline. A simple extraction: numbed, loosened, lifted out.
  • Partially eruptedPart through. Usually a surgical extraction, and often the most infection-prone of the three because of the flap of gum over it.
  • Fully impactedTrapped in bone or soft tissue. A surgical extraction with an incision, and sometimes a little bone removed to reach it.

Why examinations matter more than symptoms here

You might not notice any pain or discomfort around your wisdom teeth, but that does not necessarily mean they are healthy. Even wisdom teeth that fit properly can be the target of a future infection. It is essential to keep up with regular examinations so that a trained team can take a close look at them.

An impacted wisdom tooth is something to talk to the doctor about. Impacted teeth can cause infection and damage to surrounding teeth, and it is extremely important these issues are addressed early on. Your wisdom teeth are hard to clean at the back of your mouth, and an infected tooth not receiving proper care can be a breeding ground for bacteria leading to infection and gum disease.

An infection of your wisdom teeth can lead to oral diseases, but it can also lead to more serious complications: the AAOMS explains that oral bacteria getting into your bloodstream can lead to heart, kidney and other organ infections.

The Alternatives

Sometimes the answer is to leave them.

This page is not an argument for taking every wisdom tooth out. It is an argument for knowing what yours are doing. Some erupt cleanly, sit in line, and can be cleaned — those get monitored, not removed. What decides it is the imaging and the examination, not the presence or absence of pain.

What you can do meanwhile

The practice’s own advice, and it is unglamorous.

  • Keep the examinationsThe practice cannot overstate this one. Wisdom teeth are assessed at every routine visit.
  • Stay vigilant with hygieneDaily, and especially at the very back where the brush barely reaches.
  • Mention any changePressure, a bad taste, a gum flap that is sore. These are early and easy to treat.
  • Do not wait for painEven wisdom teeth that fit properly can be the target of a future infection.

Questions

Questions patients ask before they decide.

Do all wisdom teeth have to come out?

No. Some erupt cleanly, sit in line and can be cleaned properly, and those are monitored rather than removed. Dr. Sung assesses them on imaging and will tell you which yours are.

Mine do not hurt. Is that fine?

Not necessarily. You might not notice any pain or discomfort around your wisdom teeth, but that does not mean they are healthy — even ones that fit properly can be the target of a future infection. This is why the examination matters more than the symptom.

What does impacted mean?

A tooth unable to grow properly through the gums because of a lack of space. According to the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, as many as 90% of patients have an impacted wisdom tooth.

When do wisdom teeth come in?

Typically between the ages of 17 and 25. Wisdom tooth monitoring is part of routine care through late adolescence for exactly that reason.

Can a wisdom tooth really affect my general health?

An infection can. The AAOMS explains that oral bacteria getting into the bloodstream can lead to heart, kidney and other organ infections. A tooth that is hard to clean and gets infected is not only a dental problem.

Will I be awake?

That is your choice. Local anaesthetic is standard, and oral sedation, nitrous oxide or IV sedation are all available — see sedation dentistry. Say what you would prefer when you book.
