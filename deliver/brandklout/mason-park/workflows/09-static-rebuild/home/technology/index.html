The Instruments

Seven, and what each changes for you.

Our state-of-the-art technologies ensure accuracy, efficiency, and comfort. Our dental equipment is safe, effective, and well-maintained.

01

Digital x‑rays

Magnified and enhanced on a screen in seconds, at much less radiation than film.

02

Panoramic x‑ray

Rotates around your head for a 360-degree view of teeth, sinuses and jaw structure.

03

Nomad™ handheld x‑ray

Battery-powered and cordless, usable in any room, with less radiation than most conventional systems.

04

Intra-oral camera

The size of a pen, so you see what we see. A new disposable cover every time.

05

Intra-oral scanner

3D digital impressions from a hand-held wand. No tray, no setting paste, no gag reflex.

06

Soft tissue lasers

Precise, largely pain-free, and they seal vessels as they work. See laser dentistry.

Same Day

The crown made while you wait.

Ceramill Motion 3

A CAD/CAM system that designs and mills a restoration in the building. Walk in with a problem and leave with the finished result, rather than a temporary and a second appointment in three weeks.

*Same-day services depend on patient eligibility, case complexity, and resource availability. Some cases may require follow-ups for the best results.

In the practice’s words

This advanced Dental CAD/CAM technology transforms how we deliver dental restorations and implants. Our in-house dental service isn’t just a convenience; it’s a game-changer for your dental care. We’ve streamlined the entire process to ensure you receive fast, accurate, personalized treatment. We enhance quality and efficiency by controlling every step, from design to placement.

Pearl AI

A second pair of eyes on every x‑ray.

Pearl is an AI platform cleared by multiple international regulatory bodies including the FDA, trained on the world’s largest collection of expertly annotated dental radiographs. It reads your x‑rays alongside the dentist.

It finds what is easy to miss

Identifying subtle patterns or anomalies that can be overlooked, and helping diagnose cavities, periodontal disease and malocclusion.

You can see it too

The practice’s stated reason for it: transparency and objectivity, and making it easier for you to understand what is going on in your own x‑rays.

It assists, it does not decide

The practice describes it as an always-on assistant that strengthens diagnostic performance. The diagnosis remains the dentist’s.

What the practice says it has changed day to day

Pearl has significantly improved our daily workflow. It swiftly analyzes dental x-rays, allowing us to dedicate more time to patient consultation and treatment planning. This AI platform ensures every patient receives thorough care in a timely manner.

There have been several cases where Pearl’s precision has been instrumental. One such instance involved a patient with ambiguous oral symptoms. Pearl’s capability to accurately identify hard-to-detect pathologies proved invaluable.

Pearl empowers us to make accurate, evidence-based diagnoses faster than ever before — and makes understanding the diagnosis easier for our patients. This directly translates to trustworthy, higher-quality care.

Our investment in Pearl signifies our dedication to providing you with the highest level of oral health care. Schedule an appointment to discover how this AI technology is being used here — our team is ready to show you how Pearl works and how it contributes to your dental health.

How it compares.

Digital scanner vs Impression tray
A small hand-held wand. A tray of soft material setting in your mouth.
No gag reflex triggered. The commonest complaint in dentistry.
Proven accuracy, even on difficult cases. Retakes when it distorts.
Less lab preparation, so faster results. Posted, poured and prepared.
