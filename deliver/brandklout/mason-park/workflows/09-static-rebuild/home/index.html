The Relay

Most families in Katy run two dental offices. One for the adults. One for the children. Two waiting rooms, two drives across town, two sets of forms, and nobody who has seen the whole family.

It is not a small inconvenience. It is the reason a child's check gets postponed to a term that never comes, and the reason an adult's own cleaning is the appointment that gets dropped.

Here it is one building, one team, one afternoon.

Before you book

Do you accept new patients?

Katy, TX dentist welcomes new patients. Contact our office to schedule an appointment.

What do I need to bring to my first dental appointment?

Typically, you will be asked to bring a photo ID and a copy of your insurance details (if any) to your first appointment. Sometimes, you can complete your new patient forms ahead of time. ContactMason Park Family Dental of Katy for more information or to schedule an appointment.

How often should I visit the dentist?

The American Dental Association recommends that most people visit the dentist every 6 months. However, specific needs may require more frequent visits. ContactMason Park Family Dental of Katy for more information or to schedule an appointment.

What services does a general dentist offer?

Dentist in Katy offers a variety of preventive, cosmetic, and restorative dental solutions to best care for the needs of you and your family. Contact our office for more information.
