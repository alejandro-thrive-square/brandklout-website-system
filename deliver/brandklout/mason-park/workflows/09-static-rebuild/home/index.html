The Relay

Most families in Katy run two dental offices. One for the adults. One for the children. Two waiting rooms, two drives across town, two sets of forms, and nobody who has seen the whole family.

It is the reason a child’s check gets postponed, and the reason an adult’s own cleaning is the appointment that gets dropped.

Here it is one building, one team, one afternoon.

Before you book

Do you accept new patients?

Mason Park Dental of Katy welcomes new patients, adults and children alike. See what a first visit looks like, or book online in about a minute.

What do I need to bring to my first dental appointment?

Typically, you will be asked to bring a photo ID and a copy of your insurance details (if any) to your first appointment. Our new patients page walks through the whole visit, and patient resources explains how the forms are handled. Contact Mason Park Dental of Katy for more information or to schedule an appointment.

How often should I visit the dentist?

The American Dental Association recommends that most people visit the dentist every 6 months. However, specific needs may require more frequent visits. Read about cleanings and exams, or contact Mason Park Dental of Katy to schedule an appointment.

What services does a general dentist offer?

Mason Park Dental of Katy offers preventive, cosmetic and restorative care for you and your family. See every treatment we offer, or contact our office for more information.
