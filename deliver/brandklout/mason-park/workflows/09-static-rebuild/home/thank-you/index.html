What Happens Next

Three things, in order.

Nothing else is needed from you until the phone rings.

  1. Within office hours

    The front desk calls you.

    To confirm the time you asked for, or to offer the nearest one. Tell them who is coming; adults and children can share one afternoon.

  2. Before the visit

    Forms, ahead of time.

    Ask for the new patient forms when they call and complete them at home. Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of the medications you take.

  3. The visit

    An exam, a plan, a price.

    A full exam and digital x-rays, with everything on a screen you can see too. Nothing is started on the day you arrive; the plan and the price are written out before any work.
