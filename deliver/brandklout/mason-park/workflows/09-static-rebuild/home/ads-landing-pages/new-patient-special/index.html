Questions

What first visits ask.

What does the $189 new patient special include?

A comprehensive dental exam, x-rays, a cleaning and a full treatment plan in writing, regularly $517. It is for new patients only, cannot be combined with dental insurance, and does not apply in cases of periodontal (gum) disease, where a different cleaning is needed and is quoted before it is done.

Can I use the $189 special with my insurance?

No. The special cannot be combined with insurance, and that is not a catch: using your insurance is usually the better deal. Tell the front desk which plan you have and they will tell you which way round works out best for you.

What should I expect during my first visit?

You begin with some paperwork that includes your health history, sent ahead if you ask for it. Then either Dr. William Sung or Dr. Dave Cho conducts a thorough dental exam: checking your teeth and gums, evaluating your oral health, and discussing any concerns you may have.

What if the exam finds something?

You get a written plan and a price, with what insurance covers and what it does not. Nothing is started the same day unless you ask for it, and a free second opinion on a plan you were given elsewhere is always available.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Your insurance card if you have one, a valid photo ID, and a list of the medications you currently take, including anything over the counter.

Can I request a tour of the practice first?

Yes. There is no fee to tour the practice. Call and ask for a quiet weekday hour to see the rooms and meet the team before you become a patient.

How do Dr. Sung and Dr. Cho approach new patient care?

They take the time to listen to your concerns, answer your questions, and discuss the best treatment options for you. The aim is a welcoming visit that builds trust and open communication about your dental health.
