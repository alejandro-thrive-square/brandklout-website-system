Questions

Everything patients ask before booking.

What does Invisalign® cost at Mason Park?

The regular price is $4,800. With the current offer it is $3,800, and a further $500 comes off when treatment is prepaid in full. That price includes the consultation, the 3D scan and x-rays, every aligner, refinements, retainers and Kör whitening gel. Cherry financing splits the balance at 0% APR with no hard credit check.

How long does Invisalign® treatment take?

Most patients complete treatment in 12 to 18 months, but some may see results in as little as 6 months.

Is Invisalign® painful?

Some patients experience mild discomfort as their teeth adjust, but it is far less painful than traditional braces.

Can I eat and drink while wearing Invisalign®?

You should remove aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water to prevent damage or staining.

How often do I need to visit during treatment?

You will need check-ups every 4 to 6 weeks to monitor progress and receive new aligners.

Does Invisalign® work for severe misalignment?

Invisalign® can treat most cases, but severe alignment issues may require alternative treatments. The consultation is where you find out, and it costs nothing.

Why is Invisalign® a good option for teens?

Aligners are clear and discreet, comfortable, removable for brushing and sports, carry no brackets or wires to break, and many teens finish in less time than with traditional braces.

Does dental insurance cover Invisalign® treatment?

Many dental insurance plans cover Invisalign® as part of their orthodontic benefits. Coverage varies by plan, and we check yours and explain any out-of-pocket cost before treatment starts. With the offer, insurance reimbursements are returned directly to you.
