How To Use This

Ask which of these is yours.

Six lists, because six different mouths need six different things. A dry mouth needs the opposite of what a whitening patient needs, and buying the whole page helps nobody.

  • Start with oneThe floss you will actually use beats the water flosser sitting in a cupboard. Pick the thing that fits your evening.
  • Bring it to your cleaningWe will tell you whether it is doing anything. Some of these matter enormously for one person and not at all for another.
  • These are not prescriptionsHigh-fluoride gels and prescription rinses are a separate conversation, and one we have in the chair.
  • DisclosureThe links below go to Amazon and carry an affiliate tag, which means a purchase may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It does not change what we recommend.
