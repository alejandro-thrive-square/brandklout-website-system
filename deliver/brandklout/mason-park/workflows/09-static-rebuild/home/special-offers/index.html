Read the conditions before you book

In the practice’s words

$189 New Patient Special. Welcome to your new dental home! New patients can receive a comprehensive dental exam, x-rays, a cleaning, and a full treatment plan for just $189, saving you $328. Please note, this offer is exclusive to new patients and cannot be used in conjunction with insurance or in cases of periodontal disease.

Pediatric Dental Care Offer: $129 New Patient Exam. Start your child’s dental journey on the right foot with our New Patient Exam for children under 13, priced at just $129.

Brighten Up: $350 New Patient Whitening. Achieve a brighter smile with our Kör Whitening service, complete with at-home trays for $350. This new patient offer is the first step towards the radiant smile you’ve always desired.

Invisalign® Special: $1000 Off. Originally $4800, we’re offering this transformative treatment for just $3800. Take advantage of an additional $500 off by prepaying in full, with insurance reimbursements directly returned to you. In-office and third-party payment plans are available.

Dental Implants Special: $1000 Off. Regular price $3800, special price $2800. Includes implant consultation, necessary radiographs and 3D scan, implant, custom abutment and implant crown.

Free Second Opinion. Are you confused about a treatment plan, or skeptical about a doctor’s opinion? At Mason Park Dental, educating our patients is a top priority. See a doctor you can trust with a free second opinion consultation.
