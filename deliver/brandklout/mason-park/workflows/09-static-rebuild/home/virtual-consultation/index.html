How It Works

Three steps, and the first one is a phone call.

Seamless dental advice from the comfort of home, bridging the gap between your dental needs and a busy schedule.

(281) 769‑8878Step one

Call and say you would like a virtual consultation, and what it is about. The front desk takes your details.

02

Send photographs, if they help

The team will tell you where to send them and what to photograph. Useful for a chipped tooth, a gap, or something you would rather show than describe.

03

We call you back

An online appointment is arranged with Dr. Dave Cho, Dr. William Sung or Dr. Tam Nguyen, depending on what you are asking about.

What it is good for, and what it is not

  • Good forA first opinion on Invisalign®, a cosmetic question, whether something needs seeing urgently, or a second opinion on a plan from elsewhere.
  • Good forDeciding whether to take time off work at all. Sometimes the answer is that it can wait until your next cleaning.
  • Not a diagnosisPhotographs give a preliminary, non-diagnostic preview only. Treatment recommendations require a complete clinical examination — see the Terms of Service.
  • Not for an emergencyIf you are in pain, have swelling, or have knocked a tooth out, call and say so — that is a same-day appointment, not a virtual one. See emergency dentist.

Why

Who this actually suits.

Connect with Dr. Dave Cho, Dr. William Sung and Dr. Tam Nguyen for professional advice on everything from Invisalign to pediatric dental care, without leaving your home.

You cannot take the time off yet

Find out whether it needs an appointment before you book one.

You are weighing up a treatment

Aligners, veneers, implants. A conversation costs nothing and narrows it down.

You have a plan from another dentist

The practice offers a free second opinion, and this is a way to start it.

The Invitation

Your journey is a phone call away.

For expert dental guidance at your fingertips, call (281) 769-8878 to arrange your virtual consultation. If you would rather come in, book online.
