Questions patients ask about paying

Do you take my insurance?

We accept all PPO insurance plans, and we also take Delta Dental and Medicaid. Bring your card to the first visit, or read it to the front desk when you book, and we check what your plan covers before treatment starts.

What if I have no insurance?

The practice runs an annual membership plan for patients without insurance. It includes two exams and two cleanings a year and discounts on standard procedures, with no pre-authorization and no exclusions for pre-existing conditions. The dental savings plan.

Does financing need a credit check?

Cherry financing splits the balance at 0% APR with no hard credit check. In-office and third-party payment plans are also available; ask the front desk which fits the treatment you are planning.

When do I find out the price?

Treatment is planned and priced in writing before it starts, with what insurance covers and what it does not. No treatment is started on the day you arrive.
