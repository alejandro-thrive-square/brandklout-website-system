Our Dental Family

Our esteemed practitioners, Dr. William Sung, Dr. Dave Cho, and Dr. Tam Nguyen, bring a wealth of knowledge, experience, and genuine care to every interaction at our Katy dentist office. A Smile's Best Start

Dr. Nguyen, a proud Houston native and a board-certified pediatric dentist, is passionate about cultivating healthy oral habits from a young age. A graduate of Boston University, she specializes in pediatric dental care, providing a fun, gentle, and nurturing environment for children and parents alike. Emphasizing preventative dentistry, Dr. Nguyen and her team are committed to guiding families on a journey to lifelong dental health.

Building Relationships, One Smile at a Time

Dr. Cho’s journey from biochemistry to dentistry underscores his dedication to oral health and patient care. His extensive skills in oral surgery, endodontics, and cosmetic dentistry, complemented by a laid-back approach, ensure a relaxing experience for patients across Katy, TX. His commitment to continued education keeps him at the forefront of advanced technology in dental care, from Invisalign to sedation dentistry.

Crafting Confidence Through Dental Artistry

Dr. William Sung, with deep family roots in dentistry, brings his love for healthcare and advanced expertise to Katy, TX. Excelling in various dental procedures, from extractions to dental implants, he is devoted to enhancing patient confidence and comfort. Dr. Sung’s pursuit of excellence is evident in his fellowship with the Academy of General Dentistry and his commitment to continuous learning.
