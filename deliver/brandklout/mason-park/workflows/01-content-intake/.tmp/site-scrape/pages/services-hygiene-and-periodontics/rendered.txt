🧼 Why Are Hygiene and Periodontics Important?

Many people think brushing and flossing alone are enough—but even the best home care can’t remove tartar buildup or address deep pockets of infection. Regular professional dental cleaning and periodontal care play a critical role in keeping your gums disease-free and your teeth securely in place.

Without proper gum care, harmful bacteria can lead to gingivitis, periodontitis, and even tooth and bone loss. Our dental hygiene in Katy, TX focuses on prevention, early detection, and personalized treatment.

📖 Understanding Gum Disease: Gingivitis vs. Periodontitis

Gingivitis is the early stage of gum disease, where the gums become red, swollen, and prone to bleeding. If left untreated, it progresses to periodontitis—a more advanced condition where pockets form around the teeth, leading to bone loss and tooth mobility.

With timely scaling and root planing in Katy, TX, we can stop the disease from advancing.

💡 FAQ – Dental Hygiene & Periodontics in Katy, TX

❓ Why are dental cleanings important?
Regular dental cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing can’t. They prevent gum disease, cavities, and freshen your breath.

❓ What is scaling and root planing?
This deep cleaning treatment removes bacteria and tartar below the gumline. It’s used to treat periodontitis and promote healing.

❓ How often should I get a dental cleaning?
Most patients benefit from cleanings every six months. Those with a history of gum disease may need periodontal maintenance every 3–4 months.

❓ Is periodontal disease reversible?
Gingivitis, the early stage, is reversible with good hygiene. Periodontitis can’t be reversed but can be managed with proper periodontics care.

❓ Do I need a referral for periodontal treatment?
No. You can book your appointment directly with Mason Park Dental of Katy for all hygiene and periodontics services.
