We get it—“How much does Invisalign cost?” is usually one of the first questions people ask. If you’re teaming up with Dr. Dave Cho, you can expect the total to land between $3,000 and $5,500.

Because no two smiles are built the same. Your treatment is custom, not cookie-cutter. Here’s what moves the dial:

The longer your journey, the more trays you’ll need. A short trip costs less than a cross-country road trip.

3D iTero scans

Check-in appointments
Final retainers to lock in your results

Behind the Price Tag with Dr. Cho: What You’re Really Paying For

Invisalign treatment with Dr. Dave Cho isn’t a generic, one-size-fits-all fix—it’s a guided transformation, crafted just for you. The cost isn’t just about the aligners. It’s about the precision, technology, and expertise behind every step.

Here’s where the value shows up:

1. A Digital Start, Not a Goopy Mold


Forget those uncomfortable impressions. Dr. Cho begins with a 3D iTero® digital scan, which maps every detail of your teeth with incredible accuracy. It’s quicker, cleaner, and far more precise.

2. A Time Machine for Your Smile
Before you even start treatment, you’ll see a personalized simulation of your results. This sneak peek—created using advanced software—shows how your teeth will move week by week. It’s your smile’s future, visualized.

3. Trays That Don’t Wing It
Each aligner tray is built just for your mouth, based on that digital roadmap. There’s no guesswork—just exact movements, planned down to the millimeter.

4. Monitoring That Doesn’t Miss a Beat


Dr. Cho tracks your progress with routine in-person or virtual check-ins, adjusting as needed. It’s proactive care, not passive monitoring.

5. Bonus Value: Less Disruption, More Confidence
The trays are nearly invisible, removable, and designed for comfort. You can keep eating your favorite foods, brushing like normal, and smiling through work meetings or school photos.

6. It’s Not Just the Aligners—It’s the Experience


From the technology to the support team to Dr. Cho’s guidance, this isn’t just a dental purchase. It’s an investment in confidence, health, and long-term results.
