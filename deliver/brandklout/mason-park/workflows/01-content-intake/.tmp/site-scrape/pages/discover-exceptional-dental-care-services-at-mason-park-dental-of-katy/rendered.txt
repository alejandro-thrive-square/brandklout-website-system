Our Services
Preventative Dentistry

Preventative care is the cornerstone of maintaining optimal oral health. Our Preventative Dentistry services include routine cleanings, exams, and fluoride treatments designed to prevent dental issues before they arise. By addressing potential problems early, we help you avoid more complex and costly procedures in the future.

Children’s Dentistry

Our Children’s Dentistry services are designed to meet your child’s needs. Dr. Tam Nguyen, our board-certified pediatric dentist, creates a friendly and comfortable environment to help children develop good dental habits from a young age. We focus on making dental visits a positive experience for your little ones.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Achieve the smile of your dreams with our Cosmetic Dentistry services. From teeth whitening to veneers and bonding, we offer a range of treatments to enhance the appearance of your smile. Our team uses advanced techniques to ensure results that look natural and beautiful.

Restorative Dentistry

Our Restorative Dentistry services restore oral health and function when dental issues arise. We provide treatments such as fillings, crowns, and bridges to repair damaged or missing teeth and improve overall dental health.

Hygiene and Periodontics

Maintaining healthy gums is essential for overall oral health. Our Hygiene and Periodontics services focus on preventing and treating gum disease. Regular cleanings and periodontal treatments help keep your gums healthy and avoid issues affecting your teeth and overall health.

Invisalign

For a discreet and comfortable way to straighten your teeth, consider Invisalign. This orthodontic treatment uses clear aligners to gradually shift your teeth into the desired position, offering an alternative to traditional braces. Enjoy the benefits of a straighter smile without the visible brackets and wires.

Root Canal Therapy

If you’re experiencing severe tooth pain, Root Canal Therapy might be necessary to save a damaged or infected tooth. Our team uses advanced techniques to perform root canal procedures efficiently and comfortably, helping you preserve your natural teeth.

Oral Surgery

In some cases, oral surgery is needed to address complex dental issues. Our Oral Surgery services include extractions and other surgical procedures performed with precision and care to ensure comfort and recovery.

Advanced Technology for Enhanced Care

At Mason Park Dental, we leverage the latest advancements in dental technology to provide the best possible care. Our state-of-the-art equipment includes:

Cone Beam Imaging: Provides detailed 3D images for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.
Digital X-rays: Reduce radiation exposure while offering clear, detailed images of your teeth and bones.
Intraoral Camera: This lets us capture high-resolution images of your mouth, helping you understand your dental health.
Soft Tissue Laser: Used for precise and comfortable treatment of soft tissue issues.

For more information on our technology and how it benefits your dental care, visit our Technology page.

Flexible Scheduling and Emergency Care

We understand that dental emergencies can happen at any time. That’s why we offer flexible scheduling options and prompt emergency care to address urgent issues as quickly as possible. We aim to provide you with the care you need when you need it.
