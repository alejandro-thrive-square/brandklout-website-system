At Mason Park Dental, we offer a wide array of dental services to ensure every patient receives the personalized care they deserve. Our team combines cutting-edge technology with a compassionate approach to provide outstanding dental care.

Explore Our Wide Range of Services
Preventative Dentistry

Our Preventative Dentistry services are designed to help you maintain optimal oral health and prevent dental issues before they arise. Regular cleanings, exams, and fluoride treatments are key to our preventative care, ensuring your smile remains healthy and vibrant.

Children’s Dentistry

Creating a positive and comfortable dental experience for children is crucial. Our Children’s Dentistry services are tailored to meet the needs of young patients, making dental visits enjoyable and stress-free. Dr. Tam Nguyen, our board-certified pediatric dentist, is dedicated to providing gentle and effective care for children.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Transform your smile with our Cosmetic Dentistry services. From teeth whitening and veneers to bonding, we offer a range of treatments designed to enhance the appearance of your smile and boost your confidence.

Restorative Dentistry

Our Restorative Dentistry services can restore both function and esthetics for patients with damaged or missing teeth. We provide fillings, crowns, and bridges to repair and replace damaged teeth, ensuring you enjoy a healthy and complete smile.

Hygiene and Periodontics

Maintaining healthy gums is essential for overall oral health. Our Hygiene and Periodontics services focus on preventing and managing gum disease through thorough cleanings and advanced treatments.

Invisalign

Achieve a straighter smile discreetly with Invisalign. This dental treatment uses clear aligners to gradually move your teeth into place, offering a comfortable and nearly invisible alternative to traditional braces.

Root Canal Therapy

If you experience severe tooth pain or infection, Root Canal Therapy may be necessary to save your tooth. Our team performs root canal procedures precisely, ensuring a comfortable experience and effective treatment.

Oral Surgery

Our Oral Surgery services include extractions and other surgical procedures for more complex dental issues. We handle these procedures with care and expertise to ensure minimal discomfort and optimal results.

Advanced Technology for Superior Care

At Mason Park Dental, we leverage state-of-the-art technology to enhance your dental care experience. Our advanced equipment includes:

Cone Beam Imaging: Provides detailed 3D images for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.
Digital X-rays: Offer clear images with reduced radiation exposure.
Intraoral Camera: Captures high-resolution images of your mouth for better understanding and communication.
Soft Tissue Laser: Used for precise and comfortable treatment of soft tissue issues.

Learn more about our technology and how it benefits your care on our Technology page.

Flexible Scheduling and Emergency Care

We understand your time is valuable, so we offer flexible scheduling options to accommodate your busy lifestyle. In a dental emergency, we are ready to provide prompt and effective care to address urgent issues and relieve your discomfort.
