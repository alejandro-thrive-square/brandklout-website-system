How Invisalign® Treatment Works
Step 1: Initial Consultation

Your Invisalign® journey begins with a consultation with Dr. William Sung. During this visit, he will carefully assess your teeth, discuss your dental history, and understand your goals for a straighter, healthier smile. This is the perfect opportunity to ask questions about the process, expected results, and any concerns you may have.

Step 2: Digital Scanning & Customized Treatment Plan

Next, we use advanced 3D imaging technology to take precise digital scans of your teeth. These scans allow us to create a fully customized treatment plan that maps out each step of your Invisalign® journey. You’ll even get a preview of how your smile will transform over time! Unlike traditional impressions, our digital approach ensures accuracy and comfort.

Step 3: Aligner Fitting & Wear Instructions

Once your aligners are ready, you’ll receive your first set, custom-made to fit snugly over your teeth. Dr. Sung will provide detailed instructions on how to wear them, emphasizing the importance of keeping them in place for 20-22 hours per day. You’ll only remove them for eating, drinking (except for water), and oral hygiene. Wearing your aligners as directed is key to achieving the best results within the estimated timeframe.

Step 4: Periodic Progress Checkups

Every 4-6 weeks, you’ll return for a progress checkup, where Dr. Sung will evaluate your teeth’s movement and provide your next set of aligners. Each new set gradually shifts your teeth closer to their ideal positions. These visits are essential to ensure your treatment stays on track and adjustments can be made if necessary.

Step 5: Completion & Retainers

Once your Invisalign® treatment is complete, you’ll see the beautifully straight smile you’ve been working toward. To maintain your results, Dr. Sung will recommend a retainer to keep your teeth in their new positions. Wearing your retainer as instructed helps prevent shifting and preserves your confident new smile for years to come.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1.How long does Invisalign® treatment take?
Most patients complete treatment in 12-18 months, but some may see results in as little as 6 months.

2.Can I eat and drink while wearing Invisalign®?
You should remove aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water to prevent damage or staining.

3.Is Invisalign® painful?
Some patients experience mild discomfort as their teeth adjust, but it is far less painful than traditional braces.

4.How often do I need to visit the dentist during treatment?
You will need check-ups every 4-6 weeks to monitor progress and receive new aligners.

5.Does Invisalign® work for severe misalignment?
Invisalign® can treat most cases, but severe alignment issues may require alternative treatments.

6. Why Invisalign® is a Game-Changer for Teens
If you’re a parent (or a teen yourself!) exploring orthodontic options, Invisalign® offers some unique benefits tailored just for teenagers. Here’s what sets Invisalign® apart when it comes to teen orthodontics:

Nearly Invisible: Forget the days of conspicuous metal wires. Invisalign® aligners are clear and discreet, making it easier for teens to smile confidently during treatment, without the need to hide in photos or worry about school events.
Comfort First: With smooth, custom-fit aligners, none of the poking or irritation comes with traditional braces. Teens can focus on what matters without constantly adjusting to metal brackets.
Removable for Real Life: Aligners can be taken out for brushing, flossing, sports, and oral hygiene. This means keeping teeth clean is simple, and there are no food restrictions, so favorite snacks aren’t off the table.
Fewer Emergencies: Since there are no brackets or wires, there’s much less risk of sudden orthodontic emergencies or surprise trips to fix broken hardware.
Faster Treatment Timeline: Many teens complete their Invisalign® treatment in less time compared to traditional braces, depending on their specific alignment needs. With regular check-ins and high-tech monitoring, progress stays on track.

Invisalign® empowers teens to straighten their teeth seamlessly without disrupting their daily lives or confidence.
