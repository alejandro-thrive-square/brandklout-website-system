What Is Dental Bonding?

Dental bonding is a cosmetic dental procedure in which a durable, tooth-colored resin is applied to your teeth and hardened with a special curing light. This resin is shaped and polished to match the rest of your smile, resulting in a natural-looking restoration.

You may benefit from tooth bonding if you have:

Small chips or cracks in your teeth
Gaps between your front teeth
Discolored enamel that doesn’t respond to whitening
Misshapen or uneven teeth
Slightly worn-down edges

Because the procedure is non-invasive, dental bonding is ideal for patients who want immediate results without the commitment of more extensive treatments like veneers or crowns.

How the Dental Bonding Procedure Works

The entire process can often be completed in under an hour per tooth:

Evaluation: During your consultation, Dr. William Sung will assess your goals and examine your teeth.
Tooth preparation: A gentle etching process roughens the tooth surface to help the bonding resin adhere.
Application: Tooth-colored resin is applied and shaped to the ideal contour.
Curing: A UV light hardens the resin in just seconds.
Polishing: The bonded area is refined for a seamless, natural finish.

No drilling, no needles—just a simple, comfortable enhancement that looks like your real tooth.

How Long Does Dental Bonding Last?

With proper care, dental bonding can last 3–10 years. Key factors include:

Oral hygiene habits
Location of the bonded tooth (front teeth may last longer)
Avoiding habits like biting fingernails, chewing on pens, or using teeth as tools

Regular visits to our office help monitor your cosmetic dental bonding and ensure lasting results.
