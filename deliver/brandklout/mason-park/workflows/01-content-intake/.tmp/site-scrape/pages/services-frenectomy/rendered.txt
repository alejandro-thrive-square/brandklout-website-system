What Is a Frenectomy?

A frenectomy is a minimally invasive dental procedure that involves the removal or modification of a frenum—a small fold of connective tissue located in the mouth. There are two common types of oral frena:

Lingual frenum: Connects the underside of the tongue to the floor of the mouth
Labial frenum: Connects the inside of the upper lip to the gum above the front teeth

When either of these frena is too short or tight, it can lead to restricted movement. This condition is commonly referred to as a tongue-tie (ankyloglossia) or lip-tie and may cause problems such as difficulty breastfeeding, poor oral hygiene, speech delays, or dental complications. The goal of a dental frenectomy is to restore mobility and eliminate these restrictions, thereby improving oral function and overall comfort.

A laser frenectomy in adults can improve tongue range of motion, reduce muscular strain, and support better dental health.

Understanding Tongue-Tie and Lip-Tie

A tongue-tie (ankyloglossia) is a condition where the lingual frenum is too short or tight, restricting tongue movement. This may cause speech impediments, difficulty swallowing, and complications during breastfeeding.

A lip-tie, on the other hand, occurs when the labial frenum is overly restrictive. This can prevent proper lip movement, contribute to a gap between the front teeth, and make tasks like brushing or flossing more difficult. Lip-tie surgery in Katy is often recommended to prevent long-term oral health issues.

Both conditions are more than just physical restrictions—they can impact emotional well-being, social interactions, and everyday comfort. That’s why Dr. William Sung offers comprehensive laser frenectomy treatment tailored to each patient’s needs.

1. What is a frenectomy and who needs it?
A frenectomy is a quick dental procedure that releases tight oral tissue (frenum) under the tongue or behind the upper lip. It’s often recommended for patients experiencing speech problems, feeding issues, or restricted oral movement.

2. Is the laser frenectomy procedure painful?
The procedure is virtually painless. Dr. William Sung uses a soft-tissue laser, which minimizes bleeding, eliminates the need for stitches, and allows for a faster, more comfortable recovery.
