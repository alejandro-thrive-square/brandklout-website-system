What Are Night Guards and How Do They Work?

Night guards are specialized dental appliances designed to protect your teeth while you sleep. Custom-made to fit the unique shape of your mouth, they create a comfortable, durable barrier between your upper and lower teeth — minimizing the harmful effects of nighttime habits like grinding and clenching.

Here’s how night guards work and why they’re essential for many patients:

Prevent Teeth Grinding (Bruxism):
Bruxism, or unconscious teeth grinding, can cause significant dental damage over time. Night guards cushion your teeth, preventing direct contact and absorbing the pressure created by grinding. This protects against chips, fractures, and flattening of the tooth surfaces.

Alleviate Jaw Tension and TMJ Pain:
Grinding and clenching strain the muscles and joints that control jaw movement, often leading to tension, soreness, and temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders. A properly fitted night guard helps relax these muscles by slightly repositioning the jaw, reducing stress on the TMJ and alleviating painful symptoms.

Protect Enamel from Premature Wear:
Your enamel — the hard, outer surface of your teeth — doesn’t regenerate once it wears away. Night guards act as a shield, preserving the integrity of your enamel and preventing the erosion, sensitivity, and structural damage that bruxism can cause.

Improve Sleep Quality by Reducing Muscle Strain:
Muscle tension caused by clenching or grinding can disrupt sleep cycles, leading to restless nights and morning fatigue. Night guards minimize this tension, allowing your jaw muscles to relax and promoting deeper, more restorative sleep.
