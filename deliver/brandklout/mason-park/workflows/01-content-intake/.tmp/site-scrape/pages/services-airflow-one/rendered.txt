🔬 How Does Airflow® Teeth Cleaning Work?

The Airflow® system combines three elements: air, water, and a gentle cleaning powder (often erythritol or bicarbonate-based). Here’s how the process unfolds:

Preparation: We assess your teeth and gums to customize your cleaning.
Biofilm Disclosing: A harmless dye may be used to highlight biofilm for targeted removal.
Airflow® Application: The handpiece sprays a controlled mist of air, powder, and water to remove plaque, biofilm, and stains.
Polishing: Your teeth are left smooth, bright, and incredibly clean.
Final Rinse: A thorough rinse ensures no residue is left behind.

📈 Airflow® and Dental Biofilm Management

Understanding dental biofilm cleaning is critical. Biofilm is a sticky, colorless layer of bacteria that forms on teeth and under gums. If untreated, it can cause:

Gum disease
Tooth decay
Bad breath
Implant failure

Airflow® teeth cleaning disrupts and removes biofilm more effectively than traditional methods, ensuring better protection against oral diseases.

FAQs About Airflow® Teeth Cleaning

Q1: What is Airflow® teeth cleaning?
Airflow® teeth cleaning is a professional cleaning method using air, water, and fine powder to gently and effectively remove plaque, stains, and dental biofilm.

Q2: Is Airflow® dental cleaning better than traditional scaling?
Yes! Airflow® dental cleaning offers a more comfortable, efficient, and thorough clean compared to traditional methods, especially for sensitive teeth and gums.

Q3: Is Airflow® cleaning painful?
No. Airflow® teeth cleaning is designed to be extremely gentle, making it an excellent choice for patients who typically experience discomfort during cleanings.

Q4: How often should I get Airflow® cleaning?
Most patients benefit from Airflow® dental cleaning every 6 months during routine dental visits. Patients with gum disease or high-staining habits might need more frequent cleanings.

Q5: Can Airflow® remove coffee and tobacco stains?
Absolutely! Airflow® dental cleaning is highly effective at lifting stains caused by coffee, tea, wine, and tobacco products, leaving your teeth looking whiter and brighter.
