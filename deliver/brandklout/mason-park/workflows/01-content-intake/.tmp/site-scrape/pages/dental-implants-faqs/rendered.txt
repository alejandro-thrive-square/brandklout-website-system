Dental Implant Safety & Success
  1. Are dental implants safe?

Yes. Dental implants are a safe and clinically proven treatment. They are made from biocompatible titanium that integrates naturally with the jawbone. When placed by an experienced implant dentist, dental implants are one of the safest procedures in modern dentistry.

  2. What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a success rate of over 95%. Long-term success depends on proper placement, good oral hygiene, and routine dental checkups.

  3. Do dental implants look and feel natural?

Yes. Dental implants are custom-designed to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth. They feel secure, function like real teeth, and blend seamlessly into your smile.

Longevity & Durability
  4. How long does a dental implant last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The implant post is permanent, while the crown may need replacement after many years due to normal wear.

  5. Are dental implants a permanent solution for missing teeth?

Yes. Dental implants are considered a permanent solution because they fuse with the jawbone and do not rely on adjacent teeth for support.

  6. Can dental implants fail over time?

Implant failure is rare but may occur due to smoking, poor oral hygiene, uncontrolled medical conditions, or gum disease. Regular dental visits significantly reduce this risk.

Pain, Comfort & Recovery
  7. How painful is getting a dental implant?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation, so patients feel little to no pain. Post-procedure discomfort is typically mild and manageable with medication.

  8. How long is the recovery after dental implant surgery?

Most patients return to normal activities within 24–48 hours. Complete healing and bone integration usually take 3 to 6 months.

  9. What can I expect after dental implant placement?

Mild swelling or soreness may occur for a few days. Following post-operative instructions helps ensure smooth healing and long-term success.

Dental Implant Procedure & Timeline
  10. Will I be without teeth during the implant process?

No. Temporary teeth or restorations are typically provided so you never have to go without a smile during treatment.

  11. Can dental implants be done in one day?

In some cases, yes. Same-day dental implants may be possible depending on bone density and oral health. A consultation is required to determine eligibility.

  12. Can I get dental implants years after a tooth extraction?

Yes. Even if a tooth was removed years ago, dental implants are often still an option. Bone grafting may be recommended if bone loss has occurred.

Candidacy & Health Considerations
  13. Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Good candidates have healthy gums, sufficient jawbone, good overall health, and are committed to maintaining proper oral hygiene.

  14. Who is not a candidate for dental implants?

Patients with uncontrolled diabetes, heavy smoking habits, severe bone loss, or certain medical conditions may require additional treatment before implant placement.

  15. Can patients with diabetes get dental implants?

Yes. Patients with well-controlled diabetes can safely receive dental implants. Dr. Sung will carefully evaluate your medical history before treatment.

Age & Special Considerations
  16. Is there an upper age limit for dental implants?

No. There is no upper age limit. Many seniors successfully receive dental implants and enjoy improved comfort and function.

  17. Are dental implants suitable for children or teenagers?

Dental implants are usually not recommended until jaw growth is complete, typically in the late teenage years.

  18. Can seniors benefit from dental implants?

Absolutely. Dental implants help seniors eat comfortably, speak clearly, and enjoy improved confidence compared to traditional dentures.

Maintenance, Food & Alternatives
  19. Are dental implants removable?

No. Dental implants are permanently anchored in the jawbone. However, some implant-supported dentures may be removable depending on the design.

  20. Does food get stuck under dental implants?

Food can collect around implants if oral hygiene is inadequate. Daily brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings help prevent buildup.

  21. How can I prevent food from sticking around my dental implant?

Brush twice daily, floss regularly, use a water flosser, and schedule routine dental visits to keep implants and gums healthy.
