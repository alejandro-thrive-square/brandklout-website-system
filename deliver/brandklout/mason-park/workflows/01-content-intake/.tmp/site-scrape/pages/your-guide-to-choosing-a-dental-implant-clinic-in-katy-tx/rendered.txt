It’s Not Just What We Use-It’s How We Use It

When exploring dental implant treatments with Dr. Dave Cho in Katy, TX, what truly sets the experience apart isn’t just the technology-it’s the thoughtful way it’s integrated into every step of your care.

Before a single procedure begins, Dr. Cho uses advanced Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) to capture a detailed 3D image of your entire oral anatomy. This scan doesn’t just show teeth-it maps nerves, sinuses, bone density, and jaw contours with remarkable clarity. It’s how we identify the safest, strongest foundation for your implant. The result? No surprises, just precision from the start.

Digital Smile Design:

Imagine being able to see your new smile before treatment even begins. With Digital Smile Design, that’s exactly what happens. This isn’t a one-size-fits-all approach. Dr. Cho uses computer-generated surgical guides tailored to your anatomy. These guides direct the exact angle, depth, and position for implant placement. It’s surgery with intention-minimizing trauma to surrounding tissue and maximizing long-term stability. That means less discomfort, reduced risk of complications, and a more predictable recovery.

FAQs: 

Q1: How long does a dental implant treatment take from start to finish?
A: Most cases span 3–6 months, depending on healing, bone readiness, and whether extractions or grafting are needed. The timeline is always shared upfront.

Q2: Do I need to replace every missing tooth with a separate implant?
A: Not always. Dr. Cho can often anchor multiple teeth on fewer implants using bridges or All-on-4-style treatments.

Q3: Is implant surgery painful?
A: Thanks to local anesthesia, sedation options, and advanced techniques, discomfort is typically minimal. Most patients return to work the next day.

Q4: Can smokers or older adults still get implants?
A: Yes, with proper screening and bone health checks. Dr. Cho evaluates your overall health to ensure your implants succeed long term.

Q5: What if I had a failed implant before?
A: Revision cases are welcome. We assess what went wrong and create a tailored plan to restore your confidence-and your bite.
