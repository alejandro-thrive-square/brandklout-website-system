Daily Brushing and Flossing Techniques That Actually Work

Most people think they know how to brush and floss—but most people also miss key spots every time.

Our brushing and flossing tips include:

Holding your toothbrush at a 45-degree angle

Brushing in small, circular motions—not side to side

Using soft bristles to protect enamel

Flossing gently against the sides of teeth, not just between them

Adding a fluoride rinse for extra protection


Simple changes. Big difference.
