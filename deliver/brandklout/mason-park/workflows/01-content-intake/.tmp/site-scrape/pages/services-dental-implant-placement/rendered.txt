What Is Dental Implant Placement?

Dental implant placement is a surgical procedure where a small titanium post is inserted into the jawbone to serve as an artificial tooth root. This procedure:

Replaces missing teeth effectively
Provides a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures
Helps maintain jawbone health by stimulating bone tissue
Offers a permanent tooth replacement that feels natural

At Mason Park Dental of Katy, we ensure the dental implant procedure is smooth and successful, using a detailed planning and placement process.

Understanding the Dental Implant Placement Process

Our detailed, patient-centric process for dental implant placement in Katy, TX includes:

1. Comprehensive Consultation

We start with a thorough evaluation to determine if you’re a candidate for dental implants. This includes digital imaging and discussing your health history.

2. Customized Treatment Planning

Using state-of-the-art imaging, Dr. William Sung designs a personalized plan for your dental implant placement based on your jawbone structure and esthetic goals.

3. Surgical Placement

During the implant surgery, a titanium post is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation. This post acts as a sturdy foundation for your future tooth restoration.

4. Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant fuses naturally with the jawbone — a process called osseointegration — ensuring stability for the final restoration.

5. Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, a custom-crafted crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant to complete your smile transformation.

How Dental Implants Compare to Other Tooth Replacement Options

When evaluating tooth replacement options, many patients wonder how dental implants measure up compared to alternatives like dentures or bridges.

Dental Bridges: Bridges rely on adjacent healthy teeth for support, which may compromise their strength over time. Dental implants are independent, preserving the health of surrounding teeth.

Dentures: While dentures are removable and less invasive initially, they often become loose over time and can cause discomfort. Implant-supported dentures offer superior stability without adhesives.

Dental Implants: Offer a permanent, durable solution that maintains bone health, restores function, and looks completely natural.

If you’re weighing your options, Mason Park Dental of Katy can help you determine if implant placement is the best solution for your smile.

What Happens If You Delay Tooth Replacement?

Choosing not to replace missing teeth promptly can lead to several long-term issues:

Bone Loss: Without stimulation from a tooth root or implant, the jawbone naturally deteriorates.

Teeth Shifting: Adjacent teeth can shift into the gap, leading to misalignment and bite problems.

Changes in Facial Structure: Missing teeth contribute to a sunken facial appearance over time.

Increased Oral Health Issues: Gaps can harbor bacteria, increasing your risk for gum disease and decay.

Dental implants act quickly to stop this cycle, preserving your facial esthetics and oral health.

How to Maintain Your Dental Implants for Life

Maintaining your dental implants is simple with the right care:

Daily Oral Hygiene: Brush and floss carefully around implants just like natural teeth.

Regular Dental Visits: Visit Mason Park Dental of Katy every 6 months for checkups and cleanings.

Avoid Bad Habits: Refrain from chewing on hard objects like ice or pens.

Manage Health Conditions: Conditions like diabetes should be managed, as they can affect implant success.

A few small daily habits will protect your investment for decades to come.
