Biography

For Dr. William Sung, dentistry isn’t just a job – it’s a family business! Dr. Sung’s father and two of his uncles are dentists. Dr. Sung was born and raised in California, and graduated from Pacific Union College in Angwin, California with a B.S. degree. He then went on to receive his dental degree from Tufts University in Boston, Massachusetts. It was in Boston where he met his wife, and after practicing for several years in Massachusetts, she decided to move back to her hometown of Houston, Texas. While he is still acclimating to the humidity, Dr. Sung has come to love and appreciate the friendly people, great food, and diverse culture that Texas has to offer.

Though there are many aspects of dentistry he enjoys, Dr. Sung’s main motivation is helping others. Whether he’s easing pain through a much needed dental procedure, or improving confidence with cosmetic dentistry, with every patient he treats, he is reminded that he’s achieved his lifelong dream of working in healthcare.

Dr. Sung has made a lifelong commitment to learning and keeping up with the evolving field of dentistry by taking several continuing education courses each year. He averages almost 100 hours of CE credits each year, which is over eight times the yearly requirement for a dentist in the state of Texas.

Dr. Sung also holds a Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry which is held by only 7% of all general dentists in the State of Texas. To keep up with the latest technologies, procedures, and materials, Dr. Sung is a part of several well known groups and study clubs in an effort to provide the most comprehensive care for his patients.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Sung can be found exploring nature with his wife. They enjoy hiking and an item on their bucket list is to visit all of the National Parks! Dr. Sung also enjoys scuba diving and is fascinated by the underwater world. Like his fellow Texans, he is an avid sports fan. During basketball and football seasons, he stays busy trying to win his fantasy leagues! As an active member of the community, Dr. Sung actively gives back through volunteer work whenever possible.
