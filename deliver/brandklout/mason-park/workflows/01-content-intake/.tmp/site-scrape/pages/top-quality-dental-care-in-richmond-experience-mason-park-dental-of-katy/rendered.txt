Why Richmond Residents Choose Mason Park Dental of Katy
Comprehensive and Personalized Dental Services

At Mason Park Dental, we pride ourselves on offering comprehensive, personalized care. Our practice combines cutting-edge technology with a patient-centered approach to deliver the highest standard of dental care. Whether you need a routine check-up or specialized treatment, we support oral health.

 

Our Extensive Service Offerings
Preventative Dentistry

Maintaining a healthy smile starts with Preventative Dentistry. Our Preventative Dentistry services include regular cleanings, exams, and fluoride treatments to prevent dental issues before they arise. By addressing potential problems early, we help you avoid more complex procedures in the future.

 

Children’s Dentistry

Our Children’s Dentistry services are designed to create a positive and stress-free dental experience for children. Dr. Tam Nguyen, our board-certified pediatric dentist, provides gentle care and helps children understand the importance of good oral hygiene from an early age.

 

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance the appearance of your smile with our Cosmetic Dentistry services. We offer treatments such as teeth whitening, veneers, and bonding to improve the esthetics of your teeth and boost your confidence. Our advanced techniques ensure natural-looking and beautiful results.

 

Restorative Dentistry

Our Restorative Dentistry services can restore function and appearance if you have damaged or missing teeth. We provide fillings, crowns, and bridges to repair and replace damaged teeth, helping you achieve a complete and healthy smile.

 

Hygiene and Periodontics

Healthy gums are vital for overall oral health. Our Hygiene and Periodontics services include thorough cleanings and treatments for gum disease. By focusing on gum health, we help prevent issues affecting teeth and overall well-being.

 

Invisalign

For a discreet way to straighten your teeth, consider Invisalign. This dental treatment uses clear aligners to gradually move your teeth into the desired position without traditional braces. Enjoy a straighter smile with the comfort and convenience of Invisalign.

 

Root Canal Therapy

If you experience severe tooth pain or infection, Root Canal Therapy may be necessary to save your tooth. Our team performs root canal procedures with precision and care, using the latest techniques to ensure a comfortable experience and effective results.

 

Oral Surgery

For more complex dental issues, our Oral Surgery services may be required. We offer extractions and other surgical procedures performed with skill and attention to your comfort, ensuring you receive the care you need.

 

Advanced Technology for Superior Care

At Mason Park Dental, we utilize advanced technology to enhance your dental experience and ensure accurate and efficient treatment. Our state-of-the-art equipment includes:

Cone Beam Imaging: Provides detailed 3D images for precise diagnosis and treatment planning.
Digital X-rays: Offer clear and detailed images with minimal radiation exposure.
Intraoral Camera: Captures high-resolution images of your mouth, helping you understand your dental health.

Soft Tissue Laser: Used for accurate and comfortable treatment of soft tissue issues.

Flexible Scheduling and Emergency Care

We understand that busy schedules can make finding time for dental appointments challenging. That’s why we offer flexible scheduling to accommodate your needs. We provide prompt and efficient care to address urgent issues in a dental emergency.
