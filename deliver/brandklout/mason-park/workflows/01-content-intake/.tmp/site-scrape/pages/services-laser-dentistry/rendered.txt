What is Laser Dentistry?

Laser dentistry is the use of focused light beams to treat various dental conditions with extraordinary precision. Unlike traditional methods that often involve scalpels and drills, lasers allow us to treat the soft tissues and hard structures of the mouth more gently.

The laser works by delivering energy in the form of light. Depending on the procedure, it either cuts or vaporizes the tissue it comes into contact with or strengthens the bond between the tooth and the filling material. Treatments like gum reshaping with laser, laser cavity removal, and laser gum treatment are just a few examples of how we use this technology to enhance patient care.

At Mason Park Dental of Katy, Dr. William Sung uses the latest dental laser systems to ensure treatments are comfortable, efficient, and highly effective for every patient.

FAQs 

Q1: What is laser dentistry and how does it work?
A: Laser dentistry uses targeted light beams to perform precise treatments on teeth and gums. It can cut or reshape tissues without causing significant discomfort, often eliminating the need for traditional tools like scalpels or drills.

Q2: Is laser dentistry safe for all ages?
A: Yes. Laser dental care is safe for children, adults, and seniors when performed by trained providers like Dr. William Sung. Its precision and minimal invasiveness make it a great choice for sensitive patients.

Q3: Will I still need anesthesia for laser procedures?
A: In many cases, laser treatments require little to no anesthesia. However, this depends on the type of procedure and your individual comfort level.

Q4: How soon can I return to normal activities after a laser treatment?
A: Recovery after laser dental treatments is typically much quicker than traditional methods. Most patients can resume normal activities the same day or shortly after their procedure.

Q5: What kinds of dental problems can be treated with lasers?
A: Lasers are used for gum reshaping with laser, laser gum treatment, laser cavity removal, cold sore relief, and even soft tissue surgeries like frenectomies. Your dentist will recommend the best options based on your needs.
