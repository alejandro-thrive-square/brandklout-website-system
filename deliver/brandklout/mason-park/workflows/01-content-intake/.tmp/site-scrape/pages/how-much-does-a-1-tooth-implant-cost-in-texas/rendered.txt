What Is a Single Tooth Implant?

A single tooth implant is a long-lasting, natural-looking solution for replacing a missing tooth—without affecting the surrounding healthy teeth. This dental restoration closely mimics the function and appearance of a natural tooth and is made up of three essential components:

Implant Post: A small titanium screw that is surgically placed into the jawbone. This acts as the artificial root, bonding with the bone through a process called osseointegration, providing a stable and durable foundation.
Abutment: A connector piece that is attached to the implant post. It serves as the link between the implanted root and the visible crown.
Crown: The top, tooth-shaped cap made from porcelain or other tooth-colored materials. This is custom-crafted to match the shape, size, and color of your surrounding teeth, ensuring a seamless smile.

Average Cost of a Single Tooth Implant in Texas

Replacing a missing tooth with a dental implant is one of the most reliable and long-lasting solutions in modern dentistry. In Texas, the average cost of a single tooth implant typically ranges between $3,000 and $5,500, though this may vary based on several key factors like the dentist’s expertise, geographic location, and the complexity of the case.

🔍 Cost Breakdown for a Single Tooth Implant

To better understand where the cost comes from, let’s break it down into its main components:

Implant Surgery:
Estimated cost: $1,500–$2,500
This involves the surgical placement of a titanium post into the jawbone, which acts as a root replacement. The cost may vary depending on bone density, sedation needs, and the type of implant system used.
Abutment Placement:
Estimated cost: $300–$500
The abutment is the connector piece placed on top of the implant that holds the crown. Custom abutments tend to be more expensive than standard ones.
Crown Placement:
Estimated cost: $1,000–$2,500
This is the final visible part of the implant—a lifelike ceramic or porcelain crown customized to match your natural teeth.

💡 In some cases, bone grafting or sinus lifts may be necessary before implant placement, which can increase the total cost.

Factors Affecting Cost

When calculating the final cost of a tooth implant, here are the Factors affecting implant cost in Texas by Dr. Dave Cho:

Bone grafting: Needed when bone loss has occurred. Cost: $300–$1,500
Tooth extraction: $100–$500 per tooth
Sedation method: Oral or IV sedation adds to the bill
Geographic location: Texas averages are slightly lower than the national average
Material of crown: Porcelain-fused-to-metal or zirconia vary in price

FAQs

1.Is a dental implant painful?
A. Most patients report minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia and optional sedation make the procedure manageable.

2.How long does a single tooth implant take?
A. From implant placement to crown attachment, expect 4–6 months for full completion.

3.Are tooth implants covered by insurance?
A. Some dental insurance plans cover part of the procedure. Check your benefits or ask Dr. Cho’s team for help.

4.Can you get a same-day implant?
A. Same-day implants are available for certain eligible candidates. Dr. Cho will determine if this applies to your case.

5.What happens if I delay replacing my missing tooth?
A. Delays can lead to bone loss, shifting teeth, and bite problems. Early intervention is always recommended.
