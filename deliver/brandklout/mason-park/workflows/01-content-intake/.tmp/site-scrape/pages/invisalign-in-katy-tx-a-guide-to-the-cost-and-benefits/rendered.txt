What is Invisalign?
Definition and explanation of Invisalign

Before we delve into the cost and benefits of Invisalign, it’s crucial to grasp the essence of this innovative treatment. Invisalign is a contemporary teeth-straightening method that employs clear aligners, offering a discreet and comfortable alternative to conventional metal braces. 

These aligners are designed to gradually shift the teeth into the desired position. What sets them apart is their customization for each individual, ensuring a comfortable and effective treatment process.

How Invisalign works

Understanding how the treatment works is crucial for those considering Invisalign. Invisalign aligners are virtually invisible, making them a popular choice for individuals looking to enhance their smile without the conspicuousness of metal braces. These transparent aligners are removable, allowing for easy cleaning and maintenance and the freedom to enjoy meals without restrictions.

Benefits of Invisalign
Esthetically pleasing

Cost of Invisalign 

To get a clear picture of the cost of Invisalign treatment, it’s crucial to consider various factors influencing the final price.

Factors affecting the cost
The severity of the dental misalignment
The length of the treatment
The specific Invisalign provider 
Additional treatments needed alongside Invisalign

Assume that the average cost of Invisalign treatment at reputable clinics ranges from $3,000 to $8,000.

The average cost of Invisalign 

The average cost of Invisalign can vary based on the factors mentioned above. However, it should be noted that Invisalign treatment typically costs around $5,000 on average in this area.

It’s important to consider this average cost as a starting point, as the final price may change based on your specific dental needs and the treatment plan your dentist recommends.

Comparison of comfort and convenience

Regarding comfort and convenience, Invisalign offers flexibility that traditional braces struggle to match. Patients often appreciate the ease of keeping Invisalign aligners for meals, ensuring comfort and confidence throughout the treatment process.

In terms of cost, while Invisalign treatment may have a higher initial price point, the benefits of comfort and esthetics can outweigh the financial considerations in the long run.

The Invisalign Process
Initial consultation and treatment planning

For those considering Invisalign treatment, the journey begins with an initial consultation. Dr. Sung & Dr. Cho will assess your teeth and discuss your treatment goals during this appointment. They will create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs and desired outcomes using advanced technology, such as digital scans and imaging.

Customized aligner creation

One key benefit of Invisalign treatment is the creation of customized aligners. These clear, removable aligners are designed to shift teeth into the desired position gradually. Using the latest technology, including 3D printing, each set of aligners is precisely crafted to fit your unique smile, ensuring optimal comfort and effectiveness.

Invisalign treatment involves wearing a series of aligners, typically changing to a new set every 1-2 weeks, as directed by Dr. Sung & Dr. Cho. These aligners should be worn for 20-22 hours a day, only removing them for brushing, and flossing. The aligners should be cleaned regularly with lukewarm water and a soft toothbrush to maintain proper hygiene and clarity.

Follow-up appointments and progress tracking

Throughout your Invisalign treatment, Dr. Sung & Dr. Cho will schedule regular follow-up appointments to monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments. These appointments are crucial for tracking your teeth’s movement and ensuring your treatment is on track. Dr. Sung & Dr. Cho will provide guidance and support to help you achieve the best results from your Invisalign treatment.

FAQ
Q: What is Invisalign, and how does it work?

A: Invisalign is a clear aligner system that helps straighten teeth without using traditional braces. It works by gradually shifting your teeth into the desired position using custom-made aligners.

Q: How much does Invisalign treatment typically cost?

A: The cost of Invisalign treatment can vary depending on individual needs, but it generally ranges from $3,000 to $8,000. Factors such as the severity of the case and the treatment length can impact the total cost. 

Q: What are the benefits of choosing Invisalign over traditional braces?

A: Invisalign offers several benefits, including improved comfort, convenience, and esthetics. The clear aligners are removable, making it easier to maintain oral hygiene and virtually invisible, providing a more discreet teeth-aligning treatment option.

Q: How long does the Invisalign treatment process usually take?

A: The duration of Invisalign treatment varies depending on individual needs, but on average, it can take anywhere from 6 to 18 months. Your dentist will create a customized treatment plan tailored to your specific case to help achieve the best results promptly.

Q: Who is a good candidate for Invisalign treatment?

A: Dr. William Sung, DMD and Dr. Dave Cho can determine if you are a good candidate for Invisalign treatment during a consultation.
