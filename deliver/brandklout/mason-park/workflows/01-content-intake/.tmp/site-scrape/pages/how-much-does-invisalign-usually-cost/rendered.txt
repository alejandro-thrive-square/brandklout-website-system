Let’s Get Real About Invisalign and the Cost of a Confident Smile

There’s something transformative about a smile that you’re proud of. It shows up in the way you carry yourself, the photos you stop avoiding, and even the way you interact with the world around you. At Mason Park Dental, we see this transformation happen every day—through a clear, flexible, and convenient system called Invisalign.

But here’s the question almost everyone asks:


How much does Invisalign usually cost?

We get it. Before anyone commits to anything, they want to know if it’s affordable, practical, and actually worth it. You’re not just paying for plastic trays—you’re investing in something personal. This blog is your insider’s look at Invisalign pricing with Dr. William Sung, what’s included, and how to make the process doable financially and personally.

Let’s dive in.

Clear Aligners, Clear Expectations

You’ve probably seen someone wearing Invisalign and didn’t even know it. That’s kind of the point. These custom-fitted aligners are nearly invisible, and they work while you go about your day—at work, school, dinner, or even during a Zoom call.

If you’re wondering whether it’s effective, the short answer is yes. For many patients, Invisalign can correct crooked teeth, close gaps, and align bites without metal brackets or wires. And for patients at Mason Park Dental, it’s all managed under the guidance and support of Dr. William Sung, who has helped hundreds of patients reshape their smiles comfortably.

Now, let’s talk numbers.

So, How Much Does Dr. William Sung Charge for Invisalign?

The average Invisalign treatment at Mason Park Dental ranges between $3,500 and $6,000. That’s the broad range, but the final cost depends on a few personal factors:

How complex your tooth movement needs are

How long your treatment will take

Whether refinements are needed along the way
What’s included in your specific treatment plan


This means that for someone with a mild issue that requires less time, the cost may be on the lower end. For more advanced cases needing longer treatment and more aligners, the price may be higher.

And yes, Invisalign treatment fees Dr. William Sung provides always include the essentials—initial consultation, digital scans, aligners, routine check-ins, and post-treatment retainers. No hidden surprises.

What Are You Actually Paying For?

Let’s clear something up—Invisalign isn’t just about the plastic trays. It’s a carefully designed, high-tech, personalized process that brings your smile from where it is today to where you’ve always wanted it to be. When people ask about the Invisalign cost with Dr. William Sung, they’re often thinking only about the physical aligners. But what you’re actually paying for is the complete experience of thoughtful, hands-on care—and that matters more than you might realize.

Here’s the behind-the-scenes breakdown of what goes into the cost:

Custom 3D Digital Planning

Everything starts with precision. At your first visit, you’ll skip the old-school, messy impressions and instead experience the iTero scanner. This advanced tool captures a full digital map of your mouth in minutes. It’s painless, quick, and accurate.

But this isn’t just a fancy picture. Dr. Sung uses that digital model to simulate your entire smile journey—step-by-step, tooth-by-tooth—before you even put on your first tray. You’ll actually get to preview your final smile from the very beginning.

This kind of digital planning ensures your treatment is not only customized but also predictable. That means fewer surprises and a more confident path forward.

A Series of Aligner Sets

You’re not just getting one tray and hoping for the best. You’ll receive a series of clear aligners, each one slightly different from the last, designed to move your teeth gradually over time.

Every one or two weeks, you’ll switch to the next set in your series. The shifts are gentle but effective. Each new tray builds on the progress of the last, nudging your teeth closer to their final, aligned position.

These aligners are designed using patented SmartTrack material, which helps ensure they’re not only effective but comfortable and snug. This isn’t a generic solution—it’s engineered for movement, made for your mouth.

Office Visits and Real-Time Adjustments

You’re not left to figure it out on your own. Every 6 to 8 weeks, you’ll come in for a quick check-in with Dr. Sung. These visits are an essential part of the treatment.

During these appointments, we make sure everything is moving according to plan. If your teeth need a little encouragement or a mid-course adjustment, that’s when it happens. Sometimes, attachments (those tiny tooth-colored buttons) are added to help guide movement more precisely. Other times, refinements might be made to improve the outcome.

These visits are included in the Invisalign treatment fees Dr. William Sung offers—there are no surprise upcharges for doing things right.

Vivera Retainers: Your Smile’s Security System

After the active alignment phase is done, your teeth will look great—but they’ll still need a little help staying in place. That’s where retainers come in.

Vivera retainers, made by the same team behind Invisalign, are designed to be strong, durable, and nearly invisible. They’re crafted with the same precision as your aligners and are built to last far longer than traditional retainers.

And yes—they’re included in your treatment plan. No extra fees. Just one more way Dr. Sung helps you protect the investment you’ve made in your new smile.

Dedicated Support, Start to Finish

From the moment you walk in the door to the day you finish treatment (and even beyond), Dr. Sung and the team are with you every step of the way.

Got a question about your trays? Wondering how long to wear them before switching? Need a quick fit-check before vacation? We’re here for it.

This isn’t a mail-order smile. This is local care, hands-on guidance, and real-time expertise. At Mason Park Dental, Dr. William Sung Invisalign pricing reflects the value of personalized support, not just a product in a box.

Real-Life Value: What Invisalign Gives You That Money Can’t Measure

Here’s the truth: the benefits of Invisalign go way beyond straight teeth. The emotional and practical value is harder to quantify, but no less important.

You’ll brush and floss with ease

You’ll enjoy eating without limitations

You’ll stop covering your mouth when you laugh

You’ll feel more like yourself in social situations

You’ll stop worrying about food stuck in brackets


And the boost in confidence? Priceless.

That article emphasized how small daily habits add up over time for your dog’s health. The same applies here: wearing your aligners consistently and caring for your smile now means fewer dental issues down the line.

But Is It Really Worth the Cost?

Here’s a question we often hear:

Is Invisalign worth the price tag?

If you’ve ever hesitated to smile in photos, or dreaded the thought of metal braces as an adult, then yes—it’s worth it. Because this isn’t about vanity. It’s about reclaiming how you feel when you show your teeth. And it’s about making oral care easier and more effective.

At Mason Park Dental, we’ve seen Invisalign change lives—and that’s something no dollar amount can capture.

Invisalign vs. The “Wait and See” Approach

Some patients think, “I’ll do it next year.” But here’s what often happens when you delay:

Crowded teeth get harder to clean

Jaw discomfort worsens over time

Small gaps or shifts grow larger

Dental treatments get more expensive


Choosing Invisalign now, with a custom plan from Dr. William Sung, means stopping those small issues before they turn into big ones.

And remember: early action usually means a shorter, more affordable treatment.
