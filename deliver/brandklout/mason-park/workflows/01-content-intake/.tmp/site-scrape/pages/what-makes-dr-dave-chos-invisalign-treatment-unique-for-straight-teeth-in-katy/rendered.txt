What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a modern dental solution designed to discreetly and effectively straighten teeth. Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners custom-made for your unique dental structure. Each aligner is crafted from a patented thermoplastic material known as SmartTrack, ensuring comfort and safety—free from BPA, latex, or gluten.

These clear aligners work gradually, moving your teeth into the desired position without the need for wires or brackets. The process begins with advanced digital scanning, which allows us to create a precise 3D model of your mouth. This model forms the basis of a customized treatment plan that maps out the entire journey to your new smile.

Considerations Before Choosing Invisalign

While Invisalign offers numerous advantages, it is essential to understand a few considerations before embarking on your treatment journey:

1. Commitment to Wearing Aligners

The effectiveness of Invisalign is largely dependent on patient compliance. For the treatment to work as intended, aligners must be worn for the recommended 20-22 hours per day. Non-compliance can extend the treatment duration and compromise results.

2. Suitability for All Cases

Not every dental alignment issue can be corrected with Invisalign. Severe malocclusions or complex dental cases might require traditional braces or additional treatment options. A thorough evaluation during your consultation will determine if you’re an ideal candidate for this treatment.

3. Regular Maintenance

While Invisalign aligners are easier to maintain than traditional braces, they still require proper cleaning to prevent bacterial buildup. At Mason Park Dental, we provide clear instructions on how to care for your aligners to ensure optimal hygiene throughout your treatment.
