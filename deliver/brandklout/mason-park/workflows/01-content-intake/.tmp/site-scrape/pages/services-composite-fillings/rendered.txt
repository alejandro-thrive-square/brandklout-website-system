What Are Composite Fillings?

Composite fillings are made from a blend of plastic resin and finely ground glass particles. This combination produces a material that closely matches the color and translucency of natural teeth. These tooth-colored fillings offer a discreet way to restore decayed, chipped, or broken teeth without leaving noticeable signs of dental work.

Unlike older silver or metal fillings, composite fillings bond directly to the tooth structure. This bonding process not only strengthens the tooth but also requires less removal of healthy enamel, helping to preserve as much of your natural tooth as possible.

When placed by an experienced dental team like ours at Mason Park Dental, composite fillings in Katy, TX provide a durable, seamless repair that supports long-term oral health.

With composite materials, less healthy tooth structure needs to be removed compared to amalgam fillings. This more conservative approach means that more of your natural tooth can be preserved, which is important for the long-term durability and function of your teeth. We believe in protecting as much of your natural tooth as possible.

Mercury-Free Peace of Mind

Traditional silver fillings contain mercury, a topic that has caused concern among many patients. Composite fillings are completely mercury-free, offering a safer, metal-free alternative for those who prioritize biocompatibility and peace of mind in their dental care decisions.

Once your composite filling is sculpted and polished by Dr. William Sung, it feels just like a natural part of your tooth. There are no rough edges or uneven surfaces — just a smooth, natural bite that allows you to chew, speak, and smile with total confidence.

Our Composite Filling Procedure

At Mason Park Dental, we believe that restoring your teeth should be a smooth, stress-free experience. When you visit us for composite fillings, Dr. William Sung and our caring team guide you through each step with a focus on your comfort and long-term oral health. Here’s what you can expect from start to finish:

1. Comprehensive Examination

Every successful dental treatment begins with a thorough evaluation.
During your visit, Dr. William Sung will carefully examine the affected tooth or teeth, looking for signs of decay, cracks, or structural damage. To ensure an accurate diagnosis, we may take digital X-rays for a clearer, more detailed view of the internal structures.

Once the examination is complete, Dr. Sung will walk you through your treatment options. If a tooth-colored composite filling is recommended, he will explain why it’s the best choice to restore your tooth while maintaining a natural appearance.

Key goals during this stage:

Identifying all areas of concern
Ensuring early detection of hidden cavities
Providing a customized plan tailored to your needs
2. Gentle Preparation

Patient comfort is a top priority for us.
Before starting the procedure, we will gently administer local anesthesia to numb the area around the tooth, ensuring that you remain comfortable throughout your treatment. Dr. Sung takes special care to make the numbing process as smooth and painless as possible.

Once the area is numb, the decayed or damaged parts of the tooth are carefully removed. Our technique is minimally invasive, meaning we preserve as much healthy tooth structure as possible. By doing so, we ensure better long-term strength and support for the tooth.

Our gentle approach includes:

Thorough cleaning of the affected area
Removing only compromised tissue
Protecting healthy tooth structure
3. Layered Filling Placement

After the tooth is properly prepared, the composite filling material is placed in carefully controlled layers.
Each thin layer is applied and then hardened (or cured) using a special dental curing light. Layering not only strengthens the filling but also allows Dr. Sung to shape the material with precision, adapting perfectly to the natural form of your tooth.

This step is crucial because it:

Provides a strong bond to the tooth
Reduces the chance of gaps, leaks, or future issues
Ensures an even distribution of material for a solid, long-lasting restoration

The filling material is selected to match the exact shade of your natural teeth, creating a virtually invisible restoration.

4. Shaping and Polishing

Once all layers have been cured and the tooth has regained its full shape, Dr. Sung begins the final touches.
He will sculpt and contour the filling to match the natural anatomy of your tooth, ensuring that your bite feels completely natural and comfortable.

After the shaping is complete, the tooth is polished to a beautiful, glossy finish. This not only improves the appearance but also prevents staining and plaque buildup.

At the end of the process:

The filled tooth blends seamlessly with your surrounding teeth
You’ll enjoy a smooth, natural feel when you bite and chew
Your smile is restored in both function and esthetics
Aftercare for Composite Fillings

Taking good care of your composite fillings ensures they last as long as possible. Here are some simple tips:

Brush your teeth twice a day with fluoride toothpaste.
Floss daily to remove plaque from between your teeth and along the gumline.
Avoid biting down on hard foods like ice, hard candies, or popcorn kernels, especially on freshly restored teeth.
Limit highly pigmented foods and beverages such as coffee, red wine, and tea, which can sometimes stain resin material over time.
Visit Mason Park Dental of Katy regularly for checkups and professional cleanings to monitor your fillings and overall oral health.

With proper care, composite fillings in Katy, TX typically last anywhere from 5 to 10 years, and sometimes longer depending on factors like the size of the filling and your oral habits.

FAQs 

1. What are composite fillings made of?
Composite fillings are crafted from a combination of plastic resin and finely ground glass particles. This mixture produces a durable, tooth-colored material that bonds tightly to your natural tooth structure for a seamless repair.

2. Is getting a composite filling painful?
The process of getting a composite filling is generally painless. Local anesthesia is used to numb the area completely before treatment begins. Most patients experience little to no discomfort during or after the procedure.

3. Can I replace my old metal fillings with composite fillings?
Yes. Many patients choose to replace old silver amalgam fillings with tooth-colored fillings for a more natural appearance. Dr. William Sung can assess your current restorations and recommend safe replacement options if appropriate.

4. How do I care for my composite fillings after placement?
Caring for your resin fillings involves brushing and flossing daily, avoiding excessive biting on hard objects, and maintaining regular dental checkups. Good oral hygiene helps protect both your natural teeth and your restorations.

5. Do composite fillings stain over time?
While composite fillings are resistant to stains, they can occasionally pick up discoloration from foods and beverages like coffee, tea, and red wine. Regular cleanings at Mason Park Dental help minimize staining and keep your fillings looking great.
