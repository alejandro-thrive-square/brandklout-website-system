During your first visit, you will receive personalized attention from Dr. William Sung or Dr. Dave Cho. Our team will conduct a thorough dental exam and discuss your concerns. With new patient specials, your initial visit will focus on understanding your dental health comprehensively.

FAQ:

Q: What should I expect during my first visit?

A: During your first visit, we focus on providing a comprehensive new patient experience. You will begin by filling out some paperwork that includes your health history. Afterwards, either Dr. William Sung or Dr. Dave Cho will conduct a thorough dental exam. This will include checking your teeth and gums, evaluating your oral health, and discussing any concerns you may have.

Q: Are there any new patient specials available for my first visit?

A: Yes, we offer new patient specials. These promotions can make your first visit more affordable while ensuring you receive the top-quality care you deserve. Please contact our office directly to learn more about the current new patient specials we have available.

Q: What new patient services are provided at your clinic?

A: Mason Park Dental provides services tailored for new patients. This includes comprehensive dental exams, professional cleanings, and treatment planning to address your needs. Our team ensures you feel comfortable and informed about your dental health and any necessary treatments.

Q: How do Dr. William Sung and Dr. Dave Cho approach new patient care?

A: Dr. William Sung and Dr. Dave Cho prioritize personalized care for each new patient. They take the time to listen to your concerns, answer any questions, and discuss the best treatment options for you. They aim to create a welcoming environment that fosters trust and open communication about your dental health.

Q: What should I bring to my first appointment?

A: To make the most of your first appointment, please bring your insurance card (if applicable), a valid ID, and a list of medications you currently take. This information will help us provide the best new patient care and assess your dental needs accurately.
