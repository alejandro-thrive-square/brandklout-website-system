Meet Dr. Noura Fadel
Dr. Noura Fadel, DDS is the founder of Tulip Dental in Redlands, CA, where she combines her passion for dentistry with a genuine dedication to helping patients achieve healthier, more confident smiles. 

With years of clinical experience, Dr. Noura is known for her gentle approach, diligence, and commitment to patient education. She believes dentistry should be a partnership, where patients are empowered to understand their oral health and feel confident in every decision. 

Dr. Noura served as an Assistant Professor at Loma Linda University and brought over a decade of comprehensive clinical experience to her academic and professional endeavors. She is a Fellow and active member of the Academy of General Dentistry, reflecting her commitment to excellence and continued advancement in the field. 

Dedicated to lifelong learning, Dr. Noura continuously seeks to integrate the latest advancements in dental technology and evidence-based practices into patient care. Her clinical philosophy centers on providing individualized, patient-focused treatment, with a strong emphasis on comfort, precision, and comprehensive oral health. 

Dr. Noura earned her dental degree and completed a post-doctoral residency at Ain Shams University in Cairo, Egypt, in 2005. She subsequently pursued advanced clinical training at the University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry in Rochester, New York.
