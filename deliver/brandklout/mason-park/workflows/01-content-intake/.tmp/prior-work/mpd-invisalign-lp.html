Invisalign can help in treating different bite problems.
Some of these are:

01-Crossbite:

This happens when the upper and lower jaws are misaligned. This can be the reason for degradation of teeth, loss of bone and periodontal disease.

02-Gapped teeth:

The gaps in between the teeth may occur due to abnormal growth of jawbone or lack of surrounding teeth allowing them to shift into the created space.

03-Underbite:

This may take place due to inadequate upper teeth or growth anomalies in the jaw causing an inappropriate bite.

04-Overbite:

This occurs when the upper jaw is just over the lower jaw. An overbite may cause wearing of teeth on the lower jaw, joint problems and gum problems.

05-Crowded teeth:

If the crowded teeth are not treated, then they may become extremely crooked. It might be difficult for you to clean misaligned teeth that can cause gum disease, plaque and tooth decay.

06-Open bite:

An open bite happens when the teeth in the upper and lower jaws do not seem to meet properly.

In case of more complicated problems, you might be the candidate for fixed, lingual or clear braces.
