On The Label

Four things worth checking.

1

Fluoride, at the right strength

Toothpastes and mouth rinses with fluoride are available over the counter, and stronger rinses can be prescribed. Dr. Nguyen will advise you on your child’s fluoride needs.

2

A soft brush, and a small head

Small enough to reach the back molars of the mouth your child has now, not the one they will have in three years.

3

Low abrasiveness

The RDA value, which almost never appears on the tube. Our table has ninety of them, and a child’s paste should be under 70.

4

No whitening claim

Whitening and tartar-control formulations are the abrasive ones. Neither is something a child’s teeth need.
