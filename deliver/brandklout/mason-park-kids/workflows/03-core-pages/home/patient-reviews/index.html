The Reasons

There’s a reason parents choose us.

01

A practice just for children

Every room, routine and conversation is planned around a child, with a pediatric dentist at the chair.

02

Six months to high school

From the first tooth to the last day of high school, one practice keeps every check-up, x-ray and plan in one place.

03

Sensory-friendly, on purpose

Routine-based visits, quiet low-light rooms and tools like headphones or weighted blankets for the children who need them.

A Platinum Invisalign® provider

Done Right

Kids’ dentistry, done right.

Our kid-friendly environment, gentle explanations, and calming techniques help children feel relaxed and safe throughout their visit.

  • Gentle explanations before every step
  • Longer appointments and desensitization visits
  • Nitrous oxide, oral conscious and IV sedation, closely monitored
