Patient resources

How rough is the toothpaste you are already buying?

RDA is Relative Dentin Abrasivity: a measure of how much a toothpaste wears the tooth as it cleans it. Higher is not better, and for a child it is usually worse.

Ask at your next visit The bands
HomePatient resourcesToothpaste RDA table

The Bands

Four bands, and where to aim.

The scale runs from 0, a brush with plain water, to 250. Anything above 250 is not permitted for sale.

0 to 70

Low abrasive. Where a child’s toothpaste should sit, and where most sensitivity formulations are.

70 to 100

Medium abrasive. Most ordinary family toothpastes are in this band.

100 to 150

Highly abrasive. Usually a whitening or tartar-control claim on the front of the tube.

150 to 250

Regarded as the harmful limit. Not a band to put a child’s enamel in twice a day.
