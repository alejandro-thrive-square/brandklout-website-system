For referring clinicians

Send us the details and we will call the family.

For dentists, pediatricians and lactation consultants referring a child to a board certified pediatric dentist. The child goes back to you afterwards unless you ask us to keep them.

Call the practice How to refer
HomePatientsRefer a patient

How

Two ways, and what happens next.

Sending the referral

Call the practice and we will take the referral over the phone, or email the patient’s details and any radiographs and we will contact the family directly to schedule.

Call: (281) 578-7100
Email: office@masonparkdental.com

After the appointment

We will send you a report after the appointment, and the child goes back to you for their routine care unless you ask us to keep them.

What For

What we most often see on referral.

Infants and tongue-tie

A frenectomy takes less than fifteen minutes, and Dr. Nguyen’s residency covered children as young as six months.

Special needs patients

Sensory-friendly, routine-based care, longer appointments and desensitization visits, with sedation where it is the kind option.

Children who need sedation

Nitrous oxide, oral and IV sedation, and general anesthesia.

The child who will not open

Knee-to-knee exams, and a practice arranged around the children other practices find difficult.

The Invitation

Call and we will take it over the phone.

Or email the patient’s details and any radiographs to office@masonparkdental.com and we will contact the family directly to schedule.
