Meet the doctors

One specialist. One generalist.

The Specialist

Dr. Tam Nguyen, DMD

Board certified pediatric dentist

“My philosophy focuses on promoting healthy lifestyles and oral habits from a young age. By introducing parents and patients to the dentist at an early age, my staff and I are able to guide parents and children as they grow, and build trusting relationships with our families.”

Dr. Nguyen is proud to call Houston her home. She was born and raised in southwest Houston, where she attended DeBakey High School for Health Professions and graduated with a Bachelor’s degree from the University of Texas at Austin. She obtained her dental degree from Boston University in Boston, MA where she also met her husband, William.

The rest of her story

After practicing in Massachusetts, they decided to move back to her hometown of Houston, Texas. Following several years of work as a general dentist in Richmond, Dr. Nguyen realized her passion was to work with kids and she decided to go back to school to specialize in pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Nguyen returned to Boston University to serve her pediatric residency and earn a specialty degree. During the residency program, Dr. Nguyen gained insight and knowledge about treating children as young as 6 months old as well as patients with special needs. We provide treatment in a fun, gentle, and relaxing environment and believe in the importance of working together to provide the best treatment for your child.

The Generalist

The dentist the parents see.

Dr. Cho met Dr. Nguyen at dental school in Boston. He practices in the family side of the building, and parents who mention him in their reviews of the children’s practice are describing a visit where the whole family was seen at once.

General dentist

Dr. Dave Cho

Dr. Cho enjoys seeing patients of all ages and his favorite part about dentistry is forming lifelong relationships with his patients. His laid back attitude and calm demeanor immediately puts patients at ease. He is experienced in oral sedation, endodontics, oral surgery, implant placement, and cosmetic dentistry. He strives to provide quality dentistry by attending several continuing education courses each year.

How he got here

Dr. Cho grew up in Southern California and attended the University of California, Riverside where he received his Bachelor’s degree in Biochemistry. He considered dentistry while in college, but was offered an opportunity at a pharmaceutical company where he worked for five years. Dentistry was always in the back of his mind and he decided to pursue a career change. This led him to Boston where he obtained his dental degree from Tufts University, and also where he met Dr. Nguyen, moved to Houston to be closer to family, and the rest is history!

Dr. Cho lives in Houston with his wife, Ellie, and their puppy, Mimi. They are first time parents to their newborn daughter, Claudia, and he is a proud #girldad!
