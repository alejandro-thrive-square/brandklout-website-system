Ways to Pay

Six ways to settle the visit.

Payment is due at the time of service. We accept these forms of payment: cash, check, and all major credit cards. We also accept Alphaeon, Lending Club and CareCredit for those in need of a finance option.

The three finance options are offered by third parties on their own terms. The practice does not set the rates; ask the front desk which one fits before treatment begins, so the plan is in place when payment is due.

The Plan

No dental insurance? There is a plan for that.

We understand that not everyone in Katy has access to dental insurance. We know that should not prevent you from receiving quality dental care at an affordable price! That’s why we’ve started an annual membership plan for our patients.

The dental savings plan

Questions

Before you hand over a card.

From the practice’s own pediatric dentistry FAQ, word for word.

Do you accept insurance?
Yes! Mason Park Dental accepts most PPO insurance plans and Medicaid for qualified patients. We also offer transparent billing and flexible financing.
Are there new patient specials?
We offer special discounted exams, X-rays, cleanings, and fluoride treatments for new patients ages 12 and under and ages 13+. Emergency exams are also available at a reduced price. See new patients.
Can a special be combined with insurance?
No. The published specials cannot be combined with insurance, are for new patients only, and are not valid in cases of periodontal disease.
What if we have no insurance at all?
The practice runs an annual dental savings plan: two exams and two cleanings a year, discounts on standard procedures, no pre-authorization and no exclusions for pre-existing conditions.
