The Basics

First visits, and how often.

When should my child first visit the dentist?

Children should visit a pediatric dentist by age one or within six months of their first tooth. This early visit helps prevent dental issues and establishes a positive relationship with the dentist.

How often should kids come in?

Kids should come in every six months for cleanings and checkups. Some children may need more frequent visits depending on their oral health needs.

Why a pediatric dentist rather than a family dentist?

Pediatric dentists receive specialized training in child behavior, growth, development, and children’s oral health needs, making them the best choice for infants, kids, and teens.

Cleanings

What happens at a cleaning.

What happens during a cleaning?

A cleaning includes gentle plaque removal, polishing, fluoride application, and an oral exam to check teeth, gums, and bite development.

Is fluoride safe for children?

Yes! Fluoride is safe and helps strengthen tooth enamel, making teeth more resistant to cavities. We customize fluoride use based on your child’s age and needs.

How can I prevent cavities at home?

Brush with fluoride toothpaste twice daily, floss daily, limit sugary snacks, and schedule regular dental visits every six months.

Cavities

Decay, fillings, and baby teeth.

Why do children get cavities more easily than adults?

Children’s enamel is softer, making teeth more prone to decay. Diet, brushing technique, genetics, and bacteria levels can all contribute to cavities.

What is a tooth-coloured filling?

Tooth-colored fillings are natural-looking restorations used to repair cavities while blending seamlessly with your child’s teeth.

Do baby teeth really matter?

Absolutely. Baby teeth help with speech development, chewing, and guiding permanent teeth into proper alignment.

The Nervous Child

Fear, and what to do about it.

How do I prepare a nervous child?

Use positive language, read dental-themed books, and bring comfort items like a blanket or toy. Avoid words like “hurt” or “pain.”

What do you do to help a frightened child?

Our kid-friendly environment, gentle explanations, and calming techniques help children feel relaxed and safe throughout their visit.

Can I stay in the room with my child?

Yes! Parents are always welcome to accompany their child if it helps them feel more comfortable.

Special Needs

Care that is arranged around the child.

Do you treat children with special needs?

Yes. We specialize in sensory-friendly care, using routine-based visits, gentle communication, quiet rooms, and tools like headphones or weighted blankets.

What do you do differently for them?

We offer longer appointments, desensitization visits, low-light rooms, and flexible approaches customized to each child’s comfort and abilities.

Can my child be sedated if they need to be?

Yes. We provide carefully monitored sedation options-including nitrous oxide, oral sedation, and IV sedation-to ensure a calm and safe experience.

Emergencies

When something has already happened.

What should I do if my child knocks out a tooth?

Call us immediately. For permanent teeth, gently place the tooth back in the socket or store it in milk. Avoid touching the root.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Toothaches, swelling, knocked-out teeth, chips, broken fillings, infections, or trauma are all considered dental emergencies.

Do you see emergencies the same day?

Yes. We provide rapid, calm, and sensory-friendly emergency treatment with minimal wait times and personalized support.

Insurance and Booking

Paying for it, and getting in.

Do you take insurance?

Yes! Mason Park Dental accepts most PPO insurance plans and Medicaid for qualified patients. We also offer transparent billing and flexible financing.

Is there anything for families without a plan?

We offer special discounted exams, X-rays, cleanings, and fluoride treatments for new patients ages 12 and under and ages 13+. Emergency exams are also available at a reduced price.

How do I book?

Booking is easy! Call (281) 578 to 7100 to schedule your child’s visit or request an appointment through our website.
