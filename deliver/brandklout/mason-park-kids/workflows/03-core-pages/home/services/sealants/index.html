Preventive dentistry

The grooves in a new molar are where cavities start.

Sealants are highly effective in preventing tooth decay and cavities on your child’s molars and premolars. These areas are the most susceptible to cavities due to the anatomized grooves.

The Treatment

When they go on, and what they do not do.

The years they cover

Typically, children get sealants on their permanent molars and premolars as soon as these teeth come in. These sealants can protect the teeth through the cavity-prone years of ages 6 to 14. However, adults without decay or fillings in their molars can also benefit from sealants.

What they do not replace

Sealants are not a substitute for brushing your teeth. It is important to maintain proper oral hygiene and keep up with professional dental cleanings. Also, sealants do not protect between the teeth, so it is essential to continue to floss daily.

The Path

Four steps, one appointment, no drill.

1

The teeth are cleaned.

Thoroughly, because a sealant has to bond to the tooth surface and not to anything sitting on it.

2

The teeth are dried.

A dry surface is the whole trick. Nothing is removed and nothing is injected.

3

The solution goes on.

A liquid solution is put on the chewing surfaces of the teeth, where the grooves are.

4

It is cured.

The solution is cured using a special curing light, and your child can eat straight afterwards.
