The Need

Why the ordinary appointment does not always fit.

Drs. Sung and Nguyen have found that many children with special needs have unique dental care needs that require a flexible approach. Certain patients might have medication or dietary needs that can compromise their dental health. Other patients have difficulty maintaining good oral hygiene habits. We understand that some children might find it difficult to sit still for a dental examination.

What that means for the teeth

In some cases, special needs children have a higher risk of developing tooth decay, gum disease, tartar buildup, and oral trauma.

Our part

Our team is here for you and your child. With years of experience, we will personalize your child’s care and treatment to meet their needs. Our gentle, compassionate manner of care will ensure your child’s dental health is in good hands with our team.

How We Adapt

Six things we change, and will change again.

None of this is unusual for us. Dr. Nguyen’s residency covered patients with special needs alongside children as young as six months.

Longer appointments

Booked long enough that nothing has to be rushed, and short enough that it can end before your child is finished.

Desensitization visits

A visit whose only purpose is to see the room, meet the person, and leave. Sometimes several of them before anything is done.

Low-light rooms

And a quieter part of the day, when the waiting room is not full.

Routine-based care

The same room, the same people, and the same order of events every time, so nothing has to be worked out again.

Parents in the room

Always welcome, at any age, for as long as it helps.

Sedation, if it helps

Carefully monitored options including nitrous oxide, oral sedation and general anesthesia, when the alternative is the treatment not happening.

Why Here

The specialist training is the whole point.

A pediatric residency is three extra years, and behaviour, development and special needs care are most of what those years are about.

What Dr. Nguyen brings to it.

  • Board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.
  • A residency covering children as young as six months as well as patients with special needs.
  • Knee-to-knee exams for children who cannot use the chair.
  • A team that has done this before and does not need to be briefed twice.

Questions

What families ask us first.

From the practice’s own pediatric dentistry FAQ.

Do you treat children with special needs?

Yes. We specialize in sensory-friendly care, using routine-based visits, gentle communication, quiet rooms, and tools like headphones or weighted blankets.

What do you do differently?

We offer longer appointments, desensitization visits, low-light rooms, and flexible approaches customized to each child’s comfort and abilities.

Can my child be sedated if they need to be?

Yes. We provide carefully monitored sedation options-including nitrous oxide, oral sedation, and IV sedation-to ensure a calm and safe experience.

Can I stay with my child?

Yes! Parents are always welcome to accompany their child if it helps them feel more comfortable.
