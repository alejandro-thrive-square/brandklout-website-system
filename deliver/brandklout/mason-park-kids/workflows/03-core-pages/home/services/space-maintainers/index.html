The Appliance

What it does, and what happens without one.

Space maintainers are needed if your child loses a tooth early or have a baby tooth extracted due to dental decay. A space maintainer is designed to hold the remaining teeth in a position that will allow the future permanent tooth to erupt in the proper location. If a space maintainer is not places, the teeth will shift into the open areas, making it difficult for the permanent tooth to erupt.

When

Three moments it comes up.

1

After an early extraction

When decay has taken a baby tooth years before the permanent one underneath is ready to arrive.

2

After an accident

A knock that takes a tooth out leaves the same gap, and the same problem with the teeth on either side of it.

3

At the age-seven check

Where we can see that a tooth was lost early and the neighbours have already started to move.

Living With It

What your child will notice.

A space maintainer is small, fixed in most cases, and forgotten within a week. It is checked at the ordinary six-month visits.

What to expect.

  • A few days of being aware of it, then nothing.
  • Brushing as usual, with a little more care around the appliance itself.
  • Checked at each six-month visit as the permanent tooth moves up.
  • Removed when the permanent tooth is ready to come through.
