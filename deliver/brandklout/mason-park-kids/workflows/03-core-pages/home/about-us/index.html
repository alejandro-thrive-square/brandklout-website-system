The Building

The corner of Mason Road and Kingsland Boulevard.

Crafted with your whole family in mind. While little ones explore our dedicated play zone, older kids and adults can unwind in their own space. Every treatment room features ceiling-mounted TVs and modern technology to keep your visit completely comfortable and stress-free.

A dedicated side of the office for children and their needs.

Mason Park Dental for Kids is led by a board certified pediatric dentist, Dr. Tam Nguyen. Our sister practice Mason Park Dental of Katy is a general, cosmetic, and restorative practice for adults. Both dedicated to treating you, your six month old, and their grandmother, the same afternoon.

Pay It Forward

As pediatric dentists in Katy, we wholeheartedly believe in giving back to the community that supports us. That’s why we’re proud to offer our Pay It Forward program.

Parks Youth Ranch

A portion of profits from all new patient exams goes to Parks Youth Ranch, a nonprofit focused on providing a stable temporary home for displaced children ages 7 to 17.

As a new patient, you can take part in helping kids who have been abused, abandoned, or neglected. Your donated dollars help provide a safe and supportive environment while a child waits for their new home.
