The Building

The corner of Mason Road and Kingsland Boulevard.

Behind the Ranch 99 complex, suite 104B on the ground floor. Free parking at the door, a waiting room with a screen, and treatment rooms painted for the people who use them.

Children on one side of the hall, their parents on the other.

Two practices share this address. Mason Park Dental of Katy is a general, cosmetic and restorative practice for adults. Mason Park Dental for Kids is led by a board certified pediatric dentist. Your six month old and their grandmother, the same afternoon, the same front desk.

Two Practices

Which door is yours.

Parental involvement in your child’s dental care is highly encouraged. We want to work with you and your family to create beautiful, healthy smiles, which is easier when the whole family is seen in one place.

Pay It Forward

Pediatric dentist in Katy wholeheartedly believe in giving back to the community that serves us so well. That’s why we’re proud to offer our Pay It Forward program.

Parks Youth Ranch

A portion of profits from all new patient exams goes to Parks Youth Ranch, a nonprofit focused on providing a stable temporary home for displaced children ages 7 to 17.

As a new patient, you can take part in helping kids who have been abused, abandoned, or neglected. Your donated dollars help provide a safe and supportive environment while a child waits for their new home.
