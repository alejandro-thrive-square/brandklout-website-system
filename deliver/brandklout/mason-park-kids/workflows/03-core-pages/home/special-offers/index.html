The Detail

Three things worth knowing before you book.

The Invisalign consult is free

A complimentary Invisalign consultation is available. Insurance payments would be reimbursed back to the patient, and in-office and third-party payment plans are available.

The second opinion really is free

Are you confused about a treatment plan, or skeptical about a doctor’s opinion? At Mason Park Dental for Kids, educating our patients is a top priority. See a doctor you can trust.

Insurance, or the special, not both

The new patient price is an alternative to running it through your plan. Ask the front desk which comes out cheaper for you.

If you have no plan

The dental savings plan is the other way in, and it covers the visits rather than discounting one of them.
