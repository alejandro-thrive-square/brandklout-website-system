What Happens Next

Three things, in order.

A request is not yet an appointment. The front desk confirms the time with you first, so the slot is real before you plan around it.

1

We call you back.

Anabel, Gia or Kebra will call during office hours to agree a time, confirm which insurance you carry, and answer whatever you wrote in the request.

2

The forms arrive.

Our patient forms can be filled out online, or downloaded and filled out at home. If you cannot, arrive 20 minutes early and the front desk will help.

3

The visit.

About forty minutes for a first visit. Infants and anxious toddlers are examined knee to knee on your lap; you stay in the room throughout.
