By Age

By how old they are.

Ages are a guide, not a rule. The same team sees a child from the first tooth to the last day of school, and the care changes as they do.

6months

The first tooth

A first visit on your lap, within six months of the first tooth or by the first birthday.

Knee-to-knee examHygiene education
3to 5

The habit years

Every six months. Cleanings, fluoride, and sealants when the first molars arrive.

CleaningsFluorideSealants
6to 9

Losing teeth, growing teeth

Fillings if a cavity gets through, space maintainers if a tooth goes early, and an early look at the bite.

FillingsSpace maintainersEarly intervention

A mouth guard for the season, orthodontics when the timing is right, and cleanings that keep up.
