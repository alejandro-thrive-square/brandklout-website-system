Before You Come

Three things worth doing first.

1

Fill the forms in at home

Our patient forms can be filled out online, or downloaded and filled out at home. If you cannot, arrive twenty minutes early and the front desk will help.

2

Bring the insurance card

And the details of anyone else on the policy. We accept most PPO plans and Medicaid for qualified patients.

3

Whatever you thought of at 2am. There is time in the appointment for them, and they get forgotten otherwise.

If It Is Urgent

Call. Do not book online.

The online form goes to a queue. A phone call goes to a person, and today’s emergency slots are not on the booking page.

What counts as today.

  • A knocked-out tooth, which is the one where minutes matter.
  • A chipped or cracked tooth, especially with bleeding or a sharp edge.
  • Swelling in the face or gums, or a fever with a toothache.
  • Any pain that stopped your child sleeping.
