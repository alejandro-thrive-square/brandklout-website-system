Dental savings plan

No insurance? No problem.

At Mason Park Dental, we understand that not everyone in Katy has access to dental insurance. We know that should not prevent you from receiving quality dental care at an affordable price. That’s why we’ve started an annual membership plan for our patients.

Dental Savings Plan

Annual membershipFor families without insurance

The Plan

One yearly fee. The basics covered.

If you’re without insurance but still want to improve or maintain your family’s oral health, this plan will cover all your basic needs.

We want everyone in Katy to feel welcome at our practice, regardless of their current financial or medical situation. Give your family something to smile about.

Inside

What the plan includes.

The plan includes two exams and two cleanings per year from our team here at Mason Park Dental. It also provides discounts on all of our standard procedures, including dental composite fillings, sealants, and veneers.

Who It Is For

Families this plan was started for.

The plan exists for one reason: a lack of insurance should not decide whether a child gets two cleanings a year.

  • A family between jobs, or between plansThe two visits a year happen anyway.
  • Self-employed parentsOne yearly fee instead of a premium, and no pre-authorization paperwork.
  • A child with a condition insurance would excludeWe don’t disqualify anyone due to pre-existing conditions.
  • A family whose plan does not cover children’s dentistryThe plan covers the basics for the child; keep the adult plan for the adults.
  • Anyone who would rather know the year’s cost up frontTwo exams, two cleanings, and a discount on anything else, all agreed on day one.

Join

Sign up today.

The plan is signed up for at the front desk, in a few minutes, and can be used from the same visit.

Step one

Call, or ask at the desk.

Call (281) 578-7100 or ask Anabel, Gia or Kebra at your next visit. They will confirm the current annual fee and what it covers.

Step two

Sign up on the day.

One form per family, no pre-authorization and no medical questionnaire that could disqualify a child.

Step three

Book the first cleaning.

The two exams and two cleanings start with the next appointment, and the discounts apply to anything the exam finds.

For more detailed information the practice publishes a brochure; ask the front desk for the current copy, which also states the annual fee.

The Invitation

Give your family something to smile about.

Call (281) 578-7100 and ask for the dental savings plan. The front desk will tell you the annual fee and sign you up on the spot, or at your child’s next visit.
