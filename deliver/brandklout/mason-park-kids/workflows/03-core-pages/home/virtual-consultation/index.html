The Path

Three steps, and a call back.

1

Get in touch.

Call (281) 578 to 7100 or email office@masonparkdental.com and say you would like a virtual consultation.

2

Send the images.

Photographs of your child’s teeth, and anything else that will help: a treatment plan from another practice, or a radiograph if you have one.

3

We call you back.

To arrange the online appointment, or to tell you straight away that this is one to bring in.

It is an opinion, not a diagnosis.

Anything that needs looking at properly still needs looking at properly. What this saves is the trip that turns out not to have been necessary.

What It Is Good For

Three questions that answer well over a photograph.

A second opinion

On a treatment plan your child has been given somewhere else. The practice also offers a free second opinion in person.

Is this urgent?

A chip, a mark on a tooth, or a gum that looks wrong, where the real question is whether it can wait until the next visit.

Is my child a candidate?

For Invisalign in particular, where the honest answer often needs the mouth in front of us, but the first pass can be done from photographs.

One exception

If your child is in pain, or a tooth has been knocked out, do not use this. Call the office.
