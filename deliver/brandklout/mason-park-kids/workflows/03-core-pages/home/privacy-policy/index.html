The site

This site is static. It has no accounts and no tracking.

What follows is what this website does, plainly. It is not the practice’s clinical privacy notice, which is a separate document you are given at the practice.

The Website

What this website collects.

The pages themselves

This site is static: it has no accounts, no database and no tracking or advertising scripts. Ordinary web-server logs record the request, page, time, browser and IP address, and are kept only for security and troubleshooting.

Forms and booking

Appointment requests and virtual-consultation submissions are handled by third-party services embedded in the pages that use them. What you type there goes to that service and then to the practice, under that service’s own privacy terms. Do not use a web form to send anything urgent, call (281) 578-7100.

Fonts and maps

Typefaces are self-hosted and served from this site, so loading a page makes no request to a font provider. The contact page and several others embed a Google Map, which is loaded only when you scroll to it; opening that map is a request to Google and is covered by Google’s own privacy policy.

You

Messages, children, and your information.

Text messages

If you give us a mobile number and agree to appointment reminders, we use it for that. Reply STOP to any message to end them. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to reminders is never a condition of treatment.

Children

This site is written for parents and carers. We do not knowingly collect information directly from a child through this website.

Asking us about your information

To ask what the practice holds about you or your child, or to have it corrected, contact us at office@masonparkdental.com or (281) 578-7100. Health records are handled under the practice’s HIPAA notice, not under this page.

One thing to note

This notice will be replaced by the practice’s reviewed policy. Until then it is what governs this website.
