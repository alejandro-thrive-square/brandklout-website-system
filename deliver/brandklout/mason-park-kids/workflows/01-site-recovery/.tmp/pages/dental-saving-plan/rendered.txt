Dental Savings Plan
For Patients Without Insurance Coverage

At Mason Park Dental, we understand that not everyone in Katy has access to dental insurance. We know that should not prevent you from receiving quality dental care at an affordable price! That’s why we’ve started an annual membership plan for our patients. If you’re without insurance but still want to improve or maintain your family’s oral health, this plan will cover all your basic needs.

The plan includes two exams and two cleanings per year from our team here at Mason Park Dental. It also provides discounts on all of our standard procedures, including dental composite fillings, sealants, and veneers. Unlike many insurance providers, we don’t have any pre-authorization requirements and don’t disqualify anyone due to pre-existing conditions. We want everyone in Katy to feel welcome at our practice, regardless of their current financial or medical situation. Give your family something to smile about. Sign up today!
