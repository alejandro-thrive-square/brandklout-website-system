Invisalign® FAQs – Mason Park Dental For Kids, Katy, TX

Straightening your child’s smile is a big decision, and parents often have many questions about Invisalign® treatment. At Mason Park Dental For Kids, we want you to feel confident, informed, and fully supported as your child begins their dental journey. Below, you’ll find answers to the most commonly asked questions about Invisalign® for kids and teens.

General Invisalign® Questions
1. What is Invisalign®?

Invisalign® is a modern dental system that uses clear, removable aligners to gently shift teeth into their ideal position. It offers a discreet alternative to traditional metal braces.

Comfort & Care
1. Is Invisalign® painful for kids?

Your child may feel mild pressure when switching to a new set of aligners, but this discomfort is typically minimal and far less than with traditional braces.

Eating & Oral Hygiene
1. Are there food restrictions with Invisalign®?

No! Because aligners are removable, your child can continue eating their favorite foods without restrictions.

Candidacy & Suitability
1. Is my child a good candidate for Invisalign®?

Kids and teens with mild to moderate misalignment, crowding, gaps, overbites, or underbites are often great candidates for Invisalign®.
