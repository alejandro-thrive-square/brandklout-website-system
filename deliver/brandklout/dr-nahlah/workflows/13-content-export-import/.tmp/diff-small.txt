=== veneers
  LOCAL  Veneers hide what you do not want seen: chips, cracks, gaps, crowding, deep staining. Thin porcelain, hand-designed to your lip line, your gum line and your own colouring, and prepared minimally, so what is already yours stays yours.
  LOCAL  Book a smile evaluation See veneer cases
  LOCAL  < 1 mmMinimally invasive prep
  LOCAL  A minimally invasive prep, and a lab in the building.
  LOCAL  Minimally invasive preparation
  LOCAL  Less than a millimetre of enamel is removed from the front surface, so the veneer sits flush and feels like a tooth rather than a layer stuck on one. Enamel does not grow back, which is the whole reason the prep is kept as conservative as the case allows.
  LOCAL  The prepared teeth are scanned, and each veneer is hand-designed in the practice’s own digital laboratory, one in McLean and one in Alexandria, to the ideal size, shape and colour for your face.
