  LOCAL  FP1, FP2, FP3, and why we plan for the first.
  LOCAL  Every full-arch implant prosthesis is one of three types. What separates them is how much of it is teeth, and how much is pink acrylic standing in for gum and bone. Most practices fit one kind. Here the arch is planned as thin as the case will carry.
  LOCAL  Replaces teeth only
  LOCAL  Natural crown-and-bridge look
  LOCAL  No added pink, no extra bulk
  LOCAL  Typically on more natural gum architecture
  LOCAL  Longer teeth, minimal pink
  LOCAL  Longer teeth, minimal pink acrylic
  LOCAL  The pink that is added still follows the natural gum contour
  LOCAL  Rarely indicated, and often not ideal
  LOCAL  Replaces teeth, gums and bone
  LOCAL  Thick pink acrylic stands in for gum and bone
  LOCAL  Thicker, stronger prosthesis
  LOCAL  Typically best where there is bone loss, and a lower hygiene risk
  LOCAL  The same arch, three ways. The pink is the difference.
