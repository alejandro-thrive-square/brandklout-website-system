## Dental Implants: The Gold Standard for Missing Teeth


Unlike traditional dentures, dental implants are permanently anchored to the jawbone, meaning no slipping over time and no painful friction as a result of rubbing on the gums while eating and speaking. Implants consist of three main parts: a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jaw, an abutment which acts as an anchor for the replacement tooth, and finally, the customized replacement tooth (prosthetic) itself. At Dental Group of Tysons in McLean, VA, we offer free second opinions on dental implant surgery, as well as five-year warranties on all of our implant procedures (warranties mandate pre-care general cleanings every six months).

## Personalized Attention, Customized Results


Depending on your unique situation, several different dental implant options can help you achieve a perfect smile and a set of stunning, brilliantly white new teeth. Single dental implants are often the best option for a single tooth that has been damaged due to physical impact. Implant supported dentures, full mouth dental implants and implant-supported bridges are practical options that can replace several (or all) of your missing teeth. Full-mouth dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth, while implant-supported bridges and dentures offer an intermediate option in terms of both price and functionality. Depending on your personal situation and the current state of your oral health, Dr. Nahlah will determine the perfect implant option for you in terms of helping to craft a stunning smile and giving you back the quality of life you deserve.

## How the Dental Implant Process Works


The first step of any dental implant process is to schedule an appointment with Dr. Nahlah for an initial consultation. The surgical process itself is carried out in a single visit, followed by an approximately four-month healing process (during which the implant posts will permanently fuse with the jawbone). After your surgery, you will come in for two separate appointments: a routine checkup followed by a final visit. In this final visit, Dr. Nahlah will place your permanent set of new teeth! Dr. Nahlah uses state-of-the-art zirconia fixed bridges for full mouth implant surgery, which represents the pinnacle of oral health restoration. Our team also offers a variety of sedation options, ensuring your dental implant surgery will be as smooth and pain-free as possible. Find out today if you are a candidate for world-class dental implants.


## Fixed Implants vs. Traditional Dentures


Fixed dental implants are quickly becoming the gold standard for missing teeth replacement. While dentures are prone to slipping and movement over time, implants are permanently fused with the jawbone, meaning little to no possibility of movement, even after years of use. Implants also support the jaw and facial bones, and due to the fusing of the titanium implant post, actually limit jawbone recession! Also, although the initial cost of dental implants is higher than that of dentures, implants typically require less maintenance and repair, meaning lower cost over the long-term. No matter your personal situation, an investment into dental implants is a long-term investment into your health, happiness and overall quality of life.
