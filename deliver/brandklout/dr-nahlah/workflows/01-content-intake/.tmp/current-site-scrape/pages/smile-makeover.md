## Flawed Smile? We'll Make it Flawless.


Aside from those lucky few people that boast perfect oral genetics, achieving a brilliant, world-class smile is something that takes work and precision artistry under the guidance of an elite cosmetic dentist. At Dental Group of Tysons in McLean, VA, this is exactly what Dr. Esam Nahlah and his elite team of professionals brings to the table. Our team offers complete Smile Makeover treatments that can transform even the worst set of teeth into something that is truly stunning. We also offer a variety of dental payment options, ensuring that each and every patient has practical and affordable access to their perfect smile.


## Simple, Easy Steps to a Stunning Smile


The life-changing effects of a cosmetic smile makeover cannot be understated. If you’re one of the millions of people who suffer from a lack of confidence due to broken, crooked, misshapen and/or severely stained teeth, Dental Group of Tysons offers a variety of options that can transform your smile from something you’re ashamed of into something you’re proud of. No matter what the current state of your oral health is – even if you’re missing some or all of your teeth – we offer options that can give you the perfect, stunning smile you’ve always thought was out of your reach.

The first step of any Smile Makeover transformation is to visit Dr. Nahlah for an initial consultation at our practice in McLean, VA. During this consultation, Dr. Nahlah will conduct a full oral examination – including any necessary imaging scans – in order to determine which process(es) may be necessary to craft your unique and perfect smile. You’ll also discuss your personal oral health and cosmetic goals, which will allow Dr. Nahlah to create a unique, fully customized treatment plan that will optimize your unique features, including your unique facial esthetics, lip and gum line symmetry, and more.


## A Variety of Options for a Complete Smile Makeover



### Dental Crowns


Crowns are custom-fabricated caps that slip over and adhere to your natural tooth. Crowns can be fitted for one tooth or several teeth, and can restore severely damaged and/or severely stained teeth back to a state of brilliance and cosmetic perfection.


### Dental Bridges


Bridges involve placing a series of crowns onto several adjacent teeth. If you have teeth that have been damaged or that are in advanced stages of decay - or even if you're missing several teeth - Dr. Nahlah can place a state-of-the-art zirconia fixed bridge that looks, feels and performs just like your natural teeth.


### Veneers (Porcelain and Composite)


For a cosmetic approach, porcelain veneers offer one of the best options available for achieving brilliantly white teeth and a perfect, esthetically pleasing smile. Veneers involve placement of very thin layers of custom-fabricated material over your tooth enamel, and can repair and correct tooth cracks, chips, gaps and severe discoloration.


### Professional Whitening


Few things can bring out the brilliance of your smile more than advanced professional whitening solutions. At Dental Group of Tysons, we offer state-of-the-art whitening treatments that can grant you the perfect smile you’ve always wished for in just a single visit to our McLean, VA office.


### Dental Implants


In instances of severe oral damage and decay – or in cases where a patient is missing some or all of their teeth – dental implants offer an advanced solution to full mouth dental restoration. No matter your current state of oral health, our dental implants can give our patients a truly life-changing Smile Makeover
