## Should I Upgrade My Smile From Traditional Dentures To All-On-4® Dental Implants In McLean, VA?


December 2, 2023


#### Share This Post


Traditional dentures are commonly used when people have lost all or most of their teeth due to dental disease, injury, or age. A traditional denture has a flesh-colored acrylic base, which fits over the patient’s gums, and teeth, which are usually made of acrylic or porcelain material. When people no longer want, or no longer can, wear traditional dentures, then they should upgrade their smile to All-On-4® dental implants in McLean, VA.

Interested in learning more about why people should upgrade their smile from traditional dentures to all-On-4 dental implants? Continue reading to learn more about why people should upgrade their smile to All-On-4 dental implants.


## The Reasons Why You Should Upgrade Your Smile From Traditional Dentures To All-On-4 Dental Implants In McLean, VA


All-On-4 dental implants offer a host of advantages over traditional dentures. Here are the specific reasons why people should upgrade their smile from traditional dentures to All-On-4 dental implants.


### Permanent Full Arch Tooth Replacement Option:


All-On-4 dental implants should replace traditional dentures because they are a permanent full arch tooth replacement option. Traditional dentures are made to just sit on the patients gums and do not become a permanent part of their smile. The prosthesis for All-On-4 implants, on the other hand, are fixed to the patients mouth with four dental implant posts.

When placed in the patient’s jawbone, the dental implant posts fuse with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. After the dental implant posts have fully fused with the jawbone, they become a permanent part of the patients smile. Once the prosthesis is attached to the dental implants, patients will have a beautiful and long-lasting new smile.


### Give Patients A Beautiful New Smile:


In terms of aesthetic beauty, All-On-4 dental implants give patients a better-looking new smile than traditional dentures. This is because the color, shape, and size of the full arch prosthesis is custom made to look natural looking and aesthetically enhance the patient’s smile. Traditional dentures, on the other hand, are bulky and will not give patients a natural and better-looking new smile.


### Preserves Jawbone Density:


Unlike traditional dentures, All-On-4 dental implants preserve the patients jawbone density. This is because the dental implant posts, in an All-On-4 dental implant procedure, stimulate the patients jawbone once they have been permanently placed in it. This stimulation regenerates jawbone density, giving patients a strong and healthy new smile that they can depend on to live their life normally.


### Are Cost-effective:


All-On-4 dental implants are a more cost-effective full arch tooth replacement option than traditional dentures. Although the upfront cost of traditional dentures might be lower than All-On-4 dental implants, the maintenance and replacement costs that come with traditional dentures can add up over time.

On the other hand, with proper care and regular dental check-ups, All-On-4 implants can last a lifetime, making them a worthwhile full arch tooth replacement option.


### Gives A New Smile In One Day:


For All-On-4 dental implant procedures, when the dental implant posts have been securely placed in the patient’s jawbone, the temporary prosthesis can be attached to them in the same surgical appointment. The temporary prosthesis is what allows patients to get a brand-new smile in one day. This is a stark contrast to traditional dentures, which may require several fittings and adjustments over a span of weeks or months.

For individuals who have experienced significant tooth loss, the decision to upgrade from traditional dentures to All-On-4 dental implants can be life-changing. Not only do All-On-4 dental implants offer superior comfort, functionality, and aesthetics to traditional dentures, they also contribute to better oral health and overall quality of life for the patient.
