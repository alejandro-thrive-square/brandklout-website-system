## Sedation Options at Dental Group of Tysons



#### Oral and IV Sedation


Depending on the extent of your treatment and/or your personal health, we use a medical anesthesiologist to administer either oral or IV sedation options at Dental Group of Tysons. IV sedation is typically the strongest type of sedation available, and is reserved for patients who suffer from severe dental anxiety. Both oral and IV sedation typically require arrangement of transportation to and from your dental appointment.


#### Nitrous Oxide Sedation


Commonly referred to as “laughing gas,” nitrous oxide is a common dental sedation option that is typically milder than IV and oral sedation options. Some patients do, however, fall asleep in the chair (which may be exactly what you want!). Not only are the effects of nitrous sedation faster-acting than oral and IV options, but nitrous oxide typically requires shorter recovery times, as well, allowing patients to drive themselves home after their procedure in most cases.


## Don't Let Your Dental Anxiety Stand in the Way


No matter how much you may suffer from dental anxiety, don’t let your fears get in the way of receiving the oral care and treatment you need. Adequate oral health can even have major repercussions on the health and well-being of your entire body. In fact, gum disease and other types of oral disease can increase your risk of a number of physical health complications, such as diabetes, stroke and cardiovascular disease. If you prolong dental treatment due to fear or anxiety, you may be putting your physiological health at risk in ways that you’re not even aware of.

At Dental Group of Tysons in McLean, VA, we are here to ensure that your treatment is as comfortable and pain-free as possible. We also offer affordable financing and payment options, as we believe no one should go without receiving the dental care and treatments they need in order to live a happy, healthy life.
