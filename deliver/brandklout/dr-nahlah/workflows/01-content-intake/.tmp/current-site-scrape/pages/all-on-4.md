## A Stunning Smile Transformation


For patients who are missing some or all of their teeth, All-on-4 dental implants offer a permanent, lasting solution that perfectly replicate the look and feel of your natural teeth. While traditional (single) implants attach one tooth to one implant, All-on-4 implants fully reconstruct a patient’s upper and lower arch, using just four titanium implant posts.

All-on-4 dental implants are far superior to traditional removable dentures for a variety of reasons. They are less prone to slippage and movement over time, they produce less friction on your gums (meaning decreased risk of pain, inflammation and infection), and they are far more comfortable in terms of routine day-to-day maintenance and functionality. If you’re missing some or all of your teeth, All-on-4 dental implants are the best available way to a brand-new and perfect set of teeth.

## How All-on-4 Dental Implants Work


All-on-4 implants work in a similar manner to single dental implants, but rather than a single tooth, they replace a full (upper and/or lower) arch of teeth! With over 20 years of experience in implant surgery, Dr. Esam Nahlah possesses the intricate skill and technique necessary to engineer a perfect, natural-looking arch of teeth for any patient who is in need of All-on-4 implants.

Implant surgery involves permanent fixation of titanium posts into the jawbone, and this requires adequate dental health to ensure the most effective results. ### Step 1: Come in for a Personalized Consultation


The first step of All-on-4 dental implant full mouth restoration is to schedule an appointment to meet with Dr. Nahlah. During your initial consultation, he will conduct a full mouth oral examination (including any necessary imaging scans) in order to determine the state of your current oral health.

In order for the fixed permanent implants to adequately fuse with the jawbone, a patient needs to have minimal bone loss. During your initial consultation, Dr. Nahlah will perform an extensive 3D scan of your mouth using a Cone-beam CT scanner. This will allow Dr. Nahlah and his team to adequately determine if All-on-4 implants are a practical option for you.

Together we’ll discuss the best option for you and if you’d like, you can see before and after case studies from real patients including how their treatment truly changed their lives. Dental implant pricing, insurance coverage, and financing options will also be discussed. If dental implants are best for you, our McClean, VA implant dentist completes precise surgical planning to ensure each implant placed is in the exact angle, depth, and location for optimal, lasting success, and ideal aesthetics.


### Step 2: Surgical Placement of Your All-on-4 Implants


The second step of All-on-4 implants is Transformation Day, when the four titanium posts into the jawbone. In most instances, surgery is a single visit process wherein you will leave the office with your polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA) provisional prosthesis. In order to make your surgery experience as seamless and pain-free as possible, Dr. Nahlah offers a full range of sedation options, including nitrous, IV and oral.

Dr. Nahlah also uses advanced, state-of-the-art surgical computer guide benefits in order to deliver All-on-4 implants with an unparalleled level of precision and accuracy. This results in a look, feel and functionality that is nearly identical to that of your natural teeth.


### Step 3: Come in for Your Follow-up Appointments


The fourth and final step of your All-on-4 implant surgery is placement of the final zirconia prosthesis (your new permanent artificial teeth). From the time of initial surgery (when the titanium posts are inserted into the jaw), roughly three to four months of healing are required until the final prosthesis can be placed. After you receive your new teeth, an additional two- to four-month period is usually required in order to achieve complete healing.

Dental Group of Tysons offers warranty on All-on-4 with Precare, which mandates general cleanings every six months. We also offer free second opinions, as well as immediate load solutions, which means you will receive a fully functional set of PMMA teeth as your temporary prosthesis on the same day of your implant surgery.
