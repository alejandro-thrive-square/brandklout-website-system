## Diving into Dental Implant Placements


A dental implant is a small titanium screw that serves as the replacement for the root portion of a missing natural tooth. Its structure is made to mimic that of a natural tooth, consisting of the implant itself (which replaces the root), the abutment, and the crown.

Dental implants don’t only look and function like natural teeth, they’re also designed to be long-lasting. They preserve oral health by protecting surrounding healthy teeth from any shifting or damage.

The longevity of a dental implant can be influenced by several factors including oral hygiene, overall health, and lifestyle habits such as smoking.

However, with proper care, hygiene, and regular dental visits, dental implants can potentially last a lifetime. It’s not uncommon to see dental implants still functioning well after 25 years.
