## Considering Getting Treated With All-On-4® Dental Implants In McLean, VA? This Is Why You Should


November 4, 2023


#### Share This Post


All-On-4® dental implants in McLean, VA are a proven and reliable full arch tooth replacement option that uses four dental implant posts to support a prosthesis. When people go to a skilled and trusted dental professional’s office to get treated with All-On-4® dental implants, they will get the beautiful, functional, and healthy new smile they need and deserve with the latest techniques and technologies.

There are a number of specific reasons why people should get treated with All-On-4 dental implants. Here is more detailed information on why people should get treated with this transformative full arch tooth replacement option.


## The Reasons Why You Should Get Treated With All-On-4 Dental Implants In McLean, VA


When people have a full arch, or arches, of missing teeth, they should get them replaced with state-of-the-art All-On-4 dental implants. People should replace their full arch of missing teeth with All-On-4 dental implants because only four dental implants are needed to support a full arch prosthesis, they are more reliable than traditional dentures, and they are made to be long-lasting.

Continue reading to get more specific explanations about why people should get treated with All-On-4 dental implants.


### Four Dental Implants Support The Prosthesis:


The All-On-4 dental implant procedure is precisely designed to maximize the healthy and dense areas of the patient’s jawbone that can successfully support their new smile. During an All-On-4 dental implant procedure process, four dental implants are strategically angled and placed in the patients jawbone so they can securely support the full arch prosthesis.

Because the dental implants are placed in areas of the patients jawbone where there is enough jawbone density to support them, the patient won’t first have to get treated with bone grafting. When the four dental implants are securely holding the prosthesis in place, the patient will be able to bite, chew, and speak normally and comfortably for years to come.


### More Reliable Than Traditional Dentures:


All-On-4 dental implants are more reliable than traditional dentures for several reasons. First of all, their design: All-On-4 dental implants are securely anchored to the patient’s jawbone, giving them the dental stability and comfort that they need to live their life normally. This stands in contrast to traditional dentures, which will often move around or fall out of the patient’s mouth, causing them dental discomfort and instability.

All-On-4 dental implants are also made to function like natural teeth, giving the patient better biting and chewing capabilities. Traditional dentures, on the other hand, can limit the types of food people can eat, impacting their diet and overall quality of life.

Finally, All-On-4 dental implants promote jawbone health because the dental implant posts stimulate the patients jawbone like natural tooth roots do. This jawbone stimulation gives patients a strong and healthy platform for their new smile to be placed on. Unlike with traditional dentures, All-On-4 dental implants won’t cause patients to have jawbone resorption.


### Made To Be Long-Lasting:


All-On-4 dental implants are designed to be a durable and long-lasting full arch tooth replacement option because the dental implant posts are made to be like natural tooth roots. When the biocompatible dental implant posts are securely placed in the patient’s jawbone, they fuse with it in a natural process called osseointegration.

Once the dental implant post has completely fused with the patient’s jawbone, it becomes a permanent part of their smile. After the personalized prosthesis is attached to the dental implants, and with proper care and maintenance, patients will get a long-lasting new smile with their All-On-4 dental implant procedure.
