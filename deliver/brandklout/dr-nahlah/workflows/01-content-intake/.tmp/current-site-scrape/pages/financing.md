## Insurance and Financing Options for Any Treatment


No matter what type of treatment or procedure you need, Dental Group of Tysons makes it a priority to offer practical, affordable payment options for patients in any financial situation. Cost should never be a deterrent to receiving quality dental care, and this is something Dr. Esam Nahlah and his team in McLean, VA wholeheartedly believe in. Oral health has a direct correlation to physical health, which makes it even more important to stay on top of your dental health and make sure you get the care you need. No matter your budget limitations or current financial situation, give us a call today and speak with one of our team members about finding an insurance or payment plan that works for you.

## Learn About Our Payment and Financing Options



### Cash, Check, or Credit Card


Dental Group of Tysons accepts payment via cash, check and credit card (most major credit cards are accepted).


### Dental Insurance


We gladly accept most Dental insurance PPO Plans.


### CareCredit®


Patients eligible for CareCredit financing can manage treatment costs with flexible and affordable monthly payment options. Visit www.carecredit.com or call 1-800-677-0718 for more information.


### Proceed Finance


Patients with low credit scores can receive affordable, no-collateral loans up to $60,000 with flexible terms up to 96 months at fair, fixed rates. Visit www.proceedfinance.com or call 844-272-7587 for more information.


### LendingClub


Recognized by Inc. magazine, the New York Times and over 3 million members, Lending Club has refined their process to make it easy on you to pay for dental as well as medical treatments. Apply from your phone within minutes, select the rate, term and payment options that fits your budget, and send cash directly to your account for a lower APR. Visit www.lendingclub.com or call 888-596-3157 for more information.
