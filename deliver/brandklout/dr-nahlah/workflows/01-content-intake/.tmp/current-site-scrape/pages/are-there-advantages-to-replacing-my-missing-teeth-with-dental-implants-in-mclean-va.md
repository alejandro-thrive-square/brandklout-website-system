## Are There Advantages To Replacing My Missing Teeth With Dental Implants In McLean, VA?


September 5, 2023


#### Share This Post


Dental implants in McLean, VA are a popular and effective long-term tooth replacement option for people who have one or more missing teeth. Depending on the number of teeth they are missing, and the number of dental implants they need to restore their smile, patients can get treated with single dental implants, implant supported dentures, and full mouth dental implants.

Interested in learning more about the advantages of replacing missing teeth with dental implants? Continue reading to to find out more.


## The Advantages To Replacing Missing Teeth With Dental Implants In McLean, VA


By getting treated with gold standard dental implants, the beauty, functionality, and health of people’s smiles are restored. Patients will even have a comfortable and anxiety-free dental implant procedure experience with the dental practice’s sedation dentistry options. The following are more detailed explanations of what the advantages of dental implants are:


### Limits Jawbone Recession


One of the greatest advantages of dental implants, is their ability to limit jawbone recession and support facial bone structures. When a tooth is missing, the tooth root is no longer stimulating the jawbone. This lack of jawbone stimulation can lead to jawbone density loss over time, altering the persons facial structure and leading to them to having a more aged looking appearance.

Dental implants are made to act like natural tooth roots, stimulating the patients jawbone so that it is kept healthy and strong. With dental implants, patients can have a healthy and beautiful looking new smile.


### Are Durable and Functional


Dental implants are made to be a durable and functional tooth replacement option. Specifically, dental implants can return over 99% of the durability and functionality of the patient’s natural teeth. Once the dental implants have been permanently placed in the patient’s jawbone, and the custom-made prosthesis is attached to them, patients will be able to eat, speak, and smile confidently for years to come.


### Look, Feel, and Function Like Natural Teeth


The color, shape, and size of the prosthesis is custom made for patients’ dental implant procedures. With dental implants, patients can have a beautiful and natural looking new and improved smile. The best part is, with accurately placed dental implants, patients will be able to eat and speak with ease. By getting treated with personalized dental implants, patients will get natural looking, feeling, and functioning new teeth.


### Easy To Maintain


Dental implants, much like natural teeth, require consistent and regular maintenance so that they can be healthy and successful for a long time. By regularly brushing and flossing their dental implants, as well as visiting a trusted dentist for teeth cleanings biannually, patients can keep their smile in great condition.

By getting treated with state-of-the-art-dental implants, patients will be able to maintain a normal diet, speak in comfort, and smile with ease and confidence. A skilled dental professional can predictably place a patient’s restorative dental implants with the latest techniques and technologies.
