## Elite Dental Care



#### All-on-4® Implants


All-on-4 dental implants offer the most advanced solution to full mouth rehabilitation and dental reconstruction. For patients who are missing some or all of their teeth, or for patients looking to transition from removable dentures, full mouth dental implants provide a permanent, fixed solution that fully restores a patient’s natural, perfect smile.


#### Sedation Dentistry


If you’re one of the millions of patients who suffer from dental anxiety, Dental Group of Tysons offers a complete range of sedation options to help make your treatment(s) as comfortable, convenient and pain-free as possible. Dr. Nahlah offers IV and oral sedation options, as well as nitrous oxide under the guidance of a medical anesthesiologist, to make your dental experience as smooth and hassle-free as possible.


#### Smile Makeover


Boasting over 20 years of experience in cosmetic dentistry applications, Dr. Nahlah employs a unique set of skills and dental artistry techniques in order to recreate each patient’s unique natural smile. If you’re looking for brilliant, stunning teeth and a truly perfect smile, get in touch with Dr. Nahlah today to schedule your own personalized Smile Makeover.
