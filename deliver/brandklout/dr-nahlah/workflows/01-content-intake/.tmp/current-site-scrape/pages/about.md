## Your New Dental Home is Right Here


Are you looking for a new dental home in the Washington, D.C. area? At Dental Group of Tysons, Dr. Esam Nahlah offers world-class dental care through a variety of cosmetic and surgical treatment options using advanced, state-of-the-art dental technology. As a board-certified prosthodontist, Dr. Nahlah boasts over 20 years of experience in full-mouth reconstruction, and has placed over 7,000 full mouth dental implants. Whether you’re looking for a complete cosmetic Smile Makeover or are in need of an entire full arch of new teeth with All-on-4® dental implants, Dr. Nahlah and his expert dental team offers the professional care and expert services you need.

Our practice in McLean, VA also offers same-day emergency appointments, as well as free second opinions, so you can be absolutely sure you’re getting the dental care and attention you need. Dental Group of Tysons is built on patient relationships, and we’re here to serve you on a personal level. Give us a call and connect with our dental family today. You’ll be glad you did!

## A Personalized Approach to Each and Every Patient


Dental Group of Tysons offers a truly unique set of dental services for patients in the McLean, VA and Washington, DC areas. First, we make it a priority to develop real, meaningful relationships with every patient we see. This allows not only for a more personalized approach to your dental care and needs, but also goes a long way toward easing some of the fears and anxieties associated with trips to the dentist office.

We also offer the most advanced state-of-the-art dental technologies, which allow us to revolutionize the patient experience and ensure that each patient’s experience is as seamless, convenient and pain-free as possible. Dr. Nahlah offers an in-house digital lab and in-house milling MMA machine, as well as an advanced cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) scanner and intraoral scanner for precise digital impressions. We also utilize an in-house zirconia/porcelain oven for prosthetic fabrication, and offer minimally invasive computer-guided surgeries for an unparalleled level of precision. We proudly guarantee our cosmetic dentistry work, and are happy to replace any failed part for free with our five-year warranty. Give us a call today and connect with the friendliest and most professional dental team in McLean, VA!
