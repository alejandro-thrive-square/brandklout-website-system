## How Exactly Do Implant Supported Dentures In McLean, VA Benefit My Smile?


October 5, 2023


#### Share This Post


Implant supported dentures in McLean, VA are a tooth replacement option that uses dental implants to support a denture. If people don’t replace their missing teeth with implant supported dentures, they run the risk of losing jawbone density, losing dental functionality, and losing the confidence to show their smile.

Luckily, implant supported dentures can benefit patients’ smiles in many ways. Continue reading to learn in more detail how implant supported dentures benefit patients’ smiles.


## How Implant Supported Dentures In McLean, VA Benefit My Smiles


Here are the specific ways that implant supported dentures can benefit patient’s smiles:


### Dentures Are Made from Acrylic or Porcelain Material:


The dentures for implant-supported procedures can be made from either acrylic or porcelain material. Both materials offer unique advantages. Acrylic, being lightweight and less expensive, can be more comfortable and affordable for patients. On the other hand, porcelain dentures are renowned for their durability and natural appearance. They are resistant to wear and stains, making them a long-lasting tooth replacement option.


### Two or More Dental Implants Support the Denture:


Two or more dental implants are used to support the denture in an implant-supported denture procedure. These dental implants are strategically placed into the patient’s jawbone, giving their denture a secure and stable foundation to be attached to.

The number of dental implants needed in an implant supported denture procedure varies from patient to patient. With the two or more dental implants, the denture stays in place in the patient’s mouth, preventing them from slipping or causing the patient discomfort. Securely placed implant supported dentures also help patients eat and speak properly, improving the functionality of their mouth.


### Restores Dental Stability:


Implant supported dentures effectively prevent the denture from slipping, loosening, or falling out of the patient’s mouth. Dental implants, which are made to function like natural tooth roots, serve as a sturdy foundation for dentures to be attached to.

When dental implants fuse with the patient’s jawbone over time, they become a permanent part of their smile. This restored dental stability means that the dentures are securely held in place, preventing them from slipping, loosening, or falling out of the patient’s jawbone. With implant supported dentures, patients get a stable and secure new smile.


### Improves Jawbone Health:


Implant supported dentures play a pivotal role in maintaining and improving jawbone health. Besides giving patients a permanent new smile, when the dental implant posts are placed in the patient’s jawbone, it stimulates it so its volume and density are restored.

With a restored jawbone, implant supported denture patients won’ t have to worry about jawbone resorption or having a sunken-in looking face. By replacing their missing teeth with implant supported dentures, patients not only enhance the look of their smile, they also improve their overall oral health by preserving their jawbone.
