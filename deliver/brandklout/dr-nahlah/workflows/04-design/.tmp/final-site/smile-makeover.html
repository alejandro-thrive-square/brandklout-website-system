The Approach

Prosthodontics is the specialty that does exactly this.

Most cosmetic dentistry is sold procedure by procedure, and the result looks like it. A prosthodontist is trained for three additional years in the opposite habit: designing the finished result first, then working backwards to what has to happen to produce it.

Meet the specialist

The Tools

Five ways to get there. Most plans use two or three.

01

Porcelain veneers

Thin layers of custom porcelain bonded over enamel, for chips, cracks, gaps and deep discoloration in the teeth that show.

Veneers in detail
02

Dental crowns

Custom caps bonded over a natural tooth, where a tooth is too damaged or too stained for a veneer to sit on.

03

Dental bridges

A run of crowns spanning adjacent teeth. Zirconia fixed bridges behave like the natural teeth beside them.

04

Professional whitening

Often finished in a single visit, and usually the last step, once the shade of everything else is settled.

05

Dental implants

Where teeth are missing rather than damaged, the design starts at the root. Implants carry the new teeth instead of the gum.

Implants in detail
+

Digital Smile Design

A free preview of the finished smile, from your own photographs and scans, before a single tooth is prepared.

The Sequence

How a makeover actually runs.

01

Smile evaluation

Candidacy, a free Digital Smile Design preview, pricing and options, and time with Dr. Nahlah directly.

02

Examination & imaging

A complete oral examination with any scans needed, to find what is achievable and what has to be treated first.

03

The design

Shape, shade, proportion, lip line and gum symmetry settled as one design. You approve it before preparation starts.

04

Build it

The treatments in the plan, in the order the design requires, with the in-house lab adjusting shade and fit in minutes.
