Your Patient

What the patient you send here actually walks into.

Seasoned dental professionals, minimally invasive laser technology, and a comfortable, welcoming atmosphere. The practice is set up around the assumption that a referred patient is arriving somewhere unfamiliar for something they are not looking forward to.

Practically, that means sedation is available where it is needed, the first appointment is a conversation and a scan rather than a procedure, and the case is explained in full before anything is agreed to.

Second opinions are free, which matters when you are sending a patient for confirmation rather than for treatment.

Capability

What can be handled without a second referral.

The whole chain sits in one building, so a complex case does not fragment across three practices and a laboratory in another state.

01

Diagnosis & planning

CBCT imaging and intraoral scanning on site, with the implant position planned on the scan before anything is placed.

02

Surgical

Extractions, bone grafting and computer-guided implant placement across six surgical suites, under sedation.

Oral surgery
03

Prosthetic

Design, milling and firing in the in-house digital laboratory, one in each practice, which is what removes weeks from a full-arch case and makes a remake days rather than a month. Full arches are planned as a thin FP1 wherever the bone allows.

The facility
04

Full-arch reconstruction

All-on-4 and fixed full-arch work, which is the case type most general practices refer out and the one this practice is built around.
