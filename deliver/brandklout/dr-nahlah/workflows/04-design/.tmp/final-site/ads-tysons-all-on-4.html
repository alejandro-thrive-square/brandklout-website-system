What Happens, And When

Your new teeth should not look or feel like dental work.

Four steps, and one of them is just healing.

1

Consultation and 3D scan

The cone-beam scan shows whether four posts will carry your case. You leave knowing whether you are a candidate, what it costs, and how long it takes. No cost.

2

Surgery and same-day teeth

Extractions where needed, four implants per arch through a guide, and a fixed PMMA provisional arch fitted the same visit. Under sedation, in one appointment.

3

Integration

Bone grows onto the posts over three to four months. You wear the provisional arch throughout, on a soft diet at first, and are seen along the way.

4

The definitive arch

The zirconia arch, designed and made in the laboratory in the building, fitted by the specialist who planned it. A further two to four months completes healing around it.

Before You Book

Everything patients ask before booking.

Is full-arch surgery painful?
It is done under local anesthetic with sedation, nitrous oxide or oral, whichever suits you, so you stay relaxed throughout. Swelling and soreness in the first days are normal and managed with what you are sent home with.
Do I really leave with teeth the same day?
Yes. A fixed PMMA provisional arch is fitted the same visit, so you do not leave without teeth. The definitive zirconia arch replaces it once the implants have integrated, usually three to four months later.
I have been told I have lost too much bone.
Bone loss is often what makes you a candidate rather than what rules you out. The two rear implants are angled forward into the denser bone at the front of the jaw, which is how four posts carry an arch without rebuilding the back of the mouth. Some cases still need grafting, and the scan decides.
How long does it last?
Implants have held a published success rate above 95% since they were introduced. General cleanings every six months are what keep a full arch healthy.
When will I find out the cost?
At the consultation, once the scan has shown which arch is being planned and whether extractions or grafting come first. No honest figure exists before then. You get it in writing before anything is booked, and second opinions on another practice's quote cost nothing.
Do you take my insurance?
Most dental PPO plans are accepted. Coverage for full-arch work is usually limited, but limited is not nothing, and the practice files the claims on your behalf and tells you what is likely to be covered before you commit.
What is the process for financing?
Handled at the consultation. Once your plan is set, the team runs the application with you through CareCredit, Proceed Finance or LendingClub and brings back the monthly options. Nothing is agreed until you have seen the numbers in writing.
