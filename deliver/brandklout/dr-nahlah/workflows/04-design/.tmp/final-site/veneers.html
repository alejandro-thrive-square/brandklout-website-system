Candidacy

Cosmetic problems, not functional ones.

Virtually every patient in good oral health is a candidate. What veneers are not is a fix for a bad bite. Within the smile zone, they handle:

  • Chipped or cracked teeth
  • Gapped or crowded teeth
  • Crooked or malformed teeth
  • Discolored teeth that whitening will not shift
  • Worn-down teeth

If your bite is the real problem, you will be told so at the evaluation rather than sold veneers over the top of it.

The Process

A minimally invasive prep, and a lab in the building.

01

Smile evaluation

Candidacy, a free Digital Smile Design preview of the result, pricing, and time with Dr. Nahlah rather than a treatment coordinator.

02

Minimally invasive prep

Less than a millimeter of enamel comes off the front surface, so the veneer sits flush instead of bulking the tooth out. Enamel does not grow back.

03

Design & fabrication

Each veneer is hand-designed in the practice’s own lab, one in McLean and one in Alexandria, to the size, shape and color for your face.

04

Fit & bond

The fit is verified, then the veneers are bonded. No special maintenance afterwards: brush and floss as before.

Questions

The questions people always ask.

Do veneers ruin your teeth?
Preparation removes less than a millimeter of enamel, enough for the veneer to sit flush. It is a permanent modification, which is exactly why the design is previewed and agreed before anything is prepared.
Will they stain?
Porcelain does not stain the way natural enamel does. Brush twice a day and floss once, as before.
Can veneers fix my bite?
No. A bad bite is a functional problem and needs a functional answer, which is often the reason a prosthodontist is the right person to assess it.
How long does it take?
As few as two once the design is settled: one to prepare and scan, one to verify fit and bond.
