Technology

Six machines, and what each one saves you.

Equipment is only worth listing if it changes the appointment.

01

CBCT scanner

Images the jaw in three dimensions, so bone volume and nerve position are measured before surgery rather than found during it.

02

Intraoral scanner

Digital impressions. No tray, no putty, no gagging, and an accuracy a physical impression cannot reach.

03

Guided implant surgery

Every implant is planned on the scan and placed through a printed guide, at the planned angle and depth. Smaller incisions, shorter recovery.

04

In-house digital lab

Both practices have one. A shade or a shape can be argued about in person instead of described on a form.

05

Milling machine

The design is cut here. Nothing is posted to another state and waited on, which is what removes weeks from a full-arch case.

06

Zirconia & porcelain oven

Final fabrication and firing happen in the building, so a remake is days rather than the better part of a month.

Why It Matters

The whole chain, in one building.

Any one of these machines can be rented time on. What is unusual is holding the entire chain, from scan and plan through surgery, design, milling, firing and fit, inside a single practice, under one person's responsibility.

A prosthesis that seats a fraction proud is a same-week fix here and a six-week round trip almost anywhere else.

  • Fewer appointments across the whole treatment
  • Days, not weeks, when a remake is needed
  • One practice accountable for the fit, the bite and the look
