The Principle

Affordable and accessible, whatever the treatment.

A stated position rather than a promotion, and it comes from a specific belief: oral health has a direct correlation to physical health. Which makes deferred dental care a health decision, not a budget one.

Payment

Five accepted forms.

  • Cash
  • Check
  • Credit card: most major cards accepted
  • Dental insurance: most PPO plans
  • Third-party financing: the three partners below

Bring your plan details to the first visit. Claims are filed on your behalf rather than left to you.

Financing

Three partners, with their actual terms.

Approach any of them directly, or let the financial coordinator apply for you.

CareCredit

For patients eligible for CareCredit financing, treatment costs are managed through flexible monthly payment options.

Proceed Finance

No-collateral loans of up to $60,000, with flexible terms of up to 96 months at fair, fixed rates. The longest term of the three, which is what makes full-arch work reachable.

No Insurance?

You have got options. That is the whole message.

The most common reason people assume treatment is out of reach, and very often wrong: the terms above are not contingent on having a plan.

The financial coordinator walks you through the application. Many patients are approved instantly, in some cases without a credit check.

Bring the estimate you already have. Second opinions are free, and they occasionally change the number as well as the plan.
