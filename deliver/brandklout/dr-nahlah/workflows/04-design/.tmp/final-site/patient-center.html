Welcome

Visiting a dental office is not most people’s priority.

The practice starts from that assumption. Seasoned staff, minimally invasive laser technology, a deliberately calm atmosphere, and sedation offered as needed rather than treated as an unusual request.

Insurance and financing

Your First Visit

Eight steps, in this order.

If you are coming in for an emergency this sequence changes: you will be treated first and processed afterwards.

01

A warm welcome

Somebody is there the moment you step inside. Building relationships at the front desk is the first thing the practice tries to be good at.

02

Meet the team

You meet the people who look after you on every visit. It is fine not to remember their names; they remember yours.

Meet the team
03

An office tour

Exam rooms, restrooms, exits. Knowing the way out is a small thing that matters to anxious patients.

See the facility
04

Consultation

A personal conversation with the dentist, and the moment to raise whatever you have been living with.

05

Digital X-rays

High-resolution images make cracks, cavities and early problems visible before they announce themselves.

06

Examination

Tissues, gums, bone and teeth, checked for decay, disease and infection, including an oral cancer screening.

07

Hygiene cleaning

Plaque and tartar removed, every tooth flossed individually, then polished.

08

You leave with gifts

Floss, toothpaste and a new toothbrush, every time, so the routine carries on at home.

Affordability

Healthy smiles for life, at a price that survives contact with a budget.

Many plans are accepted, and the practice helps you get the most out of the benefits you already have, which is frequently more than people realize.

No insurance is a different conversation, not the end of one: third-party partners offer low monthly payment plans for the costlier procedures.
