The Choice

Fixed, or removable. Everything else follows.

One question decides it: does the replacement come out, or does it stay in? Removable covers traditional and implant-supported dentures. Fixed means a bridge of teeth permanently carried on four to eight implants.

Comparison

Three tiers, listed honestly.

Every line is from the practice’s own comparison, including the unflattering ones.

01

Traditional dentures

Around 20% of natural function. Artificial teeth on an acrylic base that sits over the gums. The bottom tier of tooth replacement, and the cheapest.

  • Made from acrylic
  • Offers minimal support
  • Will need relines and repairs
  • Often loosens or falls out
  • Does not halt jawbone loss
  • Must be removed to clean
  • May be bulky or ill-fitting
  • Low-cost, and qualifies for financing
02

Implant-supported dentures

Around 60% of natural function. A removable acrylic denture, but stabilized by two or more implants so it stops moving.

  • Acrylic or porcelain
  • Support from two or more implants
  • May still need relines or repairs
  • Will not slip, loosen or fall out
  • Greater stability for eating
  • Removable for easy cleaning
  • Improves jawbone health
  • May not look or feel fully natural
  • Moderate cost
03

Full-arch fixed teeth

Over 98% of natural function. A zirconia bridge fixed permanently to four to eight implants. It does not come out, and it is cared for like teeth.

  • Zirconia porcelain
  • Fixed to four to eight implants
  • Comparable to natural teeth
  • Promotes a healthy jawbone
  • Resistant to bacteria and decay
  • Fracture- and chip-resistant
  • Improves oral and overall health
  • No additional maintenance
  • Highest cost

The Process

Four stages, and you are not toothless for any of them.

01

Consultation

A plan that fits your smile goals, your oral health and your budget rather than the practice’s preference.

02

Preparation, if needed

Extractions, bone grafting or gum therapy first, to build a sound foundation. Skipping this is what makes implants fail later.

Oral surgery
03

Surgery

In this office under sedation, guided by the scan, generally finished in a few hours. Most patients leave with temporary teeth, so you are functional while you heal.

04

Final teeth

Once healing is complete the final set is crafted to your specifications and attached. That is the version you keep.

Affording It

A lifelong investment, paid for monthly.

The cheapest option on this page is also the one that does nothing for the jawbone underneath, and that is the trade worth thinking about before the price is.

No dental insurance does not put fixed teeth out of reach. A financial coordinator helps you apply and set up a payment plan. Many patients are approved instantly, sometimes without a credit check. Second opinions are free.
