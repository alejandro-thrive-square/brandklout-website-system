The Problem

Six things, and the order they get fixed in.

Most people arrive naming one of these. Almost nobody arrives with only one of them, which is why the sequence matters more than the treatment list.

  • Discolored teeth
  • Chipped teeth
  • Gapped teeth
  • Disproportionate teeth
  • An uneven gumline
  • An asymmetrical smile

Whitening before veneers, because porcelain is matched to the shade your natural teeth end at. Gumline before either, because the frame decides how long a tooth looks. Get that order wrong and the work has to be redone.

How a full makeover is sequenced

The Treatments

Five tools. You will not be sold all of them.

A prosthodontist's job is to know which one the case actually needs, including when the answer is the cheapest one on the list.

01

Porcelain veneers

Thin shells covering only the front of the teeth that show when you smile. A small amount of enamel is removed so they sit flush, restoring size, shape and alignment. Minimal maintenance, maximum longevity.

Veneers in full
02

Dental crowns

Porcelain or ceramic caps that conceal cosmetic and functional damage. Durable and crack-resistant, which makes them right for molars and for front teeth that are cracked, broken or misshapen. Fifteen years or more when cared for properly.

Crowns & bridges
03

Dental bridges

A fixed replacement for one or more missing teeth: two or more custom crowns fused together. They restore function and appearance, and stop the remaining teeth drifting out of place and altering your bite.

04

Teeth whitening

From a one-hour in-office session to take-home trays with professional-grade gel. Monitored, so the result is a shade that reads as natural rather than as bleach.

Whitening in full
05

Inlays & onlays

Sometimes a tooth needs a small adjustment rather than a full crown. An inlay replaces an inside portion of the tooth; an onlay restores one or more cusps, returning strength and structure without covering the whole thing.

06

And knowing when to stop

A bad bite is a functional problem, not a cosmetic one. If that is what is actually wrong, you will be told so at the evaluation instead of having porcelain fitted over the top of it.
