The Rating

Five out of five, and what that is worth.

The practice reports an average rating of 4.9 out of 5 stars across the reviews it has collected. That is their published figure, and it is the one used on this site.

A perfect average is worth reading carefully, on any practice’s site. What is harder to fake is a patient sitting in front of a camera, named, describing a specific treatment they had. That is why this page leads with the films.

If you want to talk to someone who has been through the procedure you are considering, ask at the consultation. It is a normal request.
