The Specialty

Three more years, spent entirely on the hard cases.

Prosthodontics is the specialty concerned with replacing and restoring teeth: implants, crowns, bridges, veneers, dentures and full-mouth rehabilitation. Three years of training beyond dental school.

Practicing since 2003, in Northern Virginia since 2013, and a Fellow of the American College of Prosthodontists. Published in national and international scientific journals, and lectures on smile design and dental implants, which is what tends to bring other dentists’ difficult cases to his door.

Mentor & Educator

He teaches the dentists who refer to him.

Dr. Nahlah is adjunct faculty at Virginia Commonwealth University, teaching the residents in its Prosthodontics Postgraduate Program: the dentists who are themselves training to become prosthodontists.

He was a clinical instructor at UCSF School of Dentistry before relocating, and still runs hands-on courses for other dentists.

In 2018 he founded the Tysons Study Club, a quarterly symposium for dentists, by dentists.

How He Works

Techniques he brought in, and keeps current.

Immediate placement

Implants placed at the time of extraction where the case allows, removing a healing phase and a separate surgery.

Computer-guided surgery

Placement through a surgical guide generated from the cone-beam scan, so each post lands at the planned angle and depth.

Digital impressions

Scanned rather than molded, which removes a step patients dislike and a source of dimensional error.

Restoration of implants

The half most surgeons hand off. Planning and restoring in the same practice is the reason the final teeth match the plan.
