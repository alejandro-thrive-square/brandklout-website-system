Request A Consultation

Tell us what you are dealing with.

A consultation includes the 3D scan and a straight answer about what your case actually needs. Second opinions on implant surgery are free.

Prefer to speak to someone? Call (571) 619-9442 for McLean or (571) 410-3430 for Alexandria. If anxiety is what has kept you away, say so when you call. It changes how the appointment is planned.

Before You Come

What to bring, and what to expect.

  • Any existing treatment plan or quote. Second opinions on implant surgery are free
  • Recent imaging, if another practice has already scanned you
  • Your insurance details; most dental PPO plans are accepted and claims are filed for you
  • A list of medications, particularly if sedation is likely
  • Time. A first consultation includes an examination and a cone-beam scan
