One tooth

Single implant

Two implants carry a span of teeth between them, without putting the load on the natural teeth beside the gap.

Removable

The Anatomy

Three parts. One of them is yours within four months.

Bone grows onto the titanium post and locks it in place. That middle step is the reason implants do not slip, rub or need adhesive.

Which is why planning matters more than placing. A post in the wrong position integrates just as firmly as one in the right position, and then the tooth on top of it can never be right.

Before You Book

The questions people actually ask.

Will the surgery hurt?
It is done under sedation. You feel nothing during it, and most patients have no memory of the appointment. Afterwards there is minor discomfort that over-the-counter medication handles.
Am I a candidate?
Many patients already are. Others qualify after bone grafting or extractions. Candidacy is determined from a comprehensive consultation and a 3D scan, not guessed at.
What is recovery like?
Rest for a few days. Most people are back to normal activity within a week. A soft-food diet is needed for the next several months while the implant integrates with the jawbone.
How long do implants last?
They are designed to last decades, and can last the rest of your life. Surgical precision, materials and your own habits are what decide it. Implants have held a published success rate above 95% since they were introduced.
Do I still need check-ups?
Yes. Implant restorations resist decay, but the gum around an implant can still develop peri-implantitis from plaque and tartar. Brush twice daily, floss once, and keep the six-month hygiene appointments.
Do you take my insurance?
Most dental PPO plans are accepted. Coverage for implants is often limited, but limited is not nothing, and the practice files the claims on your behalf and works the plan for every benefit it entitles you to.
