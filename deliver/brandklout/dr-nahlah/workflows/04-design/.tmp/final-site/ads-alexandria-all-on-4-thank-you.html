Before You Call

Any questions before you call?

Is full-arch surgery painful?
It is done under sedation: nitrous oxide, oral or IV, the last two administered by a medical anesthesiologist. Most patients have no memory of the appointment. Swelling and soreness in the first days are normal and managed with what you are sent home with.
Do I really leave with teeth the same day?
Yes. A fixed PMMA provisional arch is fitted the same visit, so you do not leave without teeth. The definitive zirconia arch replaces it once the implants have integrated, usually three to four months later.
I have been told I have lost too much bone.
Bone loss is often what makes you a candidate rather than what rules you out. The two rear implants are angled forward into the denser bone at the front of the jaw. Some cases still need grafting, and the scan decides.
How long does it last?
Implants have held a published success rate above 95% since they were introduced. General cleanings every six months are what keep a full arch healthy.
When will I find out the cost?
At the consultation, once the scan has shown which arch is being planned and whether extractions or grafting come first. You get it in writing before anything is booked, and second opinions on another practice's quote cost nothing.
Do you take my insurance?
Most dental PPO plans are accepted. Coverage for full-arch work is usually limited, but limited is not nothing, and the practice files the claims on your behalf.
What is the process for financing?
Handled at the consultation. Once your plan is set, the team runs the application with you and brings back the monthly options. Nothing is agreed until you have seen the numbers in writing.
