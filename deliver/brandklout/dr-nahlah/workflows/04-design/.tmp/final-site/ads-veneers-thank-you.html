Worth thinking about before you come in

  • ›Bring photographs of your own smile from years ago, if you have them. They are the most useful reference there is
  • ›Bring photographs of smiles you like, and be ready to say what it is about them
  • ›Decide roughly how many teeth you are thinking about. The smile zone is usually six to ten
  • ›If you grind your teeth, or your bite has ever been called a problem, say so on the call

That last one matters most. Veneers cannot fix a bite, and a bite that is not dealt with first is the usual reason cosmetic work does not last.
