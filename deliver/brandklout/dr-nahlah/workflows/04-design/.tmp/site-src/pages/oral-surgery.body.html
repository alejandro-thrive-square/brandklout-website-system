Why Here

The person who removes it also replaces it.

Elsewhere the extraction goes to a surgeon and the replacement to somebody else. Between them sits a handover, and the handover is where the plan gets lost.

Procedures

Three procedures, in the comfort of the office.

Each one done here, kept minimally invasive by the technology used to plan it.

01 · Extraction

Tooth extraction

The tooth is eased out without disturbing healthy bone or gum, which matters enormously if anything is going back in the space later. Wisdom teeth are the more complex form.

02 · Grafting

Bone grafting

Bone loss after a missing tooth is ongoing, not one-off, and has to be treated before an implant has anything to hold on to. Placed, sutured, and left a few months to integrate.

03 · Placement

Implant surgery

The post is placed into the jawbone to anchor a new tooth or a full set. CT-guided placement makes it precise, and qualifying patients leave with a temporary the same day.
