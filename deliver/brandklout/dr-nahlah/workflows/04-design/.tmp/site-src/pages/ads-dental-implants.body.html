Before You Book

Everything patients ask before booking.

Is the implant procedure painful?
It is done under local anesthetic, with nitrous oxide, oral or IV sedation available, so you should not feel it. Some soreness during early healing is normal and manageable. Most patients tell us it was far easier than they expected.
Can I really get a tooth the same day?
Sometimes, yes. Depending on your bone and your overall health, a temporary tooth can be fitted the day the implant goes in. The 3D scan at the consultation is what tells us whether you qualify.
What if I have been told I do not have enough bone?
Do not rule yourself out. Bone grafting, sinus lifts and angled implant approaches make treatment possible for many patients who were told no elsewhere. The scan shows exactly what is possible in your case.
How long do implants last?
The post is designed as a lifetime replacement root, and implants have held a published success rate above 95% since they were introduced. The crown on top may need maintenance over time, like any tooth. The practice's implant work carries a five-year warranty, conditional on the Precare cleanings every six months.
When will I find out the cost?
At your consultation, once the scan shows what your case actually needs. Two people missing the same tooth can need very different treatment, so no honest figure exists before then. You get it in writing, and grafting or extractions are quoted with it, before anything is booked.
Do you take my insurance?
Most dental PPO plans are accepted. Coverage for implants is often limited, but limited is not nothing, and the practice files the claims on your behalf and walks you through what is likely to be covered before you commit to anything.
What is the process for financing?
Handled at the consultation. Once your plan is set, the team runs the application with you through CareCredit, Proceed Finance or LendingClub and brings back the monthly options. Nothing is agreed until you have seen the numbers in writing.
