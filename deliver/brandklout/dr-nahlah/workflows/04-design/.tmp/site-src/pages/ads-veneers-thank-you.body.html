Before You Call

Any questions before you call?

Do veneers ruin your teeth?
Preparation removes less than a millimeter of enamel, enough for the veneer to sit flush. It is a permanent modification, which is exactly why the design is previewed and agreed before anything is prepared.
Will they look like veneers?
The size, shape and shade are chosen for your lip line, your gum line and your own coloring, and you see that design at the evaluation before any enamel is touched.
Will they stain?
Porcelain does not stain the way natural enamel does. Brush twice a day and floss once, as before.
Can veneers fix my bite?
No. Veneers are a cosmetic treatment for the smile zone. A bad bite is a functional problem and needs to be addressed as one, which is often the reason a prosthodontist is the right person to be assessing it.
How long does it take?
As few as two appointments once the design is settled: one to prepare and scan, one to verify the fit and bond.
When will I find out the cost?
At the evaluation. It depends on how many teeth are in the plan and what else is going on in the mouth. You get the number in writing before anything is booked.
What is the process for financing?
Handled at the evaluation. Once your plan is set, the team runs the application with you and brings back the monthly options. Nothing is agreed until you have seen the numbers in writing.
