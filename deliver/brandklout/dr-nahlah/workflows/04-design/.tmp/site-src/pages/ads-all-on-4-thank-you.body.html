Why another year matters

  • ›Bone recedes where teeth are not loading it, and time gone is the biggest predictor of whether anything has to be rebuilt first
  • ›Less bone can narrow the implant options open to you, and a thin FP1 arch is the first thing it rules out
  • ›A denture resting on gum tissue keeps accelerating that loss rather than slowing it
  • ›More extensive treatment later usually means grafting, more healing time and a higher number

None of that is a reason to rush a decision. It is the reason to get the scan done, so the decision is made against what your bone is actually doing rather than against a guess.
