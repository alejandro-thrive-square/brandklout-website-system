A Dental Home

Your new dental home is right here.

Most people arrive here having already been somewhere else. They have a plan they do not understand, a quote they cannot reconcile, or a mouth that has been treated one tooth at a time for a decade without anyone standing back to look at the whole thing.

That is the work of a prosthodontist. Dr. Esam Nahlah is board certified in it, has practiced it for over twenty years, and has placed more than 7,000 full-mouth dental implants. Whether the answer is a complete cosmetic smile makeover or a full arch of new teeth on All-on-4 implants, it is designed as one result rather than assembled out of separate appointments.

The practice also holds room for same-day emergency appointments and offers free second opinions, because the most common reason someone hesitates is not the treatment, it is not being sure the treatment is the right one.

The Approach

A personal approach to every patient.

The practice makes it a priority to build a real relationship with every patient it sees. That is not only how care gets personalized; it is the single most effective thing anyone has found for the fear and the anxiety that keep people out of a dental chair for years at a time.

The technology exists to serve the same end. An intraoral scanner replaces the tray of impression material. Computer-guided implant surgery means smaller incisions and shorter recovery. A digital lab inside each of the two practices means fewer visits and fewer weeks between them. None of it is there to be impressive; it is there so the appointment is shorter, more precise and less unpleasant than the one you are picturing.
