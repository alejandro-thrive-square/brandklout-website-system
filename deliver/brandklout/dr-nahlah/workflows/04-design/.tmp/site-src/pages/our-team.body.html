In-House

The lab is a room here, not a shipping address.

A dental laboratory is normally a separate business in a separate city: impressions go out, weeks pass, a box comes back, and a bad fit restarts the cycle. This practice keeps the lab in the building, with a designer on staff. That changes three things:

  • Fewer appointments, because adjustments happen while you are still in the chair
  • Shorter waits, because nothing is in transit
  • A shade and shape agreed with the person who will cut them, not described on a form
