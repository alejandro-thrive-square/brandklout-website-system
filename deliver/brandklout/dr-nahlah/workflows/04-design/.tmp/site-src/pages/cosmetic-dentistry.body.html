The Problem

Six things, and the order they get fixed in.

Almost nobody arrives with only one, which is why the sequence matters more than the list.

  • Discolored teeth
  • Chipped teeth
  • Gapped teeth
  • Disproportionate teeth
  • An uneven gumline
  • An asymmetrical smile

Whitening before veneers, because porcelain is matched to the shade your natural teeth end at. Gumline before either, because the frame decides how long a tooth looks. Get that order wrong and the work has to be redone.

How a full makeover is sequenced

The Treatments

Five tools. You will not be sold all of them.

Knowing which one the case needs, including when it is the cheapest on the list.

01

Porcelain veneers

Thin shells over the front of the teeth that show when you smile. A small amount of enamel is removed so they sit flush.

Veneers in full
02

Dental crowns

Caps that conceal cosmetic and functional damage. Crack-resistant, so right for molars and for broken front teeth. Fifteen years or more.

Crowns & bridges
03

Dental bridges

Two or more custom crowns fused together to replace missing teeth, and to stop the remaining ones drifting out of place.

04

Teeth whitening

A one-hour in-office session or take-home trays. Monitored, so the result reads as natural rather than as bleach.

Whitening in full
05

Inlays & onlays

Sometimes a tooth needs a small adjustment rather than a full crown. An inlay replaces an inside portion; an onlay restores a cusp.

06

And knowing when to stop

A bad bite is functional, not cosmetic. If that is what is wrong, you will be told at the evaluation instead of having porcelain fitted over it.
