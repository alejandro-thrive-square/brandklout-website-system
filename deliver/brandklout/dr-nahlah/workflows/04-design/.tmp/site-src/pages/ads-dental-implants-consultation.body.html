Before You Send It

Four things people want to know first.

Is the consultation really free?
Yes. The consultation, including the 3D scan where it is useful, costs nothing, and a second opinion on implant surgery costs nothing either. Bring another practice's plan and scan if you have them.
Am I a candidate?
Many patients already are. Others qualify after bone grafting or extractions. Candidacy is determined from the examination and the scan, not guessed at over the phone.
Do you take my insurance?
Most dental PPO plans are accepted. Coverage for implants is often limited, but limited is not nothing, and the practice files the claims on your behalf. Monthly payment plans are available through CareCredit, Proceed Finance and LendingClub.
What happens on the call?
We ask what is going on, what you have been told already, and what you are hoping for, and we find a time. Nothing is decided on the call and nothing is charged.
