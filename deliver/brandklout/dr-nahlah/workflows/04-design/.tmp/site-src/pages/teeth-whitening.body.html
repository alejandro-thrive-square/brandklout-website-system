The Advantage

You have almost certainly tried this yourself.

Most methods will lift a stained tooth slightly. Some are abrasive, and enamel does not grow back: a thinner tooth reads more yellow, not less, because the dentine underneath starts to show through.

Afterwards

Keeping it is three habits, not a product.

Keep the routine, keep the professional cleanings, and go easy on what stains: coffee, tea, red wine, tobacco.

  • Quick treatment: generally about one hour
  • Immediate and dramatic results
  • Little to no tooth or gum sensitivity
  • No messy or abrasive products
  • A white shade customized to you

One sequencing note: if veneers or crowns are anywhere in your plan, whiten first. Porcelain is matched to the shade your natural teeth finish at, and it will not bleach later.
