Are You A Candidate

Cosmetic problems, not functional ones.

Virtually every patient in good oral health is a candidate. Within the smile zone, veneers handle:

  • Chipped or cracked teeth
  • Gapped or crowded teeth
  • Crooked or malformed teeth
  • Discolored teeth that whitening will not shift
  • Worn-down teeth

What veneers are not is a fix for a bad bite. A bad bite is a functional problem and needs a functional answer, which is often exactly why a prosthodontist is the right person to be assessing it. If your bite is the real problem you will be told so at the evaluation, rather than sold veneers over the top of it.

Before You Book

The questions people actually ask.

Do veneers ruin your teeth?
Preparation removes less than a millimeter of enamel, enough for the veneer to sit flush. It is a permanent modification, which is exactly why the design is previewed and agreed before anything is prepared.
Will they look like veneers?
The size, shape and shade are chosen for your lip line, your gum line and your own coloring, and you see that design at the evaluation before any enamel is touched. If the preview is not what you wanted, nothing has happened yet.
Will they stain?
Porcelain does not stain the way natural enamel does. That is not a reason to change your routine: brush twice a day and floss once, as before.
Can veneers fix my bite?
No. Veneers are a cosmetic treatment for the smile zone. A bad bite is a functional problem and needs to be addressed as one, which is often the reason a prosthodontist is the right person to be assessing it.
How long does it take?
As few as two appointments once the design is settled: one to prepare and scan, one to verify the fit and bond.
What does it cost?
It depends on how many teeth are in the plan and what else is going on in the mouth, so no honest figure exists before the evaluation. You get the number in writing before anything is booked, and monthly payment plans are available through CareCredit, Proceed Finance and LendingClub.
