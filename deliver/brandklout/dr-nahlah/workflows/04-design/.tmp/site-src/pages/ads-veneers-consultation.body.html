Before You Send It

Four things people want to know first.

Is the evaluation really free?
Yes, and so is the Digital Smile Design preview of the result. You are not committing to treatment by coming in and looking at the design.
What if I do not like the design?
Then nothing has happened. The preview is made before any enamel is prepared, precisely so that the design is the thing you agree to rather than the thing you find out about afterwards.
What if veneers are not the right answer for me?
You will be told so. Veneers are a cosmetic treatment for the smile zone and cannot fix a bad bite, which is a functional problem. A prosthodontist is the right person to tell those apart.
What happens on the call?
We ask what you would change and what you are hoping for, and we find a time. Nothing is decided on the call and nothing is charged.
