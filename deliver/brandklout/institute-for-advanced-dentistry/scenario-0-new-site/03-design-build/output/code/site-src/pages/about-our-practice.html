Arizona's first prosthodontic practice, serving one smile at a time.

With a legacy since 1954, we offer precision, results-based treatment for teeth replacement, smile makeovers and oral functional rehabilitation, led by Dr. Jonathan Bensoussan.

Our HistoryBefore dental implants existed

Our practice started in 1954, the first prosthodontic office in Arizona, before dental implants even existed. Our technology and treatments have come a long way since. Our purpose remains the same: to serve the community one smile at a time.

All in one office

All your dental work, in-house

Dr. Bensoussan does all the planning, surgery and placement of new teeth (crowns, veneers or bridges) in our office.

You get specialist-level implant surgery, smile makeovers and functional rehabilitation, without having to go to another office.

Complex dental conditions

A specialist in complex cases

Dr. Bensoussan is extensively trained in advanced technology and state-of-the-art procedures for complex dental conditions.

He gives you an accurate diagnosis and treatment plan, and premium care to fully resolve your situation.

Our dental services

Results-based warranty

We stand behind our work

The warranty covers surgical procedures and the restorations we provide, such as crowns, bridges and veneers.

If the final outcome doesn't meet the treatment goals we agreed on, we'll make it right at no extra cost. Our only requirement is that you follow the recommended care and maintenance plan.

Premium materials and labs

Temporaries made here, finals by top US labs

We use the highest grade porcelain or zirconia for crowns, bridges and veneers: strong, esthetic and natural-looking.

Our own lab makes temporary teeth in-house, so they fit precisely and look good. Permanent teeth come from top technicians at top US dental laboratories.

State-of-the-art technology

A completely digital workflow

3D CT and intraoral scanners, live 3D guided surgery with the X-Guide, and virtual smile design.

Implant surgery and the fit of every crown, bridge and veneer are planned precisely. That means more efficiency, faster healing, less discomfort and predictable outcomes.
