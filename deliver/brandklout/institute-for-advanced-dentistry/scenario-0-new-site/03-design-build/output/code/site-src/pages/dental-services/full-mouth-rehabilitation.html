Correcting the bite

A jaw in a neutral position

An essential element is putting your jaw into a neutral position, where it is not causing problems for your teeth and jaw joint. A bad bite is a frequent culprit for multiple oral issues.

Using models of your teeth, Dr. Bensoussan focuses on getting your bite correct, so you won't have repeat issues.
