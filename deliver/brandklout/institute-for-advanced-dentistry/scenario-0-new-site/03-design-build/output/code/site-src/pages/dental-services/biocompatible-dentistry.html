Why It MattersOral and systemic health

The materials you put in your body can make a difference to your oral and systemic health. At every stage of treatment, we use non-toxic, safe and carefully selected materials.

Metal-FreeFillings, crowns, implants

Where metal used to go, and what we use instead.

Tooth-colored fillings, all-ceramic crowns and bridges, and a choice of titanium or zirconia implants.

No metal fillings

Tooth-colored, never amalgam

Traditional amalgam fillings contain mercury, silver, tin, copper and zinc. Some of these metals can cause allergic reactions or sensitivities, and mercury can be released as a toxic vapor.

Dr. Bensoussan only uses safe, biocompatible, tooth-colored material to fill a tooth.

Safe amalgam filling removal

Dr. Bensoussan removes amalgam fillings using a rubber dam to isolate the patient from mercury vapor, and powerful suction to quickly remove the vapor and amalgam particles.

He follows strict safety protocols during every procedure to protect patients, reduce exposure risks and make the transition to healthier restorative materials smooth.

Metal-free crowns and bridges

All-porcelain, all-ceramic

Crowns fused to metal can contain gold, nickel, chromium and cobalt. These metals can be a source of allergic reactions, especially nickel, which is commonly used because it is affordable.

Dr. Bensoussan fabricates strong, all-porcelain or all-ceramic crowns and bridges: durable, stain-resistant and designed to blend with natural teeth.

Zirconia dental implants

Titanium, or a ceramic alternative

We offer titanium and zirconia dental implants. Titanium is itself considered a biocompatible metal and rarely causes adverse reactions.

Some patients opt for zirconia implants: a strong, fully biocompatible ceramic with natural aesthetics, for those wishing to avoid any type of metal.

BPA-Free OfficeDown to the temporaries

Every material in our office is BPA-free.

  • BPA (bisphenol A) is a chemical compound used in plastics.
  • Studies have shown that BPA can leach out and cause health effects such as hormone disruption.
  • Some dental materials contain derivatives of BPA that can leach out over time into the mouth.
  • All materials in our office are completely BPA-free, even our temporary crowns and bridges, made in our own dental lab.
