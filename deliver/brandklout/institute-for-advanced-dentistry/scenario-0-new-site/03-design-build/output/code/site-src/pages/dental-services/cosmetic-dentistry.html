Smaller refinements

Dental bonding and teeth whitening

Dental bonding covers minor imperfections in a tooth, such as chips, cracks, small gaps and stains.

For whitening we use the KöR system, especially effective on difficult discoloration such as tetracycline-stained teeth, with a desensitizing protocol that significantly reduces sensitivity.

The ProcessDesigned, worn, then made

How we design a beautiful smile.

Nothing permanent is made until you have seen your new smile, and approved it.

Exam and x-rays

Your smile, your say

Once you decide to move forward, we take images of your smile. Together with Dr. Bensoussan, you decide exactly how you want it to look.

An oral exam and digital x-rays find any dental issues, and we take care of those first.

Detailed planning

A virtual model of your future smile

Digital scans and photographs become a detailed virtual model of your new smile, tweaked until you are fully satisfied.

A physical model then confirms the teeth are positioned for a comfortable bite.

Test driving your smile

You wear it before it is made

For veneers, crowns or New Teeth in One Day, Dr. Bensoussan makes a physical simulation of your future smile that fits over your teeth. You see the new smile directly on you.

  • Longer or shorter teeth, a change of shape: adjusted until you are fully satisfied.
  • Many are 3D-printed in our office on SprintRay printers.

Your new smile

Strong, healthy and lasting

With the design approved, Dr. Bensoussan carries out your makeover: porcelain veneers, whitening, crowns, bonding for minor blemishes, or implants to replace missing teeth.

Only then are the final ceramics made, by laboratories that execute his design.
