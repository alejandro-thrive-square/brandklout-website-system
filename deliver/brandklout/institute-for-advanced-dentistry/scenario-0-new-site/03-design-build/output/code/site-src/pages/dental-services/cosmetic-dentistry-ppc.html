The ProcessHow we design your makeover

You approve the smile before it is made.

Four steps, from the first images to the final teeth.

  1. Comprehensive exam and x-rays

    Your smile, your say

    We take images of your smile, and together with Dr. Bensoussan you decide exactly how you want it to look. An oral exam and digital x-rays find any issues to treat first.

  2. Detailed planning

    A virtual model of your future smile

    Digital scans and photographs become a detailed virtual model, tweaked until you are fully satisfied, then checked on a physical model for a comfortable bite.

  3. Test driving your smile

    See it directly on you

    For veneers, crowns or New Teeth in One Day, a model that fits over your teeth shows the new smile on you. Longer or shorter teeth, a new shape: adjusted until you are fully satisfied.

  4. Your new smile

    Strong, healthy and lasting

    Then the makeover itself: porcelain veneers, teeth whitening, crowns, dental bonding for minor blemishes, or dental implants to replace missing teeth.
