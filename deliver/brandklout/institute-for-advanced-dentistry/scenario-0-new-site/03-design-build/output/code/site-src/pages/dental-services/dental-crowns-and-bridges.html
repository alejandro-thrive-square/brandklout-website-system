Three ways to restore a tooth.

We keep treatment as conservative as possible, removing only the tooth structure needed to clean out the decay.

Crowns

Strength, bite and appearance

Crowns strengthen teeth that have decayed, improve a bite for more comfortable chewing, improve appearance, and attach to dental implants.

Each one is made from high-grade porcelain or zirconia, and exactly fitted so it stays secure.

Partial crowns · onlays and inlays

Only as much as the tooth needs

When decay is too extensive for a filling, but not severe enough for a crown, Dr. Bensoussan uses a partial crown whenever he can.

  • Inlay. Fits within the cusps, the raised edges of a tooth.
  • Onlay. Covers a larger portion of the tooth for added strength, and preserves more healthy structure.

Bridges

Replacing missing teeth

A bridge replaces decayed or missing teeth. The teeth beside the gap are cut down, capped, and used as anchors.

Because of that, Dr. Bensoussan will usually recommend dental implants instead.

Our LabsIn-office, and the best in the US

Your temporary crowns and bridges are designed and made in our own lab. Dr. Bensoussan personally reviews the design of every permanent one before it is milled.

Why the lab matters

The aesthetics and longevity of a new crown, bridge or implant-supported teeth depend greatly on the quality of both the materials and the dental lab.

We use a combination of our in-office lab and top-quality US dental labs. Your permanent crown or bridge is made in partnership with dedicated crown and bridge labs, chosen for their high-quality work. The result: natural-looking crowns and bridges that will last.
