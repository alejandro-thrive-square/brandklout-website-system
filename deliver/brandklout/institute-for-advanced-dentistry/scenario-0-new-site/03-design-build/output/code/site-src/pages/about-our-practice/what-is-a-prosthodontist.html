What is a prosthodontist?

A dental specialist who completed dental school plus at least three additional years of advanced training in an accredited prosthodontic graduate program. Dr. Jonathan Bensoussan is one.

The WordProstho and dontist

“Prostho” means replacement, and “dontist” means dealing with teeth. Prosthodontists are the recognized experts when anything needs to be replaced in your mouth, from a single tooth to all the teeth and gums.

The DifferenceSpecialist and general dentist

How a prosthodontist differs from other dentists.

Many dentists can do some of these treatments. Prosthodontists are the specialists dedicated to this type of care.

Advanced training

Three more years, after dental school

Prosthodontists are highly trained in cosmetics, dental implants, crowns, bridges, dentures, temporomandibular disorders (TMJ/TMD) and more.

Dr. Bensoussan completed a three-year specialty residency in Prosthodontics at UCSF, then a further year mentored by a prosthodontist surgeon.

When to See OneIs this for you?

When a prosthodontist is the right call.

  • Several teeth are missing or failing
  • Dentures are uncomfortable or unstable
  • Dental implants are needed
  • A full-mouth restoration is required
  • Bite issues affect comfort or chewing
  • Esthetics and function must be carefully balanced

A general dentist provides preventive care, cleanings, fillings and many routine procedures, and plays a crucial role in maintaining oral health. A prosthodontist completed at least three more years of training in an accredited prosthodontic program after dental school, and specializes in advanced restorative dentistry, particularly when several teeth are involved or when function, bite balance and long-term durability are critical.

Anything that needs to be replaced in your mouth: a single tooth, multiple teeth, or all teeth and gums. That includes dental implants, crowns and bridges, dentures and partial dentures, full-mouth rehabilitation, bite alignment and jaw function, and esthetic smile design.

Dr. Bensoussan makes high-quality temporary restorations in his own in-house lab, and collaborates closely with top dental labs on your permanent new teeth, in the highest grade porcelain or zirconia.
