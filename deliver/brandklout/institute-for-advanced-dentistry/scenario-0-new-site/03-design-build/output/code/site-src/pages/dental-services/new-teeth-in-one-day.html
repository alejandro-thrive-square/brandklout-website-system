What It IsA full arch on a few implants

New Teeth in One Day uses four to five dental implants in the upper or lower jaw to hold a new set of teeth in zirconia or acrylic. The teeth come out, the implants go in and your new teeth are attached, all in the same visit.

The ProcessSeven steps, one surgical visit

Planned from the end back.

Dr. Bensoussan plans the treatment meticulously, so that on surgery day it is smooth and precise, and the result is exactly what you expect.

  1. Step 1 · Free

    A comprehensive consultation

    A full exam, 3D x-rays and a consultation. Dr. Bensoussan learns your goals for your teeth and smile, explains the procedure and answers your questions.

  2. Step 2

    Meticulous planning

    3D CT and intraoral scans, photos and measurements. The surgery and the final teeth are planned in 3D implant-planning software.

  3. Step 3

    Your final smile, modelled

    From precise measurements of your mouth, jaw and teeth, he creates a custom model of your new smile and adjusts it until it is just right.

  4. Step 4

    3D navigation for precision

    The X-Guide system, synced with the plan, gives real-time feedback on each implant's position, angle and depth. No guesswork.

  5. Step 5 · Surgery day

    One visit, fully relaxed

    Oral or IV sedation, or general anesthesia with a medical anesthesiologist. The teeth are removed, the implants placed and surrounded with a bone graft, and your provisional teeth attached.

  6. Step 6 · The same day

    Walk out with a new smile

    Your provisional teeth are made in our own lab while the surgery is completed: functional, esthetic, a natural color and a bright polish.

  7. Step 7 · Three months later

    Your permanent teeth

    Upper teeth in high-grade zirconia for strength and esthetics; lower teeth generally in an acrylic that withstands biting, so they won't need replacing every five or ten years.

Why saving teeth is not always best

Weakened teeth are hard to save

Any work done can make them weaker. Crowns, bridges, extractions and replacing old dental work can shorten their life, and you may lose the teeth anyway or have to repeat the repair.

Dr. Bensoussan examines your teeth, bone and oral health and gives you an informed evaluation of the best strategy for your goals, with comparison costs for different options.

Prevents bone loss

Teeth that keep the jaw working

With several missing teeth or dentures, the jawbone lacks stimulation and can absorb back into the body. The lower face gradually wears away, and looks prematurely aged.

New Teeth in One Day gives you strong teeth and prevents bone loss.

If you're dealing with missing, decayed or broken-down teeth, ongoing dental problems, pain or difficulty chewing, or you're simply tired of dentures, New Teeth in One Day may be the ideal solution. Dr. Bensoussan examines your teeth, bone and oral health and gives you an informed evaluation of the best strategy for your goals.

Dr. Bensoussan uses implants placed on an angle to get maximum bone coverage, so bone grafting is usually not needed. In rare cases of severe bone loss he might do bone grafting ahead of time.

The force from chewing and biting is evenly distributed between the implants, so only four to five are needed. Dr. Bensoussan uses a titanium bar under the teeth to add strength, flexibility and shock absorption.
