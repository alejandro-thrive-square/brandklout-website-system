Institute for Advanced Dentistry PLLC, also referred to as Institute for Advanced Dentistry or IFA Dentistry, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through https://www.ifadentistry.com/ (the Site) and through our text messaging program. This Privacy Policy explains the information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to information collected through the Site and our SMS messaging program. Information collected from you as a patient in connection with dental care is governed by our separate Notice of Privacy Practices under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, or HIPAA, which is available at our offices and upon request.

1 Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

We collect information you choose to provide when you:

  • Submit a contact, appointment request, patient referral, or other website form
  • Use an online appointment scheduling tool made available through the Site
  • Sign up for special offers, newsletters, or educational resources
  • Request a consultation or submit photographs or other information for our review, when that option is available
  • Opt in to receive text messages by affirmatively selecting an SMS consent option and providing a mobile number
  • Communicate with us by phone, email, or text message

The information you provide may include your name, email address, telephone or mobile number, mailing address, date of birth, insurance information, reason for contacting us, appointment preferences, photographs, and other information you choose to share.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect information about your device and how you interact with the Site, including:

  • Your Internet Protocol address and approximate location
  • Browser type, operating system, device type, and device identifiers
  • Pages visited, time spent on the Site, interactions with Site features, and referring URLs
  • Information collected through cookies, analytics tools, pixels, and similar technologies

Health Related Information

If you submit photographs or describe symptoms through a consultation or contact form, we handle that information with care. Website forms, standard email, and SMS are not secure channels for detailed medical histories or sensitive health, financial, or insurance information. For sensitive matters, please call us at (602) 242-2256 or use a secure channel that we direct you to.

2 How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule or manage appointments
  • Provide requested dental services and related administrative support
  • Send appointment confirmations, reminders, follow-up communications, and office updates by phone, email, or SMS
  • Exchange conversational text messages in response to questions or to coordinate care
  • Send promotional or marketing communications only when permitted by law and, for marketing text messages, after you opt in
  • Verify insurance benefits and support payment or financing processes
  • Operate, secure, analyze, and improve the Site, our services, and the patient experience
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, professional, and recordkeeping obligations

3 SMS and Text Messaging Program

IFA Dentistry may offer an SMS text messaging program for appointment messages, responses to your questions, care coordination, office updates, and promotional messages when you have separately consented to receive them.

How You Opt In

You may opt in by checking an SMS consent box on an IFA Dentistry website form, providing your mobile number, and affirmatively agreeing to receive text messages, or by using another written or electronic opt-in method that we make available. Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services or receiving dental care. By opting in, you agree to receive recurring automated and conversational messages from IFA Dentistry at the mobile number you provide.

Types of Messages

  • Appointment messages (transactional) Confirmations, reminders, rescheduling notices, and follow-up care information
  • Conversational messages (customer care) Two-way replies from our team about your inquiry, appointment, or visit
  • Promotional messages (marketing) Special offers, service announcements, and practice updates when you have opted in to marketing messages

Program Details

  • Message frequency varies according to your appointments and interactions with us
  • Message and data rates may apply under your mobile carrier plan; IFA Dentistry does not charge a separate fee for its text messages
  • To stop messages, reply STOP to any message; we may send one final message confirming your opt-out
  • To request help, reply HELP or contact us at (602) 242-2256 or info@ifadentistry.com
  • The program is intended to work with major United States wireless carriers; carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages
  • If you opt out and later wish to rejoin, you may complete a new opt-in through an available IFA Dentistry form or contact our office

Mobile Information and Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number, SMS opt-in data, or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may share this information with subcontractors that help us operate the program, such as our messaging platform provider and telecommunications carriers, only as needed to deliver and manage messages. Standard SMS is transmitted in plain text and is not a secure channel. Do not send sensitive health, financial, or personally identifiable information by text message.

4 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose information as needed to operate our practice and serve you, including to:

  • Service providers Companies that support website hosting, scheduling, communications, SMS delivery, payment processing, security, and analytics, subject to contractual limits on their use of information
  • Insurance carriers and benefit administrators When you ask us to verify benefits or submit a claim
  • Healthcare partners Specialists, laboratories, imaging providers, and others involved in your care, as permitted by law
  • Legal and regulatory authorities When required by applicable law, subpoena, court order, regulatory request, or to protect rights, property, safety, or the integrity of our services

If the practice is sold, merged, reorganized, or transfers assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5 Third Party Tools and Embedded Content

The Site may use third-party services to operate and improve its features, including:

  • Online appointment scheduling and patient communication tools
  • SMS messaging platforms and telecommunications providers
  • Payment, financing, or prequalification tools
  • Embedded maps and directions
  • Embedded videos, social media features, and patient review widgets
  • Website analytics, performance, advertising, and security services

These third parties may collect information about your visit and operate under their own privacy policies. Their services may change over time. We encourage you to review the privacy terms presented by a third-party service before using it.

6 Cookies and Similar Technologies

A cookie is a small data file stored on your device when you visit a website. We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, keep the Site functioning, understand how visitors find and use the Site, measure content and advertising performance, and improve the user experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the operation of some Site features.

7 Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link in an email or contacting us
  • Opt out of text messages by replying STOP to a message
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we maintain about you, subject to applicable law and recordkeeping requirements
  • Adjust cookie settings through your browser and use other preference tools that the Site may provide

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy law may provide additional rights. To submit a request, use the contact information in Section 12. We will respond within the period required by law and will not discriminate against you for exercising a legal right. Protected health information maintained as part of your dental record is governed by HIPAA and applicable state law, which may limit deletion or modification of clinical records.

8 How We Protect Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission over the internet or SMS, and no form of electronic storage, is completely secure. For your protection, do not send confidential health information through general website forms, text messages, or unencrypted email.

9 Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal and professional obligations, resolve disputes, protect our rights, and enforce agreements. SMS consent records and message logs may be retained as required by telecommunications and consumer protection laws. Dental records are retained in accordance with HIPAA, Arizona requirements, and other applicable law.

10 Children Privacy

The Site and SMS program are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information or SMS opt-in data directly from children under 13 through these services. We may treat children as patients; in those cases, information is collected from or with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian and handled under HIPAA and applicable law. If you believe a child has submitted information through the Site or SMS program without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can review and address the matter.

11 Links to Other Websites

The Site may link to websites that we do not control. We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of those sites. Review the applicable privacy policy before sharing information with a third party.
