What It IsA new root, then a new tooth

A dental implant is a replacement tooth root in biocompatible titanium or zirconia, topped with a crown, a bridge, a denture or a whole new set of teeth. It acts and feels very much like a natural tooth, and unlike a bridge, the teeth beside it are never shaved down.

The VisitsWhat actually happens

From the first scan to the final teeth.

If the bone needs rebuilding, the graft usually goes in with the implant. With little or no bone, it may need to grow back first.

  1. Visit 1 · Free

    Exam, 3D x-rays and a plan

    Digital x-rays, a dental CT scan and a thorough exam. You meet Dr. Bensoussan, ask everything, and leave with a treatment plan.

  2. Before surgery

    Planned with the end in mind

    The implant's position, angle and depth are set in 3D around the tooth or teeth it will carry.

  3. Surgery day

    Out, in, and a temporary tooth

    In most cases the tooth is removed, the implant placed and a temporary tooth attached in the same visit, so no gap shows.

  4. After healing

    The final teeth

    Crafted with top laboratory technicians in porcelain, zirconia or acrylic, and fitted by the doctor who planned them.

  5. For the years after

    A results-based warranty

    If the result doesn't meet the goals we agreed on, we make it right at no extra cost. Our only requirement is that you follow the care plan.
