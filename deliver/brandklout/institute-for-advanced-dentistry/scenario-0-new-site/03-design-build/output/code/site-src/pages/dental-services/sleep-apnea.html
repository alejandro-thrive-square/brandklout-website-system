The AirwayWhy it closes

The most common type, obstructive sleep apnea, occurs when the muscles in the throat relax, causing the airway to narrow or close.

The ApplianceAn alternative to CPAP

A custom mouthguard that holds the airway open.

Oral appliance therapy is an alternative to CPAP for patients with mild to moderate sleep apnea, or who can't tolerate the CPAP machine.

The OASYS Hinge Appliance

Custom-fit, with a hinge

Dr. Bensoussan uses the OASYS Hinge Appliance, custom-fit for each patient. It is designed to gently shift the lower jaw forward during sleep.

The hinge allows for natural movement and maximum comfort.

Keeping your jaw healthy

A morning reset for the jaw

Dr. Bensoussan adds an extra step: a custom repositioning device you use for 30 minutes in the morning.

It helps relax and reset the jaw muscles and joints after a night of sleeping with the jaw held open. It "deprograms" them, which reduces the risk of TMJ (jaw joint) issues.

TMJ treatment

Oral appliance therapy for snoring

And a quieter night for two

Snoring occurs when the muscles in the throat relax, causing the soft palate, uvula and tongue to partially block the airway.

The oral appliance keeps the jaw in a forward position, preventing the obstruction. No testing or medical prescription is needed for snoring treatment.
