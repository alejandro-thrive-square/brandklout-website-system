His ApproachCalm and thoughtful

Patients appreciate his calm, thoughtful approach, and the way he clearly explains each step of treatment so they feel confident and at ease. Every patient receives a plan designed for their health, lifestyle and smile goals.

In His WordsWhy he became a dentist

“I became a dentist because I had dental phobias and low self-esteem when smiling because I was missing my two front teeth for a long time. So I decided to become a dentist to help others improve their confidence, self-esteem and image.”

Dr. Jonathan Bensoussan · Prosthodontist

Born and raised in Paris, he moved to the United States as a teenager. A traumatic dental experience in his developing years affected his smile and self-confidence, and sparked a deep interest in how advanced dental care can transform lives.
