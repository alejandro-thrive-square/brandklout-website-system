What It IncludesA comprehensive diagnosis

Everything we look at, in one visit.

A full view of your dental situation, and the treatment options that would best take care of it.

Digital and 3D x-rays

The x-rays a correct diagnosis needs

We take the necessary digital x-rays to get the information we need to make a correct diagnosis.

A cone beam CT scan, the 3D x-ray also called a dental CT scan, is included.

Thorough oral examination

Teeth, gums and bone, in full color

Dr. Bensoussan looks at the health of your teeth, gums and surrounding bone.

He takes digital photos as needed to view the teeth and gums in full color.

Smile evaluation

A preview of your new smile

Everyone wants a beautiful smile. Dr. Bensoussan can discuss with you what you'd like to improve about yours, and explain your treatment options.

He can even take photos to create a virtual simulation of how your new smile could look.

Jaw joint (TMJ) and bite evaluation

How your teeth come together

A correctly positioned bite is essential for a healthy jaw joint, comfortable chewing, and to avoid chipping or wearing down of teeth.

Dr. Bensoussan does a full bite and jaw joint evaluation and will assess if there are any issues to address.
