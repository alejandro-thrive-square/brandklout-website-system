What They AreThin shells of porcelain

Veneers are thin shells of porcelain placed over the front of your teeth. One versatile answer to chips, cracks, discoloration, worn enamel, small gaps and irregular shapes.

Is It For MeWhat veneers can fix

What porcelain veneers can fix.

  • Yellowed, stained teeth, where whitening can't get good results or needs constant redoing
  • Chipped or worn teeth: veneers cover chips and lengthen worn-down teeth
  • Misaligned, overlapping or mildly crowded teeth, visually corrected
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Irregular or unevenly shaped teeth, for a more harmonious smile
  • Teeth that appear too small

The ProcedureMeticulous, step by step

Four steps to your new smile.

01/ 04

Step 1 · Detailed planning

A virtual model of your future smile

Dr. Bensoussan starts with digital scans and photographs, and tweaks the design until you are fully satisfied.

A physical model confirms a comfortable bite, guides minimal preparation and ensures a precise fit.

Step 2 · Test driving your smile

See it on you first

A model that fits over your teeth shows your new smile directly on you.

Longer or shorter teeth, a new shape or color: Dr. Bensoussan adjusts it until you love your new smile, before anything is permanent.

Step 3 · Placement

Minimally invasive

Your teeth are prepped so the veneers adhere firmly and won't pop off, shaving off the least tooth structure possible. In some cases, no-prep veneers mean no alteration at all.

You go home with temporary veneers. Three weeks later, you receive your permanent ones.

Step 4 · Your new smile

Aesthetic and long-lasting

Front teeth get hand-layered, hand-crafted porcelain for a natural, translucent look. Hard-biting teeth get an especially strong porcelain.

In the span of a few weeks, you walk out with the smile of your dreams.
