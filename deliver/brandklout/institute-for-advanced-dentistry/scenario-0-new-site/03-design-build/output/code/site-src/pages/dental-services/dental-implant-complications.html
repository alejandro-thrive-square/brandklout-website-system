Common ProblemsWhat we see most

A problem with one implant, or several.

  • Loose or cracked prostheses (the attached teeth).
  • Loose implants.
  • Loose or broken attachments (abutments).
  • An implant that fails to integrate.
  • Peri-implantitis: infection and inflammation around the implant, similar to gum disease.

Dr. Bensoussan will examine your teeth and implants and provide a treatment plan to fix the issues.

The CausesWhere complications start

Planning, bone, or hygiene.

Generally, complications come from faulty original planning and implant surgery, insufficient bone or poor oral hygiene.

Biological

Poor oral hygiene, smoking, gum disease, systemic diseases such as diabetes, certain medications, and inadequate post-surgical care.

Structural

Insufficient bone density or volume, implant malposition, or prosthetic overload from biting forces.

Dentist

Surgical errors, improper implant selection, inadequate treatment planning, or lack of proper follow-up maintenance.

Long-term effects, if left untreated

When complications occur, they affect the implant's function, aesthetics and overall oral health. Implant failure, peri-implantitis, bone loss, gum recession, infection, nerve damage, sinus issues, fractured components and compromised bite alignment can lead to discomfort, difficulty chewing and aesthetic concerns.

Left untreated, these complications can result in the loss of the dental implant and its supporting structures, requiring additional treatments, potential additional costs, longer recovery times and further corrective procedures.

TreatmentMatched to the problem

Fortunately, there are solutions.

The approach depends on the nature and severity of the complication. Through a personalized plan, Dr. Bensoussan finds the solution that restores the health and function of your implant.

  1. Visit 1 · Free

    Find the cause

    A thorough exam of your teeth and implants, with digital and 3D x-rays, to assess what is behind the problem.

  2. Then

    Stabilize the symptoms

    Symptoms are stabilized, and a targeted plan is made to restore function, comfort and long-term stability.

  3. For peri-implantitis

    Clean, treat and graft

    Thorough cleaning of the implant surface, antimicrobial therapy and bone grafting may be recommended to eliminate infection and promote healing.

  4. For too little bone

    Bone grafting or a sinus lift

    Surgical interventions such as bone grafting or sinus lift procedures may address inadequate bone volume or density.

  5. In more severe cases

    Removal and replacement

    Implant removal and replacement may be necessary.

PreventionHow to avoid complications

Implants planned to last.

Dr. Bensoussan is a very experienced implant surgeon, and plans surgeries very carefully for a successful, long-lasting outcome.

3D guided surgery

The right position, depth and angle

During surgery, a 3D guided navigation system gives him a real-time view of your surgery.

It helps him guide each implant into its correct position, depth and angle.

The teeth, and the care after

A bite that comes together

He plans the shape and position of the teeth attached to your implant, so they come together correctly and don't cause grinding, chipping and wear.
