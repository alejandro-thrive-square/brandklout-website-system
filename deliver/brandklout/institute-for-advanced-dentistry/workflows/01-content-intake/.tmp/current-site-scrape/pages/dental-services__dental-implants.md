Book a ConsultationSee Implant OptionsWhat is a Dental Implant?A dental implant is a replacement root tooth using biocompatible titanium or zirconia that is attached to a crown, bridge, denture or a brand new set of permanent teeth. Dental implants act and feel very much like natural teeth. When you replace a tooth with a dental implant, you can feel peace of mind that you have a strong, functioning tooth once again. And, unlike a dental bridge, you don’t have to shave down and cap the adjacent teeth in order to hold the bridge in place.Your browser does not support the video tag.Our Implant Options for Teeth ReplacementDental implants are the most stable and natural tooth replacement options available. Implants are anchored in the jaw and provide a more permanent alternative to a dental bridge or dentures. Dr. Bensoussan has several implant options for replacing missing or falling teeth and will help you choose the best solution to fit your goals for your oral health.1. Precision Implant SurgeryFor efficient and predictable surgery, Dr. Bensoussan plans implant cases ahead of time using state-of-the-art 3D digital technology. He plans the surgery with the exact end in mind of how the implant and final tooth or teeth should be positioned. During surgery, Dr. Bensoussan receives real-time feedback using the X-Guide—a 3D navigation system that enables precise placement of the implant’s position, angle, and depth. This eliminates any guesswork and makes the outcome predictable.Latest Evidence-Based Treatment GuidelinesDr. Bensoussan keeps up with the latest research and technologies in implant treatment. He adopts  evidence-based techniques and technologies that will improve precision, efficiency, comfort for the patient and desirable final outcomes. This evidence-based care is present in all steps of your dental implant treatment.Faster Healing and Implant Stability with State-of-the-art Nobel Biocare Dental Implants™This includes Dr. Bensoussan’s choice of dental implants. He uses implants from one of the top implant providers, Nobel Biocare, that have a special surface on the implant that promotes integration with the jawbone, speeding up healing and increasing bone stability. This surface is also very gentle to the bone and oral cavity and lessens risk of peri-implantitis (implant gum infection). A. The collar of the implant has a smooth, non-corrosive surface, designed to be easy on your gum tissue and reduce plaque accumulation. B. The body of the implant has a textured, porous surface that makes it easier for the bone to grip and it attracts healing fluids to speed up recovery. Same-Day ImplantsIn most cases Dr. Bensoussan can extract your tooth, place a dental implant and attach a temporary tooth in the same visit.
Dr. Bensoussan can extract any teeth needing replacing and place a dental implant in the same visit. If bone is deficient and needs augmenting, he can usually place the implant and a bone graft at the same time. Patients with little-to-no available bone might have to wait for bone to grow back before placing the implant. At the end of your visit, Dr. Bensoussan attaches a temporary tooth or provides you with a snap-on tooth or teeth for the visible area of your smile, so no gaps are showing. Beautiful New Teeth and SmilesThe strength and longevity of your new teeth (crown, bridge or full set of new teeth) are based on how well the implants and the teeth are positioned, and the quality of the material used. Dr. Bensoussan does extensive planning and preparation to ensure that your implants and teeth are correctly positioned, and he uses only the highest grade porcelain, zirconia and acrylic. Dr. Bensoussan is not only a specialist in dental implant surgery and teeth replacement, he is also a specialist in esthetic dentistry, and he knows how to create a stunning smile.
