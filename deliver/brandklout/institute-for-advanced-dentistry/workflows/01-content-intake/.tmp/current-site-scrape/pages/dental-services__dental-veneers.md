Dental VeneersVeneers offer a transformative solution to perfect your smile, addressing imperfections like discoloration, chips, and gaps. Achieve a radiant and natural-looking result with custom-designed veneers that enhance your teeth's appearance and boost your confidence.Book a ConsultationSee Our Veneers ProcedureDental VeneersOur Meticulous Treatment ProcedureDetailed PlanningDr. Bensoussan starts with digital scans and photographs to create detailed virtual model of your future smile, tweaking it until you are fully satisfied. He uses a physical model to make sure the teeth will be correctly positioned for a comfortable bite. This process verifies occlusion, guides minimal preparation, and ensures precise fit before final restoration fabrication.Test Driving Your Smile Dr. Bensoussan creates a physical simulation of your future smile using a model that fits over your teeth. You can now see how your new smile will look directly on you! Dr. Bensoussan makes adjustments, such as longer or shorter teeth, change of tooth shape or color, until you love your new smile, ensuring comfort, precision, accurate bite, and final esthetic harmony before permanent placement.Minimally Invasive Placement of Your VeneersOnce you’ve approved your smile, you’re ready for your veneers. Dr. Bensoussan preps your teeth so that the veneers will adhere firmly and won’t pop off. He keeps the procedure minimally invasive, shaving off the least amount of tooth structure possible. In some cases, Dr. Bensoussan can use no-prep veneers, meaning there’s no alteration to your tooth structure. You’ll go home with temporary veneers and three weeks later you’ll receive your permanent dental veneers. Your New Smile-Aesthetic and Long-LastingDr. Bensoussan uses high-grade porcelains to craft your final dental veneers. To give your front teeth a natural, translucent look, he’ll use porcelain that is hand-layered and hand-crafted. For hard biting teeth, he’ll use an especially strong porcelain, ensuring durability, longevity, precise color matching, functional balance, and esthetic integration.In the span of a few weeks, you’ll walk out of our office with the smile of your dreams, customized for comfort and natural harmony.Free Exam, Smile Preview and ConsultationFind out more about our virtual smile design and simulation along with our expert treatment plan.
