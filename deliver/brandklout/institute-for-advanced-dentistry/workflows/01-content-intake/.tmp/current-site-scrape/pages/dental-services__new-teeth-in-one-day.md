New Teeth in One DayReplace missing or failing teeth with a complete set of fixed, natural-looking teeth, all in a single day. Dr. Bensoussan uses advanced technology and implant techniques to restore your smile quickly, comfortably, and reliably.Book a ConsultationSee How the Process WorksGetting New Teeth in One Day with Dr. BensoussanDr. Bensoussan meticulously plans the treatment so that on surgery day treatment is smooth, precise and the results are exactly what you expect. He starts with the end in mind and makes sure each step leads to a great end result for you. 1. Comprehensive ConsultationThe process starts with full exam and 3X-rays and a consultation. Dr. Bensoussan learns about your goals for your teeth and smile, explains the New Teeth in One Day procedure and answers any questions you have. 2. Meticulous Planning Gives Predictable OutcomesOnce you give the go-ahead for New Teeth in One Day, Dr. Bensoussan begins his planning. He takes 3D CT and intraoral scans, photos and measurements and plans the surgery and the final teeth placement using 3D implant-planning software. This ensures an efficient, comfortable procedure and successful outcome. 3. Planning Your Final SmileProper alignment of your teeth and bite is essential for both comfort and long-term success. Using precise measurements of your mouth, jaw, and teeth, Dr. Bensoussan creates a custom model of your new smile—carefully adjusting the positioning until it’s just right.4. 3D Navigation System For Precision SurgeryDr. Bensoussan uses the X-Guide 3D navigation system to receive real-time feedback during surgery. He syncs it with his implant-planning software and uses it to help navigate the implants into their precise placement—the position, angle and depth. This eliminates any guesswork and makes the outcome predictable. 5. Surgery DayYou can get oral conscious sedation or  IV sedation or general anesthesia with a medical anesthesiologist, depending on the length of the surgery. Either way, you will be fully relaxed throughout your surgery. In one surgical visit, Dr. Bensoussan removes all teeth, places the implants, surrounds them with a bone graft for optimum stability, and attaches your provisional teeth. 6. Walk Out With a Brand New Smile!Using the X-Guide, Dr. Bensoussan takes precise digital impressions of the mouth, jaw and implants so he can fabricate your provisional teeth. This is done in our own lab while Dr. Bensoussan is completing your  surgery. The provisional teeth will be both functional and esthetic; we give them a natural color and polish them so they have a bright sheen. 7. Your Permanent, Beautiful SmileThree months later you’ll get your permanent teeth. Dr. Bensoussan designs your teeth to be natural-looking, fit your features and to give you a beautiful smile. We make upper teeth from high-grade zirconia, which provides strength and esthetics, and generally for the bottom teeth we use a beautiful acrylic so that they can withstand the pressure and wear from biting, and won’t have to be replaced every five or ten years. He has been providing New Teeth in One Day treatment since 2016, giving patients confident smiles, strong chewing power and overall better quality of life. He uses the state-of-the-art technologies, and continually incorporates new, proven technologies to make surgery more precise, comfortable and efficient.Specialist in Implant DentistryDr. Bensoussan is a surgical prosthodontist, a dentist with three years of specialist training in dental implant surgery and treatment, treatment planning, esthetic dentistry, crowns, bridges, veneers, the TMJ (jaw joint) and oral function. In addition, he spent a year being mentored by a highly trained and experienced prosthodontist surgeon. He has placed over 2,500 dental implants and has collaborated with general dentists and other specialists on over 1,500 implant cases.
His training and experience, coupled with his use of the latest technology, mean he can help most people who need teeth replaced—no matter how complex the case—benefit from dental implant treatment and regain solid chewing and a beautiful smile.What is New Teeth in One Day?New Teeth in One Day is a procedure that uses four to five dental implants in the upper or lower jaw to hold in place a new set of teeth made from zirconia or acrylic. If there has been bone loss, Dr. Bensoussan uses implants placed on an angle to get maximum bone coverage. In rare cases of severe bone loss he might to do bone grafting ahead of time. Only a few implants are needed as the force from chewing and biting is evenly distributed between the implants. Dr. Bensoussan uses a titanium bar under the teeth to add strength, flexibility and shock absorption. Dr. Bensoussan can extract teeth, place the dental implants and attach an esthetic and functional new set of teeth, all in the same visit.
