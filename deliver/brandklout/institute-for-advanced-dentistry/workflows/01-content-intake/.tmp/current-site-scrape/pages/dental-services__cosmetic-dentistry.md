It’s deep-penetrating formula makes it one of the few systems that can tackle stubborn cases.
KöR uses a unique desensitizing protocol and gentle formulation, significantly reducing teeth sensitivity.With a combination of powerful in-office initial treatment and deep at-home whitening, you can maintain permanent whitening. For hard biting teeth, he’ll use an especially strong porcelain.
