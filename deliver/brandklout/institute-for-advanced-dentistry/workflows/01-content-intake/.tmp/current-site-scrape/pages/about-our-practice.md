About Our PracticeWith a legacy since 1954, our practice offers precision, results-based treatment for teeth replacement, smile makeovers, and oral functional rehabilitation. We provide specialist-level care, all performed in-house with state-of-the-art digital technology, to ensure optimal function, esthetics, and long-term comfort for your smile.Book a ConsultationPrecision, results-based treatment for teeth replacement, smile makeovers and oral functional rehabilitation Our practice started in 1954, the first prosthodontic office in Arizona — before dental implants even existed! Since then, our dental technology and treatments have come a long way, but our purpose remains the same — to serve the community one smile at a time.
Prosthodontics is a recognized dental specialty relating to the diagnosis, treatment planning, rehabilitation and maintenance of the oral function of patients with missing or compromised teeth. We restore function, esthetics and long-term comfort All Your Dental Work Done In-HouseDr. Bensoussan does all the planning, surgery and placement of new teeth (crowns, veneers or bridges) in our office. You will get specialist-level implant surgery,  smile makeovers and functional rehabilitation, without having to go to another office!Specialist in Complex Dental ConditionsDr. Bensoussan is extensively trained and experienced in advanced dentistry technology and state-of-the-art procedures for treating complex dental conditions and restoring optimum function and esthetics. He’ll give you an accurate diagnosis and treatment plan, and premium dental care to fully resolve your situation. To learn more about advanced treatment click here.Results-Based WarrantyWe stand behind our work with a results-based warranty for both surgical procedures and the restorations we provide, such as crowns, bridges and veneers. That means if the final outcome doesn’t meet the treatment goals we agreed on, we’ll make it right — at no extra cost to you. Our only requirement is that you follow the recommended care and maintenance plan.Premium Materials & Dental LabsWe use the highest grade porcelain or zirconia for our crowns, bridges and veneers. They are crafted to be strong, aesthetic and natural-looking.
We have our own dental lab to fabricate temporary teeth in-house, saving time and ensuring your temporaries fit precisely and look good. For your permanent teeth, we use top US dental laboratories and work with their top technicians. State-of-the-Art TechnologyOur workflow is completely digital so that we can precisely plan implant surgery and the structure and fit of crowns, bridges and veneers. It also means more efficiency, faster healing, less discomfort and predictable outcomes. Our technology includes 3D CT and intraoral scanners, live 3D guided surgery with the X-guide and virtual smile design.
