What Does “New Smile in One Day” Really Mean?
Getting a new smile in one day, or new teeth in one day, means you can replace all your failing or missing teeth with a full-arch restoration supported by dental implants—typically using an advanced method such as the All-on-4 or All-on-X implant system.
Instead of waiting months for traditional dentures or implants to heal before receiving teeth, you leave the office the same day with a secure, functional, natural-looking set of teeth.
This treatment is especially helpful for people with:

Multiple missing teeth

Loose or painful dentures

Severe decay

Broken or cracked teeth

Long-term dental issues that have affected eating or confidence

How Do You Get a New Smile in One Day in Peoria AZ?
Patients often wonder: “How is it possible to replace all my teeth in just one day?”Here’s how it works step-by-step.
1. Comprehensive Consultation & 3D Imaging
Your same-day transformation starts with a full evaluation, including 3D scans that show bone levels, gum health, and the exact condition of your teeth. This allows your dentist to create a precise plan.
2. Customized Smile Design
Your new teeth are designed to match the shape of your face, the curve of your smile, and the look you want—bright, natural, or somewhere in between.
3. Implant Placement
Using guided implant technology, your dentist places 4 to 6 implants per arch. These implants act like anchors to support your new teeth.
4. Same-Day Teeth Delivery
This is the exciting part:You leave the office with your new smile in one day—secured, stable, and ready to use.
This isn’t a temporary denture glued in. It’s a strong, fixed set of teeth attached to implants so you can chew, talk, and smile immediately.
5. Healing & Final Restoration
Once your implants fully integrate with the bone (usually a few months), you receive your final, perfectly polished, custom-crafted teeth.

What Are the Benefits of New Teeth in One Day?
A new smile in one day offers powerful advantages:
Immediate Confidence
You walk out the same day with a full smile—not months later.
Strong Chewing Power
Implant-supported teeth allow you to eat comfortably again, even crunchy or chewy foods.
A Natural, Beautiful Look
Your new smile is custom-designed to look natural and enhance your appearance.
No More Denture Slipping
Because your new teeth are secured with implants, they don’t move around or click.
Supports Long-Term Oral Health
Implants help preserve jawbone and facial structure.
Life-Changing Comfort
Patients often describe the treatment as the moment they finally feel like themselves again.

Who Is a Candidate for a Same-Day Smile?
Many people assume they aren’t eligible for same-day implants, but most patients actually are.
You’re likely a candidate if you have:

Missing teeth in one or both arches

Dentures that don’t fit well

Advanced gum disease

Extensive tooth fractures

Teeth that cannot be restored with crowns or fillings

Bone loss (which can often still be treated with All-on-4 or All-on-X techniques)

The best way to know is through a full evaluation and 3D scan.

Is Getting a New Smile in One Day Painful?
This is one of the most common questions patients ask:“Is same-day dentistry painful?”
Most patients report far less discomfort than expected. With modern sedation, gentle techniques, and precise imaging, the appointment is surprisingly smooth. Post-procedure soreness is normal for a couple of days, but most people return to work within 24–72 hours.

How Long Do Same-Day Teeth Last?
Your new teeth in one day can last decades with proper care.
Implants have a success rate of 95–98%, making them one of dentistry’s most reliable solutions. The final restoration is made from strong, long-lasting materials built to withstand daily chewing.
