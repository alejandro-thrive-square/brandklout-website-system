Biocompatible DentistryBiocompatible dentistry focuses on using non-toxic materials and holistic approaches to ensure your dental treatments support your overall health and well-being. This approach prioritizes your body's natural systems, offering safer and healthier alternatives for restorations and procedures, while reducing sensitivities, minimizing risks, and promoting balance between oral health and overall wellness.Book a ConsultationNon-toxic materials for better oral healthThe materials you put in your body can make a difference to your oral and systemic health. Biocompatible Dentistry ensures that at all stages of treatment we are using non-toxic, safe, and carefully selected materials to support lasting wellness.No Metal FillingsTraditional amalgam fillings contain mercury, silver, tin, copper, and zinc. Some of these metals can cause allergic reactions or sensitivities, and mercury can be released as a toxic vapor. Dr. Bensoussan only uses safe, biocompatible, tooth-colored material to fill a tooth, ensuring natural appearance, comfort, long-term protection, and a healthier alternative for patients seeking modern dental care without exposure to harmful metals.Safe Amalgam Filling RemovalDr. Bensoussan provides amalgam filling removal using a rubber dam to isolate the patient from mercury vapor and powerful suction to quickly remove the vapor and amalgam particles. He follows strict safety protocols during every procedure to protect patients, reduce exposure risks, and ensure a smooth, efficient transition to healthier restorative materials.FREE Comprehensive Exam, CBCT & Smile Design PreviewDiscover what it’s like to have your treatment handled start-to-finish by a specialist — not bounced between providers.

Metal-Free Crowns & BridgesVarious metals can be used in crowns that are fused to metal, including gold, nickel, chromium, and cobalt. These metals can be a source of allergic reactions, especially nickel. Nickel is commonly used to fill crowns due to its affordability.With biocompatible dentistry Dr. Bensoussan fabricates strong, all-porcelain or all-ceramic crowns and bridges, carefully crafted to ensure comfort, function, and aesthetics without the risks associated with traditional metal-based options. These restorations are highly durable, stain-resistant, and designed to blend seamlessly with natural teeth, offering patients a safer, healthier alternative that maintains strength while avoiding complications often linked to metal crowns.Zirconia Dental ImplantsWe offer titanium and zirconia dental implants. Titanium is itself considered a biocompatible metal and rarely causes adverse reactions. Some patients opt for zirconia implants, a strong, fully biocompatible ceramic alternative that provides excellent durability, natural aesthetics, and an option for those wishing to avoid any type of metal. Both implant types are carefully selected to suit individual needs, offering reliable function, long-term stability, and peace of mind for patients seeking safe, modern solutions for missing teeth replacement.BPA-Free Office BPA (bisphenol A) is a chemical compound used in plastics. Studies have shown that BPA can leach out and cause health effects such as hormone disruption. Some dental materials contain derivatives of BPA that can leach out over time into the oral cavity.All materials in our office are completely BPA-free, even our temporary crowns and bridges, which are made in our own dental lab. This commitment reflects our philosophy of biocompatible dentistry, ensuring safer, non-toxic choices that protect both your oral health and overall wellness, while promoting long-term harmony between advanced dental care and your body’s natural systems.Real Patients, Real Results“I value that Dr. Bensoussan gives me options for treatment and spends a lot of time with me.
