Payment Options
Ensuring your access to quality dental care is a top priority, which is why we offer a variety of flexible payment options to fit your budget. Our friendly staff is here to help you navigate the process, making your desired treatment achievable and stress-free.Book a ConsultationThird-Party Financing Optionsand more...Our friendly staff will help you get the treatment you needCash, check and all major credit cards are accepted. Affordable third-party financing is available through Alphaeon® Credit, Proceed Finance, CareCredit and other third-party lenders. We are not in network with any dental insurance but we will bill your insurance provider on your behalf to request reimbursement. Our staff handles this process so you don’t need to worry about paperwork or dealing with insurance companies. Our treatment coordinator can also help you explore other payment options, including Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC), credit union or personal bank loan, retirement plan loan—401(k) or 403(b) and Flexible Spending Account (FSA).
