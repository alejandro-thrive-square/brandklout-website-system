Meet Dr. BensoussanDr. Bensoussan is a prosthodontist, a dental specialist who has completed dental school plus three additional years of advanced training.Dr. Bensoussan is expert in cosmetic dentistry, dental implant surgery, implant-supported teeth, crowns, bridges, dentures, TMJ/TMD disorders and more. His advanced prosthodontics and implant treatment training enables him to treat complex dental issues.He is dedicated to continuing education and stays informed on the latest scientific and technological advancements. He uses the latest dental technologies in his diagnosis and treatment, and is always adding new, proven technologies to further increase precision, efficiency and comfort. Patients appreciate his calm, thoughtful approach and the way he clearly explains each step of treatment so they feel confident and at ease. Why Dr. Bensoussan became a dentistDr. Jonathan Bensoussan was born and raised in Paris and moved to the United States as a teenager. In his developing years, he had a traumatic dental experience that affected his smile and self-confidence. This early challenge sparked a deep interest in dentistry at a young age, inspiring him to explore how advanced dental care could transform lives. His journey ultimately led him to pursue the passion he has today for helping patients restore their smiles, regain confidence, and experience the life-changing benefits of modern dentistry delivered with compassion and precision.“I became a dentist because I had dental phobias and low self-esteem when smiling because I was missing my two front teeth for a long time. So I decided to become a dentist to help others improve their confidence, self-esteem and image.”
