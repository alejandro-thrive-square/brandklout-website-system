Do you have various dental issues and you’d like a correct diagnosis and treatment plan? We can help you! Dr. Bensoussan is a prosthodontist, a dental specialty pertaining to the diagnosis, treatment planning and rehabilitation of oral function and the appearance of teeth. He is especially qualified to resolve your dental issues and return your teeth and smile back to full health and beauty. Tools Dr. Bensoussan Uses for a Full-Mouth RehabilitationAdvanced Diagnostic ToolsDr. Bensoussan uses advanced diagnostics like X-rays and 3D scans to plan your treatment with precision. Based on your needs, he may recommend dental implants, crowns, bridges, veneers, onlays (partial crowns), or even gum recontouring, all carefully chosen to restore both function and aesthetics.
Correcting the BiteAn essential element in your dental rehabilitation is putting your jaw into a neutral position where it is not causing problems for your teeth and jaw joint. A bad bite is a frequent culprit for multiple oral issues. Using models of your teeth, Dr. Bensoussan focuses on getting your bite correct so that you won’t have repeat issues.
