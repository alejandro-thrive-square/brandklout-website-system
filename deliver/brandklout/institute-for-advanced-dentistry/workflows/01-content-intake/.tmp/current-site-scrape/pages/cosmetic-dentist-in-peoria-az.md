What Does a Cosmetic Dentist in Peoria, AZ Do?
A cosmetic dentist focuses on the appearance and harmony of your smile while maintaining optimal oral health. From subtle improvements to full transformations, cosmetic dentistry blends precision and creativity.

What Are the Most Popular Cosmetic Dentistry Treatments?
Because every smile is unique, cosmetic dentistry offers a wide range of personalized options:
1. Professional Teeth Whitening
Stains from coffee, tea, or aging can make your smile appear dull. Professional whitening from a cosmetic dentist in Peoria AZ safely brightens your teeth by several shades in just one visit or with take-home kits designed for comfort and long-lasting brilliance.
2. Porcelain Veneers
Veneers are ultra-thin shells of porcelain placed over the front of teeth to mask discoloration, chips, or uneven spacing. Dr. Bensoussan custom-designs veneers that complement your facial features for a flawless, lifelike result.
3. Dental Bonding
For minor chips or small gaps, tooth-colored composite bonding offers a fast, affordable fix. It’s a favorite among patients looking for quick improvements from their cosmetic dentist in Peoria AZ without extensive treatment.
4. Smile Makeovers
If you want to address several concerns at once, a smile makeover combines multiple procedures into one cohesive plan — giving you a balanced, confident, and photo-ready smile.
5. Gum Contouring
An uneven gumline can make teeth look shorter. With gentle laser technology, your cosmetic dentist reshapes the gums for better symmetry and a brighter overall appearance.

Why Choose a Cosmetic Dentist Instead of a General Dentist?
While general dentists can perform some aesthetic procedures, a cosmetic dentist has advanced expertise in dental artistry, materials, and facial balance. This combination of science and design ensures your results don’t just look good — they fit your face naturally.
At the Institute for Advanced Dentistry, every smile begins with a digital preview, allowing you to see potential results before treatment even starts.

How Long Do Cosmetic Dental Treatments Last?
Durability depends on the procedure and your care routine:

Whitening results can last up to three years with touch-ups.

Porcelain veneers and ceramic crowns often last over a decade.

Bonding may need refreshing after several years.

Your cosmetic dentist will provide personalized care instructions to help your results last as long as possible.

Can Cosmetic Dentistry Improve Oral Health?
Yes — cosmetic dentistry doesn’t just enhance appearance; it can improve bite alignment, strengthen teeth, and make cleaning easier. Treatments like veneers and crowns restore structure, while gum contouring can eliminate hard-to-clean areas. A healthy foundation always supports a beautiful smile.
