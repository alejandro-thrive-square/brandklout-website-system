What’s a Crown Anyway?


## Why Crowns Are Awesome


Porcelain crowns are tooth-shaped caps that are placed over damaged or decayed teeth to restore their shape, size, strength, and appearance. They are made from high-quality porcelain, which mimics the natural translucency of teeth, making them an ideal choice for a natural-looking restoration. Whether you need to protect a weakened tooth, cover a discolored tooth, or support a tooth with a large filling, porcelain crowns provide a versatile and effective solution.

Strengthening Teeth: To cover and protect teeth that have been severely weakened by large cavities or extensive decay.

Post-Root Canal and Implants: To protect teeth after root canals and to attach to dental implants for replacing missing teeth.

Aesthetic and Functional Improvements: Enhance the color, shape, and alignment of teeth, and correct bite issues.

## Getting a Porcelain Crown is a Straightforward and Stress-Free Experience


At Oceanic Dental, getting a porcelain crown is a simple and stress-free experience. Our dental experts ensure that every step is tailored to your specific needs, providing you with a high-quality crown that's durable and natural-looking, restoring your smile to its best.


### Free Consultation & Personalized Planning


Start with a complimentary consultation where you'll meet our friendly doctors, have photos taken, and discuss your dental goals. We’ll evaluate your teeth, explore treatment options, and answer all your questions. With detailed X-rays, 3D oral scans, and precise planning, we ensure your crown will be a perfect fit.


### Preparation and Custom Crafting


We carefully prepare your tooth by removing any decay and shaping it for the crown. An impression is then taken to create your custom crown. You'll receive a temporary crown while the permanent one is crafted.


### Placement and Final Adjustments


Once your permanent crown is ready, we will schedule an appointment for its placement. Our dentists will ensure a precise fit, making any necessary adjustments to ensure your comfort and bite alignment. This process ensures you receive a high-quality porcelain crown with minimal hassle, restoring your smile to its best.

## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about dental crowns.

How long do porcelain crowns last?

Porcelain crowns typically last between 10 to 15 years with proper care. Regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene can help extend their lifespan.

Does getting a crown hurt?

The process is usually pain-free as we use local anesthesia to numb the area. You might experience mild discomfort after the procedure, but it typically subsides within a few days.

How much does a crown cost?

The cost of a high-quality porcelain crown typically ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 per tooth, depending on the complexity of the case and the materials used. Each case is unique, and we’ll provide you with a personalized estimate during your consultation. Flexible payment options are available to help manage the cost.

What is the difference between a crown and a veneer?

A crown covers the entire tooth and is often used for structural repairs, while a veneer is a thin layer that only covers the front surface of the tooth to improve appearance.

Can crowns be whitened?

No, crowns are made from materials that do not respond to traditional whitening treatments. If you’re considering whitening, it's best to do so before getting a crown so the crown can be matched to your desired shade.
