## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about our new patient exam process.

What happens during a new patient exam?

During a new patient exam, we conduct a thorough assessment of your oral health, including a detailed review of your medical and dental history. This exam typically includes X-rays, Intraoral scans, a teeth cleaning, and an evaluation of your gums, teeth, and bite. We will also discuss any concerns or dental goals you may have.

How long does a new patient exam take?

A new patient exam usually takes between 60 to 90 minutes, depending on your individual needs and whether any additional procedures (like additional X-rays, oral screenings, or cleanings) are performed during the visit.

Do I need to bring anything to my first appointment?

Yes, please bring a valid ID, your dental insurance card (if applicable), and a list of any medications you are currently taking. If you have previous dental records or X-rays, it can be helpful to bring them as well.

Will my insurance cover the new patient exam?

Most dental insurance plans cover the cost of a new patient exam, including X-rays and cleanings, as part of preventive care. We can help verify your insurance coverage before your visit and provide any details regarding your benefits.

What if I need additional treatment after the exam?

If your exam reveals the need for additional treatment (such as fillings, crowns, or other restorative procedures), we will provide a detailed treatment plan and explain your options. We can also schedule follow-up appointments to complete the necessary treatments.
