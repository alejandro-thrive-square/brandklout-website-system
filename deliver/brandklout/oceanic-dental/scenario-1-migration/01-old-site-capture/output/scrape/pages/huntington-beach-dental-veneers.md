## What Are Dental Veneers?



### Beautiful and Natural-Looking Smile with Dental Veneers


Dental veneers are thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth, offering a natural-looking solution to enhance your smile. These cosmetic restorations can completely transform your appearance and boost your confidence. Our experienced dentists provide fast and lasting smile makeovers, addressing a variety of imperfections with veneers.

Discolored or Stained/Yellow Teeth: Brighten your smile by covering stains or discoloration.

Gaps and Chipped Teeth: Achieve a smooth, even look by closing gaps and repairing chips.

Mildly Crooked or Misshapen Teeth: Create a more uniform appearance by aligning and reshaping teeth.

Transformative Benefits of Dental Veneers


## Benefits of Dental Veneers


Dental veneers offer a range of benefits that can enhance both the appearance and functionality of your teeth. By improving the aesthetics of your teeth, veneers can significantly boost your confidence and overall self-esteem.


### Natural Appearance


Veneers are designed to look just like natural teeth, providing a seamless blend with your existing smile.


### Durability


With proper care, porcelain veneers can last over a decade, offering a long-term solution to various dental imperfections.


### Stain Resistance


Unlike natural teeth, porcelain veneers are highly resistant to stains from coffee, tea, and other common culprits, helping you maintain a bright smile.


### Versatility


Veneers can address a wide range of cosmetic issues, including discoloration, gaps, chips, and misaligned teeth, giving you a uniform and attractive smile.

## No-Prep Veneers in Huntington Beach



### Veneers Without the Drills


Traditional veneers often require shaving enamel to make room for porcelain — a step many patients prefer to avoid.

For selected smiles, Dr. Krystal Pham offers true no-prep veneers: custom, additive porcelain placed without intentional enamel reduction, designed to look refined rather than overdone.

With UCSF training, AACD involvement, and 20+ years in cosmetic and family care, Dr. Pham prioritizes candidacy honesty over a one-size-fits-all pitch. You’ll get a careful smile evaluation — and clear guidance when no-prep is (or isn’t) the right path — so the result stays natural-looking and conservative.

Superior Materials: We use E-max veneers, which are thinner, stronger, and more durable than traditional veneers. They resist chips, fractures, and stains, ensuring your smile stays beautiful longer.

Expert Solutions for Dark Teeth: For very dark teeth, we use Zirconia covered in clear porcelain to effectively mask discoloration while maintaining a natural look.

Expertise: Our lead dentist, Dr. Pham is an expert in cosmetic dentistry. She is a member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry and taught at UCSF School of Dentistry which is widely regarded as a top dental school.

## Getting Dental Veneers is Straighforward: The Process at Oceanic Dental



###### Free Consultation


Start with a free consultation to meet with our doctors, have photos taken, and discuss your smile goals. We’ll evaluate your smile, review treatment options, and answer all your questions to ensure you feel confident.


###### Personalized Planning


We use X-rays and 3D oral scans to understand your preferences. Using specialized templates and custom shades from our cosmetic lab, we plan your veneers. If needed, we’ll create a wax-up model of your new smile for your review.


###### Preparation & Preview


We gently remove a small amount of enamel and apply temporary veneers while your permanent ones are crafted. This step ensures comfort and lets you preview your new smile.


###### Placement


Your veneers are custom-crafted in our lab to match the exact shape and shade of your teeth. We bond the veneers to your teeth and make any necessary adjustments for a perfect fit and comfortable bite to ensure you’re delighted with your new smile.


## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about dental veneers in Huntington Beach.

What are dental veneers made of?

Veneers can be made from porcelain (most durable and stain-resistant) or composite resin (more affordable, completed faster). We’ll help you choose what’s right for your needs and budget.

How long do veneers last?

Porcelain veneers typically last 10 to 15 years, or longer with proper care. Maintaining good oral hygiene and regular dental checkups can help extend their lifespan.

Are veneers painful to get?

Not at all. We use gentle numbing when needed, and the procedure is minimally invasive. Most patients feel little to no discomfort during and after the process.

Will my veneers look fake?

Not with us. We customize the shape, size, and shade to match your facial features and preferences. Our goal is a beautiful smile that still looks naturally like you.

Do veneers damage your natural teeth?

We remove a small amount of enamel to place porcelain veneers, but it’s very conservative. Your natural teeth remain strong and protected underneath.

Can I get veneers on just one tooth?

Absolutely. While many people get 4–10 veneers for a full smile makeover, we can also treat just one or two teeth to match the rest of your smile.

Are veneers covered by insurance?

Veneers are considered cosmetic, so they’re typically not covered. We offer flexible financing and payment plans to help make treatment accessible.

How much do veneers cost?

The cost of porcelain veneers varies based on the number of veneers needed and the complexity of the case. On average, veneers range from $900 to $2,500 per tooth. We’ll provide a detailed cost estimate during your consultation, and flexible payment options are available.

What’s the difference between porcelain and composite veneers?

Porcelain is more durable and stain-resistant, with a more natural translucency. Composite is more budget-friendly and done in fewer visits, but may stain or chip more easily over time.

How do I get started?

Just book a consultation online or call our Huntington Beach office. We’ll listen to your goals, take digital scans, and guide you through your personalized veneer options.
