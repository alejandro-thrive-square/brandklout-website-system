# All-on-X Solutions: Everything You Need to Know


If you’ve lost most or all of your teeth, you know the struggle: slipping dentures, avoiding favorite foods, or covering your mouth when you smile. These challenges aren’t just physical — they can impact your confidence, social life, and overall well-being.

‍All-on-X dental implants offer a permanent, stable, and natural-looking solution. By placing just a few implants in strategic positions, we can anchor a full arch of teeth that look, feel, and function like your own. Many patients walk in with failing or missing teeth and walk out the same day with a brand-new smile.

This guide explains everything you need to know — from what All-on-X is, to who it’s right for, the treatment journey, and how to keep your new smile healthy for years.

By

Oceanic Dental

August 17, 2025


# What Is All-on-X?


“All-on-X” refers to replacing a full arch of teeth (upper, lower, or both) with a fixed bridge supported by a small number of implants. The “X” stands for the number of implants — usually four to six per arch, depending on bone quality and anatomy.

The implants act like artificial tooth roots, anchoring a bridge of natural-looking teeth. This creates a stable, long-lasting foundation, unlike traditional dentures that sit loosely on the gums.


## Why All-on-X Is Changing Lives


Dentures can feel loose, limit your diet, and affect your confidence. All-on-X offers a secure, fixed alternative that feels like natural teeth — often placed in a single day.

Patients tell us the difference is “night and day”: suddenly eating steak, smiling in photos, and laughing without worry.


## ‍Why Patients Choose All-on-X Over Traditional Dentures


Compared to removable dentures, All-on-X offers life-changing advantages:

No slipping or clicking – teeth stay firmly in place when speaking or eating.

Stronger bite – enjoy steak, apples, corn on the cob, and crunchy veggies again.

Bone preservation – implants stimulate the jawbone, slowing or preventing bone loss.

Natural appearance – custom bridges are shaped and shaded to complement your face.

Confidence boost – smile, laugh, and socialize without fear of dentures moving.

Convenience – no messy adhesives, no nightly soaking — they stay in permanently.


## Who Is a Good Candidate?


All-on-X may be right for you if you:

Are missing most or all teeth in one or both arches

Have adequate jawbone density (or are open to grafting if needed)

Are looking for a fixed, non-removable solution

Are in good general health for oral surgery

Are committed to maintaining excellent oral hygiene

Tip: Even if you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone for implants, advanced techniques like angled implants or zygomatic implants may still make treatment possible.


## The Step-by-Step Process



### 1. Comprehensive Consultation


Your journey starts with a detailed exam, 3D scans, and a conversation about your goals. We’ll design a personalized treatment plan and preview what your new smile could look like.


### 2. Digital Planning


Using advanced software, we map out the exact placement of each implant to maximize strength, minimize surgical risk, and achieve the best long-term result.


### 3. Surgery Day — New Smile in One Visit


On the day of your procedure:

Implants are placed in the jaw.

A temporary but fixed bridge is attached, so you leave the office with teeth the same day.


### 4. Healing & Integration (3–6 months)


The implants gradually fuse with the bone (osseointegration). During this time, you’ll function comfortably with your temporary bridge.


### 5. Final Restoration


Once healing is complete, we replace your temporary bridge with a custom, permanent bridge crafted for your bite, comfort, and appearance.


## What to Expect During Recovery


Day 1–3: Mild swelling, bruising, or soreness — managed with medication and rest.

Week 1–2: Soft diet (soups, yogurt, scrambled eggs). Most patients return to work in a few days.

Month 1–3: Gradual transition to more foods as healing progresses.

Month 3–6: Implants fully integrate. You’ll return for your permanent restoration.

Lifestyle Tip: Think of this as a marathon, not a sprint. The temporary bridge is a stepping stone — the final prosthetic is where the artistry shines.


## How Much Does All-on-X Cost?


It’s natural to wonder about cost — All-on-X is a significant investment, and being well-informed helps you plan with confidence.

In Southern California, the average cost for a full arch (upper or lower) ranges between $20,000–$35,000 per arch, depending on several factors:

Number of implants placed (usually 4–6 per arch)

Type of prosthetic teeth used (acrylic vs. zirconia — zirconia is more durable and natural-looking but also more expensive)

Additional procedures (such as bone grafting, sinus lifts, or extractions)

Technology and materials (digital planning, 3D imaging, premium implant systems)

Experience of the provider (specialists and advanced teams often charge more, but results are typically more predictable and longer-lasting).

For both arches (full mouth), patients can typically expect a total investment between $40,000–$70,000 in Southern California.


### Insurance & Financing


Unfortunately, most dental insurance plans do not cover the full cost of All-on-X. However, portions of the treatment (such as extractions, bone grafting, or imaging) may be partially covered. It’s always worth having our team submit a pre-authorization so you know what to expect.

Because of the investment, many patients use flexible financing options to make treatment more manageable. Programs such as CareCredit or LendingClub allow you to spread payments out monthly, sometimes with promotional interest-free periods. At Oceanic, we also work with patients individually to explore additional in-house financing options that fit their budget.


### A Helpful Perspective


While the cost may feel daunting, many patients find All-on-X to be more cost-effective in the long run compared to the cycle of dentures, adhesives, relines, and the health consequences of missing teeth. It’s not just about teeth — it’s about regaining the ability to eat freely, smile without worry, and prevent further bone loss that can complicate treatment later.


## Aftercare: Protecting Your Investment


Caring for All-on-X implants is essential for long-term success:

Brush twice daily with a soft toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste.

Use a water flosser or special threaders to clean under the bridge.

Maintain regular dental checkups every 6 months for professional cleaning.

Avoid chewing ice, hard candy, or overly sticky foods.

If you grind your teeth, wear a nightguard to protect both implants and bridge.

Red Flags to Watch For:

Persistent swelling or bleeding

Looseness in your bridge

Gum soreness or unusual odor

If you notice any of these, call your dentist immediately. Early attention prevents long-term complications.


## Alternatives to Consider


All-on-X is powerful, but it’s not the only solution:

Traditional dentures – lower cost, but less stable and can accelerate bone loss.

Implant-supported dentures – removable, but more stable than conventional dentures.

Multiple single implants with crowns/bridges – great for replacing a few teeth, but costly for full-arch cases.


## A Final Note From Oceanic Dental


All-on-X is more than just a dental procedure — it’s about regaining the freedom to eat, laugh, and live without hesitation. With careful planning, skilled execution, and dedicated aftercare, it can give you a complete smile that lasts for decades.

At Oceanic Dental, we know this is more than just a dental procedure — it’s an important investment in freedom, confidence, and comfort in your daily life. That’s why we guide you through every stage, from your very first question to years after your treatment.
