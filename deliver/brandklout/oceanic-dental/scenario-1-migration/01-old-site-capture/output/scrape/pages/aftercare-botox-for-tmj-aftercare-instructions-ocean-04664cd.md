# Botox for TMJ Aftercare Instructions – Oceanic Dental


Thank you for choosing Oceanic Dental for your TMJ care. Botox treatment for temporomandibular joint (TMJ) discomfort can help reduce jaw tension, headaches, and teeth grinding. Botox treatment is quick and minimally invasive, but proper aftercare helps ensure the best, longest-lasting results. Below is everything you need to know—simple, clear, and created to support you through a smooth, confident recovery.


## 1. Immediate Post-Treatment Instructions



### What to Expect:


Mild tenderness or tiny bumps at injection sites

Slight swelling or redness

A “heavy” or tired feeling in the treated muscles

Gradual relief of TMJ symptoms over 3–10 days

These are very normal and typically resolve quickly.


### Do’s and Don’ts for the First 24 Hours:


Do’s

✔ Stay upright for at least 4 hours

✔ Use gentle facial movement throughout the day (light chewing, soft speaking, natural expressions)

✔ Apply cool compresses for comfort (15 minutes on / 15 off as needed)

✔ Use acetaminophen if needed for discomfort

Don’ts

✖ Do not touch, rub, or massage injection areas

✖ Avoid tight hats or face-down positions (massage beds, naps on your stomach)

✖ Avoid makeup on treated areas for 12 hours

✖ Avoid strenuous exercise

✖ Avoid lying completely flat for the first few hours

Why it matters: Botox needs time to settle into the targeted muscles. Pressure, heat, or vigorous activity can move the product to unintended areas.


## 2. Ongoing Care & Maintenance



### What to Avoid for 1–2 Days:


Strenuous exercise or heavy lifting

Hot yoga, saunas, steam rooms, hot tubs

Long hot showers directly on the face

Alcohol or blood-thinning medications (unless prescribed)

Heat + increased blood flow can reduce Botox effectiveness or contribute to bruising.


### How to Support Results:


Maintain a consistent TMJ-friendly routine:

Choose soft foods if the jaw feels tender

Avoid gum chewing, hard foods, or extreme jaw movements

Consider a nightguard if you grind your teeth – our team can help with custom options

Return for touch-up treatments every 3–4 months to maintain results


### When Results Start


Early improvements: 3–5 days

Full relief: 10–14 days

Duration: 3–4 months, sometimes longer with consistent treatment


## 3. Recognizing Complications & When to Contact Oceanic Dental



### Although complications are rare, please reach out if you experience:


Persistent pain, swelling, or soreness beyond a few days

Difficulty swallowing, speaking, or smiling

Signs of infection (redness, warmth, discharge at the site)

Headache that worsens over time or doesn’t respond to OTC medication

Any facial asymmetry that feels new or concerning


## 4. Recovery Tips & Best Practices



### Quick Recovery Tips:


Sleep with your head slightly elevated the first night

Stay hydrated and stick to soft foods if jaw is sore

Avoid alcohol (reduces inflammation and bruising risk)

Use SPF daily—sun exposure can worsen bruising

Keep a treatment journal to track when results kick in and how long they last(helps fine-tune your dose)

Remember: Botox works gradually. Many patients feel noticeably lighter, looser jaw muscles each day during the first week.


## 5. Follow-Up & Long-Term Care


Most patients benefit from routine Botox treatments every 3–4 months to maintain jaw comfort and minimize clenching and grinding.

At each visit, we’ll:

Evaluate your jaw muscles

Assess your progress

Adjust your dose for optimal relief

Review habits or tools (nightguards, stress management, etc.) that support long-term wellness
