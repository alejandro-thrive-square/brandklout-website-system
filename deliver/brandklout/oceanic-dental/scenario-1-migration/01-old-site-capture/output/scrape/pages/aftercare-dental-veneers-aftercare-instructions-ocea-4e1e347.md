# Dental Veneers Aftercare Instructions – Oceanic Dental


Thank you for choosing Oceanic Dental to enhance your smile. Whether you received a single veneer or a full smile makeover at our Huntington Beach or Fountain Valley office, this aftercare guide will help you protect your investment and enjoy long-lasting, beautiful results.


### 1. Immediate Post-Treatment Instructions



### What to Expect:


Mild soreness or gum sensitivity around the treated teeth

Temporary tooth sensitivity to hot, cold, or pressure, especially with multiple veneers

Numbness from local anesthesia that may last a few hours


### Do’s and Don’ts for the First 24–48 Hours:


Do avoid eating until the numbness wears off completely

Do eat soft, non-staining foods (e.g., yogurt, pasta, eggs)

Don’t chew on hard or sticky foods (ice, nuts, gum)

Don’t drink deeply pigmented liquids like coffee, tea, or red wine (especially during the first 48 hours)


### If You Received Multiple Veneers:


Expect mild tightness or an adjusting sensation as your bite adapts

Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and rinse gently with warm salt water to soothe the gums


## 2. Ongoing Care & Maintenance



### Oral Hygiene Routine:


Brush at least twice a day using a soft-bristled toothbrush

Floss daily, using a gentle technique to avoid tugging around the veneer edges

Use a non-abrasive, fluoride toothpaste to prevent scratches


### Protecting Your Veneers:


Wear a nightguard if you clench or grind your teeth

Avoid using your teeth to open packages or bite hard objects

Minimize staining by rinsing after coffee, wine, or berries


### Professional Cleanings:


Schedule cleanings every 6 months to maintain gum and veneer health

Inform your hygienist that you have veneers to ensure appropriate polishing tools are used


## 3. Recognizing Complications & When to Contact Oceanic Dental



### Please contact us if you experience:


Veneers that feel loose, uneven, or uncomfortable

Persistent gum irritation or swelling around the veneers

Chips, cracks, or discoloration in the veneer

Bite that feels “off” or causes jaw soreness after a few days


## 4. Recovery Tips & Best Practices



### Tips for Long-Term Beauty and Comfort:


Rinse with water after meals to keep veneers clean and stain-free

Keep up with excellent brushing and flossing habits

Use a straw when drinking colored beverages to minimize contact with veneers

Helpful Reminder:"Veneers are strong, but your care keeps them stunning."


## 5. Follow-Up & Contact Information


We may schedule a follow-up visit within 1–2 weeks to ensure your veneers feel and function perfectly. This is especially important for full smile transformations.
