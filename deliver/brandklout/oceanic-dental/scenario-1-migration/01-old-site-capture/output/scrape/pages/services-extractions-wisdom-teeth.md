# Tooth Extractions &Wisdom Teeth Removal


At Oceanic Dental, we prioritize saving your natural teeth whenever possible. Our experienced dentists will attempt to repair a damaged or decayed tooth with a filling, crown, or other treatments. However, when a tooth is beyond repair, a gentle extraction may be necessary.


### Gentle Tooth Extractions for Fast Healing


Our dental surgeon, with over 30 years of experience, performs tooth extractions with precision and care, ensuring your comfort throughout the process.

Whether due to severe decay, damage, or overcrowding, our experienced dental team uses the latest techniques to make extractions as smooth and painless as possible.

We focus on minimizing discomfort and promoting quick healing to help you return to your daily activities as soon as possible.


### Socket Preservation and Bone Grafting


Following an extraction, socket preservation is crucial to maintain the health and structure of your jawbone​.

Socket Preservation: To prevent bone loss and speed up healing after an extraction, we perform socket preservation. This involves filling the empty socket with bone grafting material or a collagen plug. This step helps prevent bone loss, supports the surrounding teeth, and prepares the site for potential future dental implants​.

Benefits of Bone Grafting: Bone grafting stabilizes the jaw and maintains facial structure. It reduces the risk of complications like dry socket and enhances the overall healing process​


### Gentle Wisdom Tooth Removal


Wisdom teeth, also known as third molars, typically show up in the late teens or early twenties. If they get stuck, blocked by other teeth, they can really be a pain and cause some trouble for your jaw and surrounding teeth.

Our friendly and skilled team uses the latest extraction techniques and advanced technology to carefully remove wisdom teeth, making sure to keep you comfortable afterwards and promote quick healing. We're here to provide a worry-free experience, and to help you keep your oral health in top shape.


## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about tooth extractions.

Why do I need a tooth extraction?

A tooth extraction may be necessary if the tooth is severely decayed, damaged, or infected beyond repair. It's also recommended if there's overcrowding or if a tooth is at risk of causing further dental issues. Wisdom teeth, in particular, are often removed if they are impacted or causing pain.

Is a tooth extraction painful?

The procedure itself is not painful, as local anesthesia is used to numb the area. You may experience some discomfort or swelling after the procedure, but this can be managed with pain medication and proper aftercare. The discomfort usually subsides within a few days.

How long does it take to recover from a tooth extraction?

Recovery generally takes about 1-2 weeks, during which time you may experience some swelling, bruising, and mild pain. It’s important to follow your dentist’s post-op instructions, including avoiding certain foods and maintaining oral hygiene, to ensure a smooth recovery.

When should wisdom teeth be removed?

Wisdom teeth are often removed between the ages of 17 and 25, especially if they are impacted, causing pain, or risking future dental problems. Your dentist will take X-rays to determine if removal is necessary before problems arise.

What are the risks of not removing impacted wisdom teeth?

Impacted wisdom teeth can lead to infection, cyst formation, damage to surrounding teeth, and increased risk of decay and gum disease. If they don't have enough space to emerge properly, it’s often recommended to remove them to avoid future complications.
