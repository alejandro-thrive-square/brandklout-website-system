## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about full-mouth reconstruction.

What is full mouth reconstruction?

Full mouth reconstruction involves a combination of restorative and cosmetic procedures to rebuild or replace all teeth in your mouth. It improves both the function and appearance of your smile, addressing issues like damaged, decayed, or missing teeth.

Who is a candidate for full mouth restoration?

Candidates typically have multiple oral health problems, such as significant tooth loss, extensive decay, severe wear, or jaw issues. Full mouth restoration is also ideal for those with genetic conditions or those who've experienced traumatic damage to their teeth.

How long does full mouth reconstruction take?

The process can take several months, depending on the complexity of your case. This includes time for planning, healing (especially if implants are involved), and performing the necessary procedures. Your dentist will provide a timeline during your consultation.

How much does full mouth reconstruction cost?

The cost varies significantly based on the procedures needed, ranging from $20,000 to $60,000, and in some cases $100,000 or more. Factors such as dental implants, crowns, and orthodontic work can influence the total cost. A detailed estimate will be provided after a thorough evaluation.

Will I need to visit multiple specialists for full mouth reconstruction?

In many cases, a team of dental specialists, such as prosthodontists, periodontists, and orthodontists, may collaborate on your treatment plan. At Oceanic Dental, however, we offer all the necessary expertise under one roof, streamlining the process for you.
