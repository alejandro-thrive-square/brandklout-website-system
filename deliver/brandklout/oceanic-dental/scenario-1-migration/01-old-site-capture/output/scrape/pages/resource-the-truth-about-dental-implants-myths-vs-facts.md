# The Truth About Dental Implants — Myths vs. Facts


Dental implants are one of the most reliable, life-changing solutions in modern dentistry — yet misinformation can make patients hesitant. Let’s separate fact from fiction so you can make a confident decision.

By

Dr. Krystal Pham, DDS

September 11, 2025


## Dental Implants: Common Myths, Debunked



### Myth 1: Dental Implants Are Painful


Fact: With modern anesthesia and sedation, most patients report little to no pain during the procedure — often less discomfort than a tooth extraction. Mild soreness afterward is easily managed with OTC medication.


### Myth 2: Implants Don’t Last


Fact: With proper care, implants have a 95–98% success rate and can last decades, even a lifetime. Compare that to bridges or dentures, which usually need replacement every 5–10 years.


### Myth 3: They Look Fake


Fact: Implant restorations are custom-designed to match your smile in color, size, and shape. Most patients say friends and family can’t tell the difference.


### Myth 4: Implants Are Too Expensive


Fact: While the upfront cost is higher, implants are cost-effective long term. They prevent bone loss, preserve oral health, and avoid the cycle of replacing dentures or bridges. In SoCal, expect $3,500–$6,000 per implant (including crown). Financing makes it manageable.


### Myth 5: Implants Aren’t Safe for Older Adults


Fact: Age is not a barrier. What matters is bone health and medical stability. Many patients in their 60s, 70s, and even 80s successfully receive implants.
