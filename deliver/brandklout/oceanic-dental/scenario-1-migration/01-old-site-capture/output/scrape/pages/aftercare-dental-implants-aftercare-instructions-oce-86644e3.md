# Dental Implants Aftercare Instructions – Oceanic Dental


Thank you for choosing Oceanic Dental for your dental implant care. Whether your procedure was performed in Fountain Valley or Huntington Beach, this guide will help you recover smoothly and protect your investment in long-term oral health and function.


## 1. Immediate Post-Treatment Instructions



### What to Expect:


Mild discomfort, swelling, and possible bruising around the implant site

Minor bleeding or pink-tinged saliva for the first 24 hours

Numbness for a few hours if local anesthesia was used


### Do’s and Don’ts for the First 24–48 Hours:


Do rest and keep your head elevated when lying down

Do apply an ice pack to the cheek (15 minutes on, 15 minutes off) for the first 24 hours

Don’t touch or disturb the surgical site with your tongue or fingers

Don’t smoke, vape, or use straws for at least 72 hours

Don’t consume hot or spicy foods, alcohol, or carbonated drinks


### Pain & Bleeding Management:


Take prescribed or over-the-counter pain medications as directed

Bite gently on gauze if bleeding occurs, replacing every 30–45 minutes as needed

Switch to moist heat after the first 48 hours if swelling continues


### Dietary Guidelines:


Stick to a soft, cool diet for the first few days (yogurt, smoothies, mashed potatoes, scrambled eggs)

Gradually reintroduce warmer and more textured foods as healing progresses


## 2. Ongoing Care & Maintenance



### Oral Hygiene:


Rinse gently with salt water (1/2 tsp salt in 8 oz warm water) beginning 24 hours post-op, 2–3 times daily

Use a soft-bristled toothbrush to clean surrounding teeth; avoid brushing directly over the implant site for 1 week

Avoid mouthwash with alcohol during the first week


### Healing Abutment or Temporary Crown Care:


If a healing abutment or temporary crown was placed, avoid putting pressure on it when chewing

Clean gently around it using a proxy brush or cotton swab if recommended


### Smoking & Alcohol:


Avoid smoking and alcohol for at least one week, ideally longer, as they delay healing and increase the risk of implant failure


## 3. Recognizing Complications & When to Contact Oceanic Dental



### Call us immediately if you experience:


Excessive bleeding that doesn’t stop after applying pressure

Fever or signs of infection (pus, swelling, red streaks, foul taste)

Severe or worsening pain after 3–4 days

Implant feels loose or you hear a clicking sound when chewing


## 4. Recovery Tips & Best Practices



### Support Fast Healing:


Get plenty of rest, especially in the first 48–72 hours

Stay hydrated with water and avoid dehydrating drinks like coffee or soda

Avoid strenuous activity or exercise for 2–3 days

Helpful Reminder:"Healing is a process—every gentle choice supports your new smile."


## 5. Follow-Up & Contact Information


We will schedule a follow-up within 1–2 weeks to monitor healing and remove any sutures if needed. Long-term success may include a few additional appointments to place the final crown or bridge.
