## Our Most Popular Cosmetic Dental Services in Huntington Beach


We offer a range of cosmetic dentistry procedures, from porcelain veneers to teeth whitening, to enhance your smile and boost your confidence.


### Porcelain Veneers & Crowns


Porcelain veneers are thin shells that cover the front of a tooth, addressing cosmetic concerns in two visits. Crowns, made from porcelain or a combination of porcelain and metal, provide full coverage and support for damaged, discolored, or weakened teeth.


### Dental Bonding


Bonding refers to the application of a tooth-colored resin to correct imperfections or enhance the shape of teeth. This procedure is often a favorable choice for cosmetic dental enhancements.


### Teeth Whitening


We provide in-house teeth whitening services using Zoom and Opalescence Boost, as well as offering professional-strength whitening kits.


### Dental Implants


Losing teeth can greatly affect not only the appearance of your smile, but also your overall oral health. For a reliable solution, we strongly recommend dental implants. Our team has extensive expertise in the precise placement of dental implants.


### Teeth straightening with Invisalign


With Invisalign clear aligners, teeth can be effectively and discreetly straightened. The comfortable plastic aligner trays are removable for brushing, flossing, and special occasions.


### Gum & Enamel Re-contouring


Gum re-contouring can improve an uneven gum line or a "gummy" smile. Enamel recontouring shapes natural teeth for a better appearance by removing small amounts of enamel to alter length, shape, or surface contours.

## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about cosmetic dentistry in Huntington Beach.

What’s included in a smile makeover?

A smile makeover is a personalized combination of cosmetic treatments designed to enhance your overall appearance. Depending on your needs, it can include porcelain veneers, teeth whitening, gum contouring, bonding, Invisalign, or crowns. At Oceanic Dental in Huntington Beach, we use digital smile design to plan your treatment and preview your new smile before we begin.

Are porcelain veneers permanent?

Veneers are considered long-term but not technically permanent. They last 10–15 years or more with proper care and require removing a small layer of enamel, which means you’ll always need some type of covering. Porcelain veneers are extremely durable, stain-resistant, and can transform the shape, color, and alignment of your teeth in just two visits.

What’s the difference between bonding and veneers?

Bonding is a quicker, more affordable option using tooth-colored resin to correct small chips, gaps, or discoloration. Veneers are thin ceramic shells bonded to the front of teeth to deliver a more durable and dramatic transformation. Bonding may last 3–7 years, while veneers typically last over a decade.

Is cosmetic dentistry painful?

Most cosmetic procedures are non-invasive or minimally invasive. Treatments like whitening and bonding involve little to no discomfort, while veneers may require mild enamel removal. We offer numbing and sedation options if needed, and most patients report little to no pain during or after cosmetic treatment.

How long does teeth whitening last?

In-office whitening can produce results that last from 1 to 3 years. This depends on your diet, oral hygiene, and habits like coffee or wine consumption. We also offer take-home whitening trays to help you maintain your brightness long after your visit.

Can cosmetic dentistry fix crooked teeth?

Yes—cosmetic dentistry can correct mild to moderate misalignment. Veneers or bonding can visually straighten teeth for a faster result. For more complex cases, we may recommend Invisalign to gently shift your teeth into alignment before completing cosmetic enhancements.

Will insurance cover cosmetic dentistry?

Most cosmetic procedures—like whitening, veneers, and bonding—are not covered by dental insurance because they’re considered elective. However, some treatments like crowns may be partially covered if there’s a functional need. Our team helps you review your benefits and offers flexible financing options.

How much does a smile makeover cost in Huntington Beach?

Smile makeover pricing depends on how many teeth are being treated and which procedures are used. Costs can range from $3,000 to $25,000+. We’ll create a detailed, personalized estimate at your consultation, with transparent fees and no surprises.

How quickly can I get results?

Some treatments like in-office whitening are completed in one visit. Veneers typically take two appointments over two weeks. Full smile makeovers involving Invisalign or implants may take several months. We’ll outline a clear timeline and help you plan every step of your transformation.

At Oceanic Dental, we combine artistic skill with the latest dental technology to deliver personalized, natural-looking results. Our Huntington Beach studio by the lake offers a peaceful setting and advanced tools, and our cosmetic dentists are known for their gentle care and exceptional outcomes.
