## Our Comprehensive Process for Successful Dental Implants



###### Consultation and Planning


Start with a free consultation where you'll meet our in-house specialists, take photos, and discuss your dental needs. We evaluate your teeth, review treatment options, and answer all your questions. Using X-rays and detailed planning, we ensure your implants will be perfectly integrated and secured to your jawbone.


###### Implant Placement


We surgically place the titanium implant into your jawbone, creating a stable foundation for your new tooth. After the implant is placed, we allow time for it to integrate with the bone, ensuring a secure fit.


###### Abutment and Crown Placement


Once the implant has healed, we attach the abutment and custom-made crown. This final step restores the natural look and function of your tooth, completing your smile transformation.

## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about dental implant.

How much do dental implants cost in Orange County?

The cost of dental implants can range from $3,000 to $6,000 per tooth, depending on the complexity of the procedure and any additional treatments, like bone grafting. Multi-tooth implants, such as implant-supported bridges or full arch replacements (like All-on-4), can cost significantly more.

Is All-on-X better than traditional dentures?

Yes—All-on-X offers a permanent, secure, and natural-feeling solution using 4–6 implants to support a full arch. No adhesives, no slipping.

Who is a good candidate for All-on-4 dental implants?

Patients missing most or all teeth who have sufficient bone or are eligible for bone grafts are ideal candidates. A consultation with our prosthodontist can confirm.

How long does the implant process take?

Single implants typically require 3–6 months including healing. All-on-X cases may offer same-day temporary teeth with full healing over several months.

What if I need extractions or bone grafting?

We provide all treatments in-house, including gentle extractions, bone grafts, and socket preservation—so you don’t need to be referred out.

What’s the success rate of dental implants?

With proper planning and care, dental implants have a 95–98% success rate and can last decades—or a lifetime.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients say it's easier than expected. We use local anesthesia or sedation for your comfort, and healing is usually mild.

Can I get implants in one day?

Yes. We offer same-day implant placement in select cases including All-on-X, allowing you to walk out with new teeth the same day.

Will my insurance cover implants?

Dental implant coverage depends on your insurance plan. While many dental insurance plans don’t fully cover implants, they may cover portions like extractions or crowns. Medical insurance might help if the implant is necessary due to an injury. We can help check with your provider and verify your coverage.

Why choose Oceanic Dental for implants?

We’re trusted by thousands of patients, with advanced digital planning, an in-house team of specialists, and unmatched patient care from two premier locations: Huntington Beach and Fountain Valley in Orange County.
