## Achieve a Dazzling Smile with Zoom! & Opalescence Boost



### Zoom! & Opalescence Boost


We are able to achieve teeth whitening of several shades in just one visit. Our method utilizes a combination of Zoom! laser whitening and Opalescence Boost, which we have found to be highly effective while causing minimal sensitivity. Ensuring your comfort during all procedures is a priority for us, and we can assure you that these systems are both safe and highly efficient.


### Zoom! Whitening System


Zoom! Whitening System is ideal for rapid, noticeable results. It uses a specialized hydrogen peroxide gel activated by a blue LED light, accelerating the whitening process. This breaks down deep-seated stains, revealing a visibly whiter smile in about an hour, all under careful supervision for your comfort and safety.


### Opalescence Boost Whitening System


Opalescence Boost uses a high concentration of hydrogen peroxide, freshly activated for each treatment. It achieves impressive results in two to three 20-minute applications, typically within a single appointment. Its unique PF formula with potassium nitrate and fluoride helps reduce sensitivity and strengthen enamel.

## Teeth Whitening FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about teeth whitening.

How does teeth whitening work?

Teeth whitening works by using bleaching agents to remove stains and discoloration from the surface of the teeth. The bleaching agents penetrate the enamel and break down the molecules that cause discoloration, resulting in a brighter smile.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes, teeth whitening is safe when performed by a dental professional. At Oceanic Dental, we use safe and effective whitening treatments that are tailored to your specific needs, ensuring optimal results without causing harm to your teeth or gums.

How long does it last?

Results can last from several months to a few years, depending on your oral hygiene and dietary habits. Avoiding foods and drinks that stain, like coffee and red wine, can help maintain your results longer​.

Will teeth whitening cause sensitivity?

Some individuals may experience temporary tooth sensitivity after teeth whitening. However, our dental professionals take measures to minimize sensitivity and ensure your comfort during the treatment process.

How much does teeth whitening cost?

The cost of teeth whitening can vary based on the method used. Professional in-office treatments typically range from $500 to $600 per session, while at-home kits provided by dentists can cost between $300 and $400. Over-the-counter whitening products are generally less expensive but may take longer to achieve desired results.

Why is teeth whitening so expensive?

Teeth whitening may seem like a big investment at first, but it’s worth looking at what you’re truly getting. At Oceanic Dental, both our Huntington Beach and Fountain Valley locations use high-quality materials and professional-grade systems, all under the care of skilled dental experts to give you a noticeably brighter smile that lasts—from several months to even a few years, depending on your habits, diet, and oral care routine.

Think of it this way: it’s not much more than a hair and makeup session that lasts just one night—except your radiant smile stays with you much longer and gives your confidence a real, lasting lift. It’s more than cosmetic—it’s a feel-good investment in you.
