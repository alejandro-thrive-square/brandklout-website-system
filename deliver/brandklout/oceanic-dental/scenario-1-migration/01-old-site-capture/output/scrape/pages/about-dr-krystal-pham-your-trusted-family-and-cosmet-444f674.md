# Dr. Krystal Pham – Your Trusted Family and Cosmetic Dentist


With over 20 years of experience and commitment to excellence ("America's Best Dentists 2025" by the National Consumer Advisory Board), Dr. Pham is more than just your dentist—she’s your trusted partner on the journey to a healthier smile.

Book with Dr. Pham


### Expertise Rooted in Family Tradition and Academic Excellence


Dr. Pham’s approach to dentistry is inspired by her family’s healthcare legacy and her exceptional academic background. An honors graduate of UCSF and former Assistant Clinical Professor, she emphasizes patient education and embraces technological innovation. With advanced training in implant and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Pham is a trusted choice for patients across Orange County.


### Comprehensive Dental Care Tailored to Your Needs


Dr. Pham offers a full range of dental services, from preventive care and pediatric dentistry to cosmetic enhancements. Whether you're aiming to maintain a healthy smile, enhance its appearance, or address specific dental concerns, she provides personalized solutions with exceptional care.


## Cosmetic Dentistry and Smile Makeovers


Transform your smile with tailored cosmetic solutions, including teeth whitening, veneers, and Invisalign. Dr. Pham’s artistic touch ensures that your smile reflects your personality while boosting your confidence.

Book an Appointment


## Implant Dentistry


Restore your smile with secure, natural-looking dental implants. Dr. Pham specializes in placing and restoring implants, offering patients durable solutions that look and feel just like real teeth.


## Family Dentistry for All Ages


From your child’s first dental visit to maintaining oral health as you age, Dr. Pham provides comprehensive family care. Her gentle approach and friendly demeanor make her a trusted choice for patients of all ages.


## Building Trust Through Compassionate Care and Expertise


Dr. Pham believes that every smile tells a story, and she’s here to make sure yours is a reflection of a thriving, healthy life. Her focus on patient education, comfort, and long-term health means that every treatment is designed with your best interests at heart. Whether you’re here for routine care or a full smile makeover, you can trust that you’re in experienced, caring hands.
