# Prosthodontic Expert in Huntington Beach and Fountain Valley



## A top-rated prosthodontist for Fountain Valley and Huntington Beach, Dr. Tanaka is known for his skilled and precise approach to restoring smiles. He specializes in complex dental reconstructions, implant-supported teeth, and dentures that enhance both function and appearance.



## Implant-Supported Permanent Teeth & Dentures


Regain your confidence and quality of life with implant-supported restorations. Dr. Tanaka uses the latest techniques in prosthodontics to design and place custom dentures and permanent teeth that look natural and feel secure. Whether you need a single implant or full-mouth reconstruction, he offers personalized care that meets your unique needs.

Book an Appointment


## Full-Mouth Reconstruction


If you’re dealing with multiple dental issues like worn, damaged, or missing teeth, Dr. Tanaka offers complete full-mouth reconstruction tailored just for you. Using advanced techniques such as All-on-4—which replaces an entire arch with just four dental implants—he rebuilds your smile to restore both function and confidence, helping you enjoy lasting oral health and a beautiful, natural look.


## Advanced Prosthodontics


From precise denture fittings to complex bite adjustments, Dr. Tanaka provides advanced care that addresses even the most challenging dental cases. His goal is to deliver long-lasting results with comfort and aesthetics in mind.


## Why Choose Dr. Tanaka?


Dr. Tanaka is Oceanic Dental’s go-to specialist for prosthodontics, bringing a combination of expertise and compassionate care to every patient. Recognized Diplomate of the American Board of Prosthodontics with extensive training in implant-supported restorations, full-mouth reconstruction, and complex dental cases, Dr. Tanaka’s expertise is all about restoring smiles with precision, functionality, and aesthetics.


### Academic Excellence


Dr. Tanaka’s decades-long career includes teaching roles as Assistant Clinical Professor and Boston University dental schools and academic researcher at Texas A&M where he co-authored a research paper on dentures. With board certifications and recognition for his contributions to prosthodontics, you can trust that you’re in expert hands.


### Specialized Prosthodontic Care


Dr. Tanaka focuses on life-changing treatments such as implant-supported dentures, crowns, and full-mouth reconstruction. Whether you need to restore a single tooth or rebuild your entire smile, he tailors every treatment to fit your unique needs. His attention to detail ensures that your smile is not only beautiful but functional for years to come.
