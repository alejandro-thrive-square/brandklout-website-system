# Experience the Difference of Personalized Dental Care with Dr. Ahmadi


Looking for a gentle, experienced dentist who can enhance your smile and care for your family’s dental needs? Dr. Ahmadi specializes in cosmetic dentistry and comprehensive family care right here in Fountain Valley.

Dr. Ahmadi began her dental career at the University of Maryland, where she earned her bachelor’s degree in Dental Hygiene before going on to complete her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS). With a gentle, caring approach, she is committed to making dentistry a pain-free and positive experience for every patient. She believes that everyone deserves personalized, comprehensive care—and takes the time to ensure her patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident at every step of their dental journey.

With her expertise in Invisalign, veneers, crowns, and routine family dentistry, Dr. Ahmadi tailors every treatment to fit your needs, ensuring a comfortable and stress-free experience for all ages.
