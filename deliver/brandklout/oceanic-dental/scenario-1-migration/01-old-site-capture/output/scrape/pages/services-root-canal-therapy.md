### Root Canal Therapy: Saving Your Tooth and Preserving Oral Health


Root canal therapy is a procedure performed to save an infected tooth. By removing the infected tissue, cleaning and disinfecting the inside of the tooth, and filling it with sterile material, we can prevent the need for extraction and preserve your oral health.

### Signs You Need a Root Canal


If you're experiencing severe tooth pain, swelling, or discoloration, you may need a root canal, but don't worry, our skilled team will remove the infection and restore your tooth, providing lasting relief and preserving your oral health.


###### Severe Pain

###### Discoloration


Darkening or yellowing of a tooth may be a sign that a root canal is necessary.


## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about root canal therapy.

Does a root canal hurt?

A root canal is generally not painful. Modern techniques and local anesthesia ensure that the procedure is comfortable. Many patients report that it feels similar to getting a filling. You may experience some sensitivity after the procedure, but it typically subsides within a few days.

How long does a root canal take?

A typical root canal procedure takes about 60 to 90 minutes, depending on the complexity of the case. In some instances, multiple visits may be necessary, especially if the infection is severe or the tooth requires additional treatment.

How much does a root canal cost?

The cost of a root canal varies depending on the tooth being treated and the complexity of the case. On average, it ranges from $700 to $1,500 per tooth. During your consultation, we’ll provide a detailed cost estimate and discuss any available payment options.

Will I need a crown after a root canal?

In most cases, a crown is recommended after a root canal to protect the tooth and restore its strength and functionality. After the infection is removed, the tooth may become brittle and more susceptible to breaking. A crown provides added protection and longevity.

What happens if I don’t get a root canal?

If an infected or damaged tooth is left untreated, the infection can spread to surrounding teeth and even to other parts of your body, potentially leading to more serious health issues. In addition, the tooth may eventually die, requiring extraction.
