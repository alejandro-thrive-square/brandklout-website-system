## The Composite Filling Process


Our procedure for applying composite fillings is straightforward and patient friendly. This process is typically completed in one visit, allowing you to leave with a restored, natural-looking smile. ##### Consultation


We begin with a thorough examination to determine the need for a filling


##### Preparation


Local anesthesia is administered to ensure comfort, and any decay is carefully removed


##### Bonding


The tooth is prepared with a bonding agent, and the composite material is applied in layers, each hardened with a curing light​


##### Shaping and Polishing


The filling is sculpted to match your tooth's natural contours and polished for a smooth finish


## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about Composite Tooth-Colored Fillings.

What are composite tooth-colored fillings?

Composite fillings are made from a tooth-colored resin material that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth. They are used to repair cavities, cracks, or chips, offering a more aesthetic alternative to traditional metal (amalgam) fillings.

How long do composite fillings last?

Composite fillings can last 5 to 10 years or longer with proper oral hygiene and regular dental checkups. Their longevity depends on factors like the size of the filling, the location, and how well you maintain your teeth.

Are composite fillings safe and do they look natural?

Yes, composite fillings are safe and mercury-free. They are made from a durable resin material and are often preferred for their natural look and biocompatibility. They are color-matched to your tooth, making them virtually invisible and blending seamlessly with your smile.

How much do composite fillings cost?

The cost of composite fillings varies depending on the size and location of the cavity, but they typically range from $100 to $300 per tooth. We’ll provide a detailed estimate during your consultation, and insurance may cover a portion of the cost.

How long does it take to get a composite filling?

A composite filling procedure usually takes about 30 to 60 minutes, depending on the size of the cavity. The dentist will clean the area, place the composite material, shape it, and harden it with a special light.
