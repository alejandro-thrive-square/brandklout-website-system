## Invisalign Magic: The Perks


Invisalign is a modern and discreet way to straighten your teeth without the hassle of traditional braces. Using clear, removable aligners, Invisalign gradually shifts your teeth into place, giving you a beautifully aligned smile. As a Gold Invisalign Provider in Orange County (among the first Invisalign providers in Fountain Valley, Costa Mesa, and Huntington Beach), our Certified experts ensure a comfortable and personalized experience from start to finish.

Invisible and Comfortable: Clear aligners that are virtually invisible and smooth, ensuring a comfortable fit without metal brackets or wires.

Removable and Convenient: Easily removable for eating, brushing, and flossing, allowing you to maintain oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods.

Customized and Effective: Custom-made aligners using advanced 3D imaging for a precise, effective, and tailored treatment plan.

## The Clear Path to Your Dream Smile



###### Step 1: Consultation and Digital Impressions


Start with a free consultation to meet our friendly orthodontists, discuss your smile goals, and take digital impressions. Using advanced 3D imaging, we create a custom treatment plan and show you a preview of your new smile.


###### Step 2: Custom Aligner Creation and Fitting


Your custom Invisalign aligners are made from your digital impressions. These clear, comfortable aligners gradually shift your teeth. You’ll wear each set for about two weeks. Our team ensures a perfect fit and provides easy care instructions.


###### Step 3: Regular Check-Ins


We’ll schedule regular check-ins to monitor your progress and make adjustments as needed. Most treatments take 12 months or more, but you’ll see improvements in just a few weeks. Our goal is to keep your treatment on track and achieve the best results.

## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about Invisalign.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment duration varies depending on your individual needs, but most treatments take 12 to 18 months for adults. For teens, it may take slightly longer, averaging 18 to 24 months.

How much does Invisalign cost?

The cost of Invisalign usually ranges from $3,000 to $8,000, depending on the complexity of the case and the length of treatment. Factors like insurance coverage and the specific orthodontic issues being addressed can affect the overall price.

Can I eat and drink with Invisalign aligners on?

No, you should remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. This helps prevent staining and damage to the aligners. Always brush your teeth before putting them back in​.

Will Invisalign affect my speech?

Some people may experience a slight lisp or speech adjustment when they first start wearing aligners, but this typically goes away within a week as you get used to them.

How often do I need to wear Invisalign aligners?

For the best results, you should wear your Invisalign aligners for 20 to 22 hours a day, only removing them to eat, drink, brush, and floss. Wearing them consistently helps ensure your treatment stays on track and you achieve your desired results within the expected timeframe​.
