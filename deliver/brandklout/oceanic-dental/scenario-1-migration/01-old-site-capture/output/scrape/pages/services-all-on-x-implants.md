# Restore Your Smile with All-on-X Implants


If you’re dealing with missing or severely damaged teeth, Dr. Tanaka’s All-on-X dental implant solution can help restore your smile and improve your quality of life. This advanced procedure replaces an entire arch of teeth with just 4 or 6 strategically placed implants, giving you a stable, long-lasting, and natural-looking smile.


### Why Choose All-on-X?



#### Permanent Solution


Say goodbye to dentures. All-on-X implants offer a permanent, secure replacement for missing teeth.


#### Quick Recovery


With Dr. Tanaka’s expertise, many patients enjoy faster recovery times and leave with a new smile the same day.


#### Fewer Implants, Full Results


The procedure uses fewer implants to support a full set of teeth, making it less invasive while still providing full-mouth restoration.


#### Natural Appearance


Your new teeth are custom-made to match the look and feel of natural teeth, giving you a beautiful, natural smile.


## Why Dr. Tanaka?


With extensive experience in full-mouth reconstruction and prosthodontics, Dr. Tanaka has mastered the All-on-X implant procedure. His meticulous approach and commitment to detail ensure that every patient receives personalized care tailored to their unique needs, resulting in durable, natural-looking smiles.


### Academic Excellence


Dr. Tanaka’s decades-long career includes teaching roles as Assistant Clinical Professor and Boston University dental schools and academic researcher at Texas A&M where he co-authored a research paper on dentures. With board certifications and recognition for his contributions to prosthodontics, you can trust that you’re in expert hands.


### Specialized Prosthodontic Care


Dr. Tanaka focuses on life-changing treatments such as all-on-x, implant-supported dentures, crowns, and full-mouth reconstruction. Whether you need to restore a single tooth or rebuild your entire smile, he tailors every treatment to fit your unique needs. His attention to detail ensures that your smile is not only beautiful but functional for years to come.
