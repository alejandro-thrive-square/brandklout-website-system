## Meet Dr. Krystal Pham


Welcome to Oceanic Dental, where Dr. Krystal Pham leads our practice with a genuine passion for transforming smiles and enhancing lives.

To Dr. Pham, a smile is much more than just a set of teeth; it's a reflection of your life’s vibrancy and joy. She believes every smile tells a story, and she’s honored to be part of that journey. Whether you're in for a routine check-up or an advanced cosmetic procedure, Dr. Pham’s goal is not just to enhance your smile but to boost your confidence and, ultimately, your overall well-being.

With over 20 years of experience, Dr. Pham’s commitment to excellence is evident in her dedication to ongoing training, patient care, and hands-on expertise—not only at her clinic but also as a Assistant Clinical Professor at the University of California, San Francisco. Her extensive background makes her not just a great dentist, but a trusted partner on your path to better oral health and a radiant smile.


## Creating Beautiful Smiles That Reflect Vibrant Lives



### Providing High-Quality Concierge Dental Care


Our journey started in 2010 with a mission: to deliver high-quality, personalized dental care with a concierge touch, combining advanced technology and comprehensive services—all in a welcoming environment in Huntington Beach. The trust and support from our community led us to open a larger clinic in Fountain Valley.

Today, we’re honored to have cared for over 15,000 patients across Orange County, from Huntington Beach to Fountain Valley, Newport Beach, Irvine and Costa Mesa to Long Beach, Santa Ana, Anaheim, Buena Park and beyond. Our commitment remains the same: to provide comprehensive, high-quality, and comfortable care, bringing out your best smile—one visit at a time.


## Our Mission: Bringing Out Your Best Smile



### Transforming Smiles, One Visit at A Time


We understand that your smile is an essential part of who you are, and we are dedicated to helping you maintain and enhance it through personalized and high-quality dental care. At Oceanic Dental, our world-class dentists and highly trained staff are committed to bringing out the best smile you deserve always with the concierge level service experience.

Our approach combines the latest dental technologies with a compassionate touch, ensuring that you receive the best possible treatment tailored to your unique needs. Whether you’re seeking preventive care, restorative treatments, or cosmetic enhancements, our team is here to support you every step of the way.
