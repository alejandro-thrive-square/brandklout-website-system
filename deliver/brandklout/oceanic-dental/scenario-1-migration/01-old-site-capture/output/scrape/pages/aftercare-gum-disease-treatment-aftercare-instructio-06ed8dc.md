# Gum Disease Treatment Aftercare Instructions – Oceanic Dental


Thank you for choosing Oceanic Dental for your periodontal health. If you’ve received treatment for gum disease – including deep cleanings, localized antibiotics, or laser therapy – this guide will help you heal well and protect your long-term oral health. Please follow these aftercare instructions carefully.


## 1. Immediate Post-Treatment Instructions



### What to Expect:


Mild soreness or gum tenderness for 24–72 hours.

Light bleeding when brushing or flossing for a few days.

Possible temporary tooth sensitivity.


### Do’s and Don’ts for the First 24–48 Hours:


Do rinse gently with warm salt water (1/2 tsp salt in 8 oz water) up to 3 times a day.

Do eat soft, cool-to-lukewarm foods for the first 24 hours.

Don’t smoke or use tobacco products – it delays healing.

Don’t chew hard, crunchy, or spicy foods on the treated area.


### Pain Management:


Take over-the-counter pain relievers such as ibuprofen or acetaminophen as directed.

Use a cold compress (15 min on, 15 min off) if you experience swelling or discomfort.


### 2. Ongoing Care & Maintenance



### Oral Hygiene Routine:


Brush gently with a soft toothbrush starting the day after treatment.

Floss carefully to avoid irritating healing gums.

Use any prescribed antimicrobial rinse (e.g., chlorhexidine) exactly as directed.


### Products We Recommend:


Desensitizing toothpaste for temporary sensitivity.

Electric toothbrush with a gentle setting.


### Lifestyle Tips:


Avoid tobacco and alcohol for at least 48 hours.

Stay hydrated and maintain a healthy, balanced diet to support healing.


## 3. Recognizing Complications & When to Contact Oceanic Dental



### Please contact us if you notice:


Persistent bleeding that lasts more than 2 days

Increased or severe swelling

Signs of infection (pus, fever, or foul odor)

Loose teeth or sudden gum recession


## 4. Recovery Tips & Best Practices



### Support Healing With These Habits:


Rinse after meals to keep your mouth clean.

Avoid chewing on the treated side for a few days.

Keep up with excellent daily brushing and flossing – this is key to gum recovery.

Helpful Reminder:"Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile – small steps each day make a big difference."


## 5. Follow-Up & Contact Information


We’ll likely schedule a follow-up within 4–6 weeks to check your healing and measure improvements in your gum health.
