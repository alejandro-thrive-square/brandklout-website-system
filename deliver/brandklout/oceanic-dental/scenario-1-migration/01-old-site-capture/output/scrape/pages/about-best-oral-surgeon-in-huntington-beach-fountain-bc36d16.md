# Dr. Noh – Trusted Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon in Huntington Beach & Fountain Valley



## Dr. Noh is recognized for his precise, compassionate approach to oral surgery. He specializes in wisdom teeth removal, dental implant surgery, corrective jaw procedures, and TMJ disorder treatments—delivering outcomes that restore comfort, function, and confidence.



## An Oral Surgeon Patients Can Trust


When it comes to oral surgery, experience and precision matter. Dr. Noh is a highly trained Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon with dual degrees in dentistry and medicine, offering advanced care to patients in Huntington Beach and Fountain Valley. Known for his skill, compassion, and dedication to evidence-based care, he ensures every patient feels at ease and confident in their treatment.

Book an Appointment


## Comprehensive Oral Surgery Services


Dr. Noh earned his dental degree from the University of the Pacific and completed a rigorous six-year oral surgery residency at the University of Pennsylvania, where he also served as Chief Resident. Today, he continues to blend clinical excellence with a gentle approach, specializing in:

Pre-prosthetic procedures to prepare for dental implants and restorations

Orthognathic (jaw) surgery

TMJ disorders and corrective therapies

Wisdom teeth extraction and complex oral surgeries

Facial cosmetic surgery

His thoughtful, transparent approach ensures that patients fully understand their treatment options and feel supported every step of the way.


## The Perfect Surgical–Restorative Team


At Oceanic Dental, Dr. Noh’s expertise in surgical care is complemented by the restorative talents of Dr. Tanaka (Prosthodontist) and Dr. Cantrell (General & Cosmetic Dentistry). Together, they form a seamless team that delivers comprehensive results—whether it’s a single implant, a full-mouth reconstruction, or advanced bite correction. This collaborative approach ensures:

Precise surgical foundations

Beautiful, functional restorative results

A smooth, well-coordinated patient experience


## Specialized Care for TMJ Disorders


Dr. Noh has advanced training in the diagnosis and treatment of TMJ (temporomandibular joint) disorders, which can cause chronic jaw pain, headaches, and difficulty with daily function. His comprehensive approach includes everything from splint therapy to corrective surgery, depending on the patient’s needs.

At Oceanic Dental, we also offer Botox therapy for TMJ, a minimally invasive option to ease muscle tension and reduce pain. This complementary service can provide significant relief and help restore comfort to everyday life.
