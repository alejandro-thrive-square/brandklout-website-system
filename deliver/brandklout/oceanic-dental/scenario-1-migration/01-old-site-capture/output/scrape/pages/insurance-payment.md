### Still Unsure about How to Manage the Cost of Your Care? We’ve Got You Covered


Get the dental treatments you need without financial worries. We offer 0% interest financing for qualified applicants and flexible payment plans tailored to fit your budget.


### Avytree


Avytree provides flexible payment plans, offering the convenience of 0% interest on financing for up to 18 months. The application process is swift and transparent, allowing you to divide your treatment total into smaller, more manageable monthly payments. Our plans entail no hidden fees, deferred interest, or hard credit checks.

Apply Now


### CareCredit


CareCredit covers your deductibles, co-payments and any fees that are not listed under your current insurance plan. You can have the convenience of 0% interest on financing for up to 12 months or low interest financing from 24 to 60 months!


### Lending Club


We are delighted to offer flexible payment plans through Lending Club. It is simple to calculate your potential monthly payments. Just complete a brief application to view the fixed rates and monthly payments, all without affecting your credit score. For more information, simply ask a team member or visit lendingclub.com/patients

Learn More
