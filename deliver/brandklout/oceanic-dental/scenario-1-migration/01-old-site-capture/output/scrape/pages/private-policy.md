# Privacy Policy


Effective Date: November 6, 2024

Krystal Pham DDS, Inc. DBA Oceanic Dental (“Oceanic Dental,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices for collecting, using, and sharing information, including Personal Information and Protected Health Information (“PHI”), as it relates to our services provided through our website and third-party booking platforms.

This Privacy Policy applies to all who access, use, or interact with our services, including those who submit information through online forms for inquiries, appointment scheduling, insurance verification, and general communications with Oceanic Dental.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our services, you agree to the terms outlined herein. If you do not agree to this Privacy Policy, please refrain from using our services.


## 1. Information We Collect


We collect various types of personal and health information when you use our services, interact with our website, or communicate with us. This includes:

Personal Identifiers: Information that can directly identify you, including your name, date of birth, contact information such as email, phone number(s), postal address, and insurance details.

Protected Health Information (PHI): Medical and dental information required for treatment and healthcare operations, in compliance with HIPAA regulations.

Internet and Electronic Activity: Device information, IP addresses, browsing activity on our website, and preferences.


## 2. How We Use Your Information


We use your information for purposes necessary to provide, improve, and protect our services, including:

Providing and Enhancing Services: For scheduling, insurance verification, patient communication, updates, and treatment.

Communication: To inform you of appointments, treatments, updates, offers, new services, or respond to inquiries.

Analysis and Improvement: To understand site usage, customize user experiences, and improve our services.


## 3. Text messaging and mobile privacy


Oceanic Dental may send appointment and scheduling messages, replies to your questions, care coordination messages, office updates, and promotional messages if you enroll in the applicable program. You may opt in through an affirmative SMS consent option on a website or booking form, in writing, or through another clearly disclosed method. Consent to promotional texts is not a condition of purchase or dental treatment.

You may receive up to four messages per week. Actual frequency depends on your appointments, requests, and the program for which you enrolled. Message and data rates may apply under your mobile plan. Oceanic Dental does not charge a separate text messaging fee.

Reply STOP to an Oceanic Dental text to opt out. We may send one final confirmation. To receive texts again, you must provide new consent. Reply HELP for assistance, email [email protected], or call (714) 378-0085 for Fountain Valley or (714) 841-0203 for Huntington Beach. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile telephone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. This restriction applies to the mobile information described here regardless of other sharing described in this policy. Our messaging platform, wireless carriers, and service providers may process mobile information only as needed to send or support our messages or as required by law. Consent to receive Oceanic Dental texts is not transferred to another business.

Text messages are not fully secure. Please do not send detailed medical histories, clinical images or records, payment card data, or other sensitive information by SMS. Call us to arrange a secure way to send clinical information.


## 4. How We Protect Your Information


We implement security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. While we strive to safeguard your data, transmission over the internet involves inherent risks. You agree to assume these risks and understand that no system can be guaranteed fully secure.


## 5. HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices


Oceanic Dental follows the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) to safeguard Protected Health Information (PHI). This notice describes how we use and disclose your PHI and your rights concerning it.


### Scope of Notice


This notice applies to all PHI we maintain. PHI includes identifiable information related to your medical and dental health, treatment, and payment details.


### Our Responsibilities


We are required by law to:

Maintain the confidentiality of PHI.

Provide notice of our privacy practices.

Notify individuals if their unsecured PHI is compromised.


### How We Use and Disclose Your PHI


Your PHI may be used or disclosed without authorization for the following purposes:

Treatment: Disclosing PHI to healthcare providers to facilitate care.

Payment: Using PHI for billing, claims processing, and insurance purposes.

Healthcare Operations: For activities such as quality assessment, training, service improvement, and customer feedback.

Business Associates: We may share PHI with trusted vendors under contracts requiring data protection.


### Your Rights Regarding PHI


You have rights concerning your PHI, including:

Right to Access: To review and obtain a copy of your PHI.

Right to Amend: To request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

Right to Accounting: To request a record of disclosures made outside of treatment, payment, and healthcare operations.

Right to Restrict Disclosures: You may request additional restrictions on PHI disclosures. We will accommodate reasonable requests as required by law.

For questions or to exercise your rights, please contact us:

Mail: Oceanic Dental, Attn: Privacy Officer, 17991 Euclid St, Fountain Valley, CA 92708

Phone: (714) 378-0085

Email: [email protected]

Please send DMCA notices to:
