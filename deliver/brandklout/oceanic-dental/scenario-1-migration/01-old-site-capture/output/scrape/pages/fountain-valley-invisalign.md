## Invisalign Can Help With


Invisalign isn’t just about straighter teeth—it’s about improving your bite, function, and confidence. At Oceanic Dental in Fountain Valley, we use clear aligners to treat a wide range of concerns, including:

Crooked or crowded teeth: When teeth overlap or twist due to lack of space, Invisalign gently moves them into alignment for a more balanced smile.

Gaps between teeth: Spaces between teeth can affect both appearance and oral health. Invisalign closes these gaps for a more even bite and smile.

Mild bite issues (overbite, underbite, crossbite): Invisalign can correct certain bite problems by guiding your teeth and jaw into a healthier, more functional position.

Teeth shifting after braces: Invisalign is a great option if your teeth have started to move after previous orthodontic treatment, helping restore your straight smile without metal braces.

## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about Invisalign in Fountain Valley.

What is Invisalign and how does it work?

Invisalign is a clear aligner system that straightens teeth using a series of custom-made, transparent plastic trays. Each aligner is worn for 1–2 weeks, gradually moving your teeth into the desired position. Unlike braces, Invisalign is removable and nearly invisible, making it ideal for both adults and teens who want a discreet way to improve their smile.

Is Invisalign as effective as braces?

Yes—Invisalign can be just as effective as traditional braces for treating mild to moderate alignment issues such as crowding, spacing, and some bite problems. Success depends on proper wear (20–22 hours/day). At Oceanic Dental in Fountain Valley, we tailor Invisalign treatment plans to achieve the same results you'd expect from braces—without the brackets and wires.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most Invisalign cases are completed in 6–12 months, although complex cases can take up to 18 months. The exact duration depends on the severity of your alignment issues and your consistency in wearing the aligners. We use digital scanning and progress tracking at our Fountain Valley office to keep you on schedule.

Does Invisalign hurt?

Invisalign is generally not painful, but you may experience mild pressure or soreness when switching to a new tray. This feeling typically lasts a day or two and is a sign that your teeth are moving. The aligners are smooth and comfortable—no sharp brackets or wires to cause irritation.

Can I eat and drink with Invisalign?

No, you should remove your aligners when eating or drinking anything other than water. This prevents staining and warping of the trays. The benefit is that there are no food restrictions—you can enjoy your favorite meals as long as you brush before putting the aligners back in.

How do I clean my Invisalign aligners?

Clean your aligners daily with lukewarm water and a soft toothbrush, or use Invisalign cleaning crystals. Avoid hot water, as it can distort the shape. Proper hygiene keeps your trays clear and odor-free throughout treatment.

Will people know I’m wearing Invisalign?

Invisalign aligners are designed to be discreet. They're made from clear, medical-grade plastic that fits closely to your teeth, making them barely noticeable in everyday conversation or photos.

Is Invisalign suitable for adults?

Yes—Invisalign is an excellent choice for adults who want to straighten their teeth without the look of braces. It's especially popular among working professionals and parents who want effective results without disrupting their lifestyle.

Does insurance cover Invisalign?

Many PPO dental plans offer partial orthodontic coverage for Invisalign. Our Oceanic Dental team will verify your benefits and provide a breakdown of costs. We also offer 0% interest financing to make treatment affordable, even without insurance.
