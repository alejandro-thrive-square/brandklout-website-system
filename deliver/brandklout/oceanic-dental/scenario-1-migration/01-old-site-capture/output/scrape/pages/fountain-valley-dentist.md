## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about our premier cosmetic, implant, and restorative dental services in Fountain Valley.

What makes someone a good candidate for cosmetic dentistry?

A good candidate is someone with healthy teeth and gums who wants to improve their smile’s appearance—whether it’s correcting stains, chips, gaps, or misalignment. At Oceanic Dental in Fountain Valley, we always start with a personalized consultation to understand your goals and evaluate what treatments are right for you.

Is a smile makeover permanent?

Smile makeovers are designed to last a long time, especially when veneers, crowns, or implants are involved. However, good home care, regular dental checkups, and healthy habits all help maintain your results. We’ll go over realistic timelines for each procedure during your consultation at our Fountain Valley office.

What is the difference between a crown and a veneer?

A veneer covers just the front of the tooth and is mostly cosmetic. A crown covers the entire tooth and is used when there's more structural damage. Both can improve your smile, but they serve slightly different purposes—and we’ll guide you to the right choice.

How do I know if I need full mouth reconstruction?

If you're dealing with multiple issues like broken teeth, missing teeth, jaw pain, or difficulty chewing, full mouth reconstruction might be a solution. It’s a comprehensive approach to restoring both the look and function of your smile. It starts with a full assessment and a custom plan. Please contact our Fountain Valley office at 714-378-0085 to schedule a consultation.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 20+ years or even a lifetime. They’re one of the most durable and natural-feeling options for replacing missing teeth. We also help monitor your implant health at routine visits.

Are All-on-X implants better than dentures?

All-on-X is more stable and secure than traditional dentures and doesn’t require adhesive. For many patients, they feel and function more like natural teeth. That said, everyone’s situation is different, so we’ll help compare both options with your needs in mind.

Will dental insurance cover cosmetic procedures?

Most cosmetic procedures like veneers or whitening aren’t covered. However, if a procedure also restores function—like a crown or implant—it may be partially reimbursed. Our team is happy to help review your benefits and offer flexible options.

Do dental implants hurt?

Most patients say the procedure is easier than expected. We use numbing and sedation to keep you comfortable, and post-procedure soreness is usually mild and manageable. We'll talk you through each step and what to expect during healing.

How long does a smile makeover take from start to finish?

It depends on what’s involved. Whitening can be done in a single visit, while veneers or crowns may take a few weeks. Full mouth reconstruction or implants take longer. We’ll map out the timeline with you so everything feels clear and manageable.

What if I have anxiety about going to the dentist?

You’re not alone—many of our patients feel the same way. We offer gentle care, explain everything ahead of time, and provide comfort options like sedation if needed. We want you to feel relaxed and in control, every step of the way.
