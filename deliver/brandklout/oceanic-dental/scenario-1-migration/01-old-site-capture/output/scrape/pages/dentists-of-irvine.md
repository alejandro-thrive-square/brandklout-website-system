# Comprehensive Dental Care Serving Irvine


Discover a full range of dental services designed to promote a healthy and beautiful smile for Irvine's residents. From routine cleanings to advanced cosmetic procedures, our team is dedicated to providing comprehensive care for your dental needs.


### General Dentistry



### Welcome to Your First Visit & New Patient Exam


Your first dental visit should not be stressful. Our welcoming staff will assist you as we take X-rays, perform a gentle examination, and create a personalized treatment plan.


### Cleanings & Gum Disease Treatment


We provide regular professional cleaning, and our in-house board-certified periodontist provides gum treatment, including gum surgery, extraction, bone graft, and implants.


### Tooth-Colored Fillings for a Natural Look


At Oceanic Dental, we offer durable composite tooth-colored fillings that closely match your teeth's natural color. Our fillings blend seamlessly with your smile, restoring your teeth to their natural beauty.


### Cosmetic Dentistry



### Cosmetic Dentistry & Teeth Whitening


Enhance your smile with Cosmetic Dentistry and Teeth Whitening, which can address imperfections like gaps, misalignment, discoloration, and even a gummy smile, for a more confident and radiant appearance.


### Beautiful Porcelain Crowns and Veneers


Our experienced dentists specialize in porcelain veneers, crowns, and correcting imperfections like discoloration, gaps, chips, and misalignment. Contact us for in-person free cosmetic consultations.


### Straighten Your Teeth with Invisalign®


Invisalign provides a comfortable and removable alternative to traditional metal braces. As a trusted Invisalign provider, Oceanic Dental offers personalized cosmetic consultations to assess your suitability for Invisalign treatment.


### Restorative Dentistry



### Full-Mouth Restoration


Full-mouth restoration encompasses a comprehensive array of procedures, including treatment for gum disease, fillings, crowns, dental implants, dentures, and straightening, to achieve your desired results.


### Root Canal Therapy


Our Board-Certified Endodontic Specialist performs root canal therapy in our office to save infected teeth. We use the latest dental technology for a more comfortable and thorough procedure.


### Tooth Extractions


If a tooth has sustained significant damage, it may need to be extracted. Our in-house oral surgeon uses gentle extraction techniques and provides IV sedation for those who prefer to sleep during the procedure.


### Sedation Dentistry


Experience relaxation with our sedation dentistry services, ensuring a comfortable dental care experience. It's like a soothing nap while we work on enhancing your smile!


### Dental Implant Services


Our specialists have 30+ years of experience with dental implants, which are permanent tooth replacements. They can be used with dentures, crowns, or bridges to restore your smile.


### All-on-X Implants


All-on-X dental implant solution replaces an entire arch of teeth with just 4 or 6 strategically placed implants, giving you a stable, long-lasting, and natural-looking smile.


## Our Team of General Practice & Specialists


Discover the exceptional care provided by our dedicated team of dental professionals, who are committed to serving the communities of Irvine with top-notch dental services and personalized attention.

Dr. Krystal Pham

Dr. Pham, with 20+ years in dentistry, is the lead dentist at Oceanic Dental. Her passion for dentistry is rooted in a family tradition of healthcare. Inspired by her mother’s practice, she saw the power of a healthy smile early on. Dr. Pham offers a wide range of services, from preventive to cosmetic and implant dentistry, always striving for excellence, compassion, and innovation.

Dr. Chris Bonin

Dr. Bonin, boasting a 30-year illustrious career, excels in surgical procedures and comprehensive mouth reconstructions. His skill set includes implant dentistry, bone grafting, and wisdom teeth extraction, with a special focus on sedation for patients with anxiety. He is a respected member of notable dental associations such as the International Congress of Oral Implantologists, the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Brittany Foster

Dr. Brittany Foster is a skilled general dentist with a Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Roseman University of Health Sciences and an extensive background in cosmetic and restorative dentistry, implants, and pediatric care. Known for her gentle approach and commitment to high-quality care, Dr. Foster focuses on delivering comprehensive and personalized treatments for patients of all ages.

Dr. Mamoru Tanaka

Dr. Tanaka is a skilled prosthodontist specializing in full-mouth reconstructions. Dr. Tanaka’s passion for dentistry runs in the family, having been inspired by his father, a respected dentist in Japan. With over a decade of experience and advanced training from Tufts University, where he earned his Certificate in Advanced Prosthodontics with honors, Dr. Tanaka is committed to providing top-tier care to his patients.

Dr. Shenika Cantrell

Dr. Cantrell brings both expertise and personal insight to Oceanic Dental. With a Doctor of Dental Medicine and an MBA from Roseman College of Dental Medicine in Utah, along with her active membership in the Academy of General Dentistry, she combines advanced dental knowledge with patient-focused care.

Dr. Katy Vu

Dr. Vu is a distinguished dentist with comprehensive expertise in dentistry. Since graduating from the University of Buffalo in 2015, she has become a prominent figure in pediatric care, winning over patients with her empathetic approach. Dr. Vu’s commitment extends beyond her profession, as she is actively involved in charitable work in Haiti and the Dominican Republic. Her philanthropic efforts and steadfast dedication inspire those around her.
