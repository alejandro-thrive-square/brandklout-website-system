# Cosmetic Dentistry Costs in 2025



###### Enhancing your smile through cosmetic dentistry can significantly boost your confidence and overall well-being. At Oceanic Dental, we understand that one of the primary concerns for our patients is the cost associated with these transformative procedures. Below, we've outlined various cosmetic dental treatments along with their average costs to help you make informed decisions about your dental care.​

## Teeth Whitening Cost



###### A brighter smile is often just a whitening treatment away. Professional in-office teeth whitening typically costs between $500 and $800, depending on the specific treatment and location. Over-the-counter options are available at lower prices, but professional treatments offer more immediate and noticeable results.



## Cost of Dental Veneers



###### Veneers are thin, custom-made shells designed to cover the front surface of teeth, improving their appearance. The cost of veneers varies widely, generally ranging from $1,000 to $2,500 per tooth. Factors influencing the price include the material used and the complexity of the individual's case.



## Cost of Dental Implants



###### For those missing one or more teeth, dental implants provide a durable and natural-looking solution. The average cost for a single dental implant ranges from $3,000 to $5,000. This estimate includes the implant placement, abutment, and crown.



## Cost of Invisalign (Clear Aligners)



###### Invisalign offers a discreet way to straighten teeth using clear aligners. Treatment costs typically range from $3,000 to $8,000, depending on the complexity of the case and the duration of treatment. Many dental insurance plans provide partial coverage for orthodontic treatments like Invisalign.



## Cost of Dental Crowns



###### Crowns, or caps, restore the shape, size, and function of damaged teeth. The cost of a dental crown generally falls between $1,150 and $1,750 per tooth. The price can vary based on the material chosen (porcelain, metal, or porcelain-fused-to-metal) and the tooth's location.



## Cost of Dental Bonding



###### Dental bonding involves applying a tooth-colored resin to repair chips, cracks, or gaps. This cost-effective procedure typically costs between $300 and $400 per tooth. Bonding is an excellent option for minor cosmetic enhancements.​



## Cost of All-On-4 Dental Implants



###### For patients seeking a full-arch replacement, the All-On-4 dental implant technique offers a comprehensive solution. As of 2025, the cost starts at approximately $25,000 per arch. This price includes the surgical placement of four implants and a fixed prosthesis.​



###### Note: The prices shared are general estimates meant to give you a helpful starting point. Actual costs can vary based on your unique needs, treatment details, and the materials involved. For the most accurate pricing and personalized recommendations, we’d be happy to schedule a consultation with you.
