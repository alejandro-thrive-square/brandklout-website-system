# Wisdom Teeth Removal Aftercare Instructions – Oceanic Dental


Thank you for choosing Oceanic Dental for your oral surgery. Whether your wisdom teeth extraction was done in Huntington Beach or Fountain Valley, this guide is here to help you heal safely and comfortably.


## 1. Immediate Post-Treatment Instructions



### What to Expect:


Mild to moderate swelling, jaw stiffness, and discomfort for several days

Minor bleeding or pink-tinged saliva for the first 24 hours

Facial bruising may appear around day 2–3


### Do’s and Don’ts for the First 24–48 Hours:


Do keep your head elevated when resting or sleeping

Do bite on gauze pads (change every 30–45 minutes) to control bleeding

Do apply ice packs to reduce swelling (20 min on/20 min off)

Don’t spit, rinse forcefully, or use straws

Don’t smoke, vape, or consume alcohol

Don’t eat hard, crunchy, or spicy foods


### Pain Management:


Take prescribed or recommended pain relievers as directed

Use ice in the first 24–48 hours, then switch to moist heat if needed


### Dietary Guidelines:


Eat soft, cool foods: yogurt, mashed potatoes, smoothies (no straw), scrambled eggs

Stay hydrated, sipping water from a glass


## 2. Ongoing Care & Maintenance



### Oral Hygiene:


Gently rinse with salt water (1/2 tsp salt in 8 oz warm water) starting 24 hours after surgery, 3–4 times daily

Brush the other teeth normally, avoiding surgical sites for 2–3 days

Do not use mouthwash with alcohol during the first week


### Dry Socket Prevention:


Avoid suction and dislodging the clot

Do not smoke or use straws for at least 7 days


### Activity:


Rest during the first 2–3 days

Avoid strenuous activity for 5–7 days


## 3. Recognizing Complications & When to Contact Oceanic Dental



### Call us if you notice:


Persistent bleeding beyond 4–6 hours

Severe pain 3–5 days after surgery (may indicate dry socket)

Fever, foul taste, or swelling that worsens after day 3

Numbness or tingling that doesn’t go away


## 4. Recovery Tips & Best Practices



### Tips for a Smooth Recovery:


Keep your head elevated and apply cold compresses early

Avoid disturbing the area with your tongue or fingers

Rinse after meals to keep the site clean

Stick to soft, nourishing meals and stay hydrated

Helpful Reminder:"Rest, rinse, and relax – your body knows how to heal."


## 5. Follow-Up & Contact Information


We typically schedule a follow-up within 1–2 weeks to monitor healing and remove sutures if necessary.
