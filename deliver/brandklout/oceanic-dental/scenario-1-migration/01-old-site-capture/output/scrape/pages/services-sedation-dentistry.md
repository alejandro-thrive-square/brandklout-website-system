## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about sedation dentistry.

What is sedation dentistry?

Sedation dentistry involves the use of medication to help patients relax during dental procedures. It can range from minimal sedation (feeling calm but fully awake) to moderate sedation, where patients may feel drowsy or even fall asleep but can still respond to instructions.

Is sedation dentistry safe?

Yes, sedation dentistry is generally safe when administered by a trained professional. Dentists will carefully review your medical history and monitor you throughout the procedure to ensure safety. However, certain conditions, such as pregnancy or specific medical issues, may affect eligibility​.

What types of sedation are available?

There are several types of sedation, including:

‍Nitrous oxide (laughing gas): Inhaled to create a calm feeling and wears off quickly.

‍Oral sedation: A pill taken before the procedure to relax you.

‍IV sedation: Administered through the veins for deeper relaxation, often making patients feel like they slept through the procedure

Will I feel pain with sedation dentistry?

While sedation helps reduce anxiety, it doesn't block pain on its own. Your dentist will usually pair sedation with a local anesthetic to ensure a pain-free experience.

How much does sedation dentistry cost?

The cost varies depending on the type of sedation used and the length of your procedure. Nitrous oxide tends to be the most affordable, while IV sedation is more expensive. Costs can range from $200 to $1,000 or more, depending on the sedation type and duration of your procedure.
