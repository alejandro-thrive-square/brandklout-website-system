# Dental Crowns & Bridges Aftercare Instructions – Oceanic Dental


Thank you for trusting Oceanic Dental for your restorative dental care. Whether you received a temporary or permanent crown or bridge at our Huntington Beach or Fountain Valley office, this aftercare guide will help ensure comfort, durability, and long-term success. Please follow the instructions below.


## 1. Immediate Post-Treatment Instructions



### What to Expect:


Mild soreness around the gums for a few days

Sensitivity to temperature or pressure is common, especially with temporary crowns

Numbness may last several hours if anesthesia was used


### If You Have a Temporary Crown or Bridge:


Avoid sticky, chewy, or hard foods that may dislodge the temporary restoration

Chew on the opposite side of your mouth

Brush gently and floss carefully – slide floss out instead of snapping it up to avoid pulling the temporary off


### If You Have a Permanent Crown or Bridge:


You may experience slight pressure sensitivity for a few days

Resume normal eating and hygiene routines once numbness wears off


### Do’s and Don’ts (First 24–48 Hours):


Do avoid chewing until numbness fully subsides

Do take over-the-counter pain relievers if needed

Don’t consume extremely hot or cold foods if you’re experiencing sensitivity

Don’t bite hard objects or use your teeth to open packaging


## 2. Ongoing Care & Maintenance



### Daily Hygiene:


Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush

Floss daily, especially around bridgework; consider floss threaders or water flossers for easier access


### Protecting Your Crown or Bridge:


Avoid biting into hard foods like ice or nuts with the crowned tooth

Wear a nightguard if you grind or clench your teeth

Maintain regular dental cleanings every 6 months


### Product Tips:


Use fluoride toothpaste to help protect the underlying tooth structure

Anti-sensitivity toothpaste may help if sensitivity lingers


## 3. Recognizing Complications & When to Contact Oceanic Dental



### Please contact us if you notice:


Persistent pain or discomfort when biting

Crown or bridge feels loose, cracked, or dislodged

Gum swelling, bleeding, or signs of infection

Bite feels uneven after a few days


## 4. Recovery Tips & Best Practices



### Helpful Tips for Long-Term Success:


Treat crowns and bridges like your natural teeth – clean them daily

Be gentle when flossing around bridge anchors

Use a nightguard if recommended to prevent damage

Friendly Reminder:"A crown or bridge restores your bite – your care helps it last."


## 5. Follow-Up & Contact Information


Temporary crowns or bridges may need follow-up for permanent placement within 2–3 weeks. Be sure to attend your scheduled appointment to complete treatment.
