## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about our premier cosmetic, implant, and restorative dental services in Huntington Beach.

What is included in a smile makeover in Huntington Beach?

A smile makeover at Oceanic Dental can include porcelain veneers, teeth whitening, crowns, bonding, and sometimes Invisalign. Each case is customized based on your goals, facial features, and bite health. We use digital smile design to preview your results before treatment starts.

How long does a full-mouth reconstruction take at your Huntington Beach office?

Full-mouth reconstruction timelines vary based on the complexity of your case. Most patients complete treatment in 3 to 12 months. The process may include crowns, implants, bridges, bite correction, or dentures—all coordinated in a personalized, step-by-step plan.

Are dental implants offered at your Huntington Beach location?

Yes, we offer dental implants for single-tooth replacement, implant-supported bridges, and full-arch restorations like All-on-X. Dr. Noh, our oral surgery specialist and Dr. Tanaka, our in-house prosthodontist, specialize in restoring function and aesthetics using the most advanced implant systems.

What is the difference between All-on-X and regular dentures?

All-on-X uses 4 to 6 dental implants to support a fixed, full-arch prosthesis. Unlike traditional dentures, it doesn’t move, doesn’t need adhesive, and feels more like natural teeth. It's an ideal solution for patients seeking long-term stability and comfort.

How do I know if I need full-mouth reconstruction instead of individual procedures?

You might be a candidate if you have multiple worn, broken, or missing teeth, or if you're experiencing jaw pain or bite collapse. Full-mouth reconstruction combines function and aesthetics to restore your entire smile. Our team performs a full evaluation to determine the best path forward.

What types of veneers offered in Huntington Beach?

At Oceanic Dental, we offer both porcelain veneers for durability and esthetics, and composite veneers for quicker, more affordable cosmetic corrections. Veneers can address gaps, discoloration, uneven shapes, and chipped teeth as part of a smile enhancement plan.

Will my dental insurance cover cosmetic procedures like veneers or whitening?

Cosmetic procedures are typically not covered by dental insurance. However, if the treatment improves function—like crowns, implants, or reconstructive work—it may be partially reimbursed. Our team will help you navigate coverage and financing options.

Is cosmetic dentistry painful?

Most cosmetic procedures, like veneers or whitening, are minimally invasive and not painful. For more complex treatments like crowns or implants, we use gentle techniques and offer sedation options to ensure your comfort every step of the way.

How long does a smile makeover take from start to finish?

It depends on what’s involved. Whitening can be done in a single visit, while veneers or crowns may take a few weeks. Full mouth reconstruction or implants take longer. We’ll map out the timeline with you so everything feels clear and manageable.

Why do Huntington Beach patients choose Oceanic Dental for smile makeovers?

Oceanic Dental is known for combining cutting-edge technology with an artistic approach to cosmetic and restorative dentistry. Patients love our warm, spa-like studio, compassionate team, and the personalized attention we give every smile makeover and full-mouth restoration.
