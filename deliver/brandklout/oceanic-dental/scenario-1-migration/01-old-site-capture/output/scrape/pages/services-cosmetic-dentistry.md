# Crafting Your Radiant Smile


We offer a range of cosmetic dentistry procedures, from porcelain veneers to teeth whitening, to enhance your smile and boost your confidence.


### Porcelain Veneers & Crowns


Porcelain veneers are thin shells that cover the front of a tooth, addressing cosmetic concerns in two visits. Crowns, made from porcelain or a combination of porcelain and metal, provide full coverage and support for damaged, discolored, or weakened teeth.


### Dental Bonding


Bonding refers to the application of a tooth-colored resin to correct imperfections or enhance the shape of teeth. This procedure is often a favorable choice for cosmetic dental enhancements.


### Teeth Whitening


We provide in-house teeth whitening services using Zoom and Opalescence Boost, as well as offering professional-strength whitening kits.


### Dental Implants


Losing teeth can greatly affect not only the appearance of your smile, but also your overall oral health. For a reliable solution, we strongly recommend dental implants. Our team has extensive expertise in the precise placement of dental implants.


### Teeth straightening with Invisalign


With Invisalign clear aligners, teeth can be effectively and discreetly straightened. The comfortable plastic aligner trays are removable for brushing, flossing, and special occasions.


### Gum & Enamel Re-contouring


Gum re-contouring can improve an uneven gum line or a "gummy" smile. Enamel recontouring shapes natural teeth for a better appearance by removing small amounts of enamel to alter length, shape, or surface contours.

## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about cosmetic dentistry.

What is cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth, gums, and smile. Popular treatments include teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, crowns, and orthodontic solutions like Invisalign. These procedures enhance aesthetics, giving you a brighter, more confident smile.

How much does cosmetic dentistry cost?

Costs vary depending on the procedure. For example, teeth whitening may cost $200-$600, while veneers or crowns can range from $1,200 to $2,500 per tooth. We will provide a personalized estimate during your consultation, and flexible payment options may be available.

Is cosmetic dentistry covered by insurance?

Most cosmetic procedures, such as teeth whitening and veneers, are not covered by insurance because they are considered elective. However, if a procedure improves function (e.g., crowns or orthodontics), partial coverage may apply. We’ll help you verify your insurance benefits.

How long do cosmetic dental treatments last?

Longevity depends on the treatment and your oral care routine. For example, professional teeth whitening results can last up to a year, while veneers and crowns can last 10-15 years or longer with proper care. At Oceanic Dental, all procedures are done with your comfort and safety in mind, using advanced techniques and high-quality materials to ensure long-lasting, beautiful results.

Can cosmetic dentistry fix chipped or uneven teeth?

Yes, cosmetic dentistry offers several solutions for chipped or uneven teeth, including veneers, bonding, or crowns. We’ll recommend the best option for your specific case during your consultation.
