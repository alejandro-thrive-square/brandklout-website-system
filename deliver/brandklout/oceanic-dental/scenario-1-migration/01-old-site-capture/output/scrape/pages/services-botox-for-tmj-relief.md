# Botox for TMJ& Teeth Grinding


If you suffer from teeth grinding (bruxism), TMJ pain, or jaw tension, Botox may provide relief. At Oceanic Dental, we use Botox as part of a comprehensive TMJ treatment plan to help relax overactive jaw muscles, reduce pain, and prevent long-term dental damage.


### What is TMJ?


The temporomandibular joint (TMJ) is the hinge that connects your jawbone to your skull, allowing you to chew, speak, and yawn. When the joint or surrounding muscles become inflamed or dysfunctional, it leads to TMJ disorder (TMD), which can cause pain, stiffness, and restricted jaw movement.

Common Causes of TMJ Disorders

‍- Teeth grinding & clenching – Puts excessive strain on the jaw muscles.

- Jaw injuries – Trauma or impact can damage the joint.

- Arthritis – Can wear down the TMJ, leading to dysfunction.

- Stress – Triggers muscle tension and jaw clenching.


### Symptoms of TMJ Dysfunction



### Jaw & Facial Pain


Discomfort ranging from mild aches to severe pain affecting daily activities.


### Jaw Dysfunction


Clicking, popping, stiffness, or lockjaw, making chewing and speaking difficult.


### Head, Neck & Ear Pain


MJ tension can trigger headaches, neck pain, and earaches.


### Swelling & Tooth Sensitivity


Inflammation may cause facial swelling and pressure on teeth.

## How Botox Helps with TMJ & Teeth Grinding


Chronic clenching and grinding put excessive strain on the temporomandibular joint (TMJ), leading to pain, stiffness, headaches, and tooth damage. Botox relaxes these overworked muscles, easing discomfort and protecting your teeth from wear, fractures, and costly restorative treatments.

Botox treatment is minimally invasive, requires no downtime, and takes just a few minutes. Results typically last 3-4 months, sometimes up to 6 months, depending on the severity of the condition. At Oceanic Dental, we take a holistic approach to TMJ treatment, with Botox as part of a comprehensive care plan. Once pain, swelling, and discomfort are under control, we can more easily explore long-term solutions, such as bite realignment, oral appliances, or other corrective treatments, to address the root cause of your TMJ disorder.

### Aesthetic Benefits of Botox for TMJ


Excessive jaw muscle tension can create a wide or bulky lower face. By relaxing these muscles, Botox slims and contours the jawline, enhancing overall facial harmony. Since facial muscles are interconnected, treating TMJ tension can also reduce stress lines on the forehead and around the eyes, resulting in a refreshed, natural look.

## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about Botox for TMJ.

How does Botox relieve TMJ pain?

Botox blocks nerve signals in the jaw muscles, reducing excessive contraction and tension. This helps relieve jaw pain, headaches, and teeth grinding while allowing the jaw to function more comfortably.

How long does Botox last for TMJ treatment?

Results typically last 3-4 months, sometimes up to 6 months, depending on the severity of your symptoms. Regular treatments may be recommended for long-term relief.

Will Botox completely cure my TMJ disorder?

Botox provides temporary relief but does not address the root cause of TMJ issues. Once pain and tension are under control, we can explore long-term solutions such as bite realignment or oral appliances.

How much does TMJ Botox treatment cost?

The cost of TMJ Botox treatment ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the number of units needed and the severity of your condition. A consultation will determine the best treatment plan for you.

Can Botox change my facial appearance?

Yes, Botox relaxes overactive jaw muscles, which can slim a bulky jawline and restore facial balance. It can also reduce tension-related wrinkles for a more refreshed look.
