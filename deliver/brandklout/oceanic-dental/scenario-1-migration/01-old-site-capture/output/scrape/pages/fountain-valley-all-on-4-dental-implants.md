# All-on-X Dental Implants in Fountain Valley


If you’re struggling with missing teeth or loose dentures, All-on-X might be your perfect solution. At Oceanic Dental in Fountain Valley, we provide full-arch implant solutions that look, feel, and function like real teeth—restoring your smile and confidence in just one day.


## Why Choose All-on-X?


All-on-X uses 4 to 6 strategically placed implants to anchor a full arch of teeth. It's more secure than dentures and doesn’t rely on adhesives. You get immediate functionality and natural esthetics—often with a temporary set of teeth delivered same day.


#### Permanent Solution


Say goodbye to dentures. All-on-X implants offer a permanent, secure replacement for missing teeth.


#### Quick Recovery


With Dr. Tanaka’s expertise, many patients enjoy faster recovery times and leave with a new smile the same day.


#### Fewer Implants, Full Results


The procedure uses fewer implants to support a full set of teeth, making it less invasive while still providing full-mouth restoration.


#### Natural Appearance


Your new teeth are custom-made to match the look and feel of natural teeth, giving you a beautiful, natural smile.

## FAQs


Find answers to commonly asked questions about All-on-X dental implants.

What is All-on-X dental implants?

A full arch solution using 4–6 implants to support a fixed bridge. It's a modern alternative to dentures.

How long does the All-on-X procedure take?

You can receive extractions, implants, and temporary teeth all in one day. Final teeth are placed in 3–6 months.

Who is a candidate for All-on-X?

Anyone missing most or all teeth, or frustrated with dentures. Bone grafting can be added if needed.

Is it painful?

With sedation and proper aftercare, most patients report minimal discomfort. Pain is manageable and temporary.

What’s the difference between All-on-X and dentures?

All-on-X is fixed and feels like real teeth. No adhesives, no movement, and better chewing power.

How much does All-on-X cost in Fountain Valley?

Full cases range from $19,000–$30,000 per arch. We offer transparent quotes and financing.

Will my dental insurance cover All-on-X?

Insurance may help cover extractions, scans, or parts of the procedure. We help verify your benefits.

Do I need bone grafting first?

Not always. The angled placement of implants in All-on-X often avoids the need for grafting.

How long will it last?

With good care, All-on-X can last 20+ years or a lifetime.
