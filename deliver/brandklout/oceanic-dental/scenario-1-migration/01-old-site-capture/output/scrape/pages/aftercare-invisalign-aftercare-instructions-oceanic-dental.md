# Invisalign Aftercare Instructions – Oceanic Dental


Congratulations on starting your Invisalign journey with Oceanic Dental! Whether you’re being treated at our Huntington Beach or Fountain Valley location, we’re here to make your smile transformation smooth and successful. These aftercare tips will help you get the best results from your aligners while keeping your teeth and gums healthy along the way.


## 1. Immediate Post-Treatment Instructions



### What to Expect:


Mild soreness or pressure when starting a new set of aligners is completely normal.

A slight lisp may occur at first but usually resolves within a few days.

Increased saliva production or dry mouth may occur temporarily.


### Do’s and Don’ts for the First 24–48 Hours:


Do wear your aligners for at least 20–22 hours per day.

Do keep your aligners in, even while sleeping – they only work when you wear them!

Don’t remove your aligners unnecessarily, especially during the initial adjustment period.

Don’t drink anything other than plain water with aligners in.


#### Comfort Tips:


Use orthodontic wax on sharp edges if needed.

Switch to new aligners at night so you can sleep through the initial pressure.

Take mild pain relievers like ibuprofen if discomfort occurs.


## 2. Ongoing Care & Maintenance



### Daily Oral Hygiene:


Brush and floss after every meal before reinserting your aligners.

Rinse aligners with lukewarm water – never use hot water as it can warp them.

Use a soft toothbrush to clean your aligners daily (no toothpaste – it can scratch them).


### Best Products to Use:


Invisalign Cleaning Crystals or a clear, gentle anti-bacterial soap.

Travel toothbrush and floss for on-the-go care.


### Lifestyle Tips:


Avoid smoking while wearing aligners – it can stain and damage them.

Store aligners in their case when not in use to prevent loss or damage.

Stay consistent – skipping days can delay your treatment.


## 3. Recognizing Complications & When to Contact Oceanic Dental



### Call us if you notice:


Cracked or warped aligners

Significant, persistent pain or gum irritation

Aligners not fitting snugly after the first few days of wear

Lost or broken aligners – we’ll help you stay on track!


## 4. Recovery Tips & Best Practices



### Easy Habits for Success:


Set a reminder on your phone to switch to your next set of aligners on schedule.

Keep a spare case and cleaning kit in your bag or car.

Drink lots of water – it helps keep your mouth fresh and aligners clean.

Tip to Remember:"Every tray takes you one step closer to your dream smile!"


## 5. Follow-Up & Contact Information


Regular check-ins are essential to monitor your progress and make any needed adjustments to your Invisalign plan. Make sure to attend all scheduled appointments.
