## Gotchas

- **Seedance 2.0 lives under `/v1/ai/video/seedance-2-pro-1080p`** (create and status), not
  `image-to-video/seedance-pro-1080p` (404, and its docs page 404s). Params: `image`, `image_end`,
  `duration` 4–15, `camera_fixed`, `sound_effects`. ~10 minutes a clip; output 1920×1080 24 fps.
  `seedance-2-fast-1080p` is 403 for this key.
- **Kling 2.6 creates at `kling-v2-6-pro` but reports at `/ai/image-to-video/kling-v2-6/{id}`.**
  The first A/B run 404'd on the status poll and orphaned a paid Seedance task; it was recovered from
  `GET /ai/video/seedance-2-pro-1080p` (the task list). The script now records a task id the moment
  it exists and resumes it.
- Two runs in flight overwrite each other's state file; `save()` now merges with disk.
- **A capture that shows the same frame while `currentTime` moves is a layering bug, not
  headless Chrome** — I first blamed headless and shipped a hero whose film nobody could see. Check
  what paints on top (`elementFromPoint`, z-index) before blaming the browser.
- Previewing over `python -m http.server`: it has no Range support, so seeking restarts the film
  (watch it play instead), and it holds `final-site/` open — stop it before `build-site.mjs`
  (EPERM on `rmSync`). Claude in Chrome cannot open `file://`; serve on localhost.
- Mystic draws pseudo-brand lettering on cameras and monitors; zoom every keyframe before animating —
  the video model carries the marks through every frame.
