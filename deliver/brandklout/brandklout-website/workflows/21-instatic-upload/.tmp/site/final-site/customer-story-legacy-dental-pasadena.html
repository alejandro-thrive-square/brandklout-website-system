THE CHALLENGE

Clinical quality was not enough on its own.

The practice needed its real expertise and patient experience to become visible wherever prospective patients compared their options.

01
Scattered proof

Reviews, doctor expertise and treatment results did not reinforce one another.

02
Channel-by-channel marketing

Traffic could grow without a clear line to patients, cases or collections.

03
High-value treatment competition

Invisalign growth required authority, education and consistent follow-through.

WHAT CHANGED

One doctor story, reinforced everywhere.

BrandKlout connected the doctor's expertise, patient experiences and Invisalign content into one consistent story, then tracked that work alongside new patients, treatment mix and collections.

01
Capture patient proof

Make real experiences visible across the platforms patients use to validate a provider.

02
Connect proof to treatments

Align credentials, reviews and content with Invisalign and other priority categories.

03
Measure the business impact

Track patients, treatment mix and collections alongside search and reputation signals.

Authority became measurable practice growth.

WHY THIS STORY MATTERS

The lesson is not that one tactic created the result. The advantage came from aligning reputation, visibility, content, conversion and measurement around the same doctor and the same growth goal.
