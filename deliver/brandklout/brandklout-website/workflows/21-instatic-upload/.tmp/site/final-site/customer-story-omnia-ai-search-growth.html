THE CHALLENGE

AI needs evidence it can connect.

A service page alone does not make a doctor the obvious recommendation. The systems need consistent evidence across the wider public web.

01
Provider clarity

The doctor, practice, location and services need unambiguous relationships.

02
Treatment depth

Implant and Invisalign content must answer real patient decisions, not just define procedures.

03
External validation

Reviews and third-party signals need to corroborate the story on the practice website.

WHAT CHANGED

Make the evidence easier to retrieve and trust.
