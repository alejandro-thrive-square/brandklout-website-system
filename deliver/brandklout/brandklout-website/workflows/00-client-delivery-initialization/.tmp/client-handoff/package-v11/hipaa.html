HIPAA & data handling

Public marketing forms and client data workflows should be treated differently.

Draft explanatory page for counsel and compliance review. Whether HIPAA applies depends on the service, data flow, vendor configuration and executed agreements.

Do not submit PHI through this public website

BrandKlout’s public forms are intended for business contact and practice-level information. Do not submit patient names, diagnoses, images, treatment records, appointment details or other protected health information through these forms.

Client workflows

Some client services may involve systems that contain patient or lead information. Where BrandKlout acts as a business associate and HIPAA applies, the parties should use an appropriate Business Associate Agreement and approved systems and subprocessors.

Minimum necessary access

BrandKlout’s operating goal is to limit access to the information reasonably needed for the service and to separate public marketing collection from protected clinical workflows.

Questions

Clients should raise HIPAA, BAA, security and vendor questions during onboarding so the required data path can be confirmed before sensitive information is shared.
