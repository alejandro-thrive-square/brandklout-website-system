Information we collect

We may collect contact information, practice information and details you choose to provide through forms, scheduling tools or communications with BrandKlout. We may also collect standard website analytics such as device, browser, referrer and page interaction data.

How we use information

We use information to respond to requests, evaluate market fit, provide services, improve the website, communicate about BrandKlout and protect our systems.

No patient information through public forms

Public BrandKlout website forms are not designed for protected health information. Do not submit patient names, clinical information, treatment records or other PHI through these forms.

Service providers

We may use vendors for hosting, analytics, scheduling, CRM, communications and other business operations. Their handling of information is subject to applicable agreements and settings.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of certain information by contacting BrandKlout through your existing business contact channel. Some records may be retained where required for legitimate business, security or legal purposes.

Updates

We may update this policy as our website, services and legal obligations change. The version posted here should show the effective date when finalized.
