# Dr. Maurice Srour, DDS


Board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon specializing in surgical implant placement, bone grafting, and complex extractions. NYU-trained, with hospital-based experience in trauma and reconstruction.


## Training & Surgical Background


Dr. Srour earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the New York University College of Dentistry, then completed his Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Certificate at NYU Medical Center and Bellevue Hospital Center. He is board certified and has more than 10 years of surgical experience. Dr. Srour has completed more than 5,000 full-arch cases and placed more than 20,000 dental implants. He also has advanced training in remote-anchorage implant techniques, including pterygoid, zygomatic, and transnasal implants.

Dr. Srour has also served as a key member of the team at ClearChoice Dental Implant Centers and remains on staff at Trinitas Regional Medical Center in Elizabeth, NJ, where he continues to serve the community with a focus on trauma, pathology, and reconstruction. His professional affiliations have included the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, the American College of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, and the American Dental Association. His surgical background allows Hackensack Center to handle extractions, bone grafting, and implant placement in-house, under one roof.
