# How Much Do Dental Implants Cost in Hackensack, NJ? A Complete 2026 Guide

Text Link

If you are researching dental implants in Hackensack, NJ, the cost can be difficult to understand. Every patient has different clinical needs, and the final price may be affected by the number of implants, extractions, bone grafting, sedation, temporary teeth, and the design of the final restoration.

At Hackensack Center for Dental Implants, every patient receives an individualized treatment plan after a complete evaluation. This guide explains the factors that affect dental implant costs, what to look for in an itemized estimate, how insurance may contribute, and which financing options may be available.


## Dental Implant Costs in Hackensack, NJ: What to Expect


Here are our transparent published prices for 2026, listed from the most affordable option to the most premium:

Single-tooth implant: Pricing depends on whether the estimate includes the implant post, abutment, final crown, imaging, extraction, bone grafting, and sedation.

Implant-supported removable denture: Cost varies according to the number of implants, existing bone, denture design, and materials.

All-On-X full-arch treatment: Pricing depends on extractions, implant quantity, sedation, temporary teeth, and the final fixed restoration.

Fixed zirconia or FP1 restoration: Cost varies according to the implant foundation, prosthetic design, materials, and laboratory work.

Advanced bone-loss treatment: Patients requiring grafting or another advanced surgical approach receive a customized treatment estimate.

Compare implant estimates according to what is included—not only the advertised starting price. Ask whether the estimate covers imaging, extractions, grafting, sedation, temporary teeth, the final restoration, adjustments, and postoperative care. Hackensack Center provides an itemized treatment plan so you can understand each proposed service before proceeding.


## What Affects the Final Cost of Your Dental Implants?


The starting prices above cover the implants and final restoration for a standard case. Your individual treatment plan may include additional procedures that affect the total cost. These are the most common variables:


### Bone Grafting


Some patients need bone grafting before or during implant placement because the jawbone has changed after tooth loss or long-term denture use. The need for grafting, the type of graft, and its associated cost are determined after a clinical examination and 3D imaging. Any recommended grafting should appear as a separate item in your treatment estimate.


### Sinus Lift


For upper-arch implants near the sinus cavity, a sinus lift may be needed to create enough bone for stable implant placement. Sinus lifts typically add $1,500 to $2,500 to your treatment cost. For patients with severe upper-jaw bone loss who want to avoid sinus lifts entirely, zygomatic implants offer an alternative path.


### Tooth Extractions


If failing teeth must be removed before implant placement, extractions are typically billed separately at $150 to $400 per tooth. Insurance often covers extractions even when it does not cover the implant itself.


### Sedation Options


Sedation options are available to help patients remain comfortable during implant treatment. The recommended method depends on the procedure, medical history, anxiety level, and clinical team involved. Your treatment coordinator will explain the sedation plan and whether its cost is included or listed separately before treatment begins.

Our financial coordinator walks every patient through their personalized cost breakdown and financing options before treatment begins.


## How To Make Dental Implants Affordable in Hackensack, NJ


The published prices are just the starting point. Most of our patients pair partial insurance coverage with monthly financing to make implant treatment fit their budget. Here is how it works.


### Insurance Coverage


Most dental insurance plans classify the implant itself as cosmetic and offer limited direct coverage. However, many plans do contribute toward related procedures:

Tooth extractions before implant placement

Bone grafting and sinus lifts

The final crown or prosthetic restoration

Routine cleanings during your treatment journey

Our dedicated insurance coordinator verifies every patient's benefits before treatment and submits claims electronically on your behalf. Visit our Insurance & Financing page to learn more about how we maximize your coverage.

### HSA and FSA Funds


Dental implants, including the surgery, abutment, and final crown, are typically eligible expenses under both Health Savings Accounts (HSA) and Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA). Our financial coordinator can provide all the documentation you need for reimbursement and help you maximize tax benefits.

# Frequently Asked Questions


How much does a single dental implant cost in Hackensack, NJ?

Single-tooth implant pricing depends on whether treatment includes an extraction, bone grafting, imaging, sedation, implant placement, an abutment, and the final crown. After evaluating your needs, Hackensack Center will provide an itemized estimate showing what is included and which services, if any, are separate.

How much does All-On-X cost per arch in Hackensack, NJ?

All-On-X pricing varies according to the number of implants, extractions, available bone, sedation needs, temporary teeth, and the final restoration. Following a clinical and 3D evaluation, the treatment team will provide an itemized estimate explaining every proposed component.

Why do advertised full-arch implant prices vary so much?

Advertised prices may include different services and materials. Some estimates cover only surgery and temporary teeth, while others include imaging, extractions, sedation, implants, temporary teeth, and the final restoration. Always request a written, itemized estimate and confirm exactly what is included before comparing providers.

Does dental insurance cover dental implants in Hackensack, NJ?

Coverage varies by insurance plan. Some plans may contribute toward implant treatment or related services such as extractions, grafting, crowns, or routine care. Hackensack Center can verify your available benefits and submit eligible claims, but coverage and reimbursement are determined by the insurance carrier.

Can I finance dental implants with bad credit?

Financing approval depends on the lender, applicant, requested amount, and credit profile. Hackensack Center currently works with Cherry, HFD, Proceed, and CareCredit. Each provider has its own eligibility requirements and terms, so approval, interest rates, and monthly payments cannot be guaranteed.

What payment plans are available for dental implants?

Financing options are available through Cherry, HFD, Proceed, and CareCredit for qualified applicants. Terms, interest rates, down payments, and monthly payments vary. The treatment coordinator can help you review the programs currently available.

Are sedation options available for dental implant treatment?

Sedation options are available for appropriate patients. The recommended method and associated cost depend on the procedure, medical history, and treatment team. Your sedation plan and any related charges will be explained before treatment.

Can I use my HSA or FSA for dental implants?

Yes. Dental implants including the surgery, abutment, and final crown are typically eligible expenses under both Health Savings Accounts (HSA) and Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA). Our financial coordinator can provide all the documentation you need for reimbursement and help you structure payment to maximize tax benefits.

Is an implant consultation complimentary at Hackensack Center for Dental Implants?

Yes. Hackensack Center offers complimentary implant consultations. The team will discuss your concerns and treatment goals. Any diagnostic imaging or additional services and their associated costs should be explained before they are performed.

How does Hackensack Center prepare dental implant estimates?

Hackensack Center prepares an individualized treatment plan based on your anatomy, health, functional needs, and restorative goals. The estimate should identify the proposed procedures, temporary restoration, final restoration, sedation plan, laboratory work, and any additional services so you can understand the complete expected cost.
