## Frequently Asked Questions


Am I too old for dental implants?

No. Age itself is not a barrier. Patients in their 70s, 80s, and beyond receive implants every year and heal well, provided general health supports it. What matters is your overall health and the quality of your bone, both of which are checked at your consultation.

Do dental implants hurt?

Most patients are surprised how comfortable it is. Placement is done under local anesthetic, and sedation is available for anyone who feels anxious. Any soreness afterward is usually managed with simple over-the-counter care.

How long does the implant process take?

From placement to your final tooth is usually a few months, because the implant needs time to fuse with the bone. Appointments themselves are short. Most of the timeline is quiet healing, not chair time.

How much do dental implants cost?

Cost depends on the type of case, from a single implant to a full-arch reconstruction. After your 3D scan you receive a clear written estimate with no surprises, plus financing options that spread the cost into monthly payments.

Full cost breakdown →

What if I do not have enough bone?

You may still qualify. Focused bone grafts can rebuild sites where the bone has shrunk, and angled implant designs like All-on-4 are built to work with limited bone. A 3D scan is the only way to know for certain, and many patients who were told no elsewhere still qualify here.

How long do dental implants last?

With good hygiene and regular check-ups, the implant itself can last for decades, often for life. The crown on top is durable and can be maintained or replaced over the years if needed.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We will find a way. We try every avenue, and our dedicated team of treatment coordinators will help you find a plan that fits. We work with Cherry, HFD, Proceed, and CareCredit, and we verify any insurance benefits up front.
