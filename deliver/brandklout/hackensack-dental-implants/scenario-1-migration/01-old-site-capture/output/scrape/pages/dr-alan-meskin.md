# Dr. Alan Meskin, Prosthodontist and Implant Specialist


NYU-trained and focused entirely on replacing and restoring teeth, Dr. Alan Meskin specializes in dental implants, full-mouth reconstruction, and cosmetic smile design, including the complex cases other offices refer out.


## Meet Dr. Meskin


Dr. Alan Meskin is a prosthodontist, a dental specialist trained specifically in replacing and restoring teeth. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery from NYU College of Dentistry in 2019, then completed a further certificate in prosthodontics at NYU in 2022, focusing on implant restoration and full-mouth reconstruction. Dr. Meskin describes himself simply: a prosthodontist dedicated to smile design and full-mouth rehabilitation, with a focus on implant prosthetics. That focus is deliberate. By concentrating on replacing and restoring teeth rather than spreading across every area of dentistry, he is able to bring real depth to the cases that need it most.

His work centers on one goal: rebuilding smiles so naturally that no one can tell. Patients describe him as gentle and thorough, a dentist who takes the time to explain, listens first, and puts anxious patients at ease. He provides care in English, Spanish, and Russian. His philosophy is patient-first and detail-driven. Every plan begins by listening to what the patient actually wants, and every restoration is designed to look and function like a natural tooth. He is committed to continuing education, regularly taking courses and bringing the newest techniques in digital and implant dentistry back to his patients.
