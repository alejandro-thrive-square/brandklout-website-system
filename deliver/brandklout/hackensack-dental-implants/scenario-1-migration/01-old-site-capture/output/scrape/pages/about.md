# About Hackensack Center for Dental Implants


Hackensack Center for Dental Implants is a specialist practice built around one focus: replacing and restoring teeth exceptionally well. Led by prosthodontist Dr. Alan Meskin, we combine advanced technology, an on-site lab, and genuinely personal care to rebuild smiles that look natural and last.

Dr. Alan Meskin built this practice on a simple idea: that rebuilding a smile is as much about the person as the teeth. As a prosthodontist, he completed years of specialty training at NYU focused entirely on replacing and restoring teeth, and he has devoted his career to the work a lot of general offices refer out, from full-arch implants to complex, full-mouth reconstruction.

That experience adds up. Three decades of implant and reconstructive work, with thousands of implants placed and over 1,000 full-arch cases completed. Every one of them was planned in 3D before it began, and the teeth themselves were designed and crafted in our own on-site lab, so nothing gets shipped out and lost in a handoff. It means fewer visits, tighter quality control, and a result made to fit your face rather than pulled from a catalog.

Just as important is how the practice feels. Many of the people who come to us have put off care for years, often because a past experience left them anxious or embarrassed. We built the opposite of that: a calm, judgment-free place on Main Street where questions are welcome, comfort comes first, and care is offered in English, Spanish, and Russian.

Our pledge is straightforward. We will help you understand your options honestly, plan the path that genuinely fits your needs and your budget, and stay with you from the first consultation to the final smile. Thank you for visiting, and we look forward to meeting you.

-Dr. Alan Meskin

## The Technology Behind Better Implant Results


Precision is what separates an implant that lasts decades from one that fails early. Every case here is planned digitally before any treatment begins, which means fewer surprises, fewer appointments, and a result that fits the way it was designed to.

Cone Beam 3D Imaging

Digital X-rays

Intraoral Scanners

3D Printing

CAD/CAM Design

A three-dimensional scan of your jaw that shows exactly where the bone is, where the nerves and sinuses sit, and where each implant can be placed safely. This is where your treatment is planned, before anything else happens.

Detailed images captured in seconds with significantly less radiation than traditional film. Dr. Meskin can show you what he sees on screen during your visit, so you understand your diagnosis rather than just hearing it.

A small handheld scanner takes a precise digital impression of your mouth without the mess of putty trays. Faster, far more comfortable, and accurate down to the detail your final teeth depend on.

Surgical guides, models, and provisional teeth printed in-house in our own lab. It is what allows us to place implants exactly where they were planned and hand you a new smile the same day.

CAD/CAM

Your new teeth are designed digitally, down to the shape, shade, and how they meet your bite, then produced with computer-guided precision rather than by hand. It is what lets us match a result to your face instead of pulling from a catalog.

## Frequently Asked Questions


What makes Hackensack Center different?

We are a specialist practice focused on implants and restorative dentistry, led by a prosthodontist, with an on-site lab and care in three languages. That combination is unusual, and it is what lets us take on the complex cases many general offices refer out.

What is a prosthodontist?

A prosthodontist is a dental specialist who completed years of additional training beyond dental school, focused specifically on replacing and restoring teeth. For implants, full-arch work, and cosmetic reconstruction, that specialization means more predictable, natural-looking results.

Do you only do implants?

No. Implants are our primary focus, but Dr. Meskin also does full-mouth reconstruction and cosmetic smile makeovers. Whatever your smile needs, it can be handled under one roof.

Where are you located?

We are at 163 Main Street, Suite A, in Hackensack, serving the greater Bergen County area.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We work with Cherry, HFD, Proceed, and CareCredit, and our treatment coordinators will help you find a plan that fits. We also verify any insurance benefits up front.
