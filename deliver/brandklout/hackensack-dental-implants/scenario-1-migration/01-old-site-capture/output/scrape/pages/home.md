## Frequently Asked Questions


How much do dental implants cost in Hackensack?

Dental implant costs vary depending on your treatment, since a single tooth implant costs significantly less than a full-arch restoration. After your 3D scan, you'll receive a clear written estimate with no surprises, plus financing options that can bring your cost down to a manageable monthly payment.

Does getting dental implants hurt?

Local anesthesia is used during dental implant placement to manage pain during the procedure. Sedation options may also be available for anxiety. Discomfort, swelling, or soreness afterward varies by patient. Follow your postoperative instructions, and contact the office if pain is severe or worsening.

How long does the process take?

The timeline depends on the number of implants, whether grafting is needed, and how you heal. Some eligible full-arch patients can receive fixed temporary teeth on surgery day, while implant integration may take several months before the final restoration is placed.

I was told I do not have enough bone. Can I still get implants?

Possibly. A 3D CBCT scan can show how much bone is available. Bone grafting or another treatment approach may make implants possible, but only an in-person evaluation can determine whether you are a candidate.

Can a failed or loose implant be fixed?

Often, but the right treatment depends on why the implant is loose or failing and on the surrounding bone and gum tissue. A prosthodontic evaluation and 3D imaging can determine whether the implant can be restored, revised, or needs replacement.

Why see a prosthodontist instead of a general dentist for implants?

Prosthodontists complete three additional years of accredited specialty training after dental school in restoring and replacing teeth. That training is especially relevant to full-arch rehabilitation, complex bite and cosmetic cases, and failed implant restorations.

Do you offer financing and take insurance?

Yes, we offer flexible financing through Cherry, HFD, Proceed, and CareCredit, including low monthly payment options for qualified applicants. While we are not in-network with dental insurance, we verify your benefits and apply them toward your treatment before you begin, so cost never stands between you and the smile you need.
