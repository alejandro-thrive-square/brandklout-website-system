Ask us your questions →
How do I know if I am a candidate for dental implants?

Candidacy depends on your oral health, overall health, bone structure and treatment goals. A clinical exam and 3D imaging help the team determine your options.

Can implants replace an entire arch of teeth?

Yes. Full-arch implant treatment can support a fixed restoration for qualifying patients who are missing most or all teeth in an arch.

What if I have been told I do not have enough bone?

Some patients may benefit from grafting or other surgical approaches. The appropriate option can only be determined after diagnostic imaging and evaluation.

How long does implant treatment take?

Timing varies by case. Healing, bone quality, extractions, grafting and the type of restoration can all affect the treatment timeline.

Do you offer financing?

Financing and payment options can be discussed with the treatment team after your personalized plan is established.
