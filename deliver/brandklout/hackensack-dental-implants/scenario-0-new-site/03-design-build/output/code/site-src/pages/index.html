The Visit

What to Expect

Every part of a visit to Hackensack Center is designed around your comfort, from the space you walk into, to the technology behind your care, to how we handle the cost.

Walking in should not feel like the dentist you remember. Our Hackensack office was built to feel calm, with a modern, spa-like reception, a TV in every treatment room, and aromatherapy throughout, so the clinical smell that puts most people on edge is not the first thing you notice.

From your first visit to your final smile, you are cared for by one specialist and one dedicated team, never handed off or rushed. Your first appointment is an unhurried, one-on-one consultation where Dr. Meskin listens to your goals, answers every question, and builds a plan around you. Sedation options are always available, and our coordinators stay with you at every step, so nothing about the process feels overwhelming.

Precision starts with the right tools. We use 3D CBCT imaging and digital scanners to plan every case down to the detail, and our on-site dental lab lets us design and craft your teeth in-house rather than shipping your case out. That means less guesswork, fewer visits, and for many patients, a fixed set of teeth the same day as surgery.

Most practices send your case to an outside lab and wait weeks for it to return. Ours is in the same building. Dr. Meskin and the lab technician design and craft your teeth together, refining the fit and finish in person rather than mailing a case back and forth. It means fewer visits, tighter control over the result, and for many patients, a fixed set of teeth the same day as surgery.

We will work with you using whatever financial options are available to you. We will find a way. We try every avenue, and our dedicated team of treatment coordinators is here to walk you through every detail of your treatment and financing options, so cost never stands between you and the smile you need.
