— How it works

Your Dental Work Shouldn't Look & Feel Like Dental Work

Dr. Kim created the Dedicated Comprehensive Oral Evaluation (dCOE) for patients who want the full picture before a single decision is made.

Two dedicated visits. Complete records. A clear diagnosis - because lasting implant treatment begins with understanding, not urgency.

1 Consultation & 3D scan

A full evaluation of your bone, bite, and goals with CBCT imaging — plus a clear treatment plan and exact fee before anything begins.

2 Guided placement

Your implant is placed using a digital surgical guide, with sedation available. Qualifying patients can leave with a temporary tooth the same day.

3 Your final tooth

Once healed, your custom porcelain crown is fitted — designed before day one to match your smile and last for decades.
