FAQs

Expert answers for your smile transformation.

How much do dental implants cost in Westlake, TX?

A single dental implant at Stone Dental Implant Center typically ranges from $3,500 to $6,000 in the Westlake and DFW area. Full-arch All-on-X cases vary based on materials, bone condition, and prosthetic type. We provide a full itemized quote at your consultation, including financing through Lending Club, Cherry, Sunbit and Proceed Finance.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients describe dental implant surgery as far more manageable than they expected. Dr. Stone uses IV sedation for most cases, so you are comfortably asleep during the procedure. Mild soreness for 2 to 3 days afterward is normal and easily managed with prescription or over-the-counter pain relief.

Am I still a candidate for dental implants if I have severe bone loss?

In most cases, yes. Dr. Stone handles complex cases that other clinics often turn away. Options for severe bone loss include bone grafting, sinus lifts, Bone Morphogenetic Protein procedures, and IPS Pre-Prosthetic, a custom 3D-printed titanium implant for cases where traditional implants are not possible.

What is the difference between All-on-X and IPS Pre-Prosthetic implants?

All-on-X uses 4 to 6 traditional implants placed in your jawbone to support a full arch of fixed teeth. IPS Pre-Prosthetic is a custom 3D-printed titanium framework designed for patients with severely atrophic bone where standard implants would fail. Dr. Stone offers both based on what your anatomy requires.

How long does it take to recover from dental implant surgery?

Most patients return to light work within 2 to 3 days. The implants take 4 to 6 months to fully fuse with the jawbone, during which you wear a functional temporary set of teeth. Your permanent custom-designed prosthesis is placed once integration is complete.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Some PPO dental plans cover portions of implant treatment, especially extractions, bone grafts, and sedation. Stone Dental Implant Center works with most major PPO plans as an out-of-network provider and provides insurance estimates before treatment begins. We do not accept Medicaid or government grant programs.

What sedation options do you offer for oral surgery?

Stone Dental Implant Center offers three sedation levels: Nitrous Oxide for mild anxiety, Oral Sedation for moderate anxiety, and IV Sedation for full sleep during surgery. Most complex cases including All-on-X and full-arch reconstruction are performed under IV sedation in our Westlake office.

How are wisdom teeth removed and is it covered by insurance?

Dr. Stone removes wisdom teeth using minimally invasive surgical techniques, typically under IV sedation in a single visit. Most dental and medical insurance plans cover impacted wisdom teeth removal when medically necessary. Recovery takes 3 to 5 days for most patients.

Do you accept patients traveling from outside the DFW area?

Yes. Many of Dr. Stone's patients travel from across North Texas and out of state for complex All-on-X and full-arch reconstruction cases. We offer virtual consultations for out-of-town patients, allowing you to start treatment planning before flying in.

Can I get implants and new teeth in the same day?

Yes. Same-Day Implants and All-on-X procedures allow you to leave with a full set of functional teeth on the day of surgery. Your permanent custom-designed teeth are placed once your implants have fully integrated with the bone, typically 4 to 6 months later.
