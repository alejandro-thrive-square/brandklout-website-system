**Downloaded 2026-09-01 as RESEARCH. None of them is on the site and none may go on it until it is
cleared.**

The client asked whether there were any location photographs of Stone Surgical Arts on the internet.
There are: Southlake Style covered the Westlake opening on **12 February 2021** and published eleven
frames.

<https://www.southlakestyle.com/upcoming-events/photos/photos-stone-surgical-arts-opens-in-westlake/>

## Why they are not on the site

**The copyright is Southlake Style's and its photographer's, not the practice's.** A third party's
photograph on a client's commercial website without a licence is exposure for Brandklout as well as
for the client, and the fact that the subject is the client's own building does not change who owns
the photograph of it.

This is the same class of decision as the before/after warning in
[HANDOFF.md](../../../04-design/.tmp/surgical-arts/HANDOFF.md): the material exists, it is tempting,
and the reason not to publish it is not about quality.

## The ask

One line to Southlake Style requesting permission to use **`TRG_1543`** on stonesurgicalarts.com.
Dr. Stone hosted the event, so it is very likely to be granted. If it is, ask for `TRG_1512` and
`TRG_1488` at the same time; the site has no photograph of a treatment room that is not either
generated or cluttered.

The better long-term answer is the practice's own photographer, which is already item 1 of "Before
this goes live" in the handoff.

## What else was checked, and came back dry

- **`stonesurgicalarts.com`** 301s to `stonedentalimplantcenter.com`, whose imagery is stock plus one
  portrait of Dr. Stone that is already in the library.
- **The archived image list** (`../archive-text/live/_images.txt`) is Webflow CDN URLs whose
  filenames are stock library ids (`imgi_NN_581579069` and so on).
- **The client's Drive export** holds six real practice photographs and they were all imported in an
  earlier session: the signage, the front desk, the team, a consult room, a treatment room and one
  staff portrait. Everything else in it is stock models or before/after cases.
