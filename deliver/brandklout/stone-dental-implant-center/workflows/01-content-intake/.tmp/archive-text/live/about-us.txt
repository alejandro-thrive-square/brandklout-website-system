Our Story

## Why we built a center for implants alone.

Stone Dental Implant Center grew out of a simple realization. The patients facing the hardest decisions - full-arch reconstruction, failed implants, years lost to dentures that never felt right, deserved a practice built entirely around them.

So we separated this work from everything else. Not a department inside a larger clinic, but a dedicated center where complex implant cases are the focus, not the exception.

That focus changes everything: how a case is planned, how much time it gets, and how the final result is judged. Here, success isn't only an implant that holds. It's a smile that looks like it always belonged to your face.

"Most surgeons replace what's missing. I design what belongs."
Dr. Mona Stone

Meet Your Doctor

## Dr. Mona Stone

Dr. Stone leads every case at Stone Dental Implant Center personally, from the first 3D scan to the final smile placed months later. There are no handoffs here, no rotating providers. The surgeon who plans your case is the one who carries it through.

What sets her apart isn't only surgical skill, though that's the foundation. It's the eye she brings to the work. She sees the face the way a plastic surgeon does - reading proportion, balance, the way a jawline frames everything around it. Not just what's missing, but what's possible. Not just whether an implant will hold, but how the result will look, feel, and age over the years that follow.

It's a way of working that's rare in implant surgery, and it's the reason patients travel to find her - many after being told, somewhere else, that nothing more could be done.
Meet Dr. Stone
