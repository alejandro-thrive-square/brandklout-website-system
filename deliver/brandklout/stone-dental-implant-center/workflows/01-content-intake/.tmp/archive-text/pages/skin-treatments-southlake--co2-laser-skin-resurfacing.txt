## WHAT IS CO2 ABLATIVE LASER SKIN RESURFACING?

Fractionated CO2 ablative lasers deliver intense, short microbeams of heat energy to the deeper skin layers, kickstarting the skin’s natural reparative abilities. New collagen begins to flourish, and the skin becomes tighter and smoother as a result. Fractional lasers also target discoloration without stripping away layers of skin like an ablative laser would.

Revitalize and Rejuvenate

## WHAT CAN LASER SKIN RESURFACING TREATMENT DO FOR ME?

CO2 laser skin resurfacing treatment is an excellent way to improve fine lines, moderate wrinkles, dark spots, acne scars, enlarged pores, and sun damage. It comes with a brief downtime of ten days, so you can get back to your usual daily activities without taking weeks off work. Our state-of-the-art CO2 ablative lasers are fully customizable, targeting your exact skin concerns with minimal damage to the surrounding tissues. You can use CO2 laser skin resurfacing to rejuvenate the skin nearly anywhere on your body, including your face, neck, décolletage, shoulders, arms, hands, abdomen, and legs.

- Relatively quick downtime

## Am I a candidate for Laser Skin Resurfacing?

You may be a prime candidate for CO2 laser resurfacing if you have moderate textural or tonal concerns. CO2 lasers work well on Fitzpatrick Skin Types I-III. However, it is not for patients that are pregnant, nursing, or have an active infection or sores at the treatment site. You might not be a candidate if you have a history of bleeding disorders, seizures, or photosensitivity.

Efficient and Comfortable

## Procedure

Our Laser Skin Resurfacing in Westlake takes approximately 30 minutes to an hour . Your aesthetic provider will apply a topical numbing agent. Depending on the depth of the laser settings, they may also administer a nerve block or oral sedation before your treatment begins. They will work the device over your skin in a grid-like fashion. It should not feel painful, but you will likely feel a sting or warmth as they pulse the laser over your skin. Once completed, they will apply Vaseline or Aquaphor. Then, you’ll return home to begin your recovery.

Rejuvenation Journey

## Results, Recovery, and Aftercare

Someone may need to drive you home. Your skin may look and feel badly sunburned initially.

Swelling, redness, and crusting are usually worst during the first 48 hours, but can last several days. Light peeling and oozing may occur. Allow seven to ten days before returning to work and wearing makeup. Following Laser Skin Resurfacing Westlake patients can apply cool compresses as needed. Be sure to keep your skin moisturized with an occlusive as instructed. After a week or so, you can switch to a bland moisturizer. To help prevent hyperpigmentation, avoid sun exposure until fully healed. After two weeks, apply a broad-spectrum sunblock (SPF 30 or greater) daily. Plan to return to Stone Surgical Arts ten days later so your provider can monitor your progress. It can take several weeks to months for the full benefits of your CO2 laser treatment to unfold, but the results are usually well worth the wait.

## Laser Skin Resurfacing Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Laser Skin Resurfacing painful?

There can be some discomfort, warmth, or stinging. Topical, local, or oral sedation will make the process as comfortable as possible.

## What are the risks associated with Laser Skin Resurfacing?

Infection, residual swelling, prolonged redness, oozing, and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation are possible. The providers at Stone Surgical Arts are versed in the latest and safest techniques to minimize unwanted side effects.

## Is Laser Skin Resurfacing permanent?

The results of CO2 laser resurfacing can last a long time as long as you practice sun safety and care for your skin.

## Will Laser Skin Resurfacing work with my skin type?

CO2 lasers are compatible with Fitzpatrick Skin Types I-III. Those with medium and darker complexions may not be good candidates.
