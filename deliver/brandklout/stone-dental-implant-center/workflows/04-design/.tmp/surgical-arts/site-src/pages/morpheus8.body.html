Recovery figures are typical, not a promise. Yours is set out in your written plan.

Microneedling works by injuring the skin in a controlled way so it rebuilds. Morpheus8 does the same thing but delivers radiofrequency energy from the tips of the needles, at a set depth, so the heating happens in the layer where collagen is made rather than at the surface.

That combination is why it sits between skincare and surgery. It tightens as well as resurfaces, and because the energy is delivered below the surface, the skin on top recovers in days rather than a week.

Who it suits

The patient who wants meaningful tightening, has some early laxity, and cannot take the downtime that resurfacing or surgery demands.

  • Early softening along the jawline that is not yet a surgical problem
  • Texture, large pores and acne scarring
  • Crepiness under the eyes and around the mouth
  • Skin that needs both tightening and resurfacing, in a schedule that allows neither to be dramatic

It is honest about its limits. Morpheus8 will not lift a jowl or replace a neck suspension. Where the problem is descent rather than skin quality, it is a treatment that costs you money and time and leaves the actual complaint untouched.

Sold as a course, for a reason

The practice prices a single session at $1,000 and a package of three at $2,800, and the package is not a discount tactic. Collagen remodelling is cumulative, and one session produces a fraction of the change that three spaced a month apart will.

Expect to judge the result four to six months after starting, not after the first appointment.

Often combined with

The procedure

What the week actually looks like.

01

The appointment

Numbing cream for around forty-five minutes, then thirty to sixty minutes of treatment. Depths are set by area, deeper along the jaw and shallower under the eyes.

02

The first few days

Redness and a sandpaper texture with a grid pattern that fades over two to four days. Mild swelling on the first evening. Makeup is usually fine at forty-eight hours.

03

The course

Three sessions a month apart. Tightening builds gradually and continues for three to six months after the final session, which is when the result should be judged.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

How is this different from ordinary microneedling?

Ordinary microneedling injures the skin mechanically and works mainly at the surface. Morpheus8 adds radiofrequency heating delivered from the needle tips at a controlled depth, which is what produces the tightening. The practice offers both: microneedling at $380 and SkinPen at $300 per session.

Does it hurt?

It is uncomfortable rather than painful, and topical numbing handles most of it. The deeper settings along the jawline are the ones people notice. It is over in under an hour.

Will it replace a facelift?

No, and it is worth being direct about that. It tightens skin; it does not reposition tissue that has descended. If a jowl is your complaint, this will disappoint you and surgery is the honest answer.

How long do the results last?

The collagen you build is yours and it persists, but you continue to age. Most patients maintain with a single session once or twice a year after completing the initial course.

What happens at the consultation?

You and Dr. Stone go through your concerns and your goals, she takes a medical history and assesses your general health, and photographs and measurements are taken so the plan is drawn against your own anatomy. You leave knowing whether this is the right procedure for you, and if it is not, which one is.
