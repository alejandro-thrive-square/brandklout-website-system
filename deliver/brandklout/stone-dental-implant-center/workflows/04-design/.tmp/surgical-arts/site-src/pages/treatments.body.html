Face

Structure first, skin second.

Facial surgery here starts at the bone and works outward. Implants are planned in three dimensions and made for your anatomy rather than picked from a tray of stock sizes. The practice’s own position is that a generic implant gives a generic result. Eyelid surgery tightens and smooths the upper or lower lid where drooping and puffiness make a rested face look tired. Buccal fat reduction slims a heavy mid-face, and is planned conservatively because it does not grow back.

Most face procedures are outpatient. Plan about a week away after eyelid surgery, and two weeks after a facelift.

Neck & jawline

The part of ageing that shows first.

A neck lift, a lower rhytidectomy, combines liposuction, skin removal and muscle tightening in whatever proportion your anatomy needs, and the practice puts the result at five to ten years. MyEllevate® is the lighter option: an outpatient procedure under local anaesthetic, guided by an ICLED® light, that suspends the muscle with a suture instead of opening the neck. It suits earlier laxity, where a neck lift reverses the more advanced kind. Which one you are is the question the consultation answers.

This is the work that earned Dr. Stone the name “Dr. Jawline”. Jaw and chin surgery is the practice’s most requested.

Skin

Resurfacing, and the regenerative work underneath it.

Fractionated CO2 drives short microbeams of heat into the deeper skin layers so collagen rebuilds. The practice quotes about ten days of downtime and puts it to work on fine lines, dark spots, acne scarring, enlarged pores and sun damage. Morpheus8 does related work sub-dermally, usually across one to three sessions. Chemical peels lift the top layers so newer skin comes up, and are the first answer to hyperpigmentation. PRP and PRF sit behind all of it, using your own platelets so the healing does more of the work.

Microneedling is sold in packages of three. Quoted fees are face-only, and additional areas cost extra.

Non-surgical

The maintenance that keeps surgery further away.

Neuromodulators soften the lines expression carves in, and are priced per unit rather than per area, so you pay for what you actually need. Fillers raise and fill without an incision, general anaesthetic or recovery: lips, folds, acne scarring, the hollows that come with volume loss. Sculptra works more slowly and more structurally, in a course rather than a session. Hair restoration and fat transfer round it out.

Injectables are with Brooke, a board-certified nurse practitioner and advanced aesthetic injector. Botox starts working two to three days after injection and is at its full effect around day ten.

Choosing between them

The comparisons people get stuck on.

If your question is here, the answer is the practice’s own. If it is not, it is a consultation question, and that is not a deflection, it is that the honest answer depends on your anatomy.

MyEllevate® or a neck lift: how do I know which?

A neck lift uses small incisions behind the ears and under the chin to reach the tissue underneath, and delivers a result that can last up to ten years. MyEllevate® places internal sutures through small punctures instead, and lasts around five. MyEllevate® suits earlier laxity; a neck lift reverses more advanced sagging, wrinkling or fullness. It is also a practical difference on the day: MyEllevate® needs only local anaesthetic, so you can drive yourself home.

Will a neck lift fix my jowls?

No, and this is the most common mix-up. Jowls sit low on the face, so people assume they are neck work, but they originate in sagging facial tissue and are addressed in a facelift, not a neck lift. A minimally invasive facelift is the procedure that reduces them.

Implants or filler?

Fillers are gel injectables that plump and smooth lines, acne scars and thin lips, and last from months to a couple of years depending on the product and the site. Implants are customised inserts that reshape the underlying structure and are made from a biocompatible material that stays intact, so the contour is permanent. Filler changes the surface; an implant changes the frame.

How long does each result actually last?

The practice’s own published figures: blepharoplasty 10–15 years or more; a neck lift up to ten; MyEllevate® around five; facial implants and buccal fat reduction permanent, though significant weight gain can put fullness back into the cheek elsewhere. Injectables are months, not years, which is what makes them maintenance rather than surgery.

Can I have more than one procedure at once?

Often, and it is frequently the better plan: a neck lift can be done alongside a facelift or eyelid surgery in a single procedure, with one recovery instead of two. What can be combined safely depends on your anatomy and how long you would be under anaesthetic, so it is decided at the consultation rather than in advance.
