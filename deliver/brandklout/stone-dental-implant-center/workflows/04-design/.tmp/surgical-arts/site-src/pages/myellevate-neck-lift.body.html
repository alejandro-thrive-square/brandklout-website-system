Neck & Jawline

MyEllevate® Neck Lift

Definition through the jaw and neck, achieved with a suture rather than a scar, and a recovery measured in days, not weeks.

Most people who come in about their neck are not asking for a facelift. They want the line under the chin back, the one that makes a jaw look like a jaw, and they cannot take three weeks off to get it.

MyEllevate is the procedure that sits in that gap. A light-guided suture is passed beneath the skin and used to suspend the platysma, the sheet of muscle that gives the neck its shape. Because the suture does the lifting, there is no external incision, and because nothing is cut away, the result is a repositioning of what you already have rather than a removal.

Who it suits

It works best where the skin still has reasonable tone and the problem is laxity in the muscle rather than a genuine excess of skin. In practice that tends to mean patients in their late thirties to their fifties, and patients of any age who have lost weight and been left with a slack neck.

  • Blunting of the angle between the chin and the neck
  • Vertical banding down the front of the neck when speaking
  • A jawline that has softened without much loose skin
  • A wish to avoid the recovery, and the scars, of a full neck lift

Where there is real skin excess, Dr. Stone will say so and recommend the surgical neck lift instead. Being told a procedure is not right for you is a normal outcome of a consultation here.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

Will there be a visible scar?

There is no external incision along the neck. The suture is passed through small entry points, typically under the chin and behind the ears, which settle to be very difficult to find.

How long does it last?

The practice puts MyEllevate® at around five years, against up to ten for a full surgical neck lift. The suspension itself is permanent; what changes is that your neck keeps ageing around it. Skin quality, weight stability and genetics all move that number, and Dr. Stone will give you a realistic figure for your neck rather than the average.

Can I drive myself home afterwards?

Yes. Because MyEllevate® needs only local anaesthetic, you can drive yourself home and resume most activities straight away. That is the practical difference between this and a neck lift, which is done under sedation and requires a responsible adult to bring you, wait, and drive you home.

What happens at the consultation?

You and Dr. Stone go through your concerns and your goals, she takes a medical history and assesses your general health, and photographs and measurements are taken so the plan is drawn against your own anatomy. You leave knowing whether this is the right procedure for you, and if it is not, which one is.

Is this the same as a thread lift?

No. A thread lift places barbed threads in the soft tissue and dissolves over months. MyEllevate places a permanent suture around the platysma itself, under direct light guidance, and it is a surgical procedure performed by a surgeon.

Can I have it if I have already had a facelift?

Often, yes: it is a common way to refresh a neck without repeating a larger operation. Bring the operative details of the previous surgery to your consultation if you have them.
