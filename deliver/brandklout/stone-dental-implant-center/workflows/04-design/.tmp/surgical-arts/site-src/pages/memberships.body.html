Membership questions

The small print, in plain terms.

What happens to my credit if I cancel?

It stays spendable for 90 days after your last payment, then lapses.

Can I use credit towards surgery?

Yes, on Refine and Sculpt. Credit comes off the surgical fee, and the balance can be financed through Sunbit.

Can I spend more than I have banked?

Yes: credit is applied first and you pay the difference at member pricing.

Can someone else use my credit?

On Sculpt, credit transfers to an immediate family member. Otherwise it stays with the member.

Can I move between tiers?

Up at any time, down at the end of a billing month. Banked credit moves with you.
