Where the hours were put in.

Why a maxillofacial surgeon for cosmetic work

The implants here are planned to a scan, not picked from a tray.

KLS Martin IPS® is the workflow in which an implant is designed to a particular skull from a particular scan rather than fitted from stock sizes. It is a reconstruction technique. The practice’s own position is that a generic implant gives a generic result, and this is what it does instead.

The marginal mandibular nerve runs exactly where a neck lift works. A surgeon who spent her residency rebuilding it after it had been broken knows that anatomy in three dimensions, in a particular neck, under tension, rather than as a diagram.

Beyond the practice

A public speaker, an activist, and a Formula 1 obsessive.

Dr. Stone cofounded Real Heroes Need Masks, a grassroots organisation that sourced PPE for medical staff through the pandemic, and the campaign is the reason her name turns up in a business-school newsletter as often as a medical one. She is a public speaker and a philanthropist, the mother of two small children, an avid supporter of motor sport, and the surgeon behind “Dr. Jawline”, a TikTok and Instagram following built on showing the work rather than talking about it.
