Meet the team · Westlake, Texas

The people you will actually meet.

One surgeon plans and performs every case. Around her: a nurse injector, an esthetician, an office manager, a first assistant, and the front desk.

The clinical team

Three people, and you will meet all of them.

Surgery is Dr. Stone’s alone. Injectables are Brooke’s, under her protocols. Laser and skin are Abby’s. Nobody at this practice hands a case to whoever is free that week.

Surgeon · founder

Dr. Mona Stone, DDS

Oral and maxillofacial surgeon. Biomedical engineer before dental school, a full residency across three Level 1 trauma centres after it, and an attending surgeon at Baylor and Methodist. She plans and performs every surgical case at the practice, and follows every one of them up herself.

Her page →    @drjawline

Nurse practitioner · aesthetic injector

Brooke

A board-certified family nurse practitioner and advanced aesthetic injector with a background in dermatology. She worked in the NICU for several years before her master’s degree. With over six years of injecting experience she has trained other injectors, makes safety a priority, and is, in the practice’s own words, a perfectionist about natural-looking results.

Married, with two girls, Tenley and Saylor; travelling, swimming and country music when she is not here.
