Face

Brow & Forehead Lift

The upper third of the face, opened back up. Often the reason a face reads as tired when nothing else about it has changed.

The forehead and brows descend before almost anything else, and they do it slowly enough that most people never identify them as the problem. What they notice is that they look cross, or tired, or that eye makeup has stopped sitting where it used to.

A brow lift repositions the brow and releases the muscles that pull it down. It is a smaller operation than most people expect and it changes expression more than any other procedure on this list.

Who it suits

The test is simple and you can do it in front of a mirror: lift the skin at the tail of the brow with a fingertip. If that single millimetre opens the eye and softens the expression, this is your operation rather than an eyelid one.

  • Brows that sit low or have flattened out at the tail
  • A heavy, hooded look through the upper lid that lifting the brow relieves
  • Deep horizontal lines that no longer soften when the face is at rest
  • A permanently stern or tired expression that does not match your mood

Where the heaviness is skin on the eyelid itself rather than a descended brow, blepharoplasty is the correct answer and a brow lift would over-open the eye. Getting that distinction right is most of the consultation.

Often combined with

The upper third is rarely operated on alone:

  • Upper blepharoplasty, where both the brow and the lid skin are contributing. Planned together so neither is overdone.
  • Neuromodulators afterwards, to keep the muscles that pulled the brow down from doing it again. See injectables.

The order matters. The brow is lifted first, then the lid is assessed against its new position, because doing it the other way round removes skin the brow was going to give back.

Why proportion decides it

There is a height at which a brow looks alert and a height at which it looks startled, and the distance between the two is a few millimetres. It is also different for every face, and different between men and women, where a flatter, lower brow is usually the goal rather than an arched one.

This is why the planning is drawn on your own measurements rather than applied from a template. A brow lift that is technically clean and two millimetres too high is a bad result.

The procedure

What the day actually looks like.

01

In theatre

One to two hours. Sedation is enough for most cases; general anaesthetic where it is combined with a larger procedure. You go home the same day with someone to drive you.

02

The first week

Swelling settles downward into the eyelids for the first few days, which is expected and passes. Numbness across the scalp is normal early on. Most people are back at a desk at around ten days.

03

Settling

The brow drops slightly from its immediate post-operative position over the first weeks, which is planned for. The final height is what you see at three to six months.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

Will I look permanently surprised?

That is the classic bad brow lift and it comes from over-elevating the medial brow, the end nearest the nose. The modern approach lifts the tail rather than the middle and releases the muscles that depress the brow, which opens the eye without raising the whole forehead. It is a planning decision, made against your measurements, before anything is touched.

Where are the incisions?

That depends on the technique chosen for your anatomy and hairline, and it is settled at the consultation rather than assumed here. In every version they sit within or behind the hairline where the anatomy conceals them.

Could I just use Botox instead?

For a small amount of tail elevation, sometimes yes, and it is a reasonable place to start if you are not ready for surgery. A neuromodulator relaxes the muscles pulling the brow down and can buy a millimetre or two. It cannot reposition a brow that has genuinely descended, and it needs repeating three or four times a year.

Will it help my hooded eyelids?

Often substantially, because a good deal of what people call hooding is brow sitting on lid. Whether you also need eyelid skin removed is judged with the brow held in its intended new position, not with it where it is today.

What happens at the consultation?

You and Dr. Stone go through your concerns and your goals, she takes a medical history and assesses your general health, and photographs and measurements are taken so the plan is drawn against your own anatomy. You leave knowing whether this is the right procedure for you, and if it is not, which one is.
