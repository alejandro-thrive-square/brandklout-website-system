Before you book

The five questions we are asked most.

What does the consultation cost, and what happens in it?

$175 for Texas residents and $250 from out of state, with a $100 deposit to hold the appointment that comes off the fee. You spend the hour with Dr. Stone rather than a treatment coordinator, and you leave with the options, the honest limits of each, and what each one costs.

How much time will I need away from work?

The practice's own guidance: about a week after eyelid surgery, about ten days after CO2 laser resurfacing, and two weeks after a facelift. Plan for one to two weeks of private time and come back early if recovery goes better than expected. It is always easier that way round than the reverse.

Can I finance treatment?

Yes. The practice works with Sunbit, CareCredit and Scratchpay, and is a preferred provider with all major dental and medical carriers where a procedure is covered. A membership is the other route: it banks credit month by month rather than borrowing against it.

Will I need someone to drive me home?

For anything under intravenous sedation, yes. A responsible adult has to bring you, stay in the office through the procedure and drive you home, and you should not drive or operate machinery for 24 hours afterwards. Treatments done under local anaesthetic alone do not require it.

When can I wear makeup again?

In most cases 48 hours after sutures come out. After CO2 laser, about ten days. Sun avoidance matters more than makeup does in that window: sun on healing skin is what darkens a scar.
