Banked

Memberships · banked credit, no expiry

Credit that waits for you.

Most memberships are a use-it-or-lose-it subscription. This one is not. Your monthly amount becomes credit that accumulates until you decide what it is for: a syringe this spring, or a surgical fee next year.

01

It banks

Your monthly amount lands as credit on your account. Skip three months and you have three months of credit, and nothing expires while the membership is active.

02

It spends on anything

Injectables, skin treatments, or against a surgical fee. Credit is credit; it is not restricted to one category or one product line.

03

It buys at member pricing

Members pay less per unit and per syringe than the published fee, so banked credit goes further than the same money spent as a walk-in.

Membership questions

The small print, in plain terms.

What happens to my credit if I cancel?

It stays spendable for 90 days after your last payment, so nothing you have already paid for is lost. After that it lapses.

Can I use credit towards surgery?

Yes, on Refine and Sculpt. Credit comes off the surgical fee, and the balance can be financed through Sunbit.

Can I spend more than I have banked?

Yes: credit is applied first and you pay the difference at member pricing. There is no requirement to wait until the balance covers the whole treatment.

Can someone else use my credit?

On Sculpt, credit is transferable to an immediate family member. On the other tiers it stays with the member.

Can I move between tiers?

Up at any time, and down at the end of a billing month. Credit you have already banked moves with you.
