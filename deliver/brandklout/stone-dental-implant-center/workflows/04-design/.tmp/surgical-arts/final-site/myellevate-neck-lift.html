01 · What it does

The suture does the lifting, so nothing is cut away.

Most people who come in about their neck want the line under the chin back, and cannot take three weeks off to get it. This procedure sits in that gap.

Surface Skin Fat Muscle Deepest

The platysma, suspended

A light-guided suture passed beneath the skin suspends the platysma, the sheet of muscle that gives the neck its shape. No external incision, nothing cut away: a repositioning of what you already have.

Where the muscle has separated and the skin has gone past holding, a suture is not enough. That is the surgical neck lift.

Submental fat, if it is there

Where the fullness is fat rather than muscle, submental liposuction is done in the same visit through the same entry point.

Which it is on you is measured at the consultation, not guessed from a photograph.

The skin, left where it is

Nothing is removed. The skin redrapes over the suspended muscle, so it needs tone of its own. Where it needs help, FaceTite tightens it by radiofrequency in the same visit, priced together at $7,800.

Skin with genuine excess will not take up over a suture, and that decides between this and a neck lift.

02 · Who it suits

It works when the skin still takes up.

Skin that still has tone, and muscle laxity rather than skin excess: people in their late thirties to fifties, and anyone left with a slack neck after weight loss.

03 · How it is done

A light-guided suture, through entry points you will struggle to find.

Under local anaesthetic, a suture is passed beneath the skin along a path lit by a fibre-optic wand, so Dr. Stone sees exactly where it runs. Drawn up around the platysma, it restores the angle under the chin and the jawline.

There is no external incision. The entry points, under the chin and behind the ears, settle to very little.

About ninety minutes in the chair. Under local anaesthetic alone you can drive yourself home. With sedation, a responsible adult drives you.

07 · Questions

The six questions this procedure raises.

Will there be a visible scar?

No. The entry points, under the chin and behind the ears, settle to be very hard to find.

How long does it last?

The practice puts MyEllevate® at around five years, against up to ten for a surgical neck lift. The suspension is permanent, but your neck keeps ageing around it. Skin quality, weight stability and genetics all move that number.

Can I drive myself home afterwards?

Under local alone, yes: the practice’s own guidance is that you can drive yourself home and resume most activities straight away. With sedation, a responsible adult drives you, as for a neck lift.

Is this the same as a thread lift?

No. A thread lift places barbed threads in the soft tissue and dissolves over months. MyEllevate® is a permanent suture around the platysma itself, placed under direct light guidance by a surgeon.

Can I have it if I have already had a facelift?

Often, yes: it refreshes a neck without a larger operation. Bring the operative details if you have them.

What does it cost, and can I finance it?

$6,000 on its own, or $7,800 combined with FaceTite for the neck. Financing runs through Sunbit, and membership credit can be applied to a surgical fee. See Memberships.
