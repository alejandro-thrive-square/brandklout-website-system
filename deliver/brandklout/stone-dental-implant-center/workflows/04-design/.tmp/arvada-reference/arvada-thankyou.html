before you call

Any questions before you call?

How much does All-On-4 cost in Arvada, Colorado?

  • All-On-4 at Arvada Dental Center & Implants typically ranges from $7,895 to $17,795 per arch depending on the complexity of your case and the final restoration material chosen. We provide a full itemized breakdown at your consultation. Payment plans are most often used through CareCredit, Cherry, Sunbit, PatientFi, Proceed, and some partners.

Why does Dr. Bennett use All-On-X instead of always placing exactly four implants?

  • Every patient's bone is different. Dr. Bennett evaluates bone density, volume, and bite forces during your consultation and places between four and eight implants based on what will provide the greatest stability and longevity for your specific case. The goal is a restoration built to last many decades, not just a few years.

How is All-On-4 different from traditional dental implants in Arvada, Colorado?

  • Traditional implants replace individual teeth one at a time. All-On-4 uses four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of teeth at once. It is a more efficient and often more affordable solution for patients who need to replace a full arch rather than a single tooth.

Can I get teeth the same day as All-On-4 surgery?

  • 99.9% of the time, we always send patients home with teeth! A temporary full-arch restoration, or "healing teeth," is attached to the implants on the day of surgery so you leave with a complete smile. Your permanent final restoration, made of zirconia, is placed once the implants have fully integrated, typically three to six months later.

How long do All-On-4 implants last?

  • With proper care, All-On-4 implants are designed to last decades. The implants themselves are typically a lifetime solution. The prosthetic restoration may need replacement after 15 to 20 years depending on wear and material.

Can I get teeth the same day as All-On-X surgery?

  • 99.9% of the time, yes. A temporary full-arch restoration, also called your healing teeth, is attached to the implants on the day of surgery so you leave with a complete, functional smile. Your permanent final restoration, milled from zirconia, is placed once the implants have fully integrated, typically three to five months later.

How long does All-On-X last?

  • With proper care and regular checkups, All-On-X implants are designed to last many decades to a lifetime. The titanium posts typically never need replacing. The zirconia prosthetic is highly durable and resistant to chipping and staining, and may need evaluation after 15 to 20 years of normal wear.

Does insurance cover All-On-X in Arvada, Colorado?

  • Some dental insurance plans contribute toward All-On-X, particularly the surgical and crown portions. Coverage varies by plan. We verify your benefits before treatment begins and help you maximize what your plan provides. Financing through our dozen-plus lending partners covers any remaining out-of-pocket costs.

Am I too old for All-On-X?

  • There is no upper age limit. The procedure is regularly performed for patients in their 70s and 80s. Good general health and adequate bone structure are the primary requirements, not age.

What is the difference between All-On-X and traditional dentures?

  • Traditional dentures are removable, rest on your gums, can slip, require adhesive, and accelerate bone loss. All-On-X is permanently fixed to implants in your jawbone, functions like natural teeth, and actively preserves the bone. Most patients describe the difference as life-changing.

How do I care for All-On-X implants?

  • Care for your All-On-X just like natural teeth. Brush twice daily, use a water flosser or interdental brush to clean under the bridge, and attend regular professional cleanings at Arvada Dental Center & Implants every six months.
