      <div.axon-faq_list>
        <div.axon-faq_item>
          <h3.axon-faq_q>
            · How much do All-on-X implants cost?
          <p.axon-faq_a>
            · All-on-X in the Westlake and DFW area typically ranges from about $25,000 to $50,000 or more per arch, depending on the number of implants, materials, and whether any bone work is needed. Stone Dental Implant Center provides a full itemized quote at your consultation, with financing through Lending 
        <div.axon-faq_item>
          <h3.axon-faq_q>
            · What exactly is All-on-X?
          <p.axon-faq_a>
            · All-on-X replaces an entire arch of teeth using a few strategically placed titanium implants, usually four to six, that support a fixed set of permanent teeth. Unlike dentures, they stay in permanently and function like natural teeth.
        <div.axon-faq_item>
          <h3.axon-faq_q>
            · Am I a candidate if I have bone loss?
          <p.axon-faq_a>
            · In most cases, yes. Dr. Stone can often angle implants around areas of bone loss or perform grafting and sinus lifts when needed. A 3D scan at your consultation determines your bone density and the right plan for your case, including complex cases other clinics turn away.
        <div.axon-faq_item>
          <h3.axon-faq_q>
            · Will my new teeth look natural?
          <p.axon-faq_a>
            · Yes. This is the heart of the Stone approach. Your new arch is designed around your face, with the shape, shade, and proportion matched to your features, so the result looks like it always belonged to you.
        <div.axon-faq_item>
          <h3.axon-faq_q>
            · Does insurance cover All-on-X?
          <p.axon-faq_a>
            · Coverage varies. Some medical plans, including certain BlueCross BlueShield medical policies, may cover portions of full-arch treatment, and many PPO dental plans cover extractions, grafts, or sedation. We work with most PPO plans as an out-of-network provider and verify your benefits before treatme
        <div.axon-faq_item>
          <h3.axon-faq_q>
            · How long does the process take?
          <p.axon-faq_a>
            · Most patients leave with same-day temporary teeth on the day of surgery. The implants then integrate with the jawbone over about 4 to 6 months, after which your custom permanent arch is placed.
