— FAQ

Common Questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your first visit is a complimentary evaluation that includes a 3D scan, a review of your options, and an itemized treatment plan with exact fees — before anything begins.

Does it hurt?

Surgery is performed under anesthesia with sedation available. Mild soreness and swelling in the first days is normal and manageable — and most patients describe recovery as easier than they had braced for.

Will I be without teeth?

No — in most cases a fixed temporary arch goes in the same day as surgery. The 3D scan from your consultation visit tells us whether your case qualifies.

Am I a candidate if I was told I don't have enough bone?

Usually, yes. Dr. Stone can often angle implants around areas of bone loss, or rebuild bone with grafting and sinus lifts when needed. The 3D scan at your consultation settles what your case needs.
