What counts as a dental emergency?+
Severe toothache, a knocked-out tooth, a broken tooth, a lost filling or crown, an abscess, and bleeding you cannot control. If it is keeping you awake or getting worse by the hour, treat it as an emergency and call (973) 264-0928.
Do you offer same day emergency appointments?+
Yes. Marvel Dental holds time for dental emergencies and aims to see urgent patients the same day. The office is open Tuesday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, and Saturday, 8am to 4pm, so calling early in the day gives you the best chance of a slot.
How much does emergency dental care cost?+
An emergency exam is typically $75 to $200. What it costs after that depends on the treatment: extractions run $75 to $650, root canals $300 to $2,000, and temporary repairs less. You get an estimate before any treatment starts.
What should I do if I knock out a tooth?+
Handle it by the crown, never the root, keep it moist in milk or your own saliva, and get to the office within 30 minutes if you can. That window gives the best chance of saving the tooth.
Does insurance cover a dental emergency?+
Most dental plans cover emergency exams and necessary treatment at the level set out in your plan. The team verifies your benefits during the visit and tells you what is covered before treatment begins. Financing is available for the balance.
Can I go to the emergency room instead?+
An emergency room can manage pain and infection, but it cannot perform dental procedures, so the tooth itself goes untreated. For definitive treatment you need a dentist. Call us first and you will usually be seen faster.
I am nervous about the dentist. Can you help with that?+
Yes. Nitrous oxide, oral sedation and IV sedation are all available at Marvel Dental, and the team treats anxious patients every week. Say so when you call and the visit is planned around it.
Which languages does the team speak?+
English, Arabic, Russian and Spanish are all spoken at the practice, so you can describe what is wrong in the language you think in.
