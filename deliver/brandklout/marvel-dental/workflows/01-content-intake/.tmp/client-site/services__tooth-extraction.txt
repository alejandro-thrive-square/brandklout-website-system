What Is Tooth Extraction?
Tooth extraction is a dental procedure to remove a tooth that is severely damaged, decayed, infected, or impacted. While we always prioritize saving natural teeth, extraction becomes necessary when a tooth cannot be restored or poses a risk to your oral health. Dr. Anna Gazu performs both simple and surgical extractions with precision and care, offering sedation options to ensure your comfort during the procedure.

Benefits of Tooth Extraction
Tooth extraction offers essential benefits when a tooth cannot be saved.
This procedure protects your oral health and prevents further complications.
Eliminates Pain and Infection
Remove the source of severe tooth pain and stop the spread of infection.
Prevents Damage to Surrounding Teeth
Protect adjacent teeth from crowding or damage caused by problematic teeth.
Promotes Healthy Healing
Removing a damaged tooth allows the surrounding gum and bone to heal properly and prevents further complications.

How much does tooth extraction cost?
Simple extractions cost $75 – $300 per tooth. Surgical extractions cost $150 – $650 per tooth. Wisdom teeth removal ranges from $200 – $650 per tooth.
What is a surgical extraction?
A surgical extraction is needed when a tooth is impacted, broken below the gumline, or has curved roots. It requires incision and sometimes bone removal.
What should I expect after extraction?
Expect some swelling and discomfort for 2-3 days. We provide detailed aftercare instructions and pain management recommendations.
Does insurance cover tooth extraction?
Most dental insurance covers extractions as basic procedures at 50-80%. Surgical extractions may have different coverage levels.
Is tooth extraction painful?
We use local anesthesia to completely numb the area. You'll feel pressure but no pain. Sedation options are available for anxious patients.
How long is recovery from tooth extraction?
Initial healing takes 1-2 weeks. The socket fully heals in 3-4 weeks. We'll advise when you can resume normal activities.
