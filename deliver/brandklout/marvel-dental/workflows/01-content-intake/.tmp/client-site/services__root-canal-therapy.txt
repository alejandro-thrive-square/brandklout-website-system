What Is Root Canal Therapy?
Root canal therapy is a restorative dental procedure designed to save a tooth that has been badly damaged by deep decay, infection, or trauma. Inside each tooth is soft tissue called the pulp, which contains nerves and blood vessels. When this pulp becomes infected, it can cause severe pain and put the tooth at risk of loss. A root canal gently removes the infected tissue, cleans and disinfects the root canal space, and seals the tooth to prevent future infection. Once treated, the tooth is restored with a crown for strength and function.

How much does a root canal cost?
Root canal costs vary by tooth: front teeth $300-$1,000, premolars $400-$1,200, and molars $500-$2,000. A crown is typically needed afterward ($800-$2,500).
What is a root canal?
A root canal removes infected or damaged pulp from inside your tooth, then fills and seals the space to save the natural tooth from extraction.
How long does a root canal take?
Most root canals take 60-90 minutes in a single visit. Complex cases may require two appointments.
Does insurance cover root canals?
Most dental insurance covers root canals as basic or major procedures at 50-80%. The crown may be covered separately. We'll verify your benefits.
Is a root canal painful?
Modern root canals are no more painful than getting a filling. We use local anesthesia, and most patients report feeling comfortable throughout.
Do I need a crown after a root canal?
Yes, a crown is usually recommended after root canal on back teeth to protect the weakened tooth from fracture.
