What Is a Dental Emergency?
A dental emergency is any situation requiring immediate dental care to relieve severe pain, stop bleeding, save a tooth, or treat an infection. Common emergencies include knocked-out teeth, severe toothaches, broken or chipped teeth, lost fillings or crowns, and abscesses. At Marvel Dental, Dr. Anna Gazu provides prompt emergency care to address urgent dental problems and prevent further complications.

How much does emergency dental care cost?
Emergency exam fees typically range from $75 – $200. Total costs depend on the treatment needed: extractions $75-$650, root canals $300-$2,000, or temporary repairs. We'll provide estimates before treatment.
What is considered a dental emergency?
Dental emergencies include severe toothache, knocked-out teeth, broken teeth, lost fillings/crowns, abscesses, and uncontrolled bleeding. Call us immediately for same-day care.
What should I do if I knock out a tooth?
Keep the tooth moist in milk or saliva, handle it by the crown only, and get to our office within 30 minutes for the best chance of saving it.
Does insurance cover dental emergencies?
Most dental insurance covers emergency exams and necessary treatments according to your plan's coverage levels. We'll verify your benefits quickly during your emergency visit.
Do you offer same-day emergency appointments?
Yes, we reserve time for dental emergencies and strive to see emergency patients the same day. Call (973) 295-5443 for immediate assistance.
Can I go to the ER for a dental emergency?
ERs can manage pain and infection but cannot perform dental procedures. For definitive treatment, you need a dentist. Call us first for faster, specialized care.
