Your Options

From One Tooth To a Full Smile

Your consultation determines which path fits your bone, bite, timeline, and budget. Here's the landscape before you walk in.

One tooth

Single Implant

Replaces one missing tooth from root to crown, matched to your natural teeth. Permanent — never comes out.

2–4 teeth

Implant Bridge

Two implants anchor a bridge across the gap — without grinding down your healthy neighboring teeth.

Full arch · Fixed

All-on-X

Four to six implants support a complete arch of fixed teeth. Same-day temporary teeth are often possible.

Full arch · Snap-on

Implant-Secured Denture

A denture that snaps onto implants — no slipping, no paste, removable for easy cleaning.
