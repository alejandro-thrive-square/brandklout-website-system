Common Questions

Everything Patients Ask Before Booking

Does getting a dental implant hurt?+−
The procedure is done with local anesthesia, and sedation options are available — you shouldn't feel it. Some soreness and swelling during early healing is normal and manageable. Most patients tell us it was far easier than they expected.
How much does a dental implant cost?+−
A complete single implant — post, abutment, and crown — typically runs $1,900 to $2,800 at Livewell Dental. Bone grafting or extractions, if needed, are quoted upfront. Insurance may cover part of the crown or extraction, and financing options are available.
Can I really get a new tooth in one day?+−
Sometimes, yes. Depending on your bone quality and overall health, a temporary tooth can be placed the same day as your implant — like Jerry's front tooth. Your 3D scan at the consultation tells us whether you qualify.
What if I've been told I don't have enough bone?+−
Don't rule yourself out. Bone grafting, sinus lifts, and alternative implant approaches make treatment possible for many patients who were told "no" elsewhere. A CBCT scan shows exactly what's possible for you.
How long do implants last?+−
The implant post is designed as a lifetime replacement root. With healthy gums, daily care, and regular checkups, implants can last decades — the crown on top may need maintenance over time like any tooth.
Do you take my insurance?+−
Insurance often covers part of the treatment, such as the crown or extraction. Our team verifies your benefits before treatment and walks you through financing options — you'll know your exact out-of-pocket cost before you commit to anything.
