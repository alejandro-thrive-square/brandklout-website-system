Common questions

Before you book

If your question is not here, ask it at the consultation. There is no obligation to move forward afterwards.

Will insurance cover any of this? +

Most plans treat smile makeovers as elective cosmetic care, so they are usually not covered. Individual components such as crowns can receive partial coverage when they also serve a functional purpose. Our team reviews your benefits before treatment begins.

How long does a smile makeover take? +

Bonding only cases can be done in one or two visits. A full veneer case usually runs three to six weeks from preparation to final placement. Dr. Chris gives you a clear timeline at the consultation based on your specific plan.

Can I really see my new smile before committing? +

Yes. Before any permanent work begins, a physical preview is made so you can wear the new smile and judge it in real life. If the shape, shade or proportion needs adjusting, that happens before anything is finalized.

Do I need veneers, or something else? +

It depends on your goals. A smile makeover can involve veneers, whitening, bonding, gum reshaping or alignment. If only the front teeth need attention, veneers alone may be enough. The consultation clarifies exactly what your smile needs, no more and no less.

What happens at the free consultation? +

A comprehensive exam with full imaging and clinical photography, followed by a case presentation where you see everything Dr. Chris sees before any recommendation is made. No pressure and no obligation to proceed.
