Sound Familiar?

A missing tooth never stays "just one problem."

  • Avoiding certain foods because chewing is difficult
  • A gap you cover up when you smile or laugh
  • A loose bridge, partial, or denture that shifts when you eat or talk
  • Told the bone has deteriorated too much to treat simply

"When a tooth is missing, the bone underneath it starts to deteriorate — often faster than people realize."

Implants are the only replacement option that stops that process, preserve your facial structure, and give you back a fully functioning tooth root. Dr. Chris places and restores implants entirely in-house, from the initial scan to the final crown.

How It Works

One dentist, one plan, start to finish.

Here's what your implant journey actually looks like at Livewell.

1

Consultation & 3D Scan

A digital scan and full exam map your bone, bite, and goals so Dr. Chris can confirm you're a candidate and plan the exact implant position.

2

Guided Placement

Using a surgical guide printed from your scan data, the implant is placed exactly where it was planned — done in-house by Dr. Chris.

3

Custom Restoration

Once healed, your custom crown, bridge, or denture is fabricated, tried in, and verified for fit and bite before final placement.
