## What Is a Sinus Lift?


A sinus lift is a bone grafting procedure for the upper back jaw, usually near the molars and premolars.

When teeth have been missing in this area, the bone can shrink over time. At the same time, the sinus cavity may sit lower, leaving limited space for a dental implant.

During a sinus lift, the sinus membrane is gently lifted and bone grafting material is placed underneath it. As the area heals, new bone forms and creates stronger support for a future implant.

For many patients, a sinus lift makes implant treatment possible when there was not enough bone before.


## Are You a Candidate?


This could be right for you if you have:

Missing upper back teeth

‍Bone loss is common after molars or premolars have been missing for a long time.

Been told you do not have enough bone for an implant

‍A sinus lift can help create the vertical bone height needed for implant placement.

A sinus that sits close to the jawbone

‍Some patients naturally have less space between the sinus and upper jaw, even before tooth loss.

A plan for upper dental implants

‍If implants are part of your future smile plan, a sinus lift may be the step that helps make them possible.

## The Sinus Lift Process


Here’s what you can expect.

Consultation & 3D Imaging

We begin with a detailed exam and digital imaging to evaluate your sinus position, bone height, and implant options.

Personalized Treatment Plan

Dr. Chris explains whether a sinus lift is needed, how it fits into your implant plan, and what to expect before treatment begins.

Gentle Sinus Elevation

The sinus membrane is carefully lifted to create space for new bone support in the upper jaw.

Bone Graft Placement

Grafting material is placed beneath the sinus membrane to help rebuild the area where support is needed.

Healing & Implant Readiness

Over time, your body integrates the graft. Once the area is ready, we can move forward with implant placement or the next phase of your plan.

A sinus lift adds bone in the delicate space beneath your sinus membrane so it can support future implants. We spin a small sample of your blood chairside and blend the resulting PRF into the graft. Its concentrated growth factors speed new bone formation, help protect the membrane, and calm inflammation, so the site integrates more predictably.

Why would I need a sinus lift?

You may need a sinus lift if there is not enough bone in the upper back jaw to safely support a dental implaint.

Is a sinus lift the same as bone grafting?

A sinus lift is a specific type of bone grafting. It is used in the upper back jaw when more bone height is needed near the sinus.

Does a sinus lift hurt?

We use local anesthesia and a comfort-focused approach during treatment. Some soreness, swelling, or pressure afterward is normal and usually manageable with post-care instructions.

How long does a sinus lift take to heal?

Healing time varies depending on your anatomy and the amount of grafting needed. Many patients need a few months of healing before implant placement.

Can I get an implant at the same time as a sinus lift?

Sometimes, yes. If there is enough existing bone stability, the dental implant may be placed during the same visit. If not, the graft will need time to heal first.

How is a sinus lift different from ridge augmentation?

A sinus lift adds bone height in the upper back jaw near the sinus. Ridge augmentation rebuilds the width or shape of the jaw ridge after bone loss. Both procedures can help prepare the mouth for implants, but they solve different bone problems.

How much does a sinus lift cost?

A sinus lift typically runs $500 to $2,500. Pricing depends on whether one or both sides need treatment, the graft material used, and the complexity of the case.
