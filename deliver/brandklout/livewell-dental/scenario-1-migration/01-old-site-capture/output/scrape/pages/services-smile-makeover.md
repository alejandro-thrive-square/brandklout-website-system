## Smile Makeovers, Done the Livewell Way


Your makeover follows a blueprint. Every step is designed, reviewed, and confirmed before anything permanent happens. You will know what your result looks like before we begin.

Discovery and Design

We photograph your face, your teeth, and your smile from every angle that matters. Using dentofacial analysis and digital smile design, Dr. Chris maps the relationship between your teeth, your lips, your jawline, and your facial proportions. The goal is a design that belongs to your face, not a template pulled from a catalog.

Preview You Can Try On

Before any tooth is touched, you see your result. A physical preview is built so you can wear your new smile temporarily, check it in different lighting, and decide whether it feels right. If something needs to change, it changes now, not after the final restorations are placed.

Preparation

Once the design is locked, the teeth are prepared. For veneers, this means minimal reshaping of the front surface. For crowns, a more complete reduction. For bonding, often no preparation at all. The approach depends on the tooth and the restoration, not a standard formula.

Handcrafted Fabrication

Your restorations are built by experienced ceramists who receive the full photography package, digital design, and specific shade instructions captured with PolarEyes. The goal is a close partnership where the ceramist understands the same details Dr. Chris sees.

Delivery and Final Verification

Each restoration is tried in, checked against the original design, and verified for shade, fit, and bite before it is permanently bonded. We photograph the final result and compare it to the plan. What you approved is what you get.

What is a smile makeover and what does it include?

A smile makeover is a combination of cosmetic dental procedures planned and executed together to transform the appearance of your smile. It can include porcelain veneers, dental crowns, teeth whitening, bonding, gum recontouring, and orthodontic alignment. The specific combination depends on your goals, your facial structure, and the current condition of your teeth. At Livewell, every makeover is designed using photography, digital mapping, and a physical preview before anything permanent begins.

Can I see what my smile will look like before I commit?

Yes. Before any permanent work begins, a physical preview is created so you can wear your new smile temporarily and evaluate it in real life. If the shape, shade, or proportion needs adjusting, those changes happen before anything is finalized. You approve the result before it becomes permanent.

How long does a smile makeover take from start to finish?

The timeline depends on the scope. A bonding-only case may take one or two visits. A full veneer case typically takes three to six weeks from preparation to final placement. Multi-phase makeovers that include orthodontics or gum work can extend to several months. Dr. Chris gives you a clear timeline at your consultation based on what your plan involves.

Are smile makeovers covered by insurance?

Most dental insurance plans classify smile makeovers as elective cosmetic treatment and do not cover them. However, individual components like crowns may receive partial coverage if they also serve a functional purpose. Our team reviews your benefits before treatment begins and walks you through what, if anything, applies.

What is the difference between Perfect 1, Magic 4, and Social 10?

These describe the scope of the makeover. Perfect 1 focuses on a single tooth, matched perfectly to the surrounding teeth. Magic 4 covers your four front teeth, transforming the center of your smile. Social 10 addresses all ten upper teeth visible when you smile or speak. The right scope depends on your goals and your starting point, which is determined during your consultation.

How do I know if I need a smile makeover or just veneers?

A smile makeover may include veneers, but it can also include whitening, bonding, gum reshaping, alignment, or crowns. The difference is that a makeover is a coordinated plan where every element is designed together for a cohesive result. If only your front teeth need attention and the rest of your smile is in good shape, veneers alone may be enough. If the changes involve multiple areas or procedures, a makeover gives you a single plan rather than piecemeal treatments.
