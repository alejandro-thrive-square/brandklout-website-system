## Thinking About Improving Your Smile, But Not Sure Where to Start?


Our complimentary Cosmetic Smile Virtual Consult is an easy way to begin. Whether you are curious about no-prep veneers, improving one tooth, enhancing your smile for photos, or simply learning what may be possible, this is a simple first step with Dr. Chris.

Start now. No Pressure. Just Possibilities

## This virtual consult is idealif you are interested in:



#### No-prep veneers


Reshape and brighten your smile with little to no tooth shaving, whenever the case allows.


#### Fixing One Tooth


A chip, old bonding, or the one tooth you notice in every photo — often a small change with a big difference.


#### A More Confident Smile in Photos


Cleaner, brighter, more even — so you stop holding back your smile on camera.


#### Knowing Where to Start


A simple way to learn whether your case is straightforward or more involved, before any decision is made.


## How the virtual smile consultation works


01


### Add a Close-Up Smile Photo


You do not need professional photos. Phone photos in good natural light are perfect.

A smiling selfie (full face)

Good natural light, facing a window

No filters needed

A close-up of your smile

02


### Share your smile goals


Tell us what you notice about your smile and what you’d love to change. Your goals will help Dr. Chris understand what matters most to you and personalize your consultation.

Whiter, brighter teeth

Improved tooth shape, size, or symmetry

Fixing chips, gaps, wear, or uneven edges

A natural-looking smile you feel confident sharing

03


### Book your virtual consult


Once your form and photos are submitted, book a complimentary 20-minute Google Meet with Dr. Chris. Consults are scheduled on the hour and half-hour to give each patient enough time.

This is not a final diagnosis. It is a thoughtful first step.

04


### Meet with Dr. Chris


Dr. Chris reviews your photos, listens to what you are hoping for, and helps you understand possible next steps.

No-prep or porcelain veneers

Cosmetic bonding or whitening

Orthodontics, if it fits your goals

Or a more comprehensive evaluation
