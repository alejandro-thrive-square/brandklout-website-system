## What Are Veneers?


Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin shells of dental ceramic bonded to the front surface of your teeth. They change the shape, color, size, and symmetry of your smile without altering the tooth underneath more than necessary.

What makes veneers different from whitening or bonding is permanence and precision. They are custom-fabricated by an elite ceramist, matched to the specific translucency and shade of your natural teeth, and designed to look like they belong there. Not like veneers. Like your teeth, but the version of them you always wanted.

At Livewell, veneers are never an impulse treatment. Every case starts with a full evaluation of your bite, your tooth structure, and your facial proportions to make sure the result holds up functionally as well as aesthetically.

Veneers are the right fit for people who want a lasting change. Dr. Chris will tell you honestly if you are one of them.

## Veneers, Done the Livewell Way


It is more than putting porcelain on teeth. It is a process designed for lasting function and beauty.

Discovery & Design

Your first visit is not about picking a shade from a chart. Dr. Chris evaluates your oral health, bite, tooth structure, and facial proportions. He asks what you want your result to look like and listens carefully. This conversation shapes everything that follows. No two design plans are identical because no two patients are.

Master-Crafted Porcelain

Your case is created with an experienced ceramist using the specifications Dr. Chris builds around your face, bite, and anatomy. Each veneer is crafted to match the texture, translucency, and depth of natural enamel. This is not a generic lab order. It is a detailed collaboration.

Precision Prep

When preparation is needed, Dr. Chris removes only the minimum amount of tooth structure required for the veneer to bond correctly and sit flush with your gumline. When a no-prep approach is appropriate, nothing is removed at all. Either way, the goal is to preserve as much natural tooth as possible.

Functional Harmony

This step helps reduce unnecessary stress on the veneers and supports a longer-lasting result.

Finishing Touches That Belong

The goal is a smile that looks natural enough to feel like it has always belonged to you.

Are veneers permanent?

Traditional veneers require a controlled amount of enamel removal, which makes the decision permanent. The veneers themselves typically last 10 to 20 years, at which point they can be replaced. No-prep veneers, where no enamel is removed, can in most cases be reversed. Dr. Chris will recommend the right approach during your consultation and explain exactly what the commitment looks like for your specific case.

Do veneers damage your teeth?

For traditional veneers, a small amount of enamel is removed so the veneer sits flush and bonds correctly. At Livewell, Dr. Chris removes only what is necessary, no more. When a no-prep approach is appropriate, nothing is altered at all. The goal in every case is to preserve as much natural tooth structure as possible while achieving a result that holds up over time.

Will my veneers look fake?

Veneers should not look fake when they are carefully designed, shaded, and shaped around your natural smile. Every veneer is matched to the exact translucency, surface texture, and shade of your natural teeth by an elite ceramist. The result is meant to look like your teeth, improved, not like dental work. Most patients find that no one notices unless they choose to say something.

Can veneers fix crooked teeth without braces?

In cases of mild misalignment, veneers can correct the appearance of slight spacing, unevenness, or short teeth without orthodontic treatment. For more significant alignment issues, straightening first often leads to a more conservative and longer-lasting veneer result. Dr. Chris will tell you during your consultation which path fits your situation.

What is the difference between Perfect 1, Magic 4, and Social 10?

These are the three veneer scopes available at Livewell, each defined by how many teeth are involved. Perfect 1 is a single restoration, whether a veneer, crown, or bonding, crafted to disappear seamlessly into your existing smile. Magic 4 covers your four front teeth, the zone most visible when you speak or smile. Social 10 treats all ten upper teeth, the full frame of your smile from any angle. The right scope depends on your goals and your starting point, which is determined at your consultation.

How long does getting veneers take?

A typical case at Livewell takes three to six weeks from your design appointment to final placement. The first visit covers evaluation and design. A second visit handles preparation while your veneers are being handcrafted. Final placement and refinement complete the process. If your case includes gum work or other preparatory steps, the timeline extends accordingly. You will know the full schedule before anything begins.

Can I get veneers if I grind my teeth?

Grinding does not disqualify you from veneers, but it does need to be addressed before and after treatment. Uncontrolled grinding puts veneers at risk of chipping or failing early. At Livewell, every case begins with a bite evaluation. If grinding is a factor, Dr. Chris will discuss how to protect your result long-term before a single veneer is placed.

What is the estimated cost of getting porcelain veneers?

Porcelain veneers are priced at $2,500 per tooth. Pricing covers your consultation, digital imaging, temporary veneers while your custom set is made, lab fees, and final placement.

A full smile makeover involving multiple veneers will naturally cost more, since the total scales with the number of teeth treated.
