# Cookie Policy



## Livewell Dental Cookie Policy


Livewell Dental (“we,” “our,” or “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies to help our website function properly, improve your browsing experience, understand how visitors use our site, and support relevant marketing efforts. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.


## 1. What Cookies Are


Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website recognize your device, remember certain preferences, and improve how the site works.


## 2. Types of Cookies We Use



### Essential Cookies


These cookies are needed for basic website functions such as navigation, security, contact forms, appointment requests, and access to protected areas of the site. Without these cookies, parts of the website may not work properly.


### Performance and Analytics Cookies


These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. They may show us which pages are visited most, how users move through the site, and where improvements can be made.


### Functional Cookies


Functional cookies help remember preferences such as form entries, location settings, or previously viewed pages so your experience feels smoother and more personalized.


### Advertising and Retargeting Cookies


We may use cookies from trusted advertising partners to show relevant information about Livewell Dental services and measure campaign performance. These cookies may track browsing activity across websites.


## 3. Why We Use Cookies


We use cookies to:

Improve website speed and functionality

Understand website traffic and user behavior

Improve the patient experience online

Support appointment request features

Maintain website security

Measure marketing performance

Provide more relevant content and advertising


## 4. Third-Party Cookies


Some cookies may be placed by third-party providers for analytics, advertising, or embedded content. These may include:

Google Analytics

Google Ads

Meta/Facebook Pixel

YouTube

Embedded maps or review tools

These third parties may collect and use information according to their own privacy policies.


## 5. How to Manage Cookies


You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete existing cookies, block third-party cookies, or receive alerts when cookies are being used.

Common browser settings:

Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies

Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data

Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data

Edge: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality, including forms, appointment requests, maps, videos, and other interactive features.


## 6. Do Not Track Signals


Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” setting. Our website does not currently respond to Do Not Track signals. You can still manage cookies using your browser settings.


## 7. Updates to This Policy


We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulations, or our website practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
