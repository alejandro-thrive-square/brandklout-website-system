## Implant Options at Livewell Dental


Your choice depends on how many teeth need replacing, your bone support, and whether you prefer fixed or removable teeth. Dr. Chris will explain suitable options, treatment stages, and costs at your consultation.


### Single Implants


One implant supports a custom crown to replace a missing tooth. Dr. Chris plans its position and the crown together to fit your bite and surrounding teeth. Healing generally takes several months.


### Implant Bridges


An implant bridge replaces several neighboring teeth without using the adjacent natural teeth as supports. Dr. Chris determines the number of implants needed from the size of the gap, bone support, and bite.


### All-on-X


Multiple implants support a fixed set of upper or lower teeth. Dr. Chris assesses whether this approach suits your case and whether temporary teeth can be fitted on the day of surgery.


### Locator-Retained Dentures


A removable denture snaps onto implants for added stability. You take it out for cleaning. Dr. Chris plans the number and position of implants around your jaw and denture needs.

## Technology Used in Your Implant Care


Livewell uses 3D imaging, digital records, and surgical planning tools to assess your anatomy and plan treatment. Dr. Chris selects the tools appropriate for your case.


### CBCT 3D Imaging


CBCT imaging gives Dr. Chris a three-dimensional view of your jawbone, nerves, and sinuses. It helps him assess bone support and plan implant placement.


### 3D Printing & Guided Surgery


Using data from your 3D scan, we can design a surgical guide that helps control the planned angle and depth. This supports more precise placement than relying on feel alone.


### Implant Selection


We choose implant systems with strong quality standards and biocompatible surface design. What we place in your body matters, so we do not cut corners on implant selection.


### PRF and CGF Healing Support


We may use growth factors derived from a small sample of your own blood to support tissue healing after surgery. This technique can help support the body’s natural healing response around surgical sites.


### Surgical Piezosurgery


An ultrasonic surgical tool that can help us work more precisely around delicate areas. In selected cases, it may reduce trauma to surrounding tissues compared with traditional instruments.

Where do Mantua patients go for implant treatment?

Visit Livewell Dental at 9027 Silverbrook Road, Suite F, Fairfax Station, VA 22039. Our office serves Mantua patients but is located in Fairfax Station. Call (703) 690-0102 to arrange an implant consultation. Allow travel time based on your starting point and traffic.

Can I really get new teeth in one day?

Some patients can receive temporary teeth the same day as implant surgery, especially in certain full-arch cases. It depends on bone quality, overall health, the number of implants, and whether extractions or grafting are needed. Dr. Chris will evaluate your case with 3D imaging and explain whether same-day temporary teeth are appropriate for you.

How long do dental implants last?

With healthy gums, good home care, and regular dental visits, dental implants can last many years. The implant post itself is designed as a long-term replacement root, while the crown, bridge, or prosthesis attached to it may need maintenance, repair, or replacement over time depending on bite forces, materials, and daily habits.

Is dental implant treatment painful?

Dr. Chris uses local anesthesia to numb the treatment area and discusses suitable sedation options when needed. Soreness and swelling can occur afterward. Before you leave Livewell, the team explains recovery instructions and when to contact the office. If sedation is planned, ask about escort and driving requirements before your visit.

What if I do not have enough bone for implants?

If there is not enough bone to support an implant safely, Dr. Chris may recommend bone grafting, sinus lift treatment, ridge augmentation, or a different implant approach. A CBCT scan helps us evaluate bone volume and determine what is possible before treatment begins.

How do I care for dental implants?

Dental implants require daily brushing, careful cleaning around the implant, and regular professional maintenance. Depending on your restoration, we may recommend floss threaders, interdental brushes, or a water flosser. At Livewell Dental, we review your home-care routine and monitor the implant, gums, bite, and restoration during follow-up visits.

How much will my dental implant treatment cost?

Your cost depends on the number of teeth being replaced, the restoration, and whether you need extractions or bone grafting. Ask Livewell Dental for an itemized estimate covering the implant, abutment, final teeth, and any additional treatment. Our team can discuss payment options and any applicable insurance benefits.
