## Your Orthodontic Options


Treatment choice depends on bite complexity, timeline, and goals.


### Clear Aligners


Removable plastic trays that shift teeth gradually using controlled pressure. Each set moves teeth slightly before switching to the next in the series. Treatment is planned digitally, so you see your projected result before starting. Trays are nearly invisible and come out for eating and cleaning.

Learn more


### Traditional Braces


Brackets bonded to teeth with wires that apply precise, controlled pressure. Adjusted every 4-6 weeks as teeth move into position. Offers the most control for complex movements and severe bite corrections. Works for cases where aligners can't achieve the necessary precision.

## The Technology Behind Your Treatment


Digital impressions create precise 3D models. No goop. Better accuracy.


### Intraoral Scanning


We capture digital impressions of your teeth to create precise 3D models. This eliminates uncomfortable goop trays and provides more accurate data for treatment planning and aligner fabrication.


### 3D Treatment Simulation


Before moving a single tooth, we simulate your entire treatment digitally. You see your final result, understand the timeline, and know exactly what movements are involved before committing.


### CBCT 3D Imaging


This technology evaluates your bone density, root positions, and jaw structure in three dimensions. Critical for complex cases where we need to see what's beneath the gum line to plan safe, effective tooth movement.


### Digital Progress Tracking


We monitor your tooth movement digitally throughout treatment. If something isn't tracking as planned, we catch it early and adjust rather than discovering issues months later.


### Custom Appliance Fabrication


Your aligners, retainers, and any functional appliances are designed digitally and fabricated through precision manufacturing. This ensures better fit, faster turnaround, and consistent quality.


### Bite Analysis


We don't just straighten teeth - we ensure they fit together properly when you bite down. Digital bite analysis confirms your final result is functional and stable, not just visually straight.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Depends on case complexity. Clear aligners for mild crowding: 6-12 months. Comprehensive correction with braces: 18-24 months or longer. We give you a specific timeline during consultation.

Am I too old for orthodontics?

No. Adults make up a significant portion of ortho patients. As long as teeth and gums are healthy, treatment works at any age.

Do I need to wear a retainer after treatment?

Yes. Teeth naturally shift back toward original positions. Retainers hold teeth while bone and tissue stabilize. Most patients wear full-time for months, then nightly indefinitely.

Can orthodontics fix my bite, or just straighten my teeth?

Orthodontics corrects many bite issues: overbites, underbites, crossbites, open bites. Severe skeletal discrepancies may require orthodontics plus jaw surgery.

What's the difference between clear aligners and braces?

Clear aligners: removable, nearly invisible, work for mild to moderate cases. Braces: fixed, offer precise control, work for severe crowding or bite issues.

Can I eat normally during orthodontic treatment?

With clear aligners, yes. Remove them to eat. With braces, avoid hard, sticky, crunchy foods that damage brackets or wires.
