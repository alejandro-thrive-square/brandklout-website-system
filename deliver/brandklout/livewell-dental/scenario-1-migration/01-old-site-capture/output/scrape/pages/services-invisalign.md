## Most People Wait Too Long to Fix This


You've thought about it. Crowded teeth, a gap you've learned to hide, a bite that never felt quite right.

Crooked or crowded teeth - wearing unevenly, harder to keep clean

Gaps or spacing - affecting how your bite closes and how your smile looks

Bite misalignment - overbite, underbite, or crossbite creating long-term wear

These aren't just cosmetic problems. Left alone, they can compound, and fixing them later may require more than aligners.


## What Is Invisalign?


Invisalign is a series of custom clear aligners that move your teeth in small, controlled steps. You wear each set for one to two weeks, then advance to the next. No brackets, no wires, nothing cemented to your teeth.

What most people don't realize: the aligner brand isn't what determines the result. Case planning does.


## We Ask What's Causing This Before We Discuss What to Fix


Start With a Consultation

Most offices take a scan, show you a simulation, and hand you aligners. We slow down before that step. Orthodontic problems often have an underlying cause - a bite that's off, crowding driven by jaw structure, or wear patterns that tell a story about how your teeth function together.

We take a step back to look at the full system:

Is the crowding purely cosmetic, or is it creating bite stress?

Are there signs of grinding or jaw tension that need to be addressed first?

Will straightening alone solve the problem, or does the bite need correction too?

Are there restorations (crowns, veneers) that factor into the treatment plan?

What does your gum health look like before we start moving teeth?

We won't talk you into Invisalign if something else should come first. And if Invisalign is the right move, we'll plan it with long-term stability in mind, not just a straight smile on the day treatment ends.


## Three Reasons Invisalign Works - When It's Planned Right


01


### No Disruption to Your Life


The aligners come out for meals, brushing, and flossing. You don't change how you eat or how you clean your teeth. Most patients stop noticing the aligners within a few days.

02


### Precision Moves, Tracked Over Time


Every aligner set is designed to move specific teeth specific distances. We monitor your progress at regular check-ins and adjust the plan if your teeth respond differently than projected. No case goes on autopilot.

03


### A Smile Designed Before Treatment Starts


Before you wear a single aligner, we show you a digital preview of your projected result. You know what you're working toward - you're not hoping it works out. You're watching the plan execute.


## Invisalign, Done the Livewell Way


How We Plan Your Case

We Start With the Diagnosis, Not the Sales Pitch

Your first appointment includes a clinical exam, intraoral scan, and bite analysis. We're not trying to qualify you for a product - we're trying to understand your mouth. That means looking at your gum health, wear patterns, jaw function, and the full picture before recommending anything.

Your Digital Plan Is Built Before You Commit

We use your 3D scan to map out the full tooth movement sequence. You see the projected before and after on screen. We walk through the plan together, answer your questions, and make sure the goal matches what you actually want.

One Doctor, Every Appointment, Start to Finish

Dr. Chris handles every check-in personally. There's no handoff to an assistant or a hygienist. When your teeth move slower in one area or faster in another, the person who built your plan is the one making the call.

Progress Tracked at Every Stage

We photograph your teeth at each visit and compare them against your plan. If a tooth isn't tracking correctly, we catch it early - before it throws off the rest of the sequence. Refinements are part of the process, not a sign something went wrong.

Final Result Documented. Retention Plan Set

When treatment is complete, we document your result with professional photos and set you up with retainers designed to hold the position. The work doesn't stop when the last aligner comes out - because that's when most people lose their results.

## Start With Clarity, Not Commitment


At Livewell, Invisalign treatment begins with a clear evaluation before any decision is made. You leave your visit understanding what is happening with your teeth, bite, and gum health, and whether clear aligners are the right fit for your goals.

How long does Invisalign take?

Mild cases can be complete in 6 to 12 months. More involved cases with bite correction typically run 12 to 18 months. We give you a realistic timeline after reviewing your scan - not a number designed to sell you on starting.

Does Invisalign hurt?

You'll feel pressure when you switch to a new aligner set, usually for a day or two. Most patients describe it as noticeable but manageable, and it fades quickly as your teeth adjust.

Can I eat and drink normally?

Yes. You remove the aligners before eating and drinking anything other than water. That also means your teeth are easier to keep clean during treatment - no food trapped around brackets.

How many hours a day do I wear them?

20 to 22 hours per day. The more consistently you wear them, the more predictably your teeth move. Skipping wear time is the most common reason cases run longer than planned.

Is Invisalign right for complex cases?

Invisalign can handle cases that once required braces. Whether it's right for your situation depends on your bite, gum health, and bone structure. We'll tell you honestly if braces would serve you better.

What happens after I finish treatment?

We fit you with custom retainers to hold your result. Teeth have memory - without retention, they drift back. We talk through a retainer plan before you finish treatment so there are no surprises.

How much does Invisalign cost?

Invisalign uses a series of clear aligners to gradually straighten your teeth, and most patients pay $4,000 to $7,000 for treatment. Where you land in that range depends on how much movement your teeth need, how long treatment takes, and how many aligners are required.

It's also worth asking what's included in your quote, since some cover the consult, scans, refinements, and retainers, while others charge those separately.

Good to know: if your dental plan covers aligners, that can meaningfully lower your out-of-pocket cost.
