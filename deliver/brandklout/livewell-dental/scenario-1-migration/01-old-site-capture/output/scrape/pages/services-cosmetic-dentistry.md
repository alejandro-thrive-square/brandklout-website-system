## Why Cosmetic Dentistry Here Is Different


Most cosmetic dentistry focuses on making teeth look good. We focus on making them look good and function correctly for decades. A beautiful smile should not only look good on day one. It should feel comfortable, function properly, and be planned to hold up over time.

At Livewell, cosmetic cases start with diagnosis. We evaluate your bite, your jaw function, your facial proportions, and the health of your teeth and gums. Then we design a plan that solves the cosmetic concern and addresses the root cause. The result looks natural, feels comfortable, and lasts.

We treat cosmetic dentistry as precision work, not assembly line production

Every case is designed individually based on your anatomy and goals

We use conservative, tooth-preserving techniques whenever possible

Dr. Chris performs all cosmetic procedures personally from start to finish

## Find the Right Solution for Your Smile



### Porcelain Veneers


Thin ceramic shells that cover the front surface of teeth to improve shape, color, size, and symmetry. Veneers are more involved than bonding, but they are longer-lasting and more stain-resistant.

Best for:

Transforming multiple teeth

achieving permanent color change

correcting shape and alignment simultaneously.

Learn More About Veneers


### Dental Bonding


Composite resin is sculpted directly onto the tooth to repair chips, close small gaps, or reshape edges. It is conservative, usually completed in one visit, and preserves natural tooth structure.

Single-tooth cosmetic issues

small repairs

patients who want to preserve natural tooth structure.

Learn More About Bonding


### Teeth Whitening


Professional whitening lightens the overall shade of your teeth and can improve stains that brushing alone cannot remove.

Healthy teeth with yellowing or staining

Patients who want a brighter smile without changing tooth shape

Smile refreshes before veneers, bonding, or other cosmetic work

Learn More About Whitening

## How Cosmetic Cases Work at Livewell


Cosmetic dentistry here follows a structured process designed to deliver results that last.

Comprehensive Evaluation

We start with our DCOE protocol. This includes clinical examination, digital imaging, bite analysis, and a conversation about what you want to achieve and why. We look at your teeth, your bite, your jaw function, and your facial proportions.

Digital Smile Design

Before we touch your teeth, we create a digital preview of your result. You see what the final outcome will look like. We refine it together until the design matches your goals and works with your anatomy.

Treatment Planning

We walk you through the recommended procedures, the timeline, and the investment required. You get a clear picture of what's involved before committing to anything.

Precision Execution

Dr. Chris performs all cosmetic work personally. No handoffs to associates or lab techs making decisions without seeing you. Every case gets individual attention and the time it needs to be done right.

Long-Term Care

We follow up to make sure your results hold up. Cosmetic dentistry isn't fire-and-forget. We monitor your bite, check for wear, and address any issues early.

## The Technology Behind Your Smile Transformation


Explore our technology


### Digital Smile Design


Before starting any cosmetic case, we create a digital preview of your final result. You see what veneers, bonding, or crowns will look like on your face before we prep a single tooth. Changes happen on screen, not in your mouth.


### eLab Digital Design Lab


Your veneers and crowns are designed in-house using digital models of your teeth. Dr. Chris works directly with the lab technician to ensure shape, color, and fit match the plan. No outsourcing. Faster turnaround. Better results.


### Intraoral Scanning


We capture precise digital impressions of your teeth without the gag-inducing trays full of impression material. The scanner maps your teeth in 3D, which means better-fitting restorations and a more comfortable experience for you.


### PolarEyes Shade Matching


Matching tooth color isn't guesswork here. PolarEyes captures the exact shade of your natural teeth under different lighting conditions so your veneers, bonding, or crowns blend seamlessly. What looks right in the office looks right everywhere else too.


### Professional Studio Photography


We document your smile with professional-grade photos before, during, and after treatment. This helps us design your result, track progress, and show you the transformation. You get a clear record of where you started and where you ended up.


### Dentofacial Analysis


Your smile doesn't exist in isolation. We analyze how your teeth relate to your lips, your facial symmetry, and your overall proportions. Cosmetic work designed around your face looks natural. Cosmetic work designed in isolation looks like dentistry.

How long do cosmetic dental results last?

It depends on the procedure and how well you care for them. Porcelain veneers typically last 15 to 20 years or longer. Bonding lasts 5 to 10 years. Teeth whitening results fade over time and may need touch-ups every few years. Proper oral hygiene, avoiding hard foods, and addressing bite issues extend the lifespan of any cosmetic work.

Is cosmetic dentistry covered by insurance?

Most cosmetic procedures are not covered by dental insurance because they're considered elective. However, if a procedure is partly restorative (like a crown that fixes a broken tooth and improves appearance), insurance may cover a portion. We'll help you understand what's covered and provide financing options if needed.

How do I choose between veneers and bonding?

It varies widely depending on what's involved. A single veneer is very different from a full smile transformation with veneers, crowns, and gum contouring. We provide a detailed estimate during your consultation once we understand your goals and evaluate your case.
