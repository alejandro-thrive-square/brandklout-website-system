Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes. We accept most dental insurance plans and can help verify your benefits before treatment.

Will you check my insurance benefits for me?

Yes. Our team can review your coverage, estimate your benefits, and explain what your plan may contribute.

Will I know the cost before treatment begins?

Yes. We believe in clear pricing, so you will receive a treatment estimate before moving forward.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We offer flexible financing options, including in-house payment plans and third-party financing when available.

What financing options do you work with?

Livewell Dental works with options such as Cherry Financing, Alphaeon Credit, and One Patient at a Time.

Do you have options for patients without insurance?

Yes. The Livewell Membership is designed for patients without dental insurance or those who prefer a simpler alternative.

Can I use financing for cosmetic or implant treatment?

Yes. Financing may be used for many cosmetic, restorative, and implant treatments, depending on approval and the treatment plan.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, personal checks, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and One Patient at a Time.
