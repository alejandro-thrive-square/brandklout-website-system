# Welcome to Livewell Dental



## A Space Designed Around Thoughtful Care


Livewell Dental was designed to feel calm, clean, and intentional, but not just for appearance. The space supports the way we care for adults who want thoughtful dentistry: time to talk, room to listen, photography to see the details clearly, and technology that helps us make better decisions before treatment begins.


## Keeping You Comfortable


Small comforts can change the way a dental visit feels.

We’ve designed the space to help you feel more relaxed, supported, and at ease.


### Aromatherapy


A calming detail that helps the office feel less clinical from the moment you arrive.


### Warm Blankets


A simple comfort to help you settle in during longer visits.


### Pillows


Added support to help you feel more comfortable in the chair.


### Ear Plugs


Helpful for patients who feel sensitive to dental sounds.


### Noise-Canceling Headphones


A quieter experience for patients who want more calm during treatment.


### Boxed Water


A small refreshment to make your visit feel more personal and cared for.


## Take a Look Around Livewell Dental


A calm, refined office designed for thoughtful care. Explore the rooms, details, and quiet touches that shape the Livewell experience from the moment you arrive.
