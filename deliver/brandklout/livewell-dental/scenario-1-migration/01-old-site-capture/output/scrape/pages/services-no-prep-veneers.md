## You Want a Better Smile. You Don’t Want to Regret the Process.


Veneers can feel exciting until one question gets in the way:

Will healthy teeth have to be shaved down?

That hesitation is reasonable. Enamel is valuable, and once it is removed, it cannot be put back. At Livewell Dental, we begin with a more careful question: can we create the smile you want while preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible?

A permanent decision

‍Your smile should be improved with care, not rushed into irreversible treatment

A conservative first look

‍We study your tooth shape, enamel, bite, and smile goals before recommending veneers.

A clearer path forward

‍Sometimes no prep is possible. Sometimes another option is better. Either way, you should know why.

The goal is not just a prettier smile. It is a smile you can feel good about choosing.

## What Are No Prep Veneers?


Thin Porcelain Layer

Natural Edge Shape

No prep veneers are thin porcelain veneers placed with little to no removal of healthy enamel. For the right patient, they can improve the shape, color, size, and balance of the smile by adding carefully designed porcelain instead of aggressively reducing natural teeth.

A brighter smile without an artificial look.

More even edges, proportions, and symmetry.

A fuller look for small, narrow, or worn teeth.

A broader smile when teeth sit slightly inward.


## Are You the Right Candidate for No Prep Veneers?


No prep veneers work best when your smile has room to accept a small amount of added porcelain. That usually means the teeth can be enhanced without looking thick, heavy, or unnatural.

Teeth that feel small or narrow

‍No prep veneers may add fuller shape and better balance.

A smile that feels slightly set back

‍Porcelain can sometimes bring the smile forward in a natural way.

Worn or uneven edges

‍Careful design can restore length, shape, and symmetry.

Healthy enamel

‍Strong enamel helps create a more predictable bond.

Find Out If No Prep Is Right for You

If no prep is not the right fit, that is not bad news. It simply means your smile may need a different conservative path.

Natural-looking veneers depend on details most people never see. Shade, translucency, texture, edge shape, facial balance, and how the teeth reflect light all matter.

At Livewell Dental, digital planning and eLab-style communication help Dr. Chris and the lab design veneers with more precision before anything is finalized. The goal is simple: a smile that looks polished, but still belongs to your face.

Am I a candidate for no prep veneers?

You may be a good candidate if your teeth are slightly small, narrow, worn, or set back, and there is room to add porcelain naturally. If your teeth are crowded, prominent, very dark, or heavily restored, another option may be more appropriate.

Will no prep veneers look bulky?

They can look bulky if they are forced into the wrong case. That is why planning matters. Dr. Chris evaluates your tooth position, smile shape, enamel, and bite before deciding whether no prep veneers make sense.

Can veneers fix small or narrow teeth?

Yes, in many cases veneers can improve tooth size, shape, and smile width. No prep veneers are often most successful when the smile can be enhanced by adding carefully designed porcelain. For broader cosmetic planning, you may also want to review our smile makeover page.

What if I am not a no prep candidate?

That does not mean you are out of options. Depending on your smile, Dr. Chris may recommend teeth whitening, dental bonding, gum recontouring, alignment, minimal-prep veneers, or a more complete cosmetic plan.

How long do no prep veneers last?

With good planning, strong bonding, proper bite management, and regular dental care, porcelain veneers can last many years. Longevity depends on your enamel, bite, habits, and how well the veneers are maintained.

What photos should I send for a virtual consultation?

Send a full smile photo, a relaxed resting photo, a side or angle photo, and a close-up smile photo. You can also include an inspiration photo if there is a smile style you like. No filters or special lighting are needed.

Can I get veneers if I grind my teeth?

Grinding does not disqualify you from veneers, but it does need to be addressed before and after treatment. Uncontrolled grinding puts veneers at risk of chipping or failing early. At Livewell, every case begins with a bite evaluation. If grinding is a factor, Dr. Chris will discuss how to protect your result long-term before a single veneer is placed.
