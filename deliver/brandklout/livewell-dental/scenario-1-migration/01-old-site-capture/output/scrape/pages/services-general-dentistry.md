## Most Dentists Tell You What's Wrong. We Tell You Why It Keeps Happening.


A cavity isn't just a cavity. It's a sign that something in your oral environment - bacteria levels, bite forces, diet, or hygiene habits - is working against your teeth. Most offices fix the cavity and send you home. We ask what created it in the first place.

That question changes everything about how we practice general dentistry. We track your data at every visit, test your bacterial load when the pattern suggests we should, and build a care plan around your specific risk profile - not a generic six-month protocol.

One patient at a time - no double-booking, no rushed appointments

Data tracked at every visit and compared against your last

Treatment recommendations grounded in evidence, not habit

Same doctor manages everything from your cleaning to your crown

Start With a Full Evaluation


## The Mouth You Have at 60 Is Built by the Decisions Made Today


Learn About Our Approach

Most dental problems are preventable. The ones that aren't are almost always detectable early - when the solution is simple, fast, and inexpensive. The patients who end up needing the most complex treatment are rarely the ones who were unlucky. They're the ones whose warning signs weren't caught, or weren't explained.

At Livewell, we tell you exactly where you stand at every visit. Not vague reassurance - actual numbers, actual comparisons, actual risk assessment. So you can make informed decisions about your own health.


## Find the Right Solution for Your Situation



### Preventive Care


Prevention isn't just cleaning your teeth twice a year. It's tracking bacterial load, measuring gum health at every visit, and matching your care intensity to your actual risk level. The goal is a mouth that stays healthy between appointments, not one that resets every six months.

Explore Preventive Care


### Dental Bridges


When a tooth is missing, a bridge fills the gap by spanning from one supporting structure to another. We offer three types - tooth-supported, implant-supported, and Maryland bridges - because the right choice depends on your specific situation, not what's easiest to place.

Explore Dental Bridges


### Root Canal Treatment


A root canal removes infected tissue from inside a tooth and seals it to stop the spread of damage. Done well, it saves a tooth that would otherwise need to be extracted. Done at Livewell, it's precise, comfortable, and built to last.

Explore Explore Root Canal Treatment


### Dental Emergencies


A broken tooth, severe pain, a lost restoration, or a dental abscess - when something can't wait, we make room for you. We also spend time understanding what caused the emergency so the same problem doesn't repeat itself.

Explore Emergency Dentistry


### Professional Cleaning


We use EMS Airflow - a warm water and powder system that removes biofilm more thoroughly than traditional scaling, with less abrasion and less sensitivity. It reaches below the gumline and around implant surfaces where standard instruments can't. Not the same cleaning you've had before.

Explore Professional Cleaning


## How We Work Through Every Case


Not All General Dentistry Is the Same - and That Matters More Than You Think

We Start With the Full Picture

Your first visit includes a comprehensive clinical exam, digital X-rays, intraoral scan, and a full review of your dental and medical history. We're not looking for what to bill - we're building an accurate picture of your oral health so every recommendation that follows is grounded in real data.

We Tell You What We Found and Why It Matters

After the exam, Dr. Chris walks you through the findings in plain language. What's stable, what's at risk, what needs treatment now, and what can wait. You leave the first visit knowing exactly where you stand - not just that you have "a few small cavities."

We Build a Plan Around Your Priorities

Some patients want everything addressed immediately. Others need to phase treatment over time. We work around your schedule, your budget, and your comfort level - without compromising on what actually needs to happen for your long-term health.

Treatment Is Done With Precision, Not Speed

Dr. Chris works under magnification on every procedure. Rubber dam isolation is used for every bonding case. We don't rush through appointments because we don't double-book. One patient at a time means the work gets done right the first time.

We Track, Compare, and Adjust Over Time

At every recall visit, we compare your current data against previous visits - gum measurements, bacterial levels, X-ray changes. If something is trending in the wrong direction, we catch it early. If everything is holding steady, we tell you that too.

## The Technology Behind General Dentistry at Livewell


See All Our Technology


### EMS Airflow


Not a standard cleaning. A more thorough one.

A warm water and powder spray removes biofilm, staining, and deposits more completely than metal scalers - without the abrasion or sensitivity. It reaches subgingival areas and implant surfaces that traditional instruments can't access safely.


### OralDNA Salivary Testing


Gum disease has a cause. This is how we find it.

A saliva sample identifies the specific bacteria driving periodontal risk - and at what levels. That information turns a generic treatment protocol into a targeted one. Different bacteria require different responses.


### Intraoral Scanning


Digital records that improve every time we use them.

A 3D scan of your teeth and bite creates a precise digital model used for treatment planning, restoration design, and baseline comparison at future visits. No trays, no impression material.


### CBCT 3D Imaging


When a flat X-ray isn't enough, we go three-dimensional.

For complex diagnoses, bone evaluation, or cases where anatomy affects the plan, CBCT gives us a full 3D model of your bone, roots, and jaw structure. Used selectively, when the case warrants it.


### High-Powered Loupes


Every procedure done under magnification. Every time.

Dr. Chris works under magnification on every patient, every appointment, without exception. Margins, cracks, and early decay that are invisible to the naked eye become detectable and manageable.


## What Patients Say About Their Results


STORIES OF THE LIVES WE’VE TOUCHED

What does general dentistry include?

General dentistry covers the full range of care most patients need - preventive cleanings and exams, fillings, bridges, root canals, emergency treatment, and ongoing health monitoring. At Livewell, it also includes salivary bacterial testing, comprehensive risk assessment, and data tracking across every visit.

How often should I see a general dentist?

Twice a year is the standard recommendation. At Livewell, the frequency depends on your actual risk level. Low-risk patients may maintain well on two visits per year. Patients with active gum disease or elevated bacterial risk may need more frequent monitoring. We tell you what your data suggests, not what the calendar says.

What if I haven't been to the dentist in several years?

Come in. We see patients who haven't had care in two years, five years, or longer - without judgment. We assess where things stand, tell you what's urgent, what can be phased, and what the path forward looks like. The goal is to get you stable and keep you that way.
