## THE QUESTIONS OTHERS OVERLOOKED


While Most Dentists Focus on "What's Broken?" Dr. Chris Kept Asking "What's the Root Cause?"

During his time at UCSF dental school, he began to see something troubling. Patient after patient returned with the same recurring problems, including new cavities, replacement crowns, and ongoing gum treatments. It was a cycle that never seemed to end.

But the deeper questions went unanswered:

What triggered this in the first place?

Could it have been prevented?

How do we break the cycle for good?

Those unanswered questions stayed with him long after graduation. That's when the realization hit. Treating symptoms wasn't enough. He wanted to get to the source.


## THE ART OF DOING IT RIGHT


Deliberate, Patient, Refreshingly Thorough

Nothing is rushed here. Not the way Dr. Chris listens, plans, or builds your trust. First visits often stretch beyond an hour without a single instrument being picked up. The point isn't to jump into treatment. The point is to truly understand:

What brought you in

What's been overlooked

What's standing between you and better health

Livewell Dental was founded on a simple belief: take the time, do it once, do it right.

"Some patients come in convinced they need a crown. What they actually need is someone willing to slow down and ask the right questions."

## The Person Behind the Care


Before Dr. Chris is a dentist, he is a husband, a father, and someone who understands how much the little moments matter. Life with Sara and their son Ollie has made him even more aware that people do not just want excellent dentistry. They want to feel safe, understood, and cared for by someone who is fully present.

Outside the office, you may find him making pasta at home, enjoying coffee, skiing, training, or looking at the world through a camera lens. Those parts of his life keep him grounded. They remind him to slow down, notice the details, and treat each person with the same care he hopes his own family would receive.
