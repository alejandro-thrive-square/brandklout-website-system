## Choosing the Right Window for Culver City Patients


The best time to have wisdom teeth removed is before they cause an acute problem. The American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons recommends evaluation in the late teens and early twenties, when root development is incomplete and bone is less dense, leading to faster, more predictable recovery. Patients in their late twenties and thirties still receive excellent outcomes, but root development and bone density differences can extend the recovery window slightly.

For Culver City professionals, the practical message is to schedule during a period when you can commit two to three days to rest and recovery. That window is the acute phase. Most patients are functional for desk or remote work by day three or four and cleared for all activity within a week. Planning that window around a production schedule, a hiatus, or a quiet patch on the calendar makes the experience far less disruptive than waiting for a symptom to force the issue at the worst possible moment.

Production Hiatus

A planned break in production provides a natural window for a procedure with a two-to-three day acute recovery. Schedule your consultation well in advance to secure the surgical date you need.

Long Weekend

A Thursday or Friday procedure gives four to five days of recovery before returning to office or set work, which is sufficient for many straightforward wisdom tooth extractions.

Summer or Holiday Break

Longer breaks offer the most flexibility. Many Culver City professionals handle longer-deferred procedures during summer or between December and January when studio activity slows.

Urgent Need

When infection or acute pain makes waiting impossible, we work to evaluate and treat urgent cases promptly. Do not delay if symptoms are escalating.


## In-House Imaging and Precision Surgical Planning


Every wisdom tooth case at Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center begins with a detailed evaluation using Cone Beam 3D imaging. This technology gives us a three-dimensional view of each tooth’s position, root development, angulation, and proximity to the inferior alveolar nerve and other critical structures. We use this data to plan the surgical approach for each tooth before any instrument is used, which supports more predictable results and more informed patient consent.

Our complete pre-operative guidelines and pre-anesthesia instructions are provided well in advance so you arrive fully prepared. IV sedation is available for all wisdom tooth cases and typically results in no memory of the procedure. A responsible adult must drive you home if sedation is used. Our team provides detailed post-operative care instructions at discharge and is available for follow-up questions throughout recovery.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Wisdom Teeth Removal near Culver City


How long is the drive from Culver City to your office?

From central Culver City, the drive to our office via Sepulveda Boulevard or National Boulevard heading north to Wilshire is typically 10 to 15 minutes. Our address is 12300 Wilshire Blvd #326, Los Angeles, CA 90025. Building parking is available.

Can I book a consultation and surgery appointment on the same day?

Same-day consultation and surgery is possible when the clinical situation allows, the fasting requirement for sedation can be met, and surgical availability exists. Contact our office in advance to discuss whether this is feasible for your case. When same-day surgery is not available, we work to minimize the gap between consultation and procedure for patients with tight scheduling windows.

How many days off should I plan for?

Plan for the day of surgery plus two full days of rest for most straightforward cases. Day three and four typically see significant improvement, and most patients doing sedentary creative or professional work are functional by day three. Physical activity, heavy lifting, and anything that raises blood pressure should be avoided for a full week.

What if I have dental insurance through a studio or streaming company?

We work with all major PPO dental carriers. Studio and streaming company dental plans are typically through Aetna, Delta Dental, Cigna, or MetLife, all of which we work with. We verify your specific plan benefits before scheduling surgery. Contact our office with your insurance information before your consultation to confirm participation.

My wisdom teeth are not hurting — do I still need to have them removed?

Not always, but symptoms are not a reliable indicator of clinical need. Many impacted wisdom teeth develop problems without causing noticeable pain until the problem is advanced. We evaluate each tooth on its own imaging — position, angulation, root proximity to nerves, and whether any pathology is developing — and make a recommendation based on clinical evidence rather than symptoms alone.

Is it safe to have all four wisdom teeth removed at once?

For most patients, removing all four at once under IV sedation is the most efficient and clinically sensible approach, as it means a single recovery period instead of two or more separate procedures. Our surgeons evaluate your case individually and discuss whether a phased approach is preferable based on your specific anatomy, health history, and circumstances.
