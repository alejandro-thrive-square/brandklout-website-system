# About Us



## About Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center


Choosing an oral surgeon is one of the more consequential healthcare decisions you can make. Whether you need a wisdom tooth removed, a dental implant placed, or a more complex surgical procedure, the training, technology, and environment your surgeon provides will shape every part of that experience. At Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center, our surgeons and staff have built a practice in West Los Angeles that meets that standard at every level: board-level credentials, advanced in-office technology, and the kind of personalized attention that makes a real difference when you are facing oral surgery.

We serve patients from across the Westside, including Beverly Hills, Santa Monica, Brentwood, Culver City, Westwood, and the surrounding communities. Our office at 12300 Wilshire Blvd #326, Los Angeles, CA 90025 offers a modern, comfortable surgical environment where you can receive the full scope of oral and maxillofacial care without being referred across town for imaging, anesthesia, or additional procedures.

Board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeons complete four to six years of hospital-based residency training after dental school, rotating through anesthesiology, general surgery, internal medicine, and other disciplines. The American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery requires candidates to pass written and oral examinations reviewed by a panel of peers before certification is granted. This level of training distinguishes oral and maxillofacial surgeons from general dental providers, particularly for cases that involve bone loss, anatomical complexity, sedation, or multiple procedures in a single visit.
