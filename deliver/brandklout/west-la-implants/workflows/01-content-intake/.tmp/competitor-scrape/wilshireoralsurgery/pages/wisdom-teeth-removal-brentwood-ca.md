## Why Wisdom Teeth May Need to Come Out


Third molars, commonly called wisdom teeth, typically emerge between the ages of 17 and 25. Because the jaw is often too small to accommodate them fully, wisdom teeth may become impacted, meaning they are unable to erupt normally through the gumline. Impacted wisdom teeth can remain entirely beneath the bone, emerge at an angle that crowds adjacent teeth, or partially break through the gum in ways that create persistent infection risk.

Common reasons patients from Brentwood seek removal include pain or swelling at the back of the jaw, pressure behind the second molars, recurring infections around a partially erupted tooth, and recommendations from an orthodontist or general dentist following panoramic imaging. According to the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, the majority of young adults have at least one wisdom tooth that requires monitoring or removal. Earlier removal during the late teenage years or early twenties typically results in shorter recovery times, because root development is incomplete and the surrounding bone is more flexible.

Fully Impacted

Tooth is completely below the bone and gumline, causing pressure or cyst formation.

Partially Erupted

Tooth has broken through partially, creating a gum flap that traps bacteria and leads to pericoronitis.

Angularly Positioned

Tooth is growing sideways into the adjacent molar or at a forward angle, creating pressure and crowding.

Symptom-Free but Monitored

No current symptoms, but imaging reveals developing complications that make proactive removal advisable.

If you are a parent booking for a student who will be home through August, or a student planning your own care before returning to school, we recommend scheduling your consultation as early in the summer as possible. ## Frequently Asked Questions — Wisdom Teeth Removal Near Brentwood


How far is Wilshire Oral Surgery from Brentwood?

Our office is approximately 1.2 miles from the center of Brentwood, located at 12300 Wilshire Blvd #326, Los Angeles, CA 90025. Most patients drive east on Wilshire Boulevard and arrive in under 10 minutes. We are also accessible via San Vicente Boulevard and Sunset Boulevard.

Can I have my wisdom teeth removed with sedation?

Yes. We offer IV sedation administered directly by our board-certified surgeons. Most patients who opt for sedation have no memory of the procedure and recover comfortably at home the same day. You must have a responsible adult drive you home and stay with you for the first few hours. We can review your sedation options during your consultation.

When is the best time for a college student to have wisdom teeth removed?

Summer break is widely considered the best window for college-age patients. Recovery from a standard four-tooth extraction is typically three to seven days, and having that time at home before returning to campus makes the process much more manageable. We recommend booking as early as possible during the summer, as our schedule fills quickly between June and August.

Do you accept PPO dental insurance for wisdom tooth removal?

Yes, we work with a wide range of PPO dental insurance plans and will verify your coverage before your procedure. Medically necessary wisdom tooth removal, including impacted teeth, is commonly covered at least in part. We provide clear estimates before surgery so you know what to expect. For those without insurance or with limited coverage, we offer flexible payment options.

What imaging do you use for wisdom tooth evaluation?

We use in-house Cone Beam 3D imaging, which gives our surgeons a precise, three-dimensional view of your wisdom teeth including root position, angle, and proximity to surrounding nerves and structures. This allows us to plan the safest and most efficient surgical approach without requiring an outside referral for imaging.

How long does recovery take after wisdom tooth removal?

Most patients feel significantly better by day three or four. Swelling and mild discomfort are most pronounced in the first 48 to 72 hours and gradually improve. We provide detailed post-operative care instructions and are available by phone if any concerns arise during your recovery. Patients who have all four teeth removed simultaneously may experience slightly longer recovery than those having one or two teeth extracted.
