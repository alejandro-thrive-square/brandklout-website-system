## What First-Time Patients Should Know About Wisdom Teeth


Third molars are the last set of permanent teeth to develop, usually beginning to emerge between the ages of 17 and 25. Most people’s jaws do not have adequate space for all four, which leads to impaction. An impacted wisdom tooth is one that cannot fully erupt into its normal position, whether because of bone, adjacent teeth, or its own growth angle. Left in place, impacted wisdom teeth can cause infection, cyst formation, crowding, and damage to neighboring roots.

The American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons recommends evaluation in the late teens or early twenties while roots are still developing and bone is less dense, which leads to shorter, more predictable procedures and faster recovery. For Palms patients who are in that window and have already received a referral from their dentist or orthodontist, the right next step is a consultation with a board-certified oral surgeon, not further delay.

Impaction = Surgical Removal

Impacted wisdom teeth require a surgical approach — local or IV sedation, removal of overlying tissue and sometimes bone, and tooth sectioning in many cases. This is routine for an oral surgeon.

Earlier Is Easier

Root development in the late teens means shorter, less curved roots and softer surrounding bone. Recovery is typically faster and the procedure is less complex than waiting until the mid-twenties or later.

Sedation Makes It Manageable

Most patients choose IV sedation and have no memory of the procedure. Anxiety about the surgery itself is the most common concern — sedation addresses it directly and effectively.

Insurance Usually Applies

PPO dental plans typically cover wisdom tooth removal, especially for impacted teeth. We verify coverage before scheduling so you know the cost before committing.


## What to Expect at Your First Oral Surgery Appointment


The consultation is not the surgery. The first visit is about understanding where your wisdom teeth are, what the recommended approach is, and what the cost will be. We use Cone Beam 3D imaging to get a precise three-dimensional view of each tooth’s position, root anatomy, and proximity to the inferior alveolar nerve. We explain what we find in plain terms. If you have questions about the procedure, the recovery, the sedation, or the cost, we answer all of them before you make any commitment.

The surgical appointment is scheduled separately, typically within a few weeks. Our pre-operative guidelines and pre-anesthesia instructions are sent to you well in advance so you arrive prepared. If you choose IV sedation, you will need a responsible adult to drive you home. The procedure itself takes under an hour for most patients. Recovery at home follows, with our complete post-operative care instructions provided at discharge.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Wisdom Teeth Removal near Palms


I’ve never had oral surgery before — what is the first step?

The first step is scheduling a consultation. Bring any X-rays from your dentist or orthodontist, or we take in-house 3D imaging. We walk you through what we find, explain what the procedure will involve, and provide a full cost estimate. Nothing is scheduled until you are comfortable with the plan and the information we have given you.

Will IV sedation make me completely unaware of the surgery?

Most patients who choose IV sedation have no memory of the procedure at all. The sedation produces a deeply relaxed state and most patients are genuinely surprised when they are told surgery is over. Waking up feels like coming out of a short, comfortable sleep. For anxious patients, this is one of the most consistently reassuring aspects of the experience.

How far is the Palms neighborhood from your office?

From the Palms area, Sawtelle Boulevard heading north to Wilshire or Barrington Avenue north to Wilshire are the most direct routes, typically 10 to 15 minutes depending on traffic. Our address is 12300 Wilshire Blvd #326, Los Angeles, CA 90025.

What if I can’t afford the full cost upfront?

We offer financing through CareCredit, Alphaeon, and Cherry, all of which allow you to spread the cost over monthly payments. Some plans offer promotional deferred-interest periods depending on the amount and your credit. We walk through financing options at your consultation and verify your insurance coverage so the total financial picture is clear before you commit to any treatment.

How bad will the pain be during recovery?

Recovery discomfort is most significant in the first 48 to 72 hours and is generally well-managed with the medication regimen we prescribe or recommend. Most patients describe the discomfort as moderate and manageable during this period. By day three or four, the majority of patients feel significantly more comfortable. We provide complete post-operative care guidance and are available for follow-up questions throughout your recovery.

What is the difference between a board-certified oral surgeon and a general dentist doing extractions?

A board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon has completed dental school followed by a minimum of four years of hospital-based residency training, including surgical management of impacted teeth, sedation and anesthesia, trauma, and oral pathology. Board certification requires additional written and oral examinations beyond residency. General dentists perform routine extractions but typically refer complex or impacted wisdom teeth to oral surgeons for this reason.
