# Hidden Benefits of Dental Implants: Things You Should Know


Are you on the fence about dental implants? When you lose a tooth, it's difficult to know how to handle the next steps. With other options like dentures or bridgework available, you may be wondering: why implants? Most people know dental implants as a more expensive cosmetic option, but they actually have hidden advantages that can make a higher upfront cost worthwhile. Let’s dig into some of the hidden benefits of dental implants:


## Dental Implants Don’t Need To Be That Painful


Going into any dental surgery, it’s normal to be wary about pain. However, dental implant surgery can cause minimal discomfort and do wonders for your oral health long term. People even return to work the day after getting implants! Your dentist will use local anesthesia, so you likely won’t feel pain during the surgery itself. Most patients report feeling pressure during surgery, but not pain. Many patients also opt for sedation during the surgery, and take over-the-counter pain relief or prescription medication after. Take your surgery as an opportunity to stock up on ice cream, pudding, and other fun foods to eat while you’re recovering. If implants are on the horizon for you, you can experience lower pain levels with proper preparation!


## Your Lifestyle Can Stay The Same After Getting Dental Implants


Unlike dentures, dental implants allow you to fully taste your food, and chew on whatever you’re craving– provided you’re healed from surgery, of course. Your oral hygiene also stays the same after getting dental implants, because you can care for them like regular teeth. Implants are an excellent way to take care of your oral health while maintaining your current lifestyle. You can brush, floss, and eat with implants functioning just like your natural teeth. Dental implants may have a higher initial cost, but they keep your diet and care consistent.


## Dental Implants Can Look Like Natural Teeth


Worrying over the look and feel of your new dental implants is normal. For the life-changing benefits that implants can offer, it’s worth moving past these concerns. Remember that modern implants are customizable and skilled dentistry can match your existing teeth. Any teeth you’re currently missing may also impact your pronunciation, and implants can provide a foundation for clear speech. So not only can implants look great, they can help you sound great too. Your confidence may even benefit from dental implants!


## Dental Implants May Last A Lifetime


Dental implants are known as a more expensive option, but they’re also the longer-lasting one. While alternatives like dentures will provide a less expensive fix initially, they require more frequent maintenance. Dentures require regular refitting, but implants can last a lifetime with proper dental care. This means the upfront cost can have a huge benefit to your long term oral health.


## Dental Implant Rejection is Rare


Many hopeful patients have worries for the implant process, particularly that their body will reject their dental implants. In reality, dental implants have a 95% success rate. This is because dental implants act like your real teeth. Dental implants are installed with titanium, a material the body naturally accepts. Patients with complications generally have other competing medical conditions. You can check with your dentist about potential complications connected to your medical history, but remember that these complications are rare. With modern technology, precise planning is possible, upping your likelihood of success. Worries are normal for any procedure, but don’t let dental anxiety stop you from finding the best solutions for you.


## Getting Dental Implants can Aid Oral Health and Prevent Bone Loss


Intervention from dental implants can also prevent further bone loss in your mouth and jaw. Missing teeth can lead to bone deterioration and can cause a “sunken face” appearance over time. Dental implants can stimulate your jawbone just like your other teeth, preventing that deterioration. They also protect healthy neighboring teeth and maintain proper spacing and bite.


## So, Will You Choose Dental Implants?


Not everyone is a candidate for dental implants. If you’re curious about what implants can offer, ask your dentist if you may qualify for them. Check in about what your dental insurance covers for dental implant procedures. When talking with your dentist, don’t be afraid to express potential concerns about the success of dental implants. Your dentist has likely walked patients through the same thing many times!

If you’re looking for implant dentistry in the Los Angeles area, look into Wilshire Oral Surgery. Wilshire offers a clear explanation of dental implant costs and other options for replacing missing teeth. Wilshire’s dental implant experts can reassure you of any concerns and help you take good care of your teeth and gums.
