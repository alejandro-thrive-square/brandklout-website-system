## Why Santa Monica Patients Choose Implants Over Other Options


A dental implant is not just a cosmetic decision. For many patients, it is the difference between preserving jawbone density long-term and experiencing progressive bone loss that limits future treatment options. When a tooth is lost, the bone that once supported it begins to resorb within months. An implant, which acts as an artificial root, stimulates the bone and halts that process in a way that bridges and dentures cannot.

For Santa Monica patients who value longevity in their healthcare decisions, an implant typically represents the best long-term investment. It does not involve reducing adjacent healthy teeth the way a bridge does, and it does not require adhesives or overnight removal the way a partial denture does. According to the National Library of Medicine, dental implants are one of the most well-documented tooth replacement options available, with outcomes established over decades of clinical research.

Preserves Bone

An implant stimulates the jawbone just like a natural root, preventing the bone resorption that begins within months of tooth loss.

No Impact on Adjacent Teeth

Unlike a traditional bridge, an implant does not require grinding down neighboring healthy teeth to serve as anchors.

Fixed in Place

An implant-supported crown functions like a natural tooth. It does not shift, click, or require removal for cleaning the way a partial denture does.

Long-Term Solution

With proper care, implants are designed to last decades. They are built to integrate permanently with the surrounding bone.


## Our Implant Process From Consultation to Crown


The first step is a consultation that includes a clinical evaluation and Cone Beam 3D imaging of the implant site. This imaging gives us a precise three-dimensional view of your bone volume, root proximity, and anatomy, which we use to plan placement before any surgery begins. We share the imaging findings with you and explain the recommended approach clearly before you commit to any treatment.

If your bone volume at the implant site is insufficient, a bone grafting procedure may be recommended prior to implant placement. Once adequate bone is present, the titanium implant is placed surgically under local anesthesia or IV sedation. After a healing period for osseointegration, your general dentist or prosthodontist completes the final crown, which we coordinate closely with your referring provider throughout.

Consultation + 3D Imaging

We assess bone volume, anatomy, and overall health. You receive a clear treatment plan before any procedure is scheduled.

Bone Graft (if needed)

If bone volume is inadequate, grafting rebuilds the site. Healing time depends on graft size and whether placement is simultaneous.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed surgically. Recovery for most patients is two to three days before returning to normal activity.

Crown Placement

After osseointegration, your final crown is placed. We provide post-operative care instructions and coordinate with your referring dentist.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Dental Implants near Santa Monica


How long is the drive from Santa Monica to your office?

Most patients from Santa Monica reach our office in 10 to 15 minutes heading east on Wilshire Boulevard or Olympic Boulevard. There is no freeway required. Our address is 12300 Wilshire Blvd #326, Los Angeles, CA 90025, which is near the corner of Wilshire and Bundy.

How many implants will I need?

This depends on how many teeth are missing and the condition of your remaining teeth and jawbone. A single missing tooth requires one implant. Multiple missing teeth may be replaced with individual implants or with an implant-supported bridge. We assess this during your consultation and make a recommendation based on your imaging and clinical findings.

Will I need a bone graft before my implant?

Not all patients do. A bone graft is needed when there is insufficient bone volume to securely support an implant. This is more common when a tooth has been missing for some time and bone resorption has occurred. We determine this through Cone Beam 3D imaging at your consultation.

Does Delta Dental cover dental implants in California?

Some Delta Dental PPO plans provide partial coverage for implant-related procedures, particularly when the implant replaces a tooth lost due to a covered condition. Coverage varies significantly by plan and employer. We contact your insurer before your procedure to verify exactly what is covered and what your estimated out-of-pocket cost will be.

Can I finance my implant treatment?

Yes. We work with CareCredit, Alphaeon, and Cherry financing, all of which offer payment plans designed for elective and surgical dental procedures. Many plans have low-interest or deferred-interest options depending on your creditworthiness. We can walk you through financing options at your consultation.

How long does recovery take after implant surgery?

Most patients return to normal daily activity within two to three days of the implant placement procedure. The longer healing process involves osseointegration, where the implant fuses with the surrounding bone, which typically takes three to six months. This phase is largely passive and does not affect your daily routine.
