## When Santa Monica Patients Need Their Wisdom Teeth Out


Third molars rarely announce themselves with a clear schedule. For some patients, the recommendation comes after a routine X-ray at their general dentist. For others, it is sudden pain at the back of the jaw, swelling near the gum line, or recurring discomfort that finally prompts the visit. Whatever the trigger, acting sooner rather than later typically results in a shorter, more straightforward procedure.

Wisdom teeth that are impacted, partially erupted, or growing at an angle create ongoing risks including infection, crowding of adjacent teeth, and cyst formation. The American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons notes that early removal, ideally before the mid-twenties when roots are still developing, leads to faster recovery and fewer complications. If your dentist has flagged your wisdom teeth, the right move is a consultation with a board-certified oral surgeon who can evaluate the imaging and recommend a clear plan.

Impacted Wisdom Teeth

Teeth fully trapped beneath the bone require surgical extraction. Left in place, they can cause pressure, cysts, and damage to adjacent roots.

Partial Eruption

A tooth that has broken through the gum partially creates a pocket where bacteria accumulate, leading to recurring infections called pericoronitis.

Crowding

Wisdom teeth growing at an angle can put lateral pressure on the second molars, contributing to shifting and crowding of adjacent teeth.

Proactive Removal

Some patients have no symptoms but opt for removal on their surgeon’s recommendation after imaging reveals a developing problem best addressed early.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Wisdom Teeth Removal near Santa Monica


How far is your office from Santa Monica?

We are approximately 10 to 15 minutes from most of Santa Monica heading east on Wilshire Boulevard or Olympic Boulevard. Our address is 12300 Wilshire Blvd #326, Los Angeles, CA 90025, just east of Bundy Drive. No freeway required.

Can I get my wisdom teeth removed and be back in Santa Monica the same day?

Yes. Most wisdom tooth procedures are completed in our office in under an hour. If you choose IV sedation, you will need a driver to take you home, and you should plan to rest for the remainder of the day. The drive back to Santa Monica is short. Most patients are comfortable enough to be up and moving around at home the same day.

Do all four wisdom teeth need to come out at once?

Not necessarily. While removing all four at once is the most common approach because it means a single recovery period, some patients remove only one or two teeth based on clinical need. We evaluate each tooth individually during your consultation and make a recommendation based on your imaging, symptoms, and goals.

What is pericoronitis, and should I come in right away?

Pericoronitis is an infection of the gum tissue surrounding a partially erupted wisdom tooth. Symptoms include pain, swelling, and sometimes difficulty opening the mouth fully. If you are experiencing these symptoms, you should contact us promptly. Active infection may need to be treated before surgery can be scheduled safely, depending on severity.

What imaging do you use to evaluate wisdom teeth?

We use in-house Cone Beam 3D imaging, which provides a three-dimensional view of your wisdom teeth, root length and curvature, proximity to the inferior alveolar nerve, and surrounding bone structure. This allows for more precise surgical planning than standard panoramic X-rays alone.

How long is recovery after wisdom teeth removal?

Most patients feel noticeably better by day three or four. Swelling and discomfort are most significant in the first 48 to 72 hours. Full tissue healing takes several weeks, but the acute recovery window is short for most patients. We provide complete post-operative care instructions and are available for follow-up questions throughout.
