## Sedation Options for Brentwood Patients Who Need Comfort During Oral Surgery


Dental anxiety is one of the most common reasons patients delay care, and for many people in Brentwood, that delay compounds into a more complex problem by the time they finally come in. If you have been putting off a necessary oral surgery procedure because of fear, discomfort, or a difficult past experience, sedation may be the factor that changes everything. At Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center, located at 12300 Wilshire Blvd #326 just minutes from Brentwood, IV sedation and general anesthesia are administered by our board-certified surgeons, not a separate provider, making the experience streamlined, safe, and cost-effective.

Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center offers the full range of sedation options for oral surgery procedures, from local anesthesia for simple cases to deep IV sedation and general anesthesia for complex or anxiety-driven situations.


## Types of Sedation We Offer


Not every procedure or patient requires the same level of sedation. Our surgeons evaluate your medical history, anxiety level, and procedure complexity to recommend the right option for you. We discuss this thoroughly at your consultation so there are no surprises on the day of surgery.

Local Anesthesia

Numbs the surgical site only. You remain fully awake and aware. Appropriate for straightforward extractions and minor procedures.

Oral Sedation

A prescription medication taken before your appointment that reduces anxiety. You remain conscious but significantly more relaxed throughout the procedure.

IV Sedation

Administered intravenously by our board-certified surgeons. You are deeply relaxed with no memory of the procedure. The most common choice for wisdom teeth and implant surgery.

General Anesthesia

Full unconsciousness for more complex surgical cases or patients with significant medical or behavioral factors. Administered in-office by our credentialed team.


## Why Surgeon-Administered Sedation Matters


Many patients do not realize that the person administering sedation varies widely between dental and surgical offices. At some practices, sedation is handled by a separate anesthesiologist brought in from outside, adding cost and coordination complexity. At Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center, IV sedation and general anesthesia are administered directly by Dr. Jonathan R. Shadi and Dr. Saman Vahedi. Both surgeons completed hospital-based oral and maxillofacial surgery residencies where surgical sedation was a core competency, and both hold the required anesthesia permits issued by the state of California.

This matters for Brentwood patients because it means you are never dealing with a separate scheduling chain, a second provider who does not know your case, or additional facility fees for sedation services. Everything is coordinated by the same team that performs your surgery.

## What to Expect Before, During, and After Sedation


Sedation requires advance preparation. You will need to follow specific pre-anesthesia instructions provided at your consultation, which typically include fasting for a set period before surgery and stopping certain medications. On the day of surgery, a responsible adult must drive you to and from our office and stay with you for the first few hours of recovery. Do not plan to drive, operate machinery, or make significant decisions for the remainder of the day after IV sedation.

Most patients wake from IV sedation feeling calm and comfortable. Some experience mild grogginess or nausea in the first hour, which typically resolves quickly. We provide complete post-operative care instructions before you leave and remain available for follow-up questions during your recovery. Our team can also walk you through what to expect over the phone if you have concerns before your appointment.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Sedation Dentistry near Brentwood


Will I be completely unconscious under IV sedation?

IV sedation produces a deeply relaxed state where most patients have no memory of the procedure, but it is technically not the same as general anesthesia, where you are fully unconscious. The distinction is clinical, not practical for most patients. If full unconsciousness is required for your case, general anesthesia is also available.

Can I request sedation even if my procedure is straightforward?

Yes. Dental anxiety is a valid medical consideration, and we take it seriously. If you have significant anxiety about oral surgery regardless of procedure complexity, we will discuss sedation options during your consultation and determine the safest and most appropriate level for your situation.

Who administers the sedation at your practice?

Sedation is administered directly by Dr. Shadi or Dr. Vahedi, both board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeons with California anesthesia permits and hospital-based residency training in surgical sedation. We do not use outside anesthesiologists for in-office procedures.

How long does IV sedation last after the procedure?

The sedative effects typically begin to wear off within one to two hours of your procedure ending, but residual drowsiness, impaired judgment, and slowed reflexes can persist for the remainder of the day. You should not drive, use machinery, or make important decisions until the following day.

Do I need to fast before IV sedation?

Yes. You will receive specific pre-anesthesia instructions at your consultation, which typically include fasting from solid food for a minimum period before surgery. Following these instructions exactly is important for your safety. We will walk you through all requirements well before your appointment date.

Is sedation covered by insurance?

Coverage depends on your specific plan and whether the sedation is deemed medically necessary. When sedation accompanies a covered surgical procedure such as impacted wisdom teeth removal, many PPO plans cover at least a portion. We verify your benefits before your appointment and provide a full cost estimate so there are no surprises.
