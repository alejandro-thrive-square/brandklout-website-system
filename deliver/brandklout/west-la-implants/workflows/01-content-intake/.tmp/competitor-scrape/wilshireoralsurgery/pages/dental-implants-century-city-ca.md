## What Makes Dental Implants the Right Choice for Century City Patients


Century City patients include executives, attorneys, entertainment industry professionals, and finance professionals who understand long-term value and tend to make healthcare decisions accordingly. A dental implant is the only tooth replacement option that addresses bone loss at the root level, functions exactly like a natural tooth, and requires no special maintenance beyond normal brushing and flossing. It does not require altering adjacent teeth the way a bridge does, and it does not shift or require adhesive the way a partial denture does.

Research published through the National Library of Medicine confirms dental implants as a well-documented, effective tooth replacement modality with outcomes established across decades of clinical use. For patients who value durability and minimizing the long-term management of their dental care, the implant remains the standard of care.

Root-Level Bone Stimulation

The titanium implant stimulates the jawbone just as a natural root would, halting the progressive bone resorption that begins within months of tooth loss.

Independent Structure

The implant stands alone without reducing or connecting to neighboring teeth. Adjacent healthy teeth remain completely untouched.

Permanent Solution

Designed to integrate with bone permanently. With proper care, an implant is built to last decades without the periodic replacement cycles of other restorations.

Natural Function

An implant-supported crown functions and feels like a natural tooth. There are no dietary restrictions, no removal for cleaning, and no adjustment period.


## From Consultation to Final Crown — Our Implant Process


Every implant case at Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center begins with a consultation that includes Cone Beam 3D imaging of the implant site. This gives us a precise, three-dimensional view of bone volume, width, root anatomy, and proximity to nerves and sinus structures before any surgical planning begins. The consultation results in a clear treatment plan with timeline and cost estimates before you commit to anything.

If bone volume at the implant site is insufficient, we may recommend bone grafting prior to or concurrent with implant placement. Once the site is ready, the implant is placed under local anesthesia or IV sedation, depending on your preference and case complexity. After osseointegration, your general dentist or prosthodontist places the final crown. We provide complete post-operative care instructions and coordinate with your referring provider throughout the process.

Consultation + 3D Imaging

Comprehensive bone and anatomy assessment. Treatment plan, timeline, and cost estimate provided before any procedure is scheduled.

Bone Graft if Needed

When bone volume requires augmentation, we perform grafting and allow time for healing before implant placement, or combine both procedures when clinically appropriate.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed under local anesthesia or IV sedation. Most patients return to work within two to three days.

Crown Placement

After full osseointegration, your restorative provider places the final crown. The implant is now a permanent part of your dental structure.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Dental Implants near Century City


How far is your office from Century City?

Our office is approximately two miles west of Century City on Wilshire Boulevard. The drive typically takes five to ten minutes without freeway involvement. We are located at 12300 Wilshire Blvd #326, Los Angeles, CA 90025, near the corner of Wilshire and Bundy Drive.

Is an implant better than a bridge?

For most patients, yes, primarily because an implant does not require reducing adjacent healthy teeth and because it preserves the jawbone at the site of tooth loss. A bridge is a fixed option but relies on the strength of the crowned teeth on either side. In the long run, implants tend to involve fewer downstream dental complications related to the replacement tooth.

How long will I be out of work after implant surgery?

Most patients return to office and desk work within two to three days of implant placement. Physical activity and heavy labor should be avoided for about a week. If you had IV sedation, plan not to drive or make significant decisions for the remainder of the day of surgery.

Do you place the implant and the crown?

We perform the surgical component, meaning implant placement and any associated bone grafting. The final crown is typically placed by your general dentist or prosthodontist after osseointegration is confirmed. We coordinate closely with your referring provider and communicate about the case throughout the process.

What sedation options are available for implant surgery?

Single implant placements are commonly performed under local anesthesia alone, which is comfortable for most patients. For patients who prefer deeper sedation, or for cases involving multiple implants or simultaneous bone grafting, IV sedation administered by our board-certified surgeons is available. We discuss the right option for your case during your consultation.

What if I waited too long and lost bone?

Bone loss is common when a tooth has been missing for some time, but it does not necessarily prevent implant placement. We evaluate bone volume using Cone Beam 3D imaging at your consultation. In many cases, bone grafting can restore sufficient volume for a stable implant. We assess this individually and give you honest guidance about what is possible given your current anatomy.
