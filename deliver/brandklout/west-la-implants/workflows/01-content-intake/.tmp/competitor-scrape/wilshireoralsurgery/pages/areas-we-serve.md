# Areas We Serve


Proudly serving West Los Angeles and surrounding communities

Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center is conveniently located on Wilshire Blvd in West Los Angeles, giving patients across the Westside easy access to board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeons. Whether you’re in Santa Monica, Brentwood, Century City, or just minutes away in Culver City, Mar Vista, or Palms — our team is ready to provide expert care for dental implants, wisdom teeth removal, bone grafting, and more.
