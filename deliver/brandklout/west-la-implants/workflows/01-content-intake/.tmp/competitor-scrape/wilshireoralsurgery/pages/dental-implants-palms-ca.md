## Understanding Your Implant Options Before You Commit


Many Palms patients arrive at the implant conversation after a tooth was extracted, a bridge failed, or a partial denture became difficult to live with. Understanding what an implant actually is, and how it compares to the alternatives, helps make the decision clearer. An implant is a titanium post placed into the jawbone that serves as an artificial root. Once it integrates with the bone through osseointegration, a crown is placed on top, restoring the tooth completely. It does not require modifying the adjacent teeth, does not need to be removed at night, and does not rely on adhesive or clasps.

The long-term advantage is bone preservation. According to the National Library of Medicine, the clinical evidence base for dental implants spans decades and consistently supports their durability and safety as a tooth replacement modality. For patients who want to solve the problem once rather than manage it repeatedly, the implant is the right solution.

Implant vs. Bridge

A bridge replaces the crown but requires reducing healthy adjacent teeth. An implant is independent, preserves neighbors, and prevents bone loss that a bridge does not address.

Implant vs. Partial Denture

A partial denture is removable and can shift or feel unstable. An implant is fixed, requires no adhesive, and integrates permanently into the bone.

Long-Term Cost

Bridges require eventual replacement. Implants are designed to last decades. The higher upfront cost often reflects a better long-term value compared to the cumulative cost of alternatives.

No Special Maintenance

An implant-supported crown is cleaned exactly like a natural tooth. Brush, floss, and visit your general dentist on a normal schedule — no additional tools or routines required.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Dental Implants near Palms


How much does a dental implant cost in the Palms and West LA area?

The cost depends on the number of implants, whether bone grafting is required, and what your insurance covers. We provide a full itemized estimate at your consultation after reviewing your imaging and verifying your benefits. We do not quote costs without evaluating your specific case, because the range is meaningful depending on your individual situation.

Do you offer payment plans for dental implants?

Yes. We work with CareCredit, Alphaeon, and Cherry, all of which offer installment plans designed for high-value dental and medical procedures. Promotional low-interest and deferred-interest options are often available depending on your creditworthiness. We walk you through the financing options at your consultation alongside your insurance estimate.

Is an implant consultation free?

Please contact our office directly to ask about consultation fees and what is included. Some consultations may include in-house imaging. We are happy to answer your questions over the phone before you schedule.

How long does osseointegration take?

Osseointegration, the process by which the implant fuses with your jawbone, typically takes three to six months. During this period, daily activity is largely unaffected after the initial post-surgical recovery of two to three days. You are not sidelined for months — the healing is largely passive and does not interfere with your normal routine.

Can I get an implant if my tooth has been missing for several years?

Yes, in many cases. Long-term tooth loss leads to bone resorption, which may require a bone grafting procedure before implant placement. We evaluate the extent of bone loss using Cone Beam 3D imaging and give you an honest assessment of what is needed and whether it is feasible at your consultation. Many patients who assumed they had waited too long are good candidates after grafting.

Will my regular dentist place the crown?

Yes. Our role is to perform the surgical phase, including implant placement and any bone grafting. Your general dentist or prosthodontist places the final crown after osseointegration is confirmed. We communicate with your provider throughout the process and provide them with complete documentation of the implant position and specifications for a smooth handoff.
