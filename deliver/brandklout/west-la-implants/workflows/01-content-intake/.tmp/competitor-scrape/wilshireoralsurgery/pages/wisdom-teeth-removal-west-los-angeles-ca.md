## How Wisdom Teeth Become a Problem in West LA Patients


Third molars are the last teeth to develop, usually between the late teens and mid-twenties. Because the modern jaw rarely provides adequate space for a full set of wisdom teeth, these teeth commonly become impacted, meaning they are prevented from erupting normally. Impaction leads to a range of clinical problems that worsen over time without intervention.

West LA’s younger professional and student population tends to delay this care until symptoms force the issue. A far better approach, supported by the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, is to evaluate wisdom teeth in the late teens or early twenties and remove them before root development is complete and surrounding bone becomes denser. Early removal leads to shorter procedures, faster recovery, and fewer complications.

Bone Impaction

The tooth is fully encased in bone and gum tissue. Surgical extraction requires removing bone to access and section the tooth.

Soft Tissue Impaction

The tooth has partially erupted through the bone but remains covered by gum tissue, creating a breeding ground for bacteria and infection.

Distal Angulation

The tooth grows at an angle tilted away from the second molar, which can damage the distal surface of that adjacent tooth over time.

Asymptomatic Impaction

No current symptoms, but imaging reveals positional or developmental factors that make removal advisable before complications develop.

How many days will I need off work for wisdom tooth removal?

Most patients take two to three days off and return to desk work by day four. Physical labor, heavy exercise, and anything that significantly raises blood pressure should be avoided for the first week. Swelling peaks at 48 to 72 hours and then steadily improves. If you work from home, some patients find they can return to light computer work by day two.

Do you accept Google or Hulu employee dental plans?

We work with all major PPO dental insurance carriers, which cover most employer-sponsored plans from West LA’s major employers. We verify your specific coverage and benefits before your procedure. Contact our office with your insurance information and we can confirm participation in your plan before you schedule.

What is the difference between a partially and fully impacted wisdom tooth?

A partially impacted tooth has broken through the overlying gum tissue to some degree but has not fully erupted into the mouth. A fully impacted tooth remains entirely within the bone or gum tissue with no visible presence in the mouth. Both types are removed surgically, though the approach varies based on position, angulation, and root anatomy.

What should I eat after wisdom tooth surgery?

For the first 24 hours, stick to soft foods and liquids that require minimal chewing, such as yogurt, smoothies, mashed potatoes, and soups at room temperature. Avoid hard, crunchy, or chewy foods for at least a week. Our post-operative care instructions include a detailed guide to diet and activity during recovery.
