## The Case for Implants in the West LA Market


West Los Angeles patients are accustomed to evaluating their healthcare options carefully. When it comes to tooth replacement, implants stand apart from bridges and removable partial dentures in ways that matter long-term. A titanium implant integrates with the jawbone through osseointegration, providing root-level support that stimulates bone and prevents the progressive resorption that follows tooth loss. A bridge avoids this by resting on adjacent teeth, which must be reduced to serve as anchors. A partial denture avoids bone contact altogether, leaving the underlying ridge to resorb over time.

For West LA patients who intend to keep their oral health at a high level for decades, the implant is typically the best long-term investment. According to the National Library of Medicine, implant dentistry is among the most well-documented tooth replacement modalities available, with a clinical track record built over more than four decades.

Single Implant

Replaces one tooth root-to-crown without altering adjacent teeth. The standard of care for isolated tooth loss in healthy bone.

Multiple Implants

Two or more implants placed at adjacent or distributed sites. Can support individual crowns or an implant-supported bridge.

Full-Arch Restoration

For patients missing most or all teeth in one arch, four to six implants support a complete, fixed prosthesis in a single surgical phase.

Ridge Augmentation

When bone volume has been lost prior to implant planning, ridge augmentation rebuilds the site to prepare for a stable, long-term implant.


## Technology That Changes the Outcome


Implant placement is not a procedure where guesswork serves anyone well. At Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center, we use Cone Beam 3D imaging to generate a precise three-dimensional map of each implant site before we plan a single incision. This technology shows bone density, width, height, root proximity to adjacent teeth, and the precise location of nerves and sinus structures that must be avoided.

The surgical plan is built around this data, not modified to fit it after the fact. For West LA patients who appreciate technology-forward healthcare, this is the planning standard you would expect. When bone grafting is necessary before placement, we perform that as well, often on the same visit using platelet-rich fibrin to support faster healing. Full post-operative care is covered by our detailed post-operative instructions.

Does my employer dental plan cover implants?

Coverage depends on your specific plan design. Many employer-sponsored PPO plans in West LA, particularly those from large tech, media, and healthcare employers, include some implant coverage when the procedure is medically indicated. We verify your benefits prior to any scheduled procedure and give you a clear cost estimate upfront.

What is osseointegration and how long does it take?

Osseointegration is the biological process by which the titanium implant fuses with the surrounding jawbone, creating a stable, permanent anchor for the crown. This process typically takes three to six months and is largely passive from the patient’s perspective. Daily activity is not significantly affected during this period.

What if I don’t have enough bone for an implant?

Insufficient bone is one of the most common reasons patients are told an implant is not possible at their current dentist. In many cases, a bone grafting procedure can rebuild the site adequately for implant placement. We evaluate bone volume using Cone Beam 3D imaging and discuss the options, including timing and whether grafting can be done simultaneously with implant placement.

Do you offer full-arch implant restoration?

Yes. For patients who are missing most or all of their teeth in one arch, full-arch restoration using four to six implants to support a fixed prosthesis is available. This is sometimes referred to as “teeth in a day” or All-on-X. We discuss candidacy, timeline, and costs thoroughly during your consultation.
