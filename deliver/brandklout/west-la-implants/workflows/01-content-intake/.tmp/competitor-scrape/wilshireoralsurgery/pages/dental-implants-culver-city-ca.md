## Why Culver City Patients Invest in Dental Implants


A dental implant is not just a cosmetic restoration. It is a root-level intervention that replaces the full biological function of a natural tooth, preserves the jawbone at the site of loss, and protects adjacent healthy teeth from the structural compromise that comes with traditional bridges. For Culver City’s entertainment and tech professionals, who often include dental care in their overall health investment strategy, the implant’s long-term durability and low maintenance profile are significant advantages.

Research through the National Library of Medicine documents dental implants as one of the most rigorously studied and clinically supported tooth replacement modalities in modern dentistry. The outcomes data built over more than four decades supports implants as the standard of care for single and multiple tooth replacement in healthy bone.

Bone Preservation

The implant provides root-level stimulation that prevents the progressive bone resorption that begins within months of tooth loss and continues under bridges and dentures.

Zero Impact on Adjacent Teeth

Implants are structurally independent. Unlike a bridge, the adjacent healthy teeth are not reduced or used as anchors, preserving their full structural integrity.

No Ongoing Maintenance Burden

An implant-supported crown is brushed and flossed exactly like a natural tooth. No special tools, no removal, no adhesive. It integrates seamlessly into a normal oral care routine.

Long-Term Value

Implants are designed for permanence. The upfront investment reflects a solution that avoids the periodic replacement and downstream dental complications of other restorations.


## Technology and Planning That Support Precision Outcomes


Implant outcomes are only as reliable as the planning that precedes them. At Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center, every implant case begins with Cone Beam 3D imaging that maps the bone volume, nerve location, sinus anatomy, and root positions at the implant site with millimeter-level precision. The surgical plan is built around this data before any procedure is scheduled, and we walk you through the findings clearly before you commit to anything.

When bone grafting is required prior to or concurrent with implant placement, we perform that procedure as well, sometimes with platelet-rich fibrin to support faster healing. IV sedation is available for the implant procedure and any associated surgical steps, administered by our board-certified surgeons in-office. After osseointegration is confirmed, your general dentist or prosthodontist places the final crown, and we coordinate with your referring provider throughout. Our post-operative care instructions guide each phase of recovery.

Consultation + Imaging

Cone Beam 3D imaging, bone assessment, and a complete treatment plan with timeline and cost estimate provided before any procedure is scheduled.

Grafting if Needed

When bone volume is insufficient, bone grafting builds the implant foundation. May be done separately or concurrent with placement.

Implant Placement

Placed under local anesthesia or IV sedation. Most Culver City patients return to their professional schedule within two to three days.

Final Crown

After osseointegration, your restorative dentist places the crown. The implant is permanently integrated and requires no special maintenance going forward.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Dental Implants near Culver City


What is the best route from Culver City to your office?

From central Culver City, Sepulveda Boulevard heading north to Wilshire Boulevard is the most direct route, typically 10 to 15 minutes without heavy traffic. National Boulevard to Bundy is another option. Our address is 12300 Wilshire Blvd #326, Los Angeles, CA 90025. Building parking is available.

Can I schedule around a production or studio schedule?

We accommodate professional schedules and understand that Culver City patients often work in industries with irregular or intensive timelines. Contact our office to discuss availability that fits your production or work calendar. Most implant consultations are under 90 minutes, and the surgical procedure itself typically requires only a single appointment.

How soon can I go back to work after implant placement?

Most patients return to desk or office work within two to three days of the implant procedure. If you had IV sedation, the day of surgery should be reserved for rest. Strenuous physical activity and anything that significantly raises blood pressure should be avoided for at least a week. Creative and production work that is sedentary can typically resume by day three.

Do you handle both the implant and the crown?

We perform the surgical phase — implant placement, any bone grafting, and related procedures. The final crown is placed by your general dentist or prosthodontist after osseointegration is confirmed. We communicate and coordinate closely with your restorative provider throughout the process so the handoff is seamless.

What happens during the osseointegration phase?

Osseointegration is the period during which bone cells grow into and around the titanium implant surface, creating a permanent biological bond. This typically takes three to six months. During this phase, the implant site is largely unobtrusive. You continue your normal activities with no significant restrictions after the initial post-surgical recovery period of two to three days.

What if I’ve had a tooth missing for years — is it too late for an implant?

Years of tooth loss often mean some bone resorption has occurred at the site, but this does not necessarily prevent implant placement. We evaluate bone volume using Cone Beam 3D imaging and determine whether bone grafting is needed to create an adequate foundation. Many patients who assumed it was too late are good candidates after a grafting procedure.
