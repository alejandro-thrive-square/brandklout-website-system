## What Makes a Dental Implant the Right Choice


A dental implant replaces a missing tooth at the root level, providing a stable titanium foundation that integrates with the surrounding jawbone through a process called osseointegration. Once integrated, a custom crown is placed on top, restoring the full appearance and function of a natural tooth. Unlike a bridge, an implant does not require reducing adjacent healthy teeth. Unlike a removable partial denture, an implant is fixed in place and does not shift during eating or speaking.

Patients from Brentwood seek implants for a range of reasons, including tooth loss from decay, fracture, or failed prior restorations, as well as planned extractions where the tooth cannot be saved. According to the National Library of Medicine, dental implants have become a standard treatment modality in dentistry with well-established long-term outcomes when placed by trained surgeons. Early consultation is important because bone preservation is optimal when an implant is placed shortly after tooth loss.

Single-Tooth Implant

Replaces one missing tooth with a titanium post and custom crown. No impact on adjacent healthy teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridge

Two implants support a fixed bridge to replace two or more consecutive missing teeth without a removable appliance.

Full-Arch Restoration

Four to six implants support a full arch of fixed teeth, replacing an entire upper or lower row in one surgical phase.

When bone volume is insufficient for implant placement, a grafting procedure rebuilds the ridge before or at the time of implant surgery.


## The Implant Process at Wilshire Oral Surgery and Implant Center


The implant process typically begins with a consultation and Cone Beam 3D imaging to assess bone volume, tooth position, and anatomy. Our surgeons use this precise three-dimensional imaging to plan placement angle, depth, and position before any surgery begins. This planning step reduces guesswork and allows for a more predictable surgical outcome.

If bone grafting is required due to atrophy or prior extraction socket loss, that procedure is typically completed first and allowed to heal before implant placement. In cases where bone volume is adequate, implant placement and grafting can sometimes occur simultaneously. After the implant integrates with the bone over a healing period, your general dentist or prosthodontist places the final crown. We coordinate closely with referring providers throughout this process.

Consultation and Imaging

We evaluate bone volume, tooth anatomy, and overall oral health using Cone Beam 3D imaging before recommending a surgical plan.

Bone Grafting (if needed)

If bone volume is insufficient, we perform bone grafting to create a stable foundation. Healing time varies by graft type and volume.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed under IV sedation or local anesthesia depending on the case. Most patients resume normal activity within a few days.

Final Restoration

After osseointegration, your referring dentist or our team completes the crown or prosthesis. We provide detailed post-operative care instructions throughout.


## IV Sedation Options for Implant Surgery


Implant surgery is performed under local anesthesia for most straightforward cases. For patients who prefer a deeper level of comfort, or for more complex procedures involving multiple implants or simultaneous bone grafting, IV sedation is available. Our board-certified surgeons administer sedation directly, which keeps the process streamlined and cost-effective compared to a separate hospital setting. Under IV sedation, you remain deeply relaxed throughout the procedure with no memory of the surgical experience. You will need a responsible adult to drive you home and stay with you for the first few hours after surgery.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Dental Implants near Brentwood


How far is Wilshire Oral Surgery from Brentwood?

Do I need a bone graft before getting an implant?

Not all patients require bone grafting. Whether a graft is needed depends on the volume and density of bone at the implant site, which we assess using Cone Beam 3D imaging during your consultation. If grafting is required, we discuss the timeline and options with you before any treatment begins.

Can I be sedated for implant surgery?

Yes. We offer IV sedation administered by our board-certified surgeons for patients who prefer a deeper level of comfort or for complex multi-implant cases. Most patients who receive IV sedation have no memory of the procedure. A responsible adult must drive you home and stay with you for the first few hours after surgery.

Does PPO dental insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage varies significantly by plan. Some PPO plans provide partial coverage for implant placement, particularly when the tooth was lost due to a covered condition such as trauma or infection. We verify your benefits before your appointment and provide a clear estimate of out-of-pocket costs before any procedure begins.

How long does implant recovery take?

Most patients return to normal activity within two to three days of implant placement. Osseointegration, the process by which the implant fuses with the bone, takes several months. We provide detailed post-operative care instructions and remain available for follow-up throughout the healing process.
