## The Right Solution for Long-Term Residents Who Think Ahead


Mar Vista homeowners and long-term residents tend to think in terms of durable, low-maintenance solutions, and that same logic applies to dental care. A dental implant is the only tooth replacement option that addresses bone loss at the root level. When a tooth is lost, the jawbone that supported it begins to resorb within months in the absence of mechanical stimulation. A bridge does not prevent this process. A partial denture does not either. An implant, which integrates with the bone through osseointegration, does.

For Mar Vista patients who want their oral health decisions to hold up over decades rather than require revision every few years, the implant is consistently the better investment. The National Library of Medicine documents dental implants as one of the most well-supported and widely studied options in restorative dentistry, with clinical outcomes data built over more than forty years of use.

Stops Bone Loss

The titanium implant stimulates the jawbone through daily function, halting the resorption that begins after tooth loss and progresses without root-level replacement.

No Modification of Neighbors

The implant stands on its own. Healthy adjacent teeth are never reduced or crowned as part of the restoration, unlike a traditional three-unit bridge.

Natural Daily Function

An implant-supported crown chews, brushes, and flossed like a natural tooth. No dietary restrictions, no removal, no adhesive.

Designed to Last

With proper care, the implant fixture itself is designed for permanent integration. It does not carry the replacement cycles that come with conventional restorations.


## Our Implant Process — From Mar Vista to Your Final Crown


The consultation starts with Cone Beam 3D imaging at our office, which generates a precise three-dimensional map of the implant site, including bone volume, width, nerve location, and adjacent root positions. We use this to build your surgical plan and share the findings clearly before anything is scheduled. You will have a complete picture of what is involved, how long it will take, and what it will cost before you commit to treatment.

If bone grafting is needed, we perform bone grafting in-office, sometimes combined with implant placement when the clinical situation allows. IV sedation is available for the surgical appointment and administered directly by our board-certified surgeons. After osseointegration, your restorative dentist places the final crown and we coordinate with them throughout. Our complete post-operative care instructions guide you through each recovery phase.

## Frequently Asked Questions — Dental Implants near Mar Vista


How do I get to your office from Mar Vista?

From Mar Vista, Centinela Avenue north to Pico Boulevard to Bundy Drive, or Barrington Avenue straight north, both connect to Wilshire Boulevard in about 10 to 15 minutes. Our address is 12300 Wilshire Blvd #326, Los Angeles, CA 90025, with parking available in the building.

Do I need a referral to see an oral surgeon for an implant?

No referral is required to schedule a consultation with us. Many patients come in directly after being told by their general dentist that they need an implant or after deciding on their own to explore options. If your dentist has already taken X-rays, you are welcome to bring them. We can also take our own in-house 3D imaging at your consultation.

What is the difference between a dental implant and a bridge?

A bridge replaces the visible tooth by spanning the gap and anchoring to the adjacent teeth on either side, which must be reduced and crowned in the process. An implant replaces the tooth at the root level without touching neighboring teeth. Implants also prevent jawbone resorption, which continues unaddressed under a bridge.

Are implants covered by my dental insurance?

Many PPO dental plans include partial coverage for implant-related procedures when medically indicated. Coverage varies significantly by plan and employer. We verify your specific benefits before scheduling and provide a full cost estimate so you know exactly what your plan covers and what you will pay out of pocket before committing to treatment.

Can I eat normally after the implant is placed?

In the first few days, soft foods are recommended to avoid stressing the surgical site. As healing progresses over the following weeks, you can gradually return to a normal diet. During the osseointegration phase, we recommend avoiding hard, crunchy, or very chewy foods directly on the implant site until the crown is placed and integration is confirmed.

Is IV sedation available for implant surgery at your office?

Yes. IV sedation is available for implant placement and is administered by our board-certified oral surgeons in-office. It is particularly common for patients placing multiple implants simultaneously or undergoing bone grafting at the same visit. A responsible adult must drive you home if sedation is used.
