# Dr. Igal Elyassi



## FOUNDER


Dr. Igal Elyassi graduated from University of Southern California School of Dentistry. Established Wilshire Smile Studio in 2002. He is the former president of the Los Angeles chapter of Alpha Omega International as well as an active member of the American Dental Association and California Dental Association.

Dr. Elyassi believes that dentistry is a partnership between patient and doctor; providing education on oral health to prevent dental problems, as well as clear explanations of treatment options. To offer the best possible patient care, he employs only the very best general and specialty dentists, assistants, hygienists, technicians, and office staff.

He also knows the importance of continuing education for himself and his staff at Wilshire Smile Studio. As a result, it is one of the most highly-rated smile makeover practices in Southern California; drawing patients from all over the world.

Dr. Elyassi loves to travel. He is a fan of the FC Barcelona soccer team and Los Angeles Laker. He favors sushi and Italian food, and says his favorite movie is “The Godfather”.
