# All-on-4 Implants


- Based in Los Angeles -

All-on-4 dental implants are a revolutionary type of dental implant that allows you to restore your smile with permanent teeth that can be cleaned just like your natural teeth. There is no need for denture adhesives, they do not rub or irritate your gums, they maintain your bite force, they allow you to enjoy your favorite foods, and they can prevent bone deterioration, which can help preserve your facial features. Our expert in Los Angeles can help you learn more about the process and decide if they are right for you.

Unlike traditional dental implants, All-on-4 implants require only four dental implants in each arch. Each arch is placed at an angle to maximize contact with bone tissue, and they can be customized to better fit your jaw and support your new teeth. They can save you time and money, and they are far more comfortable than traditional dentures. If our dentist determines that you are a candidate for the procedure, we will begin by placing the implants. This is usually an outpatient procedure and may be done right in our office.


## Work with an All-on-4 Dental Implants Specialist in Los Angeles


Once your healing is complete, which means that the All-on-4 implants have integrated with your jaw bone, our expert will place your permanent denture.

You can have just one arch replaced using All-on-4 implants, or we can use them for both arches. We will work with you to decide on the solution that best meets your needs and budget. Our expert will provide you with aftercare instructions so that you can keep your new smile clean, healthy, and attractive. Give us a call today to learn more or to schedule your appointment with our dentist in Los Angeles. We also serve surrounding cities including Beverly Hills, Santa Monica, and West Hollywood.
