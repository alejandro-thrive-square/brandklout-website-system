# Dental Implants


- Based in Los Angeles -

Each tooth in your mouth is held securely into place by ligaments, soft tissues, and alveolar bone. When a tooth is lost the soft tissues are no longer needed, and the bone resorbs this tissue which means that that the body absorbs it. Over time, this bone loss can be significant and can even threaten the health of your other teeth. Our Los Angeles dental implants expert can help.

Dental implants are titanium rods that act as roots for restoration teeth. This design helps combat the bone loss that occurs after tooth loss. Instead of resorbing, the bone instead grows into and around the implant which is then able to hold the new tooth securely into place. After dental implants have been placed, you will be able to eat, drink, talk, and smile without having to worry about your replacement tooth or teeth. Our expert in dental implants in Los Angeles can help you decide if you are a candidate for dental implants.


## Dental Implants Los Angeles


Our Los Angeles dental implants expert may recommend dental implants if you have lost a tooth due to trauma, tooth decay, gum disease, excessive wear, root canal failure, or congenital defects. Dental implants can also be used when you are missing several or all of your teeth. We will explore your options with you and explain the treatment process so that you can decide if dental implants are right for you.

The process can require several appointments scheduled over several months. At your first appointment, our expert in dental implants in Los Angeles will examine your teeth, gums, and underlying bones as we plan your treatment. The implants can be placed in an outpatient surgical procedure, and then the site will be allowed to heal. This process can take a few weeks or a few months. After the bone has fused with the implant an abutment will be attached, and your final restoration will be made.

The success rate for dental implants is extremely high, and they can last a lifetime. Most failures that do occur are a result of poor dental habits, including inadequate dental hygiene or smoking. Contact us today to learn more or to schedule your appointment with our Los Angeles dentist. We also serve surrounding cities including Santa Monica, West Hollywood, and Beverly Hills.
