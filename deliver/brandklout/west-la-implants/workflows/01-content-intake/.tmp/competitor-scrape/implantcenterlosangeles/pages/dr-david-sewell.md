# Dr. David Sewell



## COSMETIC DENTIST


Dr. David Sewell is a highly accomplished dentist with over 30 years of experience, specializing in cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, and full-mouth rehabilitation. A graduate of the prestigious Southern Illinois School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Sewell earned his degree with honors in 1992.

Following his graduation, Dr. Sewell established a thriving dental practice in Colorado, focusing on cosmetic dentistry and creating stunning smiles. To further expand his expertise, he attended the renowned LA Institute of Clinical Dentistry, where he mastered advanced techniques in implant placement, restoration, and full-mouth rehabilitation.

Dr. Sewell’s dedication to his craft is reflected in his prior membership in leading professional organizations, including the American Dental Association (ADA), California Dental Association (CDA), Metro Denver Dental Society (MDDS), American Academy Of Implant Dentistry, and the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD). Beyond his clinical achievements, Dr. Sewell has demonstrated a strong commitment to giving back, having led multiple dental mission trips to Central America, providing essential care to underserved communities.

Originally from the St. Louis area of southern Illinois, Dr. Sewell now calls southern California home. Outside of dentistry, he is a man of many talents and interests. Dr. Sewell enjoys acting, screenplay writing, and composing music. He has also pursued adventurous hobbies like Arabian horse endurance racing, hiking, and skiing during his time in Colorado.

Dr. Sewell is excited to bring his wealth of knowledge, skill, and passion for excellence to Wilshire Smile Studio, where he continues to help patients achieve their healthiest and most beautiful smiles.
