# Dental Implant Center


- Based in Los Angeles -

When you are missing one tooth or several teeth, your remaining teeth have to work harder. This can lead to an increased risk of damage or decay. Over time, these teeth may even shift position, which can cause misalignments and tilting or tooth loss. At our Los Angeles dental implants center, we can use dental implants to replace your missing teeth and prevent further damage or disease to your teeth.

Implants can be a great option for just about anyone who is missing one or more teeth. They preserve the long-term functionality of your smile and offer greater success rates than any other form of tooth restoration, including traditional bridges and dentures. Although dentures, removable partial dentures, and fixed dental bridges have long been used to restore missing teeth, they all have drawbacks and can increase your risk of suffering tooth decay, tooth loss, and bone loss. Dental implants can help you avoid these risks and keep your smile healthier longer. At our dental implants center in Los Angeles, we can help you learn more about the procedure and determine if you are a candidate.


## Los Angeles Dental Implants Center


Dental implants are designed with two parts. The implant itself is a biocompatible titanium screw-like rod that fits into the upper or lower jaw. The restoration part of the implant is a crown, bridge or denture that functions as the crown of the replaced tooth. Our Los Angeles dental implants center will work with you to create the best treatment plan for your needs. We will explore various options and make recommendations that are based on your goals, preferences and short- and long-term dental health needs.

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you are missing one, several or all of your teeth. The implant will usually be placed in one procedure and allowed to heal. During this time, the jawbone will fuse to the implant to create a durable, stable support for your restoration. At subsequent appointments, the restoration will be designed and then placed.

Our dental implants center in Los Angeles can help you learn more about your options. Contact us today to schedule your appointment.
