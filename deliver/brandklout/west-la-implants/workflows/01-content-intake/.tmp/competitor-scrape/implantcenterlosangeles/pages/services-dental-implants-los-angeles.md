# Los Angeles Dental Implants


- Based in Los Angeles -

Dental implants are one of the most innovative developments in dentistry. They have provided dentists with a way to not just replace missing teeth but also replace the roots of those teeth. While the goal of dentistry is to preserve natural teeth whenever possible, some teeth must be extracted to protect the rest of the teeth. Dental trauma can also result in the loss of a tooth.

Our Los Angeles dental implants expert can help you understand the pros and cons of the various tooth replacement options so that you can make the decision that is best for your health.

Bridges and dentures have long been used to restore missing teeth, but neither one of these is designed to restore the tooth root. Dental implants use titanium rods that are designed for maximum biocompatibility. This rod is implanted into the jawbone, which grows around and into it to create a sturdy foundation. The restoration is then attached to the implant. Restorations restore the actual crown of the tooth and can consist of porcelain crowns, bridges or dentures based on your specific needs. Our expert in dental implants in Los Angeles can help you choose the best restoration and implants solution.


## Los Angeles Dental Implants


You may be a candidate for dental implants if you are missing one or many teeth. Our Los Angeles dental implants expert is experienced with both placing and restoring implants. He may recommend them for patients who have adequate bone density and good general health. Good oral hygiene is also important since it promotes healing and reduces the risk of complications. Smokers, patients who have certain health problems or patients who are using certain types of medications may not be candidates for implants.

Dental implants offer many benefits to patients. They are a long-lasting restoration that provides better retention for dentures and does not require the alteration of healthy tooth tissue for bridges, and they look and feel just like your own natural teeth. Contact our dental office to learn more about dental implants or to schedule a consultation with our expert in dental implants in Los Angeles.
