# Dental Implant Dentist


- Based in Los Angeles -

If you have one or more missing teeth, you may be a candidate for dental implants. Dental implants are made of two parts: a titanium root device and a restoration that replaces the crown of the tooth. They are able to replace both the crown and the root of the tooth for the healthiest, most natural tooth replacement possible.

Our Los Angeles dental implants dentist can help you determine if you are a candidate for dental implants.

Dental implants may be a good choice for you if you have good dental and general health, do not smoke and do not take certain medications. The treatment is completed in stages and can take several months to complete. At your initial appointment, our dental implants dentist in Los Angeles will evaluate your smile. X-rays may be taken along with 3D scans that allow us to better identify underlying structures. We will also create models of your jaws to ensure an accurate restoration. The implants can then be placed immediately after extraction or delayed until the extraction site is healed depending on your needs.


## Los Angeles Dental Implants Dentist


After our Los Angeles dental implants dentist has placed the implant, your jawbone will begin to heal around it. This process can take three to four months. The bone anchors the implant into place so that it can be used to support the restoration, which may be a dental crown, dental bridges or dentures. We will work closely with you to ensure that your is the exact size, color top, and shape that it needs to be to match your other teeth.

Dental implants offer many benefits over traditional tooth replacement options. They can help keep the jawbone strong by continually stimulating the tissue, and they look, act and feel just like your own natural teeth. One of the biggest benefits of dental implants is that they are designed with longevity in mind: Most dental implants that are placed are still going strong after 15 years of service. If you are ready to learn more about your tooth replacement options, we can help. Give us a call today to schedule your consultation with our dental implants dentist in Los Angeles.
