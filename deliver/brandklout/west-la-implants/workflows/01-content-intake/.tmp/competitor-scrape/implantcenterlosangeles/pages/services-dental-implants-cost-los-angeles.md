# Dental Implant Cost


- Based in Los Angeles -

Dental implants have changed the way dentists replace missing teeth today. In the past, when a tooth was lost, patients might have been able to choose between dental bridges, which required damaging healthy teeth, and dentures, which could feel unstable and make patients feel self-conscious.

Now we have dental implants, which restore teeth naturally without affecting the health other teeth and securely for the most comfortable restoration possible. Our Los Angeles dental implants cost is often even more cost-effective over time for patients than other restoration options.

Unlike dental bridges, which can fail and may lead to the loss of other teeth, and dentures, which must be regularly adjusted, dental implants are permanent. Our dental implants cost in Los Angeles can include the price of the procedure, the implant and the restoration. Success rates for dental implants are close to 100 percent and provide patients with a lasting restoration that is both more comfortable and healthier than other restorations.

Most patients who have good general and dental health are candidates for dental implants, but some may need additional procedures to reduce the risk of complications.


## Los Angeles Dental Implants Center


If you are a candidate for dental implants, your first concern may be about our Los Angeles dental implants cost. We can provide you with personalized information and recommendations about your treatment options at your consultation. If you are missing one tooth, we can use an implant-supported crown to replace it.

If you are missing several or all of your teeth, we may recommend several strategically placed implants that are used to support a bridge or denture. Implants are easy to maintain and require the same good dental hygiene that all your teeth need.

Brush, floss and visit us every six months to keep your smile on track and to ensure that your dental implants stay in great shape.

Our dental implants cost in Los Angeles can vary based on whether you need any preparation before your procedure, such as bone or tissue grafts, the number of implants and the type of restoration.

Contact our Los Angeles dental implants dentist today to learn more or to schedule your appointment.
