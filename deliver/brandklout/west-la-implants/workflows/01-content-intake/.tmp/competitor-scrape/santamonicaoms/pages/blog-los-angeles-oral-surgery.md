# Los Angeles Oral Surgery at Santa Monica OMS


When it comes to smiles ready for the spotlight, Santa Monica Center for Oral Surgery & Dental Implants (Santa Monica OMS) has quietly set the stage behind some of Los Angeles’s most radiant and camera-ready smiles for nearly 50 years. At Santa Monica OMS, oral surgery transcends routine care, it's an exceptional, personalized experience tailored for patients who expect superior outcomes. Guided by board-certified surgeons, advanced technology, and concierge-level care, Santa Monica OMS invites you into an elite circle of patients who trust their smiles to the best in Los Angeles oral surgery. Discover the unparalleled level of skill, precision, and comfort awaiting you at this premier practice.


## Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery in Los Angeles


Santa Monica OMS offers a complete range of oral and maxillofacial procedures under one roof, including dental implants, wisdom-tooth extraction, corrective jaw surgery, bone grafting, sinus lifts, and facial trauma treatments. The state-of-the-art facility features:

Advanced in-house CBCT imaging for detailed 3-D planning

Operating suites accredited to stringent hospital standards

Comprehensive anesthesia options, including local anesthesia, IV sedation, and general anesthesia


## Meet the Surgical Team


Santa Monica OMS’s exceptional reputation stems from its highly credentialed surgical team. Each surgeon holds dual degrees in dentistry (DDS or DMD) and medicine (MD) and is board-certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery.


### Dr. Howard H. Park, DMD, MD


Specializing in dental implants, advanced bone grafting, and "teeth-in-a-day" full-arch restorations. Dr. Park is board-certified and serves on the faculty at UCLA, bringing extensive experience in innovative implant techniques.


### Dr. Richard H. Ting, DDS, MD


Expert in corrective jaw surgery and facial-trauma reconstruction, available for emergency care through local hospitals. Dr. Ting is the Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery department chair at Providence Saint John's Health Center and an anesthesia examiner for the Dental Board of California.


### Dr. Gil B. Ivry, DDS, MD


Focused on TMJ disorders, nerve reconstruction, and prioritizing patient comfort during complex procedures. Dr. Ivry also specializes in treating complex oral pathologies and facial pain syndromes.


### Dr. Jeffrey M. Tanner, DDS, MD


Renowned for minimally invasive wisdom-tooth extractions and ensuring comfortable, smooth recoveries. Dr. Tanner frequently lectures on pain management and is recognized for his patient-focused approach.


### Dr. Derek L. Vajda, DDS, MD


Experienced in managing pathology, complex extractions, and salvage procedures for dental implants. Dr. Vajda also specializes in reconstructive solutions following implant complications, prioritizing long-term dental health.


### Dr. Harry Schwartz, DMD, MD, FACS


Board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon with broad expertise in facial reconstruction, implant dentistry, and surgical treatments for oral pathologies. Dr. Schwartz is recognized as a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons, emphasizing excellence in patient care and surgical outcomes.


## Santa Monica’s Best in Oral Surgery



### Dental Implants and Full-Arch Solutions


The practice offers individual implants, bridges, and innovative All-on-Four® full-arch restorations, utilizing precise guided-surgery techniques for efficiency and accuracy. Comprehensive treatment planning ensures predictable outcomes and tailored solutions for every patient’s unique dental needs.


### Wisdom-Tooth Extraction Under IV Sedation


Impacted or challenging wisdom teeth are comfortably removed in the practice's dedicated surgical suites, ensuring rapid recovery and minimal discomfort. IV sedation provides stress-free procedures, significantly reducing patient anxiety and enhancing overall comfort during surgery.


### Corrective Jaw (Orthognathic) Surgery


The surgical team works alongside orthodontists to treat patients with bite discrepancies, facial asymmetry, and airway concerns, improving both function and aesthetics. Detailed virtual planning and precise surgical execution optimize results and minimize recovery time.


### Sinus-Lift and Bone-Grafting Procedures


Advanced techniques are used to reinforce bone in preparation for implants, ensuring long-lasting stability and successful dental implant placement. Procedures leverage state-of-the-art materials and minimally invasive approaches to maximize patient comfort and healing.


### Comprehensive Anesthesia Care


Each surgeon is fully certified to administer all levels of anesthesia, ensuring patient safety, comfort, and peace of mind throughout every procedure. Continuous monitoring and customized sedation plans guarantee optimal patient relaxation and enhanced surgical experiences.


### Emergency Facial-Trauma & TMJ Management


Santa Monica OMS surgeons provide rapid response to facial trauma and TMJ emergencies, partnering with local hospitals to deliver timely, effective care. Their experience in acute interventions ensures optimal recovery outcomes, restoring patients’ health, comfort, and functionality swiftly.

## Frequently Asked Questions



## Q: How long is recovery after wisdom-teeth removal?


A: Most patients resume normal activities within three to four days, though recovery varies based on individual healing and procedure specifics. Swelling and discomfort typically subside significantly within a week.


## Q: Can you get a full set of implants in one day?


A: Yes, in select cases with adequate bone structure, Santa Monica OMS offers advanced full-arch implant solutions such as "teeth-in-a-day." Your surgeon will evaluate your candidacy during the consultation using CBCT imaging.


## Q: Do you accept PPO insurance?


A: Yes. The office assists with insurance pre-authorization and clearly outlines any expected out-of-pocket costs upfront.


## Q: What financing is available?


A: Flexible financing options, including interest-free promotional periods through CareCredit®, are available to help manage the cost of treatment.


## Q: What should I expect during my first consultation?


A: Your initial consultation involves a thorough examination, CBCT imaging if necessary, and a detailed discussion of treatment options, costs, and expected outcomes. This visit is designed to ensure clarity and comfort about your treatment plan.


## Q: Is sedation dentistry safe?


A: Absolutely. Our team includes board-certified surgeons trained in advanced anesthesia techniques. Continuous monitoring ensures your safety and comfort throughout any sedation procedure.
