### Training and Education


Residency:

University of California, Los Angeles

Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery

Medical School:

Doctorate of Medicine

Dental School:

University of Southern California

Doctor of Dental Surgery

Dr. Ting practices the full scope of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. His practice predominantly consists of reconstructive dental implant cases and complex bone grafting procedures. He also has extensive experience in pathology and reconstruction in the Maxillofacial region.

Dr. Ting is the department chair of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at Providence Saint John’s Health Center. Additionally, he is a Lecturer at UCLA’s department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. He serves as an examiner for General Anesthesia for the Dental Board of California.

Dr. Ting is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. He maintains membership in multiple professional organizations including American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, American Medical Association, and the California Dental Association.

He is a Los Angeles native, and attended dental school at the University of Southern California, graduating with honors. After receiving his doctorate in dentistry, he continued his education at the University of California, Los Angeles earning his Medical degree. He also completed his residency training in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery which included training in General surgery at UCLA.
