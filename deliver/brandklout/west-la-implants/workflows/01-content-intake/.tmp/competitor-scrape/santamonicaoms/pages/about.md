Visit our oral surgery practice in mid-city Santa Monica. With 50 years of experience, we specialize in various oral health treatments, from reconstructive to orthognathic surgery.

We are proud to serve residents from Santa Monica, Pacific Palisades, Malibu, Brentwood, Westwood, Marina Del Rey, Playa Del Rey, Playa Vista, Westchester, Culver City, West Los Angeles, Venice, Century City and the San Fernando Valley.

We will schedule your appointment as promptly as possible. If you have emergent pain or discomfort, we will do our best to see you that day.

We make every effort to stay on schedule and minimize your wait time. However, Santa Monica Center for Oral Surgery and Dental Implants provides many types of surgical services and various circumstances may lengthen the time allocated for a procedure. Emergency cases may also arise and cause delays. We appreciate your patience and understanding.
