# Beverly Hills Oral Surgery by Santa Monica OMS: Your Path to a Confident Smile


Facing oral surgery can feel like a big deal—maybe you’re nervous about pain or just want to smile without thinking twice. At Santa Monica Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (OMS), we’re here to take the stress out of it. Our Center for Oral Surgery & Dental Implants is all about making you feel comfortable while creating a smile you’re proud of. Our Beverly Hills oral & facial surgeons are experts in tooth implants in, wisdom tooth removal, and more, working closely with you to build a plan that feels right. Let’s explore how we can help you shine.


## Services That Put You First


Surgery isn’t just about fixing teeth—it’s about getting back to being you. We focus on what matters to you, offering procedures that blend skill with care:

Wisdom Teeth Removal: We keep it quick and gentle, so you’re back to your routine with minimal discomfort.

Jaw Surgery: Struggling with a misaligned bite or jaw pain? We realign things to help you eat, talk, and feel better.

TMJ Relief: Jaw pain or clicking driving you nuts? Our treatments ease the ache and restore smooth movement.

Facial Injury Care: If an accident’s left you hurt, we repair facial injuries with precision and compassion.

Reconstructive Surgery: Need to rebuild your jaw or face? We restore function and boost your confidence.

Custom Plans: We listen to your needs and create a plan just for you, no one-size-fits-all solutions.

With tools like 3D imaging, we plan every step to ensure you’re at ease and the results are spot-on


## Dental Implants: A Smile That Feels Like You


Missing a tooth or tired of loose dentures? Teeth implants can change everything. These are sturdy titanium posts we place in your jaw to hold new teeth that look and act like the real thing. Here’s why they’re so popular:

Built to Last: With a little care, implants can stay with you forever.

Look Natural: They blend in so well, you’ll forget they’re not your original teeth.

Work Like Teeth: Enjoy your favorite foods, chat freely, and laugh without worry.

Our Beverly Hills oral & facial surgeons use precise techniques, like computer-guided placement, to get it perfect. If your jawbone needs extra support, we offer bone grafting to make implants possible. Whether it’s one tooth or a whole set, we’re here to help you love your smile again (Dental Implants).


## Our Surgeons: Skilled Hands, Caring Hearts


Our surgeons aren’t just experts—they’re people who genuinely care about making your experience positive. Here’s who you’ll meet at our office:

Dr. Howard H. Park, DMD, MD: Dr. Park brings over 26 years of experience, with a DMD from Harvard School of Dental Medicine and an MD from UCLA, where he also completed his oral surgery residency. He teaches at UCLA School of Dentistry and specializes in implants, wisdom teeth, and jaw surgery, known for his steady hands and kind approach.

Dr. Richard H. Ting, DDS, MD: With 18 years in the field, Dr. Ting earned his DDS and MD at UCLA, completing his residency there too. He’s a pro at implants and jaw fixes, always taking time to explain things clearly and keep you comfortable.

Dr. Gil B. Ivry, DDS, MD: Originally from Russia, Dr. Ivry moved to the U.S. in 1992 and trained at UCLA for his DDS, MD, and residency. With over 11 years of experience, he tackles complex cases like reconstructive surgery and facial trauma with a warm, patient-focused touch.

Dr. Jeffrey M. Tanner, DDS, MD: Inspired by his father’s dental practice, Dr. Tanner got his DDS from the University of Missouri and trained at UCLA. With 9 years of experience, he’s meticulous about implants and wisdom teeth, putting your comfort first.

Dr. Derek Vajda, DDS, MD: Dr. Vajda brings extensive experience to Santa Monica OMS, with a DDS earned with highest distinction from Indiana University School of Dentistry and an MD from UCLA, where he completed his oral and maxillofacial surgery residency. A diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, he serves on the medical staff at St. John’s Hospital and UCLA, handling facial trauma calls. Specializing in orthognathic surgery, he practices the full scope of OMFS and is deeply dedicated to the Santa Monica community

Each surgeon is board-certified and active in groups like the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, staying sharp on the latest techniques (Surgeons).
