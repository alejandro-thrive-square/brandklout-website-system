##### What Happens Next?


After you complete the form, we will contact you to schedule your appointment via your preferred method. If we can't meet your exact request, we will reach out to arrange a convenient time. We will do our best to accommodate your schedule!


##### Emergencies


If you are experiencing pain or an emergency, we will do our best to see you the same day. We strive to stay on schedule to minimize waiting, but sometimes procedures take longer or emergencies cause delays. We appreciate your understanding and patience.


##### Cancellation Policy


We set aside dedicated time in our office for your dental appointment. If you find it necessary to cancel, please provide 24-hour advance notice.
