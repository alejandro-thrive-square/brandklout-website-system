# Why Teeth in a Day Should be Your First Choice for Dental Implants


Research shows that over 97% of dental implants stand the test of time - even after 22 years! If you're interested in this dental procedure, you're making the right move for your overall oral health.

In particular, you should consider Teeth in a Day as your first choice for dental implants. That's right - you can get a full arch treatment for your teeth in under 24 hours. This procedure is quick, convenient, and permanent.

If you're still not sure, that's okay. This article goes into detail about the benefits of these implants compared to others. Continue reading to see why they'd be ideal for you.


## What Is "Teeth in a Day"?


If you're looking for a way to restore a full arch of teeth, or even just a few, look no further. You can get this taken care of with a single visit to your reliable dentist. That's why it's called "Teeth in a Day."

You'll leave the dentist's office with a new set of teeth that look and feel like your natural teeth. You won't have to worry about the stress of removable dentures or multiple visits to get this done. Learn more here about these groundbreaking dental implants.


## How It Works


First, your dentist will perform a thorough examination of your mouth. They'll take digital impressions of your teeth and jaw. This information will be able to help create a custom treatment plan for you.

On the day of the procedure, you'll receive a local anesthetic to numb the area. Your dentist will then place the implants in your jawbone, which will serve as the foundation for your new teeth.

Next, your dentist will attach a temporary set of teeth to the implants. So, you'll leave the office with a complete set of functional teeth.

Over the next few months, your implants will fuse with your jawbone. This creates a strong and stable foundation for your permanent teeth.

Once the healing process is complete, you'll return to your dentist's office. At this point, you'll have your temporary teeth replaced with your final set of teeth.


## Advantages of These Implants


If you're considering Teeth in a Day treatment, you're probably wondering about the patient experience. Our goal is to make the treatment process easier and more comfortable for you.

Compared to traditional approaches, Teeth in a Day offers several advantages. There are many benefits of implants compared to removable dentures, for one thing.

Further, with traditional implant treatment, you may need six or more implants. Plus, it can take up to 18 months to complete the treatment.

You may also have to wear temporary dentures during this time. This can be uncomfortable and inconvenient.

With Teeth in a Day, you can avoid all of that. You only need four to six implants, and the entire treatment can be completed in just one day.

Another major advantage is that it's less invasive than traditional implants. With traditional treatment, you may need multiple surgeries and a longer recovery period. This can be uncomfortable and disruptive to your daily life.

Another benefit of Teeth in a Day is that it has a high success rate and long-term outcomes . With proper care, your Teeth in a Day implants can last for many years. This will assuredly provide you with a beautiful and functional smile for a long time to come.


## Who Can Benefit


Are you wondering if you're a suitable candidate for Teeth in a Day treatment? Let's discuss the factors that determine candidacy for this innovative treatment.

First, you should have good overall health. You shouldn't have any underlying medical conditions that could interfere with the healing process.

You should also have enough jawbone density to support the implants. If you don't, your dentist may recommend bone grafting prior to the procedure.

Other factors that can impact your candidacy for Teeth in a Day include your age, smoking status, and oral hygiene habits. If you smoke, your dentist may recommend that you quit before the procedure. Smoking can interfere with the healing process.

You should also remember that maintaining proper dental hygiene is essential for the success of the procedure. Talk with your dentist about any other concerns you may have about whether this oral surgery is ideal for you.


## The Role of Technology


A large part of this innovative implant solution is thanks to technology. As these tools continue to advance, so does dentistry as a whole, as you'll see here.

Before the procedure, your dentist will use advanced imaging techniques. This involves cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT). The purpose here is to create a detailed 3D model of your mouth.

This allows your dentist to visualize the placement of the implants. That way, they can ensure that they're placed in the optimal position for stability and functionality.

During the procedure, computer-guided surgery places the implants with precision and accuracy. This technology even allows your dentist to use a computer-guided drill.

Doing so can create precise channels in your jawbone for the implants. The computer results in a more precise and accurate placement.


## After the Procedure


After the procedure, you may experience some discomfort and swelling for a few days. Your dentist will provide you with detailed instructions for pain management.

This post-operative care is crucial to ensure a smooth, comfortable recovery. Be sure to follow these instructions carefully, and ask your dentist if you have any additional questions.

You'll need to follow a soft-food diet for a few weeks after the procedure, too. Avoid any hard or crunchy foods that could damage the temporary bridge.

You'll also need to keep the area clean by brushing and flossing regularly. Lastly, continue visiting your dentist for regular check-ups and cleanings throughout the future.
