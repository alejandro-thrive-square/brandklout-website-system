# Wisdom Teeth Removal Recovery: Essential Post-Operative Care Guide


Wisdom teeth extraction is a common oral surgery procedure, typically performed between ages 17-25. At Santa Monica Center for Oral Surgery & Dental Implants, we understand that proper post-operative care is crucial for a smooth recovery. This guide will walk you through essential steps to ensure optimal healing after your wisdom teeth removal.


## Understanding the Recovery Process


Recovery from wisdom teeth extraction usually takes a few days to a week. Most patients can return to normal activities within a week, but following proper care instructions is vital for preventing complications and ensuring comfort.


### What to Expect Immediately After Surgery


Mild swelling and discomfort are normal

Some bleeding may occur for the first 24 hours

You may experience limited jaw opening


## Essential Post-Operative Care Tips



### 1. Rest and Activity Limitation


Rest for the remainder of the day after surgery

Avoid strenuous activities for at least 2-3 days

Gradually return to normal activities as you feel comfortable


### 2. Managing Swelling and Discomfort


Apply ice packs to the cheeks in 20-minute intervals for the first 24 hours

After 24 hours, switch to warm compresses to promote healing

Take prescribed pain medication as directed by your oral surgeon


### 3. Proper Oral Hygiene


Avoid brushing the surgical area for the first 24 hours

After 24 hours, gently rinse with warm salt water 2-3 times daily

Continue brushing other teeth normally, being careful around the extraction sites


### 4. Diet and Nutrition


Stick to soft foods for the first few days (e.g., yogurt, smoothies, mashed potatoes)

Avoid hot liquids and alcoholic beverages for at least 24 hours

Do not use straws for at least a week to prevent dry socket


### 5. Avoiding Complications


Don’t smoke for at least 72 hours after surgery

Avoid touching the surgical area with your tongue or fingers

If stitches were used, they typically dissolve on their own within 7-10 days


## When to Contact Your Oral Surgeon


While some discomfort is normal, contact Santa Monica Center for Oral Surgery & Dental Implants if you experience:

Excessive bleeding that doesn’t subside

Severe pain not relieved by prescribed medication

Signs of infection (fever, excessive swelling, pus)

Persistent numbness or tingling


## FAQs About Wisdom Teeth Removal Recovery


Q: How long does it take for the extraction site to fully heal?

A: Complete healing typically takes 3-4 weeks, although the initial recovery is much shorter.

Q: When can I resume exercise after wisdom teeth removal?

A: Light exercise can usually be resumed after 3-4 days, but avoid strenuous activities for at least a week.

Q: Is it normal to have bad breath after wisdom teeth extraction?

A: Some bad breath is normal for a few days post-surgery. Proper oral hygiene and salt water rinses can help.

Q: How soon can I eat solid foods after the surgery?

A: Most patients can return to a normal diet within a week, but start with soft foods and gradually introduce solids as comfort allows.
