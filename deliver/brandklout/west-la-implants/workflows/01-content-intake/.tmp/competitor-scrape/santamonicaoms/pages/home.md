Incredibly positive experience! A day after 3 extractions- no pain, no bleeding and very minimal swelling. Dr Tanner was highly professional, very engaging and most importantly he clearly does excellent work( he came highly referred)! Suzy Richman

Dr Ting explained everything he was doing and always made sure I was feeling okay. He is gentle and kind with a great “bedside manner”. Also the staff working with him was competent and kind. A well run team. I have had LOTS of oral surgery and Dr Ting and the Center are among the best. Have been here twice for tooth/implant extractions and bone grafts and will be back for implants. A bit pricey but worth it.

##### P

I am extremely fortunate to have been referred to Dr. Richard Ting. Not only is he gentle, patient and kind, he is a very skillful surgeon. I've had two surgeries, both of which went extremely well. ##### J


Jessica Shusko

Dr. Ting and staff were incredible. I was referred to Dr. Ting by my dentist to have a wisdom tooth removed. Dr. Ting was incredibly thorough during the consultation and his entire staff was friendly, thoughtful and mirrored Dr. Tings thoroughness. I hardly had any bleeding or swelling and didn’t need any pain medication after. I can not express enough how painless and smooth this process was.
