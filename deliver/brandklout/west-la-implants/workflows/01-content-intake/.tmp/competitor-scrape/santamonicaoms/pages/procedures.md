# Procedures


We provide comprehensive oral and maxillofacial surgery, including jaw correction, wisdom tooth removal, facial pain and injury treatment, TMJ disorder care, dental implants, and bone grafting.


## Dental Implants


This procedure not only restores the appearance of natural teeth but also improves functionality, allowing patients to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Learn More


## All-on-Four Dental Implants


The All-on-Four technique provides a full set of teeth using just four strategically placed implants in the upper or lower jaw.


## Wisdom Teeth Removal


Removal of these teeth, typically performed under sedation, helps prevent complications such as impacted teeth or damage to adjacent teeth.


## Facial Trauma


Facial trauma refers to injuries involving the face and jaws, including fractures, lacerations, and damage to soft tissues.


## Sedation Dentistry


Sedation dentistry involves the use of sedative medications to help patients relax and remain comfortable during dental procedures.


## Sinus Lift


A sinus lift is a specialized bone grafting procedure that augments the bone in the upper jaw, specifically in the area of the molars and premolars.


## Teeth-In-One-Hour™


Teeth-In-One-Hour™ is a revolutionary procedure that allows patients to receive dental implants and fully functioning teeth in just one hour.


## Orthognathic (jaw) Surgery


Corrects jaw misalignment, improving function, bite, and facial balance. This procedure is ideal for those with issues in chewing, speaking, or breathing, and significantly enhances functionality and aesthetics.
