###### Post-Op Instructions


We understand that your time is valuable. We kindly ask that all new patients or patients we have not seen in 2 years, complete our secure online Patient Registration Form prior to your appointment with us. You will also be asked to review and acknowledge our Privacy Practices and Financial Policy.

After you have completed the form and reviewed our policies, please make sure to press the Submit button at the bottom to automatically send us your information.

The security and privacy of your personal data is one of our primary concerns and we have taken every precaution to protect it.
