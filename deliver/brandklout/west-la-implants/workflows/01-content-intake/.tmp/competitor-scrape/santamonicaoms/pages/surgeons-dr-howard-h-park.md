### Training and Education


Residency:

University of California, Los Angeles Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery

Medical School:

University of California, Los Angeles Doctorate of Medicine

Dental School:

Harvard School of Dental Medicine Doctor of Dental Medicine

Dr. Park graduated with a Bachelors of Science degree in Information and Computer Science from the University of California, Irvine in 1987. He continued his education in dentistry at Harvard School of Dental Medicine. After receiving his Doctorate from Harvard, he began his residency training in oral and maxillofacial surgery at the University of California, Los Angeles during which he received his Medical Degree in 1995.

Dr. Park has been in private practice in Santa Monica and a member of the Medical Staff at Providence Saint John’s Health Center since 1999. He is also a member of the Medical Staff at UCLA, Ronald Reagan and on the teaching faculty at UCLA School of Dentistry in the Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. Dr. Park maintains membership in several professional organizations including the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, American Medical Association, and the Academy of Osseointegration. He is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and practices the full scope of oral and maxillofacial surgery with special interest and expertise in dental implants.
