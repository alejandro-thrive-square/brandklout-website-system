# Preparing for Your Oral Surgery: Essential Pre-Operative and Post-Operative Care Tips


Oral surgery is an important step toward improved oral health, but its success hinges on thorough preparation and diligent recovery care. At Santa Monica OMS, we blend advanced technology with personalized care to ensure every patient experiences a safe procedure and a smooth, comfortable recovery. Below are key tips to help you prepare for your surgery and maintain optimal healing afterward.


## Pre-Operative Care: Laying the Foundation


Comprehensive Consultation: Meet with our board‑certified oral surgeons to discuss your medical history, current medications, and any concerns. This detailed evaluation allows us to create a customized treatment plan that minimizes risks.

Personalized Treatment Planning: We develop a plan tailored to your unique dental structure and health needs, ensuring that every aspect of your procedure—from anesthesia to surgical technique—is optimized for you.

Medication Management: Follow specific instructions regarding medication adjustments. For example, if you’re taking blood thinners, our team will advise you on temporary discontinuation to reduce bleeding risks.

Fasting and Preparation Guidelines: Adhere strictly to fasting instructions (usually an eight‑hour fast) to prevent complications related to anesthesia. Preparing your home in advance with soft foods and a comfortable recovery area is also recommended.

Before Intravenous Anesthesia Sedation: You may not have anything to eat or drink (including water) for eight hours before the appointment. Click here for the full list of advanced instructions before intravenous anesthesia sedation.

Logistics and Transportation: Arrange for a trusted companion to drive you home post-surgery, as the effects of anesthesia can impair your ability to drive safely.


## Post-Operative Care: Promoting a Smooth Recovery


Managing Discomfort and Bleeding: It’s normal to experience minor bleeding and discomfort after oral surgery. Follow your surgeon’s post-operative care instructions for pain management and gently bite on gauze to control bleeding. Use ice packs intermittently (20 minutes on, 10 minutes off) during the first 48 hours to reduce swelling.

Maintaining Oral Hygiene: Continue to care for your mouth by gently rinsing with a warm saltwater solution (after the first 24 hours) while avoiding the surgical site. This helps keep the area clean and promotes healing.

Dietary Adjustments: Start with clear liquids and soft foods, such as soups and mashed potatoes. Gradually reintroduce solid foods as healing progresses, but avoid hot, spicy, or crunchy foods that could irritate the surgical area.

Activity and Rest: Rest is critical post-surgery. Avoid strenuous activities for several days to prevent complications like increased bleeding or swelling. Follow our specific guidelines regarding when it’s safe to resume your normal routine.

Follow-Up Appointments: Schedule follow-up visits with our team to monitor your recovery, address any concerns, and adjust your care plan if necessary. Continuous support is essential for ensuring a long-lasting, successful outcome.


## Your Next Steps


Your oral surgery experience should be as smooth and comfortable as possible. At Santa Monica OMS, we are dedicated to providing exceptional care every step of the way—from your initial consultation to your final follow-up appointment. If you’re preparing for oral surgery, take control of your recovery by following these essential care tips. Our expert team is here to support you before, during, and after your procedure, ensuring you achieve optimal healing and long-term oral health.
