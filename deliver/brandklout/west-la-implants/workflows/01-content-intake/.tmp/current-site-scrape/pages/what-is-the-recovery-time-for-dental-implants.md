Interested in getting a brand-new smile? Then dental implants may be a great option for you. Dental implants are a great way to achieve a beautiful smile if you are experiencing any issues with your teeth in Los Angeles, CA, including damaged or missing teeth.

Dental implants can help improve or enhance your look and give you a renewed sense of self-confidence with a new set of pearly whites! Plus, in most cases, dental implants can be installed by a dentist in one day with very minimal recovery time. In fact, most patients can return to daily activities –including work and school – the very next day! Check out more information about dental implant recovery here and see how a dentist can help you achieve your best smile!


## How Is The Dental Implant Process Performed?


Dental implants can often be placed in one day using advanced technology and proper planning for each patient, ensuring a customized restoration option is available and supplied to each patient.

Pre-planning technology allows dentists to precisely analyze your smile and plan a specific treatment route for you to make sure your new smile is the perfect match; specifically designing your new implants based on the original analysis. This planning occurs before placing the new dental implants and can include full arch dental implants, all on four to six dental implants, or individual implant placements.

Once the implants are placed with the first round of temporary restorations placed on top to give you an immediate smile makeover, your customized implants that are created in a dental lab are placed, replacing the temporaries as final dental implant restorations.

The recovery time following dental implant placement is typically around 4 to 6 months. However, your implant dentist will determine just how much time would be necessary for recovery, with most cases needing very minimal time off. Therefore, it is important to choose the best dental professional to execute these extraordinary restoration techniques to ensure your recovery time is small and your new smile is big and bright!
