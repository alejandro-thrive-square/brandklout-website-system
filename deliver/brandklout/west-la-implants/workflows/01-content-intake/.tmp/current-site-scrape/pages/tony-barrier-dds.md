## Dr. Barriere


Dr. Tony Barriere is a highly trained implant and cosmetic dentist. Having completed over 1000+ hours of continuing education training, he is passionate about providing the best treatment and care for his patients using the latest techniques and top of the line materials. Dr. Barriere completed his dental training at The Ohio State University College of dentistry and completed an optional yearlong surgical residency through NYU Langone Hospital. Dr Barriere invites you to come experience the West LA Implants difference and looks forward to providing you with First-class dental care. Dr. Barriere is a member of The American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, American Academy of Implant Dentistry and the ADA.
