If you are experiencing tooth loss or decay, you might be interested in learning about options to remedy this issue. If so, a dentist may suggest placing dental implants in Los Angeles, CA.

Dental implants are a great way to get a new beautiful smile, and there are several options that offer this result in a natural looking and functioning way, that will allow you to execute daily activities with a new look and smile!


## How Do Dental Implants Work?


You may be wondering about how the dental implantation process works and how your results may look. Dental implants are placed in the bone of the jaw and are then covered with a natural looking crown or tooth cap to replace any teeth that are unhealthy, crooked, or not suitable for a healthy oral environment.

Before getting dental implants placed, a dental specialist will assess the health of your jaw, mouth, and gums to make sure you are the right candidate for dental implants, as this can affect the longevity of the implants.

Although dental implants function like natural teeth, it is important to care properly for them as they are an investment that is meant to last a lifetime. This means ensuring that they are cleaned per instructions from a dentist. Making sure to keep up with regular dental appointments to make sure things are going smoothly are one of the most important things you can do.


## So What Can I Eat?


You’ll want to make sure your diet mainly consists of soft, gentle foods following dental implant surgery, including items like applesauce, avocados, baked beans, bananas, pasta, or smoothies. Avoiding any harsh, acidic, hard, spicy, or sticky foods will be in your best interests.

After receiving your permanent restorations and fully healing from your implant surgery, you should be able to eat almost anything that you would be able to with normal teeth! Because of the way that dental implants are anchored into your mouth, they function like a natural tooth root and provide the same kind of support that a natural tooth root would provide.
