Do you have a low tolerance for pain? Are you scared to correct your teeth because of the pain that may come from it? Have you heard horror stories about the pain that comes from dental implants?

Well, everyone’s experience is different. You may or may not experience pain when it comes to dental implants. A dentist in Los Angeles, CA is more than happy to assist you with letting you know what the experience will be like when you get dental implants.


## What Should You Expect While Getting Dental Implants?


When you are having dental implantation surgery, it is usually done under local anesthesia, IV sedation, or general anesthesia which means you shouldn’t feel any pain during the dental implant surgery.

With recovery, there could be some pain and or discomfort and can last a few days, but it is often cured with using medication. If you have pain that lasts more than a few days and/or the pain gets worse, your team of dental professionals will be able to instruct you on the best plan to take. Post-surgical pain symptoms following dental implant surgery included:

Surgery site bleeding

Face and gum swelling

Minor bruising

Pain in the jaw

A proper after care routine will be given to you by the oral surgeon once the dental implant surgery is complete which will include but not be limited to:

Pain medications like over the counter or prescription

Try to avoid hot or hard foods as much as possible

Reduce swelling with ice packs

Depending on how you feel, rest the day of the procedure and maybe a few days after

Surgical care site instructions
