## Discover the Difference Advanced Technology Can Make


In the field of dentistry, it’s critical to stay on top of the latest advancements. This is especially true when placing dental implants and performing oral surgery. West LA Implants is proud to offer a variety of options in robot-assisted oral surgery in Los Angeles, CA, including dental implant surgery.


## Yomi® Dental Implant Robot


The Yomi robot is one of the newest additions to the West LA Implants toolkit. This robot allows Dr. Barriere to precisely place full-mouth dental implants without the need for a surgical guide. In some cases, he can even perform surgery in less than three hours, thanks to the speed and efficiency the Yomi robot grants him.

This robot reduces the risks that come with dental implant surgery. Because it reads your cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) scans directly, it can guide Dr. Barriere to the strongest parts of your jawbone. This protects your nerves and your sinuses.

Yomi is not a replacement for Dr. Barriere’s expertise. Rather, it enhances his skills, allowing him to focus on your implants.

## NDX® nSequence


The NDX nSequence guided implant surgery kit allows Dr. Barriere to combine CBCT scans and CAD/CAM technology to plan your dental implant surgery. This enables you to walk away from your implant surgery with a temporary set of nanoceramic replacement teeth! It also reduces the length of your surgery, allowing you to get on with your life sooner.

Our surgical guides are made from 3D printed chrome. This means that your guide is fully customized to your mouth, and we can guarantee a perfect fit.
