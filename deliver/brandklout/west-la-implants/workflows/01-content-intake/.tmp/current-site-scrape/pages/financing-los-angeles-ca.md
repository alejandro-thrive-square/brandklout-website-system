## Providing Affordable Dental Care in Los Angeles



## We Make Your Oral Health a Priority


At West LA Implants, we believe that improving your oral health leads to an improvement in your medical and psychological well-being, as well as your quality of life. Finances shouldn’t be a barrier to the care you need, whether you’re getting full mouth dental implants, periodontal treatments or general dentistry. We encourage you to contact our office to learn about the financial options we can offer you.

## Your Payment and Financing Options



#### Cash, Check, or Credit Card


We allow patients to pay for dental services using cash, check and credit cards, and we accept most major credit cards.


#### Dental Insurance


We are in-network with most PPO only dental insurance carriers, including United, Concordia, Principle, GIHA, Emeritus, and Guardian


#### Lending Club


Lending Club allows you to apply for credit for medical and dental treatments from your phone in minutes. You can select rates, terms and payment options that fit your budget, and even send cash directly to your account for a lower APR. Visit www.lendingclub.com or call 800-630-1663 for more information.


#### CareCredit®


CareCredit financing lets you manage treatment costs with affordable, flexible monthly payments. Go to www.carecredit.com or call 800-677-0718 for more information.


#### Proceed Finance


With Proceed Finance, you can receive affordable, no-collateral loans of up to $60,000 and flexible terms of up to 96 months. Go to www.proceedfinance.com or call 844-272-7587 for more information.


#### Alphaeon Credit


The Alphaeon Credit card enables you to select between a variety of monthly payment options for your medical and dental treatment. The card provides special financing options for purchases over $250 and credit lines up to $25,000. Visit www.goalphaeon.com to learn more.


#### Cherry Financing


Cherry makes it easy to split your treatment cost into simple monthly payments. Apply in seconds, get approved quickly, and choose a plan that fits your budget—with no hard credit check. Visit pay.withcherry.com to learn more.


#### Sunbit


Sunbit technology is used by millions of people to pay over time for in-person services and more. The majority of people who apply are approved, interest rates are fair and transparent, and there are absolutely no late fees, origination fees, penalty fees, or penalty APRs. Visit sunbit.com to learn more.


## About the West LA Implants Dental Savings Plan


Even if you don’t have dental insurance, we believe you should be able to access affordable dentistry. That’s why we offer the West LA Dental Savings Plan to all our patients. We offer three plans to choose from, all of which include preventive care, X-rays and emergency visits.


#### Executive Plan


Adults: $400/year

• $300.00 Off Orthodontics (Invisalign)

• 20% off all procedures on the Dental Services List (except orthodontic treatment)

• New patient comprehensive exam and X-rays, periapical and bitewing x-rays

• 2 periodic exams

• 2 regular cleanings (excludes SRP-deep cleanings however ok to use 20% discounted)

• 2 fluoride treatments

• Oral cancer screening

• 2 emergency visits

• Perio-maintenance - &150.00 (excludes discount)


#### Premier Plan


Adults: $300/year

• $150.00 Off Orthodontics (Invisalign)

• 10% off all procedures on the Dental Services List (except orthodontic treatment)

• 2 regular cleanings (excludes SRP-deep cleanings however ok to use 10% discount)

• Perio-maintenance - $150.00 (excludes discount)

## Limitations and Exclusions


Patients can not use other dental coverage or dental insurance plans in conjunction with this savings plan.

The fee for the savings plan is for the included services and represents a discount of those services.

The fee for this plan is not a membership fee or insurance plan.

Any unused services are not transferable to the other patients and don’t roll over from year to year.

This plan begins the date of purchase and is valid for one year thereafter.

Patient must be 18 years of age and is not transferable to other patient or family members.

Once plan is purchased it is non-refundable

If using outside financing (lending houses) , the discount rate will not apply to dental services list
