## Your Home for General Dentistry in Los Angeles, CA


Finding a general dentist in Los Angeles can be harder than you think, which is why we make this frustrating process easier by combining everything you need under one roof! We work with multiple insurance and financing companies as well as providing a dental savings plan, and we will gladly help you file your insurance claims. We’re also the most technologically advanced dental practice in Los Angeles, using tools such as cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) scans and the Yomi® robot to complement our desire to create bonds with all of our patients. Our office also offers convenient hours to accommodate even dental emergencies.

Having your general dentistry taken care of by the same team that handles your dental implants and veneers has a lot of advantages. Dr. Barriere uses the same advanced technology, whether you’re in our office for a regular teeth cleaning or for full mouth dental replacement. Regardless of what brings you in to our office, you’ll benefit from his more than 10 years of experience.


## Select Dental Services We Offer



#### Preventive Dentistry


From teeth cleanings and digital X-rays to oral cancer screenings, West LA Implants is a one-stop shop for preventive dentistry. Each visit will be tailored to your specific needs, while always offering the best technology and highest standard of care. It’s recommended that you come in for a routine cleaning twice each year, in order to spot early signs of tooth decay, or to prevent this decay altogether.


#### Emergency Dentistry


At West LA Implants, we realize that emergencies never happen at convenient times. This is why we offer a selection of same-day and next-day emergency appointments for cracked or broken teeth, broken restorations, knocked-out teeth, toothaches and bleeding gums in Los Angeles.


#### Pediatric Dentistry


You’re never too young to take care of your smile! That’s why West LA Implants offers dental care to children of any age. Our goal is to empower your children to make the right decisions about their own smiles as they grow up.


#### Crowns and Bridges


If you have missing teeth or severe cavities, we offer custom crowns and bridges in Los Angeles to fit any smile. Crowns restore function to damaged teeth or teeth that have been treated by root canals, while bridges replace teeth by connecting to the neighboring teeth. West LA Implants offers same-day crowns and bridges, allowing you to leave the office with a fully restored smile.


#### Cavity Fillings


If you have a toothache that just won’t go away, you may need a cavity filling. Dr. Barriere offers these as a first solution to anyone who has cavities in their teeth. He also provides dental sealants to ensure that the fillings last as long as possible.


#### Root Canals


For more severe tooth decay, a root canal may be in order. Dr. Barriere is experienced in providing root canal therapy, and thanks to his knowledge of sedation dentistry, he can make it a pain-free experience for you.


## Lifetime Relationships, A Lifetime of Health


At West LA Implants, we don’t just offer general dentistry. We want to build a relationship with you. With each visit to our Los Angeles dental office, we’ll get to know you better, enabling us to provide you with a better experience each time. This also enables us to tailor your treatment plan to your specific needs and to help you build healthy oral hygiene habits, which can improve your health throughout your lifetime! Many studies have shown links between oral health and overall health, and we want to help you live your healthiest life.
