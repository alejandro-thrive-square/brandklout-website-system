## We Excel at Implant Dentistry



## Take the First Step Toward a Lifetime of Smiles


Whether you need to replace a single tooth or all your teeth, dental implants are an excellent long-term investment in your health. The first step in the process is scheduling your consultation with Dr. Barriere at West LA Implants. After we guide you through the process of getting your dental implants, we’ll customize your treatment plan. From there, you can expect a dramatically better smile and improved overall health! Even if other dentists have told you that you don’t qualify for dental implants, Dr. Barriere may be able to help you. Schedule your free second opinion today!

## The West LA Implants Process



#### Meet Our Team


The entire West LA Implants team is skilled at providing dental implants in Los Angeles, CA, and from the first time you enter our office, our goal is to treat you like family. Dr. Barriere places hundreds of dental implants each year, and he is likewise experienced in providing related services, including tooth extraction and bone grafting.


#### Plan Your Perfect Smile


Intraoral scanners and cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) scans allow Dr. Barriere to create a precise roadmap of what your treatment will look like, and smile simulation technology allows him to share this vision with you. You can give feedback and make changes before you even begin treatment! The more ideas you have about your perfect smile, the more tools we’ll have to achieve it!


#### Create a Custom Treatment Plan


We don’t offer “cookie-cutter” treatment at West LA Implants. Instead, we’ll offer you customized treatment plans based on your vision and your exact needs. Your smile should be as unique as the rest of you! We want you to take an active role in your oral health, and that means you get to choose what works best for you.


#### Cost and Payment Options


The West LA Implants team believes that cost shouldn’t be a barrier to lasting health. That’s why we offer in-house and third-party financing on dental implant surgery, and we are also in-network with many leading insurance providers. We even offer a dental savings plan!


#### Receiving Your Dental Implants


If you decide that dental implants are the best option for you, we’ll schedule you for your dental implant procedure. Dr. Barriere uses the most advanced technology for implant surgery, including the Yomi® robotic assistant, which means that your procedure will be done quickly and efficiently. We offer IV sedation as well as oral sedation and nitrous oxide (known commonly as “laughing gas”), so your nerves don’t have to stop you from getting the care you need.
