While people may know that dental implants are used to replace one or more missing teeth, they may not know the specific parts that make up a dental implant in Los Angeles, CA. Dental implants are composed of three main parts, the dental implant post, the abutment, and the dental crown/prosthesis. Here is what each dental implant part is and how it works to improve the patient’s smile.


## Why Are The Parts That Make Up A Dental Implant In Los Angeles, CA, Necessary?


Similar to traditional dentures, dental implants are even more specifically designed to look and perform like natural teeth. They are made up of three main parts, including the:


### Dental Implant Post:


A dental implant post is a screw-like structure that is made to function like a natural tooth root. The dental implant post is precisely placed into the area of the patients jawbone where it has enough density to support it. The dental implant post provides a strong, permanent, and sturdy foundation for the patient’s new smile.


### Abutment:


The abutment is the part of the dental implant that connects the dental implant post to the crown/prosthesis.


### Dental Prosthesis (or crown):


The dental crowns/ prosthesis are the parts of the dental implants that are visible above the gumline. The crown/prosthesis is custom designed to look like the patient’s natural teeth.

Once the dental implant post, abutment, and crown/prosthesis are securely placed in the patient’s jawbone, it gives them a reliable new smile that lets them eat, speak, and smile normally for years to come. Dental implants are custom designed for every person’s smile so that they get the exact new smile they need and deserve.
