## Excellent Dental Care In A Comfortable Environment


The staff, at West L.A. Dental Implants, are committed to providing excellent dental care in a comfortable environment where patients can relax and feel at ease. We are a West L.A. implant dentist office and are trusted by patients throughout the Los Angeles CA area because we work to make patients feel as comfortable as possible while delivering stunning results. We offer unparalleled quality, expertise, and technology but in a very comfortable, personable and professional environment.


#### A West L.A. Implant Dentist Office


We treat all of our patients with respect and take the time to listen to their needs and concerns, making us a trusted provider of dental implants West Los Angeles patients love. We invite you to visit us and experience why we are the best West L.A. cosmetic dentist. Here you will find information on what to expect during your first dentist appointment along with links to patient forms and paperwork.


#### Making an Appointment


It is easy to make an appointment for your initial examination and dental treatments with our Los Angeles dentist office. Simply call (310) 481-0111 and one of our friendly team members will be happy to assist you. If you schedule an appointment with our dentist office and something comes up, please call and cancel 24 hours in advance so that we can accommodate other patient requests. We provide emergency dentistry services and will do our best to return phone calls after hours as quickly as possible.


#### Your First Visit


Now you can have a smile makeover in a beautiful and convenient location. Walk into our lobby where you will be greeted by our friendly staff. We will provide you with new patient paperwork that includes a health questioner, request for your insurance information and contact information.


#### Initial Examination


Your initial dental exam will take around one hour. The expert staff at West L.A. Dental Implants will ask you questions, get to know your oral hygiene routine, and listen to your health concerns. We provide pediatric dentistry services and believe in educating patients and empowering them to make good choices to improve their oral health, regardless of how old they are. We will demonstrate how to properly care for your teeth and make additional suggestions as needed.


#### Best Course Of Treatment


Our Dental Hygienist will clean your teeth then Dr. Barriere will complete your examination and make recommendations for proposed dental treatments. As an implants dentist, we offer a wide range of cosmetic dentistry services and can advise patients on the best course of treatment for their health and their appearance. If a restoration is needed, Dr. Barriere offers same day crowns to help patients recover quickly. He is an expert Los Angeles dentist and able to keep patients on the path of good oral health from our convenient location.


#### Pain Concerns


We work to ensure that our patients feel comfortable, and that includes minimizing their pain as much as possible. As a veneers dentist, we provide pain relief when getting a dental implant (or dental implants), having dental veneers placed, or dentures installed with the All-on-Four procedure, among others. If you are undergoing a dental procedure or getting West L.A. dental implants, Dr. Barriere will meet with you to discuss your pain tolerance and create a plan for pain management.


#### Get Back To Your Life Faster


Working together, we will treat your dental health problems and provide general dentistry services, in a way, that minimizes pain and enables you to get back to your life faster. Additionally, Dr. Barriere offers orthodontics and gives patients a way to straighten their teeth without the discomfort.


## Premier Destination for Implant Dentistry


We will also provide you with information to familiarize you with our cosmetic dentist office and how we handle things like patient privacy. If you would like to complete these forms before the appointment, please let us know. We are the best dentist in town and will work to make your experience as pleasant as possible. As an emergency dentist, we offer same day procedures when necessary, and we are the premier destination for implant dentistry West L.A. and throughout the greater Los Angeles area.
