## Your Tooth Replacement Options


At West LA Implants, Dr. Tony Barriere offers a variety of solutions to missing or failing teeth: dentures, implant supported dentures and All-on-4 dental implants. When you need to replace all of your teeth, it’s critical to know what your options are, and we want you to make an informed decision.

While they are the most affordable option initially, dentures aren’t a long-lasting solution to tooth loss. In many cases, they may end up causing problems such as jawbone recession or friction sores. Additionally, they require adhesive and special cleaners, and the expense and hassle of these can add up quickly.

Implant supported dentures are the next step up when replacing a full mouth of teeth. These fully removable dentures snap onto fixtures that are surgically implanted into your mouth. Due to these implants, they tend to stay on longer than traditional dentures, and they can also reduce jawbone loss. They even cost less than full mouth dental implants!

Finally, seen as the “gold standard” for full mouth dental replacement, All-on-4 dental implants are zirconium replacement teeth attached to four or more posts that are surgically implanted into your jaw. They restore nearly all of your biting and chewing power, and you take care of them the way you would take care of your original teeth.
