Imagine walking into a dentist’s office with an arch of failing or missing teeth and walking out a few hours later with a set of natural-looking replacement teeth. It might seem like a fantasy, especially if you have dealt with tooth troubles for decades. Yet for people who opt for All-on-4® treatment in Los Angeles, CA, being able to improve their smiles in one day is a reality.


## How Can A Dentist Replace A Full Arch Of Teeth At Once?


Although people have been losing teeth to age, decay, trauma, and gum disease for millennia, they have never had as many options to replace their teeth as they do now. Certainly, removable dentures have been a tried and true tooth replacement choice for a long time. However, technological advancements now allow patients to replace their teeth with a denture prosthesis that is firmly anchored to several strategically placed dental implants.

What is a dental implant? Shaped like a screw, it is a durable post that is surgically placed into the upper or lower jaw. Once in place, a dental implant fuses with the bone over a period of several months. Essentially, the implant becomes a part of the jaw. But people who receive implants do not need to wait for months to restore their smiles. Instead, they can rejuvenate their appearance and regain some functionality immediately.


## The “Magic” (Science, Really) Of All-On-4 Dental Implants In Los Angeles, CA


As alluded to, a highly trained dentist can place implants and then use them as a foundation to permanently secure a set of replacement teeth. The replacement teeth stay in place and can absorb practically all the chewing and biting forces that normal teeth can.

At the practice in Los Angeles, CA, the TeethToday® system is followed. Directly after placing dental implants, a dentist affixes a temporary set of replacement teeth made from nanoceramic materials to the dental implant posts. The outcome is an instant smile that looks very natural.

After several months, the temporary teeth will be replaced with a full-arch prosthesis made of zirconia.


## Will Anyone Notice All-On-4 Dental Implants?


The before-after transformation for people who receive All-On-4 permanent full mouth dental implants is nothing short of dramatic. Accordingly, if a patient and their dentist feel that All-On-4 dental implants are the right choice, then expect that people will notice the difference. This is positive, though! Many people are thrilled by how much more confidence they have, especially after getting compliments on the way they look after an All-On-4 treatment.
