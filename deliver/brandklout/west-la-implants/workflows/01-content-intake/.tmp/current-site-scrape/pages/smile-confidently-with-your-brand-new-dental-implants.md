## Improving Your Smile


Many Americans experience tooth loss or injury, which, if left untreated, can lead to atrophy or weakening of the jaw muscles. Dental implants can correct this issue AND provide you with a beautiful new smile. Since dental implants also mimic the look of natural teeth, you can rest assured that they will give you a great look.

Additional advantages of dental implants are:


### Enhanced Appearance:


They look and feel like your own teeth. They also become permanent.


### Better Speech:


Your teeth don’t slip, causing you to mumble or slur your words


### Easier Eating:


Dental implants are made to make it easier for you to ear. With regular dentures you have to worry about them sliding and making chewing very difficult. With dental implants functioning like your own teeth you are allowed to eat your favorite foods without pain and confidence.


### Increased Durability:


The dental implants can last a lifetime with good care.


### More Convenience:


Removable dentures are just that, they come out. Dental implants eliminates the inconvenience of removing dentures and no messy adhesives because the implants fuse to your bone.
