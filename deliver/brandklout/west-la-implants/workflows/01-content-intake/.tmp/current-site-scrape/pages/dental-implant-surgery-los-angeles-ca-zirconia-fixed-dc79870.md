## A Superior Alternative to Dentures and Hybrids


If you’re considering replacing your missing teeth or are looking for an alternative to dentures, we provide one of the best solutions available today: full mouth dental implants with a zirconia fixed bridge in Los Angeles, CA. Leaders in implant dentistry, including our experienced dental team at West LA Implants, consider this to be the superior tooth replacement solution due to its unparalleled functional and esthetic results. Fully customized to your smile, this fixed bridge of teeth is fabricated from a solid block of zirconia, one of the strongest materials in dentistry. Zirconia is biocompatible and naturally tooth-colored. Unlike acrylic dentures and even other full arch options, zirconia full mouth dental implants won’t stain, chip, or break. They also provide permanent function and beauty, just like (and often better than) healthy natural teeth. A zirconia fixed bridge secured by dental implants is truly one of the best choices for permanently replacing your failing or missing teeth!

## Supporting Long-Term Function


Your zirconia fixed bridge has the exceptional strength comparable to natural teeth that is needed for proper biting and chewing of food for years to come. These bridges get their incredible strength and durability from how they’re fabricated—from one solid piece of zirconia. Zirconia bridges are made from a material that has similar physical properties to titanium metal but esthetic properties that offer attractive results. Traditional dentures and some hybrids are made by securing porcelain teeth into an acrylic base, which is essentially plastic. Zirconia bridges eliminate the weaknesses common with the two-material design of these traditional solutions. Your zirconia fixed bridge will withstand everyday wear and tear and offer significantly strong bite forces without fracturing. When permanently attached to dental implants, this system will help ensure your jawbone remains stimulated and healthy, too.


## Permanently Restoring Smile Esthetics


In addition to its strength, a bridge made from zirconia has the color and translucency of natural teeth. These features are currently unmatched by any other material in dentistry today. We customize this restoration to your smile specifications and to complement your facial features, gum and lip line, and even your skin tone. Your new teeth will also be custom colored to match your natural gums as well as shaded to the brightness you desire. Unlike acrylic and porcelain materials, the smooth and polished surface of the zirconia material will feel comfortable against your gums and resist staining. If you receive a zirconia fixed bridge in Los Angeles, CA from our skilled cosmetic dentist, you can expect your new brilliant smile to last for decades—no one will know they aren’t natural teeth!
