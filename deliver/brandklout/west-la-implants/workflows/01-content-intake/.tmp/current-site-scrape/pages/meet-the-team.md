#### Dr. Barriere


Dr. Tony Barriere is a highly trained implant and cosmetic dentist. Having completed over 1000+ hours of continuing education training, he is passionate about providing the best treatment and care for his patients using the latest techniques and top of the line materials.

#### Tielar A.



#### Dental Assistant


I’ve been a dental assistant since 2019. I love that there is always something new and innovative in dentistry. There is always something new to look forward to and the way dental technology is advancing it creates excitement for both patients and myself. My favorite aspect about working in dentistry is having the satisfaction of knowing I made a difference in someone’s smile. I’m from Los Angeles born and raised. I like to spend my free time with friends and family enjoying the fruits of my labor.


#### Veronica V.



#### Office Manager


My life has had many twists and turns (haven’t we all?) Dentistry has definitely taught me to go with the flow. Before dentistry I was the Surgical Coordinator and Operatory Materials Manager at an outpatient surgical center for 19 years. My years connecting to patients from all walks of life has assisted me to develop people skills that I now use in my adventures with dental office management. I feel blessed and honored to work with an outstanding team of professionals that have supported me and given me new tools of the trade, to make sure our patients have the BEST experience ever in a dental office no matter what the procedure or treatment. My desire to make all patients that walk through our doors leave in the most optimal conditions possible.

#### Delia E.



#### Registered Dental Assistant & Back Office Manager


I have been in the dental field since 2011 as a Registered Dental Assistant. Having braces at a young age is what gave me the interest in having a career in the dental field. I love learning new dental methods and how we can help the patient feel most comftorable. I believe we should treat each patient as a family member because most patients have a dental fear, so treating them with kindness makes their dental visit more comforting. The best part of dentistry is seeing patient satisfaction after their appointments.

I have lived in Los Angeles all my life. I love being outdoors enjoying the lovely LA weather. I also enjoy traveling and bing wathcing shows.
