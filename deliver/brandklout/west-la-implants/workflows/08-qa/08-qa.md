# Workflow: 08 - QA

## Client-Facing Overview
Before launch, we test the staged site across devices, links, forms, SEO basics, performance, and visual accuracy.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Integrity Rule**: Any change to the Python executor or technical logic of this workflow must be documented here immediately.

## Operational Purpose
Verify the staged delivery build against quality standards: pixel fidelity, CMS/content structure, responsiveness, functionality, SEO readiness, accessibility, and performance. The QA surface changes by offer: full Webflow builds require full-site QA, migrations verify the outputs of `12`, `13`, and `14`, improvement work verifies the scoped plan from `15`, and AI Vercel/Airtable builds verify the code package and runtime content routes from `17`.

## Inputs From Sales / Previous Phase
- Staging or preview URL and build manifest.
- Approved design reference screenshots and final content.
- Known critical user paths, forms, analytics, and integration requirements.

## Execution Steps
1. Compare staging templates against approved designs across breakpoints.
2. Crawl pages for SEO, metadata, headings, links, schema, and alt text.
3. Test forms, CTAs, navigation, CMS edge cases, analytics, and responsive behavior.
4. Triage defects, then block launch until critical/high issues are resolved.
5. **Content-fit pass before any human review** (added 2026-09-24, first run on West LA Implants).
   The human reviewer should never be the one to find a photo that contradicts its copy, a face
   cropped out of a hero, or a heading with one word on its last line. Run it after the build is
   gated and before the site goes to the reviewer (for Brandklout, before it goes to Jessica):
   1. **Back up first**: git tag + `backup-live.mjs` API snapshot with published HTML + `VACUUM INTO`
      copy of `cms.db` on the container (the Instatic upload directive has the commands). Instatic
      has no branches; page "versions" hold only title and slug. The three layers are the backup.
   2. **Measure**: `node 08-qa/.tmp/qa-fit.mjs` (copied per client like `check-local.mjs`) renders
      every page under reduced motion at 1440/991/390 and writes `fit/copy-fit.json`
      (overflow, runt, button, measure, tall, tiny, uneven) plus `fit/image-context.json` and
      contact sheets `fit/image-sheets/sheet-NN.png`: each image placement's rendered crop at
      1440 and 390 beside its heading, copy and alt, with repeats and upscaling flagged.
      `--base=<live url>` drives the published site; `--no-images` skips the crops.
   3. **Judge the images**: a model reads the sheets (split ~10 sheets per subagent) and writes
      `review-*.json` findings of kind MISMATCH / CROP / QUALITY / ALT / REPEAT, each with a
      concrete fix drawn from the client's own media library.
   3b. **The bar is relevance 3, not "not contradictory"** (Alejandro, 2026-09-24): every photo
      must show the specific subject of its copy (3), not a generic on-topic scene (2) or a
      mismatch (1), and be sharp at 1440 (source ≥ ~1000px tall for a portrait hero). A first pass
      that only removed contradictions still left 167 of 370 placements failing this bar.
      Sourcing, in order: the client's own library (look at the photo, not its filename) →
      licensed Freepik stock through the MAGNIFIC key (`03-graphic-resources/.tmp/stock/`:
      `candidates.mjs briefs.json` builds a sheet of candidates per brief, picker subagents choose
      ids with a focal point, `fetch.py picks.json` downloads full-res, resizes to 2400px and logs
      every id in `stock-ledger.json`) → Unsplash (`unsplash.mjs`) for named places. Real people
      (doctor, staff, named patients) and before/after results are never stock or AI-upscaled;
      named patients appear only beside their own treatment. A place photo must be verifiably
      that place; if none exists, use the practice's own interior rather than a generic city shot.
   4. **Fix at the source** and rebuild: crops with a focal point (`pos: 'x% y%'` on the image
      object, emitted as an `fp-x-y` class; never inline styles), swaps and alts in the page source
      (alt is per asset on Instatic, so one alt per photo everywhere), type fixes in the tokens.
      Re-run until only accepted findings remain, then push, publish and re-gate live.
   5. **Hand over** `docs/qa-report.md`: what was fixed, what was accepted and why, and the short
      list the human should still look at (things only a person or the client can decide).
   Lessons from the first run: the portrait cover crops a 2:1 photo to a third of its width, so
   centred photos lose their subject (33 crops); a template fallback image repeated 8x on one index;
   `text-wrap: balance` on display type and `pretty` on copy cleared ~500 runts in one rule, but
   `balance` gives up past 6 lines.

## Offer-Specific Adaptations
- **Development from Scratch**: Run full-site QA across design fidelity, content, CMS, responsiveness, forms, SEO, performance, accessibility, and launch readiness.
- **Figma to Webflow**: Prioritize visual fidelity against the approved Figma file, responsive behavior, CMS templates, interactions, forms, and SEO basics.
- **Migration to Webflow**: Verify migrated content against `13-content-export-import` and redirects/metadata against `14-migration-seo-redirects`.
- **Webflow Improvements & Updates**: Scope QA to the changed pages/components and regression surface defined in `15-improvement-intake-audit`.
- **AI Site with Claude, Vercel & Airtable**: QA the Vercel preview/site package from `17-ai-site-build-airtable`, including Airtable route behavior, secret boundary checks, loading/empty/error states, motion, responsive behavior, and the scoped SEO rendering expectation.

## Version Control
- All changes to this workflow must follow the version control rules in [SYSTEM_RULES.md - Section 4](../../../SYSTEM_RULES.md).
- **`.tmp/` folder**: Commit lightweight artifacts that support review, replay, handoff, or agent continuity. Binary/media assets remain gitignored and synced to the VPS.
- **Branch naming**: `deliver/[client-name]-qa-<change>` once cloned into a client folder.
- **Commit discipline**: Atomic commits with descriptive messages. One logical change per commit.
