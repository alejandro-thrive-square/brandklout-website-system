## Findings

1. **There is no vector logo anywhere on the site, and the raster master is unusable.**
   The only two brand marks are `westlaimplants_logo_web.png` and
   `westlaimplants_logo_white_web.png`, both **250 x 200 px at about 5 KB**. That is smaller
   than a favicon on a retina display. It cannot carry a hero lockup, a large footer mark or
   any print use. **Request the original vector (AI / EPS / SVG) from the client before
   `04-design` starts.** This is a blocking asset request, not a nice-to-have.
2. **Named patient photography and video is the strongest asset class here.** Leslie
   (All-on-4), Emily (implants and crowns) and Michael (full-mouth reconstruction) appear as
   both stills and B-roll, and their stories are the homepage testimonials. This is exactly
   the material the competitor across the street does not have. **Confirm patient photo and
   video consent is on file before republishing any of it.**
3. **Licensed stock photography is in use.** Ten `shutterstock_*` files are present. Stock
   licences do not automatically transfer to a rebuilt site or to a new agency. Confirm
   coverage or replace.
4. **At least one asset is named for a different practice.** `alluredental-hero-girl-1.png`
   (1.6 MB, the largest single PNG on the site) is Allure Dental artwork, which suggests some
   imagery came from a template or a prior agency and may not be licensed to West LA Implants
   at all. Same pattern seen on Dr. Nahlah.
5. **Five assets are still referenced from a dead staging host.** `3e3a6af526.nxcli.io`
