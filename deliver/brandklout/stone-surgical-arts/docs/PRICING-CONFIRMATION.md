approving the tiers.

## 6. Also stated on the site, and worth confirming

- **Published fees cover the face** unless stated otherwise; additional areas are priced separately.
- **Anaesthesia adds $300 per fifteen minutes** on top, where needed.
- **Microneedling is sold in packages of three.**
- **Financing runs through Sunbit**, and membership credit can be applied to a surgical fee.
- The written plan states the total, **with nothing added afterwards**.

## What happens with the answers

Fees live in the page markup, not in a data file or a CMS field, so each correction is an edit to
`site-src/pages/*.body.html` followed by a rebuild and republish. The pages that carry a given fee
are listed above where it matters; `treatments.html` carries all of them. Once confirmed, the
"reconfirmed for 2026" hedge in the `treatments` footer should come out.
