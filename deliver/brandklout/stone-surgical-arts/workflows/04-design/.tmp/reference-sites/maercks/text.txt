Why Dr Rian?

With over 20 years of experience, Dr. Maercks is a board-certified plastic surgeon known for delivering refined, natural results. His training extends far beyond traditional plastic surgery residency. Following residency, he completed advanced fellowship training with some of the world’s leading craniofacial and aesthetic plastic surgeons across Europe, Central America and South America, including in Paris, France; Mexico City, Mexico; and Brazil.

​

Dr. Maercks was the final craniofacial and aesthetic fellow trained by the legendary Fernando Ortiz-Monasterio, allowing him to refine his surgical techniques under the guidance of some of the true masters of the field before establishing his own practice.

​

Trained at one of the United States’ leading medical institutions and further shaped by internationally recognised masters of craniofacial and aesthetic surgery, he combines advanced surgical expertise with a deeply personalised approach to patient care.

​

He has built an international reputation for excellence in aesthetic and reconstructive surgery, with a focus on safety, precision, and long-term outcomes. His work is guided by both science and artistry, ensuring results that enhance, rather than alter, each individual’s natural identity.

​

Patients travel from around the world to his Miami and East Hampton clinics for bespoke treatment plans across facial rejuvenation, body contouring, and reconstructive procedures.

​

Above all, Dr. Maercks is known for his thoughtful, detail-oriented approach and his commitment to building lasting patient relationships.

The Zero Experience™
A new approach to facial rejuvenation, designed around you.

The Zero Experience™ was created to redefine what facial rejuvenation can feel like — combining advanced surgical techniques with a philosophy centred around comfort, precision, discretion, and naturally refined results.

​

At The Maercks Institute, every stage of your experience is thoughtfully considered. From your initial consultation and surgical planning through to your recovery, our approach is designed to minimise discomfort, reduce unnecessary downtime, and make the entire process as seamless as possible.

​

The Zero Experience™ goes beyond the procedure itself. It is about creating a personalised experience that respects your time, your privacy, and your individual goals. Using refined techniques and meticulous attention to detail, the focus is on restoring balance and definition while avoiding an artificial, pulled, or overdone appearance.

​

The result is not a different version of you. It is a refreshed, rested and naturally rejuvenated version of yourself - with results designed to feel as effortless as the experience.

Less downtime. Less disruption. More confidence.
