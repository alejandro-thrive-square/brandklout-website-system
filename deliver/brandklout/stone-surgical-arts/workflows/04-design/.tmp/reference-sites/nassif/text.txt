TRAINED BY THE BEST. TRUSTED BY THE SAME PATIENTS.

Since 2019, Dr. John Frederick has been an integral part of the Nassif practice, personally selected by Dr. Paul Nassif as a trusted surgical counterpart and a natural extension of his renowned standard of care. Working closely alongside one of the world's most recognized facial plastic surgeons, Dr. Frederick offers patients continuity, precision, and confidence throughout their surgical journey.

With a focus on rhinoplasty, revision rhinoplasty, and facial rejuvenation, Dr. Frederick brings a refined understanding of facial balance, aging, and natural-looking results. His approach often incorporates advanced techniques such as CO₂ laser resurfacing and PDGF to help support recovery, skin quality, and the overall patient experience.

Dr. Frederick also offers select facial procedures—including upper blepharoplasty, fat grafting, lip lift, and brow lift—without general anesthesia when clinically appropriate. This provides patients with thoughtful options tailored to their goals, comfort, recovery expectations, and level of commitment.
