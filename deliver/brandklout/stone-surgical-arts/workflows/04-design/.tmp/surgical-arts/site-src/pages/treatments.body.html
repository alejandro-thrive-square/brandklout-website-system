Choosing between them

The comparisons people get stuck on.

MyEllevate® or a neck lift: how do I know which?

A neck lift uses small incisions behind the ears and under the chin, and can last up to ten years. MyEllevate® places internal sutures through small punctures, and lasts around five. MyEllevate® suits earlier laxity; a neck lift reverses advanced sagging, wrinkling or fullness. MyEllevate® also needs only local anaesthetic, so you can drive yourself home.

Will a neck lift fix my jowls?

No, and it is the most common mix-up. Jowls sit low, so people assume neck work, but they originate in sagging facial tissue. A minimally invasive facelift is the procedure that reduces them.

Implants or filler?

Fillers are gel injectables that plump and smooth lines, acne scars and thin lips, lasting months to a couple of years by product and site. Implants are customised, biocompatible inserts that reshape the structure beneath and stay intact, so the contour is permanent. Filler changes the surface, an implant the frame.

How long does each result actually last?

The practice’s published figures: blepharoplasty 10–15 years or more; a neck lift up to ten; MyEllevate® around five; facial implants and buccal fat reduction permanent, though significant weight gain can return fullness to the cheek elsewhere. Injectables are months, not years: maintenance, not surgery.

Can I have more than one procedure at once?

Often, and frequently the better plan: a neck lift alongside a facelift or eyelid surgery means one recovery instead of two. What combines safely depends on your anatomy and time under anaesthetic, decided at the consultation.

Why do some rows say “at consultation” instead of a price?
