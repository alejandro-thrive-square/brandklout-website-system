Nurse practitioner · aesthetic injector

Brooke

A board-certified family nurse practitioner and advanced aesthetic injector with a background in dermatology, and several years in the NICU before her master’s degree. With over six years of injecting experience she has trained other injectors, puts safety first, and is, in the practice’s own words, a perfectionist about natural-looking results.

Married, with two girls, Tenley and Saylor; travelling, swimming and country music when she is not here.
