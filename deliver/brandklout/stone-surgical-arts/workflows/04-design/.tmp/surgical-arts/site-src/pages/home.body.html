Before you book

The five questions we are asked most.

What does the consultation cost, and what happens in it?

$175 for Texas residents, $250 from out of state, with a $100 deposit that holds the date and comes off the fee. The hour is with Dr. Stone, and you leave knowing your options and their costs.

How much time will I need away from work?

About a week after eyelid surgery, about ten days after CO2 laser resurfacing, two weeks after a facelift. Plan for one to two weeks and come back early if recovery allows.

Can I finance treatment?

Yes. The practice works with Sunbit, CareCredit and Scratchpay, and is a preferred provider with all major dental and medical carriers where a procedure is covered. Or a membership, which banks credit rather than borrowing it.

Will I need someone to drive me home?

Under intravenous sedation, yes. A responsible adult must bring you, stay in the office and drive you home, and you should not drive or operate machinery for 24 hours. Local anaesthetic alone does not require it.

When can I wear makeup again?

Usually 48 hours after sutures come out, and about ten days after CO2 laser. Avoid the sun in that window: it is what darkens a healing scar.
