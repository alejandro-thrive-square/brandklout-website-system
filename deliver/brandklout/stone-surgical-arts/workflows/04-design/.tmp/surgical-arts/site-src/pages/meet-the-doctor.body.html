Dr. Stone’s surgical approach

Her training shapes the way she plans each face.

Dr. Mona Stone’s background in biomedical engineering, facial reconstruction, and oral and maxillofacial surgery informs how she evaluates more than the feature a patient wants to change. She considers the relationship between bone, soft tissue, and facial proportions when discussing a treatment plan.

As a KLS Martin IPS®-certified surgeon, she has training in patient-specific implant planning. When an implant is appropriate, three-dimensional imaging can help guide a design suited to the patient’s anatomy. She brings that same attention to the structures of the jawline and neck when considering cosmetic procedures.

Beyond the practice

A public speaker, an activist, and a Formula 1 obsessive.

Dr. Stone cofounded Real Heroes Need Masks, a grassroots organisation that sourced PPE for medical staff through the pandemic. She is a public speaker and a philanthropist, the mother of two small children, an avid supporter of motor sport, and the surgeon behind “Dr. Jawline”.
