Why a maxillofacial surgeon for cosmetic work

The implants here are planned to a scan, not picked from a tray.

KLS Martin IPS® is a reconstruction workflow in which an implant is designed to a particular skull from a particular scan rather than fitted from stock sizes. The practice’s own position is that a generic implant gives a generic result.

The marginal mandibular nerve runs exactly where a neck lift works. A surgeon who spent her residency rebuilding it after it had been broken knows that anatomy in three dimensions, in a particular neck, rather than as a diagram.
