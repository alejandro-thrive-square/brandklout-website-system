## Your First Visit, Designed Around You
A conversation, not a sales pitch. It is where Dr. Stone listens to your goals, examines what is possible, and maps a plan built around your face, not just your teeth.
Call (817) 383-8361 Request appointment

## What Happens at Your First Visit
1. A Real Conversation
We start by listening: your goals, your concerns, your history. No rushing, no pressure.

2. A Comprehensive Exam
Dr. Stone evaluates your oral health, bite, bone, and facial structure, not just the missing teeth.

3. Advanced 3D Imaging
Detailed scans give a precise view of your bone and anatomy, the foundation for accurate, predictable planning.

4. Your Options, Explained Honestly
A straight assessment of whether you are a candidate and what the realistic outcomes are. If a simpler path is better for you, we will tell you.

5. A Personalized Plan
You leave with a clear understanding of your treatment, timeline, and the investment involved.

## How to Prepare
A little preparation helps us make the most of your visit. Please bring:
A photo ID

Your insurance or dental plan information, even though we are out of network

A list of any medications you currently take

Any prior imaging, x-rays, or records from another provider

A list of questions you want answered

Common Questions

What happens at a dental implant consultation?

At your consultation, Dr. Stone reviews your goals and medical history, examines your bite and bone, takes 3D imaging, and explains your options honestly. You leave with a personalized treatment plan, timeline, and cost estimate, with no pressure to commit.

How much does a dental implant consultation cost in Westlake, TX?

Consultation fees depend on what your visit involves, such as imaging and the complexity of your case. Call Stone Dental Implant Center at 817-383-8361 and our team will walk you through the details, then explain the full investment for your recommended treatment before anything begins.

How long does the first visit take?

Plan for about an hour. That gives Dr. Stone enough time to examine you thoroughly, complete 3D imaging, answer your questions, and build a plan without rushing.

Do I need a referral to see an oral surgeon for dental implants?

No referral is required. You can book a consultation with Dr. Stone directly, even if you have not seen an oral and maxillofacial surgeon before.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage varies by plan. Stone Dental Implant Center is out-of-network with PPO plans, and we provide itemized documentation you can submit for possible reimbursement.

What financing options do you offer for dental implants?

We accept Lending Club, Cherry, Sunbit and Proceed Finance, among others, so you can spread the cost of treatment over time. You can apply online before or after your visit.

Will I get a treatment plan and cost estimate the same day?

In most cases, yes. You leave your first visit with a clear plan, timeline, and estimate. Complex cases may need a short follow-up to finalize details.

Can I do a virtual consultation first?

Ask our team about a virtual consultation option when you call 817-383-8361 if an in-person visit is difficult to schedule right away.
