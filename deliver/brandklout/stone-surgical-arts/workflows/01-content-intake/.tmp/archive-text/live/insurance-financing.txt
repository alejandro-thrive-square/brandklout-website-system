Insurance and Financing: Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with insurance for dental implants?

Yes. Our team will review your benefits with you, help you understand what your plan may cover, and provide the documentation you need. We are always happy to answer questions about your coverage.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage varies by plan, and many plans cover only part of implant treatment. Our team will go over your benefits with you and provide the paperwork you need so you can make the most of your coverage.

What financing options do you offer for dental implants?

We offer and accept a variety of financing options, including Lending Club, Cherry, Sunbit and Proceed Finance, among others. You can apply online in just a few minutes, before or after your consultation.

Can I make monthly payments for All-on-X?

Yes. Through our financing partners, you can divide the cost of All-on-X and other treatments into manageable monthly payments. Our team can help you find the option that fits your budget best.

How do I apply for financing?

Choose any of our financing partners and apply directly through the links on this page. Approval is often quick, and you are welcome to apply before your visit so your options are ready when you plan treatment.

What if I have questions about cost?

Just reach out. Our team will walk you through the full investment for your recommended treatment and the payment options available, so there are no surprises along the way.
