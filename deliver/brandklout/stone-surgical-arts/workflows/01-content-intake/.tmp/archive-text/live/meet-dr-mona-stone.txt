About Dr. Stone

## A surgeon's training. An artist's eye.

Biomedical Engineer
3 Level 1 Trauma Centers
Pinnacle Faculty
Baylor & Methodist
KLS MARTIN IPS® CERTIFIED

Dr. Stone's path to surgery started in engineering. Before dental school she earned a degree in biomedical engineering, then her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Missouri-Kansas City, followed by a full residency in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery in Fort Lauderdale across three Level 1 trauma centers. She is an attending surgeon at Baylor and Methodist, and implant surgery is the heart of her practice.

What truly sets her apart is how she sees. Where many surgeons focus only on whether an implant will hold, Dr. Stone reads the whole face: proportion, balance, the line of the jaw, the way a smile sits on a person. She designs each result the way a facial surgeon would, considering not just function today, but how it will look, feel, and age over the years that follow.
