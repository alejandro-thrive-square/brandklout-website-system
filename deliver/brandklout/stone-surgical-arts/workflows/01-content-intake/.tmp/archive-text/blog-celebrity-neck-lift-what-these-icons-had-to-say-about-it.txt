Looks count in the society we live in. That’s even more clear in Hollywood. A celebrity’s appearance can help or hinder their career. It’s why cosmetic procedures have become as common as changing one’s hairstyle.
Procedures like neck lifts are popular among celebrities. A neck lift surgery can tighten and remove sagging skin in the neck area. The result is a youthful and sharp appearance.
Many A-listers today are candid about their experiences with neck lifts. They’re not shy about sharing insights into how it affected their confidence and careers. Here’s what they have to say.
Jane Fonda

Jane Fonda is a legendary actress. She’s also a beauty icon. The “On Golden Pond” actress has been open about her cosmetic procedures. She admitted in interviews that she embraces aging. But she also said a neck lift surgery gave her a confidence boost. Fonda also said the procedure gave her a fresh appearance but didn’t compromise her natural look.

TV personality Sharon Osbourne is famous for speaking about her cosmetic enhancements. She often talked about how getting a celebrity neck lift was a big part of her journey to improve her looks.
Osbourne noted that her neck lift made a drastic difference. It helped her achieve a smoother, more youthful neckline. Ozzy Osbourne’s wife said cosmetic procedures should enhance one’s appearance. They should not change it.
Joan Rivers

Joan Rivers's name has become synonymous with cosmetic surgery. The comedian is famous for joking that she had over 700 procedures done. A neck lift, breast reduction, and two facelifts are all included in that long list.
