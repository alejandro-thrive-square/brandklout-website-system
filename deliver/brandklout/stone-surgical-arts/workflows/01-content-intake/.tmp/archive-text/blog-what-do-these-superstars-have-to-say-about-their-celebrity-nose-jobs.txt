What Do These Superstars Have to Say About Their Celebrity Nose Jobs
July 10, 2024
Rhinoplasty is one of the top cosmetic surgeries done in the United States. More than 350,000 individuals had this procedure done in 2020.
Rhinoplasty is commonplace in the entertainment industry. People often talk about celebrity nose jobs. Many of Hollywood’s elite had the procedure to improve their appearance. Others did it for medical reasons.
Here’s what three celebrities have to say about their nose jobs.
Cardi B
Cardi B has always been candid about the plastic surgeries she has done. The rapper talked about how she decided to get fillers in her nose. The non-surgical procedure made her nose longer and wider though.
Cardi B explained that she felt she had no option but to get rhinoplasty. The process helped fix the damage done by the fillers. The singer said she’s vocal about her experience to let people know what to expect.
Iggy Azalea
Iggy Azalea is another celebrity who admitted to getting rhinoplasty. The Australian rapper is so happy about having one of the best celebrity nose jobs. She’s so proud of it that she even greeted her plastic surgeon on his birthday. Azalea also thanked him for giving her a fabulous nose.
Azalea said she didn’t get a nose job because of insecurity. She also said people shouldn’t feel ashamed about getting one.
Howard Stern
Radio host Howard Stern was famous for his blunt questions and his distinct crooked nose. That nose appeared thinner after 2011. The change sparked debates on whether he had a nose job.
Stern kept the procedure a secret at first. He later told his colleagues about it. He said he admitted to it because he worried about the side effects. Stern said he read that voice loss was sometimes an offshoot of the procedure. Fans of the media personality did notice a drastic nasal change to his voice around that time.
