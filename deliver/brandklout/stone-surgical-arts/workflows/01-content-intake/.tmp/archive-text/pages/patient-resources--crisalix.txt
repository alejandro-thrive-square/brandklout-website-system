## Crisalix 3D & 4D Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality Medical Aesthetic Imaging

Used around the world as one of the top applications for pre-plastic surgery simulation, Crisalix 3D & 4D imaging offers a unique way for patients to preview thousands of different cosmetic procedures, both surgical and non-surgical. Patients can view a 3D image of their face or body with the cosmetic changes they are considering on their phone or in-office. The truly innovative part of Crisalix is the 4D augmented reality feature, where you can see yourself on a large screen with the cosmetic changes applied in real time.

## Crisalix Imaging at Stone Surgical Arts

At Stone Surgical Arts in Westlake, TX, we are committed to modern techniques and patient education. Giving our patients as much information as possible about a procedure they may be considering has been our longtime goal. With Crisalix, we can go a step further and offer the opportunity to actually see in a highly realistic way what the results of their procedure will look like for even better decision-making power.

## What is Crisalix?

This is a truly revolutionary technology that has been developed to improve the outcomes of cosmetic surgery before a patient ever goes under the knife. Crisalix uses virtual reality and augmented reality to generate a high-definition image of what a patient would look like after a procedure is performed. The software can perform an assessment of the particular body area to be treated and will also provide automated measurements so your provider has a precise overall picture of your face or body. Not just for surgical use, the potential results of other treatments like fillers or laser procedures can also be shown for a test run.

## How does Crisalix work?

Part of the great benefit of Crisalix is it can be used by the patient and the provider separately or together to view images together, and the provider can make adjustments in real time . Prior to a consultation appointment, you can set up a Crisalix account to get started with your assessment. Once you’ve created an account, you’ll be able to upload a few pictures for the software to use to generate the sample 3D images. When you’re signed in, you’ll have access to a wide variety of procedures you can “try on” and preview. Your provider can even have a virtual consultation with you and view your simulations together.

## How does Stone use Crisalix Imaging?

The most exciting thing about Crisalix is the 4D augmented reality feature which is used in-office. A tablet is connected to a large screen and your customized Crisalix information is brought up on the tablet. Then, the large screen can be used as a mirror to see what the cosmetic changes would look like in real life as you move and turn your face and body. This see-it-in-your-space feature is incredibly effective at giving patients a realistic representation of what the cosmetic possibilities are for them . As you view yourself on the screen, your provider can make tiny adjustments to further customize your potential results and get a clear, concise idea of what exactly your aesthetic goals are.

## Face

If you are interested in facial plastic surgery or non-surgical facial treatments, Crisalix can take images of your face and apply an individual procedure or multiple combined procedures. Your provider also has the ability to delicately fine-tune each part of your face to pinpoint exactly the look you’re going for. They can adjust your eyelids to be a bit more lifted, your lips a bit more plump, or your chin a bit more narrow. With Crisalix, you can preview procedures, including facelift, rhinoplasty, dermal fillers , lip augmentation, buccal fat removal , laser procedures, and more.

## Body

Body procedures can also be previewed with Crisalix . In the same way as with facial procedures, tiny details can be adjusted and simulated to see changes in even a large area of the body. The body can be virtually contoured and lifted, or skin can be smoothed for a dynamic look into what many different types of procedures can offer. See yourself with a neck lift, hair restoration, Morpheus 8, laser treatments, and so much more.
