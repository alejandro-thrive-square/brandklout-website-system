## Pricing Stone Surgical Arts

Fee Estimates

We frequently receive requests for fee estimates. Some surgeons refuse to

provide any estimate without a formal consultation; however some patients want to know a

ballpark figure to see if it is something that they can afford at present, which is a fair

request. We provide fee ranges for the specific reason that it is sometimes impossible to give a

precise fee without seeing the exact situation.

Depending upon the amount of aging or other factors, some procedures are

small, medium and large and therefore have a range. Other procedures, such as Botox or fillers

have a fixed fee, but may be lower when used in combination or with various promotions from the

manufacturers. For all these reasons, a fee range is presented. All surgical fees include

routine post op visits and follow up.
