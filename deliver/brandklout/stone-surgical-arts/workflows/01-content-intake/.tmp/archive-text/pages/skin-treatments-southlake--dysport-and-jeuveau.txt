What is Dysport?

Dysport injectable contains FDA-approved neurotoxins that help relax the muscles and smooth out the lines on your face without the need for any surgical procedure. It has been proven especially effective for moderate to severe glabellar lines that appear on your forehead and can become prominent even when your face is relaxed. Dysport’s unique formulation with a lower molecular mass is best used for those who want to treat a larger area, like the forehead. The result of this quick injection, administered at our state-of-the-art plastic surgery office, can usually be seen in as little as 24 hours and can last for up to five months without maintenance.

What is Jeuveau?

Jeuveau is the latest neurotoxin that was FDA-approved in 2019, and like Dysport, it is also formulated to treat undesired lines in your face that you can’t help but notice every time you look in the mirror. Its properties are meant solely for aesthetic purposes and help address the “11” lines or the moderate to severe vertical lines between the eyebrows . It is widely reported to be the fastest-acting neurotoxin with little to no side effects.

Aftercare and Results

## DYSPORT TREATMENT AFTERCARE AND RECOVERY

Immediately after injections with Jeuveau or Dysport, patients may experience some minor side effects like minimal redness, swelling, and bruising. These should improve after a few days. It is best to avoid consuming alcohol, touching your face, and exercising for the first 24 to 48 hours after each treatment to optimize Dysport aftercare and recovery. Rest assured, we will provide detailed aftercare instructions to ensure a smooth healing process. ## Dysport Frequently Asked Questions

## Are Dysport injections painful?

There may be some discomfort with the injections; however, topical numbing cream can be applied to minimize this. Afterward, you could feel some tenderness, and over-the-counter pain relievers can typically help resolve this.

## Do Dysport and Jeuveau injections come with any risks?

It is important to us to ensure your safety and reduce any risks associated with injectable treatments. We take care to thoroughly evaluate each patient to verify that they are a good candidate for any procedures we offer.

## Are the results of Dysport permanent? How often are follow-up treatments needed?

Jeuveau and Dysport are not permanent solutions to lines and wrinkles . However, when administered under the guidance of a professional, you can see optimal results for several months. We are happy to customize your treatment plan depending on your needs.

## Is Dysport safe if I am pregnant or breastfeeding?

It is recommended to avoid any neurotoxin injections, including Dysport and Jeuveau, if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

## Will I need to change my sleep position during recovery?

For the first two to three nights, you will want to sleep in a position that does not put any pressure on the areas that have received an injection.

## Will I be able to drive myself home after Dysport injections?

Since Dysport injections are non-invasive , you can resume normal activities afterward, such as driving.

## How much do treatments with Dysport cost?

The cost of Dysport and Jeuveau varies based on the number of areas treated and the specific injectable you receive. You can speak with our staff at your consultation to discuss payment options.
