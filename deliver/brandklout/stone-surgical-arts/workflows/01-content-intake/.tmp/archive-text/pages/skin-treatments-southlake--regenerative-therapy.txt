## PRP Treatment in Westlake, Texas

An exciting area of aesthetic dermatology, regenerative therapy marshals the body’s own mechanisms for tissue regrowth in the fight against a variety of cosmetic concerns including wrinkles, acne scarring, skin laxity, and hair loss.

Conveniently located to serve the Westlake, Texas, area, Stone Surgical Arts offers platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) treatments to dramatically reverse signs of aging by stimulating the regrowth of age-damaged skin cells. PRP and PRF treatments are also offered in combination with other non-surgical and surgical procedures to promote healing and boost results. Schedule a consultation to learn more about regenerative therapy with PRP or PRF and receive a customized treatment plan.

True Rejuvenation with PRP or PRF

## What is PRP Treatment?

Therapeutic application of stem cells

At Stone Surgical Arts, we offer platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) treatments, two forms of regenerative therapy that restore depleted skin cells using injections of your body’s own growth factors . Both procedures begin with a blood draw followed by the processing of the sample to isolate growth-factor-containing platelets and encourage the development of fibrin, a component that acts as a scaffold for the formation of new cells.

The growth-factor-rich solution is then injected into the skin to encourage cellular rejuvenation. A key difference between the two types of therapy is that PRP releases growth factors more quickly while PRF releases them over time. During your consultation, we can help you choose the best regenerative therapy or combination of therapies to meet your needs.

From Your Body. For Your Body.

## What are the benefits of PRP Treatment?

The beauty of PRP and PRF is that your body is activated to do the work of rebuilding tissues for you, providing stunning results.

Regenerative therapies including PRP and PRF treatments represent a more natural, state-of-the-art, and minimally-invasive approach to turning back the clock on skin aging .

PRP Treatment is administered via injection or microneedling, making it a very safe, non-surgical option for reducing signs of aging and improving the appearance of the skin. This is truly a case of medicine “working smarter” so that your body doesn’t have to work harder to recover from a more invasive procedure. Patients are amazed by the naturally beautiful lifting and rejuvenation provided by PRP and PRF regenerative therapy.

## How do PRP and PRF treatments work?

## Your PRP Treatment procedure

As the first step in a PRP or PRF treatment, you will have a blood draw. The collected blood will be processed to isolate a high concentration of natural growth factors like platelets and fibrin. Once the PRP or PRF solution is ready, the procedure site will be numbed with local anesthesia.

Then, the processed growth factor solution will be carefully injected at key points or delivered via microneedling throughout the treatment area. The injection process typically takes 20-30 minutes . Since general anesthesia is not needed when receiving PRP or PRF as a stand-alone treatment, you will be able to drive yourself home after your visit.

After your procedure

## PRF & PRP Treatment recovery

Following PRF or PRP Westlake patients usually recover in about two weeks. Some mild swelling or bruising is normal and will subside on its own. When PRP or PRF treatment is combined with another procedure, healing times will vary. You will be provided with detailed information about what to expect after treatment and how to care for your healing skin.

Daily improvement as you recover

## PRP Treatment results

When used as a skin rejuvenation treatment, the results of PRP or PRF injections will be visible within a few weeks of your procedure. These results will continue to improve over the next 1-2 months as your growth factors work on your tissues. The smoothing and lifting effects of regenerative therapy will last for 1-2 years. When used as a method for hair restoration , it may take 2-3 months for PRP results to take effect , which will then last for 1-2 years. Regenerative therapy treatments may be regularly repeated as needed.

## PRP Treatment Frequently Asked Questions

## How much does PRP cost?

The cost of your PRP or PRF treatment will depend on the type of treatment used and the number of injections needed. Call 817-918-3666 to schedule a consultation and learn more about pricing for your customized procedure.

## Do I need to have someone drive me home because of anesthesia?

When platelet-rich plasma (PRP) or platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) are used as stand-alone treatments, you will be able to drive yourself home after the procedure. If you are having PRP or PRF treatment performed at the same time as a procedure requiring general anesthesia or sedation, you will need someone to drive you home afterward.

## What's the difference between platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich plasma (PRF)?

The difference between these two treatments has to do with the way your blood sample is processed to collect a concentration of platelets and fibrin. Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) is created with the aid of an anticoagulant additive and releases its growth factor into the target tissue more quickly, while platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) does not contain any additives and provides a more slow-release approach to rebuilding cells.
