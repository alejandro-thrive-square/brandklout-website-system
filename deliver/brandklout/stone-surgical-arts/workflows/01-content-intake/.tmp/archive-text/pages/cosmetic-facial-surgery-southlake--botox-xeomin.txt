## Botox and Xeomin Treatments

Wrinkles can be made less visible through Botox and Xeomin treatments. This will help make the skin look smoother, younger, and more vibrant. A scheduled consultation with Dr. Mona Stone can yield more information about Botox and Xeomin treatments in Westlake.

## What Are Botox and Xeomin Treatments?

Botox and Xeomin injections are cosmetic correction options that address issues like wrinkles. These injectables are brands of botulinum toxin type A, which comes from the Clostridium botulinum bacterium.

These treatments combat fine wrinkles by relaxing overworked facial muscles. Over time, we form the same facial expressions and make the same facial movements over and over again. This causes stiffness in the muscles, making it harder for them to return to their at-rest positions. With Botox and Xeomin, the muscles are allowed to relax, letting the overlying skin lie flat again.

## Am I a Good Candidate for Botox and Xeomin Treatments?

For patients in need of smoothing out fine wrinkles and shaving a few years off their appearance, Botox and Xeomin treatments are excellent options. These neuromodulators effectively treat crow’s feet, vertical lines between the brows, horizontal forehead wrinkles, chin wrinkles, perioral lip lines, and more.

Patients must be in good health and would have to realistic expectations. Ideal candidates are not allergic to any of the ingredients present in both Botox and Xeomin.

## Your Botox and Xeomin Treatments Consultation

When patients come in for a Botox or Xeomin consultation, they will first be asked about their goals. The areas that need to have cosmetic corrections will be examined to ensure that they can be effectively addressed with a neuromodulator treatment.

Patients will also be asked about their medical history to ensure that there won’t be any complications resulting from the treatment. Patients can ask questions so that they can make informed choices. With this information, they can choose either Botox or Xeomin to address their wrinkles.

## Your Botox and Xeomin Treatments Session

Prior to the administration of Botox or Xeomin injections, the injection sites will be marked as a guide. The chosen injectable will then be delivered into the target areas.

After the injectable has been administered, patients will be given time to relax and allow the mixtures to do their work. Following a brief respite, patients can return to their everyday activities. Many patients get this procedure done during their lunch break and then go right back to work.

## Botox and Xeomin Treatment Aftercare

Patients of Botox and Xeomin treatments do not need any downtime or recovery time. During the next 24 hours of the treatment, they will be discouraged from putting any pressure on the affected areas. Rubbing or squeezing the spots where the mixtures were injected, for example, is highly discouraged.

## How Much Do Botox and Xeomin Treatments Cost?

The cost of Botox and Xeomin treatments varies depending on a few things. The choice of treatment will have a major impact on price, as will the number of units needed and the number of treatment sessions involved.
