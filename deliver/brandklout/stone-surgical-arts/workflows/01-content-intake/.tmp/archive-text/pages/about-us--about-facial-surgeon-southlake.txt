About Us Stone Surgical Arts

Dr. Mona Stone

Dr. Mona Stone is not only an esteemed oral and maxillofacial surgeon practicing full scope surgery, she is a public speaker, an activist, philanthropist and the cofounder to the grassroots organization Real Heroes Need Masks.

After receiving her dental degree from the University of Missouri-Kansas City, she went on to do a year of internship at Truman Medical Center and then a residency in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at Broward General Medical Center.

Dr. Stone practices the full scope of the specialty, including maxillofacial pathology, dentoalveolar surgery, implant surgery, maxillofacial reconstruction, microvascular surgery, facial cosmetic surgery and trauma surgery. She is also an attending surgeon at Baylor Medical Center Grapevine, Irving and at Methodist Hospital.

In addition to her role in the community, she is the mother of two beautiful toddlers, Max (1) and Sofia (3), an avid supporter of motor sports and Formula 1, and a TikTok creator and social media activist.

## Meet The Team

Brooke

Brooke is a Board-certified Family Nurse practitioner and advanced aesthetic injector with a background in dermatology.

She worked in the NICU for several years prior to receiving her Masters Degree. She is very detailed and a perfectionist. Brooke is passionate about creating natural looking results and making each client the best version of themselves. She is outgoing, positive, creative, and highly dedicated to aesthetics.

She loves learning and makes continuing education a priority. With over 6 years of injecting experience, she has trained multiple injectors and makes safety a priority.She is married with two girls (Tenley and Saylor) and enjoys spending time with her family and friends, traveling, swimming, and listening to country music.
