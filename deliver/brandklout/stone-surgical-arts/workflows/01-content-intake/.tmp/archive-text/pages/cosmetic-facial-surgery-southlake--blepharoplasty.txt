## REVEAL BRIGHTER, YOUNGER-LOOKING EYES WITH BLEPHAROPLASTY SURGERY IN WESTLAKE, TEXAS

Over time, the skin around the eyes may become droopy, saggy, or puffy, causing you to look older or more tired. At Stone Surgical Arts in Westlake, Texas, a blepharoplasty surgery (or eye lid lift surgery) restores smoother, tighter, younger-looking skin in the eye area . Blepharoplasty or eyelid surgery is more than just a medical procedure; it is a surgical art. Learn more about blepharoplasty and schedule your consultation with Dr. Mona Stone today.

Blepharoplasty: the “Eyelid Lift Surgery”

## WHAT IS BLEPHAROPLASTY SURGERY?

Blepharoplasty, also known as eyelid surgery, smooths and tightens the skin of the upper or lower eyelids, giving your eye area a refreshed, youthful appearance. Consider experiencing the transformative benefits of this procedure to rejuvenate your look and enhance your confidence.

Choosing a skilled blepharoplasty surgeon is crucial for achieving optimal eyelid surgery results. These professionals are experts in tailoring the procedure to meet your unique needs, ensuring that the outcome not only revitalizes your eye area but also looks natural and harmonious with your facial features. The successful results of blepharoplasty can significantly improve your visual appearance and self-esteem, making it a compelling option for those looking to turn back the clock on their eye aesthetics.

A more beautiful gaze

## WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF BLEPHAROPLASTY

Westlake eyelid blepharoplasty patients can expect to see the skin around their eyes look noticeably lifted, tighter, and more symmetrical.

Individuals experiencing obstructed vision as a result of drooping skin may regain a wider field of vision. Blepharoplasty typically takes one hour or less. Blepharoplasty recovery time is fairly straightforward, with most healing occurring in the first two weeks after surgery.

At Stone Surgical Arts, we offer a specialized local anesthetic that provides extended pain relief after going home, reducing the need for narcotic medications. Additionally, you will have the option of receiving a Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) or Platelet Rich Fibrin (PRF) treatment with your procedure to promote increased healing at the surgery site.

Look refreshed and alert

## AM I A GOOD CANDIDATE FOR BLEPHAROPLASTY?

As you age, you may notice changes in the skin around your eyes such as drooping, puffiness, crepey skin, or asymmetry. These changes can cause you to look tired or less alert, detracting from the natural beauty of your eyes. Individuals with hooded or heavy eyelids may be especially prone to drooping or lost elasticity. Blepharoplasty or eyelid surgery can be beneficial to people of any age who are experiencing excessive eyelid drooping, puffiness, asymmetry, or vision obstruction. If you would like to improve the tone and tautness of sagging skin around your eyes, you may be a good candidate for blepharoplasty.

Preparing for eyelid blepharoplasty involves key steps to ensure a successful eye lid lift surgery. It's important to consult with a qualified surgeon, understand the procedure, and follow pre-operative instructions, which may include medication adjustments. Arrange for post-surgery transportation and assistance, and set up a comfortable recovery area at home. By adequately preparing, you can facilitate a smoother recovery and enjoy the benefits of a more youthful and alert appearance.

## Your blepharoplasty procedure

## What to expect during blepharoplasty surgery

Blepharoplasty or also known as eyelid blepharoplasty is also a solution for individuals experiencing vision obstruction caused by sagging skin, fat, and muscle tissue. Typically performed as an outpatient surgery using local or general anesthesia, blepharoplasty takes about one hour. A local anesthetic will be applied to the surgery site. Stone Surgical Arts uses a specialized anesthetic that may provide pain relief for up to 40-72 hours post-surgery, reducing the need for stronger narcotic medications.

Depending on the location of the surgery site and the type of work to be performed, you may also be given general anesthesia. For blepharoplasty of the upper eyelids, a small incision is made where the skin naturally folds; excess skin, muscle, and fat is then removed and/or repositioned to achieve a smoother, more youthful appearance. Removal of tissue from the under-eye area is performed via a tiny incision near the lashes or just inside the lid, ensuring minimal scarring. You will have the option of receiving a Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) or Platelet Rich Fibrin (PRF) treatment to promote healing at the surgery site.

Healing Time

## BLEPHAROPLASTY RECOVERY

Blepharoplasty recovery and healing time varies, but most patients are able to return to normal activities within two weeks or less following a blepharoplasty. Temporary side effects may include bruising or mild swelling, tenderness, watery eyes, blurred vision from ointment applied for the procedure, light sensitivity, or double vision. Your surgical team will provide you with information about optional Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) and Platelet Rich Fibrin (PRF) treatments, which can be given during your procedure to encourage better blepharoplasty recovery. Aftercare instructions will include guidance for alleviating possible side effects.

A difference you can see

## Blepharoplasty Results

Eyelid lift surgery can dramatically smooth the skin around your eyes while maintaining the natural shape of your eye area and highlighting the beauty of your gaze. While non-surgical treatments may be a good fit for those with light eyelid wrinkles and sagging, blepharoplasty is the gold standard for treating moderate to severe drooping and puffiness, allowing for precision shaping and lasting results. Blepharoplasty patients report high levels of satisfaction with the results of their blepharoplasty procedures.

## Blepharoplasty FAQs Frequently Asked Questions

## Will it hurt?

In addition to local anesthesia, you may receive general anesthesia or a sedative to make you comfortable during your Blepharoplasty procedure. Your surgical team will work with you to determine the best method(s) of pain relief during and after your procedure.

## What are the risks?

Potential complications associated with blepharoplasty surgery and any surgery include the risk of infection and blood clots. In rare cases, follow-up surgery may be necessary.

## How long will my blepharoplasty results last?

Blepharoplasty results can last 10-15 years or more. Some patients may elect to repeat the procedure to correct subsequent sagging that can naturally occur over time.
