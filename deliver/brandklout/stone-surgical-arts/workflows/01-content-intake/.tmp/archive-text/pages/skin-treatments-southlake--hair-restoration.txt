## HAIR RESTORATION IN WESTLAKE & SOUTHLAKE TEXAS

Hair loss is a common condition affecting both men and women that may occur as a result of hereditary pattern balding, alopecia, hormone changes, various medical conditions, or a variety of other factors. Hair loss can be a distressing occurrence, but there are solutions.

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) treatments offer a state-of-the-art approach to promoting hair growth through cellular rejuvenation. Offered at Stone Surgical Arts, these regenerative therapies focus on hair restoration, using your own growth factor to stimulate dormant hair follicles. A far less invasive approach than surgery, regenerative therapy uses injections instead of transplanted grafts.

## WHAT IS HAIR RESTORATION TREATMENT?

Looking for a solution to hair loss? Finding the right approach can be confusing.

It can seem like there are as many products and procedures for this common complaint as there are reasons for hair loss. Both men and women can experience hereditary pattern balding. Hair loss may also occur in response to medication, stress, alopecia, hormonal changes, or tension placed on the follicles by styling.

At Stone Surgical Arts, we offer injectable regenerative therapies like platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) to address hair loss caused by a wide variety of factors. PRP and PRF treatments use growth hormones from your own blood supply to stimulate cell growth and activate dormant hair follicles. The effects of PRP and PRF hair transformation treatments will be visible within three months of your first treatment and will continue to improve as hair regrows naturally over time.

Turn back the clock on hair loss.

## WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF ADVANCED HAIR RESTORATION TECHNIQUES?

Hair loss can be a stressful occurrence that negatively affects your confidence and self-image.

Experience the rejuvenating benefits of advanced hair restoration techniques like platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) treatments, offering a minimally-invasive approach to regaining a youthful, vibrant appearance.

Following PRF or PRP hair restoration treatments , Westlake patients will be ready to return to normal activities right away. Hair regrowth takes place gradually as the follicles are stimulated back into action, with results becoming visible in about three months. Regenerative hair restoration therapy allows you to regrow your own hair without incisions or a lengthy recovery period, making it an ideal solution for countering any type of hair loss.

Hair loss affects men and women alike.

## Am I a good candidate for hair restoration?

Regenerative therapy is a minimally-invasive option for hair restoration that is safe for most people. During your consultation with one of our aesthetic specialists at Stone Surgical Arts, you will discuss your medical history, hair loss symptoms, and cosmetic goals to ensure that platelet-rich plasma (PRP) or platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) treatments are right for you. You will also learn about the differences between PRP and PRF so that your treatment plan can be customized to your unique needs. Men and women suffering hair loss as a result of genetics, illness, or any other reason may be good candidates for regrowing hair with regenerative therapy.

## What to expect during treatment

## Your hair restoration procedure

The growth-factor-rich solution used in our non-surgical hair restoration procedure, derived from platelet-rich plasma (PRP) or platelet-rich fibrin (PRF), begins with a blood draw to isolate natural growth factors. The sample is then placed in a centrifuge and/or mixed with an anticoagulant agent in a process designed to isolate the highest percentage of natural growth factors.

Once the growth-factor-rich solution is ready, your provider will use a syringe to inject it at key points in areas of hair loss. An anesthetic cream may be applied to ensure your comfort during this process, which typically takes around 15 minutes.

The entire hair restoration procedure will take about an hour, including the time required to process your blood sample. Because no general anesthesia is required for this minimally-invasive approach, you will be able to drive yourself home and resume most normal activities right away. Approximately four sessions, repeated at intervals, may be required to achieve optimal results.

Regenerative hair restoration aftercare

## Hair Restoration Recovery

There is little to no downtime associated with a platelet-rich plasma (PRP) or platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) treatment for hair loss. The injection sites may feel sore, but pain and swelling is generally mild and resolves quickly. You will be able to shower and wash your hair soon after the procedure. Your aesthetic provider will provide detailed instructions on how to care for the treatment area. Results take about three months to become visible as the cells and follicles of the treatment area are rebuilt and stimulated into action.

Fuller, thicker hair for restored confidence

## Hair Restoration Results

Although regenerative therapy does not yield instantaneous results, many Westlake hair restoration patients enjoy the gradual, natural-looking hair regrowth process enabled by injections of platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF). After your first treatment, your cells will be stimulated to regrow “behind the scenes,” a process that eventually activates the follicles to produce new hair growth. You can expect to see results in about three months, which will continue to improve with repeated sessions. Clinical trials have shown a marked increase in retention of follicles and new follicle growth following PRP/PRF treatments. Patients love the natural-looking hair regrowth made possible by PRP/PRF therapy and the confidence boost that comes with it.

## Hair Restoration Frequently Asked Questions

## Will it hurt?

Regenerative platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) therapy is a minimally-invasive treatment that uses injections of growth factors from your own blood supply to stimulate hair growth. Numbing cream will be used to minimize the discomfort of the injections; pain and discomfort are minimal compared to more invasive procedures and will resolve quickly after the procedure.

## What are the risks?

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) are derived from your own blood supply, so the risk of an adverse reaction is extremely low. Because PRP/PRF therapy is not recommended for individuals with certain conditions that could impair healing, your provider will review your medical history to ensure that regenerative therapy can be performed safely.

## What's the difference between platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) therapy?

The difference between platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) has to do with how your blood sample is processed to isolate platelets and fibrin. PRP is derived with the aid of an anticoagulant additive and releases its growth factor into the target tissue more quickly; PRF does not contain any additives and provides a more slow-release approach to rebuilding cells.
