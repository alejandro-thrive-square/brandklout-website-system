## NECK LIPOSUCTION CONVENIENTLY LOCATED IN WESTLAKE, TEXAS

No matter how hard you work out, some body parts are just next to impossible to trim down. The neck, in particular, is an area resistant to weight loss and exercise. If you are bothered by heavier fat distribution on the neck that weighs down your overall appearance, neck liposuction may be the solution. Neck liposuction targets the neck, creating a slimmer, more elegant, and elongated profile and allowing the chin to appear more sculpted and shapely. Dr. Mona Stone of Stone Surgical Arts in Westlake, Texas, can customize a surgical approach tailored to your goals and unique facial structure.

Following neck liposuction, patients embark on a recovery process that is meticulously planned to ensure comfort and optimal healing. Dr. Stone and her team provide comprehensive guidance on neck liposuction recovery, including personalized aftercare instructions. This dedicated approach helps minimize downtime and maximizes the aesthetic outcomes, allowing patients to enjoy a reduction in neck fat and a more refined neck contour swiftly and safely.

Targeted contouring for a common problem area

## WHAT IS NECK LIPOSUCTION?

Plastic surgery neck liposuction provides targeted removal of fat tissue from the neck to create a more slender, contoured neck shape.

The procedure is called neck liposculpture when liposuction is combined with treatments like heat or ultrasound therapy to provide more refined tightening and shaping. Neck liposuction surgery can reduce the appearance of a weak or double chin and whittle down the neck for a slimmer appearance. This minimally invasive surgery is performed in our surgical center with local anesthesia or intravenous sedation. Dr. Stone brings an artist’s eye and surgeon’s skill to every neck liposuction procedure, carefully shaping the neck for stunning results.

The neck liposuction procedure is meticulously designed to ensure safety and comfort, emphasizing precise fat removal for a significantly more defined neck line. Patients benefit from Dr. Stone's expertise in neck liposuction surgery, experiencing transformative outcomes that enhance both their profile and confidence.

A More Youthful-Looking Face & Body

## WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF NECK FAT LIPOSUCTION?

A thick neck or “double chin” can cause you to look heavier than you are and drag down the overall appearance of your face and upper body.

Removal of fat from the neck or under-chin area creates a more athletic, graceful silhouette, highlighting the natural beauty of all of your features. Neck liposuction surgery is performed as an outpatient procedure, with full recovery taking approximately two weeks. A small incision is accessed via a surgical cannula, or tube, which is used to suction away excess fat deposits. Depending on your cosmetic goals, other facial rejuvenation treatments, such as procedures to tighten the skin or augment the chin, may be performed in the same session as neck liposuction. If you are bothered by a thick or heavy neck area, neck liposuction can help you obtain a more sculpted neck and accentuated jawline, enhancing your entire appearance.

Eliminate your double chin.

## HOW DO I KNOW IF I AM A CANDIDATE FOR NECK LIPOSUCTION PROCEDURE?

Often, patients struggle with feeling like their face makes them look heavier than they are. In many cases, weight loss does not diminish fat in and around the face, so some patients require a minimally-invasive procedure or neck liposuction surgery for best results. Neck liposuction allows for contouring of this common problem area. If you have fat underneath the chin that creates the appearance of a weak chin, then you may be a good candidate for neck liposuction.

Neck liposuction is considered a safe procedure for patients who are in good general health. Neck liposuction results are permanent, although later weight gain could cause changes in the shape of the neck. For this reason, neck liposuction will be most effective for individuals who are at a stable weight. If some skin tightening will be required, then certain liposuction techniques can be used like heat or ultrasound energy. Dr. Stone will assess your goals and medical history to make sure that facial liposculpture is your best option.

## What happens during neck liposuction?

## What to expect during your procedure

Facial liposculpture uses liposuction to remove excess fatty tissue present in the face to recontour the profile and reshape the overall face. Your Neck Liposuction procedure will be performed in Dr. Mona Stone‘s surgery center located in Westlake, TX. This procedure can be performed under local anesthesia or IV sedation. Then, small incisions are made so that a surgical cannula can be inserted for fat removal with a surgical vacuum. Westlake neck liposuction provider, Dr. Stone, will ensure that the proper amount of fat is removed in order to create the right contours for your profile and goals. In many cases, additional facial rejuvenation procedures can be performed at the same time as a buccal fat removal. For example, heat or ultrasound energy can also be used to help tighten the skin of the neck.

Stone Surgical Arts offers platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) and platelet-rich plasma (PRP) treatments at the time of your surgery to optimize the healing process. You will be provided with detailed instructions on caring for the surgery site and should be able to return to full activity levels within two weeks.

Healing from your facial liposuction procedure

## WHAT CAN I EXPECT FROM NECK LIPOSUCTION RECOVERY?

Following neck liposuction Westlake patients are usually required to wear a supporting chin strap. This needs to be worn for approximately three to four days after treatment to promote optimal recovery. Patients may experience a little bruising and swelling after a neck liposuction procedure, but this is completely normal and will resolve within two weeks of the surgery. Patients can get back to work within a week of the neck liposuction surgery and can resume their daily/regular activities within two weeks. The neck liposuction results are permanent. To preserve the quality of their results, it is recommended that the patient maintain their current weight. Results will be fully visible as soon as swelling has diminished.

An outpatient procedure that delivers permanent effects

Plastic surgery neck liposuction creates a tighter, more athletic and elegant-looking neck. Maintaining a stable weight helps preserve the results of your procedure, which can last for life. The sculpting effects of neck liposuction help provide balance to your face and highlight the beauty of your natural features. Patients tend to be highly satisfied with the long-lasting results that can be achieved through this minimally-invasive outpatient procedure.

## Neck Liposuction FAQs Frequently Asked Questions

## Will I have loose skin after neck liposuction surgery?

While some patients can undergo neck liposuction alone, with impeccable results, those with significant localized fat and skin laxity may require combined treatment to achieve their most sculpted, youthful look.

## Is neck liposuction the same as a neck lift?

Neck liposuction removes excess fat, while a neck lift involves the removal of excess skin and often includes neck liposuction for superior results.
