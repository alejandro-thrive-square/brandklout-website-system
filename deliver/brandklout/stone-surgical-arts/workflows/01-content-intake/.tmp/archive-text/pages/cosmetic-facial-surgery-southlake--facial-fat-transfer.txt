Smooth and rejuvenate the face using your own tissues

## WHAT IS FACIAL FAT TRANSFER SURGERY?

Facial Fat Transfer is a type of surgical procedure that involves harvesting small amounts of extra fat from one part of the body and injecting it into a different area in order to augment, plump, or reshape the target area. Acclaimed oral and maxillofacial surgeon Dr. Mona Stone specializes in using Facial Fat Transfer to smooth, shape, and rejuvenate facial skin, minimizing signs of aging or scarring.

Facial fat transfer recovery is a critical phase following the procedure, where patients may experience swelling and bruising, but with proper care, these symptoms typically subside within a few weeks, revealing the final, natural-looking results. To ensure the best outcomes, it's essential to choose among the best facial fat transfer surgeons, like Dr. Mona Stone, who are renowned for their expertise and innovative techniques in this field. The facial fat transfer procedure itself is meticulously performed, with fat being carefully harvested and then injected into the facial areas requiring enhancement.

Long-lasting, natural results

## WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF A FACIAL FAT TRANSFER?

Facial fat transfer makes it possible to enjoy the benefits of dermal fillers using your own tissues to provide long-lasting smoothing and toning. Once the transferred fatty tissue is established, results are permanent. Facial fat transfer to the face can be used to plump out deep creases and crow’s feet, lift depressed acne scars, and smooth away the appearance of sagging jowls.

Furthermore, understanding the facial fat transfer recovery time is crucial for patients considering this procedure. Typically, the recovery time after facial fat transfer varies among individuals, but most patients observe significant improvement in swelling and bruising within the first few weeks. A detailed facial fat transfer recovery day by day guide provided by the surgeon can help patients navigate the healing process smoothly, ensuring they are well-informed about what to expect at each stage. This guidance is vital for achieving the best possible results and comfort during the recovery phase.

## Your Facial Fat Transfer procedure

## What to expect

The duration of your procedure will depend on the areas to be addressed. During your consultation for Facial Fat Transfer, Dr. Stone will determine the best site for fat extraction and create a plan for transferring the tissue to key areas of the face to create a smooth, lifted appearance.

The facial fat transfer procedure itself consists of three steps, all of which can be performed at our modern Westlake, Texas, office, Stone Surgical Arts. First, fat will be taken from the collection site identified for the procedure using a needle. The fat will then be processed and purified in preparation for the transfer. Finally, a small needle will be used to inject fat into creases or depressed areas in the face in the same manner as dermal fillers. A numbing cream may be applied for your comfort during the procedure.

After your procedure

## FACIAL FAT TRANSFER RECOVERY

After the procedure, it is normal to see some swelling and bruising around the injection areas. Westlake Facial Fat Transfer patients may experience swelling which typically resolves within a few days to a week, but it is possible for mild residual swelling to last longer. Pain medication is generally not required but may be prescribed if needed. During your procedure, extra fat may be injected to compensate for the natural reabsorption of some of the transferred tissue into the body. Depending on the level of reabsorption, it is occasionally necessary to perform a second procedure to achieve optimal results.

During the facial fat transfer recovery, closely following your surgeon’s guidance ensures the best outcome. Opting for the best facial fat transfer surgeons is crucial, as their expertise significantly influences the recovery process and final results, highlighting the importance of selecting a top professional for this delicate procedure.

Possible complications are rare and include tissue bulging, edema, and infection.
