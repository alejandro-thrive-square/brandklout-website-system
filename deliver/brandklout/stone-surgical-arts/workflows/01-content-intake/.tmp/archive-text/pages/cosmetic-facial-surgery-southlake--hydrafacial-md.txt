HydraFacial MD® Westlake TX

Hydrafacial is a gentle but very effective treatment to clear your pores and rejuvenate your skin. If you’re just starting out on your skincare journey, a Hydrafacial in Westlake at Stone Surgical Arts is a great place to start!

Why have a HydraFacial?

HydraFacials are great for all skin types, but they are especially beneficial for those with oily or acne-prone skin, as they help to unclog pores and reduce oiliness. In addition, HydraFacial can help to diminish the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles and nourish aging or dry skin, leaving it deeply cleansed and hydrated.

Beyond their immediate cleansing and hydrating effects, HydraFacial treatments offer long-term benefits that contribute to the overall health and resilience of the skin. Regular HydraFacial sessions can lead to a noticeable improvement in skin texture and tone, making it an ideal addition to any skincare routine. The versatility of the HydraFacial treatment allows it to address a range of concerns, from reducing hyperpigmentation and evening out skin tone to enhancing skin elasticity and firmness. With its ability to tailor to individual skin needs, the HydraFacial benefits extend beyond mere aesthetic improvements, promoting healthier skin that feels as good as it looks.

Can a HydraFacial rejuvenate and restore your glow?

A HydraFacial is a three-step process that cleanses, exfoliates, and extracts impurities from your skin. This is followed by a customized chemical peel, hydration, antioxidants, and a choice of corrective booster serums that can target a variety of skin issues. The whole process takes about 45 minutes, and there is no downtime or side effects – just healthy, glowing, hydrated skin. It is a perfect treatment to do before an event.

Following the HydraFacial MD treatment, proper aftercare enhances the lasting effects of this rejuvenating process. Clients are advised on a personalized aftercare regimen, which typically includes gentle skincare products and sun protection to maintain the skin's hydration and protect it from environmental damage. The immediate radiance and smoothness post-treatment make it an ideal choice for anyone looking to present their best self at any event, with advice on HydraFacial aftercare ensuring that your skin remains in peak condition long after leaving the clinic.

What does a hydrafacial do?

- The treatment begins with our medical aesthetician cleansing your skin, then exfoliating using the Hydrafacial’s vortex technology, getting rid of excess oil, impurities and dead skin cells.

- Hydrafacial then replenishes your skin with antioxidants, peptides, and hyaluronic acid.

- Antioxidants fortify cells by trapping and containing free radicals and boosting cell function.

- Peptides — links of amino acids — boost collagen and elastin production, reducing the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles while stimulating the body’s natural antioxidant response.

- Hyaluronic Acid — naturally produced by the body — gives skin its volume and fullness.

- HydraFacial MD’s low molecular weight Hyaluronic Acid plumps and deeply hydrates the skin.
