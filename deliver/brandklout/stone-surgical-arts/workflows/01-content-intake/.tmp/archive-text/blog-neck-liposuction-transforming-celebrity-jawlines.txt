Appearance plays a significant role in a celebrity’s career and public image. It’s why cosmetic procedures are par for the course in Hollywood. Plastic surgery is always associated with female celebrities. But now more male stars are embracing it too.
Neck liposuction has seen a surge in popularity in recent years. This is due to how it enhances jawlines and facial profiles.
A neck liposuction procedure removes excess fat from under the chin and neck. It’s popular with men seeking a more defined and youthful look. Several notable male celebrities have reportedly undergone this procedure.
