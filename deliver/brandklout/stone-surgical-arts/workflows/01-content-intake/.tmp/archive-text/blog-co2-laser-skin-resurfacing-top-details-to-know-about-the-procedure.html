CO2 Laser Skin Resurfacing: Top Details to Know About the Procedure

December 14, 2024

Age doesn’t need to come with fine lines and wrinkles. Many cosmetic procedures can rejuvenate the skin and make it look and feel younger.

One of the more popular skin treatments is CO2 laser skin resurfacing. This cutting-edge cosmetic procedure improves the texture and tone of the skin. It can reduce fine lines and remove scars. The treatment can also clear up sun damage. Here are three key things you should know about the procedure.

The Secret is in the Dual Action

This latest skin treatment uses CO2 lasers to remove the damaged outer layers of the skin. The targeted light beams also stimulate collagen production in the deeper skin layer. The dual action works to reveal fresh, smoother, and firmer skin.

Dermatologists can also tailor the treatment to the patient. They can adjust the intensity of the laser based on skin type. The procedure is also customizable to focus on specific skin issues.

It Has a Longer Recovery Period

You might need time off if you get this powerful treatment. The recovery time for a CO2 laser skin resurfacing procedure can run for two weeks. Patients reported redness, swelling, and peeling for 7 to 10 days. It also took months before they saw the full results.

It’s Ideal for People with Serious Skin Issues

The CO2 laser skin treatment is great for those with moderate to serious skin problems. The treatment can handle deep wrinkles, uneven skin texture, and extensive sun damage.

The procedure isn’t ideal for patients with darker skin because it could change skin pigmentation. Talk to your dermatologist to see if you’re a good candidate.
