3 Surprising Celebrities Who Have Had Liposuction
July 17, 2024
Body transformations due to cosmetic surgery always attract attention. It's especially true in Hollywood. People are always curious about celebrities who have had liposuction.
Botox and fillers are quite common. Fans even expect it from their favorite actors and actresses. Liposuction is a different matter though. Many stars deny or don’t talk about any nips or tucks they’ve done.
It’s easy to see why many people don’t believe these denials. Social media has made it easier to see any marked changes in a celebrity’s body. Let’s look at these three examples.
Kim Kardashian
Kim Kardashian has always claimed she never had anything done on her face. Except for a little Botox and eyelash extensions.
There have been reports Kardashian had liposuction after she gave birth. Celebrity watchers believe it’s behind her rapid 17-pound weight loss.
Experts are also saying Kardashian’s famous hourglass figure had a little help. They say liposuction helped to sculpt and shape some regions.
Dwayne Johnson
It’s hard to miss Dwayne Johnson even in a crowd. The popular wrestler turned Hollywood star has a commanding presence. His height and sculpted physique draw attention.
Many people say liposuction has helped enhance The Rock’s physical attributes. The actor reportedly had gynecomastia surgery done back in 2005. The procedure removes excess fatty tissue on a man’s chest. Experts claimed Johnson did this because his chest grew too big due to wrestling.
Mariah Carey
Stars who gained back their pre-pregnancy body are always talked about. And many credit celebrity liposuction for this.
That was the case when Mariah Carey was quick to bounce back after giving birth to her twins. The singer shed 70 pounds six months after welcoming her children. The superstar credited it to the Jenny Craig diet. But many speculate the Grammy-award winner underwent liposuction.
