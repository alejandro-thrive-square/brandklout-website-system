People have been looking for ways to improve themselves for centuries. One way they’ve been doing this is through plastic surgery.
Plastic surgery is a specialized branch of surgery. It focuses on reconstructing or restoring the human body. These procedures also alter the human body and make it better. There are two main categories of plastic surgery – reconstructive and cosmetic.
Plastic surgery has been around for more than 4,000 years. It’s one of the world’s oldest healing arts. Historical records show that doctors in India have been doing skin grafts since 800 BC. Pioneering doctors continued to build on this knowledge until today.
There’s a vast range of procedures that people can choose from. This post provides an overview of the various procedures we offer at Stone Surgical Arts.
Jaw Surgery
Orthognathic surgery may be necessary due to congenital abnormalities, trauma, or growth disturbances. Some patients may seek jaw surgery for cosmetic reasons to achieve a more sculpted or v-shaped jawline.
This corrective surgery may require several phases to fully improve the patient’s appearance as well as the function of chewing, speaking, and breathing.
Facial Trauma
This type of face surgery can also correct issues from birth defects or trauma. It also helps relieve breathing problems. Motor vehicle accidents, accidental falls, sports injuries, violence, or work-related injuries may cause facial trauma. Recovery time and subsequent treatments, surgical or otherwise, will depend on the severity of the trauma.
Liposuction
Liposuction removes excess fat deposits from certain areas of the body.  A Westlake cosmetic surgeon will remove fats from the patient’s abdomen, thighs, and arms. The procedure is also done to help contour the body and improve its proportions.
Liposuction can remove stubborn fat pockets. That’s a boon for the millions of people who have tried all types of diet and exercise to no avail. That’s why liposuction is one of the most popular types of cosmetic surgery.
Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty)
Blepharoplasty rejuvenates the appearance of the eyes. A surgeon will remove the excess skin, fat, and muscle from the eyelids. This corrects drooping upper lids. It also reduces puffy bags below the eyes. Eyelid surgery can give an individual a more youthful and refreshed look.
Jaw Surgery
A tummy tuck is another popular procedure. Data shows that there are around 140,000 abdominoplasty surgeries in the US every year.
This surgery removes excess skin and fat from the abdomen. The surgeon also tightens the underlying muscles. Tummy tucks are especially popular among women who have given birth. It’s also a common procedure done by people who have experienced significant weight loss. An abdominoplasty gives them a firmer and flatter abdomen.
Facelift
The pursuit of a wrinkle-free face is universal. It’s also the driving force behind facelifts. This type of face surgery reduces signs of aging by tightening sagging skin. Surgeons can also smoothen deep folds and lift facial tissues.
A facelift is a comprehensive procedure that addresses many areas of the face and neck. It also provides dramatic anti-aging results. It’s why facelifts are one of the most beloved types of cosmetic surgery.
Dermal Fillers
Dermal fillers are not considered a surgical procedure. But they are very popular for their ability to restore volume and smooth wrinkles. Fillers can also enhance facial contours.
These are injectable treatments. They offer quick results with minimal downtime. This makes them a favorite of people looking for a subtle beauty treatment. They also love fillers because of their big impact.
