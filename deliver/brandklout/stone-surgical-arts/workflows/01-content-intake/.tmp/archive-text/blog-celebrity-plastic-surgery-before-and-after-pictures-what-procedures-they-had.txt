It’s hard to quantify the impact of plastic surgery. It’s a billion-dollar industry. Experts say the market will grow to $59.77 billion in 2024.
Celebrities have always had a big impact on cosmetic surgery, for good or bad. Their influence can set trends.
Many celebrities are normalizing plastic surgery. They do this by having an open discussion of the procedures they have followed. Most have shared the good and bad side of their experience. The following celebrity plastic surgery before and after pictures reinforce their stories.
Bella Hadid
Bella Hadid revealed that she had a nose job when she was young. The fashion model had the procedure done when she was 14 and regretted it. Hadid said she believed she would have grown into her nose.
Hadid denied that she had gotten lip fillers or had an eye lift. She also said she never had her jaw shaved. The model pointed out that people’s faces change. She said that how one looks at 13 isn't how they'll look today.
Chrissy Teigen
This model is always on the list of celebrity plastic surgery before and after pictures. Teigen is very open about why she’s in that rarefied group. She admitted to undergoing a buccal fat removal procedure. She also had three breast surgeries. Teigen also had liposuction.
The TV personality says these procedures made her feel more confident. Teigen also described getting cosmetic surgery as one of the dumbest things she ever did. But she says she has no regrets.
Khloe Kardashian
Khloe Kardashian is no stranger to the controversies around plastic surgery. The reality star says people often think she’s had several face transplants. She claims she only had one nose job.
The media personality also had facial fillers. She says she had them dissolved because it changed her face. Kardashian admits to feeling scared of getting fillers now because of the experience.
