What are the Top Types of Plastic Surgery in the World?
September  5, 2024
People have been looking for ways to improve themselves for centuries. One way they’ve been doing this is through plastic surgery.
Plastic surgery is a specialized branch of surgery. It focuses on reconstructing or restoring the human body. These procedures also alter the human body and make it better. There are two main categories of plastic surgery – reconstructive and cosmetic.
Plastic surgery has been around for more than 4,000 years. It’s one of the world’s oldest healing arts. Historical records show that doctors in India have been doing skin grafts since 800 BC. Pioneering doctors continued to build on this knowledge until today.
Plastic surgery is now a $56 billion industry. There’s a vast range of procedures that people can choose from. This post provides an overview of today's most popular types of plastic surgery.
Breast Augmentation
Breast augmentation is reportedly the top cosmetic procedure done worldwide. There were 2.2 million procedures in 2022 alone. That's according to the International Society of Aesthetic Plastic Surgery.
The procedure uses implants or fat transfer to increase the size of a woman’s breasts. It’s also done to reconstruct or improve their shape. Women often choose this surgery to enhance their appearance. It’s also done to help restore lost breast volume after weight reduction or pregnancy.
Rhinoplasty
Many people know rhinoplasty as a nose job. It’s done to reshape the nose to improve its appearance or function.
This type of face surgery can also correct issues from birth defects or trauma. It also helps relieve breathing problems.
Liposuction
Liposuction
removes excess fat deposits from certain areas of the body.  A Westlake plastic surgeon will remove fats from the patient’s abdomen, thighs, and arms. The procedure is also done to help contour the body and improve its proportions.
Liposuction can remove stubborn fat pockets. That’s a boon for the millions of people who have tried all types of diet and exercise to no avail. That’s why liposuction is one of the most popular types of plastic surgery.
Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty)
Blepharoplasty
rejuvenates the appearance of the eyes. A surgeon will remove the excess skin, fat, and muscle from the eyelids. This corrects drooping upper lids. It also reduces puffy bags below the eyes. Eyelid surgery can give an individual a more youthful and refreshed look.
Tummy Tuck (Abdominoplasty)
A tummy tuck is another popular procedure. Data shows that there are around 140,000 abdominoplasty surgeries in the US every year.
This surgery removes excess skin and fat from the abdomen. The surgeon also tightens the underlying muscles. Tummy tucks are especially popular among women who have given birth. It’s also a common procedure done by people who have experienced significant weight loss. An abdominoplasty gives them a firmer and flatter abdomen.
Facelift
The pursuit of a wrinkle-free face is universal. It’s also the driving force behind facelifts. This type of face surgery reduces signs of aging by tightening sagging skin. Surgeons can also smoothen deep folds and lift facial tissues.
A facelift is a comprehensive procedure that addresses many areas of the face and neck. It also provides dramatic anti-aging results. It’s why facelifts are one of the most beloved
types of plastic surgery
.
Dermal Fillers
Dermal fillers are not considered a surgical procedure. But they are very popular for their ability to restore volume and smooth wrinkles. Fillers can also enhance facial contours.
These are injectable treatments. They offer quick results with minimal downtime. This makes them a favorite of people looking for a subtle beauty treatment. They also love fillers because of their big impact.
Botox Injections
Botox injections
are another non-surgical option. They’re also very popular. These injections can reduce the appearance of facial wrinkles. It’s a temporary solution though.
Botox relaxes the underlying muscles. It smooths out lines and prevents the formation of new ones. These injections are the go-to treatment for those who want a youthful appearance.
