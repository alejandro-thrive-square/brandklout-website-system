It’s all about the chin these days. Stars like Jenna Ortega and Lily-Rose Depp have made people want to have defined chins. The desire to have a jawline like supermodel Bella Hadid has made chin sculpting popular.
Chin sculpting and reshaping are minimal to non-invasive procedures. They enhance a person’s profile. Many celebrities have had chin liposuction and other chin surgeries. Most have kept mum on what they had done, but some are brave enough to talk about it. Here are some chin sculpting & reshaping celebrities.
Blac Chyna
https://i.redd.it/jdhtjvtmpiqa1.jpg
Blac Chyna is one celebrity who’s been open about various cosmetic procedures she did. The “Keeping Up with the Kardashians” alum admitted to getting a chin augmentation.
Chyna revealed she had chin liposuction because she wanted a more defined jawline. The entrepreneur said it was part of her body transformation journey. The chin sculpting did give her a sharper, more sculpted look.
Jane Fonda
https://www.sieberplasticsurgery.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/jane-fonda-before-after-plastic-surgery-1.jpg
Fonda is famous for her candidness about plastic surgery. The acting legend admitted she had work done on her chin and neck. She also revealed that she also had the bags under her eyes removed.
The Oscar-winning actress said she’s happy with the cosmetic procedures she did. Fonda says she now looks like how she feels because of them.
Sia
https://img.buzzfeed.com/buzzfeed-static/static/2023-12/11/23/campaign_images/441aa4632cbb/sia-revealed-why-she-recently-got-liposuction-as--3-6591-1702337241-0_dblbig.jpg
Sia is famous for her unique voice and wigs that cover her entire face. The Australian is also known for admitting to getting cosmetic procedures. She revealed she got a liposuction on her double chin.
There were some concerns that the chin surgery might affect her singing. All’s well though as Sia is still a phenomenal singer. She said the procedure also made her more confident.
