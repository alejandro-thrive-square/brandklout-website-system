In the past, many people viewed celebrities getting cosmetic procedures as negative. They may feel that it takes away the stars’ natural beauty and makes them feel manufactured. One of these cosmetic procedures criticized is a celebrity boob job.
Today, many celebrities who had breast surgery spoke up. They share their stories on their decision. Their stories may go beyond the bright spotlight. They sparked a real conversation with the public. Boob jobs are often tied to health or societal pressure. Let's talk about celebrities before and after boob job.
One of Hollywood's best is Angelina Jolie. She shared in an opinion article for the New York Times the need for her to get a celebrity boob job. She needed a double boob job.
Jolie found out she had a BRCA1 gene mutation. It raised her chances of breast and ovarian cancer. Jolie’s family has a history of ovarian cancer. Her mother had it. It influenced her to get a celebrity boob job. She got it to reduce the effect of the gene.
Jolie has been open about her health. It has inspired many people to consult their doctors about their possible predispositions.
Another Academy-Award-winning actress, Kathy Bates, possesses the same courage.
Her peers celebrated her for her versatility in theater, TV, and film. Bates also had a celebrity boob job in 2012. After beating ovarian cancer in the early 2000s, Bates chose to have a double mastectomy. This was to reduce the spread of her breast cancer.
Bates has since become an advocate for breast cancer awareness. She encourages her fellow women to ask their doctors about their health. This is especially important near menopause.
Shannen Doherty won the audience’s hearts with her acting skills. Doherty starred in many iconic TV shows and films, such as 90210 and Heathers.
To battle her breast cancer, Doherty elected to have a celebrity boob job. She had a single mastectomy in 2012. Her cancer came back in 2019 after a period of remission. She has shared her emotional journey of undergoing chemotherapy. She discusses the obstacles she encounters in battling the disease.
Doherty proves she is resilient despite her diagnosis. Many wish for her full recovery.
For celebrities before and after boob job, life is never the same.
