LeAnn Rimes and Her TMJ Disorder: How She Lived with It

October 21, 2024

LeAnn Rimes is famous for her powerhouse vocals that gave her a Grammy at 14. She’s also known for her candid approach to her battle with TMJ disorder. It's devastating to someone who makes a living off her voice but Rimes handled it with grace. Her journey has put a spotlight on a condition that millions suffer from.

Temporomandibular Joint Dysfunction is a condition that affects the jaw and facial muscles. It impacts the temporomandibular joint, which connects the jaw to the skull. This joint is critical in everyday activities, like chewing, talking, and yawning.

A damaged or misaligned temporomandibular joint causes pain and stiffness. You’ll have a hard time moving your jaw.

People with TMJ often experience symptoms like jaw pain or tenderness. They also hear clicking or popping sounds when opening or closing their mouth. They could also have problems chewing or speaking. Headaches, earaches, or facial pain are also symptoms of this disorder. Rimes once revealed that her whole head was pounding because of the pain in her jaw.

Rimes has also been open about how the disorder affected her career and personal life. She said that the condition has forced her to postpone or cancel performances. Rimes once had to leave her concert early because her jaw popped out.

The singer has taken proactive measures to manage the disorder. Rimes says she meditates to help reduce tension in her jaw. She also made changes to her diet. She’s eating more soft food to prevent straining her jaw. The “How Do I Live” singer also had jaw surgery to correct her TMJ.
