Famous People with Sleep Apnea and What They Did to Handle the Disorder
December 3, 2024
Sleep apnea is a common yet serious health issue. It causes breathing to stop and start again and again during slumber. These interruptions occur throughout the night and result in poor sleep quality. Sleep apnea could also cause serious health complications, like stroke and heart disease.
People should watch out for certain symptoms. Loud snoring, gasping, or choking during sleep are signs of sleep apnea. People with this disorder could also experience morning headaches, drowsiness, and daytime fatigue. They can also exhibit mood swings. Sleep apnea affects millions around the world, including some celebrities. Here are some famous people with sleep apnea and what they did to handle it.
Shaquille O’Neal

Athletes are often the epitome of good health, so hearing O’Neal has sleep apnea is surprising. He experienced years of restless nights and morning tiredness before his diagnosis. The NBA legend took proactive steps to manage his sleep apnea. He joined a sleep study using a Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (CPAP) machine. O’Neal is a strong advocate for sleep health. He encourages others to seek medical advice if they think they have sleep apnea.
William Shatner
https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5529f0c3e4b01f43e14adb5b/1497630968609-8Q53SRLBMASSVU75LGH8/William+Shatner
The beloved “Star Trek” star also uses a CPAP machine to combat sleep apnea. Shatner credits it for improving his sleep quality and general health. He also says people should treat sleep disorders the same way they do serious diseases.
Joe Biden

The 46th US president is using a CPAP machine to treat sleep apnea. Sleep experts say the president’s age and gender are two risk factors that worked against him. But they also pointed out that many young women have sleep apnea. Weight is also a risk factor, with obese people more prone to developing sleep apnea. Losing weight can help manage the disorder.
