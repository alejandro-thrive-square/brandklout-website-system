The Company

A love of diamonds has driven 28 years of excellence. Over the years we have earned a position as a preferred supplier to the world’s most prestigious jewellery and timepiece maisons, including direct supply to two of the five largest maisons by purchasing volume, one of them since our founding in 1998.

With offices holding inventory in Paris, Genève, and New York, and in-house diamond polishing in Jaipur, India, we are among the few suppliers that combine senior leadership close to our maison clients with a directly owned manufacturing atelier. This means faster quotations, faster feasibility and design studies, more direct communication, and full control over sensitive projects from sourcing through to delivery.

30% of our team have been with Excellent Facets since the start of the journey, and 100% of us share an unwavering commitment to excellence, proven by under-2% order return rates, over 99% early or on-time deliveries, and a perfect score in our most recent maison-ordered traceability and compliance audit.
