Sustainability

We Care.

Carbon Neutrality & Reporting

We are proud to have been certified carbon neutral since 2023, with 2024 certified and 2025 in progress. We report our emissions annually in accordance with the GRI Standards and aligned with the Watch & Jewellery Initiative 2030, and we are committed to a 42% reduction in absolute Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030 under the Science-Based Targets initiative.
‍
Built for our maisons’ obligations
Our reporting and traceability systems are built to support our maison partners’ obligations under emerging EU frameworks, including the CSRD and CSDDD, and we are working with the Aura Blockchain Consortium to enable a Digital Product Passport for the diamonds we supply. In line with the WJI 2030 Nature Roadmap, we also follow a structured framework to assess, reduce, and disclose our nature-related impacts across the value chain.

Science-Based Targets Initiative

We are part of the Science-Based Targets initiative, committing to a 42% reduction in absolute Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030 from a 2023 baseline. This target was approved through the SME-exclusive streamlined validation process. We are also committed to measuring and reducing Scope 3 emissions.
Additionally, our quick progress in sustainability has been acknowledged by the United Nations Global Compact and the U.S. Federal Department of Commerce. Our Director, Amay Sheth, has represented small and medium-sized enterprises as a panelist in webinars for both organizations.

Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions

No Poverty

We prioritize suppliers and subcontractors who offer above-market wages and positive working conditions, even if it means higher costs. This is especially crucial with vendors in India. Additionally, we support organizations like Diamonds Do Good and Share & Care Foundation, which empower individuals in economically challenged communities and bolster the natural diamond industry.

Good Health & Wellbeing

We adhere to the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, promoting safe and dignified working conditions. We also contribute to healthcare-focused charities, including Doctors Without Borders, UNICEF, and American Red Cross, that provide emergency relief and healthcare in impoverished regions, especially for children.

Quality Education

We offer lifelong learning opportunities to our team and their families, supporting education through upskilling and allowing team members to partially homeschool their children during work hours. Our philanthropic contributions to education-focused organizations, like UNICEF and Diamonds Do Good, help improve access to education in underprivileged communities.

Gender Equality

We support gender equality across our workforce and supplier base, and are signatories of the UN Women’s Empowerment Principles (WEPs), with 40% of our manufacturing workforce and one in three of our director-level team members being female.

Affordable & Clean Energy

We have transitioned to fossil fuel-free facilities by replacing diesel generators with clean energy sources, upgrading 70% of our lighting to energy-efficient units, and using LED lights. We also offset Scope 3 emissions by purchasing credits that support UNFCCC's Hazira Project, which replaced its use of fossil fuels with cleaner energy sources.

Decent Work & Economic Growth

We prioritize a positive work environment, paying above-market salaries, providing generous benefits, and promoting work-life balance across our New York, Paris, Genève, and Jaipur offices. We also prefer suppliers who offer positive conditions to their employees, even at a higher cost.

Industry Innovation & Infrastructure

Our operational practices use advanced technologies, like in-house machines to test for synthetic diamonds and iTraceiT, a blockchain-based traceability solution. This enhances the quality and integrity of our sourcing processes.

Reduced Inequalities

We are committed to equality and diversity, with 100% of our New York office staff identifying as underrepresented minorities. Our Equal Opportunity Employer Policy underscores our dedication to social justice and fairness in the workplace.

Our green growth initiative promotes tree planting to sequester carbon dioxide and boost biodiversity. We also provide separate appliances and options for our vegetarian and vegan staff.
