Diamonds are forever. Suppliers can be too.

A love of diamonds has driven 28 years of excellence. Over the years we have earned a position as a preferred supplier to the world’s most prestigious jewellery and timepiece maisons. With offices holding inventory in Paris, Genève, and New York, and a directly owned polishing atelier in Jaipur, India, we combine senior leadership close to our maison clients with full control over our manufacturing, ready to serve our global clientele.

30% of our team have been with Excellent Facets since the start of the journey, and 100% of us share an unwavering commitment to excellence, proven by industry-leading quality standards, under-2% return rates, and over 99% early or on-time deliveries.

As an industry leader, we supply our partners with a curated selection of finely cut natural mêlée diamonds from 0.60 mm to 3.80 mm. Alongside mêlée, we specialize in fancy color diamonds and unique stones, including a large inventory of pink diamonds sourced from Australia’s renowned Argyle mine.

Our History

1984

1984

Deepak arrives in the United States and joins a De Beers sightholder as a trainee. Over the next 10 years, he built his book of accounts selling VVS to I2 clarity diamonds in colors D to Z to many of the top 50 retailers, jewelry manufacturers and diamond wholesalers in North America.

1997

1997

Deepak starts his own business, Excellent Facets, with a sole focus on selling top-quality melee diamonds to the most quality-conscious maisons. He saw the potential of long-term relationships and customer satisfaction and felt an independent company would be the best structure to align with customer needs and his personal strengths over the long-term.

2003

2003

We became a Rio Tinto Select Diamantaire and received our first rough allocation from its Diavik Mine in Canada, which we sought in order to have a mine-to-market supply chain. This rough produced polished diamonds of 0.30cts to 2.00cts. We used it to cut custom shaped stones for customers. We worked as a Select Diamantaire until 2009, at which point the custom shaped project was completed. We initially polished the Rio Tinto rough in our Bangkok factory. After shutting the factory down, we moved manufacturing to a third-party audited and socially compliant factory in Qingdao Province, China.

2009

2009

As a customer of Rio Tinto Diamonds, we were given the opportunity to purchase pink diamonds directly from Australia’s Argyle Mine via tender. We were impressed with the mine’s incredible provenance and the beauty of its treasures. This marked our entry into the fancy color diamond business. From here, we expanded into other rare and important stones, both in fancy color and in white. Today we have a large inventory of Argyle pink diamonds, along with other important fancy color diamonds.

2023

2023

Amay Sheth, Deepak’s son, wound down the software company that he founded in order to join the family business. Amay was always excited about Deepak’s vision of a mine-to-market supply chain, and believed the time was right to implement it more thoroughly with traceability involved. He is also passionate about sustainability, and one of his first initiatives was auditing Excellent Facets existing emissions and investing in renewable energy projects to offset those emissions, making the company net zero for 2023.

2025

2025

Amay relocates to Paris to open Excellent Facets' second headquarters at 10 Place Vendôme, bringing inventory closer to the maisons for same-day delivery. He builds a local, French-speaking team to support existing and new maison relationships, deepening the company's presence at the heart of the European luxury market.

1995

1995

Deepak establishes his first recurring business of top-quality melee diamonds (0.18cts and below) with top maisons in North America. He realizes that this business is perfectly suited to his strengths because of the attention to detail and precision in grading required.

2002

2002

We started to manufacture our own melee diamonds, rather than solely buying polished from the market, in order to ensure compliance with local labor laws and promote a better work environment. This was accomplished by both starting our own diamond cutting and polishing factory in Thailand, which we later closed down, as well as by working with manufacturing subcontractors.

2004

2004

As part of our commitment to precision, we were early adopters of Sarine technologies for the measurement of each melee diamond’s table, depth, facet angles, and girdle thickness. This optimized the brilliance of each stone. We continue to evaluate and implement new technology in order to optimize polished outcomes, as well as to ensure each and every diamond entering our inventory is 100% natural.

2016

2016

We purchased two of the most important diamonds in the history of North America. One was the Diavik Foxfire, which was the largest white diamond mined in North America, weighing over 187 carats. The other was the Radiant North, which was the largest fancy color diamond mined in North America at the time. The Diavik Foxfire was exhibited at the world-renowned Smithsonian Institute in Washington, DC for three months, where it was placed next to the famous Hope Diamond.

2024

2024

Excellent Facets establishes a Swiss company and builds a local team to serve timepiece maisons, adding traceable cut-to-measure baguettes to its established expertise in finely calibrated round brilliant mêlée. The Genève office brings inventory and a dedicated point of contact close to our Swiss clients.

2026

2026

Excellent Facets opens a tech-enabled atelier in Jaipur, India, specializing in finely calibrated fancy shapes under 0.75 carats, cut-to-measure, custom cuts, and round brilliants. It is one of the few producers to handle these categories fully in-house, offering maison clients the confidentiality and discretion their most sensitive projects require.

Sustainability.

WE CARE.

At Excellent Facets, we recognize that sustainability is non-negotiable. Our dedication to sustainability influences every aspect of our business.

We are proud to have been certified carbon neutral since 2023, with 2024 certified and 2025 in progress.

Our responsible sourcing practices ensure transparency, with all diamonds accompanied by their mine of origin disclosed and uploaded to iTraceiT, a blockchain-based and tamper-proof traceability solution.

Furthermore, our India-based manufacturing facility undergoes regular audits for social compliance.

As early adopters of ethical practices, we were among the first companies in North America to attain certification from the Responsible Jewelry Council. Additionally, we actively participate as members of the Watch & Jewelry Initiative 2030, committed to shaping the future of our industry through sustainable practices.
