# Section flows per page type

The section order for every page type in the five specialty sitemaps ([specialty-sitemaps.txt](./specialty-sitemaps.txt)).
Each flow is the best combination of what 191 sampled client pages did on 2026-09-22: every section is
on at least half of the sampled pages of that type, or is a strong pattern from one of them. Section
names follow Relume. `{S}` is the page subject, `{City}` the location. Navbar and Footer are on every
page and left out below.

The ads combo (landing, consultation, thank you) is not here: its single source is
[scenario 3, step 02](../../scenario-3-ads-landing-pages/02-build-the-combo/02-build-the-combo.md).

Generated once from the retired `section-flows.py` on 2026-09-28; edit this file directly from now on.

## `home_general`

2. **About Section**: Short introduction of the lead dentist with photo, credentials and professional affiliation logos

4. **Benefits Section**: What sets the practice apart: same-day emergencies, sedation, in-house lab, most insurance accepted, financing
5. **How It Works Section**: Your first visit in four steps: book, exam and 3D imaging, treatment plan with written quote, start treatment
6. **Gallery Section**: Before and after slider of real patient smiles, sorted by treatment, linking to the full smile gallery
7. **Testimonial Section**: Video patient stories with named patients plus the review widget score
8. **Locations Section**: Every office as a card with address, map, hours and phone, linking to each location page
9. **FAQ Section**: Top questions: insurance, first visit cost, emergencies, sedation, payment plans
