# Scenario 0 · 04 QA

## Purpose

Prove the site on staging works the way a patient and the reviewer will use it, before anything goes
to production. Launch is blocked while any critical or high issue is open.

## Inputs

- The complete site on Instatic staging (step 03).
- `docs/facts.md`, the sitemap, the GHL forms list.

## Checklist

- [ ] Every breakpoint, including 390, 1920 and 2560
- [ ] Every link, button, slider and menu clicked
- [ ] GHL forms checked
- [ ] Accessibility
- [ ] Page speed
- [ ] Copy, spelling and facts
- [ ] Images match their text and none is overused (`image-reuse.mjs --base`)
- [ ] Content-fit pass run before the site goes to the reviewer
- [ ] Safari at the reviewer's size
- [ ] SEO head, headings, schema and alt text crawled
- [ ] Every BugHerd pin closed
- [ ] Developer QA review

## Steps

1. **Page list from the site.** Take routes from the sitemap or the site, never a typed list.
2. **Breakpoints.** Playwright at 390, 479, 767, 991, 1279, 1440, 1920 and 2560, plus a short window
   (735 to 768 tall). Zero sideways overflow at 390. Hide the accessibility widget while measuring.
3. **Click everything.** `gates/click-controls.mjs --base <staging>`: menus, sliders, arrows, phone and
   email links. Screenshots alone miss dead controls.
4. **Published, not local.** Gate the published staging page, including CMS template routes
   (`gates/check-pages.mjs --base`, `gates/verify-live.mjs`).
5. **Forms.** `gates/check-ghl-form.mjs`: each form loads on the staging domain, redirects to its
   thank-you page, has *save and exit* off and the consent fields. Don't submit test leads; they land
   in the practice's CRM.
6. **Facts.** `gates/facts-sweep.mjs`: phones, hours and addresses match `docs/facts.md` and the Google
   Business listing everywhere, including JSON-LD and the open-graph store.
   `gates/cross-client-grep.mjs --base`: no other practice's details anywhere.
7. **Accessibility.** Contrast on every page, also while animations play; 44px tap targets; reduced
   motion respected.
8. **Speed.** `gates/perf-budget.mjs` and PageSpeed on the core pages; fix what is slow (usually images).
9. **Copy.** Spelling, placeholder text, em dashes.
   **Images.** `gates/image-reuse.mjs --base <staging>` ends `0 problem(s)`; then read every photo
   against its heading at 1440 and fix anything below fit score 3
   ([`IMAGES.md`](../../../docs/IMAGES.md)).
   **Content-fit pass, before any human review** (first run West LA Implants, 2026-09-24). The
   reviewer should never be the one to find a photo contradicting its copy, a face cropped out of a
   hero, or a heading with one word on its last line. Back up first (git tag, API snapshot,
   `VACUUM INTO` copy of the database). Then render every page under reduced motion at 1440, 991
   and 390 and write per-page copy findings (overflow, runts, buttons, measure, tiny text) plus a
   contact sheet of every image placement's rendered crop at 1440 and 390 beside its heading, copy
   and alt. Reference: bk-west-la-implants `workflows/08-qa/.tmp/qa-fit.mjs` (`--base=<url>` for the
   published site). A model reads the sheets (about ten per subagent) and records MISMATCH, CROP,
   QUALITY, ALT and REPEAT findings, each with a fix from the client's own library. Fix at the
   source (focal-point crops as classes, never inline styles; one alt per asset, since Instatic
   stores alt per asset), rebuild, re-run until only accepted findings remain, and put the short
   list only a person can decide in `docs/qa-report.md`. First-run lessons: a portrait cover crops a
   2:1 photo to a third of its width, so centred subjects vanish (33 crops); a template fallback
   image repeated 8x on one index; `text-wrap: balance` on display type and `pretty` on copy cleared
   ~500 runts in one rule, but `balance` gives up past 6 lines. A first pass that only removed
   contradictions still left 167 of 370 placements below fit score 3.
10. **SEO crawl.** Titles, descriptions, one H1, heading order, schema, alt text.
11. **BugHerd.** Review with the browser extension (the script is blocked by Instatic's CSP). Close every
    pin at the end of each round; pull tasks by id to see the reviewer's browser and size. Decide
    each comment's scope before fixing it ([`BUGHERD.md`](../../../docs/BUGHERD.md)): if it says
    "all pages" or sits on a shared style or component, fix the source and check every page, then
    write the scope and the pages checked in the reply.
12. Asana task *developer QA review*: in progress + comment (confirm first).

## Conditionals

- **If the site has more than one office:** each office page shows only its own phone, map, hours and
  form.
- **If a reviewer reports a size bug:** reproduce it at their browser and viewport (Safari 1512×735 is
  common) with the accessibility widget off before changing anything.
- **If a note is not a bug** (for example `tel:` links on desktop): reply on the pin and say why.

## Do / Don't

- Do test in Safari at 1512×735.
- Do check the rendered page, not the raw HTML.
- Don't fix a site-wide comment only on the page it was pinned to.
- Don't mark QA done because the automated gates pass. Click through the site yourself.
- Don't fix anything on production.
- Don't send test leads through GHL forms.
