# Workflow: 06 - QA

## Client-Facing Overview
Before launch, we test the staged site across devices, links, forms, SEO basics, performance, and visual accuracy. This phase catches issues before visitors see the website.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Integrity Rule**: Any change to the Python executor or technical logic of this workflow must be documented here immediately.

## Operational Purpose
Verify the staged Webflow build against quality standards: pixel fidelity, CMS-first structure, responsiveness, functionality, SEO readiness, accessibility, and performance.
The QA surface changes by offer: full builds require full-site QA, migrations verify the outputs of `10`, `11`, and `12`, and improvement work verifies the scoped plan from `13`.

## Inputs From Sales / Previous Phase
- Webflow staging URL and build manifest.
- Approved Figma reference screenshots and final content.
- Known critical user paths, forms, analytics, and integration requirements.

## Execution Steps
1. Compare staging templates against approved designs across breakpoints.
2. Crawl pages for SEO, metadata, headings, links, schema, and alt text.
3. Test forms, CTAs, navigation, CMS edge cases, analytics, and responsive behavior.
4. Triage defects and block launch until critical and high-priority issues are resolved.

## Offer-Specific Adaptations
- **Development from Scratch**: Run full-site QA across design fidelity, content, CMS, responsiveness, forms, SEO, performance, accessibility, and launch readiness.
- **Figma to Webflow**: Prioritize visual fidelity against the approved Figma file, responsive behavior, CMS templates, interactions, forms, and SEO basics.
- **Migration to Webflow**: Verify migrated content against `11-content-export-import` and redirects/metadata against `12-migration-seo-redirects`.
- **Webflow Improvements & Updates**: Scope QA to the changed pages/components and regression surface defined in `13-improvement-intake-audit`.
