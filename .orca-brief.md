# REBUILD BRIEF — journey architecture

**The design direction has been corrected. This is an architecture change, not a polish pass.**

The client reviewed the current output and rejected it: *"a plain card website design"* rather than
*"an interactive journey"*, and asked for it to be much closer to the Dr. Nahlah site.

Do not patch the existing pages section by section. The problem is not that there is too little
animation — it is that the pages were composed as a vertical stack of coloured bands holding grids
of cards. Adding motion to that structure will not close the gap.

## 3. What has to change

- **Every page becomes 6–8 named chapters** with `data-chapter` and `data-chapter-name`, in an order
  that cannot be shuffled. Section class names describe the **story beat**, never the background
  colour. If a class is named for a colour or a spacing value, that section has not been designed
  yet. The standard gives a 6–8 chapter arc adapted for a paid dental landing page — use it as the
  starting shape, not a template to fill in.
- **At least three chapters per page where scroll drives the content** — pinned and scrubbed. Not
  triggered. Scroll should be the medium the story is told in, not transport past static content.
- **`IntersectionObserver` + a CSS class toggle is not choreography.** It fires once, it is binary,
  and it cannot express *how far through* something you are — which is what every scrubbed moment
  depends on. Use GSAP ScrollTrigger with `scrub:` and `pin:`.
- **Each chapter arrives and departs.** The reference scrubs an exit timeline on
  `start:'top top', end:'bottom top'` so leaving a chapter feels like leaving a room. A section that
  fades in and then sits there is a band with an animation on it.
- **Continuity devices**: reading-progress indicator, chapter tags (`Chapter 04 · The
  Transformation`), and theme shifts across the arc via `data-theme` rather than two alternating
  background tints forever.
- **Fold the loose bands in.** FAQ, financing and insurance belong *inside* the beat that answers
  the objection — usually The Path or The Invitation — not as their own sections.

## 4. Keep the content

The copy, the offers, the phone numbers, the doctor names, the credentials and the real reviews are

per-page call-tracking numbers stay distinct, and nothing about a real practice gets invented.

This is a change to how the page is built, not to what it says.

## 5. Verification — both gates, no exceptions

**Scrub proof (this is the new one).** Step-scroll each pinned chapter and assert the content is at
a **different intermediate state at 25%, 50% and 75%** of its range. Something that snaps between
two states is triggered, not scrubbed, and fails. Drive the page on a **focused** tab — background
tabs freeze transitions, IntersectionObserver and timers.

Also assert: pins release cleanly at both ends, the progress indicator tracks real document
progress, `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables motion without hiding content, and there are no
console errors.

**Screenshots.** Every page at 360 / 390 / 768 / 1024 / 1440 / 1920 into
`workflows/08-qa/.tmp/screenshots/<page-slug>/<width>.png`. Review the images. Zero horizontal
overflow at 360 and 390 — assert `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= window.innerWidth`. Paid
dental traffic is mostly mobile, so 360 and 390 matter more than 1920.

## 6. GSAP notes, so you do not rediscover these

- All GSAP plugins are free since 3.13 — ScrollTrigger, ScrollSmoother, SplitText included.
- **Register plugins in `<body>`, not `<head>`** — otherwise ScrollSmoother dies silently with no
  error and the page merely feels flat.
- Scrubbed scale must never land exactly on `1` — it causes edge flicker.
- Two scrubbed tweens writing the same property on the same element will fight. The reference has a
  comment about this; it stranded a panel twice already.
- **ScrollSmoother breaks full-page captures.** Capture per-viewport-step and stitch, or disable
  smoothing for the capture pass only — never ship that change.
- A fixed header's `z-index` can trap its own close button under an open drawer.
- Do not pipe capture scripts through `head`; SIGPIPE truncates the run.
