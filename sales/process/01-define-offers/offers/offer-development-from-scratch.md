# Offer: Development from Scratch

## 1. Overview
This offer applies when a client has no existing design, wireframe, or development work. They may have a document with copy, a rough brief, or sometimes nothing beyond a verbal description of what they need. The deliverable is a fully designed and developed Webflow website.

## 2. Client Profile
- No wireframe or design file exists.
- May have a copy document (Google Doc, PDF, Notion page) or no copy at all.
- May have brand assets (logo, colors, fonts) or need those defined as well.
- Typical Upwork keywords: "build a website from scratch", "need a website designed and developed", "looking for a Webflow designer and developer".

## 3. Scope of Work

### Phase 1: Discovery & Strategy
- Kickoff call to define goals, audience, and functionality requirements.
- Competitive analysis (2-3 competitor sites reviewed).
- Sitemap defining page structure and content hierarchy.
- Content strategy: determine what copy exists, what needs to be written, and by whom.

### Phase 2: Design
- Moodboard or style direction (if no brand guidelines exist).
- High-fidelity Figma designs: homepage + inner pages.
- Desktop and mobile responsive layouts.
- Up to 2 rounds of design revisions.

### Phase 3: Development
- Webflow build: pixel-perfect translation of approved Figma designs.
- Responsive implementation (desktop, tablet, mobile).
- CMS setup for dynamic content (blog, portfolio, team, etc.) if applicable.
- Animations and interactions using Webflow native interactions and GSAP where needed.
- SEO foundation: meta titles, descriptions, Open Graph tags, alt text structure.
- Forms, integrations (e.g., Calendly, Mailchimp), and third-party embeds as specified.

### Phase 4: QA & Launch
- Cross-browser testing (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).
- Performance optimization (image compression, lazy loading, minimal custom code).
- Client walkthrough of the Webflow Editor for content management.
- Domain connection and launch.

## 4. What the Client Needs to Provide
- Brand assets (logo, color palette, fonts) if they exist.
- Copy for all pages, OR approval to write copy at an additional cost.
- Any reference sites or visual direction preferences.
- Access to domain registrar for DNS configuration.

## 5. Pricing Model
- **$200 / page design + $200 / page development = $400 / page.**
- Canonical rates and formulas: [PRICING_TIMELINE_RULES.md](../../../PRICING_TIMELINE_RULES.md).
- Copywriting, brand identity work, and custom integrations are quoted separately.

## 6. Timeline Estimate
- **Duration = pages × 2 days** (1 design day + 1 dev day per page).
- Examples: landing page = 2 days; 5-page site = 10 days; 8-page site = 16 days.
- See [PRICING_TIMELINE_RULES.md](../../../PRICING_TIMELINE_RULES.md) for the canonical rule.

## 7. Differentiators
- The client gets a complete end-to-end service: from blank slate to live site.
- Figma design ownership is transferred at the end of the project.
- Webflow Editor training included so the client can manage content independently.
- No dependency on Framer, WordPress, or other platforms. Clean Webflow-native build.

## 8. Risks & Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Client delays on copy delivery | Set clear deadlines in the contract. Offer copywriting as an add-on. |
| Scope creep on design revisions | Cap revisions at 2 rounds per phase. Additional rounds billed hourly. |
| Unclear requirements | Discovery phase serves as a gatekeeper. No design starts without an approved sitemap. |
