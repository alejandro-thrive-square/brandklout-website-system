Use the Sales workflow `process/01-define-offers` when choosing, reviewing, or updating the service offer behind a proposal.

Steps:
1. Read `01-define-offers.md` and the relevant canonical offer file in `sales/process/01-define-offers/offers/`.
2. Match the lead request to the best offer.
3. Capture offer fit, assumptions, exclusions, pricing model, timeline, and client requirements.
4. Save lead-specific notes in `.tmp/`; do not alter offer files unless the offer itself is changing.
