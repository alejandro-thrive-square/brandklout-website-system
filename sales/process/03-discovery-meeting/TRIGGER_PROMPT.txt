Use the Sales workflow `process/03-discovery-meeting` when a lead responds positively to a proposal and needs a discovery meeting.

Steps:
1. Read `03-discovery-meeting.md`.
2. Prepare questions from the selected offer, proposal, and client context.
3. Capture meeting notes, strategy insights, risks, and contract inputs.
4. Move the opportunity to `04-contract-onboarding` when scope and next steps are clear.
