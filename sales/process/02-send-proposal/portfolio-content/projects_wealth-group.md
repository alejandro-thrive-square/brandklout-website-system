# Wealth Group

is a cryptocurrency trading and educational community. Founded in 2019, it aims to connect crypto traders and provide a hub for crypto success. The platform offers personalized guidance, real-time trading signals, live streams, educational resources, and exclusive trading challenges. The group has over 4,000 members and supports various trading styles, including futures, spot, and memecoin trading.
