# Amble

is a managed services provider that connects users with licensed healthcare providers for evaluations and treatment. The company offers online services and provides technology and administrative support for medical consultations and prescription logistics. It states that it has served over 100,000 members and focuses on services related to weight loss and anti-aging. It does not provide medical advice or act as a licensed healthcare facility or pharmacy.
