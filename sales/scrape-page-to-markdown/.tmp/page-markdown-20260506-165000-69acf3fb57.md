## Detail Process

I build a system that helps me manage every step in the journey. Breaking down into phases to make sure I have a strategic approach and resonate with your target audience.

[Book a Call](https://webzitestudio.webflow.io/book-a-call)

### Strategy

We define the objectives, audience and collect information to understand the business. Use that information to build a brand identity and story that is different from the competition.

In a user-centric approach, we write a selling speech and lay down the information based on the audience's pain points. We build a sitemap with the information structure of the entire website. Making sure the user can access the information easily.

Using a project management system designed to control each phase of the project and collaborate with a team of professionals to deploy their skills therefore helping us build an excellent platform.

### Branding

In this phase, we meticulously craft a unique brand identity and story that sets the business apart from the competition.

We will go from referencing, conceptualization, sketching and naming to comply a unique logo. Choosing typography, colors and design real use cases. Finally we build brand guidelines to use in every channel of the business.

### Wireframe

We built plain layouts from the sitemap created in the strategy session to distribute all the text and graphic resources we will need. All the information we collect so far will help us to work on the websites copy.

### Graphic Resources

In the wireframe we planned all the graphics we were going to need, so in this phase, we sketched any illustration or 3D so our team could create those resources based on the Brand guidelines.

### Design

Based on the Strategy, Brand guidelines and Wireframe we apply design principles to make the information easy to digest and actionable. We keep our team updated with the best design trends to enhance the experience of the audience.

We take into account mobile responsiveness and loading speed, since if we have very heavy graphics we will have to solve that problem in development.

### Develop

Here we build the platform with webflow, custom code, and any integration needed for the functionality of the website.

We make sure to be very organized with all the components so the project will be scalable and easy to update.

We use Webflow, JavaScript, Client-first framework, API connection, Tracking Scripts, and Page Speed Insights to make sure your website is high in performance.

### Launch

We go through a testing phase to fix all the errors and bugs, set up the hosting plan and the domain of the website and finally launch it.

### Follow Up

This is the period after the project has finished, in which we offer 3 weeks of free support. A subscription to update the platform in case you want to make a new landing page or create a new feature.
